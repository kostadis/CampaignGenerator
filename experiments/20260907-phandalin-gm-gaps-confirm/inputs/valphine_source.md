# Securing the Loan

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Brewbarry uses his imposing presence to push through a business loan from the Counting House despite the banker's reservations about profit margins.
- Aurelan expresses concern over the high shipping rates of the Lionshield Coster and suggests the party look into House Margaster for cheaper distribution.
- Asked how he intends to ship his goods, Brewbarry is indignant: "What do I look like? Some merchant? You know, the logistics guy? No."
- Brewbarry uses his imposing presence to intimidate Aurelan into processing the loan despite the banker's reservations about profit margins — "You're not gonnahelp me, I'm gonna help you, old man. Don't get things confused in that little head of yours" — punctuated by pulling out his halberd and tapping it in his other hand.
- Brewbarry suspects a kickback scheme in the Margaster referral: "Did you have, like, some sweet deal with this other guy? … I have a wealth management consultant, she has a supply company, she's gonna borrow money from you, you're gonna lend it to her, and you're gonna get this done."
- Vukradin smooths the negotiation with a 17 on persuasion: "Surely there's an interest rate that you would find acceptable, given the increased risk of, you know, a lower margin distributor."
- Valphine points out that Aurelan's calculations omit two additional factors — the temple markup and the church's bulk demand (paraphrase; audio garbled) — prompting Aurelan's double-take: "Oh, you are one of the blessed of Lathander. … And you're a drow."
- Aurelan signs the paperwork for a significant loan to kickstart the bathrobe production business with Linene as the shipping agent — a small crowd having gathered around the spectacle by this point.
- The party learns that the Lionshield Coster's shipping issues stem from the failure of the Neverwinter Commission's interplanar displacement manifold, on which the Coster's distribution depended as its primary client; Aurelan knows only that "some kind of system … stopped working some time ago for reasons that are obscure," and that Coster prices have risen considerably as a result.
- Brewbarry assigns Aurelan homework: "You're gonna go to House Margaster, you're gonna find out what's going on, and I'm gonna come back tomorrow, and you're gonna tell me how I can fix it." Aurelan capitulates: "I intend to find out what I can by tomorrow. You can rely on the Counting House."
- A wealthy client in the bank, dressed in silk imitations of Uthgardtian hides, sincerely admires Brewbarry's authentic barbarian attire — mistaking it for a high-end costume and praising his "commitment to the bit." — "What a genuine costume!" He then tries to mimic Brewbarry's stance, awkwardly, and is warned: "Are you touching my clothes? Be careful," before being pointedly ignored.
