# A Banker's Revelation

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Outside the Spire of the Morninglord, the party meets with Aurelan Vance, who provides critical information regarding a missing gnome named Bimble.
- Aurelan Vance catches up with the party outside the Spire of the Morninglord, appearing out of breath after visiting the counting house, patting himself down into proper decorum before speaking.
- He explains what moved him: "Valphine's sermon was inspiring... it inspired me to action." He had planned to wait for the party at the bank, but instead went over Bimble's paperwork the very night before.
- Brewbarry and Aurelan establish a friendly rapport, with Aurelan asking, "Is it Mr. Brewbarry? Is that the correct appellation? I'm sorry, I'm not familiar with Guardian norms" — and Brewbarry replying, "So just call me Brew, that's fine." Aurelan reciprocates: "And since we're on such friendly terms, my friends call me Ori."
- Aurelan reveals he investigated paperwork for a gnome named Bimble, pulling out a piece of paper to show the correct spelling — which does nothing to stop the party from calling the gnome "Bimbo" for the rest of the evening.
- The banker explains that Bimble was on a maintenance roll for 11 years until payments abruptly stopped seven tendays ago — "on the day, not the tenday" — without severance, final reconciliation, or forwarding instructions: "That is not how a contract ends... Brew, that is how a contract is abandoned!"
- Aurelan notes that Bimble has not banked, borrowed, or left the city, suggesting someone is keeping him locally: "Your Harper friends would tell you the same if you had any, so somebody is keeping him."
- Aurelan advises the party to find who is feeding the gnome to find the gnome himself.
- Brewbarry grimly counters, "Sounds like he's feeding worms," but promises: "We'll get to the bottom of it, my friend."
- Soma remembers where she used to get the best turtle soup in the whole area — the Board Laid Bare, run by Lim — as a place with good prices, good food, and good atmosphere where a small-business proprietor might know how a hidden man could be fed.
