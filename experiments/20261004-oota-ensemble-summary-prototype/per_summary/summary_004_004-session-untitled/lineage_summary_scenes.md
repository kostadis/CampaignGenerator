# Chapter 004

### Looting the Fallen Drow 004.01

With the chaos of battle still ringing in their ears, the party wasted no time in searching the bodies of the three fallen drow guards. They agreed that henceforth every fallen enemy would be searched carefully for useful equipment—and for traps. Two of the drow wore chain shirts, which Thorin and Gyrgum claimed, while Sarith picked up the studded leather armor from one of the fallen warriors. The bodies also yielded three short swords, three hand crossbows, and a shield. Gyrgum took one of the hand crossbows, along with the three poisoned bolts found on the elite warrior Imbros. Imbros also carried a vial of poison that could be applied to a weapon to inflict additional harm. Daz briefly considered armor but discovered that wearing it without proficiency would hinder his physical abilities and prevent him from casting spells, so he wisely declined. Before the group could move, Gyrgum called upon divine power to mend Zalthir's grievous wounds, restoring him to full health but exhausting Gyrgum's available magic.

Before they could fully regroup, the sky above Velkynvelve erupted into madness. Three winged demons—vrocks—came screaming out of the darkness, fighting among themselves above the outpost. Daz recognized the creatures as demons, an alarming discovery because demons did not ordinarily appear in such numbers in the region. The drow and their quaggoth servants scrambled to their defensive positions, no longer concerned with their prisoners in the slightest. The companions recognized that they could not survive a fight against the creatures and decided to use the confusion to escape.

The party briefly considered retrieving the remaining equipment confiscated when they were imprisoned, but doing so would have required crossing to another tower while the demons attacked. Thorin cared chiefly about a sentimental pendant carved from a giant's tooth, only to learn that an object small enough to have remained around his neck would not have been taken. With nothing else worth dying for, the group abandoned the rest of its former gear. They also considered setting fire to the outpost, but Sarith—flickering between lucidity and madness—insisted that the quicker they left, the better, then cackled about how tasty revenge would be. The group agreed that damaging the outpost would only give the drow more reason to pursue them.

- Thorin establishes a standing policy for future encounters: whenever something dies, the party searches it.

- Thorin puts away his studded leather armor after upgrading to a chain shirt.
- Sarith the Drow claims a set of studded leather armor from one of the fallen warriors.
- The party discovers a shield, three short swords, and three hand crossbows.
- Gyrgum already has a shield and sword but takes one of the hand crossbows.
- On the body of the Drow Elite Warrior Imbros, the party finds a vial of poison and three poisoned crossbow bolts.
- Gyrgum takes the three poisoned bolts.
- The party learns that applying poison to a weapon requires time in combat but can provide additional damage.
- Daz considers wearing armor, then discovers that wearing armor without proficiency would impose disadvantages on physical checks and prevent him from casting spells. As the party's wizard, he elects to remain unarmored.
- The group confirms that it has no shared coins or other meaningful wealth from its previous adventures.
- Gyrgum uses divine magic to heal Zalthir's severe wounds, restoring him to full health but expending his remaining spellcasting power.
- The party remembers how vulnerable they are as first-level adventurers and begins considering an immediate escape.
- Daz identifies three flying demons, known as vrocks, appearing out of nowhere and fighting among themselves.
- The presence of several demons in the cavern is deeply abnormal and frightening.
- Once warned that the creatures are demons, the drow stop worrying about their escaped prisoners and rush to defensive positions.
- Quaggoths also begin appearing amid the confusion.
- The party considers retrieving the equipment confiscated during its imprisonment, but the cache lies in another tower.
- Thorin's only irreplaceable possession is a pendant carved from a giant's tooth, a reminder of his family. Because it was small enough to remain around his neck, the drow did not confiscate it.
- The party decides to abandon its non-essential gear and escape while the drow are distracted.
- Topsy and Turvy reveal their nature as wererats by transforming into rats and jumping down to the lower levels.

### The Descent from Velkynvelve 004.02

Topsy and Turvy, the deep gnome twins who had been imprisoned alongside the party, revealed a startling secret: they were wererats. Telling the party, “we'll meet you downstairs,” the twins transformed into rats and leapt into the darkness below. The others debated descending through the giant spider webs in the manner of the drow, using their silk rope, or entering the water below. Remembering the giant spiders lurking among the webs, they chose the waterfall and pool instead.

#### The party chooses a dangerous descent to bypass the drow defenses and encounters a lurking threat in the water below.

- The party considers burning or otherwise sabotaging Velkynvelve while the drow are distracted.
- Sarith advises against sabotaging the outpost, suggesting that a clean escape is better than inciting further drow vengeance.
- Sarith oscillates between sanity and madness, first insisting on immediate flight and then cackling that revenge would be “so tasty.”
- The group agrees that setting the outpost ablaze would make the drow more determined to hunt them.
- The party discusses using its silk rope or the existing spider silk to descend.
- The drow normally jump into the giant web below and use its silk to reach the lower levels.
- Remembering the giant spiders inhabiting the web, the party chooses to bypass it.
- The group instead descends toward the pool at the bottom of the waterfall, effectively treating the route as a water slide.
- The demon attack remains sufficiently chaotic that the drow do not interfere with the descent.
- Upon landing in the water, the party is confronted by a predatory gray ooze.
- Because they were watching for danger, the adventurers are not surprised by its appearance.

### Escape from Velkynvelve 004.03

The water was not empty. A gray ooze emerged near the escape route, but because the party had anticipated danger, it did not surprise them. The creature struck Thorin for a punishing blow, and Eldeth warned that its corrosive body could devour his newly acquired armor. Although Thorin wanted to fight back, the companions realized that the ooze moved far more slowly than they did. One after another they disengaged and ran, leaving it thrashing futilely behind them.

Their escape was not yet complete. A badly wounded vrock descended from the battle above and landed directly beside the fleeing prisoners. The party continued to withdraw, but Ront refused to run. Humiliated by his imprisonment and determined to prove himself a powerful warrior, the orc attacked with his great axe and dealt the demon a solid blow. Shuushar refused to follow him into the suicidal battle. The vrock answered with its talons and killed Ront. It then menaced Daz with its beak before taking flight, apparently deciding that the wizard was not worth the effort of killing.

The survivors sprinted into the tunnels, and just like that, they were free. Their exhilaration lasted only until they took stock of their circumstances: they were stranded deep in the Underdark without food, water, money, or a map, accompanied by former prisoners who might or might not remain allies. Daz took what comfort he could from his ability to see through darkness for 120 feet.

#### The party navigates a chaotic battlefield filled with demons and drow to make their final escape from the prison outpost.

- The gray ooze attacks Thorin first and deals twelve points of damage.
- Sarith and Shuushar favor fleeing toward the Darklake rather than heading along the route toward Menzoberranzan.
- Gyrgum disengages and runs rather than attempting to fight the ooze.
- Daz follows after confirming that there is no need to fight the giant spiders overhead.
- Thorin wants to strike back but chooses to flee after recognizing that the ooze is much slower than the party.
- Eldeth warns Thorin that gray oozes eat armor, strengthening his desire to preserve his newly acquired chain shirt.
- Zalthir and the other companions also disengage, leaving the creature to reach futilely through the water after them.
- A rock tumbles nearby as the fugitives bunch together along their chosen escape route.
- A wounded vrock demon lands beside the group before they can completely leave the battlefield.
- The party is warned that even badly injured, the demon is far beyond what they should attempt to fight.
- The adventurers continue withdrawing, with Daz forced to disengage because the vrock lands directly beside him.
- Ront, determined to recover his pride after the humiliations of captivity, remains behind and attacks the demon with his great axe.
- Ront's blow deals nine points of damage and leaves the vrock looking even more wounded.
- Shuushar refuses to follow Ront into the fight, while Stool follows Shuushar away from the danger.
- The vrock retaliates against Ront, striking him down with its talons and killing him.
- The demon menaces Daz with its beak but flies away, deciding that he is not worthy of the time and effort required to kill him.
- The party successfully flees into the tunnels of the Underdark.
- The group's initial joy at being free gives way to the realization that it has no food, water, money, or map and cannot be certain which former prisoners are truly friends.
- Daz notes that his 120-foot darkvision should at least let him see many threats before they kill him.

### Trekking into the Underdark 004.04

After an exhausted rest, the party began learning the realities of Underdark travel. Twisting tunnels, uneven ground, climbs, and indirect routes reduced travel to eight miles per day at a fast pace, six at a normal pace, or four while moving slowly. Fast travel prevented foraging and made danger harder to notice, while slow travel allowed stealth and improved the group's chances of finding food. Becoming lost was a serious threat, so Sarith stepped forward as navigator and appeared to be telling the truth about his knowledge of the passages. The party also arranged a marching order, keeping sturdier companions toward the front and the unarmored Daz farther back.

The group learned of the Faerzress, a strange and ancient magical energy that suffused parts of the Underdark. It bathed affected passages in dim light, protected creatures from divination, and made teleportation both unreliable and physically dangerous. Even a successful attempt to teleport could go badly astray. The phenomenon helped explain why the Underdark's powers could not simply teleport armies into one another's strongholds.

The survivors then considered where to go. Waterdeep remained the distant ideal, but their practical choices were Underdark settlements. Topsy and Turvy did not want to return to Blingdenstone after a disagreement with its inhabitants concerning their “profession,” reinforcing the widespread suspicion that the twins were thieves. Gracklstugh lay roughly twenty-eight days away, while Sloobludop—the home sought by Shuushar—was only eight days distant. Sarith explained that the Darklake was not a single lake but an enormous network of underground rivers, canals, natural tunnels, and flooded caverns extending for more than a hundred miles, with ceilings miles high and unfathomable depths. Reaching it would allow the party to find or steal a boat and travel onward toward Gracklstugh or Blingdenstone. Buppido approved because the route could eventually take him to Gracklstugh. The party therefore chose Sloobludop as its first destination and set out at a normal pace.

Stool, the frightened young myconid sprout, chose Gyrgum as his “bestest friend” and protector. Too small to hold the orc's hand comfortably, Stool instead walked as close beside him as possible, hoping Gyrgum would keep him safe and eventually take him home. Gyrgum did not know what responsibility he had accepted, but he was unwilling to reject a terrified childlike creature.

- Fast travel hinders perception and entirely prevents foraging.
- Slow travel allows the party to move stealthily, improve its foraging, and potentially surprise or bypass creatures.
- The party discusses marching order. Thorin offers to travel at the front, Zalthir volunteers for the front or second position, and the unarmored Daz prefers the third rank.
- The party agrees that its player characters will generally bear the brunt of danger unless they deliberately assign one of the NPC companions a particular responsibility.

- Without a navigator familiar with the Underdark, travelers can wander randomly for hours or unknowingly travel in circles.
- Someone in the group is assumed to be mapping its progress so that the route can potentially be retraced.
- The group learns about the Faerzress, a mysterious and ancient magical energy that suffuses portions of the Underdark.
- Faerzress provides dim illumination and gives creatures resistance against divination magic.

- Sarith recommends the Darklake because a boat would provide access toward Gracklstugh, Blingdenstone, and other settlements.

### The Tongue of Madness 004.05

While foraging, the group discovered a strange edible fungus resembling a large human tongue. Sarith praised it as “the tastiest thing in the world,” while Shuushar solemnly claimed that eating it allowed one to connect with the gods. Thorin, trusting his strong constitution and resistance to poison, volunteered as the guinea pig. He sensed something unusual but resisted the effect, swallowed normally, and found the mushroom quite tasty.

Daz tried it next and was not so fortunate. For the following hour, he compulsively said every thought that entered his head. When everyone began laughing, his thoughts settled into a bewildered loop: “why are all these people laughing at me?”—which only made them laugh harder. Seeing that the effect was harmless, the rest of the party ate as well. The tunnels filled with involuntary chatter, while Zalthir attempted a monk's discipline and instead repeatedly announced, “I'm meditating.” Daz retained some of the fungus for possible use in future potions or tricks.

#### While foraging for food, the party encounters a strange fungus that leads to a bout of involuntary honesty.

- The party discovers an edible fungus known as the Tongue of Madness, which looks like a large human tongue.
- Sarith describes it as “the tastiest thing in the world.”
- Shuushar claims, “It is said that when you eat this mushroom, you connect with the gods.”
- The companions initially consider gathering it without eating it.
- Thorin volunteers as a guinea pig because of his strong constitution and resistance to poison.
- Thorin resists the fungus's effect, notices only a fleeting strange sensation, and finds it quite tasty.
- Daz eats it next and begins compulsively speaking every thought aloud.
- The others' laughter causes Daz to wonder repeatedly why everyone is laughing at him, which only intensifies their amusement.
- The effect is revealed to be a familiar, harmless practical joke among the denizens of the Underdark.
- Daz retains some of the fungus in his sack for possible use in mixing potions.
- Once the risk appears limited to involuntary speech, the rest of the group eats it.
- For the next hour, the party blathers thoughts and nonsense without restraint.
- Zalthir attempts to quiet his thoughts through meditation but instead compulsively repeats, “I'm meditating.”
- After witnessing the effect firsthand, the party recognizes Tongue of Madness and will not need to repeat the experiment.
- The scene ends abruptly as the party encounters a sudden rockfall near the end of the day's travel.

### The Rockfall in the Underdark 004.06

As the day drew to a close, the tunnel betrayed them. Without warning, the ceiling gave way and heavy stone crashed onto the travelers. Those caught in the collapse suffered grievous injuries. Daz was knocked unconscious beneath the rubble, while Gyrgum was reduced to the brink of death but remained conscious through his orcish Relentless Endurance. Buppido and several other companions were also pinned. Zalthir and Thorin avoided the worst of the collapse and were left to organize a rescue. Every trapped victim faced further crushing injuries if left buried for another round, forcing the uninjured survivors to decide whom to dig out first.

#### As the party travels through the dark tunnels, a sudden rockfall traps several members of the group under heavy rubble.

- A sudden collapse of the tunnel ceiling sends heavy stones raining down on the party.
- Anyone unable to evade the collapse suffers ten points of damage.
- Daz and Gyrgum are caught directly beneath the falling debris and sustain significant injuries.
- Daz is reduced to zero hit points and knocked unconscious under the rubble.
- Daz's first death saving throw succeeds, but he remains buried and helpless.
- Gyrgum is also reduced to zero hit points, but his orcish Relentless Endurance allows him to remain conscious with one hit point.
- Several companions, including Buppido, are also pinned beneath the fallen stone.
- Zalthir and Thorin avoid the collapse and remain free to help.
- The trapped victims are marked out so the group can identify who requires rescue.
- Freeing someone requires an unburied character to spend time digging that person out.
- Anyone left beneath the rubble for another round will suffer an additional `1d6` points of crushing damage.
- The survivors must rapidly decide which companions to rescue first.
- The party is expected to reach second level after surviving the encounter.
