# Chapter 037 Slime, Spirits, and Svirfneblin Squabbles

### Discussion in Diggermattock Hall 037.01

The party found themselves in Diggermattock Hall, where Chief Dorbo and Senni engaged in a heated discussion about the future of Blingdenstone. Chief Dorbo, leader of the mining coalition, explained that mining had proceeded apace since the gnomes returned, but ooze infestations were hindering both mining operations and the construction of housing for the community. Senni, representing the non-mining population, confronted him about his failure to adequately address the problem. Dorbo insisted that he was taking action—by volunteering the adventurers to solve it.

During the discussion, the party reaffirmed themselves as the Ember Vanguard before considering a cooler counterpart to the name. After suggestions including the Pale Enclave, Cool Enclave, and Ash Enclave, they settled for the moment on the Ash Enclave, with possible phoenix imagery. They also questioned whether the bustling gnomes around them were productive or merely busy, a distinction the gnomes themselves might not have known.

A guard explained that the party should travel through the Traders' Grotto and investigate beyond a stone wall near the area from which the oozes seemed to be coming. The conversation also revealed tensions regarding the Goldwhisker Clan, a group of gnomes rumored to be wererats. The Goldwhiskers had occupied part of the area before Dorbo's faction returned and drove out the Drow, after which the newcomers effectively excluded the clan from its former territory. Chief Dorbo refused to cooperate with them, while Senni appeared more open to reconciliation. The party dryly characterized the situation as colonization and joked that the Goldwhiskers had merely failed to make sufficiently efficient use of some prime real estate—an argument Dorbo would have appreciated.

### Journey to the Cemetery 037.02

Before embarking on the ooze hunt, the party decided to honor a promise made to Pelek, a ghost who had been traveling with them. Buppido had killed Pelek, and Gyrgum carried a small bag of his surviving bones—“Catholic relic size,” rather than a complete skeleton—in the hope of finding a proper resting place. Pelek addressed Gyrgum, who confirmed that he still had the bones, and asked whether they could finally visit the cemetery.

### Pelek's Interment 037.03

A guard directed them to the cemetery beside the Temple of the Ruby in the Rough, dedicated to Segojan Earthcaller, the Svirfneblin god of deep earth and nature. There they met Glyphic Shroomlight, a priest who was initially hesitant to inter the remains of someone he did not know. Gyrgum jingled the bag to establish that he did, in fact, possess Pelek's bones. When Pelek's ghost materialized, Glyphic realized that refusing might leave him haunted for years and quickly made an exception. He directed the party to the catacombs below, asking only that Glabbagool remain upstairs to prevent accidents among the graves. Glabbagool agreed: “Okay. But if you need anything cleaned, I'm here.”

In the catacombs, Pelek expressed profound relief at reaching the end of his journey after being murdered by “Buppido, who is really an annoying person.” He had feared that he was permanently lost and doomed to torment people forever. Gyrgum found a quiet corner where the remains would not be stepped on, scooped out a shallow resting place, performed an appropriate dragon-themed ritual, and planted a gold coin with the bones as the foundation for his word. Pelek told the group, “It's been very pleasant to travel with all of you. But I really do want to end my journey.” After the party said their goodbyes, his spirit faded away, leaving them with a warm, comforting feeling that they had done the right thing.

### Encounter with Burrow Warden Jadger 037.04

Almost immediately, another ghost appeared: Burrow Warden Jadger, a three-foot-tall spirit who had once defended Blingdenstone against the Drow, oozes, and countless other Underdark threats. Jadger identified Zalthir as a potentially excellent asset because the monk carried no weapons. He explained that the Burrow Wardens had been Blingdenstone's first and last line of defense, with most dying during the Drow invasion and the rest during the gnomes' flight from the city. Since Blingdenstone's recapture, he had taken it upon himself to establish a new generation of Wardens and was secretly training promising young gnomes.

Jadger offered the party a bargain: for every ghost they put to rest in the community, he would answer one question. Pelek counted as their first completed task, and Jadger knew of two more ghosts they could help if they wanted further answers. The party immediately became anxious about accidentally wasting their lone question, treating the offer like a dangerous genie arrangement. Jadger indignantly clarified, “I'm not some trivial genie. I am Burrow Warden Jadger.” He assured them that he knew things about the Underdark “that other people have forgotten were things.”

The party chose to ask Jadger how best to deal with the ooze infestation. He explained that oozes were normally solitary creatures that stayed in their own corners unless disturbed; a large, coordinated mass of them was profoundly abnormal. His initial advice was to send a carefully organized strike force to investigate what was directing this behavior. When Zalthir mentioned a possible connection to Juiblex, Jadger offered further clarification in exchange for a demonstration of a monk technique he could teach his young trainees. Zalthir obliged, impressing the ghost, although Jadger began mimicking the movements with his fists in all the wrong ways.

After an extended, rambling demon story, Jadger explained the distinction he considered crucial: “Devils make deals,” personally corrupting people who seek ungodly power, while demons imbue benighted souls with terrifying power and then delight in the widespread destruction they cause. He advised the party to search for the person or thing motivating the oozes. He did not believe Juiblex was directly controlling them; instead, the demon had probably empowered a proxy or intermediate agent. Jadger also recommended that, once they gathered evidence, they speak with a young gnome he was mentoring who would soon become the leader of the new Burrow Wardens.

### Arrival at Traders' Grotto 037.05

With Jadger's counsel in mind, the self-appointed “ooze crew” made their way to the Traders' Grotto, Blingdenstone's bustling central market. A sergeant guided them through the cavern, proudly explaining that fungi had once grown in its moist environment until a medusa's spell petrified them centuries ago. Merchants now operated makeshift stalls among the stone growths, hawking and bartering their wares while arriving caravans lowered cargo into warehouses carved beneath the grotto. Ordinary goods and some common—and perhaps a few uncommon—potions were available, though it was not a magical-item shop.

The merchants eyed Glabbagool suspiciously and debated what to make of the outsiders and the intelligent cube at the rear of their formation. Meanwhile, the party continued workshopping its name, moving from the Ash Enclave to alternatives such as the Ash Vanguard, Ash Peloton, and the unfortunately phrased Ash Rearguard.

### Gelatinous Cube Attack in Traders' Grotto 037.06

Suddenly, several non-sentient gelatinous cubes oozed out of the rocks, causing mass panic. Gnomes scattered, and some pointed accusingly at Glabbagool while he tried to explain that he was not one of the attackers. He dismissed the hostile cubes as “mindless, silly creatures” giving intelligent gelatinous cubes a bad name. He advised the party not to be engulfed, to use acid protection if they had it, and to pull out anyone who became trapped as quickly as possible.

The party sprang into action. Zalthir rushed forward, struck one cube with two powerful blows, and dashed back before its pseudopod could hit him. Thorin kept his distance and hurled darts, while Gyrgum raised his holy symbol and channeled Divine Spark as necrotic force. The cubes advanced slowly but relentlessly, engulfing gnome merchants. Some victims managed to struggle free, but one gnome died and began dissolving inside a cube. As the crowd fled, Glabbagool began making his way toward the battle to demonstrate that he was firmly on “team sentience.”

Once the civilians and vulnerable merchandise were clear of the blast, Daz carefully placed a Fireball among the cubes. Their abysmal agility left them badly burned; one partially avoided the blast, while the others took its full force. Zalthir then activated his Eldritch Claw Tattoo, causing tentacle-like extensions to grow from his hands and allowing him to strike at range. Glabbagool exclaimed, “You've got pseudopods too,” and sincerely asked whether Zalthir was an ooze. Zalthir answered, “No, no, just an aspiring ooze. Let's say that,” prompting the observation that not everyone could be an ooze, but everyone could aspire to it.

As the fight continued, Thorin moved closer to keep throwing darts despite Dawnbringer questioning his performance, while Gyrgum struck again with Divine Spark. Daz positioned a second Fireball to catch the remaining cubes without hitting anyone else. The blast dissolved one cube and left the last with a single hit point. Zalthir casually finished it from afar with his eldritch claw just as Thorin was preparing to strike. Thorin complained, “Damn it. Let me have this.”
