# Chapter 020

### Chaos at the Egg Chamber 020.01

Deep within the Underdark, the party found themselves locked in a desperate struggle to protect a mysterious and coveted egg hidden within a dark cavern. The Derro cultists, their minds twisted beyond reason by the manipulations of sinister forces, swarmed the chamber with fanatical purpose. From the shadows, hidden spies loosed crossbow bolts at the party's allies, one bolt striking Eldeth with brutal force and dropping her to the edge of death. As if the ambush were not enough, a beholder-kin creature known as a spectator materialized from the darkness, its many eyes gleaming with malice as it joined the assault.

Chaos reigned across the battlefield as the magical darkness that blanketed the chamber turned the fight into a fumbling, frantic melee. Daz conjured a massive hand of compacted earth that surged up from the ground and seized the spectator, pinning it in place and buying the party precious moments. Zalthir, gifted with the ability to perceive through the supernatural darkness, moved with deadly precision, striking at enemies that could not even see him coming. Meanwhile, Gyrgum called upon his ghostly ally to defend the egg, and Zalthir unleashed a terrifying breath that swept across the chamber in a wide cone, killing the fanatical Derro leader Plinki outright and leaving the survivors blinded and reeling.

#### A chaotic battle erupts around a mysterious egg as Derro spies and a spectator ambush the party in the darkness.
- The party discusses the madness of the Derro and their history as slaves to Mind Flayers.
  - Opening situation, carried over from chapter 19: Plinki has been grappled (by Zalthir, at the end of chapter 19), Gyrgum was sitting over the eggs, and the crazed derro have all concluded Gyrgum "has to be killed. And his heart dropped over the egg."
  - The GM reads the derro lore: they cooperate "out of necessity and when riled up by a charismatic leader," and warns "expecting to have a rational conversation may be too much."

  - Eldeth takes 12 points of damage and is down to four hit points.
- A second spy attempts to shoot Daz but misses.
  - Gyrgum's player: "Can't have just one."

- Thorin engages a Derro warrior, utilizing his longsword to impose disadvantage on the enemy's next attack.
  - Thorin's player is learning his new Sentinel feat ("defend other people. People can't disengage").

  - DC 11 Dexterity save; the damage die rolled low, but it kills Plinki and inflicts the blinded condition; others take one or two points.

### Chaos in the Darkness 020.02

With Plinki dead, the battle shifted, but the danger was far from over. A skilled derro Master Thief proved to be a tenacious foe, shrugging off blow after blow as Zalthir repeatedly grappled and wrestled him across the battlefield. Thorin planted himself like an immovable wall, and when the Master Thief attempted to slip away, Thorin's sentinel instincts snapped into action, landing a punishing strike that rooted the thief in place. Daz, ever calculating, had already used a burst of teleportation magic to vanish from the fray, secretly concealing the egg beneath his robes as the Derro cultists scrambled blindly in the darkness, screaming about their missing prize.

#### The party engages in a frantic battle against Derro and a Master Thief while magical darkness obscures the battlefield.
- The party asks about the Master Thief; GM: "think of a fifth-level rogue," and suggests threatening him now that his boss is dead, noting he's "a good target for possession by the ghost."
- Zalthir grapples the Master Thief and maneuvers him toward Thorin, attempting to drop him off a ledge.
  - Unarmed strike with advantage hits; GM: dropping him off the ledge deals an additional 16 damage and knocks him prone.
- The ghost's first possession attempt on the Master Thief fails when he makes his saving throw ("i'll give you the impersonation anyhow ooh see i'm good at ghosts").
- Gyrgum unleashes a caustic spray that coats several enemies in corrosive fluid.
  - The GM steers him from Guiding Bolt (the thief is 10 feet below in darkness) to Tasha's Caustic Brew; Gyrgum's player notes he'd misread it as "caustic rope."
  - Rules digression on lines vs. beams: a square counts as hit if 50% or more is covered; "if it says five feet wide then use the beam."
  - The GM reminds everyone they are beneficiaries of a Bless spell.
  - DC 14 Dex save: two make it, one (#4) fails and takes 2d4 (7).
- A spy climbs up, senses the ghost, "freaks out," attacks it, and misses; a derro "too stupid to do anything otherwise" attacks Thorin and misses.
- Thorin kills derro #6 (the one with fewer hit points) for 7.
- The spectator manages to break free from the magical earthen grasp.
  - Strength 8, but it rolls a 17: "It was a low probability chance." The party helpers then hit it twice, leaving it at one hit point.
- Daz fires a barrage of magical force darts, killing one Derro and wounding another nearby enemy.
  - Second-level Magic Missile, four darts; the second target takes 9. GM: "Just make sure they didn't screw around with magic missile. If they did, I will not forgive them."
  - The GM rules Darkness blocks sight both from inside and from outside.
- Daz climbs the stairs and picks up the mysterious egg.
- The Derro enemies scramble in the darkness, fumbling blindly as they realize the egg has been taken.
  - One screams "where is the egg?" and spends his action searching; his plan had been to grab the egg and run.
- The ghost summoned by the party attempts to intimidate the attackers, while the Derro scream for the sacrifice of the cleric of Bahamut.
  - "die, cleric of Bahamut" — the swing misses. The acid-coated derro spends his action scraping off the acid "because, you know, he can't think strategically."

### Battle in the Dark for the Egg 020.03

The crazed Derro grew more frantic with every passing moment, shrieking that whoever held the egg held absolute power over them all. One cultist howled for the sacrifice of Gyrgum, the cleric of Bahamut, demanding his heart be cut out and fed to some dark entity. The ghost responded to this outburst with a voice that chilled the air, and the cultists faltered. Gyrgum unleashed streams of corrosive acid that coated the fumbling Derro and ate away at them with each passing moment; knocked to the ground more than once by the swarming attackers, he finally pulled back from the press and used restorative magic to mend his wounds.

#### The party engages in a chaotic skirmish against a derro Master Thief and crazed Derro in pitch darkness to protect and secure the mysterious egg.
- The derro Master Thief exchanges blows with the party's spectral ally and Zalthir amidst the darkness.
  - He stands up and strikes at the ghost; Thorin takes a Sentinel opportunity attack for 7. The thief hits twice, but the ghost's resistance to non-magical attacks halves it to 13.
  - GM, on ghost physics: "we are not playing that version of Dungeons and Dragons," unless the objector can explain "what does the initiative die really signify."
- Crazed Derro reinforcements arrive, frantically searching for the missing egg and shouting about its power.
  - "Whoever has the eggs is in charge." Gyrgum's player: "Oh, that's something we didn't know." GM: "Well, that's what they think."
- An enemy assails Gyrgum, knocking the cleric to the ground.
  - Six damage; Gyrgum fails a DC 9 Strength save and is knocked prone. Thorin's player asks, and the GM confirms the attacker rolled with disadvantage (14 and 16, +2).
- Zalthir relentlessly attacks and grapples the Master Thief to halt his movement.
  - Rerolls a 1 on a d6 damage die for 5 more; grapples again; two follow-up attacks miss against AC 16 even with Bless; drops him off the ledge again for 6. GM (as the thief): "what the?"
- The ghost fails to recharge possession; its withering touch attacks miss because it is also blinded.
- Gyrgum recovers his footing and holds his ground.
  - He considers a spell hitting everyone within five feet he can see; the GM and Zalthir's player offer to move the darkness, but Gyrgum declines as "only one die six damage each."
- Gyrgum unleashes a stream of corrosive acid upon the searching Derro.
  - Caustic Brew on #2 and #4; both fail; he rolls 4 and 6 and takes "the mean" — five each.
- A hidden spy becomes terrified by the spectral presence and attempts to flee the cavern.
  - "There's a ghost … I've got better things to do." Gyrgum's player: "he's been revealed as a spy, and that ruins his whole business."
- Thorin delivers a heavy blow to the Master Thief before stepping forward to shield his companions.
  - Told the thief still has 30 hit points (and hearing the GM's praise for how the party "selectively neutralize[d] him"), Thorin hits him for 13.
- The party helpers all fire short crossbows "and they all spectacularly" miss; Eldeth hangs back: "I don't want to get killed."
- Daz utilizes teleportation magic to escape danger, safely hiding the mysterious egg beneath his robes.
  - GM briefly misspoke ("He will leave the egg behind") and corrected himself; the egg is hidden under robes and only the spy could have noticed.
- Daz attempts to restrain an enemy with a conjured hand of earth, though the target manages to slip free.
- Enemies rally against Gyrgum, shouting to eliminate the cleric and knocking him prone once more.
  - Another failed DC 9 Strength save; only one hit lands, for a single point — "truly, honest to God, eppingly lame amount of damage."

### The Battle Turns and the Egg is Secured 020.04

As the battle neared its end, Zalthir delivered the killing blow to the Master Thief, finally ending the rogue's stubborn resistance, then struck a Derro warrior and pushed him off a ten-foot ledge, sending him crashing to his death below in a moment that felt ripped from legend. When Daz cried out for help as the last remaining spy cornered him, the ghost ally swept across the cavern and plunged into the spy's body, possessing him entirely and causing his eyes to glow with an eerie inner light. Thorin cut down the last Derro, who never realised the egg had long since been spirited away beneath Daz's robes, and the party stood victorious, the stolen red dragon egg safely in their possession.

Out of game: Daz's player was absent this session, so the GM ran Daz — weighing, in the GM's words, whether "should he let them die? That's what the drow in him says he should do." The session ended the moment the last Derro fell ("You have now are in possession of the egg, and we are out of time"), with play paused until after the 13th while the GM travels to Greece.
