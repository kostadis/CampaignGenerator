# Chapter 030

### Hunting the Grick Alpha 030.01

The party ventured deeper into the vast caverns surrounding Neverlight Grove. The underground landscape was filled with towering, psychedelically colored mushrooms that glowed with strange, inviting light. The GM introduced a new, larger map to convey the true scale of the area, which was far bigger than the previous map had implied.

Their quarry was a Grick Alpha, a surly predator lurking in the rocky, mossy terrain to the south. As the party set out, Loobamub warned them that a Shambling Mound also lurked nearby: "if you do find that, kill it. We don't reanimate shambling mounds." A Shambling Mound is a massive creature of living swamp matter. It does not walk around but flops down as if it were part of a mossy swamp.

Daz devised an unusual strategy. Advancing slowly within Shape Water's 30-foot range, he froze small sections of the mossy ground in a checkerboard pattern, spacing his lines so that no 10-by-10 gap could hide the Large creature. The spell only freezes water that has no creature in it, so any patch that failed to freeze would reveal the beast. The party advanced cautiously, with Thorin in front, "because that's what he does." Dawnbringer glowed fiercely in the dark, since the magical blade refuses to tolerate the absence of light.

#### The party navigates a large, mushroom-filled cavern to locate a Grick Alpha while attempting to avoid a hidden Shambling Mound.
- The party enters a massive cavern filled with psychedelic, multicolored mushrooms, far larger than their previous maps suggested. The GM swapped in a new, more detailed map for this reason.
- The party learns of a Grick Alpha and a hidden Shambling Mound near a pool of water in the southern part of the cavern, and heads roughly south toward the Grick's marked location.
- As the party leaves, Loobamub warns them to kill the Shambling Mound if they find it: "We don't reanimate shambling mounds." The GM explains that the Grick makes a perfectly fine reanimation target, but the Shambling Mound is just awkward.
- The Shambling Mound is a Large creature occupying a 10-by-10-foot space. It is hard to spot because it does not walk around but "flops down as if it was part of a mossy swamp." Shambling Mounds tend to live near water.
- Daz first proposes freezing the mound itself. The GM rules that cantrips do not work that way. Daz then realizes that Shape Water only freezes water with no creature in it, so a patch that fails to freeze would reveal the mound.
- Zalthir reminds the party of the mound's resistances. Daz considers Magic Missile and Maximilian's Earthen Grasp as follow-ups, and the GM confirms that the mound is only resistant, not immune, to cold and fire.
- Daz casts Shape Water repeatedly in a checkerboard pattern of lines so that no four-square area goes unchecked, advancing slowly because of the spell's 30-foot range.
- The party advances cautiously, with Thorin in the lead ("Thorin will be in front because that's what he does") as they move toward the Grick's suspected location.
- The Grick Alpha, disturbed by the party freezing its surroundings, wakes and bares its teeth and claws, then emerges to confront them.

### Battle with the Grick Alpha 030.02

It was the Grick Alpha that revealed itself first. The party's freezing of its surroundings disturbed it, and it woke, bared its teeth and claws, and lunged toward them with surly, cranky fury. The creature is a grotesque thing resembling a great snake with a hooked beak and writhing, talon-tipped tentacles. Zalthir immediately shrouded it in a sphere of impenetrable magical darkness and waded into the blackness. He landed a relentless series of unarmed strikes for 29 damage, though the Grick shook off his Stunning Strike. Daz could not see the creature through the darkness, so he trusted his memory of where it stood and angled a fireball to miss his allies. The Grick made its save and took only 13 damage, prompting Daz's lament: "That could have been 26."

Thorin charged in after, Dawnbringer blazing with radiant light as he hacked at the creature. Gyrgum could not use Guiding Bolt without line of sight, so he tried to drench it in Tasha's Caustic Brew, which the Grick Alpha dodged entirely. Battered and confused, the creature could not land a single blow on Thorin or Zalthir through the magical darkness. It "ponders its life for a moment," then made a desperate decision to flee. It did not get far: Zalthir's attack of opportunity hit for 13, and Thorin's blow finished it as it ran. The party harvested the creature's tough, camouflaged hide, which could be fashioned in six days into a cloak granting advantage when hiding in rocky terrain.

#### The party encounters and engages a surly Grick Alpha in the dark, utilizing magical darkness and area-of-effect spells to overcome its resistances.
- A Grick Alpha, a creature resembling a snake with a beak and octopus-like tentacles, emerges and attacks the party. The GM describes it as "a surly, cranky, miserable creature." Daz's player apologizes: "Sorry, guys, I might have pissed it off."
- The party learns that the Grick is resistant to non-magical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing, and that Gricks accumulate treasure. Thorin shows off his magical sword Dawnbringer, who is lit because she hates the dark.
- Zalthir creates a zone of magical darkness to disorient the creature and begins a flurry of unarmed strikes. His first strike hits (25 to hit) for 8 magical bludgeoning damage.
- Zalthir attempts a Stunning Strike (DC 13 Constitution), but the Grick Alpha resists it with a 14. He spends a focus point for two more attacks, hitting for 9 and 12, for 29 damage in total.
- The GM confirms that Gricks are not resistant to fire, after Zalthir's player mixes up the two monsters' descriptions.
- Daz unleashes a fireball into the darkness, catching the Grick Alpha in the blast despite being unable to see it. The GM notes that he only needs to know the target's general location. Daz angles the 20-foot radius to avoid Zalthir. The Grick makes its DC 15 Dexterity save and takes 13 damage.
- Thorin rushes into the fray wielding Dawnbringer, the blade's radiant light cutting through the gloom. Rolling with advantage, he hits for 9 radiant damage and then 6. His hits leave the creature with disadvantage on its first attack. Thorin also mentions that his Tremor Sense could help find the other creature.
- Gyrgum cannot use Guiding Bolt without line of sight, so he tries Tasha's Caustic Brew along a five-foot-wide line that misses Zalthir. The Grick makes its DC 15 Dexterity save and takes no damage.
- Frustrated, Gyrgum notes that half his spells are useless without a way to see into magical darkness. The GM suggests that a magic item, and a quest to find it, could solve this. Zalthir points out that there is a treasure-hoarding creature right here.
- The Grick Alpha attempts to retaliate against Thorin (AC 18) with its beak and tentacles, but the darkness gives it disadvantage and it misses wildly. Its tentacle attack would have dealt 15 damage.
- The Grick turns on Zalthir (AC 16) and misses again: all three of its attacks miss.
- Confused and unable to land a hit, the Grick Alpha "ponders its life for a moment" and attempts to flee the battle.

### The Grick's End and the Decision to Hunt the Shambling Mound 030.03

With the Grick Alpha dealt with, the party turned to the Shambling Mound. Strictly speaking they had only committed to the Grick. However, an Arcana check revealed that although Shambling Mounds are "notorious for not having any treasure," their vines are valued for crafting enchanted ropes. The party might trade them in Blingdenstone, where Jimjar's crew was headed, and that was motivation enough. Daz resumed his methodical freezing across the lake and eventually hit the creature lurking by a pool of water. Zalthir closed in along the shore after being warned that the mound likes to grab people and shove them under. He opened with a critical hit and stunned the massive creature with a well-placed strike. Daz followed with three scorching rays, all of which hit, though the creature's resistance halved the damage. Gyrgum then invoked Spirit Guardians, summoning spectral platinum dragons that swirled around him in a 15-foot radius. He spared Thorin and Zalthir from their effects.

#### The party finishes off the fleeing Grick Alpha and decides to hunt a Shambling Mound for its valuable magical components.
- As the Grick Alpha flees, Zalthir's attack of opportunity (with advantage) hits for 13, and Thorin's attack kills it.
- Gyrgum observes the piles of "acid soup" left around the battlefield and pulls out his toothbrush.
- The Shambling Mound has not appeared. Neither the fireball nor the party's approach touched it.
- Zalthir asks whether the party ever committed to killing the Shambling Mound. The GM confirms that they only committed to the Grick.
- The party harvests the hide of the Grick Alpha, planning to craft it into a cloak for stealth in rocky terrain. The cloak will be ready after six days.
- The party learns that Shambling Mounds are "notorious for not having any treasure," but Daz's Arcana check reveals that their vines are valued in various communities for crafting magical items, especially ropes.
- The GM suggests carrying the vines to Blingdenstone, where Jimjar's crew is headed. Daz hopes someone there would trade magical goods for the components.
- Motivated by the potential crafting value, the party decides to hunt the Shambling Mound. Daz asks, "can I restart my sunk your battleship?"
- Daz resumes freezing sections of the swamp in a checkerboard pattern and eventually hits the creature while working his way across the lake. Gyrgum comments, "You have a lot of patience, Daz."
- The party locates the Shambling Mound near a pool of water and rolls initiative for a new engagement.

### Battle with the Shambling Mound 030.04

Zalthir, now with "a reason to grapple for once," seized the stunned creature and locked it in place within the burning aura. Daz detonated another enormous fireball, having first checked that nobody would take steam damage from him "melting the lake." That blast brought the mound down to 9 hit points. The Shambling Mound thrashed with electrified tendrils and struck Zalthir, but he deflected the bludgeoning force and took only 2 lightning damage. It then engulfed Thorin within its rotting mass. Thorin, blinded and muttering that he had "smelt better cologne," fought his way free and shouted, "damn you, Gyrgum, do your thing." The spectral dragons scorched the creature once more at the end of its turn, and it collapsed into a heap of lifeless vines. A 2d6 roll yielded enough vine material for six ropes, worth 300 gold pieces.

#### The party engages in a fierce struggle against a Shambling Mound near a body of water, utilizing powerful magic and tactical grappling to defeat the creature.
- A body of water about 10 feet deep lies between the party and the mound and costs half speed to cross. The GM warns that the mound tends to grab people and shove them under, and calls wading in "a very poor tactical decision by Zalthir." Zalthir replies, "Duly noted."
- Zalthir closes in along the shore to engage the Shambling Mound directly. He lands a critical hit for 18, then 10. His Stunning Strike (DC 13 Constitution) succeeds, and the mound is stunned. He follows with two more hits for 8 and 10.
- Daz asks whether Zalthir can move his darkness to expose the mound to Magic Missile. Zalthir can only move it at the start of his turn.
- Daz unleashes scorching rays at the stunned creature, with advantage because it is stunned. All three rays hit (15, 25, and 22 to hit) for 6, 10, and 7, but the creature's natural resistance halves the 23 damage to 11.
- The stunned mound is incapacitated and can take no actions or reactions. Thorin is too far away to reach it and dashes toward the fight.
- Gyrgum moves close and summons spectral spirits resembling platinum dragons that swirl around him in a 15-foot radius. He exempts Thorin and Zalthir from the effect. The mound fails its DC 15 Wisdom save, takes 13 damage, and has its speed halved.
- Zalthir realizes the combo ("Suddenly I have a reason to grapple for once") and grapples the creature as part of an attack to keep it trapped within Gyrgum's spectral aura. He then hits for 11, and spends a focus point to hit for 14 and 10.
- Daz detonates a massive fireball that further weakens the mossy behemoth, carefully angling the blast to avoid his allies. First he checks the 20-foot radius and asks whether anyone will take steam damage from "melting the lake." The mound fails its save and takes 12 after resistance, dropping to 9 hit points.
- The Shambling Mound retaliates with electrified tendrils. One misses, and one strikes Zalthir with a discharge of lightning and bludgeoning force for 11. Zalthir deflects the bludgeoning portion and takes only 2 lightning damage. When Gyrgum asks him not to deflect it his way, Zalthir sends it "just upward."
- The GM rules that the mound cannot engulf the creature grappling it, since that would effectively end the grapple.
- The creature attempts to engulf Thorin instead and pulls him into its rotting mass. Thorin fails the DC 15 Strength save, is blinded, and takes 10 damage, muttering that he has "smelt better cologne." He then manages to struggle free.
- The combined radiant energy of the spectral guardians finally overcomes the Shambling Mound. It fails its Wisdom save at the end of its turn, takes 15 damage, and collapses into a heap of lifeless vines.
- Afterward the table discusses Spirit Guardians rules. The GM rules that the spell is not an infinite-damage hack. A creature takes damage when it is moved into the emanation and again if it stays there on its turn, and it saves only once per turn.
- Zalthir plans to drag creatures into the aura and grapple them there, possibly two at a time. The GM also suggests pummelling foes in Zalthir's darkness and then moving them out into the open to be hit by magic.

### Return to Neverlight Grove and the Circle of Builders 030.05

The party returned to Neverlight Grove and presented the remains of the Grick Alpha to Basidia. She was mildly disappointed at the state of the hide but mused, "hmm, this will be an interesting transformation. I can't wait." The party checked in on their old Myconid companions, Stool and Rumpadump. Gyrgum told them they were welcome to come adventuring, but the pair preferred to stay home. Daz wondered whether they should head on to Blingdenstone, but Basidia reminded them that she had promised magic and treasure if they explored the Circle of Masters. During a brief rest she held forth about the circles. She then led them to the Circle of Builders, a vast cavern of terraced irrigation systems and thousands of glowing fungi that filled the air with a thick, sweet smell.

There they met Gasbide, a Myconid of intense and obsessive curiosity and a supporter of Sovereign Phylo. He immediately began interrogating the party about surface-world architecture: "Describe to me your buildings." Gyrgum offered that "we have some that are tall and some that are big," which did not satisfy him. Gasbide demanded shapes, materials, and counts of windows and doors, and was captivated by the unfamiliar word "window." Zalthir conjured an illusion of a small surface building, and Gasbide was utterly transfixed. He wondered whether it was built for small people, then decided that its creator must be a member of the circle of builders in their own community. Meanwhile, Basidia rolled her eyes.

Gasbide revealed that he dreamed of building a fungal tower even greater than Yggmorgus, the colossal mushroom of Neverlight Grove. He hoped it would break through the Underdark to the surface world, "possibly with the aid of Araumycos." He asked for a construction that could shield him from the sun, and Gyrgum helpfully suggested "shade." Daz happily nerded out with him over zurkhwood and construction minutiae. Yet Gasbide had no real understanding of what Araumycos was, only that it was part of a great celebration to come. In his words: "Phylo said that there would be a great awakening, that everything will be better after the great celebration." The party noted the unsettling vagueness of his words and the blind faith he placed in Phylo's promises. Something was being planned in Neverlight Grove, and it was far larger than a simple celebration.

#### The party returns to Basidia with their prizes and is then led to the Circle of Builders, where they meet an obsessive Myconid architect.
- The party harvests magical vines from the Shambling Mound for crafting enchanted ropes. A 2d6 roll yields six ropes' worth, valued at 300 gold pieces. The GM clarifies that the vines are "not magical, but they are noted for their usefulness in creating magical ropes."
- The party returns to Neverlight Grove and presents the remains of the Grick Alpha to Basidia.
- Basidia expresses mild disappointment that the Grick's hide was harvested, but prepares to reanimate the creature as a fungal servant: "hmm, this will be an interesting transformation. I can't wait."
- Basidia offers to show the party other places. Daz raises heading on to Blingdenstone, and Basidia reminds them that she had promised magic and treasure in exchange for exploring the Circle of Masters.
- The party checks on Stool and Rumpadump, who are happy and cheering. The GM notes that Stool had been kidnapped, and that Rumpadump had been teleported by a crazed Myconid using a power Thorin refused.
- Gyrgum tells the two Myconids they are good companions and welcome to adventure with the party. They prefer to stay home but reply, "That's very sweet of you."
- The party takes a short rest to recover their strength and magical energy. The GM notes that a long rest would push into the next day and that no more combat is coming. Basidia uses the rest to talk at length about the circles.
- Basidia leads the party toward the Circle of Builders. The GM describes it: "Waters trickling from the walls of this vast cavern are channeled into a crude but effective terraced irrigation system. A thick, sweet smell fills the air from the thousands of fungi of all colors and sizes, many glowing with a strange, inviting light."
- The party meets Gasbide, leader of the Circle of Builders and a supporter of Sovereign Phylo. He notices that they are new and is intensely curious about surface-world architecture.
- Gasbide asks whether the party is from above. Gyrgum answers that they come from the "Great Bright," and Gasbide is fascinated by surface buildings and the concept of windows ("What is a window?").
- Basidia rolls her eyes at Gasbide's enthusiasm.
- Zalthir uses a minor illusion to create a scale model of a surface building, fascinating Gasbide. He asks whether it is to scale and notes, "you don't fit in those buildings. Is it for small people?"
- Gasbide asks whether the illusion's creator is an architect. On hearing "Aspiring," he decides that they are a member of the circle of builders in their own community.
- Gasbide describes several bizarre, elaborate structures he imagines building.
- Gasbide shares his dream of building a fungal tower greater than Yggmorgus to break through the Underdark to the surface world, "possibly with the aid of Araumycos."
- Daz notes that fungus only really grows where it is shaded. Gasbide asks for a construction to protect him from the sun, and Gyrgum answers, "Oh, yes, it's called shade."
- Daz and Gasbide engage in a lengthy, detailed discussion about construction materials, comparing surface wood to zurkhwood mushrooms. Gasbide asks how big a shade should be and how many pieces of wood it needs. Gyrgum offers a rule of thumb: "about three zurkhwood mushrooms to the square foot."
- Gasbide mentions a coming "Great Awakening" and a celebration involving something called Araumycos, though he is unclear on the details. He only knows that Sovereign Phylo has promised everything will be better afterward: "Phylo said that there would be a great awakening, that everything will be better after the great celebration."
