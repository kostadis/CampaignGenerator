# Chapter 005

### Rescue from the Rockfall 005.01

The aftermath of the rockfall left the party scrambling to pull their companions from the rubble before the shifting dirt and stone could claim any more lives. Freeing a buried companion took an action, and anyone left under the dirt would take damage the next round. The party worked in rough initiative order, with Zalthir going first. Zalthir, drawing on his considerable strength, heaved heavy slabs of rock aside to free those buried beneath, while Thorin focused his efforts on dragging Gyrgum clear of the debris. Stool, the small but determined sentient fungus, waddled through the chaos and asked Gyrgum if he was okay. Gyrgum answered, "Oh, I'm not doing so well. I got pretty badly hurt." At Gyrgum's urging, Stool managed to pull both Shuushar and Buppido to safety. He freed Buppido because he thought Buppido dying would be tragic. Zalthir shoved the rocks off Daz, who lay stable but unconscious, and the party breathed a collective sigh of relief as most of their companions were freed, though Eldeth and Jimjar were still trapped. Their guide had not been trapped at all.

#### The party works quickly to free their companions and allies from the aftermath of a rock slab collapse.
- Rescuing a buried companion costs an action. Anyone left covered in dirt takes damage the next round.
- The party resolves the rescue in pseudo-initiative order by Dexterity. Zalthir (DEX 17) goes first.
- Zalthir uses his strength to shove heavy rocks off his trapped companions.
- Zalthir shoves the rocks off Daz, who is stable but unconscious. Daz is the only unconscious companion, so Zalthir treats him as the urgent case.
- Thorin focuses his efforts on rescuing Gyrgum from the debris, "because he's part of our crew."
- The guide (Sarith) was never trapped under the rocks.
- Stool runs over and asks Gyrgum if he's okay. Gyrgum answers, "I'm not doing so well. I got pretty badly hurt."
- Stool, the sentient fungus, assists by pulling Shuushar and Buppido to safety. These two were the most likely to die. Gyrgum asked for Shuushar, and Stool added Buppido because he thought Buppido dying would be tragic.
- Daz is stabilized but remains in a weakened state as the party regroups.

### Ambush of the Carrion Crawler 005.02

Their relief was short-lived. A massive, writhing shape erupted from the earth with a snarl, drawn by the noise of the rescue. It was a carrion crawler, a grotesque creature resembling a colossal maggot lined with paralyzing tentacles. Zalthir went first and exhaled a searing line of fire. The crawler blew its Dexterity save and took 10 damage, though it was far from finished, and Zalthir pulled back behind the others. The crawler struck Gyrgum with a tentacle for 4 damage and dropped him, then turned its snapping jaws on Thorin and hit him for 7. Thorin answered with a precise thrust of his short sword for 4 piercing damage. The weapon's Vex property gave him advantage on his next attack, and he held his ground with grim determination.

The party's companions, Shuushar, Turvy, Topsy, Sarith, and Buppido, raised their hand crossbows and loosed a volley of bolts into the creature's hide. The volley included "a bunch of criticals," landed four hits, and dealt 20 damage. Zalthir unleashed another blast of fire, but the crawler twisted aside and took only 2 damage. Thorin caught his breath with Second Wind for ten more hit points. The crawler's jaws missed Thorin by one point against his AC, and a tentacle aimed at Sarith "misses spectacularly." Meanwhile Eldeth was nearing death, Gyrgum rolled death saves, and Daz kept rolling successes while staying stable. Thorin had Topsy and Turvy dig out Eldeth ("dwarves stick together") and Jimjar. The three companions still shooting put 15 more damage into the crawler and killed it. Once it was dead, everyone came round, with Gyrgum at one hit point. There was no loot, so the party harvested the carcass instead. Gyrgum's 2d6 roll produced nine rations, about two days' food with the mushrooms. To everyone's mild surprise, the meat tasted remarkably like chicken.

#### A scavenger of the Underdark erupts from the earth, drawn by the noise of the rescue efforts.
- A carrion crawler bursts from the ground, snarling and snapping at the party. It looks like "a huge maggot."
- The companions stay out of combat unless the party directs them.
- Zalthir (+3 initiative) goes first. He intended a cone, but the GM corrected it to a line of fire. The crawler blows its Dexterity save and takes 10 damage.
- Zalthir then pulls back to put other creatures between himself and the crawler.
- The crawler strikes Gyrgum with a tentacle, hitting his AC of 17 for 4 damage and dropping him. Gyrgum had already used the trait that would have helped.
- The crawler moves in and hits Thorin for 7.
- Daz is stable, so he does not need death saves. He can still roll for a natural 20 to regain a hit point, but he keeps rolling ordinary successes.
- The party's companions (Shuushar, Turvy, Topsy, Sarith, and Buppido) fire a volley of crossbow bolts at the monster. The GM rolls 5d20, gets "a bunch of criticals" and four hits, and deals 20 damage.
- Thorin engages the beast with his short sword, landing a precise blow for 4 piercing damage. Vex gives him advantage on his next attack.
- Zalthir breathes fire again, but the crawler makes its save and takes only 2 damage.
- Thorin catches his breath with Second Wind ("Ten more").
- Eldeth nears death.
- The crawler's jaws miss Thorin, since its roll of 13 falls short of his AC of 14. Its tentacle "misses spectacularly" against Sarith.
- Gyrgum rolls death saves and passes twice.
- The GM asks whether the companions should keep attacking or save Eldeth and Jimjar. Thorin chooses to save Eldeth because "dwarves stick together," and gives the same answer for Jimjar.
- With Topsy and Turvy sent to free Eldeth and Jimjar, the three companions still shooting put 15 damage into the crawler and kill it.
- Once the crawler is dead, everyone comes round. Gyrgum is at one hit point.
- The party finds no loot on the crawler.
- The party harvests the creature's corpse. Gyrgum rolls 2d6 for a 9, securing nine rations, about two days' food with the mushrooms. The rations "taste like chicken," which prompts a Matrix aside from the GM.

### A Moment of Respite 005.03

With their wounds tended and their spirits cautiously restored, the party settled into a defensible corner of the cavern for a long rest. During the rest everyone reached 2nd level, and Daz took expertise in Arcana. When they finally rose and prepared to march, there was a quiet confidence among them, though the Underdark had a way of punishing confidence swiftly.

#### Following the battle, the party takes a long rest to recover and reaches 2nd level.
- The adventurers settle into a defensible area of the caverns to rest and tend to their injuries.
- Everyone reaches 2nd level. Daz takes expertise in Arcana.
- Expertise doubles the proficiency bonus.
- Daz weighed Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, and Perception. With only Wisdom 14 he passed on Perception, and chose Arcana "because he's been doing all the wizardry stuff."
- The party discusses the nuances of perception and investigation as they prepare for the next leg of their journey. The GM's ruling:
  - Perception tells you that there is a trap, or that someone isn't telling the truth, but not where the trap is or what the lie is. Insight and Investigation answer those questions.
  - Somebody in the party should have high Perception, usually a rogue or cleric, since it builds on Wisdom.
  - Gyrgum notes he has +3.

### A Grim Discovery 005.04

They had barely been walking an hour when Daz and Zalthir both froze. Their passive perception caught something wrong in the darkness behind them. On the follow-up insight rolls, one came up a natural 20 and Daz rolled a 17. Zalthir's mind raced through what he knew of Drow tactics, and the truth settled over him like a cold stone. The figures stalking them were scouts from the very prison they had escaped, and where scouts moved, a full hunting party was never far behind. The party had barely survived an encounter with three Drow, much less a whole hunting party. The scouts seemed to have their scent. A natural-20 history check reminded Zalthir of a musty tome from his monastery, which taught that killing the scouts would not stop the main force. They could run, hide, or try an ambush. Stool urged running, and the decision was made quickly: they would run.

#### After preparing themselves for the journey ahead, the party begins their march through the Underdark, only to discover they are being hunted.
- The party prepares their skills and begins a cautious march through the subterranean tunnels.
- About an hour into the march, Daz and Zalthir notice through passive perception that the group is being followed. On the follow-up insight rolls, one comes up a natural 20 and Daz rolls a 17.
- Zalthir deduces that the pursuers are Drow scouts from their former prison, meaning a larger hunting party is likely close behind.
- The party had "barely survived an encounter with three Drow, much less a whole hunting party."
- The scouts seem to have the party's scent. The path branches, but the scouts are trackers who may follow anyway.
- Zalthir's history check comes up a natural 20. He recalls from "a musty tome in his monastery" that killing the scouts would not stop the main force.
- Stool cannot mind-control drow, since he has only four spores.
- Realizing they are heavily outnumbered by the impending forces, the party decides to flee rather than risk an ambush. Stool urges running.

### Flight through the Caverns 005.05

Stool could move only 10 feet, and Gyrgum, with 8 Strength, could not carry him far. So Gyrgum handed Stool up to Thorin, who took the fungus on his back ("We're doing this Yoda style"). Once they were moving, Stool released a cloud of rapport spores that wove a telepathic link between them, so they could talk through the chase without making a sound. Zalthir used both his action and bonus action to dash, sprinting ahead of the main group. He searched the rocky terrain on a roll of 17 and found a natural alcove that would give the whole party advantage on its stealth check. Gyrgum dashed to the hiding place, gaining two temporary hit points from a dash ability, and used Thaumaturgy to set the ground trembling, hoping to scare the scouts off. The drow hit a sudden drop in the tunnel but made their Dexterity save and avoided the hole. Daz dashed into the hiding spot last. The party's group stealth check against DC 12 held, and the scouts lost the scent. The scouts doubled back and disappeared down a different corridor, and the party allowed themselves one quiet, shared breath of relief. Then Zalthir reminded them, through the telepathic link, that they needed to move faster and keep moving: "If we go at this pace, they'll keep catching up."

#### The party initiates a desperate retreat through the winding tunnels of the Underdark to escape the closing Drow scouts.
- To ensure the group can move as quickly as possible, Gyrgum hands the slow-moving Stool to Thorin, who carries him on his back. Stool moves 10 feet, and Gyrgum has 8 Strength. Stool "grabs onto you for dear life."
- Stool releases rapport spores so the group can talk silently in their minds during the chase.
- The GM explains the chase rules:
  - A participant can Dash 3 + CON modifier times.
  - Each extra Dash requires a DC 10 CON save or the runner gains a level of exhaustion.
  - The quarry makes stealth checks against the pursuers' passive perception.
  - Each round a d12 complication roll adds randomness.
  - If a PC is caught, the companions with them are caught too.
- The scouts roll 12 on initiative (8 + 2).
- The party flees down the dark corridors, putting distance between themselves and the scouts. Starting 90 feet apart, the GM worked the gap out to 160 feet after some table math.
- Zalthir uses his superior speed to sprint ahead of the main group, searching the rocky terrain for a suitable place to hide. He dashes with both his action and bonus action.
- Zalthir scouts ahead and identifies a suitable hiding spot for the group while maintaining his lead. He uses investigation/survival rather than perception ("Perception lets you perceive things. It doesn't let you find things") and rolls a 17. The spot gives the group advantage on its stealth check.
- Zalthir points the group to the spot over the telepathic "party line."
- The party members dash toward the hiding place.
- Gyrgum moves 30 feet plus a bonus-action Dash and gains two temporary hit points from Adrenaline Rush (his orc trait). He uses Thaumaturgy to make the ground tremble, hoping to scare the scouts away.
- Thorin and Gyrgum each roll a 9 on their complication rolls, and no complication occurs.
- The Drow pursuers encounter a sudden drop in the cavern floor but make their Dexterity save and avoid falling into the pit.
- Daz dashes into the hiding spot and rolls a 9 on his complication.
- The group stealth check is at advantage against DC 12. If at least half the group succeeds, the whole group succeeds. Thorin warns he is at minus one on stealth.
- The group's stealth holds, and the Drow scouts lose their scent and double back down a different corridor.
- Zalthir warns the party that they must maintain a fast pace to prevent the Drow from catching up again: "we need to pick up the pace. If we go at this pace, they'll keep catching up. So we must go fast."

### Flight Through the Faerzress 005.06

The days that followed were a grueling blur of dark tunnels and aching legs as the party pushed themselves to travel at a punishing pace. They chose speed over covering their tracks, even though Eldeth (+5 survival) was the best tracker among them. On the fast march Jimjar grumbled that no one would find any food at the pace "the silent one" wanted. They passed through caverns filled with Faerzress, a strange and unsettling dim magical light that caused Shuushar visible distress. By moving quickly they barely escaped the worst of its effects. Sarith led the way through the twisting passages, his pride in his navigational abilities on full display. Then, deep in the Faerzress tunnels, he nearly led the group down the wrong path entirely. Gyrgum noticed the error, and Sarith, with barely concealed irritation, answered: "Yes, I saw that. Thank you. I didn't need your help. But it's good we're all on the same page." By the end of the third day, the party had put a meaningful lead between themselves and their pursuers, a day ahead at pursuit level four. Somewhere behind them in the dark, the Drow hunting party was still out there, and the Underdark stretched endlessly ahead.

#### The party travels at a high speed through magically charged tunnels to put distance between themselves and their pursuers.
- The GM explains the pursuit-level mechanic. The party can lower pursuit by one for:
  - each day travelling at a fast pace;
  - covering their trail at normal pace with a DC 16 Wisdom (Survival) check;
  - crossing a feature that obscures the trail;
  - splitting the party (prompting a "never split the party" tangent).
- Among the companions, Eldeth has +5 survival, Jimjar +4, and Sarith +0. Eldeth is judged the best choice to lead trail-covering.
- Fast travel and covering tracks are either/or. The party decides to travel at a fast pace for several days to decrease the threat of pursuit ("Hiding, we're eventually going to get found").
- The GM frames the Underdark as a maze of intersecting passages, where the drow can always catch up unless the party does something clever.
- Gyrgum clarifies that Thaumaturgy is sound and light effects, not destruction, so it cannot collapse a bridge behind them.
- Jimjar laments that at this pace no one will find food. The party already has rations.
- The group enters tunnels filled with Faerzress, a strange dim light that causes distress to Shuushar.
- By moving quickly, the party manages to avoid the more dangerous effects of the magical radiation. It is significant, and they barely escape it.
- By day three the party has a day's lead at pursuit level four. Level five means the drow catch up.
- Fast travel carries a risk of getting lost.
- Sarith leads the way through the twisting tunnels, narrowly avoiding getting the group lost despite the high speed of travel.
- The party determines who is best suited to assist their guide in navigating the treacherous tunnels. The best survival bonus among the PCs is +3.
- Sarith nearly loses his way while leading the group at a fast pace.
- Gyrgum notices a navigational error and corrects their course.
- Sarith begrudgingly accepts the assistance, maintaining his pride while keeping the group on the correct path: "Yes, I saw that. Thank you. I didn't need your help. But it's good we're all on the same page."
- The party successfully avoids getting lost, maintaining their lead over their pursuers.
- The session ends here.
