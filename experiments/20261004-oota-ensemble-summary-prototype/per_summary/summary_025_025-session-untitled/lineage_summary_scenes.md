# Chapter 025

### The Spoils of Whorlstone Caverns 025.01

Deep within the Whorlstone Caverns beneath the city of Gracklstugh, the party took stock of everything they had gathered underground. With every derro in the caves dead, the evidence in their possession was staggering:

- A two-headed dragon egg tied to the cult of Demogorgon.
- Proof that the derro were aligned with demonic powers.
- Knowledge that the Deepking himself was allied with assassins trying to wipe out the opposition.
- Proof that Themberchaud is in fact "Themberchaud the 28th."
- A ritual statue connected to the affliction causing stone giants to sprout second heads.

The political leverage they held over nearly every faction in Gracklstugh was immense, "Epstein level dirt on everybody," as Daz's player put it. The DM reminded them that they were "only fourth level in a city of 10,000 duergar," and that the city was barely holding together. Their original plan had been to hand the egg to Gartokkar, head of the Keepers of the Flame, and call it a day. The party debated what to do with the rest:

- **Zalthir** proposed a dead man's switch, some way to ensure the information would be released if the party were captured or killed.
- **Jimjar** urged them to hold onto everything for future use rather than hand it over too soon.
- **Thorin** floated going to the merchant they had helped, though he worried the merchant might betray them.

#### The party assesses the significant evidence and treasure they have gathered while Stool pleads with them to find his friend Rumpadump.

- The party reviews the evidence they have collected, including a two-headed dragon egg and proof of the derro's demonic alliances.

- Thorin's player rolls a die (15+ = burn it down) to decide whether his character wants to burn the place down. The roll comes up short, so he goes along with the rest of the group.

  - The king is allied with assassins "trying to wipe out the opposition."
  - Themberchaud is "Themberchaud the 28th."
- They discuss the political leverage they now hold over the various factions in Gracklstugh, including dirt on the Deepking and the Keepers of the Flame.

### Debating the Myconid Request 025.02

Before any of those plans could be set in motion, however, Stool had other ideas. The young Myconid sprout had been pleading with the party for some time to go check on his friend Rumpadump, and his patience had finally run out. He threw what could only be described as a tantrum, bouncing with agitation and threatening to go alone if the others refused. He even tried to win Thorin over by declaring him "much nicer than Zalthir."

Zalthir was deeply skeptical. He recalled the frantic, infected creatures they had previously scouted near Rumpadump's location, including a massive ape-like beast that looked anything but friendly. Stool insisted there was nothing to fear. Myconids could only calm people and talk to them, and turning someone into a "zombie" required them to be killed first. Daz's recollection of Myconid lore largely backed that up. Stool's loyalty was infectious, and one by one the party relented. Gyrgum bolstered three of his companions with Aid, granting five temporary hit points for eight hours, and the group followed the little mushroom child deeper into the caverns.

#### The party discusses whether to follow Stool's plea to visit Rumpadump, weighing the risks of fungal infection against their loyalty to their mushroom companion.

**Stool's tantrum**
- Stool throws a tantrum, insisting the party accompany him to see Rumpadump, who is reportedly upset.
  - Stool: "We don't have to save him. We just have to go talk to him."
  - Daz objects to the idea of ignoring Stool, and Thorin's player declares his character "very loyal … if he's with us, we're with him."
- Stool tries to recruit Thorin as his new friend. Zalthir calls it "splitting."

**Zalthir's objections**
- Zalthir expresses concern about the dangerous, infected creatures they previously scouted near Rumpadump's location.
  - He reminds everyone that these mushrooms can mess with minds and possibly control bodies, and that the scouted area held "a massive ape creature that looked not particularly friendly."
  - Stool calls Zalthir "a scaredy cat." He says Rumpadump is about his height, while "The ape thing looked so big."

**Stool's reassurances**

- Gyrgum offers Bless and Aid as protective options.

  - They are anthropomorphic walking mushrooms the drow used as target practice, dangerous only in large numbers.

  - Daz suggests telling him it's bedtime again. The DM: "Nice try."
- Gyrgum casts Aid on three party members to bolster their resilience before they depart. Each gains 5 temporary hit points for eight hours.
- The DM allows a short rest (but not a long rest) before they arrive. Gyrgum's player tries to heal "the 12 and five."

  - Daz: "I was going to say he's cute, but he's really not."

Three stood five feet tall, the others half that, and one of the small ones stood apart with far less frantic movements. Two large, ape-like creatures covered in yellow mold and mushroom growths were hunched nearby, and the whole scene hummed with a strange, silent energy.

Zalthir, an expert grappler, reacted quickly, grabbed him, and hauled him back around the corner. Gyrgum's passive perception caught that the agitation began the instant Sarith had line of sight.

It was then that Rumpadump appeared, calm and studious where the others were frantic, like a quiet scholar standing apart from a chaotic crowd. Stool erupted with excitement at the reunion ("I found you. I found you. I found you."). After a moment of silence, Rumpadump said simply: "something is not right here." ### Reunion with Rumpadump and the Dark Lady Revealed 025.04

- A Myconid named Voosbur had released strange spores back in their home, the Neverlight Grove.
- The next thing any of them knew, they had walked through some fungi and materialized here in the Whorlstone Caverns.
- He had avoided the madness that followed because "I did not eat the silly mushrooms." These were the same growy-shrinky mushrooms the party had gathered. Stool loudly disputed this theory.
- He feared the infection had spread back to the rest of his kin in the Neverlight Grove, and he urged the party to help him return there.
- The Myconid Sovereign who dwells in the Grove holds the accumulated knowledge of all his predecessors and might even know a way out of the Underdark entirely. Reaching the Grove, however, would mean leaving Gracklstugh first.

Meanwhile, Sarith's behavior grew increasingly alarming. He began talking about a Dark Lady calling to him, and about visions of the Dark Lady. The party's arcana checks came up empty; Daz had apparently skipped those sections of his drow training. Gyrgum, however, drew on his training as a cleric of Bahamut and rolled a 23 on religion. He recognized the reference: the Dark Lady was Zuggtmoy, the Demon Queen of Fungi, Lady of Rot and Decay. She is an alien being whose sole desire is to infect the living with her spores and transform them into mindless servants, and eventually into decomposing hosts for her fungi.

Daz recalled that such infection normally required proximity to the demonic power itself, and these dancing Myconids clearly were not it. The party wondered whether the true source lay at the very Grove they were being asked to visit. They concluded that Sarith had likely been touched by Zuggtmoy's influence well before they met him in prison, and they noted he had never been mentally stable. Since Demogorgon was already abroad, it seemed entirely possible Zuggtmoy was too. The realization cast a grim shadow over the group, but there was still the matter of Voosbur to contend with.

### The Spore Glade and the Vine Wood 025.06

The world shifted around them. They found themselves standing in a bioluminescent glade where towering mushrooms glowed in radiant hues and the ground pulsed softly beneath their feet as if breathing. Delicate spores drifted lazily through the air, shimmering like motes of dust. The scent was earthy and sweet, with an intoxicating undercurrent that made the head swim. The two moved on breathing shallowly. Thorin ran through and brushed a few things on the way, and the glowing fungi pulsed brighter when touched, a silent "hey, watch it, dude," before settling again as the pair pressed forward.

They passed into what could only be called the Vine Wood, a damp and oppressive maze of thick, twisting vines covered in translucent slime that caught the faint light like liquid glass. The vines twitched and swayed with an almost sentient awareness, and the ground beneath was tangled with root-like growths that seemed to reach upward. The pair tried to sneak through, but their group stealth check came up a mere 7. As they moved through the living labyrinth, the vines began to advance toward them, slow at first and then with sudden, grasping urgency. The two broke into a run, and the vines collapsed behind them in a crushing wave as they burst through the exit at the very last moment.

### Audience with the Lady of Rot 025.07

They emerged into a grim clearing dominated by a throne of intertwined fungal growths, ringed by twisted mushroom sentinels mottled with mold. A heavy, oppressive silence hung over everything, broken only by the faint drip of moisture and the creak of swaying tendrils. The colors were muted: sickly yellow, dull browns, and shadowed grays. Then a spectral figure shimmered into view at the center, shifting between radiant beauty and horrifying decay, her eyes glowing with an otherworldly light. This was Zuggtmoy herself, the Demon Queen of Fungi, and her voice reached their minds like a haunting melody, at once soothing and commanding. "Welcome, wanderers of flesh and bone," she said. She asked whether they feared the rot or understood its song, and offered her gift: to bloom beyond the bounds of flesh and time and become part of her endless mycelium forever.

Thorin felt the temptation keenly. Out of character, the table reasoned that if she had wanted to kill him, she already could have, and that deals with demons always grant power at great cost. For a long moment he stood on the edge of something irreversible; his deciding roll was "really close." But in the end he declined, telling the Dark Lady that he had too many responsibilities in the waking world to linger with her. Zuggtmoy regarded him with something like sorrow, lamenting that he could have been "forever part of the endless mycelium," and then the vision dissolved. Thorin and Gyrgum woke up back in the Whorlstone Tunnels, blinking in the dim light of the caverns, with their companions staring at them.

### Departure from Whorlstone Caverns 025.08

Voosbur remarked, "Hey, man, I guess you didn't want to join the party. I got to go now." He and the rest of the infected Myconids gathered at a spore cloud among the fungal growths and stepped through it, vanishing entirely. They were traveling through some vast fungal network that connected the hidden places of the Underdark. The party took a long rest, recovering their strength (and losing Aid's temporary hit points) and reflecting on everything they had seen and learned. They rose to fifth level, and Daz finally gained Fireball.

Then, after three days underground, they made their way out of the Whorlstone Caverns. What greeted them at the surface was not the relief they had hoped for. The gates into the rest of Gracklstugh were sealed shut, guarded by grim-faced Duergar in heavy armor. The drow on the other side were even more on edge than before, and the tension felt like a spark hovering over dry tinder. A particularly mean-looking guard named Gorglak stepped forward and blocked their path: "You, none of you are allowed. You should not be in here." He demanded to inspect all of their possessions for contraband. The party was left standing at the threshold of the city, holding a bag full of demonic artifacts and political secrets, with nowhere to run.
