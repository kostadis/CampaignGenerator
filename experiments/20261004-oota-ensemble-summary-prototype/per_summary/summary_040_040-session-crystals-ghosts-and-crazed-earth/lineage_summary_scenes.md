# Chapter 040 Crystals, Ghosts, and Crazed Earth

### Expedition Assignments and the Rockblight 040.01

Eldeth, Jimjar, and Spiderbait took on the ghost problem. Glabbagool initially volunteered too, but the plan changed and he remained with the main party; before that change, he wondered aloud whether he would become the first gelatinous cube ghost if he died.

#### The party confirms who will handle the ghost problem, then chooses the nearby Rockblight as its first objective and plans a long rest.

### Rest and Rumors at the Foaming Mug Tavern 040.02

That evening, the party rested at the Foaming Mug Tavern, a staggeringly large cave converted into an inn specifically built to house visitors to Blingdenstone. Its rooms had beds large enough for dwarves, elves, dragonborn, ogres, and other “normal-sized” guests rather than only deep gnomes. The tavern served Darklake Stout imported through Mantol-Derith. Its proprietor, Tappy Foamstrap, was a bored deep gnome desperate for new stories after hearing every local’s troubles dozens of times.

The tavern’s patrons supplied plenty of news. The ghost problem was worsening, with spirits now haunting Blingdenstone’s catacombs. Scouts had reportedly seen a crazy gnome from the first days of the reclamation skulking through unrecovered portions of the settlement, whom the party suspected was the Pudding King. The Stoneheart Enclave was said to be “closing in on a solution” to Ogrémoch’s Bane—a claim that amused the party, since the solution was evidently to send them to deal with it. Debate also continued over the wererats and whether they should be considered gnomes at all.

A merchant recently returned from Gracklstugh described the duergar city as outwardly calm but deeply on edge. The authorities had imposed controls while blaming the Ember Vanguard for recent chaos, and the merchant carried a wanted poster reading “wanted dead or alive,” with “alive” misspelled. He noticed the party’s superficial resemblance to the wanted fugitives, but reassured himself that the poster depicted a red rather than gold dragonborn, as well as an older and more fearsome drow, orc, and dwarf. He also mentioned rumors that the Assassin’s Guild had returned, an influx of unexplained surface coins, and summary executions, decapitations, and derro heads displayed on pikes. His companion remarked that the duergar had been emphatic about the derro needing to “learn their place.”

The merchant also described Gracklstugh’s peculiarly portly red dragon, used as an extravagant matchlighter and distinguished by a double chin and two bellies. Despite the city’s murders, assassinations, political struggles, and controlled chaos, it had not collapsed into open war. The party was relieved that its goal of leaving Gracklstugh without causing the city to burn down had been achieved.

A deep gnome returning from the Whiteshell Mine offered another strange report: he had encountered a parade of dancing myconids communicating through Rapport Spores and announcing that they were on their way to a wedding celebration. After briefly calling it a “sweating celebration,” he corrected himself and emphasized how peculiar this was. Myconids do not normally marry, and no one he knew had ever heard of them holding celebrations. The party understood the likely cause: Zuggtmoy’s intended wedding. The dancing myconids might mean Neverlight Grove was emptying—or that its inhabitants had gone completely insane.

As the evening wore on, Glabbagool cleaned the tavern floor by absorbing spilled drinks and became increasingly intoxicated. The gelatinous cube grew loopier than usual and began singing traditional gelatinous cube sea shanties. This accidental discovery inspired the party to consider using alcohol against hostile oozes and puddings.

The next morning, the party asked Tappy for barrels of old or spoiled booze. He was willing to provide it only if no one attributed its questionable quality to the Foaming Mug and damaged the tavern’s reputation. The party countered that he might instead advertise himself as the hero whose alcohol saved the city. Tappy agreed if Blingdenstone’s leaders would provide an official notice he could display behind the bar. The party also discussed using Shape Water, a seltzer bottle, mugs, or some other improvised delivery mechanism to spray the alcohol.

### Journey to the Rockblight 040.03

The party then traveled to the Cultivation Cave, a vast complex where the deep gnomes grew useful fungi for cooking and crafting. A member of the Stoneheart Enclave welcomed them and supplied the freshest fungi from the garden for their expedition. Glabbagool enthusiastically ate mushrooms and plowed through the dirt and gravel blocking the alternate entrance to the Rockblight, spraying debris aside like a gigantic transparent snowblower. After several hours of excavation, the party reached the caverns.

#### The party travels to the Rockblight area, using Glabbagool as a portable excavator to clear a path through dirt and gravel.

- The Rockblight could be entered through a fortified staging area with multiple fences or through a route obstructed by dirt and gravel.

- The party selected the gravel route and used Glabbagool as a portable excavator, imagining him plowing through debris like a gigantic transparent snowblower.

- They arrived at the Cultivation Cave, a massive complex managed by the Stoneheart Enclave and devoted to fungi used by deep gnomes in cooking and crafting.

- A member of the Stoneheart Enclave greeted them, having heard that they intended to cleanse the temple, and provided the best and freshest fungi from the garden.

- The fungi did not possess the growth- or shrinking-related magical properties of mushrooms the party had encountered previously.

- Glabbagool was delighted by the supplies and consumed mushrooms along the route while clearing a path through the cave.

- The party briefly worried about the possibility of a hallucinating gelatinous cube.

- After several hours of digging, the party successfully reached the entrance to the Rockblight caverns.

- Glabbagool initially appeared prepared to remain behind for “more important activities,” but happily accompanied the party after they made it clear that they wanted his company.

### Discovery of the Crystal Caverns 040.04

The Rockblight was dark, damp, and chill, with rubble, shallow streams, and pools of difficult terrain. In one chamber, bioluminescent fungi shone through semi-transparent crystal formations, casting a kaleidoscope of colors across the walls. A stone statue of a deep gnome cowered beside one outgrowth, holding a broken crystal toward its face as though trying to peer through it. Thorin’s natural 20, combined with Daz’s arcane expertise, revealed that looking through the crystals protected against creatures capable of harming or petrifying victims with their gaze—effectively making the crystals magical sunglasses against a Medusa. The party collected portable crystals in several colors. Although the colors did not alter this particular protective property, Daz and Thorin nearly launched into a much longer discussion about crystal growth, color, and interactions with different spells.

#### The party enters a dark cavern illuminated by bioluminescent fungi and crystal formations, discovering their protective properties.

- The Rockblight’s connecting tunnels resembled those of Inner Blingdenstone but were empty, damp, chill, and strewn with rubble.

- Shallow pools and streams crossed the area, and the pools constituted difficult terrain.

- The party entered a cavern where light from bioluminescent fungi refracted through large semi-transparent crystal formations, casting a kaleidoscope of colors across the walls.

- Images seen through the crystals appeared slightly distorted.

- They discovered a stone statue of a deep gnome cowering beside a crystal outgrowth and holding a broken piece toward his face as though trying to look through it.

- Examining the crystal from both directions revealed a distorting or protective effect.

- Thorin’s natural 20 Arcana check, combined with Daz’s expertise, revealed that the crystals provide protection against creatures with harmful gaze attacks when viewed through them, like magical sunglasses against a Medusa.

- The protection worked by allowing the bearer to look at the threatening creature through the crystal, interfering with the creature’s gaze.

- The petrified gnome had probably been trying to use the crystal but failed to position it correctly before being turned to stone.

- The party warned Glabbagool not to eat the crystals.

- They collected portable crystals of several colors, including a teal one, so they could raise them to their faces if confronted by a gaze-wielding creature.

- The crystals’ colors did not change this particular protective property. Thorin recalled that a crystal’s magic depended more on where and how it grew, while Daz noted that different colors could still produce different effects when combined with particular spells.

- The discussion threatened to become so technical and prolonged that everyone else might begin praying for a Medusa to arrive.

- The party knew the temple lay to the northeast or east but chose to explore first, reasoning that the crystal chamber had already provided something potentially useful against whatever guarded their objective.

### Encounter with Udhask’s Ghost 040.05

Exploring farther, the party entered abandoned residential caverns devoid of warmth or joy and marked by carnage from the old drow assault. A furious deep gnome ghost appeared and attacked. Zalthir struck it repeatedly, prompting the spirit to cry, “They’re coming! Everyone must hide!” The party realized it had become frightened. Gyrgum recalled that frightened ghosts sometimes retreat to their lairs—and possibly their treasure—so the party tried not to destroy it. After Gyrgum struck it once more, the ghost disengaged, turned ethereal, and fled into a nearby burrow.

The party followed and found skeletal remains beside a stone bed. Just before vanishing, the ghost had appeared to reach beneath that bed, leading the adventurers to uncover a hidden compartment containing six gems worth six hundred gold pieces each and a potion of invisibility. The bones bore no signs of violent death, leaving the exact cause of death unknown. The remains belonged to Udhask, one of the spirits Burrow Warden Jadger wanted rescued. The party collected the bones so they could return them to the temple and restore the ghost.

#### The party is attacked by a pissed-off deep gnome ghost, which they subdue and follow to its hidden lair.

- The party entered a large cavern resembling Blingdenstone’s residential districts but devoid of warmth or joy and marked by darkness and carnage.

- The chambers appeared to have been deep gnome homes before the drow invasion and slaughter.

- A hostile deep gnome ghost emerged from the ether. The party’s extensive experience with ghosts kept it from surprising them.

- Zalthir surrounded the spirit with magical darkness, which he could see through, and struck it repeatedly with his fists.

- After the blows landed, the ghost shouted, “They’re coming! Everyone must hide!”

- Zalthir’s insight revealed that the ghost had become afraid, prompting the party to reconsider simply destroying it.

- Zalthir declared, “I want to non-lethally punch the ghost,” although the group debated whether an undead spirit could actually be rendered unconscious.

- Gyrgum recalled that frightened ghosts may retreat to their lairs, where they might have treasure.

- Gyrgum reasoned, “Well, then I bet a ghost that’s been hit with a mace will be even more frightened,” and struck it without destroying it.

- The ghost disengaged, turned ethereal, and fled into a burrow, with the party pursuing it.

- Inside the burrow, the ghost disappeared near skeletal remains lying beside a stone bed.

- Just before vanishing, Udhask appeared to reach beneath the bed, directing the party’s attention to a hidden compartment.

- The party determined that the skeleton showed no signs of a violent death, though its exact cause of death remained unknown.

- The hidden compartment contained six gems worth 600 gold pieces each and a potion of invisibility.

- The party briefly felt guilty about taking Udhask’s stash but noted that he had attacked them.

- They identified the skeletal remains as belonging to Udhask, one of the ghosts that Burrow Warden Jadger wanted rescued.

- The party concluded that returning the bones to the temple would allow Udhask’s spirit to be recovered and collected the remains.

- The discovery prompted Glabbagool and the party to improvise: “When something strange in the neighborhood,” “Who are you going to call?” and “Ghost cleaners.”

### Gargoyle Ambush at the Stream 040.06

Continuing south past a waterfall, the party reached a shallow, swiftly flowing stream. Crystalline formations rose on both sides, while rough-hewn stairs climbed thirty feet to a barren ledge occupied by two winged statues. Suspecting gargoyles, the party invited Glabbagool to clean them. When the cube approached, both statues animated. Their customary plan of remaining motionless until prey drew near was disrupted by the baffling sight of a friendly gelatinous cube with googly eyes.

Zalthir rushed the first gargoyle and battered it with repeated punches. The wounded creature disengaged and flew away in search of a target it could see. Daz cast Toll the Dead, but the gargoyle resisted the cantrip and took no damage. Thorin moved closer and hurled darts at it, finishing it with his second dart despite its resistance to nonmagical piercing attacks. Gyrgum shot the second gargoyle with his crossbow, likewise dealing reduced damage. The creature retaliated by flying directly at him and striking with both bite and claws for ten points of damage, prompting jokes about gargoyle venom and rabies.

Before the party could finish the remaining gargoyle, a crazed earth elemental erupted from the ground near the water and prepared to attack. Its appearance confirmed that the warnings about elementals being driven mad in the Rockblight were true. As the session ended, the party wondered what would happen if Maximilian’s Earthen Grasp were cast upon an earth elemental.

#### The party encounters two winged statues that animate into gargoyles when approached, leading to a fierce combat.

- The party passed a waterfall and reached a shallow, swiftly flowing stream bisecting a cavern.

- Crystalline formations sprouted from the floor on both sides of the water. These were not the same kind of crystals as the gaze-protecting formations.

- Rough-hewn staircases climbed thirty feet to a barren stone ledge occupied by two winged statues.

- The statues resembled architectural gargoyles or giant flies and appeared inanimate from a distance.

- Suspecting a trap, the party asked Glabbagool to approach and clean the statues.

- Both statues animated into gargoyles when Glabbagool came within roughly twenty to thirty feet.

- Glabbagool had never seen stone transform into a living creature and asked, “Is this a bird?” Once told the creature intended to hurt the party, he became angry.

- The gargoyles failed to surprise Glabbagool. Their planned ambush was disrupted by the confusing appearance of a friendly gelatinous cube with googly eyes.

- Zalthir engaged one gargoyle in melee combat and landed four powerful punches, leaving it barely clinging to life.

- The wounded gargoyle disengaged and flew away because it could not see Zalthir through the magical darkness.

- Daz attempted to use Toll the Dead on the injured gargoyle, but it succeeded on its Wisdom saving throw and took no damage.

- Thorin moved closer and threw darts at the flying gargoyle. Its resistance halved the nonmagical piercing damage, but the second dart still finished it.

- Gyrgum advanced and fired his crossbow at the second gargoyle, hitting it but dealing reduced damage because of its resistance to nonmagical weapons.

- The second gargoyle flew directly at Gyrgum and struck him with both bite and claws for a total of ten points of damage.

- The party joked that Gyrgum would need to be checked for rabies, though Lesser Restoration was available if necessary.

- Before the next round could begin, a crazed earth elemental erupted from the ground near the water and joined the battle.

- Its hostile arrival confirmed that the corruption in the Rockblight really could drive earth elementals mad.

- The session concluded with Zalthir due to act next and the party wondering what Maximilian’s Earthen Grasp would do to an earth elemental.
