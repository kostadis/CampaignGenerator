### Arrival at Cairngorm Cavern 015.01

The party made their way to the Cairngorm Cavern, a massive and imposing underground home carved deep into the Southfurrow District, where the Stone Giants of Clan Cairngorm dwelled. At the entrance sat Hgraam, the clan leader, his stony expression unreadable as he waited with the infinite patience of his kind. He had been expecting them, and when they arrived, he simply raised an eyebrow and stood, leaving it to the party to break the silence first. The DM warned that the stone giant would sit there more or less forever, so someone had to speak first. Gyrgum was a little nervous about meeting giants, since he had not had a good relationship with them before.

### Audience with the Stone Giant 015.02

Hgraam spoke gravely of his apprentice, the Stone Giant the party had earlier tried to stop without killing. The apprentice had been driven to madness not by any enemy blade, but by the very stones themselves. Hgraam warned that something ancient and terrible was stirring in the blackest depths of the Underdark, a creeping evil that caused the rock to cry out in pain and the tunnels to shake in fury. He recited a list of omens: a cave with two faces, rock devoured, land overgrown, a pebble that believes itself flesh.

He told them that the best path back to the surface world ran through the town of the deep gnomes. He called the surface the Dreamlands, a place so foreign to him it might as well be myth. Jimjar had told them the same, though Jimjar may have had other agendas, and Hgraam did not know the way himself. When pressed about the Whorlstone Tunnels beneath the city, Hgraam grew somber. He explained that the Deepking's iron-fisted attempts to crush resistance had only driven his enemies underground, where they festered and grew beyond his reach. Zalthir's Arcana check led him to suspect that the apprentice's madness was tied to the growing presence of demons. That presence was far more widespread than the party's scattered sightings of Demogorgon, the creation the kuo-toa worshipped, the demonic manta rays and the brawling demons at Velkynvelve had suggested.

Most troubling of all was what Hgraam revealed about the Deepking himself. The ruler of Gracklstugh had once been a measured and reasonable leader, but in recent times had grown paranoid, erratic, and increasingly isolated from his oldest allies. The change, Hgraam believed, had begun when a new favored advisor named Shal had wormed his way into the king's confidence. Even the Stone Giant clan leader had never been permitted to meet this advisor. The Deepking had asked Hgraam about the Gray Ghosts, but Hgraam knew nothing of them.

### Analyzing the Giant's Gift and Planning 015.03

After a long rant, long even by Stone Giant standards, about how no one takes the time to do anything anymore, Hgraam told them "it is time for you to leave there are rocks that need to be tended." Before sending the party on their way, he presented them with a remarkable gift: a polished crystal the length of a human forearm. It was imbued with the power to commune with animals, plants, and even the dead, and to sharpen the mind's eye when searching for hidden truths.

### Consulting Buppido 015.04

With the crystal in hand and Hgraam's warnings fresh in their minds, the party turned their attention to the West Cleft District and a mysterious Derro courier named Droki. Both Errde Blackskull and Gartokkar had pointed them toward him. Before venturing into the squalid slums, they made a detour to their lodgings to consult their imprisoned companion, Buppido. The Derro laughed heartily at their questions and revealed that the entrance to the Whorlstone Tunnels was hidden within the very heart of the Derro slums. That fact suddenly explained why the Deepking had been unable to stamp out the factions hiding there. He offered to guide them through, and the party, armed with a spare Stone Guard pass, agreed to bring him along. Thorin warned him that a double-cross would end with him sliced up.

### The West Cleft District and the Whorlstone Entrance 015.05

The West Cleft District was a wretched place. Its homes were nothing more than burrows clawed into the cavern walls, and its streets were a chaotic tangle of structures piled atop one another with no plan or order. The stench was overwhelming, and the muttering, screaming, and raving of hundreds of Derro filled the air with an unnerving, discordant chatter. It was there, amid the squalor, that they spotted Droki. He was a pale-faced Derro in a floppy hat adorned with two tentacle-like protrusions that bobbed with his every frantic step. He muttered urgently to himself about being late and moved with an unnatural, almost magical speed. Before the party could approach, he vanished down a narrow fissure hidden behind scraps of canvas and moldy boxes. Buppido immediately bolted after him. Rather than cause a scene restraining him in a place full of Derro, the party let him go and then squeezed through the gap into the tunnels beyond.

The Whorlstone Tunnels were unlike anything they had encountered before. The air tasted faintly of metal, and all sound was strangely muted, as though the cavern itself had swallowed every echo. Swirling patterns of faerzress, the wild, unpredictable magical energy of the Underdark, cast dancing spirals of soft light across the jagged stone pillars lining the walls. Strange fungi clung to the rock in abundance, including peculiar mushrooms the locals called bigwigs and pygmyworts, which were said to swell or shrink whoever consumed them. The party took one of each, sensing they might prove useful, and pressed deeper into the glowing dark after Droki. They realized that following the courier might lead them to every faction he served.

#### The party enters the Derro slums with Buppido and tracks the mysterious courier Droki into a hidden fissure.

  - Buppido gleefully shows his pass, and the gate guards meet him with "the usual set of contempt."

- Thorin proposes yelling for Droki. Buppido makes clear they should stay on the down low.
- The party chooses to find Droki before the cave entrance, reasoning that Droki may know what is in the caves. Thorin: "once you go in the dungeon, you're in the dungeon."
- They spot Droki, a Derro courier wearing a strange floppy hat, muttering to himself and moving with unnatural speed. His pace seems magical.
- Droki disappears into a narrow fissure hidden by canvas and moldy boxes. Buppido says "he's going into stone tunnels. That's one of the entrances" and immediately chases after him.
- The party lets Buppido go. Thorin: "At this point, we can't coerce him without a fuss, and that could go bad."
- The party follows them into the Whorlstone Tunnels, squeezing through a gap leaking eerie fog. Thorin enters "Warily, ready for traps or the unexpected."
- The ceilings in the tunnels rise about 50 feet and drop to 25 in the narrow tunnels. Edible fungi grow everywhere.

  - Gyrgum worries about eating too much bigwig, since only a medium creature fits through the fissure.

- Droki heads north, oblivious to the party.

### Buppido's Mad Shrine 015.06

They did not have to go far before they found Buppido, but the Derro they found was not the one they had known. He stood before a makeshift altar constructed of bones and skulls, his hands wiped clean on his vest, his eyes gleaming with a terrifying and absolute conviction. He declared that his faithful had finally arrived to receive his divine truth, that his grand vision was nearly complete, and that the party had been delivered to him by destiny itself. Then he shrieked, seized a hooked spear, and lunged at them, his skeletal servants rising from the shadows to join the assault.

#### The party confronts Buppido in the tunnels, only to find he has succumbed to madness and raised a macabre altar.
- At an intersection, the party encounters Buppido standing before a makeshift altar constructed of skeletons and bones. He wipes his hands on his vest and smiles viciously.
- Buppido claims to have a divine mission and demands the party worship him as their leader. Thorin: "Just to be clear, we can take him if we need to, right?"
- Declaring the party his 'faithful,' Buppido flies into a psychotic rage and attacks with a hooked spear.

- Thorin engages the skeletons in melee combat as the battle begins.

### The Fall of Buppido 015.07

The battle was fierce and disorienting. Daz outlined three of the skeletons in pale, shimmering light that marked them for advantage, and Zalthir conjured a sphere of absolute darkness to blind and confuse them. Thorin waded into the melee, trading blows with the rattling undead. Gyrgum readied his chessboard holy symbol, waiting for the skeletons to be able to see it, while Zalthir offered to shift his darkness to help. In the chaos, Daz unleashed a volley of unerring magical force that struck Buppido squarely for 15 damage against his 13 hit points. The blow ended his delusions of grandeur and his life in the same moment. Then Gyrgum raised the holy symbol and channeled the divine power of Bahamut, sending the remaining skeletons fleeing in blind panic, and the party cut them down one by one.

When the last skeleton crumbled to dust and silence returned to the glowing cavern, the party stood amid the wreckage of Buppido's mad shrine. He had been their companion once, a guide through the horrors of the Underdark. He had also killed Yuk Yuk, the party's goblin guide, as Thorin never forgot, and whatever he had been before, the darkness had consumed him entirely. "He was crazy, but he was our crazy," Thorin reflected. The party reached fourth level. They took a moment to collect themselves, knowing that Droki was still somewhere ahead in the tunnels, and that the deeper mysteries of the Whorlstone Tunnels, and the evil that Hgraam had warned them about, still lay waiting in the dark.
