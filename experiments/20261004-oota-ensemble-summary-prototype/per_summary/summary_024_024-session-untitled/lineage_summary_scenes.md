# Chapter 024

### Chaos in the Darkness 024.01

The battle in the ritual chamber was a desperate, chaotic affair fought largely in darkness. Zalthir had conjured a sphere of impenetrable shadow at the heart of the room, and the derro cultists scrambled to navigate it. Having abandoned their chanting for cries of "die, die, die," some charged blindly outward to find the source of the magical acid burning their flesh. As the GM explained, drow cast darkness innately, so Underdark dwellers know to run in a straight line until they reach its edge. One of them found Gyrgum and drove a hooked spear into the cleric, and the force of the blow sent him crashing to the ground. The trauma shattered Gyrgum's concentration, and the corrosive magic he had been sustaining over the cultists flickered and died.

#### The party continues its battle against a group of derro cultists and an ettin amid a zone of magical darkness.
- The party engages in combat with derro cultists who were attempting a ritual to create a two-headed giant. Per the GM, "they've given up on chanting, and now they're like, die, die, die."
- Gyrgum maintains a spell that causes acidic pain to three of the derro enemies at the start of their turns. One takes 5 damage before moving.
- A derro warrior charges out of Zalthir's zone of darkness to locate the source of the magical attacks. The GM explains that drow cast darkness innately, so Underdark dwellers aren't frightened by it. They know that "if you run in a particular direction straight you will eventually get to the edge… the drow that didn't figure this out are long dead."
- Gyrgum is struck by a hooked spear and takes 2 points of damage.
- The spear blow forces a DC 9 Strength save, which Gyrgum fails. He is knocked prone.
- Gyrgum also fails his concentration check, prematurely ending his ongoing magical effects on the enemies.

### Battle in the Darkness 024.02

Even prone and bleeding, the party refused to yield. Zalthir had been frightened earlier in the fight, but Narrak's concentration had since broken, so the fear no longer held him. He dragged his sphere of darkness back over the ettin. He had already called on his eldritch tattoo while still frightened, and his face and arms were now writhing inky tendrils that stretched fifteen feet into the darkness and lashed out at the massive two-headed ettin Grula-Munga with relentless fury. The tentacles coiled around the beast and grappled it in place while Zalthir kept pummelling it from within the shadow. No one outside the sphere could see it, though they could certainly hear the results. Meanwhile, Gyrgum hauled himself back to his feet and unleashed a torrent of caustic acid over the remaining cultists. Two of them dodged the worst of it, but one was coated so thoroughly that the burning would not stop until he fell.

Daz kept the derro savant Narrak locked in the crushing grip of a great earthen hand that had erupted from the stone floor, squeezing him each time he struggled to break free. Narrak cast silence to create a zone of dead air that would stop the party's magic, but the party pressed on regardless. Daz stepped out of the darkness and sent three bolts of magical force into one cultist, dropping him where he stood. Narrak greeted the death with the cry "Fools, your sacrifice is but a step toward glory." Grula-Munga struck back hard, hitting Thorin for 13 damage. Thorin kept hammering the ettin anyway. When Zalthir's tentacles finally dropped Grula-Munga, Narrak repeated his line about glory "with a little bit less conviction this time." The party then turned its full attention to the restrained and increasingly desperate savant. Zalthir and Thorin closed in, Thorin guided by his blindsight despite the darkness.

#### The party continues its struggle against Narrak, an ettin named Grula-Munga, and several derro cultists within a zone of magical darkness.
- Daz maintains a magical earthen grip on Narrak, who is shown as grappled and unable to move.
- Narrak casts silence to stop the party's spellcasting. Because casting counts as an action rather than an attack, Thorin gets no opportunity attack.
- Grula-Munga, the two-headed ettin, stands outside the darkness beside Thorin. She swings her battle axe twice at Thorin, rolling 14 and 11 against his AC 18, and fails to connect. The first swing was also at disadvantage from Thorin's weapon effect.
- A derro cultist attacks the prone Gyrgum with a hooked spear at advantage. The best roll is a 6, and the cleric's armor holds firm.
- Zalthir is no longer frightened now that Narrak's concentration has broken. He moves his sphere of darkness so the ettin is back inside it.
- Zalthir's tattoo, activated while he was still frightened, has turned his face and arms into inky tentacles, and they lash out at the ettin from within the darkness. He attacks with advantage in the dark and hits for 8 and 5. The ettin is then at 41 hit points, and Zalthir spends a focus point for a third hit of 12.
- Zalthir grapples the ettin with his eldritch tendrils while delivering these strikes. After some rules discussion about grappling and one-armed attacks, the GM rules the ettin grappled.
- A derro steps out of the darkness and fires a crossbow at Gyrgum at disadvantage. It misses.
- Gyrgum spends half his movement to stand. He weighs a Guiding Bolt, which the GM notes would have disadvantage against the adjacent derro but not a more distant one, and instead exhales a stream of caustic acid over all three remaining derro cultists.
- Thorin presses the advantage against the massive ettin with his longsword, attacking with advantage and dealing 8 damage. He laments having only one attack. The GM suggests dual-wielding two short swords, which would mean giving up his shield, and Thorin decides to keep the longsword +1 for now.
- Daz moves out of the darkness so he can cast. He unleashes all three glowing darts of Magic Missile on a single weakened derro cultist and strikes him down.

### The Fall of the Ettin and the Savant 024.03

Narrak fought to the last. He thrust his spear at Thorin even as the earthen hand crushed the breath from him, but his strikes found no purchase against the warrior's defenses. Thorin delivered the killing blow. As Narrak crumpled to the ground, he spent his final breath on a chilling declaration: the ritual could not be undone, and a name the party did not catch, "sacrificed." The words hung in the air like smoke as the last of the cultists fell, leaving the chamber suddenly, eerily quiet. Only one figure remained, lurking in the background and trying very hard not to be noticed.

#### The party concludes its brutal confrontation with the ettin Grula-Munga and the derro savant Narrak.
- A derro hits Gyrgum with a spear for 5 damage. Gyrgum shrugs it off: "I shrug it off."
- Narrak tries to break free of the earthen hand but fails against Daz's DC 14.
- Grula-Munga lands a battle-axe blow on Thorin for 13 damage ("Ouch. That hurts a little. Won't lie."), then misses her second swing on a 16.
- Zalthir considers dragging the blinded, grappled ettin so that Narrak stands between it and Thorin, hoping its blind swings would hit the savant instead. He decides it is simpler to just kill it.
- Thorin strikes the ettin with his longsword. Zalthir's tentacle strikes, 11 and 11 damage, finally slay the two-headed beast.
- Zalthir turns his tentacles on Narrak for 11 damage. The GM rules Narrak can be grappled by both Zalthir and Daz's spell, since they are two separate sources.
- The acid-coated derro takes 6 more acid damage from Gyrgum's brew and dies without a word.
- Thorin attacks Narrak using his blindsight and uses Second Wind as a bonus action to heal 11.
- Daz uses the magical earthen hand to restrain and crush Narrak. Narrak fails the Strength save and takes 5 damage, and remains restrained. Earlier, Daz had also dispatched a derro cultist with bolts of magical force.
- Having "tried spells… tried everything," Narrak attempts to strike Thorin with a spear at disadvantage and fails to get past the warrior's defenses while restrained.
- Zalthir and Thorin focus their attacks on the grappled Narrak. Zalthir brings him down to 17 hit points and spends another focus point to attack again. Gyrgum casts True Strike and hits a derro with his mace for 9 damage.
- Thorin deals the fatal blow for 9 damage. The GM notes that Narrak was the one "practicing occult spells to turn stone giants into two-headed beasts" and a member of the Council of Savants.
- In his final moments, Narrak cryptically claims the ritual cannot be undone and gasps that someone was "sacrificed."
- The remaining derro cultists are defeated as the party clears the chamber, and the last one dies in combat ("In an honorable way, yes"). Narrak's silence spell ends.
- A figure in the background is still there, "very much trying really hard at this point to not be noticed."

### Spoils of the Ritual Chamber 024.04

With the fighting done, the party searched the ritual chamber and found a collection of dark texts. Chief among them was a volume called The Rituals of the Two-Headed Beast, which laid bare the methods the cult had used to drive stone giants mad and warp them into two-headed monstrosities. Gyrgum offered a quiet prayer to Bahamut before removing a key from around Narrak's neck. Thorin used the key to carefully open an iron chest, standing well to the side, just in case. Inside were 45 gold and 15 silver pieces in a small leather bag, alongside five doses of Keoghtom's Ointment, a rare and powerful healing salve that mends wounds and purges poison from the body.

The party then took stock of everything they had gathered: the texts, the evidence of the king's collusion with assassins, the red dragon egg, and a couple of passes that would let them walk out with all of it. Jimjar bet that they would want to hold on to some of it for later. Then the small fungal creature Stool tugged at Zalthir's sleeve and reminded him, with the cheerful persistence of a child, that they still needed to go rescue Rumpadump.
