# Chapter 023

### The Hidden Ritual 023.01

The party crept into a vast, foul-smelling cavern reeking of brimstone and fungi, where flickering campfires cast an eerie glow over a raised platform at the center. Five Derro cultists stood upon it, chanting in a discordant, maddening chorus around a pulsing ritual circle of sickly green light. At the heart of the ritual stood a grotesque stone giant statue, barely two feet tall, from which a second head was visibly sprouting and reshaping itself with each unholy verse. A restless death dog paced in a cage near the platform's edge, watched over by a nervous Derro handler. A massive female ettin named Grula-Munga lurked silently in a fungal alcove, observing the proceedings. The party had stumbled on the scene by chance. They had already recovered a trove of heretical Demogorgon texts, and they confirmed this was the same cult behind the demonic infusion of the dragon egg.

Daz's arcana check (14, against a needed 16) and Gyrgum's first religion check (10) both fell short of the full picture. Even so, the party pieced together that this ritual was likely the cause of the two-headed stone giant that had rampaged through the market only four days earlier in game time. On a second religion roll of 21, Gyrgum was inspired by Bahamut. He surmised that the statue is a focal point for the curse, and that bringing one of the corrupted statues to Stonespeaker Hgraam might allow him to reverse the curse afflicting his people.

The party debated their options in hushed tones. The locked chests scattered about the cavern hinted at valuable treasures, since Narrak, the lead spellcaster, is a member of the Council of Savants. The heretical texts they had already recovered were damning enough to bring down the entire Council for all time. The DM also pointed out that Thorin, a lover of giants, would want to put a stop to cultists manipulating giant physiology. But the party's magical reserves were dangerously low. Gyrgum admitted he was "basically out of spells," and Daz was out of first-level spells. The encounter was no small thing either: the ordinary Derro had around 13 hit points, two tougher ones had 27, and Narrak himself had 70. Along the way, Gyrgum proposed fixing the extra head with a chisel, then a magic chisel. The table also spent a spell convinced the statues were life-sized, until the DM clarified they were two-foot, 100-pound miniatures.

### Rest at Buppido's Sanctuary 023.02

Ultimately, the party marked the location on the map and retreated to Buppido's former resting place, a secluded alcove where Buppido had once sheltered without being disturbed. Stool pressed them to go find his friend Rumpadump first. He insisted that the Myconids and their spore-zombie Quaggoths were friends and that no one needed to be killed. The party deferred, and when Gyrgum suggested sleeping on it, Stool simply fell asleep and was carried along.

Before settling in for the night, their ghostly companion Pelek offered an observation. Pelek is currently inhabiting the body of an assassin, and he noted that the party had "completed a full circle." He then politely requested to be tied up while he slept, worried that the original host's consciousness might resurface and overwhelm him. When Gyrgum offered hemp or silk rope, Pelek asked specifically for silk, noting that he hadn't slept in a body in ages.

Daz, drawing on his textbook *The Undead Horde*, recalled that the possession was actually stable unless the host was knocked unconscious. He said nothing, reasoning that keeping an untrustworthy newcomer bound was simply good policy, a lesson hard-learned from their time with Buppido. "Always tie up new members," Zalthir proposed, and the party agreed.

Jimjar, ever the opportunist, offered a wager on the knots. Gyrgum was confident in his work and bet five gold pieces that Pelek would not escape his bonds by morning. By the time the party stirred again, Jimjar was paying up with a gracious "well done, well done."

### Interrupting the Cult's Rehearsal 023.03

Refreshed and restored, the party returned to the ritual cavern and found that Narrak had already completed his work on one statue and was now preparing to corrupt another. By the DM's map, there were now three statues, and Narrak was preparing the third. Narrak announced grandly that converting one more of Hgraam's giants into a double-headed beast would bring Gracklstugh to its knees: "by golly." The DM played him as a cultist who refuses to curse. "I don't curse. I just invoke demonic powers."

What followed was less than inspiring. Narrak launched into the ritual chant, including a new verse about "Whorlstone's depths." He grew increasingly furious as his followers stumbled through the chorus with all the passion of bored schoolchildren, and he made them run it again as a rehearsal. "Silence, you blithering half-wits! More vigor! This city's doom, a ritual! Dig it!" he shrieked, his squeaky voice doing little to sell the menace of his words. Meanwhile, the death dog's handler kept his head down by busily grooming the dog. With the cult distracted, Gyrgum quietly blessed his three companions. The party then beat the cultists' passive perception of 7 on a group stealth check (Gyrgum rolled a 20) and slipped forward through the shadows, undetected, earning a surprise round.

### Ambush at the Ritual Site 023.04

The ambush began with a burst of magical darkness that swallowed the platform whole. Zalthir followed it with a gout of dragonborn fire breath that scorched several of the cultists where they stood. From inside the darkness, Narrak shrieked in defiance. One Derro stepped out to stab with a short spear, and another struck blindly from within the dark, but both missed. Zalthir briefly felt the madness of the ritual circle clawing at his mind, then steadied himself with monastic focus.

Gyrgum then unleashed a stream of caustic acid that coated the chanters in corrosive liquid. Zalthir warned Thorin away from the maddening circle, and Thorin charged a cultist instead. Daz hurled blazing rays: the first finished off one of the tougher Derro, and the last seared Grula-Munga. Grula-Munga pointed out the intruders with an exasperated "Oh, brother!"

Narrak, furious and wounded, stepped out of the darkness and retaliated with a Fear spell. The wave of terror sent Thorin, Gyrgum, and Zalthir fleeing in blind panic, nearly unraveling the assault entirely. Gyrgum had blessed everyone but himself, and he failed his save. Grula-Munga waded into the fray; her battle axe glanced off Thorin, but her morning star struck the frightened warrior hard for 14 damage.

Zalthir, thinking quickly, dashed away and repositioned the sphere of darkness to cut off Narrak's line of sight. He then empowered his tattoo with Eldritch Maul. One by one, the party members shook off their fear and turned back to the fight. A flash of radiant light from Gyrgum's Guiding Bolt marked Grula-Munga for Thorin's follow-up blow, a longsword strike with advantage for 16 damage, which left her with 54 hit points.

Finally, at the DM's suggestion, Daz called upon the earth itself. A massive hand of compacted stone erupted from the ground to seize Narrak, pinning him in place and breaking his concentration, which ended the fear effect. Even restrained and battered, Narrak managed to spit out one last defiant proclamation: "You cannot stop the inevitable… The curse spreads!" He capped it with "By golly." His squeaky voice rang through the cavern as the battle hung in the balance. The session ended mid-combat, to resume after Daz's turn.
