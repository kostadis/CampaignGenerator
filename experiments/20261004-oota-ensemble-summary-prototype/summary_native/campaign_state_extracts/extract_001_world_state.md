## 1. Current Situation (end of Ch70)

- **Where the party is:** In the hidden volcanic cavern deep beneath Candlekeep, at the summit of the **Obsidian Tower**. The party is inside or immediately outside the **Inner Book Chamber**, behind the now-open **Obsidian Door**.
- **What just happened:**
  - Zalthir carried the **second Manshoon simulacrum** through the anti-magic field and threw it into the **Lava Pit**.
  - **Edvaldo**, a doppelganger serving Manshoon, smashed **2 of Alaundo's 6 unplayed prophecy crystals**. Gyrgum secured the **4 survivors**.
  - Gyrgum, as the verified Reader, spoke the riddle answer ("A candle"). The door opened.
  - Daz used Mage Hand to reach the **Book of Vile Darkness** without touching it, and covered it with an illusion that it had turned to ash. He resisted the book's influence. The book offered him a choice of self-knowledge or knowledge of the demons, and he chose the demons. The book is now giving him information about Underdark events. Its trustworthiness is deeply suspect.
- **Immediate pressures:**
  - **Miirym's boon** widened the anti-magic field to 30 ft for 10 minutes. Six minutes had elapsed when ch70 began, so the boon is expiring or expired.
  - **Candlekeep's surviving Avowed Readers** are expected to arrive shortly. They will not let artifacts of extraordinary power disappear unnoticed.
  - **Edvaldo** fled as a halfling toward the anti-magic barrier, along the only route Thorin was not blocking. His current whereabouts are unknown.
  - The **real Manshoon** is still at large. The simulacrum warned that killing it would bring the real Manshoon's vengeance.
  - **Hundreds of Glyphs of Warding** protect the tower's tomes. An explosion could trigger a catastrophic chain reaction.
- **Party level:** 8. The level-up happened in ch52, and no later level-up is recorded.
- **Party name:** The **Ember Vanguard**. Earlier joke names include "Firefly Tactics" and "Ember Grapple". The name is notorious in Gracklstugh, where it is tied to wildly exaggerated legends, including a "20th-level drow archmage". Kalan has also deputized the party as **Watchers of Candlekeep**.

## 2. The Party (Player Characters)

### Thorin Giantfriend
- **Identity:** A dwarf raised by a hill giant tribe. He speaks Dwarvish with a Giant accent, speaks Giant, and wears giant clothes. He shares the giants' dislike of dragons. He defends his adoptive "different giants" against surface-dwarf prejudice from the War of the Giants.
- **Martial kit:** Martial combatant. Recorded abilities: Hill Strike (learned from a hill giant battle master), Action Surge, Menacing Attack (frightens), sapping strikes (target has disadvantage on its next attack), Sentinel, 10-ft blindsight, and Know Your Enemy. He took Sharpshooter at level 8 with a GM house rule putting the +1 into Strength. His attack bonus with Dawnbringer is +10. He carries darts and javelins.
- **Weapon:** **Dawnbringer**, a sentient sunblade (see §6). Their relationship is ongoing: "couples counseling" with Candlekeep scholars, and he insists it is "purely platonic".
- **Temperament:** Radical candor and transparency. He dislikes NDAs and faction games and wants to "be my faction". He once played a dice-driven "blurt" mechanic. He takes a stand on rights: oozes, elemental labor, unions.
- **Recent deeds:**
  - Killed Moziqodo (ch63) and the Zhentarim assassin (ch65).
  - Solved the vault cryptogram (ch67).
  - Fell through the staircase trap and was caught mid-air by Zalthir (ch67–68).
  - Struck and frightened Edvaldo, then held the stairs to protect the prophecy stones (ch70).
- **Ties:**
  - **Eldeth Feldrun** swore that her clan will embrace him. Thorin admits he has "a thing" for her.
  - Alaundo's prophecy names a "giant-raised dwarf" (Thorin) to be summoned by deep dwarves to an ancient forge.

### Zalthir
- **Identity:** A brass dragonborn Shadow Monk from a secretive order that teaches silence and avoiding frontal conflict. Formative influences: Korrh Erann (flee drow pursuit) and "The Garden Shadow" (cutting dangerous flora). The Medusa Neheedra mocked "the circumstances of his birth", which hints at a stigmatized lineage. **[Unconfirmed — nature unknown]**
- **Capabilities:**
  - Darkness, which he can see through.
  - Shadow Step.
  - Flurry of blows and Stunning Strike.
  - Fire breath weapon.
  - Wings, which let him fly and hover.
  - Two tattoos. The Eldritch Claw / Empowered tattoo extends inky tendrils that strike and grapple at 15 ft.
  - Grappler build: Tavern Brawler and Street Justice (restrain with rope).
  - Minor Illusion.
- **Gear:** A Potion of Flying, stolen from Alkrist's safe and stored inside Glabbagool.
- **Training:** Passed Candlekeep's Trial of the Broken Mirror (Brother Khell-Vire / Master Kenshi), though he failed every skill demonstration and redeemed himself with a strike. He is mentor to **Glabbagool**, his formal shadow-monk apprentice and sidekick.
- **Obligations:** Owes **Spanner** a study session of his "mechanical symmetry", alongside Glabbagool. This is collateral for the borrowed modron tools.
- **Recent deeds:**
  - Destroyed the first Manshoon simulacrum (ch69).
  - Bound Edvaldo (ch69).
  - Grappled the second simulacrum and threw it into the lava (ch70). He accepted the risk that this might destroy the prophecy crystals.
  - Earlier: killed Asha Vandree (ch53) and the Pudding King (ch47).
- **Prophecy:** Likely the "Shadow Walker" in Alaundo's forge prophecy.

### Gyrgum
- **Identity:** An orc cleric of **Bahamut**, raised in an orc tribe and trained under the mentor Gorg'Bahamut. He holds a heterodox theology: Bahamut guided Stroud's hand against Protanther, mortal choice matters, and dragons are defined by choice, not scale color. He considers judging by race the ultimate evil.
- **Academic standing:** Founder of the **Gyrgumite School**, which proposes "fixed points" between which mortals have free action. It is a fourth position on the Question of the Age. Brother Vareth champions him, and Miirym praised his wisdom. He has agreed to co-author a monograph with Tadric, and turned down Fembris's "duograph".
- **Magic:**
  - Favorite spell: Guiding Bolt; his third-level casting killed Ilvara.
  - Spirit Guardians, Tasha's Caustic Brew, Glyph of Warding, Spiritual Weapon (cast at 5th level in ch69), Locate Creature, Bless, Healing Word, and Revivify.
  - Phantasmal Killer was listed in his arsenal (ch50).
- **Currently holds:**
  - The real High Tower key (from Tadric).
  - Kalan's fake key.
  - The stolen sapphire. Crushing it recalls the real key.
  - The **4 surviving prophecy crystals**.
- **Recent deeds:**
  - Persuaded Manshoon to move their duel away from the books (ch68).
  - Located the second simulacrum (ch69).
  - Opened the Obsidian Door as the verified Reader (ch70).
- **Style:** Plays the "Platinum Fool". Keeps meticulous notes, loves puns, and is transactional in a good-natured way.

### Daz
- **Identity:** A drow evoker wizard and an exile from Menzoberranzan who rejects slavery and Lolth fanaticism. He is the party's undisputed **"General"**, though he prefers "just Daz". He keeps a journal after the style of the author Milo Goodbarrel. He knows the surface High Forest.

## 3. Candlekeep — Political State

**Institution:** A fortress-library on the Sword Coast south of Waterdeep.
- **Entry rule:** Visitors pay with a unique book.
- **Leadership:** The First Reader heads it; the Avowed staff it. There is a Council of Twelve and nine leading Readers. **Four of the nine are eliminated**: Janussi, Bookwyrm, Alkrist, and A'lai.
- **Wards:** Normally block teleportation and suppress fire. The wards were **compromised** in ch65, when Daz's Fireball detonated. Manshoon's simulacrum burned through the shields with a **Staff of Power** and enormous magic.
- **Prison:** Bell Tower anti-magic cells, the **Null Magic Prison**. Policy is to exile, not execute.
- **The Endless Chant of Alaundo's prophecies fell silent in ch64**, which "never happens".

## 4. Beneath Candlekeep — The Vault Complex

Locations in route order:
1. **House of Alaundo** (Founders Court). Pouring ink into the brass statue's ink pot reveals the **Hidden Staircase**.
2. **Hidden Staircase**. A trap section near step 97 opens into the **Great Shaft**: about 1,000 ft deep, with a feather-fall zone near the bottom.
3. **Lava Cavern**. 200 ft tall, with a molten lake. **Miirym** (Vydykyq), the spectral silver dragon Sentinel Wyrm, appears here. She is bound as a witness, not a weapon.
4. **Obsidian Bridge**. Mechanical; the party operated it with the modron tools. Its first 10 ft are permanent anti-magic. Magical flight across that section fails.
5. **Obsidian Tower**. Three stories, lit by Continual Flame, with 4 mounted crossbows.
   - The **Iron Owlbear Guardian** asks three questions. Answers: the Hearth; the triumvirate of Oghma, Deneir, and Milil; and the High Tower's height. A previous owlbear lies broken. The guardian is neutral and offers commentary.
   - **Vault of Dangerous Secrets / Tower Library:** about 100 hazardous tomes protected by hundreds of glyphs. A note permits consultation but forbids removal, and warns that the means to destroy the collection are kept above.
   - **Upper Tower Chamber:** The prophecy tray and the **Obsidian Door**. The door bears 11 scorch marks from Manshoon's failed attempts. Its riddle must be answered by a verified Reader, in darkness, freely, and once.
   - **Inner Book Chamber:** the **Book of Vile Darkness** on a podium.
6. **Lava Pit**, just outside the tower. The second simulacrum was thrown in here.

**Open:** Whether the modron tools satisfy riddle line 4 ("dust of Mechanus on dormant gears") was never ruled on tape. The ch67 summary says the tools were ruled an alternative; the ch68 GM note says this is open. In practice the bridge worked.

## 5. Manshoon & the Zhentarim

- **Manshoon:** A legendary Zhentarim wizard tied to the Moonsea and Zhentil Keep. He reportedly died 170 years ago and keeps returning "like a bad penny". He has died more than once.
- **What he wanted:** For 11 years, through A'lai, he took selected books. His real target was the **Book of Vile Darkness**, not the prophecies.
- **Simulacrum #1:** Breached Candlekeep using the Staff of Power and massive expenditure, after the party disrupted his plans with Alkrist. It shattered the High Tower door with a wall of force. **Destroyed by Zalthir in ch69** (it melted to snow). Its **Robe of the Archmagi** is now with Daz.
- **Simulacrum #2:** Waited 6 hours in the upper chamber and offered a bargain: prophecy gems in exchange for opening the door. **Thrown into the lava by Zalthir in ch70.**
- **Retinue:** Edvaldo the doppelganger survives and fled. The two Zhentarim thugs and the assassin are dead. No Zhentarim prisoner survives.
- **Staff of Power:** Used in the breach. Whether it was recovered is **not recorded**.
- **Threat:** The real Manshoon's retaliation is expected but not yet seen.
