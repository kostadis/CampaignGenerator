### Chapter 057 — Tadric

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/057-session-candlekeep-s-cold-case-the-gyrgumite-gambit.md`
- Original heading: `Tadric`

A 23-year-old watcher with a distinctive bowl-cut haircut and dark brown hair who was tasked with guarding the door to the Keeper's chambers. He provided the party with a careful and detailed timeline of everyone who visited the room before their arrival. He admitted he was not watching closely enough to track everything the Gatewarden did inside, as his job was crowd control outside, not observation within — and he confessed he does not really like blood and murder scenes. He spoke warmly of Kalan Strongbranch and seemed troubled by the Gatewarden's removal. When asked if he had done a good job, he reflected: "I don't think I did a good job."

### Chapter 063 — Tadric

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `Tadric`

Kalan's loyal lieutenant, recognizable as a short young human with a bowl-cut hairstyle. He had previously guarded Janussi's murder scene. Kalan entrusted him with the genuine high tower key and hid him alone in the North Galleries without telling him what he carried.

Moziqodo grappled Tadric and began tearing him apart before the party arrived. The demon spawn could have killed him with a single attack and fled with the key, but the party stunned and killed it before it could act. Gyrgum healed Tadric's wounds, after which the shocked lieutenant clutched the key, identified his attacker as Sylvira's son, and asked what had happened.

### Chapter 064 — Tadric

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/064-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Tadric`

Kalan's lieutenant, freshly healed as the session opens. He had carried the genuine High Tower key all night without knowing what it was. Once he learns its importance, he refuses simply to surrender it and retire: “Now that I know what it is, I'm not giving it to you and going to bed.”

Shaken but emboldened by surviving Moziqodo's attack, Tadric insists on accompanying the party and protests, “I faced Moziqodo.” He appears seized by a desperate desire to be heroic. The sudden silence of Candlekeep's prophetic chant leaves him stunned, and it is Tadric who reminds the party, “Sylvira, someone needs to tell Sylvira that her son is dead.” He also guides the group toward the High Tower.

### Chapter 067 — Tadric

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/067-session-08-17.md`
- Original heading: `Tadric`

A Watcher and lieutenant to Kalan Strongbranch who is present during the aftermath of the High Tower battle. He recognizes the party's deputized authority and their history of aiding Candlekeep. After Gyrgum argues for the necessity of A'lai's bargain — and agrees in jest to coauthor a monograph — Tadric accepts responsibility for delivering A'lai to the null magic prison.
