# NPCs — Sarith Kzekarit

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 003 — Sarith Kzekarit

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/003-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Sarith Kzekarit`

A drow prisoner with a deeply personal grievance against his former captors. Once he reaches the armory and acquires a short sword, he attacks one of the guards while hurling bitter insults, striking twice and killing him.

During the final confrontation, Sarith is tempted to shout at Imbros loudly enough to compromise the escape. Gyrgum calms him with an appeal to spirituality and meditation. Sarith accepts the unexpected advice, focuses his fury, and delivers the killing blow to the Elite Warrior.

### Chapter 004 — Sarith

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/004-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Sarith`

A drow prisoner who oscillates between madness and sanity. He scavenged studded leather armor from a fallen drow warrior and provided tactical advice during the escape, warning that the party should leave quickly rather than sabotage Velkynvelve and provoke greater retaliation. His warning was followed by a mad cackle about the deliciousness of revenge. Sarith later served as the party's navigator through the Underdark and appeared truthful about his familiarity with the tunnels. He advocated reaching the Darklake, where the group could find or steal a boat and travel toward several major settlements.

### Chapter 005 — Sarith

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/005-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Sarith`

A Drow prisoner and guide who leads the party through the Underdark. He was not trapped by the rockfall. He is somewhat prideful regarding his navigational skills, even when nearly leading the group astray. He "claims to know where" he is going, and begrudgingly accepts assistance to keep the group on the correct path, allowing only that Gyrgum is "very wise." He helped defend the group with ranged attacks during the carrion crawler ambush and was targeted by a tentacle that missed spectacularly. His survival bonus is +0.

### Chapter 025 — Sarith

### Chapter 031 — Sarith

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Sarith`
