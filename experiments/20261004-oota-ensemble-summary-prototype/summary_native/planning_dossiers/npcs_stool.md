# NPCs — Stool

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 002 — Stool

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Stool`

A Myconid Sprout prisoner who possesses rapport spores, allowing everyone within fifteen feet to communicate telepathically as a shared “party line.” The ability belongs to Stool and functions only while creatures remain within its limited range. Stool was assigned to cleaning duties alongside Daz and was delighted by the prisoners' work, regarding the day's cleaning as “awesome sauce.” Although friendly and eager to remain with the group, the others jokingly wonder whether Myconid hospitality might eventually involve turning them into fertilizer.

### Chapter 003 — Stool

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/003-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Stool`

A small, sentient fungus creature known as a myconid sprout. He happily fills the cell with rapport spores, allowing the party and other prisoners to communicate telepathically and coordinate their escape without speaking aloud. His spores can connect multiple intelligent creatures for an hour, though communication remains limited by proximity. The party’s discussion establishes that linked creatures can direct thoughts selectively rather than broadcasting every thought to everyone.

### Chapter 004 — Stool

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/004-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Stool`

A frightened young myconid sprout with the demeanor of an eight-year-old child. Stool chooses Gyrgum as his protector and “bestest friend,” stays as close beside the orc as possible, and hopes that Gyrgum will keep him safe and eventually take him home. His body has multicolored facets that change under different lighting conditions.

### Chapter 005 — Stool

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/005-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Stool`

A small, sentient Myconid sprout who shows great concern for the party. Stool assists in rescuing companions from the rubble: he checks on Gyrgum and frees Shuushar and Buppido. Moving only 10 feet, he rides on Thorin's back during the escape to keep pace with the group. He establishes a telepathic connection among the group via rapport spores so they can communicate silently during the chase. He has only four spores and cannot mind-control drow. He urged the party to run.

### Chapter 025 — Stool

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/025-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Stool`

A young Myconid sprout and companion to the party who communicates telepathically. He is fiercely loyal and persistent, throwing a tantrum to convince the party to visit his friend Rumpadump before leaving the caverns.
- Calls Zalthir "a scaredy cat" and tries to adopt Thorin as his new friend.
- Explains Myconid abilities: calming people and communicating at close range. He insists "zombies" require death first and gives the Myconid term, roughly "creature that helps after death."
- Starts walking off alone to force the party's hand.
- Disputes Rumpadump's theory that the mushrooms caused the madness.
