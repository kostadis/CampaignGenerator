# NPCs — Manshoon (simulacrum)

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 068 — Manshoon (simulacrum)

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/068-session-08-24.md`
- Original heading: `Manshoon (simulacrum)`

Not the man himself but **a simulacrum**—the party has not been told, and nothing on tape reveals it. Found calmly reading at a desk among the forbidden tomes, he displays a surprisingly civilized demeanor. He invites the party to browse but warns that leaving with what they seek is the true challenge.

Manshoon agrees with Gyrgum that the priceless books should not be endangered by their duel and willingly relocates to the lower guardian chamber. He allows the party to descend first, then casts *Mirror Image* before following. Once below, he frames the confrontation as a formal magical duel.

He declines to spend his *Counterspell* on Daz’s *Phantasmal Killer* and makes the Wisdom save exactly, rolling 8 and adding +9 to reach DC 17. Under the standing PHB 2024 ruling, he still takes 13 psychic damage, but the spell ends and imposes no disadvantage. **He goes into next session down 13 hit points, with no disadvantage and the spell already ended.**

**A second simulacrum is present and concealed.** Nothing about this was said at the table—the word “simulacrum” is never spoken this session, and the party never asks. *GM intent for next session, not table canon:* the first body is the one now standing on the lower level; the second is still in the library, **frantically trying to get through it**, and the party’s horror when they notice is the point.
