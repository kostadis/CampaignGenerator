# NPCs — Miirym, the Sentinel Wyrm — the Spectral Silver Dragon

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 068 — Miirym, the Sentinel Wyrm — the Spectral Silver Dragon

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/068-session-08-24.md`
- Original heading: `Miirym, the Sentinel Wyrm — the Spectral Silver Dragon`

The translucent ghost-dragon guardian of Candlekeep, whose true name **Vydykyq** was the answer to the cryptogram’s first riddle—the name Sylvira Savikas handed the party in Chapter 67. Grouchy and imperious, she manifests in the lava chamber with molten rock passing through her spectral form.

Miirym reveals that Manshoon entered her keep and remains inside. She requires the party to distinguish the Reader from the witnesses, then verifies Gyrgum’s claim by demanding proof of his knowledge of Candlekeep. His learning and service to Bahamut impress her enough that she says wisdom truly flows into him from the platinum god.

Bound to act as a witness rather than a weapon, Miirym cannot join the party’s attack. She instead offers several possible boons, including teleportation denial, information about what Manshoon took, verification of everyone’s identities, or control over the anti-magic field. The party chooses to let her widen the field to thirty feet for ten minutes. At Zalthir’s request, she agrees to hold that boon in reserve for a surprise activation and approves the decision with “Well chosen.”
