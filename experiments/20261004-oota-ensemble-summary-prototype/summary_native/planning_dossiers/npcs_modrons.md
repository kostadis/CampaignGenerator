## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 067 — Modrons

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/067-session-08-17.md`
- Original heading: `Modrons`

Thirteen box-like mechanical creatures from the plane of Mechanus who work under Spanner in the House of Mechanus. Some resemble single boxes with limbs, while others appear like stacked geometric forms. They communicate in a strange mechanistic language and perform a synchronized, mechanized dance while deliberating among themselves.
