# NPCs — Buppido

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 002 — Buppido

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Buppido`

A derro prisoner with severe delusions of grandeur who appears to believe he is divine. He interprets captivity and escape alike as elements of his “divine plan.” His first strategy called for everyone else to distract the drow while he escaped through the waterfall alone. After the others appealed to his vanity, Buppido concluded that he required protectors and disciples and therefore needed to bring the party with him. He promised to reveal further mysteries of the divine plan later.

### Chapter 003 — Buppido

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/003-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Buppido`

A talkative derro prisoner who is reluctant to lead the escape and suggests that the “divine plan” requires somebody else to secure the weapons first. Once armed, he loudly insists that he is an expert in all matters related to hand crossbows. His confidence is not entirely justified, as his bolt misses the target. During the division of equipment, the group jokes that Buppido should receive one of everything “because he’s magnificent.”

### Chapter 015 — Buppido

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/015-session-04-14.md`
- Original heading: `Buppido`

A Derro who completely succumbed to madness, believing himself to be a messianic leader on a divine mission to free his people. He harbored deep paranoid resentment for the Council of Savants, guided the party into the Whorlstone Tunnels, then turned on them at his makeshift shrine of bones and was ultimately slain.

- He had 13 hit points.
- He showed his borrowed pass gleefully at the gate and bolted after Droki the moment he could.

### Chapter 016 — Buppido

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/016-session-04-28.md`
