# Spells — Enlarge

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 010 — Enlarge

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/010-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Enlarge`

An innate ability used by the duergar to double their size and increase their physical strength during the fight, making them more formidable opponents as they attempted to flee or continue fighting. It does not grant extra hit points. When the effect ended, a slain duergar shrank back to small size.

### Chapter 013 — Enlarge

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/013-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Enlarge`

A magical ability used by two of the duergar guards to physically increase their size and strength during combat with the stone giant, making them more formidable opponents. When they returned to normal size afterward, it came with the "usual popping sound."
