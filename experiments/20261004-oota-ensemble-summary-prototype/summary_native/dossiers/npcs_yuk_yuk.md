# NPCs — Yuk Yuk

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 006 — Yuk Yuk

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/006-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Yuk Yuk`

An eccentric goblin who lives in the Underdark and offers guide services through the Silken Paths. He speaks with a laid-back, surfer-like attitude and is highly skilled at sliding and surfing across the web strands while dodging giant spiders. He took Jimjar's bet with a 20-gold counter-wager and was very annoyed to learn Jimjar couldn't pay. He was also the first to sense the spiders coming.

### Chapter 007 — Yuk Yuk

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/007-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Yuk Yuk`

A goblin guide who navigates the Silken Paths with ease. He is boastful and finds the party's struggle against the spiders entertaining, calling their escape "hyper cool" while describing the far flashier move he would have made. He claims knowledge of secret paths through the webs that the drow lack and styles himself one of "the best guides." He demands payment of twenty gold from Jimjar at the end of the Silken Paths and ultimately agrees to continue traveling with the party when Jimjar promises future payment.
