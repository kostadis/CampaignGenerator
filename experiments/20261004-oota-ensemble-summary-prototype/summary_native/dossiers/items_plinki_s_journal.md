### Chapter 054 — Plinki's Journal

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/054-session-04-28.md`
- Original heading: `Plinki's Journal`

A journal recovered from the Whorlstone Caverns, written by a figure involved with Demogorgon, detailing experiments in creating demonic monsters. Donated by the party to Candlekeep as one of their entry offerings. The GM identified Plinki as the figure from the Whorlstone Caverns who was writing about how "we're gonna go create a demonic monster."
