# Locations — Rumpadump's Cave

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 025 — Rumpadump's Cave

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/025-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Rumpadump's Cave`

A natural cave within the Whorlstone Caverns where several Myconids were found dancing frantically to a silent tune, accompanied by two large mushroom-covered apes. The site of the party's encounter with Voosbur and Rumpadump.
- The dancers were three five-foot Myconids and several half-sized ones, one of which stood apart.
- A corner nearby let Zalthir pull Sarith out of line of sight.
- A spore cloud rose among the fungal growths here, and the Myconids used it to depart.
