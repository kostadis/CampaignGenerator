### Chapter 028 — Gilded Sarcophagus

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/028-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Gilded Sarcophagus`

An ornate burial container resting atop a stone bier in the lower chamber of the tomb. It serves as a tactical hiding spot for the malevolent wraith and contains a mummified corpse, a hoard of treasure, and a mysterious magical sword. Opening it costs an action, which Zalthir spent. The mysterious voice speaks from within it.
