## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 013 — Menzoberranzan

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/013-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Menzoberranzan`

Mentioned by a drow patron as the capital of drow society, known for its dramatic and lethal political conspiracies. The drow contrasted these unfavorably with the blunt, artless violence of duergar politics.

### Chapter 053 — Menzoberranzan

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/053-session-out-of-the-dark-and-into-the-darkness.md`
- Original heading: `Menzoberranzan`

The principal city of the Drow and the location where the contract protecting Daz was funded. The unknown patron paid Nym and Kaelira there, placing the arrangement within the noble-house struggles of the city. The mercenaries did not know the patron's identity, while Daz could not recall useful details about House T'sarran despite his familiarity with Menzoberranzan.
