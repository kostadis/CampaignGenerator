# Abilities — Corrosive Embrace

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 062 — Corrosive Embrace

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/062-session-academic-vindication-and-an-assault.md`
- Original heading: `Corrosive Embrace`

A ki-powered ability—not a spell—delivered by Glabbagool, a monk sidekick drawing on his own ki pool while fused to Zalthir's arm. After Zalthir lands a blow and grapples a target, Glabbagool forces it to resist the embrace and then inflicts physical acid corrosion.

Because the acid is physical rather than magical, it bypasses the helmed horrors' Magic Resistance. Glabbagool uses Corrosive Embrace twice during the battle, dealing acid damage and melting portions of the constructs' plate armor so that their defenses are reduced.
