# Spells — Silvery Barbs

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 021 — Silvery Barbs

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/021-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Silvery Barbs`

Cast by Daz as a reaction during the fight with the Trapper. Daz's first attempt didn't apply, because the Trapper made no roll of its own to envelop Gyrgum (the save was Gyrgum's), and he noted the spell for later. It was then used on the Trapper's saving throw against Toll the Dead, forcing a re-roll with the lower result, though the creature succeeded anyway. It still granted Gyrgum advantage on his next attack, ability check, or saving throw to help him escape or fight back.
