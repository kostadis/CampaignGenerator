### Chapter 056 — Bronze Cast Forearm Monument

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/056-session-candlekeep-trials-truths-and-therapy.md`
- Original heading: `Bronze Cast Forearm Monument`

A bronze sculpture of a severed forearm and hand mounted on a stone pillar in the Daggerford village square, dedicated to Travis Houlin, a paladin of Bahamut who died there three years prior fighting the Cult of the Dragon, which had been actively recruiting in the town. It bears a Dwarven inscription reading "The move is yours" — the same phrase found beneath the Castle Ward statue associated with Stroud in Waterdeep — and a smaller Common inscription on the back noting it was witnessed and recorded by Milo Goodbarrel, an Order of the Gauntlet member whose work was published by Rishaal. The casting was funded by the Metalworkers Guild, possibly with Stroud-school sponsorship, though the local priest considers that incidental.
