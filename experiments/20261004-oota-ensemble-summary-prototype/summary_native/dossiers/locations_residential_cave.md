### Chapter 038 — Residential Cave

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/038-session-wererats-wisdom-and-the-war-on-ooze.md`
- Original heading: `Residential Cave`

A former residential area with homes dug into the walls, now filled with refuse and a foul stench, where the party found evidence of ooze activity and digested gnome corpses.

The abandoned homes stand at varying heights along the walls. A central refuse pile contains two slimy, partially digested deep gnomes, and an ooze trail leads from the chamber toward a trapped hallway. This is also where Glabbagool first becomes noticeably drawn toward the Pudding King’s call.
