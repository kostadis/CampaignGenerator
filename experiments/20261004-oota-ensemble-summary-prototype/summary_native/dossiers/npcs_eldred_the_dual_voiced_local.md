# NPCs — Eldred (The Dual-Voiced Local)

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 055 — Eldred (The Dual-Voiced Local)

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/055-session-the-ember-grapple-s-journey-through-the-north.md`
- Original heading: `Eldred (The Dual-Voiced Local)`

A man in the Mountain's Mouth inn in Triboar who speaks in two voices — both his own. The first is his current voice; the second a younger version of himself, answering questions the first voice never asks. A neighbor explains he ran courier routes from Triboar to Sundabar during the war, came back from his last run two summers past, and "was both of them." The locals feed him and do not ask questions. Gyrgum and Zalthir, both rolling above 14 on insight, recognize the broken, fractured rhythm of the second voice as the same quality of madness they witnessed throughout the Underdark — Eldred's voice in his own register, but not him. The madness of below is bleeding into the world above.
