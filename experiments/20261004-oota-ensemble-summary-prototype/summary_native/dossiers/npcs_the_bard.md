### Chapter 056 — The Bard

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/056-session-candlekeep-trials-truths-and-therapy.md`
- Original heading: `The Bard`

A traveling performer at the Burning Wizard Inn whose closing number this season is a four-verse drumming drinking song about a Kenku thief who learned to steal no more, with the whole inn singing along on the chorus of "Black Hood." After receiving a silver piece from Gyrgum, she asks Gyrgum whether he is a Bahamutian or a Stroudite, then sings a love song about Stroud and the famous chess game that winds through flowers, emotions, and drinking. Gyrgum observes it is "the usual claptrap."
