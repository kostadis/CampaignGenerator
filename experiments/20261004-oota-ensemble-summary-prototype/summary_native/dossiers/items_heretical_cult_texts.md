### Chapter 023 — Heretical Cult Texts

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/023-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Heretical Cult Texts`

A collection of dark volumes found by the party. Titles include 'The Spiraling Descent,' 'A Treatise of Duality and Madness,' 'The Prince's Gift on the Creation of a Favored Spawn,' 'Rituals of the Two-Headed Beast,' 'The Y-Marked Path to Damnation', 'Conquering Gracklstugh,' and 'A Guide to Planar Convergence.' These books detail the worship of Demogorgon and the rituals being performed by the cult; Narrak reads his chant from one of them. The party notes they could be used as evidence against the Council of Savants.
