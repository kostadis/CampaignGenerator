### Chapter 059 — Kei Tigersteel

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/059-session-questions-answers-and-a-key.md`
- Original heading: `Kei Tigersteel`

A slender human-elf of around fifty years old, notable for his bald scalp and sharp black side whiskers chiseled into a goatee. He serves as the Lorekeeper of Candlekeep and priest of the Temple of Oghma, considered the library's spiritual leader. Bookwyrm dismissed him from the debrief and directed him to take Janussi's body to the Shrine of Oghma for purification.
