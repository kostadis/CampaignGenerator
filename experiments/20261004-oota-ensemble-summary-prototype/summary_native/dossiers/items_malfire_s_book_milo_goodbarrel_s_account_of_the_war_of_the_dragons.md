# Items — Malfire's Book (Milo Goodbarrel's Account of the War of the Dragons)

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 054 — Malfire's Book (Milo Goodbarrel's Account of the War of the Dragons)

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/054-session-04-28.md`
- Original heading: `Malfire's Book (Milo Goodbarrel's Account of the War of the Dragons)`

A multi-volume historical text containing detailed accounts of past events in the Forgotten Realms, frequently cited by Daz during the party's travels — to the point where the GM notes that Zalthir has perfected his Shadow Step specifically as an escape mechanism whenever Daz begins citing it. Daz privately muses about writing a counter-account designed to obscure certain truths and keep the real story larger than what is publicly known.
