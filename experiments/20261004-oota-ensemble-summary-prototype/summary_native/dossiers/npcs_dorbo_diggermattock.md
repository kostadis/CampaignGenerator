### Chapter 036 — Chief Dorbo Diggermattock

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/036-session-not-an-ooze-just-a-cube.md`
- Original heading: `Chief Dorbo Diggermattock`

A male Svirfneblin leader of Clan Diggermattock and one of the two authorities presiding over Diggermattock Hall. He sits behind a stone desk on a rock-carved dais, conferring with advisors and Quartermaster Senni Diggermattock.

He introduces himself plainly: “My name is Dorbo. And over here is my assistant, Senni. And we are in charge here. Who are you?” His clan considers intelligence about the Drow essential because the Diggermattocks took this part of Blingdenstone from them. After learning that the visitors are adventurers seeking the Overbright, Dorbo engages Senni in an animated private discussion involving muttering and finger-pointing.

### Chapter 037 — Chief Dorbo Diggermattock

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/037-session-slime-spirits-and-svirfneblin-squabbles.md`
- Original heading: `Chief Dorbo Diggermattock`

The leader of Blingdenstone's mining coalition, focused on mining interests and the expansion of housing needed by the returning community. He presents the ooze infestation as a practical obstacle to both goals and volunteers the party to solve it before he will help with their own concerns. Dorbo is resistant to working with the Goldwhisker Clan due to suspicions that they are wererats and because his faction now dominates territory the Goldwhiskers previously occupied. He favors employing gnomes and views the mining consortium's interests as predominant.

### Chapter 038 — Chief Dorbo Diggermattock

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/038-session-wererats-wisdom-and-the-war-on-ooze.md`
- Original heading: `Chief Dorbo Diggermattock`

The chief of Blingdenstone who hired the adventurers to solve the ooze problem. He is criticized by citizens for being obsessed with wealth and not helping the common man. He is initially dismissive and resistant to the party's plan, calling it the dumbest plan he has ever heard, but ultimately concedes to the party's leadership under pressure from Senni and Daz.

Dorbo’s officials present him as responsible for every success, while at least one citizen accuses him of sacrificing everything “on the altar of wealth.” Faced with the Pudding King’s army, he swings rapidly from demanding results to declaring the discovery catastrophic. He raises legitimate concerns that Blingdenstone lacks the preparation, equipment, and allies for a war against hundreds of oozes, but his political hostility toward Clan Goldwhisker complicates the response. He wants the Goldwhiskers to recognize his authority before he accepts their aid, a demand Daz overrides by placing the operation under the party’s command.

### Chapter 039 — Chief Dorbo Diggermattock

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Chief Dorbo Diggermattock`

Head of Blingdenstone's financial interests and aligned with the mining guild. He prioritizes profit but recognizes that quality of life and survival cannot be ignored indefinitely. After Daz seized control of the planning process, Dorbo backed away from direct ownership of the risky operation.

Daz recognized that Dorbo had found a convenient fall guy. If the scheme failed, Dorbo could blame the drow who had forced the plan through; if it succeeded, he could claim that supporting Daz had been his own strategy all along. Dorbo's obsession with money appears more unhinged than the circumstances warrant, possibly because the Pudding King's demonic influence is amplifying greed and aggression throughout Blingdenstone.

Dorbo proposes the Temple of the Steadfast Stone as a staging point that would draw the ooze army away from inhabited areas. When the party discovers that this proposal also serves the gnomes' desire to have adventurers cleanse the Rockblight, he immediately praises their alternative northern route. His committee later offers the party a Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals to make the cleansing mission more attractive.

### Chapter 048 — Chief Dorbo Diggermattock
