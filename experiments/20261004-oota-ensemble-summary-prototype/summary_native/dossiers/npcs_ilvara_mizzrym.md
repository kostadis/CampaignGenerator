# NPCs — Ilvara Mizzrym

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 002 — Ilvara

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Ilvara`

Ilvara Mizzrym is the cruel drow high priestess and commander of Velkynvelve. She follows Lolth and presides over the outpost from private quarters beneath a lavish spider shrine. She treats the prisoners with contempt and threatened Prince Derendil with a knife when he demanded dignity. Thorin found her smug and comfortable in Shoor Vandree's company and sensed the same disturbing instability he had begun noticing in the other drow. The party believes Ilvara's treasure chest probably holds their confiscated possessions because drow custom concentrates captured wealth in the hands of the highest-ranking leader.

### Chapter 032 — Ilvara

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/032-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Ilvara`

A drow priestess and relentless pursuer. The party discusses her as an ongoing threat, noting that she is driven by obligation to her house, the collapse of her command, the humiliation of the party’s escape, and the madness spreading through the Underdark.

The party speculates about convincing her that they died at the Circle of Welcome or luring her forces toward Neverlight Grove and Zuggtmoy’s wedding.

### Chapter 049 — Ilvara

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/049-session-a-sign-from-lolth-or-just-a-spider.md`
- Original heading: `Ilvara`

A drow priestess who has been relentlessly hunting the party across the Underdark since their escape from her prison. She had made promises to two powerful people in Menzoberranzan that the party's escape ruined. According to Valen, she found a mushroom in the Neverlight Grove, communes with a giant pulsing fungal mass she believes is Lolth, and is now fanatically devoted to an entity called "the bride," who demands the party's death. She commands a massive heart fungus creature and can track the party through the Underdark's pervasive fungi. She discarded her lover Jorlan after he was scarred.

### Chapter 050 — Ilvara

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/050-session-fungus-among-us.md`
- Original heading: `Ilvara`

A former high priestess of Lolth who has been corrupted by Zuggtmoy's influence after visiting the Neverlight Grove. She now appears visibly fungal and still carries her cat-o'-nine-tails scourge. She carries a small magical mushroom artifact that serves as a conduit for Zuggtmoy's control, kept on the ground beside her.

She possesses the ability to meld into fungal patches and reappear elsewhere, and can command the heart fungus and summon reanimated spore servants. She uses legendary actions and has a high Dexterity. She took 31 damage from Zalthir and 12 from Daz's fireball this session.

### Chapter 051 — Ilvara

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/051-session-a-spore-filled-finale.md`
- Original heading: `Ilvara`

The primary antagonist and a high-ranking drow priestess, now partially corrupted with myconid-like hybrid parts. She remains mechanically a humanoid despite the fungal corruption, preventing effects keyed specifically to fungal creatures from harming only her hybrid growths.

She is a formidable opponent who uses legendary actions to teleport, command drones, create fungal blooms, and magically frighten her enemies. After Zalthir dropped her forty feet, she frightened him so that he could no longer approach her, then commanded a drone to attack and moved toward cover. She also reshaped the terrain to prevent Jorlan from reaching her.

Ilvara wields powerful clerical magic including Insect Plague and Flamestrike. Her Insect Plague killed her own lesser minions and badly injured Glabbagool, while also creating an obstacle that disrupted the arrival of House T'sarran. Her Flamestrike targeted Daz and Nym but failed to incapacitate either of them.

She invoked Zuggtmoy and spoke of the bride — Zuggtmoy herself — bringing chaos and mayhem. Obsessed with vengeance, Ilvara continued howling threats even as the battlefield collapsed around her. She is particularly vulnerable to radiant damage, which ultimately leads to her destruction — Gyrgum's Guiding Bolt causes her to explode into a twenty-foot cloud of poisonous spores.

### Chapter 053 — Ilvara

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/053-session-out-of-the-dark-and-into-the-darkness.md`
- Original heading: `Ilvara`

The defeated leader of the House Mizzrym force that pursued the party, whose belongings were examined during this session. She had continued receiving instructions from her house while Zuggtmoy's influence took hold, then disappeared and stopped responding. Her prayer book preserves a visible timeline of that corruption through Zuggtmoy marginalia. She had been carrying Velkynvelve's payroll, confiscated prisoner belongings, House Mizzrym's increasingly frantic orders, a letter from Neverlight Grove, and a corrupted five-tailed scourge with living serpent heads. The fused ceremonial objects on her platform provide physical evidence that she had combined the worship of Lolth and Zuggtmoy.
