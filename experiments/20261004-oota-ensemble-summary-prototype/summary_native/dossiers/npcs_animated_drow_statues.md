# NPCs — Animated Drow Statues

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 041 — Animated Drow Statues

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/041-session-mirror-mirror-on-the-wall-who-s-the-maddest-of-them-all.md`
- Original heading: `Animated Drow Statues`

Stone statues of Drow warriors that animate and attack when the party enters their chamber. They are not particularly intelligent, primarily charging and attacking. They are animated by a malevolent entity and appear in waves of six, with more animating as others are defeated. The entity can only animate six at a time.
- Each has 33 hit points and AC 18.
- They appear to be Drow petrified by the Medusa, which is why their animation seemed wrong to the party.
- They stop moving when the party leaves the room and reanimate the moment anyone steps back in.
