### Chapter 038 — Find Familiar

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/038-session-wererats-wisdom-and-the-war-on-ooze.md`
- Original heading: `Find Familiar`

A spell cast by Daz to summon a bat familiar named Batman. The familiar can be sent to scout dangerous areas and telepathically communicate with Daz within one hundred feet, allowing the party to gather information without risking themselves.

Batman’s echolocation makes him well suited to the dark cave system. Daz uses him to conduct a systematic search of multiple branches, locate the Pudding King, survey the slime-filled throne room, and confirm that the projected voice belongs to the gnome on the throne. The reconnaissance prevents the party from walking blindly into an overwhelming encounter.

### Chapter 043 — Find Familiar

- Original heading: `Find Familiar`

A spell cast by Daz to summon a bat familiar for one hour. The bat is used to scout ahead into the Festering Fissure. The familiar is described as a spirit that takes animal form with all the attributes of that animal, including breathing, though breathing is not what keeps it alive. The bat has echolocation and passed its Constitution save against the fissure's spores with a natural 20.

### Chapter 049 — Find Familiar

Cast twice by Daz during the session. The first spider was left behind so his telepathic link could reveal who was following the party through the tunnels.
