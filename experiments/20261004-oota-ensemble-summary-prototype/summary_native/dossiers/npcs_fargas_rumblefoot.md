# NPCs — Fargas Rumblefoot

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 006 — Fargas Rumblefoot

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/006-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Fargas Rumblefoot`

A halfling adventurer found cocooned in the Silken Paths, stored as a snack by giant spiders. He claimed to have been fleeing mad gnolls and bargained for his rescue by promising to lead the party to a lost tomb filled with treasure. His adventuring band had been seeking that tomb, and he insists he was never infected by the gnolls. Thorin freed him.

### Chapter 007 — Fargas Rumblefoot

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/007-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Fargas Rumblefoot`

A halfling adventurer who "made a bunch of poor life choices," which left him trapped in a spider cocoon until the party rescued him. He knows about the Lost Tomb of Khaem and its hidden treasure vault, and claims his team found evidence of the secret passage from the servant's tomb. He admits he does not know the tomb's precise location, only that it lies on the northeastern edge of the Darklake. Gyrgum's Insight read him as desperate rather than boastful, but his earlier inflated claims make the party cautious. Daz examined him and found him free of gnoll spawn; Fargas was offended by the prodding and told Daz to keep away.

### Chapter 028 — Fargas Rumblefoot

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/028-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Fargas Rumblefoot`

A halfling adventurer, encountered previously, who provided the party with the crucial tip that the Lost Tomb of Khaem contains a secret or false tomb, information that other adventurers who perished here apparently lacked. He didn't know where the secret room was. As the last survivor of his own adventuring group, his explanation of why others never returned is open to doubt.
