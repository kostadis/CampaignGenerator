### Chapter 058 — Daral Yashenti

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/058-session-the-case-of-the-missing-tickles.md`
- Original heading: `Daral Yashenti`

A rival scholar and Great Reader with a long-standing animosity toward Janussi. He visited Janussi twice on Janussi's final day — once while hungover around the eleventh bell in the morning and once while heavily drunk around 10:30 p.m., the final argument before he fled down the stairs — shouting that Janussi was unfit for his position. He was seen by the cat fleeing the chamber with a book matching *The Golden Ass*. He also previously insulted the party by calling them "sell swords." The gift tag’s tone strongly implicates him.

### Chapter 059 — Daral Yashenti

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/059-session-questions-answers-and-a-key.md`
- Original heading: `Daral Yashenti`

An arrogant and frequently intoxicated Great Reader who held deep resentment for Janussi's leadership, particularly his plans to sell priceless books. He admitted freely to giving Janussi *The Golden Ass* as a Deadwinter gift, returning to reclaim it that evening, and striking Janussi during the altercation: "In defending myself, I may have caught him lightly on the nose." He was found at the Hearth in conspicuously good spirits — a mood explained by the drow party member as likely relief at not facing Underdark-style consequences for attacking his superior. He was actively reading the suspected murder weapon and licking his fingers to turn pages when the party arrived. When warned of the poison, he dropped the book in panic. He named multiple suspects while rattled: Teles Ahvoste, A'lai Aivenmore, Fheminor Scrivenbark, and Bookwyrm herself. His racial remarks about tieflings and lizardfolk drew pushback from the party. He is both a person of interest and a potential victim; suffering the poison's midnight effects would strongly argue against his being the poisoner.

### Chapter 060 — Daral

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/060-session-never-mind-the-murders.md`
- Original heading: `Daral`

A paranoid, dramatic, and complicated scholar who is terrified after realizing that Janussi died from a poisoned book taken from Daral’s private collection. He had expected Janussi to dismiss him, briefly celebrated when the murder saved his position and library, then realized the gift made him the obvious suspect and perhaps the killer’s next target. He clings to Thorin and Daz as the only people he trusts, though their willingness to consider letting the poison progress unsettles him. He directed the party to Leuwin, identified A’lai as the person most likely to frame him, and remembered the anti-divination properties of Milil’s chalice.

### Chapter 061 — Daral

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/061-session-the-colloquium-was-well-received.md`
- Original heading: `Daral`

A poet and scholar who reclaimed the book he had given Janussi after deciding the gift was insufficiently appreciated. While handling it, he licked his finger and may have exposed himself to Midnight Tears. Convinced he will die at midnight, he desperately flatters Thorin, Dawnbringer, and Gyrgum in hopes of receiving Lesser Restoration. Sylvira considers him a bitter drunk, forced to sell some of his poetry to help fund a new Great Wheel library, but ultimately doubts he possesses the organization required for the larger frame-up.

### Chapter 062 — Daral

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/062-session-academic-vindication-and-an-assault.md`
- Original heading: `Daral`

A man the party suspected had been poisoned after licking his finger while handling the poison-laced book. By this point, the party no longer believes he played any role in the murder or has useful information left to reveal.

Dawnbringer cures him in Deneir's Sanctum. In gratitude, Daral promises to make sentient magical swords an active area of research and begins an extended scholarly discourse about Dawnbringer's sentience, beauty, themes, and semiotics. Dawnbringer enthusiastically welcomes the attention. Daral remains trapped in the locked sanctum with the party when the helmed horrors attack and reacts with disbelief that the constructs could be there.

### Chapter 063 — Daral

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `Daral`

An Avowed scholar who was cured of a lingering affliction by Dawnbringer and launched into an enthusiastic monologue about making sentient magical swords an area of serious academic study. His discussion included the semiotics of sentient weapons and delighted Dawnbringer.

After the helmed-horror attack, Daral initially tried to flee, then realized that the party members were the only people who did not seem to want him dead. He remained close to Thorin and Dawnbringer for protection.

Daral used his trusty scroll of Detect Magic to prove that Kalan's supposed high tower key was mundane, declaring, “This is not the key. This is not the key.” He also explained the relationship between the high tower, Candlekeep's anti-teleportation ward, and the magical suppression of ordinary fire.
