# Spells — Fear

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 023 — Fear

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/023-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Fear`

A powerful spell unleashed by Narrak (DC 14 Wisdom save) that sent a wave of psychic terror through the party. It forced Thorin, Gyrgum, and Zalthir to dash away until their line of sight with Narrak was broken, at which point they could re-roll the save. It ended when Daz's Earthen Grasp broke Narrak's concentration.
