### Chapter 029 — Dawnbringer

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/029-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Dawnbringer`

A sentient, talking magic sword found within Brysis's sarcophagus. She can speak, read, and understand Common, and manifests a blade of pure radiance. She has a strong personality, expressing disdain for being left with a monk rather than a fighter, and a deep, persistent fear of the dark that causes her to refuse to be deactivated. She announced herself with "Dawnbringer here! Let me show you what I can do!" She met Gyrgum's surprise at her literacy with an exasperated "seriously?"

### Chapter 041 — Dawnbringer

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/041-session-mirror-mirror-on-the-wall-who-s-the-maddest-of-them-all.md`
- Original heading: `Dawnbringer`

An intelligent magical sword wielded by Thorin that emits bright light. She has a distinct personality and dislikes darkness, often engaging in conversations with Thorin. She desires a nicer scabbard and can impose her will on her wielder through charisma saving throws, though she can be persuaded with promises of better treatment.
- She prefers "a perfectly fine gargoyle target that could be killed in the bright light" over fighting in darkness.
- She proposed the "dart in and out" compromise, and Thorin swung her into the darkness and back out.
- She is "very pleased" when Thorin fights in the light, and after the scabbard deal she said "I can live with this."
- She began describing scabbard design proposals that suggest "a bigger budget than you anticipated."

### Chapter 045 — Dawnbringer

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/045-session-gnome-more-elemental-slavery.md`
- Original heading: `Dawnbringer`

A sentient, glowing sword wielded by Thorin that appears like a flaming lightsaber. The sword is famous and impresses locals, particularly children, and is "totally digging" the attention. Dawnbringer expresses her own desires and preferences, initially wanting a fancy, over-the-top scabbard but ultimately being pleased to receive the Vaultmaster locking scabbard, while muttering about adding bling. The sword would also disapprove of Thorin buying a two-handed maul. She is described as kind and feminine in nature. At this session the table treated her as genderless; the party later settled on she.

### Chapter 053 — Dawnbringer

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/053-session-out-of-the-dark-and-into-the-darkness.md`
- Original heading: `Dawnbringer`

A sentient sunblade carried by Thorin. Dawnbringer considers herself a high-quality weapon designed to kill things and objects loudly to being treated as a ritual implement. She reluctantly assisted in purifying Ilvara's corrupted scourge after being reminded of her prestigious Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard. She is enthusiastic about becoming more deadly, insists she merely dislikes rather than fears darkness, and rejects the suggestion that she needs therapy despite Thorin's interest in finding a specialist at Candlekeep.
