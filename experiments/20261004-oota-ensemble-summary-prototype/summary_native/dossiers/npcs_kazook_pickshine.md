# NPCs — Kazook Pickshine

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 039 — Kazook Pickshine

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Kazook Pickshine`

A representative of the Miners' Guild who is concerned about acidic damage to the gnome army's weapons and armor.

Kazook knows of a potion or protective treatment that can prevent acid corrosion, but he needs a large quantity of ingredients from Neverlight Grove to produce it. As an alternative, he proposes shipping salt to Gracklstugh in exchange for high-quality weapons.

The party chooses the Neverlight Grove option because Gracklstugh's present condition is uncertain and their last departure from the city was not amicable.

### Chapter 043 — Kazook Pickshine

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/043-session-harvesting-horror-in-the-underdark.md`
- Original heading: `Kazook Pickshine`

The local alchemist who recalled a legend about a 'garden of stone' and sent his scout Bimble to investigate. He is secretive about his spell gem creation process and protective of his friend Bimble, asking the party for compassion regarding Bimble's infected state. He gives off a snake-oil salesman vibe and physically blocks the party's view of his spell gem work. He had believed he had a simpler solution to the acid problem than a trip to Neverlight Grove.
