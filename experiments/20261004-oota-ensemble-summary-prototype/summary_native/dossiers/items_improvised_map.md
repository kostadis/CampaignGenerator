### Chapter 007 — Improvised Map

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/007-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Improvised Map`

A crude geographical layout drawn by Sarith using a stick and the green paint from the mushroom-moss they had gathered. It shows the interconnected tunnels of the Darklake and the surrounding settlements, including Sloobludop, Gracklstugh, and Velkynvelve, as well as the estimated location of the Lost Tomb of Khaem. It also traces the party's route from Velkynvelve toward Sloobludop, with the party roughly three days out. The DM stressed that directions are relative and nothing is to scale.
