# NPCs — Deepking Tarngardt

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 015 — Deepking

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/015-session-04-14.md`
- Original heading: `Deepking`

The ruler of Gracklstugh who has become increasingly paranoid and erratic, distancing himself from his old allies in favor of a mysterious new advisor named Shal.
- He "used to be much calmer."
- He is "always in a rush."
- His crackdown has driven factions literally underground.
- He has asked Hgraam about the Gray Ghosts.

### Chapter 018 — The Deepking

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/018-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Deepking`

The Duergar ruler of Gracklstugh, described as an increasingly paranoid figure (the party speculates about an evil vizier).
- He has granted all weapons contracts to his own clan, Clan Steelshadow, creating widespread resentment.
- According to Werz, people who compete with his trade have been disappearing.

### Chapter 021 — Deepking

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/021-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Deepking`

The current mad ruler of the Duergar city, secretly supporting an illegal Assassin's Guild. His city keeps part of its population enslaved and captures slaves. He is also withholding materials the gnome kingdoms want. The party possesses documentation proving his link to the guild but chooses to keep it secret.
