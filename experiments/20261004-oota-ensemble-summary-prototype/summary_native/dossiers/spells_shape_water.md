### Chapter 006 — Shape Water

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/006-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Shape Water`

Used by Daz to freeze collected water into portable ice blocks, allowing the party to transport their water supply without containers. The ice had to be refrozen every hour as the cantrip's effect lasted only that long, and it would melt overnight. Daz noted that the spell can freeze water only as long as no creatures are in it. It cannot create water; he does not have Create Water.

### Chapter 034 — Shape Water

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/034-session-the-wet-bet-and-the-wondrous-cube.md`
- Original heading: `Shape Water`

A cantrip used by Daz to manipulate water within a five-foot cube. Daz determines that its directional effect can last for an hour and that two non-instantaneous effects can be maintained simultaneously.

Rather than merely freezing the water, Daz redirects it from the center of each cube toward its edges, creating pockets containing no water. By maintaining two adjacent pockets, the party can occupy one while forming the next, then progress through the flooded area in five-foot stages.

The improvised technique creates a temporary refuge from the inrushing water while Glabbagool opens the damaged passage. The party jokingly compares Daz to Moses. Glabbagool cannot fit inside the pockets but does not need them because he does not breathe.

### Chapter 040 — Shape Water

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/040-session-crystals-ghosts-and-crazed-earth.md`
- Original heading: `Shape Water`

A cantrip discussed as a way to direct or spray alcohol against hostile oozes. It can alter the flow of water, move it, form it into simple animated shapes, or change its color for up to an hour.

The party debated whether alcohol or impure water qualified as “water,” noting that naturally occurring water is never perfectly distilled. They also raised—and immediately rejected—the game-breaking possibility of manipulating the water inside a living creature, with Mystra invoked as the ultimate reason such abuse would not work.

### Chapter 041 — Shape Water

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/041-session-mirror-mirror-on-the-wall-who-s-the-maddest-of-them-all.md`

A cantrip used by Daz to create an improvised ice mirror by freezing and shaping water into a highly reflective surface. The spell can also adjust the opacity and color of water, and was used in combination with gems and Dawnbringer's light to counter the Medusa's petrifying gaze.
