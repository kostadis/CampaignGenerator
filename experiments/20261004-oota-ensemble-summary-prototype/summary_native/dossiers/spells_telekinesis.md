# Spells — Telekinesis

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 064 — Telekinesis

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/064-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Telekinesis`

Cast by Daz when A'lai Aivenmore tries to smash the sapphire. Because A'lai is carrying the gem, he must succeed on a strength saving throw to retain it. He fails against Daz's spell save DC of seventeen, allowing Daz to wrench the artifact from his hand and pull it into his own possession.

Daz considers using the spell on A'lai himself but applies it only to the object, so the wizard is not restrained. The successful theft prevents A'lai from triggering the sapphire and enables Daz's concealed transfer of it to Gyrgum.

### Chapter 065 — Telekinesis

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/065-session-08-03.md`
- Original heading: `Telekinesis`

*Cast in the previous session (ch64).* The 5th-level *telekinesis* spell (Strength save, DC 17); the party had reached 9th level (GM ruling, ch64).

Daz used it to wrench the stolen sapphire directly from A'lai Aivenmore’s grasp before the wizard could smash it. He then used practiced sleight of hand to pass the artifact secretly to Gyrgum, causing the assassin to attack and search the wrong person this session.
