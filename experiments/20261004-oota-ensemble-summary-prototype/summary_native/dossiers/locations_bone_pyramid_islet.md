# Locations — Bone Pyramid Islet

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 011 — Bone Pyramid Islet

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/011-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Bone Pyramid Islet`

A diamond-shaped, 40-foot-wide limestone islet in the Darklake where the tunnel forks left and right. It features a macabre eight-foot-high, five-foot-wide pyramid of bones topped with a horned ram skull that gazes down the tunnel. It was once a shortcut used by Brannum's crew, and a survivor spoke of an "evil altar" there. Sunken boats and zombie-infested waters lie beneath it. Find Traps revealed no traps, and the party never set foot on it.
