### Chapter 055 — Silver Marches

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/055-session-the-ember-grapple-s-journey-through-the-north.md`
- Original heading: `Silver Marches`

A region on the surface where the party emerges from the Underdark near Blingdenstone's exit during mid-spring. The area has been devastated by Uthgardt barbarian raids following the War of the Giants. *Canon timeline:* the War of the Dragons (~1490 DR — the conflict Milo Goodbarrel chronicles, fought by Stroud, Protanther, Malfire, and Whistler) ended first; the War of the Giants (1491 DR, restoring the Ordning under King Hekaton) followed; the Uthgardt raids that scarred this region were a knock-on consequence of the giant war's disruption. Burned hamlets, displaced refugees, a broken standing stone, and a defiled Tempus shrine line the road. An unusual quiet — fewer bears this year, pixies not singing in the sown lands — adds an eerie atmosphere that a local explicitly attributes to something beyond the war: "My grandmother says weather. I'm not certain."
