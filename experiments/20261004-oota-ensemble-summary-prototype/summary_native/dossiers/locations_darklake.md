# Locations — Darklake

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 004 — Darklake

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/004-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darklake`

A massive network of underground rivers, natural tunnels, canals, and water-filled caverns stretching over a hundred miles. Some of its cavern ceilings rise miles overhead, while its depths are described as unfathomable. It serves as a major travel route through the Underdark and could provide access toward Sloobludop, Gracklstugh, Blingdenstone, and other destinations—provided the party can obtain a boat.

### Chapter 008 — Darklake

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/008-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darklake`

A vast underground body of water with no waves, whose shallows the Kuo-toans use for religious processions and rituals. During the session, sudden pools of blood and churning water, some where Kuo-toa had been swimming, revealed that something monstrous lurked beneath the surface.

### Chapter 009 — Darklake

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/009-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darklake`

A vast, gloomy subterranean body of water in the Underdark, serving as the party's primary escape route from Sloobludop. Zalthir considers it the safest and fastest route through the Underdark. Characterized by low ceilings (one drops to three feet above the surface), submerged stone "teeth," dangerous aquatic predators, and desperate refugees navigating its treacherous waters. Its water is drinkable once clear of the blood.

### Chapter 010 — Darklake

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/010-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darklake`

A vast, dark subterranean waterway in the Underdark where the party is navigating by their kuo-toa boat and the captured keelboat. The area features treacherous submerged sandbars, rocky outcroppings threaded with narrow navigable passages, and shallow sections where combatants can wade. Wading through these sections counts as difficult terrain, limiting Thorin to 15 feet of movement. The party was ambushed here by duergar slavers.

### Chapter 011 — Darklake

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/011-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darklake`

A vast, eerie subterranean network of waterways in the Underdark. The party navigates a narrow tunnel characterized by dripping water, blind cavefish, an eerie purple luminescence, and hidden dangers including a beholder zombie, aquatic zombies, and a hungry aquatic troll.

### Chapter 027 — Darklake

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/027-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darklake`

A vast, dark subterranean body of water within the Underdark that the party navigated for four days. The DM described it as a three-dimensional "Swiss cheese" of passages. It is home to dangerous flying predators like stirges lurking in the cavern ceiling above.

### Chapter 035 — Darklake

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/035-session-prejudice-promises-and-the-prince-of-puddles.md`
- Original heading: `Darklake`

A cold, dark body of water in the Underdark. The party reaches its shores drenched, chilled, and miserable after leaving their boat. With no pursuers immediately behind them, they safely complete a long rest there before beginning the final day’s journey to Blingdenstone.
