### Chapter 042 — Ruby Spell Gem

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/042-session-zalthir-s-reflection-thorin-s-stand.md`
- Original heading: `Ruby Spell Gem`

A magical gem given to the party by Gurnik Tapfinger, meant to be placed into a socket of the menhir in the temple to hallow the area against Ogrémoch's influence. When placed, it begins to glow and triggers the appearance of Earth Elementals. The GM described it as "a magical spell that will allow this stone that if you place inside of the head of the statue, it will automatically hallow it and nothing bad will happen."
