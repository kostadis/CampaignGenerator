### Chapter 069 — The Obsidian Door

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/069-session-one-manshoon-dead-at-least-one-or-two-more-to-go.md`
- Original heading: `The Obsidian Door`

A door of black stone with no handle, keyhole, or visible seam, located within the upper tower chamber. Eleven scorch marks form a rough circle at chest height, showing where someone—apparently Manshoon—repeatedly tried and failed to force it open.

It is protected by a riddle associated with the legendary seer Alaundo. Earlier decoded instructions state that those well-versed in Candlekeep's lore may fearlessly pass it. The answer concerns what is consumed to make light and must be spoken in darkness by the verified reader, as a gift rather than a demand, only once.
