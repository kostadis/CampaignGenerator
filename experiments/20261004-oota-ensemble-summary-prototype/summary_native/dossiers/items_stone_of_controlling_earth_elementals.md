# Items — Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 039 — Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals`

A magic stone offered by Chief Dorbo's committee as a reward for defeating Ogrémoch's Bane and cleansing the Temple of the Steadfast Stone.

The stone can summon an Earth Elemental that remains friendly to the user and their companions for a limited duration, approximately an hour, in a manner compared to the Conjure Elemental spell.

The party dislikes the implication of enslaving the elemental and prefers to treat it as a potential companion. They discuss freeing it, giving it control of its own stone, befriending it during repeated summons, or arranging for the same individual elemental to answer each call. They hope the relationship might eventually resemble Glabbagool's voluntary companionship.

### Chapter 040 — Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/040-session-crystals-ghosts-and-crazed-earth.md`
- Original heading: `Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals`

A magical stone offered as a reward for cleansing the Temple of the Steadfast Stone and dealing with Ogrémoch’s Bane. It would allow its wielder to summon an earth elemental as an ally. The party has not yet received it, and finding Entémoch’s Boon remains a separate objective.

The item would be particularly valuable against the Pudding King because earth elementals are highly resistant or invulnerable to oozes and puddings.
