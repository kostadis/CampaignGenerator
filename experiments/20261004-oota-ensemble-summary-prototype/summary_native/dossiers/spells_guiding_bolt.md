### Chapter 042 — Guiding Bolt

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/042-session-zalthir-s-reflection-thorin-s-stand.md`
- Original heading: `Guiding Bolt`

A ranged spell (120-foot range) that deals radiant damage and grants advantage to the next attack against the target. Gyrgum used this spell repeatedly throughout the session and scored multiple critical hits. One dealt 36 points of radiant damage to destroy the first Earth Elemental, and another dealt 22 points to the Medusa. Its range let Gyrgum stay outside the Medusa's 30-foot gaze. Gyrgum called it "my new favorite spell" and "an awesome spell."

### Chapter 051 — Guiding Bolt

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/051-session-a-spore-filled-finale.md`
- Original heading: `Guiding Bolt`

A flash of radiant light cast by Gyrgum. His first attempt against Ilvara missed.

Later, after checking whether Daz and Zalthir required immediate healing, Gyrgum cast Guiding Bolt again at third level. The second attack struck Ilvara directly and rolled substantial radiant damage. Her special vulnerability amplified the spell enough to kill her outright, causing her body to erupt into a twenty-foot cloud of poisonous spores.
