# Items — Corrupted Dragon Egg

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 019 — Corrupted Dragon Egg

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/019-session-05-20.md`
- Original heading: `Corrupted Dragon Egg`

A large red dragon egg stolen from the Keepers of the Flame. It sits atop one of the rocky mesa's topmost tiers in the obelisk chamber and is covered in dark incantations, being infused with demonic energy by Plinki to birth a two-headed servant of Demogorgon. Daz's natural-20 Arcana check showed it will hatch two-headed and has probably been possessed by some kind of demonic power. The DM notes red dragons are "notorious for being difficult young dragons" and "this one's going to be particularly difficult."
