# NPCs — Skiit

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 022 — Skiit

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/022-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Skiit`

A low-ranking, insane derro cultist of Demogorgon. He has:
- blue-gray skin
- pale eyes lacking irises and pupils
- greasy white hair
- grime-stained scraps of tattered leather and cloth

His job was tending the zombie pit and tossing body parts to the undead below, and he complained constantly about getting "the low end of the stick." Gyrgum's supervisor ruse fooled him at first; he answered to Plinki and asked to feed the party's goblin to the zombies. He turned hostile after Gyrgum's natural one. Gyrgum shoved him off the ledge, Daz crushed him with Maximilian's Earthen Grasp, and Toll the Dead killed him as he fled. Ulnara plans to raise him as a zombie.
