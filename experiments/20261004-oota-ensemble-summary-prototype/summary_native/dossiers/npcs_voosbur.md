## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 025 — Voosbur

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/025-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Voosbur`

A tall Myconid who released the strange spores that infected his kin and caused them to teleport to the Whorlstone Caverns. Fully embracing Zuggtmoy's influence, he speaks in a euphoric, psychedelic manner and invites others to join the 'Lady's dream' before departing through the spore network.
- He insists "this is no infection" but a gift from "the mama of the mushrooms, the queen of the dreamscape."
- Parting words to Thorin: "Hey, man, I guess you didn't want to join the party. I got to go now."
