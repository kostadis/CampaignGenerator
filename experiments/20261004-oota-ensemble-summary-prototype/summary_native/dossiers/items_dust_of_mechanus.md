# Items — Dust of Mechanus

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 067 — Dust of Mechanus

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/067-session-08-17.md`
- Original heading: `Dust of Mechanus`

A legendary and perfect lubricant sourced from the plane of Mechanus, required by the vault's riddle to be sprinkled on dormant gears. One theoretical source would be the remains of a slain modron, but Spanner considers that absolutely unacceptable. The party never obtains any: Spanner lends them a modron-made tool instead, and the GM rules the tool an alternative to the dust.
