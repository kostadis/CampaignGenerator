### Chapter 032 — Magical Scrolls

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/032-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Magical Scrolls`

A collection of scrolls found in the equipment piled near Xinaya’s burial place. They briefly become the center of a conflict when Zalthir, afflicted by spore-induced madness, hoards them and refuses to share.

Zalthir insists, “These are mine. I found them,” and later, “I’ll work it out later. These are mine. Don’t touch them.” After being cured, he apologizes and admits that possessing the scrolls had felt inexplicably critical.
