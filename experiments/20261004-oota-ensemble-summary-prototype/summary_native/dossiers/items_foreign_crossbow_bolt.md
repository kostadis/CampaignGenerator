## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 064 — Foreign Crossbow Bolt

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/064-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Foreign Crossbow Bolt`

A crossbow bolt found in the body of a dead Watcher on an indoor walkway. Daz identifies its fletching as foreign to the region and inconsistent with the bolts used by Candlekeep's Watchers, confirming that the attackers came from outside the keep. Its angle establishes the route the intruders took, and the party later finds a bow-carrying raider among the looters in the High Tower lobby.
