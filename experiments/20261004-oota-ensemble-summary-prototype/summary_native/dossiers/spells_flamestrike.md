# Spells — Flamestrike

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 051 — Flamestrike

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/051-session-a-spore-filled-finale.md`
- Original heading: `Flamestrike`

A clerical spell used by Ilvara to call down a vertical column of divine fire upon Daz and Nym Duskryn. It required Dexterity saving throws rather than attack rolls, so Daz's Shield spell offered no protection.

Daz rolled a twenty on his save, and both targets suffered thirteen damage. The result was sufficiently unimpressive that the DM described Flamestrike as “the lame alternative” available to clerics.
