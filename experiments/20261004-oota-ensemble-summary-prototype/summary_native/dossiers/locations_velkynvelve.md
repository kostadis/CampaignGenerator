### Chapter 002 — Velkynvelve

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Velkynvelve`

A treacherous drow outpost suspended high within a cavern of the Underdark. Its structures include hanging towers, rope bridges, extensive webs, spider-silk lines, a guarded slave pen, and platforms positioned far above the cavern floor. A waterfall feeds a twenty-foot-deep natural pool below, which drains into an underground river. The outpost's complex vertical layout makes ordinary movement difficult but also creates several possible routes for sabotage and escape.
