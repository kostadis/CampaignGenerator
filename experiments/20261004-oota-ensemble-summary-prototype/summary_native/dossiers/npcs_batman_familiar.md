# NPCs — Batman (Familiar)

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 038 — Batman (Familiar)

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/038-session-wererats-wisdom-and-the-war-on-ooze.md`
- Original heading: `Batman (Familiar)`

A bat familiar summoned by Daz to scout dangerous areas. It telepathically relays information about the caves and the discovery of the Pudding King to the party.

Chosen for its echolocation and ability to navigate dark caverns, Batman scouts abandoned gnomish districts, ooze-filled passages, a destroyed fungal barrier, and the Pudding King’s throne room. His reconnaissance allows the party to locate and observe the enemy without entering the slime-covered chambers or attracting the Pudding King’s attention.
