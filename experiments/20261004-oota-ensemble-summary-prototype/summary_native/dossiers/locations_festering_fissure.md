# Locations — Festering Fissure

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 043 — Festering Fissure

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/043-session-harvesting-horror-in-the-underdark.md`
- Original heading: `Festering Fissure`

A humid, hot cavern smelling of sweet vegetation rot. The walls appear to breathe and are covered in pulsing white mold. Fungi have grown into shapes resembling deep gnome children. Contains a dead giant's corpse from which Ghost Hold Lichen grows, tended by a shambling mound and violet fungi. It stands in stark contrast to the dry, cold rest of Blingdenstone ("This is feels alive"), and its mold pulses "in time with the heartbeat." It lies on the way toward Entémoch's boon.

### Chapter 044 — Festering Fissure

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/044-session-from-zuggtmoy-to-entemoch-s-boon.md`
- Original heading: `Festering Fissure`

A location in the Underdark where the party fought a Shambling Mound and charmed Violet Fungus, and successfully harvested rare lichen from a giant's corpse. Zuggtmoy has established a garden of nightmare mushrooms shaped like children here. The DM noted of the mushroom children: "these are not real children."
