### Chapter 017 — Obelisk Chamber / Gray Ghost Lair

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/017-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Obelisk Chamber / Gray Ghost Lair`

Seen in the flumph's vision: a chamber with a circular pathway, an obelisk and the stolen red dragon egg, guarded by about ten derro. The vision also showed a crude shrine to Demogorgon heaped with papers, marked nearby on the map; the tape does not say it is in the same chamber. It can be reached by a quick passageway or through a door further along the tunnels. The Council of Savants meets nearby.
