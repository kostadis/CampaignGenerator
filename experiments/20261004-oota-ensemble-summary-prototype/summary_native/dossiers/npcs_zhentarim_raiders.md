# NPCs — Zhentarim Raiders

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 064 — Zhentarim Raiders

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/064-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Zhentarim Raiders`

Mercenaries working for the Zhentarim, found looting the High Tower lobby and bagging specific books from a written manifest. Their swords are bloodied, and one carries the bow used to kill the Watcher on the walkway. They are hardened enough that one resists Thorin's Menacing Attack. Both fail to evade Gyrgum's caustic brew; one dies when the acid first burns him, while the other remains standing and coated in the corrosive substance when the session ends.
