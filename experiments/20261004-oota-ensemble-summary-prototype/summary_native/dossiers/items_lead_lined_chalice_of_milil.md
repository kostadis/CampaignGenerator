### Chapter 060 — Lead-lined Chalice of Milil

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/060-session-never-mind-the-murders.md`
- Original heading: `Lead-lined Chalice of Milil`

A brass chalice held by Milil’s statue in the Southern Dining Hall. It had previously been removed and used for midsummer wine. Its lead lining blocks divination such as *Locate Object*, making it an ideal hiding place for Janussi’s heart and the bloody cleaver.
