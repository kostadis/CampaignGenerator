Narrak's ritual chamber in the Whorlstone Tunnels beneath Gracklstugh, where derro cultists were performing a ritual to grow second heads on stone giants.

- **During the battle:** The area was engulfed in a sphere of magical darkness, and Narrak's silence zone was also active.
- **Contents:** The chamber held a two-foot-tall statue used in the ritual, Narrak's texts, and an iron chest belonging to Narrak.
- **After the fight:** One unnoticed figure still lingered in the background, trying not to be seen.
