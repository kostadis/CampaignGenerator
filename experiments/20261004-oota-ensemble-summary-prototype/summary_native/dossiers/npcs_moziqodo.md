# NPCs — Moziqodo

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 063 — Moziqodo

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `Moziqodo`

A massive and powerful demon spawn revealed to be the abyssal spawn son of Sylvira. His mother normally kept him within a magical sanctuary, but he had been outside it for some time.

Moziqodo was apparently the beast that tore out Bookwyrm's throat and took her key; he then attacked Tadric in the domed rotunda to seize the second. He was apparently expected to deliver Tadric's key to A'lai at the high tower. Thorin frightened him, Zalthir stunned him, Daz struck him with Magic Missile, and Thorin killed him with a critical blow from Dawnbringer before he could take a single action.

### Chapter 064 — Moziqodo

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/064-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Moziqodo`

Sylvira's demonspawn son, killed before this session opens. His corpse remains with the party and Tadric at the beginning of the session. His death creates an uncomfortable obligation to inform Sylvira, which the party postpones. A'lai Aivenmore remains unaware of Moziqodo's death and is waiting for him to arrive at the High Tower with the second key.

### Chapter 065 — Moziqodo

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/065-session-08-03.md`
- Original heading: `Moziqodo`

Killed by the party in a single round of combat during their approach to the High Tower. Moziqodo had murdered Bookwyrm, taken her High Tower key, and **delivered it to A'lai Aivenmore** before the party killed him. The party later stripped that key from A'lai and hid it inside Glabbagool.

Moziqodo’s death also left the party facing the prospect of informing his mother, Sylvira, though they deferred that conversation while rushing toward the High Tower.
