# Locations — Temple of the Steadfast Stone

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 039 — Temple of the Steadfast Stone

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Temple of the Steadfast Stone`

A temple within the Rockblight proposed by Dorbo as a strategic staging point for the attack on the Pudding King.

Its location would allow Blingdenstone's army to draw the ooze forces away from populated areas. The temple has fallen under malign influence and cannot safely serve this role until cleansed.

A red spell gem must be placed within its menhir and defended until three temple guardians awaken. Once restored, the temple will allow the Stoneheart Enclave to protect Earth Elementals from the madness caused by Ogrémoch's Bane.
