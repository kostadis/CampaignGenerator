### Chapter 041 — Ice Mirror

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/041-session-mirror-mirror-on-the-wall-who-s-the-maddest-of-them-all.md`
- Original heading: `Ice Mirror`

A reflective surface created by the party using Shape Water, gems, and ice. It was designed to reflect the Medusa's petrifying gaze back at her. The mirror was pre-built before entering the Medusa's chamber and used by Zalthir in combat, with Daz providing light by igniting a bed with Fireball to enhance its effectiveness.
- Daz silvered the water by changing its opacity and color.
- His attempt at prism-like refraction (Sleight of Hand 12) gave allies only "a vague idea of what's going on."
- Zalthir held it in one hand as a readied action.

### Chapter 042 — Ice Mirror

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/042-session-zalthir-s-reflection-thorin-s-stand.md`
- Original heading: `Ice Mirror`

A piece of ice used as a mirror, capable of deflecting the Medusa's petrifying gaze. Zalthir used it strategically during combat to reflect the gaze back at the Medusa, and Thorin later picked it up to use defensively.
- Using it required light, so Zalthir couldn't fight in his usual darkness.
- Picking it up cost Thorin his bonus action, and holding it ready took one of his attacks as a prepared action.
