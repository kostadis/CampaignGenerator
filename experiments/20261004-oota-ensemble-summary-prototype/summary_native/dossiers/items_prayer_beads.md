### Chapter 054 — Prayer Beads

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/054-session-04-28.md`
- Original heading: `Prayer Beads`

Religious artifacts of Bahamut held by Gyrgum and presented to the drow captive without explicit threat, simply with instruction to put them on. The religious implications — unknown Bahamut rituals, the orc presenting them, her drow racial prejudices — caused significant visible distress. They were shrunken along with the spy when the mushroom was administered, and Gyrgum and Thorin conspicuously discussed within her earshot how the shrunken beads are in fact even more potent at their reduced size.
