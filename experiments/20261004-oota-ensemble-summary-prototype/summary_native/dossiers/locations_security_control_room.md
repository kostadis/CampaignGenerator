### Chapter 065 — Security Control Room

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/065-session-08-03.md`
- Original heading: `Security Control Room`

A room within the High Tower protected by magical doors requiring two specific keys to open. It contains magical artifacts associated with control of Candlekeep’s security wards.

The party deliberately kept the two real keys apart and moved A'lai out of the High Tower lobby before questioning him. Manshoon’s simulacrum ultimately bypassed its protections by conjuring a wall of force and shattering the door, though doing so depleted much of the power used to reach Candlekeep.
