# Locations — Darklake District

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 012 — Darklake District

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/012-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darklake District`

The only area of Gracklstugh where non-duergar are permitted to stay and trade, located near the docks and containing the Blade Bazaar. The site of a rampaging two-headed stone giant attack during the party's visit.
- Fully enclosed by walls separating it from the rest of the city.
- Healing potions and heavy armor are available here.

### Chapter 014 — Darklake District

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/014-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darklake District`

A district of Gracklstugh that the party now has permission to travel beyond, thanks to the golden badges, the holy symbol of Laduguer and the gold pins provided by the Keepers of the Flame, though they remain limited in moving freely through the city.

### Chapter 017 — Darklake District

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/017-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darklake District`

A district within Gracklstugh where the party returns after exploring the lower tunnels, on the way to the Keepers of the Flame. Both the Keepers' headquarters and Themberchaud's lair lie beyond, in the Flowstone District; the Keepers were the closer of the two.

### Chapter 018 — Darklake District
