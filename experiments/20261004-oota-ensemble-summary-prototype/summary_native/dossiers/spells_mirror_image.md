# Spells — Mirror Image

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 068 — Mirror Image

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/068-session-08-24.md`
- Original heading: `Mirror Image`

Cast by Manshoon on himself as a buff spell before descending to the duel location. The enchantment surrounds him with illusory duplicates intended to confuse attackers and protect him from incoming strikes. Manshoon casts it after agreeing to move the battle but before entering the lower chamber.

### Chapter 069 — Mirror Image

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/069-session-one-manshoon-dead-at-least-one-or-two-more-to-go.md`
- Original heading: `Mirror Image`

A defensive spell used by Manshoon to create illusory duplicates of himself, causing several of the party's attacks to hit false images instead of the wizard.

The duplicates diverted one of Thorin's attacks and later prevented his javelin from delivering the killing blow. Attackers first had to determine whether they struck Manshoon or one of his remaining images before other defenses, such as *Shield*, were resolved.
