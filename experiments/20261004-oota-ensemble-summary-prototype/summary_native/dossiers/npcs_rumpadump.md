### Chapter 025 — Rumpadump

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/025-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Rumpadump`

A calm and studious Myconid friend of Stool who avoided the madness affecting his kin by not consuming the suspicious mushrooms. He explains that Voosbur's spores caused the group to teleport from Neverlight Grove and seeks help returning home.
- He is quiet and methodical, the opposite of Stool.
- Indignant at being called a mushroom: "I'm not a mushroom. I'm a Myconid."
- He points the party to the Myconid Sovereign as a possible source of a way out of the Underdark.

### Chapter 030 — Rumpadump

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/030-session-untitled.md`

A Myconid companion who traveled with the party and has returned home to Neverlight Grove. Rumpadump had been teleported by a crazed Myconid using a power Thorin refused. Rumpadump prefers to stay home but was touched by Gyrgum's offer ("That's very sweet of you").

### Chapter 031 — Rumpadump

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Rumpadump`

A nervous and energetic myconid scout who leads the Circle of Explorers. He advocates for a nomadic lifestyle, distrusts Phylo, and has prepared escape plans and nutrient caches in the exit tunnels. He offers to sneak the party into the Garden of Welcome via a secret passage. Rumpadump believes myconids belong in the darkness and regards plans to colonize the Overbright as perverse. He trusts Basidia and accepts the party solely on her recommendation.

### Chapter 032 — Rumpadump

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/032-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Rumpadump`

A Myconid companion who is persuaded by Gyrgum to flee the grove. He departs with Basidia and the other myconids.

Before leaving, Rumpadump uses rapport spores to give Zalthir a long, detailed, exhaustive, and allegedly constructive list of feedback. The telepathic review remains in Zalthir’s head for approximately fifteen minutes.
