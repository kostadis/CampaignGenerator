# Locations — Clan Ironhead Forge

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 018 — Clan Ironhead Forge

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/018-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Clan Ironhead Forge`

The place of business for Clan Ironhead, one of Gracklstugh's smithing clans. The clan is barred from taking weapon orders directly and may only take surplus work from Clan Steelshadow. It is frustrated by the Deepking's trade monopolies. The party visits as part of their cover story and receives a secret political offer from Grinta Ironhead.
