### Chapter 069 — Prismatic Gemstones

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/069-session-one-manshoon-dead-at-least-one-or-two-more-to-go.md`
- Original heading: `Prismatic Gemstones`

Ten gemstones held in an open velvet tray within the tower chamber, four of which are dark. They contain prophecies placed there by Alaundo, including one apparently concerning Thorin and Eldeth.

The second Manshoon simulacrum offered the entire tray to the party in exchange for opening the Obsidian Door and allowing him to claim a book beyond it. By picking up one of the stones during the negotiation, he also made clear his ability to threaten the prophecies if the party refused.
