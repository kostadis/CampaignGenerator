### Chapter 015 — Whorlstone Tunnels

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/015-session-04-14.md`
- Original heading: `Whorlstone Tunnels`

A vast, dangerous network of caverns beneath the city of Gracklstugh, suffused with magical faerzress that casts swirling spiral patterns of light and mutes all sound. Filled with strange size-altering fungi and rumored to be a haven for factions hiding from the Deepking's control. Contains a makeshift shrine of bones erected by the maddened Buppido.
- The entrance is a fissure hidden by cloth scraps, canvas and moldy boxes, and it leaks eerie fog.
- The ceilings are around 50 feet high, dropping to 25 feet in the narrow tunnels.
- The air tastes metallic, and dripping water makes no echo.
- Edible fungi grow throughout.

### Chapter 016 — Whorlstone Tunnels

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/016-session-04-28.md`
- Original heading: `Whorlstone Tunnels`

A complex cave system beneath Gracklstugh, currently serving as a hiding place for those working against the ruling establishment and protected by the unpredictable derro. Per the DM, it was once "a hippie place." The party tracked Droki through its tunnels, encountering:
- a dark pool;
- a cracked stone wall whose openings lead to a tunnel beyond;
- an intersection;
- a dense fungi forest.

### Chapter 017 — Whorlstone Tunnels

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/017-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Whorlstone Tunnels`

A complex network of lower caves beneath Gracklstugh filled with fungi thickets and natural caverns, currently plagued by faction warfare, failing magical energies, and sinister cult activities. The party fought centipedes here, encountered dancing myconids, and met the flumph. There are "caves under the caves," and passageways lead southwest, east, and northeast from the fungal thicket.

### Chapter 022 — Whorlstone Tunnels

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/022-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Whorlstone Tunnels`

A vast network of tunnels and chambers beneath Gracklstugh, the Deepking's city. It is marked by the eerie glow of faerzress and by pits filled with the stench of death and decomposed flesh. The party is trying to get out of this network with the egg while facing as few encounters as possible. The route includes the large double doors that the party listened at and pushed open, and a branching passage beyond them.
