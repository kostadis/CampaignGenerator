### Chapter 048 — Voucher of the Varmint

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/048-session-the-knucklebone-of-truth.md`
- Original heading: `Voucher of the Varmint`

A braided rat's foot given to Daz by Chief Chipgrin upon the party's departure. It serves as a mark of friendship recognized by wererats and outcasts throughout the Underdark, from Blingdenstone to Menzoberranzan. It is braided "in a very particular way."
