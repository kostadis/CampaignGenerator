### Chapter 044 — Resonant Geodes

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/044-session-from-zuggtmoy-to-entemoch-s-boon.md`
- Original heading: `Resonant Geodes`

Initially useless-looking rocks discovered by Daz in the Pickshine Mine. They can be arranged to mimic sounds and were used to complete the 'handshake protocol' with the Galeb Duhr. After the Galeb Duhr opened the path, one geode began to hum violently and pull magnetically toward the Whiteshell Mine, acting as a signal tracer pointing to Entémoch's location. They emit a "hollow, resonant, humming, faint, high-pitched note."
