- Original heading: `Pudding King's Throne Room`

A cave illuminated by phosphorescent lichen, with its floor and walls covered in pools and drapes of green slime. A slime-covered throne sits in the middle, facing east, where the Pudding King holds court, surrounded by hundreds of oozes.

Green slime also covers the ceiling, making aerial entry or escape dangerous. The Pudding King appears physically unimpressive, but the ooze army under his command and the hazardous terrain protect him from a straightforward attack.
