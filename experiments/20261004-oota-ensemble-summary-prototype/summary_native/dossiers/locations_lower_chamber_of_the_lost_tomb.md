# Locations — Lower Chamber of the Lost Tomb

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 028 — Lower Chamber of the Lost Tomb

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/028-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Lower Chamber of the Lost Tomb`

A hidden vault accessed through a hole beneath a rolling sarcophagus, a short drop (about five feet, per the GM) below the sarcophagus chamber. Its walls are decorated with rich pigments and semi-precious stone inlays. It contains a stone bier holding a gilded sarcophagus, an invisible stone chest, and is guarded by a malevolent wraith.
