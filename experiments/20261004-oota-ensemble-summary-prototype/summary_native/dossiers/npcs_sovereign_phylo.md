# NPCs — Sovereign Phylo

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 029 — Sovereign Phylo

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/029-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Sovereign Phylo`

One of the two co-leaders of Neverlight Grove. He welcomed the party warmly but spoke ominously of a coming 'day of joy' and referred to a mysterious 'Great Seeder' using gendered pronouns, which is unusual for myconids. He has implemented a controversial policy of segregating the myconid circles and established the secretive Circle of Masters.
- He calls the party "friendly softers" and promised to take them to the Circle of Masters and the "great celebration" tomorrow.
- He calls Basidia "my sovereign and co-equal in all things."
- He ignored the party once the tour began.

### Chapter 032 — Phylo

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/032-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Phylo`

A Myconid Sovereign fully corrupted by Zuggtmoy’s influence. He attempts to maintain a facade of normalcy when the party returns, warmly asking Basidia whether their guests enjoyed seeing the grove’s changes.

When Basidia accuses him of betraying the circle, Phylo turns against her and the grove’s vegetation begins moving to attack. He remains cheerfully deluded as everyone flees, promising that the Great Cedar will make them happy and lamenting that the party rejected the Circle of Welcome.
