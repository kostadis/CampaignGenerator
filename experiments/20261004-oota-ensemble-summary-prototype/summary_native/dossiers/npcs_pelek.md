# NPCs — Pelek

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 016 — Pelek

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/016-session-04-28.md`
- Original heading: `Pelek`

The ghost of a deep gnome traveler from Blingdenstone, bearing a gruesome cleaved head wound. He was murdered and dismembered by his former companion Buppido after being lured in with promises of business deals. He was named after an actor, and hints at a troubled childhood he would rather not discuss. He has been trapped for roughly a year, has no loyalty to any Gracklstugh faction, and calls himself "a very well-adjusted ghost."

Remarkably well-adjusted despite his fate, he agrees to travel with the party after they collect his bones. He is bound to his bones, so he can scout only a short distance: he passed through the stone cracks and reported the way clear. He offers advice about navigating Blingdenstone's cultural taboos and claims the deep gnomes there know ways to the Overbright.

### Chapter 037 — Pelek

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/037-session-slime-spirits-and-svirfneblin-squabbles.md`
- Original heading: `Pelek`

A ghost who had been traveling with the party, seeking to have his bones interred in a cemetery so he could finally pass on. Buppido murdered him, leaving only a small collection of “Catholic relic-sized” remains carried by Gyrgum. Pelek feared that he had become permanently lost and would be forced to torment people forever. He was undemanding about his grave, saying that any quiet patch of dirt would do, and told the party, “It's been very pleasant to travel with all of you. But I really do want to end my journey.” His spirit vanished after the burial rites, leaving the party with a comforting sense of closure.
