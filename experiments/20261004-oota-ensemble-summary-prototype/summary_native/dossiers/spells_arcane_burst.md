# Spells — Arcane Burst

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 064 — Arcane Burst

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/064-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Arcane Burst`

A magical ranged attack used repeatedly by A'lai Aivenmore to strike multiple party members with raw arcane energy. One burst misses Daz despite his low armor class, but another deals twenty-six points of damage. Zalthir initially takes twenty-nine points without falling. Later, after Daz steals the sapphire, A'lai unleashes four more attacks against Zalthir; enough connect to knock the monk unconscious.
