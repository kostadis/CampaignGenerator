# Items — Trillimac Cap Books

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 022 — Trillimac Cap Books

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/022-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Trillimac Cap Books`

Books with pages made from trillimac mushroom caps, laid out on the raised ritual platform in the brimstone cavern "almost but not quite in a line." They are filled with the mad scribblings of derro cultists and contain details of dark experiments. The party recognized them as possible physical evidence of demonic influence for Errde Blackskull. Narrak picked one up and claimed it held "the spell we need," saying none of his followers could read it.
