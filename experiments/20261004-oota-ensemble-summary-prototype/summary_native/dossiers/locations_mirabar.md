### Chapter 055 — Mirabar

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/055-session-the-ember-grapple-s-journey-through-the-north.md`
- Original heading: `Mirabar`

A major dwarven mining city in the Silver Marches, visible from miles away by the smoke and red light of its forges. The city held during the Uthgardt raids and is currently filled with refugees sleeping in shifts at the Smith's Gate, with the Smith's Quarter ringing with hammers all night as half the orders are for militia armor. Notable for a bronze Stroud bust at the Smiths Guildhall, a tavern named The Knight Beneath the Knight, a traveling Stroud School polemicist preaching mortal agency in the corner of the market, and an Order of the Gauntlet shrine featuring a sculpted bronze frontispiece reproduction of Milo Goodbarrel's Account, Volume 1, and a large painting of Malfire, the Vindicator of Nature, tended by two half-orcs in shifts.
