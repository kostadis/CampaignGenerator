### Chapter 031 — Central Basin

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Central Basin`

A large area of the grove containing sentient zurkhwood trees and the circle mounds used for sporing and raising new myconids. The newly spored myconids are only inches tall and initially lack awareness. Some zurkhwoods in the basin have been magically awakened, though the species is not ordinarily sentient.
