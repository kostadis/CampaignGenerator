# Spells — Hill Strike

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 041 — Hill Strike

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/041-session-mirror-mirror-on-the-wall-who-s-the-maddest-of-them-all.md`
- Original heading: `Hill Strike`

A Thorin ability, not a spell. He used it to deliver the final blow to the Earth Elemental, causing it to collapse into rubble, and again with a thrown dart against the Medusa, dealing additional damage and knocking her prone.
- It is usable once per turn with a melee or thrown-weapon hit.
- It adds 1d6 damage and forces a DC 15 Strength save or the target is knocked prone.
