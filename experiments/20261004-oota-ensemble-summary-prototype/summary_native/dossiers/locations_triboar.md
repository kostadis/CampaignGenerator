## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 055 — Triboar

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/055-session-the-ember-grapple-s-journey-through-the-north.md`
- Original heading: `Triboar`

A caravan town heavily damaged by barbarian raids, with one-third still in ruins. A cairn of silver fieldstones in the central square names 243 of the Triboar dead, surrounded by recently laid flagstones. It is crowded with caravans staging, traders, pilgrims, Concordant Order knights, Lord's Alliance couriers, and those carrying rebuild permits, making it a hub for trade route rumors. Notable for the Mountain's Mouth inn, where the party encountered Kestler, the dual-voiced local Eldred, and Glabbagool's first wheel of cheese.
