### Chapter 034 — Darts

Small projectiles used by Thorin to probe suspicious pools and attack gray oozes from a distance. Thorin strikes one ooze twice for 8 damage per hit, avoiding the risk of bringing his armor into reach of its corrosive attacks. The party notes that nonmagical ammunition can be destroyed by contact with a gray ooze.
