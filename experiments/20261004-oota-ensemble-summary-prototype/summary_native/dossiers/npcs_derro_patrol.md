# NPCs — Derro Patrol

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 022 — Derro Patrol

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/022-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Derro Patrol`

A group of four fanatical, insane derro loyal to Demogorgon, met in the western tunnels. They carried hooked spears. They asked whether the party worshipped the dual-headed master and attacked as soon as Gyrgum declared his worship of Bahamut. One screamed "death to Bahamut" and babbled about the master's two heads. All were killed in the skirmish.
