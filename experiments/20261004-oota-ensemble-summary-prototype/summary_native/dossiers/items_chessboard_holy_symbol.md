### Chapter 015 — Chessboard Holy Symbol

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/015-session-04-14.md`
- Original heading: `Chessboard Holy Symbol`

Gyrgum's sacred token, inscribed with a chessboard pattern, used to channel divine energy to repel and terrify the undead minions at Buppido's shrine.
- It is used for Turn Undead against a DC 13 Wisdom save. The undead must be able to see the symbol for the turning to work.
