# Locations — The Foaming Mug

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 038 — The Foaming Mug

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/038-session-wererats-wisdom-and-the-war-on-ooze.md`
- Original heading: `The Foaming Mug`

A tavern in Blingdenstone known for having the best beer, including Darklake Stout, and rooms with beds large enough to accommodate the party.

Because deep gnomes are roughly three feet tall, the Foaming Mug is one of the few local establishments capable of housing the adventurers. A merchant invites the party to visit after the mission and suggests that Glabbagool could meet prospective mining employers there.

### Chapter 039 — The Foaming Mug

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `The Foaming Mug`

An inn or cafe in Blingdenstone where Chief Dorbo directs the party to eat. It has tables and seating more appropriate for visitors larger than deep gnomes.

Chief Dorbo initially refers to it as the place with tables for “big people,” then acknowledges that Thorin himself is short and clarifies that the taller members of the party are the concern.
