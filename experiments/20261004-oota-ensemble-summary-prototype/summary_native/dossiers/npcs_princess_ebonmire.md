### Chapter 047 — Princess Ebonmire

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/047-session-the-fall-of-the-pudding-king.md`
- Original heading: `Princess Ebonmire`

An ooze ally of the Pudding King who attacked by throwing blobs and charging at the party. She had no ranged attacks beyond the blob, and Gyrgum likened her name to "black mud." She was killed by the party's combined efforts, and after her death, two spellbooks containing many spells were found in her stomach.
