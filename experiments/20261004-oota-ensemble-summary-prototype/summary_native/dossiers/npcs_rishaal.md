- Original heading: `Rishaal`

The proprietor of Rishaal's Pageturners bookshop in Waterdeep. He explains how a painting by Milo Goodbarrel of the war's heroes saved the print run of Volume Three: "That painting saved the print run! Milo finished it two days before the signing. We had it framed. People come here for the painting and left with a book." He describes real-world fiction enthusiasts as "exasperating, but they buy three copies of everything."
