# NPCs — Janussi

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 057 — Janussi

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/057-session-candlekeep-s-cold-case-the-gyrgumite-gambit.md`
- Original heading: `Janussi`

The late Keeper of Tomes, a human male around seventy years old. Found murdered in his chambers with his rib cage caved in by several precise axe-like blows and his heart removed. He had been engaged in academic research involving Celestial translations of The Discoveries and annotations on The Golden Ass at the time of his death. He had asked for Gatewarden Kalan Strongbranch's resignation the morning before the murder, citing concerns about Kalan's mental faculties and negligence. He was found with his white robes heavily bloodied around the torso, vacant eyes fixed on the ceiling.

### Chapter 058 — Janussi

- Original heading: `Janussi`

The deceased Keeper of Tomes and the murder victim. A scholarly man with a sense of humor who enjoyed cherry tobacco, was researching obscure spells, and had a volatile on-again, off-again romantic relationship with Sylvira Savikas. The party believed he held one of the two keys to the High Tower Library in a locket he never removed. His final written notes were on *The Discoveries*, but he had been laughing over jokes from *The Golden Ass* before his death.

### Chapter 059 — Janussi

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/059-session-questions-answers-and-a-key.md`
- Original heading: `Janussi`

The deceased Keeper of Tomes whose murder is the central focus of the investigation. He wore one of the two High Tower keys in a locket around his neck, which was stolen. His heart was removed post-mortem, most likely to prevent resurrection. At his 8 AM meeting with Kalan he rejected a proposal for increased watchers; at his 11 AM meeting with Daral he rejected the proposal to preserve a poetry collection that Janussi intended to sell. He was found that evening reading *The Golden Ass*, Daral's Deadwinter gift, and licking his fingers as he turned pages — almost certainly the means by which he ingested the midnight tears poison. He died at or near midnight. Daral referred to him as "that philistine."

### Chapter 061 — Janussi

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/061-session-the-colloquium-was-well-received.md`
- Original heading: `Janussi`

The late Keeper of Tomes and murder victim. On Deadwinter annual-review day, he blocked Kalan’s methodology, ordered Alkrist’s germ-warfare research terminated, denied Bookwyrm the succession, and left A'lai facing exile. He was poisoned by reading Daral’s tampered gift and died at midnight. Someone subsequently removed his heart and stole his locket and sapphire. The party judges that his Intelligence far exceeded his Wisdom.

### Chapter 062 — Janussi

- Original heading: `Janussi`

The deceased whose murder is the central mystery of the investigation. His practice of giving harsh reviews prompted the party to observe that any number of magically powerful academics might have wanted him dead.

Multiple characters refer to him with exaggerated reverence as “beloved by all” and lament his “untimely demise,” even though many of the people around him appear privately pleased that he is gone. He had previously rejected Kalan's investigative paper.
