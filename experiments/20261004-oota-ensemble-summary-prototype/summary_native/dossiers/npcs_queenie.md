## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 058 — Queenie

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/058-session-the-case-of-the-missing-tickles.md`
- Original heading: `Queenie`

A five-year-old female cat residing in the tower who provided cryptic but vital information about the night's events in exchange for tickles. She witnessed Sylvira's magic missile outburst, Daral fleeing with a book, and a figure that looked like Sylvira but did not smell like her leaving the tower. She dismisses the party with the line: *“You may now leave, and please give yourself a proper licking before returning to my tower.”* Her testimony was useful but filtered through feline perceptions that the party had to interpret.
