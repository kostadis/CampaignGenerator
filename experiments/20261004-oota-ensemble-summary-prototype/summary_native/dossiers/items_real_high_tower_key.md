# Items — Real High Tower Key

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 063 — Real High Tower Key

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `Real High Tower Key`

One of two essential magical artifacts required to access the high tower and the room containing Candlekeep's protective wards. Kalan entrusted it to Tadric, who was hidden in the North Galleries without knowing what he carried.

Moziqodo nearly killed Tadric to obtain it, but the party intervened and secured the key. A'lai is believed to hold its counterpart, stolen from Bookwyrm.

### Chapter 064 — Real High Tower Key

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/064-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Real High Tower Key`

The genuine artifact required to manage Candlekeep's wards and open the sealed inner door of the High Tower. This is High Tower key **#2**; key #1 is still in A'lai Aivenmore's possession, and the door on the second-floor landing requires both.

Tadric had carried key #2 all night without understanding its importance. Once informed, he hands it over but insists on accompanying the party rather than abandoning responsibility for it. Gyrgum holds the genuine key by the time the party reaches the High Tower. He also retains Kalan's fake decoy key.

### Chapter 065 — Real High Tower Key

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/065-session-08-03.md`
- Original heading: `Real High Tower Key`

The genuine magical key handed over voluntarily by Tadric. It is one of two keys normally required to open the sealed security-control-room doors.

Gyrgum held this key along with the sapphire and the fake key throughout the confrontation. A'lai incorrectly assumed the party had hidden it somewhere else in Candlekeep because only an idiot would bring both necessary keys into the room where an enemy wanted to use them.

The second real key—the one taken from Bookwyrm by Moziqodo and delivered to A'lai—was stripped from A'lai this session and hidden inside Glabbagool.
