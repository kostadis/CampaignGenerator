### Chapter 044 — Lichen

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/044-session-from-zuggtmoy-to-entemoch-s-boon.md`
- Original heading: `Lichen`

Rare and pure lichen successfully harvested by the party from a giant's corpse in the Festering Fissure. It is needed by Kazook Pickshine to brew an acid-resistant coating for weapons in preparation for an upcoming battle. The party completed the harvest with careful chanting by Gyrgum and arcane identification by Daz. The checks were Arcana 22, Nature 26, and a failed Sleight of Hand, for two of three successes.
