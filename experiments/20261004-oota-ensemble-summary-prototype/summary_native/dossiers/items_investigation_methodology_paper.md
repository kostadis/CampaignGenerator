### Chapter 061 — Investigation Methodology Paper

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/061-session-the-colloquium-was-well-received.md`
- Original heading: `Investigation Methodology Paper`

A scholarly work championed by Kalan Strongbranch and delivered to Thorin by Elian. It argues that magical evidence is adversarial and that no single spell establishes truth. Investigators must combine independent observations, physical evidence, testimony, and magical results. Institutional hostility toward the paper may have contributed to Kalan’s firing.
