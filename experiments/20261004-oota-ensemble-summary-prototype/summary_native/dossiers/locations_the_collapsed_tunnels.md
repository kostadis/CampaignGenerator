### Chapter 032 — The Collapsed Tunnels

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/032-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Collapsed Tunnels`

A maze of dark, unstable passages created when a sudden tremor causes the tunnels around the party to collapse. Their original route is sealed, although a new passage remains open to the north.

Thorin and Gyrgum begin producing a carefully mapped and tastefully labeled record of the passages while Jimjar insists the situation does not yet constitute a failure on his part.
