### Chapter 051 — The Icon

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/051-session-a-spore-filled-finale.md`
- Original heading: `The Icon`

A mysterious object present in the cavern used by the Demon Queen Zuggtmoy to communicate. The party is warned to stay away from it, as proximity to it is considered dangerous — particularly for those who have already had a deep personal encounter with its power.

When Thorin considered advancing toward Ilvara, the DM reminded him that getting too close to the icon could be especially dangerous because of his prior contact with Zuggtmoy. Thorin compared his susceptibility to someone who had already had chicken pox and was therefore at risk of shingles. This warning helped prompt the decision to attack Ilvara's bridge from a distance.
