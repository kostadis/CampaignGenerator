### Chapter 009 — Darkness

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/009-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darkness`

Spawns a sphere of magical, impenetrable shadow that blinds those within it. It was deployed by Zalthir to disorient the aquatic Ixitxachitl attackers, allowing him to use his unique sight to strike them down. ### Chapter 010 — Darkness

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/010-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darkness`

Zalthir created a 15-foot radius sphere of magical shadow that he could move at will up to 60 feet away at the start of each turn. ### Chapter 019 — Darkness

- Original heading: `Darkness`

Employed by Zalthir to create a mobile sphere of magical shadow. ### Chapter 024 — Darkness

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/024-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darkness`

A sphere of magical shadow maintained and repositioned by Zalthir throughout the battle to blind enemies and provide tactical cover for his tentacle attacks. Zalthir attacked with advantage from inside it. Derro enemies tried to get out of it by running in a straight line until they reached its edge, a known Underdark tactic because drow cast darkness innately. ### Chapter 028 — Darkness

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/028-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darkness`

A sphere of magical shadow that blocks all forms of light, including magical light, making it impossible to see within its area. The party used it to safely smash crystals without being blinded, and Zalthir deployed it repeatedly during combat in the tomb to gain a tactical advantage over enemies. Fighting inside it gave Zalthir advantage. It also blocked Daz's sight despite his darkvision, forcing him to reposition, and moving it into the lower chamber cut off his Magic Missile.

### Chapter 030 — Darkness

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/030-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darkness`

Zalthir cast this spell to create a zone of magical, ink-black shadow over the Grick Alpha. It imposed disadvantage on the Grick's attacks and forced the party to rely on area-of-effect abilities and knowledge of the creature's general location. It also blocked Gyrgum's Guiding Bolt and Daz's Magic Missile. Zalthir can only move the darkness at the start of his turn.

### Chapter 031 — Darkness

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Darkness`

A sphere of magical darkness maintained by Zalthir to provide cover and tactical advantage during the skirmish, though it conflicts with the sunlight emitted by Dawnbringer. Magical darkness overcomes Dawnbringer's light within its area. Zalthir initially considers expanding it over the battlefield but leaves the sunlight available because it weakens the adult myconids and allows his allies to see their targets. He later uses the existing darkness as cover before emerging to attack the last light-sickened myconid.
