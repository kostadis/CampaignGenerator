# NPCs — Grinta Ironhead

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 018 — Grinta Ironhead

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/018-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Grinta Ironhead`

The granddaughter of Thangus Ironhead and a politically savvy member of Clan Ironhead. She privately approaches the party as they leave, says she knows something is going on in the Whorlstone Caverns, and offers favorable trade terms if they help facilitate the Deepking's removal from power, noting that Clan Ironhead would become the premier clan as a result.
