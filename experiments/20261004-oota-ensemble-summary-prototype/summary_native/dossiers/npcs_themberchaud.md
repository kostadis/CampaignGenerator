# NPCs — Themberchaud

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 013 — Themberchaud

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/013-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Themberchaud`

A pampered red dragon kept by the Keepers of the Flame to rekindle the city's forges. He was observed flying over the city during the session. The party learned he is kept satisfied with a large hoard, but that the Keepers must periodically replace aging dragons with new ones raised from eggs (purchased or stolen) when they become too difficult to control.

### Chapter 014 — Themberchaud

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/014-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Themberchaud`

The Wyrmsmith of Gracklstugh, a massive red dragon with lava-colored scales and glowing yellow eyes. He is arrogant, overweight from lack of hunting, and speaks of himself in the third person. He recruited the party as his personal agents without waiting for their acceptance, and is unaware that the Keepers of the Flame are planning to replace him with a new dragon hatched from a stolen egg.

His other traits, as shown at the table:
- He is paranoid about invisible spies.
- He is contemptuous of Bahamut and dismissive of brass dragons.
- He approves of Drow for their "conspiracies and secrecy."
- He is pleased by agents with no one to blackmail.
- He doesn't play chess, but "another kind of game."

### Chapter 021 — Themberchaud

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/021-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Themberchaud`

A powerful red dragon and potential client who desires complete information on local events. He does not know about the dragon egg. The party considers him dangerous to inform about it, as it could send him on a destructive rampage against the Keepers of the Flame and the city. The DM also calls him "too fat and lazy": if the party simply slips away, it would take him years to realise they aren't coming back. The dragon baby rattle was suggested as a possible sweetener for him.

### Chapter 027 — Themberchaud

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/027-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Themberchaud`

The Wyrmsmith of Gracklstugh, a somewhat out-of-shape young adult red dragon who serves as a glorified lighter for the city's forges. He is vain and egotistical, styling himself "father of the flame" and "bringer of the flame," and he believes the party to be his personal agents. He offered them a ride across the city, showed off his realm, and lit the Forge of Thrazgad mid-flight. He knows little about where his "vast network" should be looking. He deposited the party at the docks, terrifying the locals with his frightful presence.
