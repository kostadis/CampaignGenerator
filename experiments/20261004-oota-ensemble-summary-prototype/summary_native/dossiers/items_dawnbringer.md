### Chapter 031 — Dawnbringer

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Dawnbringer`

A legendary sentient sunblade with a gilded longsword hilt. It can manifest or dismiss a blade of pure radiance as a bonus action and emits actual sunlight in a 15-foot radius, weakening light-sensitive creatures. It is a plus-two weapon that deals radiant damage, inflicts an additional 1d8 damage against undead, and can cast lesser restoration once per day.

Dawnbringer speaks with a kind feminine voice. After being trapped in the dark for thousands of years, it suffers from intense anxiety about darkness and abandonment. It may become distressed if Thorin stops using it, leaves it alone, or favors another weapon. When Thorin attempts to enter magical darkness, the sword may panic and try to take control of him, though he can attempt to reassure it. Zalthir's magical darkness suppresses its sunlight. Despite these difficulties, Dawnbringer already appears fond of Thorin because he carries it, speaks to it, and allows it to shine.
