# Locations — The Brimstone Cavern

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 022 — The Brimstone Cavern

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/022-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Brimstone Cavern`

A large, damp chamber filled with the oppressive smell of brimstone and chemicals.
- **Entrance:** a pit trap sits just inside, and campfire light shows from within.
- **Lighting and sound:** the eerie, hellish red glow of faerzress swirls along the walls in spiral patterns and casts dancing shadows. The air feels muted, and dripping water makes no echo.
- **Growth:** fungi cover the floors and walls.
- **Platform:** a raised natural platform about five feet high holds a glowing ritual circle used by derro cultists.

The party found books made from trillimac caps here that may serve as evidence of a demonic conspiracy.
