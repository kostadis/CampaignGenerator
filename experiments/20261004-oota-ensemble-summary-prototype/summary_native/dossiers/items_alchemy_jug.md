### Chapter 010 — Alchemy Jug

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/010-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Alchemy Jug`

A heavy ceramic jug found in a chest aboard the captured duergar boat. It appears to hold a gallon of liquid and weighs 12 pounds whether full or empty. It has the magical ability to produce various liquids on command once per day, including fresh water, wine, oil, vinegar, honey, beer, mayonnaise, acid, and poison. The chosen liquid can be changed each day at dawn. The party celebrated solving their water supply problem and immediately debated producing mayonnaise for their mushroom rations. The GM ruled that mayonnaise would stay stable for two weeks.
