# NPCs — Manshoon (Simulacrum)

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 069 — Manshoon (Simulacrum)

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/069-session-one-manshoon-dead-at-least-one-or-two-more-to-go.md`
- Original heading: `Manshoon (Simulacrum)`

A magical duplicate of the ancient and powerful wizard Manshoon, created from ice and snow. The first duplicate fought the party with spells and legendary maneuvers, using *Mirror Image*, *Shield*, Ward Eater, and extraordinary movement. Ward Eater damaged and immobilized Thorin and Edvaldo while restoring twenty-five of the simulacrum's hit points. Zalthir finally destroyed it, causing it to revert to snow and melt instantly.

The party learned that a simulacrum is partially real, has only half the original creature's maximum vitality, cannot learn or regain expended spell slots, and turns back into snow at zero hit points. Manshoon himself was reportedly killed 170 years ago but has continued to reappear “like a bad penny.”

A second simulacrum in cheaper gray robes was found waiting patiently in the upper tower chamber. It had spent six hours surrounded by warded books it could not read and an Obsidian Door it could not open. This second duplicate proposed a bargain: Gyrgum opens the door; the party receives the prophetic gemstones; Manshoon enters and takes one book. Although he insisted the proposal was not a threat, his handling of the gemstones made the danger to the prophecies clear.

### Chapter 070 — Manshoon (Simulacrum)

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/070-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Manshoon (Simulacrum)`

A magical duplicate of the infamous wizard Manshoon, dressed in shabbier clothes than his predecessor. Like all simulacra, he was fixed at the power and knowledge possessed at his creation and could never grow stronger or learn higher-level magic. Academic, theatrical, and given to evil-overlord exposition, he appeared frustrated, exhausted, and annoyed while remaining unnervingly patient.

Manshoon had spent staggering magical resources and a Staff of Power breaching Candlekeep after the party disrupted his earlier plans with Alkrist. His objective was not Alaundo's prophecies but the Book of Vile Darkness behind the last door. He used the prophecy gems as leverage because the door required a willing Reader and could not be opened by force.

Zalthir interrupted his negotiation, grappled him, and carried him into the dragon's anti-magic field. The simulacrum warned that killing him would bring the real Manshoon's vengeance, but Zalthir threw him into the lava pit anyway.
