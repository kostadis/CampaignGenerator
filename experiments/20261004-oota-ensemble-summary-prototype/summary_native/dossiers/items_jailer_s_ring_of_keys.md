### Chapter 053 — Jailer's Ring of Keys

- Original heading: `Jailer's Ring of Keys`

The keys formerly used by Velkynvelve's jailer. They would have been immensely valuable when the party was first imprisoned but were recovered only after the group had overcome the outpost and its pursuers. Gyrgum appreciated them as a symbol of how far the party had come.
