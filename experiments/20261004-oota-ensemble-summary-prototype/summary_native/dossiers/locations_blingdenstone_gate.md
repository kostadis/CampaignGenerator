# Locations — Blingdenstone Gate

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 035 — Blingdenstone Gate

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/035-session-prejudice-promises-and-the-prince-of-puddles.md`
- Original heading: `Blingdenstone Gate`

The heavily fortified main entrance to Blingdenstone. Its gate is constructed from steel and mithril, laid with magical, magic-resistant alloys, and plated with adamantine. It is meant to prevent hostile forces from entering, although visibility through the gate allows the guards and visitors to speak before it is opened.

Eight gnome guards defend the entrance. Their questioning reveals both the city’s longstanding fear of drow and its immediate concern about oozes.
