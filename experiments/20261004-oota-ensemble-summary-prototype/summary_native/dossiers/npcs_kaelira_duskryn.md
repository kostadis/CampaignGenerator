# NPCs — Kaelira Duskryn

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 051 — Kaelira Duskryn

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/051-session-a-spore-filled-finale.md`
- Original heading: `Kaelira Duskryn`

Kaelira Duskryn is a specialized drow combatant of House Duskryn, contracted with Nym to protect Daz, who emerges from hiding to pull him clear and claims they only get paid if he lives.

She tells Daz, “Come with me if you want to live, you idiot,” then adds that she will still take Daz if he does not want to live because cooperation would make the task easier. She leads Daz northeast through the Insect Plague, succeeds on her saving throw, and still takes thirteen damage in the process.

Kaelira and her allies later urge Daz to remain out of combat, but Daz refuses and tells them their best hope of getting paid is to help everyone survive. After Ilvara's death, the surviving House T'sarran elite warrior disengages and retreats, vowing, “We will meet again. Enjoy your victory for the moment.”

### Chapter 053 — Kaelira Duskryn

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/053-session-out-of-the-dark-and-into-the-darkness.md`
- Original heading: `Kaelira Duskryn`

A Drow wizard and mercenary partnered with Nym. She responds to criticism with “critics, there's always a critic” and meets violence with sardonic professional approval. After Zalthir killed Asha, she applauded and observed that Daz was not entirely defenseless. Kaelira helped interpret the extraction orders, emphasized the vast cost of hiring her and Nym, and mocked Daz's suggestion that his mysterious past made him secretly important. Her ultimate position was that she had not been paid to think hard about the reasons behind the contract.
