# NPCs — Werz Saltbaron

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 014 — Werz

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/014-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Werz`

A Duergar merchant who was specifically targeted and viciously attacked by hooded psionic assassins at the pier. He was saved by the party and, after being healed, invited them to the Shattered Spire to receive a proper reward. A fish-skin parchment bearing his charcoal likeness was found on one of the assassins, confirming he was a deliberate target.

He claims to have "no idea why I was targeted by assassins." His clan affiliation remains unknown to the party.

### Chapter 018 — Werz Saltbaron

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/018-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Werz Saltbaron`

A Duergar merchant who was recently targeted by the Empty-Scabbard Killers.
- He is smooth-talking and business-oriented, and has a suspicious depth of knowledge about assassins ("They are professionals. They're paid to do a job").
- He admits he may have helped some clans sell goods "on the down low."
- He pays the party an 1,100 gp NDA fee, gives each member an obsidian gem, covers their lodging, and hands over five spell gems as a show of good faith.
- He shares political intelligence about the clans and the Stone Guard's newfound bribe-taking.
- He promises trade favors in Blingdenstone if the party eliminates the assassins targeting him, on condition that he stays alive.

### Chapter 019 — Werz

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/019-session-05-20.md`
- Original heading: `Werz`

The merchant the Empty-Scabbard Killers have been targeting. He wants the assassins off his back and offers better trade deals with the gnomes. Eldgrim agreed to "wipe the slate for Mr. Werz" in exchange for the party dealing with the Demogorgon cultists.
