### Chapter 002 — Spider Idol

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Spider Idol`

A lifelike ten-foot-tall sculpted spider standing on a broad zurkhwood pedestal in the shrine to Lolth above Ilvara's quarters. Its eight eyes are pieces of polished jet—four small and four large. In the shrine's dim light, the carving appears disturbingly realistic.
