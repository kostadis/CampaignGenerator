### Chapter 014 — Droki

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/014-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Droki`

A derro agent for the Gray Ghosts Thieves Guild who carries messages and supplies for the organization. He is the party's primary lead in investigating the stolen dragon egg and the psionic disturbance in the Underdark, and is also being sought by the Stone Guard captain Errde.

### Chapter 015 — Droki

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/015-session-04-14.md`
- Original heading: `Droki`

A Derro courier with a pale face and a tentacled floppy hat. He moves with magical speed and mutters about being late while delivering messages between factions. He disappeared into a narrow fissure in the West Cleft District, leading the party into the Whorlstone Tunnels.
- He talks about himself in the third person ("Droki is late").
- He fails to notice he is being followed.
- Both Errde Blackskull and Gartokkar pointed the party to him.

### Chapter 016 — Droki

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/016-session-04-28.md`
- Original heading: `Droki`

A derro messenger who obsessively follows the same route through the Whorlstone Tunnels each day, passing Pelek's resting place about once a day. He leaves a trail of food droppings and makes no effort to hide his path. The DM pictures him as "this half-crazed… drug-addled mule" whom others use because they see him as utterly innocuous. He is suspected of being linked to the corruption plaguing Gracklstugh and is the party's current quarry. He somehow passed through the narrow cracks in the cavern wall.
