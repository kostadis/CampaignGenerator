### Chapter 057 — Miss Hollypocket

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/057-session-candlekeep-s-cold-case-the-gyrgumite-gambit.md`
- Original heading: `Miss Hollypocket`

Janussi's ancient gnome housekeeper, one of the first two people to discover the murder alongside A'lai Aivenmore, at around 7:30 a.m. Tadric confirmed that she would never have permitted the Keeper to let his candles burn down to nothing — which the party used to deduce that the murder had taken place many hours before the body was found.

### Chapter 058 — Hollypocket

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/058-session-the-case-of-the-missing-tickles.md`
- Original heading: `Hollypocket`

An elderly gnome housekeeper responsible for Janussi's daily needs, including bringing his morning tea and emptying his chamber pot. She provides crucial testimony about the shouting matches and visitors Janussi received on his final day, and reports seeing a glowing vision of Sylvira's face in the night. She confirms the locket was never removed and that Janussi was alive until after 11 PM.
