# NPCs — Stroud School Polemicist

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 055 — Stroud School Polemicist

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/055-session-the-ember-grapple-s-journey-through-the-north.md`
- Original heading: `Stroud School Polemicist`

A traveling human preacher encountered in Mirabar who promotes the "human agency hypothesis" — the idea that mortal agency, not gods or dragons, determined the outcome of historical conflicts. He immediately spots Gyrgum and attempts to convert him: "Brother, have you heard the witness call? Are you walking the road for Stroud?" He identifies as of the Stroudite school, rejecting both the Bahamutian hypothesis and the Protanther doctrine. After failing to win Gyrgum over (Gyrgum rolls a 6), he hands him a pamphlet and declares the debate unresolved. He is one of many indicators of a broader religious factionalism surrounding the person of Stroud.
