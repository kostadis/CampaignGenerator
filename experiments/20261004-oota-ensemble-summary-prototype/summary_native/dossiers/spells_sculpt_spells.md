### Chapter 034 — Sculpt Spells

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/034-session-the-wet-bet-and-the-wondrous-cube.md`
- Original heading: `Sculpt Spells`

A spell feature gained by Daz upon reaching level 6. It allows the caster to create pockets of relative safety within area-of-effect evocation spells, protecting selected allies who would otherwise be caught in the blast.

The party immediately identifies its most attractive use: Daz can cast a Fireball into a crowded fight without damaging companions protected by the feature.
