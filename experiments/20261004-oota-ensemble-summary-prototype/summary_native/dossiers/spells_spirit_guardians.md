# Spells — Spirit Guardians

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 026 — Spirit Guardians

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/026-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Spirit Guardians`

Cast by Gyrgum to summon protective, spectral angelic entities that inflicted damage upon Derro assassins who entered their presence during the ambush.
- It was Gyrgum's first third-level spell, new at level 5.
- It creates a 15-foot emanation that forces a Wisdom save.
- It dealt 17 and 15 damage, killing two derro.

### Chapter 030 — Spirit Guardians

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/030-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Spirit Guardians`

Gyrgum summoned spectral spirits resembling platinum dragons that swirled around him in a 15-foot radius, exempting Thorin and Zalthir. This third-level concentration spell dealt radiant damage to the Shambling Mound whenever it entered or remained within the aura, on a failed DC 15 Wisdom save, and halved its speed. Combined with Zalthir's grapple, it trapped the creature and damaged it repeatedly (13, then 15) until it was destroyed. The GM ruled that it is not an infinite-damage hack, since a creature saves only once per turn.

### Chapter 041 — Spirit Guardians

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/041-session-mirror-mirror-on-the-wall-who-s-the-maddest-of-them-all.md`
- Original heading: `Spirit Guardians`

A powerful conjuration spell cast by Gyrgum that summons fey spirits to attack enemies within an area. It deals radiant damage and was highly effective against both the Earth Elemental and gargoyle, as well as the animated Drow statues. Gyrgum can designate creatures to be unaffected by it.
- It uses a DC 15 Wisdom save and deals 3d8 damage.
- The GM ruled that exempted creatures need only be designated, not seen.

### Chapter 052 — Spirit Guardians

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/052-session-the-ember-grapple.md`
- Original heading: `Spirit Guardians`

A protective spell referenced during a discussion about damage mechanics and the importance of following established rules regarding how often a creature can be harmed by an effect in a single turn. The GM recalls his son describing Spirit Guardians as “an infinite damage hack” and flatly rejects any reading that would actually permit unlimited damage.
