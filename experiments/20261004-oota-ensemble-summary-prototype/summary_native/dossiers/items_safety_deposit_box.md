### Chapter 058 — Safety Deposit Box

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/058-session-the-case-of-the-missing-tickles.md`
- Original heading: `Safety Deposit Box`

A two-foot metal cube embedded in the tower wall, found open. It contained scattered sapphires, spell component pouches that appeared to bear the names of tomes (including *Ring of Protection* and *Minute Seeing*), and a large amount of untouched treasure including diamonds, platinum, and gold. One sapphire and one parchment were missing, leading the party to suspect someone was gathering components for a spell.
