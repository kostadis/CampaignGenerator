# NPCs — Plinki

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 017 — Plinki

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/017-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Plinki`

A derro savant obsessed with mutating a stolen red dragon egg using dark rituals. She hopes to create a two-headed pet and eventually a grand mount for Demogorgon, and she is also committed to working on the obelisk. The flumph doubts anything will come of her obelisk work.

### Chapter 019 — Plinki

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/019-session-05-20.md`
- Original heading: `Plinki`

A fanatical female derro mad scientist dedicated to Demogorgon. She has been using a massive black obelisk to infuse a stolen dragon egg with demonic energy. She is easily swayed by Gyrgum's clever deception but turns violently hostile upon revealing that the ritual requires the beating heart of a cleric of Bahamut. She is ambushed, grappled, and severely wounded by the party during the ensuing confrontation. She shows off her inscriptions with fannish glee and commands a crowd of minions, some possibly hidden or invisible. Her AC is 13.

### Chapter 020 — Plinki

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/020-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Plinki`

A Derro combatant who was determined to kill Gyrgum and claim the egg. She attacked Gyrgum with disadvantage due to the surrounding darkness and was ultimately slain by Zalthir's breath attack. At the session's start she was held in Zalthir's grapple, and the derro under her had fixed on killing Gyrgum and dropping his heart over the egg.

### Chapter 022 — Plinki

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/022-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Plinki`

A figure of authority in the derro cult of Demogorgon, killed by Zalthir's breath in chapter 20. Skiit said Plinki had promised him a better job and preached that followers of Demogorgon would make the derro "great again." Ulnara, evidently unaware of Plinki's death, asked the party to praise her to Plinki. The GM reminded the players that this is the one who had the egg.
