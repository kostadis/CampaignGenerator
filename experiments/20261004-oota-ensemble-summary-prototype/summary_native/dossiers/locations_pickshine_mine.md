### Chapter 044 — Pickshine Mine

- Original heading: `Pickshine Mine`

A rich gem mine co-owned by Dasco Pickshine where the party encountered four Galeb Duhr blocking the path. The air is thick with the smell of ozone and crushed quartz, and a low rhythmic thrum vibrates through the ground from the Galeb Duhr. It is one of the two mines (with Whiteshell) whose miners reported the rock "trying to speak."
