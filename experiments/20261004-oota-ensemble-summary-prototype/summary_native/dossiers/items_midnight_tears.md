# Items — Midnight Tears

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 059 — Midnight Tears

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/059-session-questions-answers-and-a-key.md`
- Original heading: `Midnight Tears`

A highly toxic pale ochre dust made from the pollen of a plant usually found only in the Feywild. The victim suffers no ill effect before midnight, when the poison deals potentially lethal damage. It can be delivered in food or liquid. When mixed with the sap of a specific Feywild tree, midnight tears loses its toxicity and becomes a powerful pain reliever. The party theorized it was applied to the pages of *The Golden Ass* and ingested when Janussi licked his fingers to turn pages.

### Chapter 061 — Midnight Tears

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/061-session-the-colloquium-was-well-received.md`
- Original heading: `Midnight Tears`

A rare poison stolen from the Oak Tree Apothecary. Alkrist applied it to Janussi’s gift book. Its delayed effect killed Janussi at midnight, allowing the poisoned object to be delivered and handled hours before the death. Alkrist removed the vial’s label, tied it to the Deadwinter Tree as decoration, and attempted to burn it.
