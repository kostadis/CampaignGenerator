# Locations — Circle of Growers of Neverlight Grove

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 031 — Circle of Growers

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Circle of Growers`

An area of the grove featuring terraces and gardens dedicated to the extensive cultivation of fungi for the myconid colony's food supply. Hebopbe maintains a wide variety of crops tailored to differing myconid tastes and takes special pride in his zurkhwood. Although Hebopbe is infected, the party determines that the food itself remains clean.
