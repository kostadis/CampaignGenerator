### Chapter 017 — Pelek's ghost

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/017-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Pelek's ghost`

The ghost of Pelek, a translucent, blue-skinned ethereal companion traveling with the party. It was present during the centipede fight, was included in Gyrgum's Prayer of Healing, and accompanied the group back to Gracklstugh while staying invisible to avoid panicking the locals. The flumph can speak to it telepathically.
