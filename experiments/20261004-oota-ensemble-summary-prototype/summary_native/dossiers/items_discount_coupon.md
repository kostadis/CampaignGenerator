### Chapter 038 — Discount Coupon

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/038-session-wererats-wisdom-and-the-war-on-ooze.md`
- Original heading: `Discount Coupon`

A piece of paper scribbled by a merchant, entitling the party to a ten percent discount on any goods purchased in Blingdenstone, up to a maximum amount, as a reward for saving the Traders' Grotto from gelatinous cubes.

The offer begins as an informal Chamber of Commerce reward. After the party jokes that it sounds like an advertisement, the merchant makes it literal by writing out a coupon. Gyrgum summarizes its value: “Saving the city from slimes is worth 10% off.”
