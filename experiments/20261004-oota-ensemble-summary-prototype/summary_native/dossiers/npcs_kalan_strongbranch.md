### Chapter 056 — Kalan Strongbranch

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/056-session-candlekeep-trials-truths-and-therapy.md`
- Original heading: `Kalan Strongbranch`

The Gatewarden of Candlekeep — summoned from the Arcanium to reception to address the miniature drow spy discovered in the party's bag of holding during inspection. He explains Candlekeep's position with precision: "The bell tower has cells. Comfortable, localized anti-magic. They are very rarely used. Anyone we find guilty of a major offense is exiled rather than imprisoned. Which is to say, we hold prisoners. We do not keep them. If you give her to us, what you are giving us is a delay. We will not deliver to House T'sarran, and we will not execute her. Eventually, we will release her at our gate with a warning." He leaves the choice entirely to the party.

### Chapter 057 — Kalan Strongbranch

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/057-session-candlekeep-s-cold-case-the-gyrgumite-gambit.md`
- Original heading: `Kalan Strongbranch`

The Gatewarden of Candlekeep, a man with a neatly trimmed gray-white beard and piercing blue eyes. He was publicly and abruptly stripped of the murder investigation in front of the full council and the party by Bookwyrm, who cited his troubled relationship with the victim. Janussi had asked for his resignation the morning before the murder. He looked visibly pissed at the dismissal, then recovered himself, bowed curtly, and stormed off. During his private inspection of the crime scene at 11 a.m., Tadric noted he looked more determined when he came out than when he went in — and that Kalan had appeared careful not to sully the scene. The party wonders whether he may have attempted *Speak with Dead*, but Tadric does not know what happened inside the room and the GM confirms only that a prior casting is possible. Tadric personally defends Kalan as a great and honest man.

### Chapter 059 — Kalan Strongbranch

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/059-session-questions-answers-and-a-key.md`
- Original heading: `Kalan Strongbranch`

The Gate Warden of Candlekeep, officially removed from the murder investigation by Bookwyrm. He secretly pressed a brass key into Gyrgum's hand, presented it as one of the two High Tower keys, and said he feared for his life if found with it. He is philosophical and given to sweeping pronouncements about fate, order, and futility. He shared detailed forensic conclusions — poison entered via the left hand and interacted with saliva; the heart was removed post-mortem to prevent resurrection — and provisionally suggested that Knock had opened the safe. When the party challenged his assumption that it was one culprit rather than two, he conceded without resentment. When told the party had already learned more than he had and corrected one of his assumptions, he acknowledged: "You are very good at this." He proposed a corporate-flavored collaboration framework and agreed to share findings later that evening. He suspects one of the Great Readers is the killer and that Bookwyrm may have been manipulated. He warned the party to watch for shapeshifters.

### Chapter 062 — Kalan Strongbranch

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/062-session-academic-vindication-and-an-assault.md`
- Original heading: `Kalan Strongbranch`

An academic investigator and stickler for his own Threefold Proof methodology. He argues that Alkrist's confession alone is insufficient to convict Bookwyrm, insisting that a single magical source of truth cannot establish a complete case. He refuses to ambush Bookwyrm with a Zone of Truth and favors using the naming ceremony to strip away her political protection first.

Kalan expects suspicion to prevent both Bookwyrm and A'lai from becoming the new head of the Avowed, with Teles Ahvoste or Fheminor likely to prevail. He promises a complete report and agrees to identify a book that the party can request as its reward and later use against Bookwyrm. After presenting the evidence against Alkrist, he is publicly reinstated and becomes extremely pleased that the party has vindicated his scholarly methods. His renewed authority also places him back in charge of the guard.

### Chapter 063 — Kalan Strongbranch

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `Kalan Strongbranch`

The reinstated Head of the Avowed and Gatewarden of Candlekeep, whose Threefold Proof methodology was recently vindicated. He values the validation of his academic framework so highly that, offered the choice between putting a criminal in prison and seeing his theory of crime validated, he chose validation.

Kalan admitted to giving the party a fake key as a decoy to protect the genuine artifact. His careful statement at the time — “I have given it now to someone who is not me” — allowed him to create the appearance of a transfer without surrendering the real key. He entrusted the genuine artifact to Tadric and hid him in the North Galleries.

After learning of the coordinated attacks, Kalan exclaimed, “Gadzooks. Someone is definitely after the keys.” He deputized the party as Watchers of Candlekeep, offering a badge and a purely symbolic honorarium. He directed them toward Tadric with a Watcher's stair key, though his recent deception caused Thorin to question whether even that key was real.

Kalan was a little out of shape, on the older side, and unable to keep pace with the adventurers. As he fell behind, huffing and puffing, he continued defending his decoy scheme as the safest way to draw danger away from the true artifact.
