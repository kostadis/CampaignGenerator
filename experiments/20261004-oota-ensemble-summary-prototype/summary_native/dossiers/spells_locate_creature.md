- Original heading: `Locate Creature`

Cast by Gyrgum to sense the presence of another Manshoon within a thousand-foot range. The party first had to decide whether Gyrgum was seeking the real wizard or one of his magical duplicates.

Because Manshoon had reportedly been dead for 170 years yet continued to appear, Gyrgum specified a Manshoon simulacrum. The spell successfully located another duplicate upstairs and confirmed that the party needed to continue its ascent.
