# Items — Dragon Baby Rattle

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 021 — Dragon Baby Rattle

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/021-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Dragon Baby Rattle`

A heavy rattle made of strong materials, inscribed with the name of Clan Thrazgad, with no gems or inlays. It is too heavy for a small child and appears designed for a baby dragon to chew on. As a weapon it counts as a rock (1d4). Possible uses the DM suggested:
- A gift to Themberchaud or another dragon with hatchlings on the way.
- A distraction for some other creature.
- A tool for blackmail or political leverage, including incriminating people or rallying allies.
