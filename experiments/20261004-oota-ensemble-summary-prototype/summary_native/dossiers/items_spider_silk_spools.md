### Chapter 002 — Spider Silk Spools

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Spider Silk Spools`

Large bundles of durable spider silk used by the drow guards to descend rapidly from Velkynvelve's hanging towers. Their precise locations have not all been identified, and the party cannot eliminate every one. The prisoners plan to scout those nearest the escape route and burn or fling them away, denying the drow their quickest means of pursuit.
