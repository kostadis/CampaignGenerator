# NPCs — Alkrist

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 060 — Alkrist

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/060-session-never-mind-the-murders.md`
- Original heading: `Alkrist`

A Dragonborn Great Reader who was conspicuously cheerful during the otherwise tense feast. He proposed *Tales of War by the Generals That Waged Them*, a compilation of firsthand military strategy, and received Janussi’s approval despite Teles’s mockery. Orrin saw him burn a scrap of paper when junior Avowed approached. His chambers contain diseased or poisoned rats, a locked safe, and a book bookmarked to a story closely matching Janussi’s murder.

### Chapter 061 — Alkrist

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/061-session-the-colloquium-was-well-received.md`
- Original heading: `Alkrist`

A thirty-three-year-old bronze dragonborn researcher presented as a fashionable, confident young academic or “Dragonborn Tech Bro.” Janussi ordered him to abandon his germ-warfare research and denied that he possessed leadership potential. Under Kalan’s Zone of Truth, Alkrist confesses to conspiring with A'lai, stealing Midnight Tears, poisoning Janussi’s gift, destroying evidence, and informing Bookwyrm after midnight. He denies responsibility for removing Janussi’s heart and appears genuinely shocked to learn it happened.

### Chapter 062 — Alkrist

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/062-session-academic-vindication-and-an-assault.md`
- Original heading: `Alkrist`

Bookwyrm's nephew, who has confessed to killing Janussi and has named A'lai as the mastermind. Evidence connects him to the poison and the relevant book, but the party remains convinced that his actions do not explain the missing heart, the concealed evidence, the suspected polymorph, the pigeons, or the missing key.

Kalan orders Alkrist confined securely in Candlekeep's prison so he cannot escape. His safe contains money and useful potions but no further incriminating evidence.

### Chapter 063 — Alkrist

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `Alkrist`

The individual whose confession and physical evidence linked him to the murder of Janussi. He wanted Janussi dead but apparently had no interest in the missing high tower key. His room contained a safe with gold, two potions, and a gold coin engraved with a red herring.
