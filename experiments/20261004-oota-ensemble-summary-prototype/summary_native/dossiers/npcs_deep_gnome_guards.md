# NPCs — Deep Gnome Guards

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 036 — Deep Gnome Guards

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/036-session-not-an-ooze-just-a-cube.md`
- Original heading: `Deep Gnome Guards`

A group of Svirfneblin guards who previously admitted the party to Blingdenstone and then had to hurry after them. They arrive huffing and puffing. One officer speaks in a squeaky voice and enjoys emphasizing proper military procedure, while the sergeant responsible for missing the relevant memo appears flummoxed and deeply embarrassed. The officer publicly tells him, “Shame on you,” and stresses the importance of reading orders thoroughly.

Their standing orders require all Drow escapees to be escorted to Chief Dorbo Diggermattock for questioning. Their request is genuine and free of malevolence: because Clan Diggermattock took this territory from the Drow, the guards regard intelligence about their long-term enemies as critically important to Blingdenstone’s defenses.
