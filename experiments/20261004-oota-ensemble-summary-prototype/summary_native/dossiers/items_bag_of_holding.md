### Chapter 047 — Bag of Holding

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/047-session-the-fall-of-the-pudding-king.md`
- Original heading: `Bag of Holding`

A magical bag capable of storing vast amounts of items and potentially creatures. The party discussed using it to contain the Pudding King, but the DM ruled that while it could temporarily deprive an ooze of an action, a sentient ooze could 'ooze out' of it and escape. By the rules read at the table, a creature can be forced in with an action if it fits the two-foot-wide mouth, can open the bag from inside, and eventually suffocates. While inside, the Pudding King could not use magical abilities or give commands. Glabbagool later expressed interest in being carried in it.
