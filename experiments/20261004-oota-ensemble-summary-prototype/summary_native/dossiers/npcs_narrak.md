# NPCs — Narrak

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 022 — Narrak

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/022-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Narrak`

A zealous derro cult leader and the obvious head of the crew running dark experiments in the brimstone cavern. He was overheard declaring that his work serves the elevation of the derro, the triumph of Demogorgon, and the destruction of the duergar. He guards the knowledge in the trillimac cap books jealously, telling his followers to stand back because none of them can read them. His last words of the session were that he had found the spell they needed and "All we need is parts."

### Chapter 023 — Narrak

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/023-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Narrak`

A Derro cultist leader and member of the Council of Savants with a notably squeaky voice. He orchestrates a ritual to graft second heads onto stone giant statues in the name of Demogorgon, aiming to destroy Gracklstugh. He is arrogant, dramatic, and frequently loses patience with his incompetent followers, delivering his threats in rhyming couplets. He refuses to curse, preferring "by golly" and "Gosh darn it." He has about 70 hit points and cast Fear on the party before Daz's earthen grasp restrained him during the ambush and broke his concentration. The players took an instant liking to him ("I love this guy"; "He's right out of like space balls or something").

### Chapter 024 — Narrak

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/024-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Narrak`

A derro member of the Council of Savants who led the ritual to drive stone giants mad and give them a second head. He was a powerful spellcaster who fought with a spear.

- **In combat:** He cast silence to block the party's magic. He was restrained by Daz's earthen grasp and could not break free, declaring "I will not be held in prison indefinitely. I will escape."
- **His rallying cry:** He urged his dying followers on with "Fools, your sacrifice is but a step toward glory," and repeated it with less conviction each time.
- **His death:** He was slain by Thorin. With his dying breath, he cryptically claimed the ritual could not be undone and that someone was "sacrificed."
- **Belongings:** He wore the key to his iron chest around his neck.
