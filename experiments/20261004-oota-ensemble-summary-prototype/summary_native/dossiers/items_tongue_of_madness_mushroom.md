### Chapter 012 — Tongue of Madness Mushroom

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/012-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Tongue of Madness Mushroom`

A strange mushroom found in the party's possession that, when consumed, forces the eater to compulsively speak every thought aloud for an hour. The party had Buppido eat one to reveal his true intentions, and he voiced every grievance he had been saving up.
