### Chapter 044 — Gnome Explosives

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/044-session-from-zuggtmoy-to-entemoch-s-boon.md`
- Original heading: `Gnome Explosives`

Specialized explosive devices containing 'rude powder', a substance the deep gnomes are specialists in creating. These explosives were used to breach the time-snagged wall at Whiteshell Mine, with the gnomes shaping the charge to direct the blast while Zalthir redirected the magical explosion through Shadow Step. Rival gnomes each championed their own device.
