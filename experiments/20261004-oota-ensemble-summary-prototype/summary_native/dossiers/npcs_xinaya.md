## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 031 — Xinaya

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Xinaya`

A drow scout formerly under Ilvara's command, found buried to her neck in the Garden of Welcome with her body rotting and sprouting mushrooms. She warned the party about Zuggtmoy's trap and begged for a merciful death. Xinaya had been ordered to search near Neverlight Grove because Ilvara expected the fugitives to seek shelter there. Her patrol accepted Phylo's invitation to the garden and was transformed into its victims and spore servants. She recognized Daz as being from Menzoberranzan and charged him with warning the Drow about Zuggtmoy.
