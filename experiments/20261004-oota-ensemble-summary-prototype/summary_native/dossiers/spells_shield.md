### Chapter 051 — Shield

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/051-session-a-spore-filled-finale.md`
- Original heading: `Shield`

A reactive barrier of magical force cast by Daz as a reaction when the first series of hand-crossbow attacks appeared to hit him. It would have raised his armor class for the remainder of the volley.

The spell and its damage were later rewound after the table remembered that Zalthir's Darkness imposed disadvantage on the drow attacks. Daz recovered both the hit points and the expended spell slot. He later considered using Shield against Flamestrike, but it could not help because that spell required a Dexterity save rather than an attack against armor class.

### Chapter 069 — Shield

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/069-session-one-manshoon-dead-at-least-one-or-two-more-to-go.md`
- Original heading: `Shield`

A reactive magical barrier cast by the Manshoon simulacrum when Zalthir landed what would otherwise have been a killing attack. It increased Manshoon's armor class by five and lasted until the start of his next turn.

The spell turned Zalthir's first hit into a miss, but it could not stop the follow-up blow that destroyed the simulacrum.
