# Locations — Velkynvelve Waterfall

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 002 — Velkynvelve Waterfall

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Velkynvelve Waterfall`

A natural feature formed by water venting through a crack in the cavern ceiling near the eastern wall. It pours between stalactites into a twenty-foot-deep pool on the cavern floor, then flows into an underground river that travels for miles. Thorin determined that a person could slide down the falls and land in the pool without suffering injury, making it the party's strongest known escape route.
