A collection of four scrolls recovered from the sunken chest, containing the spells Poison Spray, Burning Hands, Mirror Image (for the wizard), and Inflict Wounds (1st level, for the cleric). The DM explained that scrolls can be copied into a spellbook or kept for a single use that does not consume spell slots.
