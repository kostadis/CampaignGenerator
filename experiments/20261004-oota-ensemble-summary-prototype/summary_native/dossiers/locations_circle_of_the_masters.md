### Chapter 031 — Circle of the Masters

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Circle of the Masters`

An area dominated by a massive towering mushroom beyond the Garden of Welcome, from which the discordant music of the fungal wedding procession emanates. Mist fills the smaller cavern beyond, concealing Zuggtmoy's bridesmaids and the procession until they approach. The music issuing from it causes the garden's remaining victims to join in a telepathic wedding hymn.
