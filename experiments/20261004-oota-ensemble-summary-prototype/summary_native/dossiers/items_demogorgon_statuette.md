### Chapter 017 — Demogorgon Statuette

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/017-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Demogorgon Statuette`

A small, recently crafted, low-quality idol at the shrine to Demogorgon in the Gray Ghost lair. Gartokkar specifically requested it as proof that the Gray Ghosts are involved in demon worship, to convince the Deepking to wage war against them. In his words, "it's not even a very valuable statue. I just want the statue."
