### Chapter 042 — Hypnotic Pattern

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/042-session-zalthir-s-reflection-thorin-s-stand.md`
- Original heading: `Hypnotic Pattern`

A spell that creates a swirling pattern of colors in a 30-foot cube, incapacitating creatures that fail a wisdom saving throw and reducing their speed to zero. Daz used this to incapacitate the first Earth Elemental, allowing the party to coordinate a devastating attack. The save was DC 15 Wisdom. The effect ends as soon as the target takes damage, which is why the party readied simultaneous attacks.

### Chapter 043 — Hypnotic Pattern

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/043-session-harvesting-horror-in-the-underdark.md`
- Original heading: `Hypnotic Pattern`

A spell cast by Daz in a 30-foot cube that creates shimmering, colorful patterns. Creatures who fail a DC 15 wisdom saving throw become charmed and incapacitated with a speed of zero. Any damage to an affected creature breaks the effect. It successfully incapacitated the violet fungi and Zalthir, but the shambling mound resisted by the narrowest margin. Daz chose it knowing it would catch Zalthir, reasoning that "If I slap him and he takes any amount of damage... he comes out of it."

### Chapter 044 — Hypnotic Pattern

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/044-session-from-zuggtmoy-to-entemoch-s-boon.md`
- Original heading: `Hypnotic Pattern`

A spell successfully cast by Daz on one of the basilisks in the summoning circle, incapacitating it and reducing its speed to zero. The spell ends if the creature takes damage or someone uses an action to shake it out of its stupor.
- It was a DC 15 Wisdom save against the basilisk's -4.
- It is the same spell that earlier caught Zalthir.

### Chapter 045 — Hypnotic Pattern

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/045-session-gnome-more-elemental-slavery.md`
- Original heading: `Hypnotic Pattern`

A spell Daz cast on one of the basilisks. It charmed the creature and left it immobile and paralyzed for a full minute, with no saving throw to break free. The creature remained charmed as long as no one damaged it or shook it awake, effectively taking it out of combat for the duration needed to complete the ritual. The GM ruled that a zero-damage punch would not break it.
