# NPCs — The House T'sarran Mage

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 051 — The House T'sarran Mage

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/051-session-a-spore-filled-finale.md`
- Original heading: `The House T'sarran Mage`

A drow spellcaster who blindly fires a Lightning Bolt into the fray from the darkness, frustrated by the lack of easy kills and the presence of magical hazards. The bolt misses “everybody and their mother.”

He is criticized by the House T'sarran elite warrior for failing to bring Dispel Magic. The warrior threatens that the matron mother of House T'sarran will hear about how badly the supposedly easy assassination has gone.

Daz's Fireball badly wounds him — the fourth-level blast inflicts thirty-seven points of damage and kills the lesser guards — and Zalthir delivers the killing blow.
