# NPCs — Iron Owlbear Guardian

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 068 — Iron Owlbear Guardian

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/068-session-08-24.md`
- Original heading: `Iron Owlbear Guardian`

A tiny winged iron owlbear statue that serves as a sentinel in the first chamber of the Obsidian Tower. When activated, it opens glowing white eyes, unfolds its wings, and hovers twelve feet above the floor. It demands answers to three questions before allowing anyone to pass and claims to have spent a very long time imagining how it will tear failed petitioners to pieces.

The party satisfies it by naming the Hearth, the divine triumvirate of Oghma, Deneir, and Milil, and the height of the High Tower of Exaltation. It then permits them to pass and remains neutral during the confrontation with Manshoon. A previous iron owlbear lies broken on the floor, apparently destroyed during Manshoon’s entry before a replacement assumed its post.
