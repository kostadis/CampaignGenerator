### Chapter 003 — Magic Missile

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/003-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Magic Missile`

Three glowing darts of magical force conjured by Daz. The spell cannot miss, cannot be blocked by ordinary defenses, functions at close range without suffering the limitations of a ranged weapon attack, and proves ideal against the heavily defended Elite Warrior.

Daz’s first casting inflicts twelve points of damage. His second leaves Imbros at six hit points and helping turn the battle decisively in the prisoners’ favor.

### Chapter 007 — Magic Missile

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/007-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Magic Missile`

Daz's primary offensive tool throughout the session. He readied it to destroy a critical web intersection with all three darts, cutting two strands and the pursuing giant spiders' path. He then struck another strand while retreating off the board. Against the spectator, he fired multiple volleys, the first dealing nine damage, and his final volley dealt the killing blow.
