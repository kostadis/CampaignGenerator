### Chapter 048 — Mass Healing Word

Cast by Gyrgum to restore the party's vitality and, more significantly, to repair the physical cracks in the Earth Elemental's stone body, allowing it to relax and eventually depart peacefully. It healed everyone for 2d4+4, rolled as 8.

### Chapter 055 — Mass Healing Word

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/055-session-the-ember-grapple-s-journey-through-the-north.md`
- Original heading: `Mass Healing Word`

Employed by Gyrgum in Triboar to provide ceremonial relief to the many refugees and injured survivors of the barbarian raids. The ten-minute ritual cast (six creatures per casting) earned the attention of local Bahamutian clerics and triggered a theological debate about "the only question of the age."
