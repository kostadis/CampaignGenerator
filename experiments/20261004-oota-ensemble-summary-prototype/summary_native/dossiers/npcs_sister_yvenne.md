### Chapter 056 — Sister Yvenne

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/056-session-candlekeep-trials-truths-and-therapy.md`
- Original heading: `Sister Yvenne`

An authoritative librarian at Candlekeep who serves as "the public record" — a phrase she uses with quiet weight when explaining why she cannot speak of a sealed house in that room. She provides Daz with information on drow noble house politics, the rule of intrigue, the matron mother system, and the law of forfeiture when a targeted house is not fully destroyed. She reads him a list of extinct houses whose Fourth Seat entry alone is marked "struck," explains that "Daz'issin" is a softening name mothers used for sons they wanted to live, provides the heraldry book with the razored appendix, and communicates through careful silences and precise body language that Daz is close to something she cannot say aloud. Her final explicit statement on the matter: "It is possible your name is related, but it is unlikely. The houses that had those names have all gone."
