## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 022 — The Cauldron Chamber

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/022-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Cauldron Chamber`

A large chamber with scrap iron cages holding three resting cave bears, which give the room a heavy, unpleasant musk. A spiral path marked by small stone pylons circles in toward a tent and a large cauldron near the center. Two derro cultists were overheard muttering cryptic devotions to Demogorgon. The contents of the tent are unknown. Continuing east means passing through this room.
