# NPCs — Kuo-toan Refugees

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 009 — Kuo-toan Refugees

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/009-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Kuo-toan Refugees`

Survivors fleeing the catastrophic events at Sloobludop. They travel the Darklake in search of a safe haven, open to peaceful dialogue and trade rather than conflict. They recognize the party's boat as one of Sloobludop's, call the party "wise" for fleeing, and decline to trade. They are headed deeper into the Darklake. Their boat carries barnacle-encrusted statues of forgotten, alien deities.
