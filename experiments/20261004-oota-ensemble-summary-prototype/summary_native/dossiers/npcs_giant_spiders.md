# NPCs — Giant Spiders

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 006 — Giant Spiders

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/006-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Giant Spiders`

Predatory arachnids that inhabit the Silken Paths chasm. They continuously spin new web strands and closed in on the party after Fargas was freed from his cocoon. There are so many of them that they rebuild the webs faster than a wizard could burn them. They can spin new bridges to get around severed segments, though doing so slows them.
