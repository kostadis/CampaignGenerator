# NPCs — Medusa

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 041 — Medusa

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/041-session-mirror-mirror-on-the-wall-who-s-the-maddest-of-them-all.md`
- Original heading: `Medusa`

A female Drow with white hair formed of pale, hissing snakes. She was found sitting on a zurkhwood throne with her eyes closed and arms crossed as if in meditation. Driven mad by her time in the chamber, she immediately charged the party upon seeing them. She possesses a petrifying gaze that can turn creatures to stone and attacks with claws and venomous snake bites. She was knocked prone by Thorin's Hill Strike.
- Her gaze has a 30-foot range and forces a Constitution save.
- She saved against both Daz's Fireball and her own reflected gaze.
- She screamed at her reflection: "My hair. My hair's a mess."
- She has taken 15 from Fireball, 21 from Zalthir, and 20 from Thorin's darts so far.
