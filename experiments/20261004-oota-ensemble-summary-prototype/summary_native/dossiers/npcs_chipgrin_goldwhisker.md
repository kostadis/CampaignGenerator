# NPCs — Chipgrin Goldwhisker

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 039 — Chief Chipgrin

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Chief Chipgrin`

Leader of Clan Goldwhisker, a community of wererat gnomes. He holds court on a moss-covered rise, seated on a stone chair draped with a cave badger pelt and flanked by two giant rats.

Chipgrin initially assumes the Diggermattocks have sent the adventurers to evict his clan. He has long wanted Dorbo to speak with him but refuses to let the Goldwhiskers be placed under Dorbo's command or sacrificed in a Diggermattock-led crusade.

Daz's blunt refusal to engage in political bargaining impresses Chipgrin. After Daz accepts personal responsibility for the party's strategy and any Goldwhisker losses, Chipgrin agrees to join the campaign and attend the moot.

Chipgrin knew the Pudding King when he worked in the tunnels. His kin saw the gnome after his return, and Chipgrin knows who he is, what he is doing, and where he can be found. He helps the party recognize that the demonic corruption centered on the Pudding King is spreading greed and aggression throughout Blingdenstone.

### Chapter 045 — Chipgrin

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/045-session-gnome-more-elemental-slavery.md`
- Original heading: `Chipgrin`

Chipgrin Goldwhisker is the gnome in charge of the Goldwhisker wererat gnomes. When Thorin invoked inventive, "steampunky" gnomes, Chipgrin retorted, "those are not deep gnomes." His initial thoughts on elemental labor are swayed by the party's arguments during the meeting.

### Chapter 046 — Chipgrin

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/046-session-zalthir-s-daring-grapple.md`
- Original heading: `Chipgrin`

The leader of the wererats who offers his clan's assistance in scouting and creating diversions. He is protective of his people, takes offense at suggestions that wererats would be noisy, and shows initiative by investigating when one of his scouts goes missing. He offers the party a choice: his pack howls them through the psychic slime corridor (“the quiet path”), or the wererats stay with them for the door. He insists on "a clear chain of command," dares Daz to look at his psychic reflection, and approves when Daz refuses.

### Chapter 048 — Chipgrin

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/048-session-the-knucklebone-of-truth.md`
- Original heading: `Chipgrin`

A wererat leader encountered in the Hall of Melting Mirrors who sought the party's help neutralizing psychic noise. He later negotiated for wererat control of the upper boroughs during the council meeting, and gifted Daz the Voucher of the Varmint upon the party's departure.
- Praised Thorin's success as "the old dwarf in you," then awkwardly checked that he and Daz were "chill."
- Shared a one-use shortcut tip for bypassing the dangerous pass through faerzress.
- Argued with Diggermattock over dividing the territory while the oozes were still breaching the wall.
- Told Daz at the departure: "The Goldwhisker clan doesn't forget who stood in the light so we could fight in the dark."
