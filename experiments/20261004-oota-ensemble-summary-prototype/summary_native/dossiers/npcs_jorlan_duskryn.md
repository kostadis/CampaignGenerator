# NPCs — Jorlan Duskryn

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 002 — Jorlan Duskryn

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Jorlan Duskryn`

A drow guard whose once-handsome face was melted and scarred during an encounter with a black pudding. His sword hand was twisted and left missing two fingers. The injuries cost him his position as Ilvara's favored lieutenant and lover, and Shoor Vandree replaced him. Bitter and vengeful, Jorlan offered to leave the slave pen unlocked and create a distraction during the guard change. His motive is not compassion: he wants the prisoners' escape—or even their attempted escape—to humiliate Ilvara and Shoor. Once they reach the pool, their survival is “not my problem.”

### Chapter 050 — Jorlan

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/050-session-fungus-among-us.md`
- Original heading: `Jorlan`

A drow warrior and former lover of Ilvara who harbors deep bitterness toward her following their breakup. He is one of the few drow in the cavern not yet turned into a spore servant.

He is lured into Gyrgum's magical trap during the battle and takes 21 damage. Thorin then hits him with Dawnbringer for 14 damage, which saps him.

### Chapter 051 — Jorlan Duskryn

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/051-session-a-spore-filled-finale.md`
- Original heading: `Jorlan Duskryn`

A scarred drow warrior and former lover of Ilvara. Ilvara discarded him after he was scarred, and he remains driven by humiliation and spite.

Jorlan initially fought the party because he did not believe they could defeat Ilvara. Once he saw that she had been wounded, however, he disengaged and charged toward her, shouting, “Ilvara!” Thorin deliberately allowed him to leave rather than taking an opportunity attack.

Ilvara blocked Jorlan's pursuit with a fungal bloom that turned the path into difficult terrain. Thorin later destroyed the bridge supporting Ilvara and Jorlan, but Jorlan avoided injury in the collapse. Nym subsequently identified him as her brother, agreed that he had always been the dumber one, and allowed Kaelira to strike him with Lightning Bolt. He survived after taking fourteen damage.
