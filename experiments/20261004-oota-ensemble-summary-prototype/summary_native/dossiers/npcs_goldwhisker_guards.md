# NPCs — Goldwhisker Guards

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 039 — Goldwhisker Guards

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Goldwhisker Guards`

Four wererat gnomes guarding the entrance to the Goldwhisker Warrens. Their stringy mustaches, pronounced noses, and other features appear increasingly rat-like on close inspection.

They are accustomed to outsiders treating them with fear and hostility. The party's genuine curiosity and assurance that it does not judge lycanthropes therefore delights them.

When the party mentions the ecological importance of rats, one guard exclaims, “See, I told you,” triggering an animated philosophical argument about the ecological niches of rats and wererats. The debate continues while they escort the party to Chief Chipgrin.
