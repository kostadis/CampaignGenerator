# NPCs — Drow Spy

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 054 — Drow Spy

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/054-session-04-28.md`
- Original heading: `Drow Spy`

An agent of House T'sarran sent to the surface to track Daz and identify his mysterious protector. She is stealthy, defiant, and refuses to give her name, offering her piwafwi as a trophy of her failure. She identified herself as a member of House T'sarran. Under sustained pressure — Zalthir's Stunning Strike and grapple, Thorin's intimidation attempts, Gyrgum's disarming friendliness, the prayer beads, and Dawnbringer's light — she eventually reveals her mission: to observe and report on whoever paid for Daz's protection. She proposes a mutual information-sharing deal and then suggests being handed to Candlekeep's guards. She is ultimately subdued, reduced to tiny size with a shrinking mushroom (becoming "very squeaky and indignant"), nicknamed Polly Pocket, stored in a bag of holding, and assigned to Glabbagool's custody. The indignity is noted to be getting to her.
