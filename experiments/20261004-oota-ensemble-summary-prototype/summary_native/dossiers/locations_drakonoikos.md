### Chapter 057 — Drakonoikos

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/057-session-candlekeep-s-cold-case-the-gyrgumite-gambit.md`
- Original heading: `Drakonoikos`

A building within Candlekeep constructed from the scales and bones of Fyrentennimar, an ancient red dragon, pointed out by Fembris during the morning tour. It houses lore on fire- and lightning-breathing creatures. Fembris fumbled the dragon's name on first try before landing on Fyrentennimar, noting: "Anything you need to know about fire- and lightning-breathing lizards, you'll find in there." It contains a dragon breath simulation chamber that has proven fatal to at least one member of the Avowed; the Keeper's personal permission is now required to use it.
