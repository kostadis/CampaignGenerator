### Chapter 040 — Udhask’s Bones

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/040-session-crystals-ghosts-and-crazed-earth.md`
- Original heading: `Udhask’s Bones`

The skeletal remains of the deep gnome Udhask, found beside a stone bed in his burrow. Examination showed that he had not died violently, although his exact cause of death remained unknown. The party collected the bones so they could return them and fulfill Burrow Warden Jadger’s request to rescue Udhask’s ghost.
