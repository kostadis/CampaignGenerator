### Chapter 026 — Gorglak

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/026-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Gorglak`

A Duergar captain about four feet tall, with ashen gray skin, scars, and tattoos. He wears a heavy iron chain with a clenched fist pendant, symbolizing his loyalty to the Deepking. He is suspicious, authoritative, and open to bribery.
- His lips curl into "a thin, unsettling smile."
- He posed as standing alone while five invisible duergar backed him.
- He demanded weapon inspection and the Stone Guard salute before naming his price of 20 gp.
- He warned of the Deepking's fear of a Drow raiding party.
