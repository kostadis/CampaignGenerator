# NPCs — Spectator

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 007 — Spectator

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/007-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Spectator`

A floating, multi-eyed aberration formerly enslaved by a long-dead drow wizard. It approached the party telepathically to warn them that demons are rising in the dark, but its deeply unstable mind quickly turned to paranoia. It accused the party of working for the demons, then of being a demon pack sent to hunt it, before attacking. It has 30 hit points and AC 14, and its eye rays include confusion, paralysis, and fear. The party slew it within the Silken Paths.

### Chapter 020 — Spectator

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/020-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Spectator`

A beholder-kin creature that appeared suddenly to join the fight against the party. It was restrained by Daz's earthen magic but eventually broke free. It took 16 from the earthen hand and 10 from a helper, escaped on a lucky Strength roll of 17, and was left at one hit point by the party helpers. Its fate after that is not stated on the tape.
