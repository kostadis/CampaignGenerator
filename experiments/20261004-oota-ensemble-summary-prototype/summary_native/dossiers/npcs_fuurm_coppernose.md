# NPCs — Fuurm Coppernose

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 028 — Fuurm

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/028-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Fuurm`

A desperate human farmer and former Drow slave who entered the Underdark to rescue his sister, stolen from the Overbright, was quickly captured, and only recently escaped during a demonic attack on his captors. He and Baedora are taken in by the party. The GM describes him as a farmer "with a perverse sense of loyalty who made some terrible life choices" whose survival seems very odd. Daz's player jokes that he was bred for luck. A 24 on Insight shows he is desperate rather than a plant.
