### Chapter 007 — Tasha's Caustic Brew

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/007-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Tasha's Caustic Brew`

A line-based acid spell discussed by the party as a potential option against the spectator. Gyrgum ruled it out because it is a five-foot-wide stream and allies stood between him and the target. Zalthir noted there is "no arcing" in D&D, so a line attack can't pass over allies. Gyrgum fell back on a hand crossbow instead.

### Chapter 020 — Tasha's Caustic Brew

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/020-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Tasha's Caustic Brew`

Cast by Gyrgum as a stream of acid projected in a line, coating multiple Derro and causing ongoing corrosive damage at the start of each affected creature's turn. Gyrgum used this spell on multiple occasions throughout the battle. It is 5 feet wide, which prompted a beam-vs-line rules discussion. First cast: DC 14 Dex save, one of three failed and took 7. Second cast: both targets failed and took 5 each. A target can spend its action to scrape off the acid.

### Chapter 022 — Tasha's Caustic Brew

- Original heading: `Tasha's Caustic Brew`

Cast by Gyrgum to spray a line of corrosive acid across the battlefield during the derro patrol skirmish, coating several targets and burning them over time. Targets make a dexterity save. One derro saved and was unaffected; derro number four failed and took 2d4 acid at the start of each turn until it scraped the acid off. The GM ruled that area spells don't require seeing the target, so the line could reach into Zalthir's darkness.

### Chapter 024 — Tasha's Caustic Brew

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/024-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Tasha's Caustic Brew`

A stream of acid sprayed by Gyrgum that coated multiple derro cultists, dealing ongoing corrosive damage at the start of each affected target's turn. A target can spend an action to scrape or wash the acid off, and the effect ends if Gyrgum loses concentration, which is what happened when he was knocked prone.

Gyrgum recast it on all three remaining derro against a DC 14 Dexterity save. Two of the three evaded it and took nothing, but the one in the back was fully coated. That derro later took 6 damage and died.

### Chapter 029 — Tasha's Caustic Brew

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/029-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Tasha's Caustic Brew`

A five-foot-wide stream of caustic acid cast by Gyrgum against Brysis. Brysis failed her saving throw and was coated in the acid, suffering ongoing damage at the start of each of her turns. ### Chapter 044 — Tasha's Caustic Brew

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/044-session-from-zuggtmoy-to-entemoch-s-boon.md`
- Original heading: `Tasha's Caustic Brew`

A corrosive spell attempted by Gyrgum that creates caustic liquid. The Shambling Mound miraculously rolled a natural 20 on its dexterity save, avoiding the spell's effects entirely, much to Gyrgum's frustration. It was cast at 2nd level against a Mound with 8 hit points left. Gyrgum: "it's not called caustic for no reason."

### Chapter 064 — Tasha's Caustic Brew

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/064-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Tasha's Caustic Brew`

Unleashed by Gyrgum in a line that catches both Zhentarim thugs. Each must make a DC 17 Dexterity save, and both fail, becoming coated in acid. The spell inflicts 2d4 acid damage at the start of each affected creature's turn until that creature uses an action to scrape or wash it away.

One thug takes eight points and dies immediately. The other takes only two and ends the session still standing, covered in acid and due to suffer another 2d4 at the start of each turn unless he removes it. Concentrating on Caustic Brew causes Gyrgum to end Bless.
