# Locations — Whiteshell Mine

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 044 — Whiteshell Mine

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/044-session-from-zuggtmoy-to-entemoch-s-boon.md`
- Original heading: `Whiteshell Mine`

Blingdenstone's largest salt mine, an interconnected network of caverns producing salt for Blingdenstone and Mantol-Derith. Run by Perigrog Scrapedust, it houses giant cave lizards used as beasts of burden. The party encountered a mysterious time-snagged wall here that was impossibly dry with blinding crystallite walls and absolute silence, where shadows appeared painted rather than flickering.
- The caverns are dug into vast salt veins.
- The DM compared the mines to 19th-century mines, one leading into the next through caves.
- The name isn't about shells or whites. Gyrgum: "Is it full of whites?" DM: "God, no."
