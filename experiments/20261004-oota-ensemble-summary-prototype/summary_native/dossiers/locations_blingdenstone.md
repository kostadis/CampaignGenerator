### Chapter 016 — Blingdenstone

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/016-session-04-28.md`
- Original heading: `Blingdenstone`

The ancestral home of the deep gnomes, mentioned by Pelek as the place where his bones must be returned for a proper burial. Pelek warns the party about cultural taboos there, including avoiding the number eight and any association with Menzoberranzan or Lolth. The drow must declare himself from the Overbright. Pelek says the danger lies not in finding it but in "not getting killed on the way there."

### Chapter 035 — Blingdenstone

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/035-session-prejudice-promises-and-the-prince-of-puddles.md`
- Original heading: `Blingdenstone`

A gnome city in the Underdark that has repeatedly changed hands, passing from the gnomes to the drow and then back to the gnomes. Jimjar describes it as “a little bit of a mess,” but believes its inhabitants can help the party find a route out of the Underdark.

The city is currently threatened by an unexplained surge of unusually active oozes. Its inhabitants appear aware of the danger and are sufficiently alarmed that the sight of a gelatinous cube at their gate nearly triggers an attack.

Blingdenstone is approached by a long staircase built for gnome-sized creatures. The stairs are comfortable for gnomes, Spiderbait, and similarly sized travelers but difficult for larger members of the party.

### Chapter 036 — Blingdenstone

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/036-session-not-an-ooze-just-a-cube.md`
- Original heading: `Blingdenstone`

A deep gnome city in the Underdark, built throughout a network of caverns and stone structures. Its name is explicitly treated as a pun on “bling in the stone,” referring to gems in the rock.

Clan Diggermattock took at least part of this territory from the Drow, making intelligence about Drow activity essential to the settlement’s defenses. Blingdenstone is currently suffering from a widespread ooze infestation. The party enters through its guarded approaches and is escorted through the reception hall and Traders’ Grotto to Diggermattock Hall. Glabbagool’s presence draws uneasy looks from residents who are not accustomed to friendly gelatinous cubes.

### Chapter 039 — Blingdenstone

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Blingdenstone`

A deep gnome settlement in the Underdark and the current center of the party's efforts. Its bureaucratic leadership is divided among financial, mining, religious, civic, and clan interests.

The city is threatened by the Pudding King and an army of oozes gathered in an amphitheater beneath the settlement. Demonic influence appears to be spreading outward from the Pudding King, heightening greed and aggression among Blingdenstone's inhabitants.

The party is attempting to unite the Diggermattocks, Clan Goldwhisker, the Stoneheart Enclave, the Miners' Guild, and the ghostly Burrow Wardens for a coordinated attack.
