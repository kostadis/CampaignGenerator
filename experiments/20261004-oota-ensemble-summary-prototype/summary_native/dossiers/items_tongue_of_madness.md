### Chapter 004 — Tongue of Madness

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/004-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Tongue of Madness`

An edible Underdark fungus resembling a large human tongue. It is genuinely tasty, but a consumer who fails to resist its effect compulsively speaks every thought aloud for an hour. The fungus is familiar to Underdark inhabitants as a harmless practical joke. Daz saved some in his sack for possible use in potion-making or a future trick.
