- Original heading: `Gargoyle`

A stone creature that fought alongside the Earth Elemental. It was damaged by Daz's Fireball and ultimately destroyed by Gyrgum's Spirit Guardians spell.
- It had come down from flying to attack Gyrgum, which let Thorin engage it.
- It took 24 from Fireball and 23 from Thorin before Spirit Guardians finished it at two hit points.
