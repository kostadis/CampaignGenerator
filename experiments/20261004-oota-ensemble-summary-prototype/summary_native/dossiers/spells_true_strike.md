### Chapter 042 — True Strike

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/042-session-zalthir-s-reflection-thorin-s-stand.md`
- Original heading: `True Strike`

A cantrip that allows the caster to make a special attack with a weapon using their spellcasting ability modifier instead of strength or dexterity. Gyrgum used this with his crossbow to deal 19 points of damage and kill the second Earth Elemental; the damage was 7 + 7 plus an extra d6. The GM remarked that the spell had been "really improved... to actually make it a really useful spell."
