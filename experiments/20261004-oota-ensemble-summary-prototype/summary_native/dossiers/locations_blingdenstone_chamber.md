# Locations — Blingdenstone Chamber

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 038 — Blingdenstone Chamber

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/038-session-wererats-wisdom-and-the-war-on-ooze.md`
- Original heading: `Blingdenstone Chamber`

A meeting place within Blingdenstone where Chief Dorbo Diggermattock and Senni Diggermattock convene with other deep gnomes. The atmosphere is initially tense and confused as the party returns, but shifts to intrigue and eventual acceptance of the adventurers' leadership.

Workers and bystanders linger at the edges, pretending not to listen. The chamber’s lights are extinguished so Zalthir can demonstrate Shadow Step. It becomes the site of a sudden shift in authority when Daz rejects Dorbo’s conditions, places the party in operational command, and wins Senni’s support for recruiting Clan Goldwhisker.
