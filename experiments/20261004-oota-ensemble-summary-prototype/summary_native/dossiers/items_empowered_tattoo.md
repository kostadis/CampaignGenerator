## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 069 — Empowered Tattoo

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/069-session-one-manshoon-dead-at-least-one-or-two-more-to-go.md`
- Original heading: `Empowered Tattoo`

A magical tattoo on Zalthir's body that allows him to manifest tendrils of eldritch darkness. The tendrils can strike from fifteen feet away and grapple a target.

Zalthir used the tattoo from within his magical darkness to seize Manshoon, inflict force damage, and drag the wizard into the anti-magic zone. Zalthir carries two tattoos, though only the empowered tattoo's tendrils were central to this encounter.
