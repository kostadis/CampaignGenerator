### Chapter 009 — Escape Boat

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/009-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Escape Boat`

A cramped, foul-smelling vessel located near the docks of Sloobludop, serving as the party's only hope for a quick escape across the Darklake. Though barely functional and prone to taking on water, it serves as their primary lifeline through the subterranean sea. It is overloaded and barely floating with only two paddles, and it nearly came to grief on the submerged rocks.
