# Spells — Lesser Restoration (channeled through Dawnbringer, attuned to Thorin)

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 055 — Lesser Restoration (channeled through Dawnbringer, attuned to Thorin)

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/055-session-the-ember-grapple-s-journey-through-the-north.md`
- Original heading: `Lesser Restoration (channeled through Dawnbringer, attuned to Thorin)`

Used by Thorin to heal the ailments of orphans and the needy in Waterdeep's poorer quarters. Zalthir accompanied him; only Thorin can direct Dawnbringer's restorative charge as the attuned wielder. Thorin's explicit motivation was to do good works and to bond with Dawnbringer over helping others. Dawnbringer is described as very happy about this use.
