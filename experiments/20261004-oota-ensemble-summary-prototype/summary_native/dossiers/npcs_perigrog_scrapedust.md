# NPCs — Perigrog Scrapedust

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 044 — Perigrog Scrapedust

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/044-session-from-zuggtmoy-to-entemoch-s-boon.md`
- Original heading: `Perigrog Scrapedust`

A deep gnome who runs the Whiteshell Mine with a fair but demanding hand. He is focused on increasing mine production to restore Blingdenstone's former glory. He sought the party's help with a mysterious tunnel where time appeared to have stopped, describing it as impossibly dry with absolute silence and shadows that appeared painted on the walls.
- The DM called him "a genuine bona fide capitalist."
- He greeted the party as "The heroes of Blingdenstone."
