# Items — Stone Giant Statues

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 023 — Stone Giant Statues

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/023-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Stone Giant Statues`

Two-foot-tall, 100-pound miniature stone carvings of giants that serve as focal points for Narrak's dark ritual. Under the influence of the cultists' chanting, a second head visibly sprouts and grows from the statue's neck. By the party's return there were three statues on the map, and Narrak was preparing the third. The party considers stealing one to bring to Hgraam, believing it could be used to reverse the curse on living stone giants. Gyrgum's alternative, a chisel, is unlikely to have the desired magical effect.
