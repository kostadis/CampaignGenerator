### Chapter 014 — Themberchaud's Lair

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/014-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Themberchaud's Lair`

A massive cavern in the Flowstone District of Gracklstugh where the red dragon Themberchaud rests upon a mountain of gold. The party was brought here in private to receive the dragon's offer of employment. It is reached through Duergar-only territory, past a stone building outside a huge entrance.
