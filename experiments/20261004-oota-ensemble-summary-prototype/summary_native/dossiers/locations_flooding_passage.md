### Chapter 034 — Flooding Passage

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/034-session-the-wet-bet-and-the-wondrous-cube.md`
- Original heading: `Flooding Passage`

A 10-foot-high passage with walls that glisten with dark, dripping water. Its pools rise at an alarming rate after a tremor cracks an underground reservoir or river into the complex.

The passage contains gray oozes hidden in the water. Its mixture of natural cave formations and square, constructed chambers leads Thorin to favor the architectural route as the one most likely to offer an exit. The water initially rises slowly enough to permit exploration, likened to a poorly draining bathtub, but will eventually fill the entire complex.
