# Locations — Ooze Cavern

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 038 — Ooze Cavern

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/038-session-wererats-wisdom-and-the-war-on-ooze.md`
- Original heading: `Ooze Cavern`

A vast, stench-filled cavern with walls covered in dripping green slime. It contains a large, spherical structure at its center, held up by stone pillars, around which dozens of oozes crawl. This is where the party first encountered the overwhelming number of oozes and heard the disembodied voice.

The floor is effectively carpeted with slimes, gelatinous cubes, and other oozes. Black, yellow, and gray slime churns across the central sphere in unnatural patterns that tug at the mind. A magical voice invokes the Faceless Lord and calls Glabbagool to join the others. The concentration of creatures is so great that the party immediately rules out a frontal assault.
