### Chapter 002 — Spider Silk Cord

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Spider Silk Cord`

A thin but extraordinarily strong cord used in the lift mechanism. It runs through a series of pulleys between a hand-cranked horizontal spool and the heavy woven basket. Zalthir found it extremely difficult to cut with ordinary tools, but it burns, making his fire breath a much more effective means of severing it. The connected zurkhwood winch cannot easily be broken without tools, though it can potentially be jammed, slowed, frozen, or burned.
