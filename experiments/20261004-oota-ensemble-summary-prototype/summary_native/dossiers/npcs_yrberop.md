# NPCs — Yrberop

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 031 — Yrberop

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Yrberop`

The leader of the circle mound for new myconids. He is infected by dark spores, causing him to sway and dance to a discordant internal cacophony and speak cryptically of a “great cedar” bringing happiness. He praises Zuggtmoy's great plan, expects the newly spored myconids to join it, and sings them a lullaby about her coming wedding to the “great body.” The sounds in his mind are not truly music but a painful, rhythm-less collision of discordant noises.
