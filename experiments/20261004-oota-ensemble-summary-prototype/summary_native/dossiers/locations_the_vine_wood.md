### Chapter 025 — The Vine Wood

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/025-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Vine Wood`

A dangerous section of Zuggtmoy's spore vision characterized by twisting, slime-covered sentient vines that pulse and reach aggressively for intruders, nearly ensnaring Thorin and Gyrgum. It is damp and warm, with faint rhythmic thumps "as if the very walls breathes," and root-like growths tangle the floor.
