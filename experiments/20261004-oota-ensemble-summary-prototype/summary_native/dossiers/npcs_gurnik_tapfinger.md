# NPCs — Gurnik Tapfinger

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 039 — Gurnik Tapfinger

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Gurnik Tapfinger`

A cleric associated with the temple where Pelek was buried. He strongly desires to see the Temple of the Steadfast Stone and the surrounding Rockblight cleansed.

Gurnik provides the red spell gem containing Hallow and explains that it must be placed in the temple's menhir. Only after prompting does he make clear that Ogrémoch's Bane will send servants to remove it and that the party must defend the gem until three guardians awaken.

He and Nomi Pathshutter deliver an obviously prepared presentation advocating the use of Earth Elementals. Gurnik argues that elementals are ideal against oozes but would risk being driven mad unless the Steadfast Stone is cleansed and the Stoneheart Enclave can protect them.

He becomes visibly disappointed when the party exposes the gnome leaders' ulterior motives and proposes attacking from the north without undertaking his temple-cleansing mission.
