# Locations — Throne Room

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 047 — Throne Room

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/047-session-the-fall-of-the-pudding-king.md`
- Original heading: `Throne Room`

The Pudding King's throne room, accessed through a hole created by an Earth Elemental. It is the only opening, and the rest of the wall remains blocked. The room contains green slime hazards on the ceiling that can drop on those beneath them. Those directly under a slime are hit automatically; elsewhere the slime must roll to hit, with a DC 10 Dexterity save to avoid being covered. A covered creature must spend an action to clear it or take five damage. The room also contains acidic green pools that do not harm the oozes and in fact feed them. It served as the Pudding King's lair, granting him special lair actions until he was dragged out of it. The lair's boundary is marked by a line on the map. After his death, the slime falls only on those directly beneath it.

### Chapter 048 — Throne Room

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/048-session-the-knucklebone-of-truth.md`
- Original heading: `Throne Room`

The site of the Pudding King's defeat, where an Earth Elemental strained to prevent the ceiling from collapsing. Fungi in the area were observed growing at an accelerated rate after the battle. The hole the elemental held open remains stable without the Pudding King's manipulation. It became the subject of the council's sustainable-mining compromise.
