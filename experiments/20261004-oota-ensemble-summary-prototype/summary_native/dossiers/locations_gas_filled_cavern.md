# Locations — Gas-Filled Cavern

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 032 — Gas-Filled Cavern

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/032-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Gas-Filled Cavern`

A large underground chamber featuring a deep depression where heavy, combustible, and toxic gas has collected. The gas can cause illness or poisoning and could explode if exposed to flame.

Because it is heavier than air, the gas remains concentrated in the lower portion of the cavern. Narrow five-foot ledges along the walls provide the only safe passage around it, and one of these ledges is where the party is ambushed by a rocktopus.
