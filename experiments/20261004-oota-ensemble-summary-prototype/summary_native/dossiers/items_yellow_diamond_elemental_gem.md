# Items — Yellow Diamond Elemental Gem

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 041 — Yellow Diamond Elemental Gem

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/041-session-mirror-mirror-on-the-wall-who-s-the-maddest-of-them-all.md`
- Original heading: `Yellow Diamond Elemental Gem`

A yellow diamond gem found among the remnants of the defeated Earth Elemental. It is a one-time use item that can summon an Earth Elemental, but is cursed by the area's malevolent influence. Any elemental summoned from it would go mad and attack indiscriminately, making it potentially useful as a 'bomb' against enemies.
- Gyrgum added it to the party inventory.
- The GM notes that until the source of the curse is dealt with, even an uncursed stone would produce a maddened elemental here.
