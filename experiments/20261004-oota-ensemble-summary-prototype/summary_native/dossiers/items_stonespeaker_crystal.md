# Items — Stonespeaker Crystal

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 015 — Stonespeaker Crystal

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/015-session-04-14.md`
- Original heading: `Stonespeaker Crystal`

A large, polished crystal the length of a human forearm, gifted to the party by the Stone Giant clan leader Hgraam. It possesses magical properties that allow the wielder to communicate with animals, plants, and the dead, and grants enhanced investigative capabilities.
- It has 10 charges and requires attunement, which is possible after the next long rest.
- Speak with dead costs 4 charges and speak with animals costs 2.
- It grants advantage on Investigation.
- The party has not yet decided who carries it. Daz has the highest INT (17).
