### Chapter 057 — Chapter House

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/057-session-candlekeep-s-cold-case-the-gyrgumite-gambit.md`
- Original heading: `Chapter House`

The executive meeting room of the Exaltation bastion, located under the western gables of its green slanted roof opposite Reader's Tower. Features a distinctive rose window that allows lateral light to stream in and a dodecagonal table with places for the Keeper of Tomes, the First Reader, the eight Great Readers, the Gate Warden, and the Lorekeeper — a council of twelve. The party was officially appointed as investigators here, subject to signing confidentiality papers.
