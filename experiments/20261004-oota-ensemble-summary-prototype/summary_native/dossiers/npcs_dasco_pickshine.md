# NPCs — Dasco Pickshine

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 044 — Dasco Pickshine

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/044-session-from-zuggtmoy-to-entemoch-s-boon.md`
- Original heading: `Dasco Pickshine`

One of the owners of Pickshine Mine, described as a sales-focused gnome concerned primarily with quotas and business numbers. He was initially frustrated by the Galeb Duhr blocking his operations and attempted to force them to move. After the party resolved the situation, he offered them two ruby gems worth 500 gold pieces as a token of appreciation, though he initially tried to avoid payment.
- He was red-faced and shouting "By the contract of the Stoneheart Enclave, move."
- He was left "very confused" and "very intimidated" by Daz's threat to undo the fix, and delivered the rubies through a crowd of miners.

### Chapter 045 — Dasco Pickshine

### Chapter 046 — Dasco Pickshine

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/046-session-zalthir-s-daring-grapple.md`
- Original heading: `Dasco Pickshine`

The overseer of the Pickshine Mines is obsessed with branding and seeing heroic opportunities to increase gross margins. He offers branded tea and personally sews a Dasco Pickshine patch onto Zalthir's clothes. All of his clothing and equipment bears the Dasco Pickshine logo. His miners bridge the black pudding pit using mithral braces and salt neutralizers, but must stay behind to maintain it. He does not know what a sticker is and doesn't mine tea; he buys and packages it. He asks to call Daz "general" and is told to call him Daz.

### Chapter 048 — Dasco Pickshine

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/048-session-the-knucklebone-of-truth.md`
- Original heading: `Dasco Pickshine`

A representative of the Miners Guild who initially pushed for immediate strip mining to fund city repairs before agreeing to a sustainable mining compromise. He shook hands with the labor union over a wage bump, with the details deferred to be discussed "underground." Jimjar later credited the party with getting him "to pay a fair wage for once."
