## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 046 — Area 30 (Pudding King's Court)

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/046-session-zalthir-s-daring-grapple.md`
- Original heading: `Area 30 (Pudding King's Court)`

A grand vaulted corridor of ancient stone leading to the Pudding King's throne room. The entrance is blocked by a colossal ochre jelly that pulses with the Pudding King's heartbeat. The ceiling is covered with patches of green slime that can drop on intruders. The Pudding King holds court here with his two ooze henchmen, Princess Ebonmire and Prince Livid. The passage behind the jelly is open, and the King sits far back from the entrance.
