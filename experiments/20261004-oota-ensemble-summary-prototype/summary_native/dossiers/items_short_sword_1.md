- Original heading: `Short Sword +1`

A magical short sword found by the party in the Traders' Grotto after the battle with gelatinous cubes. It was ultimately taken by Daz as a weapon upgrade.

The party debates whether the short sword counts as a simple or martial weapon and who would benefit most from possessing a magical arm. Daz replaces his quarterstaff with it after determining that it offers improved damage.
