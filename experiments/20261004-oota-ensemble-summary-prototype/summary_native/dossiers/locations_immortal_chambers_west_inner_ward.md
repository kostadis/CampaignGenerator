### Chapter 057 — Immortal Chambers (West Inner Ward)

- Original heading: `Immortal Chambers (West Inner Ward)`

The Immortal Chambers — Candlekeep's theology and religion library in the West Inner Ward — are a series of rectangular buildings with green roofs, featuring a tile floor where each tile is adorned with a different deity's symbol. Curated by A'lai Aivenmore, the complex contains Brother Vareth's side office, where he maintains his decades-long collection of polemics and research on the Question of the Age. Gyrgum noted the danger of choosing which deity tile to step on, and tried to follow in Vareth's exact footsteps.
