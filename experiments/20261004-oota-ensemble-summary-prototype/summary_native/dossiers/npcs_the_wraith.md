# NPCs — The Wraith

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 028 — The Wraith

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/028-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Wraith`

A hateful, hovering undead spirit bound to the lower chamber of the tomb. She cackles upon the party's arrival, declaring their deaths will free her from her prison and that they will serve her in death. She screams about past defeats by drow and dwarves, is resistant to fire, and uses her gilded sarcophagus as a tactical hiding spot. She can fly, took 20 force damage from Daz's third-level Magic Missile, and missed her life-drain attack on Thorin.
