### Chapter 032 — The Majestic Mushroom

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/032-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Majestic Mushroom`

A colossal, hollow fungal structure that fills a massive cavern from floor to ceiling. Its vast central stalk splits into several lesser stalks, each capped by a mushroom broad enough to crown a great tower. Thousands of smaller fungi cling to it, and a carpet of fungi surrounds its base.

Its trunk is carved with slitted windows that emit eerie phosphorescent light. Atonal music echoes from inside, and a stench of rotten decay seems to threaten both body and soul. It serves as the staging ground for Zuggtmoy’s demonic wedding rehearsal and as the place to which her procession withdraws.
