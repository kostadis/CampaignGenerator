# Spells — Hallow

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 039 — Hallow

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Hallow`

A powerful sanctification spell contained within Gurnik Tapfinger's red spell gem.

The spell is intended to cleanse the Temple of the Steadfast Stone. Once the gem is placed in the temple's menhir, servants of Ogrémoch's Bane will attack in an attempt to remove it.

The party must hold the position until three temple guardians awaken. The completed cleansing will restore the sanctuary and allow the Stoneheart Enclave to shield Earth Elementals from the Bane's madness during the battle against the Pudding King.
