# NPCs — Gnome Chef

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 039 — Gnome Chef

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Gnome Chef`

A gnome working in the kitchen or break room within Blingdenstone's bureaucratic hub. He initially assumes the adventurers are the new heroes hired by Chief Dorbo.

Although he agrees to provide them with a snack, he is welcoming to the adventurers. Chief Dorbo becomes annoyed at the intrusion, insists that they leave the kitchen to eat, and directs them to the Foaming Mug, which has tables and chairs suited to the party's larger members.

The chef recognizes Glabbagool's natural cleaning ability and asks the gelatinous cube to clean the kitchen floor. He is pleased when Glabbagool enthusiastically accepts.
