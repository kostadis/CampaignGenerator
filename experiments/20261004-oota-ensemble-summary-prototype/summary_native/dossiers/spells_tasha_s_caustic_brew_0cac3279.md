# Spells — Tasha’s Caustic Brew

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 065 — Tasha’s Caustic Brew

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/065-session-08-03.md`
- Original heading: `Tasha’s Caustic Brew`

*Cast in the previous session (ch64).* Cast by Gyrgum, this concentration spell unleashed a corrosive spray of acid that coated both Zhentarim thugs. One died instantly as the acid melted him; the other remained soaked in the ongoing corrosive substance.

The surviving thug took another eight points of damage at the start of his turn. He could have used an action to scrape the acid away but instead attacked Gyrgum. Concentrating on this spell caused Gyrgum’s earlier *bless* to end.

The table also discovered that the spell’s flavor could be described as Gyrgum “barfing” acid, prompting Gyrgum to consider rereading the description and possibly replacing it.
