# Items — Sapphire Artifact

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 064 — Sapphire Artifact

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/064-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Sapphire Artifact`

A magical gem held by A'lai Aivenmore, who produces it while explaining that Manshoon wants a single book from Candlekeep. A'lai intends to smash the sapphire to trigger an effect associated with the High Tower keys, although the party cannot recall with certainty whether it summons something, teleports a key, or produces some other result.

Before A'lai can break it, Daz uses Telekinesis to wrench it from his grasp. Daz then pretends to retreat with the artifact while secretly transferring it to Gyrgum with a successful sleight-of-hand maneuver. Gyrgum ends the session holding the sapphire, the genuine key #2, and Kalan's fake decoy key, while A'lai believes Daz fled with the gem.
