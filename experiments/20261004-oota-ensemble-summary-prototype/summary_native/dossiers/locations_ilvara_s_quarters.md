# Locations — Ilvara's Quarters

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 002 — Ilvara's Quarters

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Ilvara's Quarters`

Mistress Ilvara's private chamber in the largest hanging tower. The approach crosses a steep rope bridge into a circular shrine to Lolth, whose floor is covered by dark silken mats patterned with pale web strands in silvery thread. A broad zurkhwood pedestal supports a lifelike ten-foot-tall spider idol with eight polished-jet eyes. Heavy silver candlesticks flank the altar, and pillows and cushions fill the shrine's rear semicircle.

A rope ladder descends from the shrine into Ilvara's living quarters, where she keeps a bathtub and a prominent treasure chest. A further passage descends toward Shoor's Quarters. Thorin observed Ilvara and Shoor Vandree here and believes the chest may contain the prisoners' confiscated equipment.
