# Locations — Faerzress Suffused Area

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 028 — Faerzress Suffused Area

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/028-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Faerzress Suffused Area`

A region of the Underdark permeated by Faerzress, featuring fist-sized quartz crystals that emit a dim radiance and can be used as primitive flash grenades. The party harvested crystals here and smashed the rest to disguise their presence. Faerzress hampers teleportation and is tied to "the blink" that drove creatures mad and is hypothesized to have brought the demons. A second, deserted crystal field was found around the sixth day of travel, where the party searched for more.
