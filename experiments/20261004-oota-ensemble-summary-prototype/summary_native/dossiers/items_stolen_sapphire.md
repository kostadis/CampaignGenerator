# Items — Stolen Sapphire

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 060 — Stolen Sapphire

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/060-session-never-mind-the-murders.md`
- Original heading: `Stolen Sapphire`

A gemstone missing from a safe and theorized to be the material focus for an instant-summoning spell. The same sapphire must be used to establish and trigger the link. Crushing or otherwise activating it could summon the associated item—possibly Janussi’s missing key—provided that item was not being possessed by a living creature.

### Chapter 065 — Stolen Sapphire

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/065-session-08-03.md`
- Original heading: `Stolen Sapphire`

A magical artifact that A'lai Aivenmore intended to smash. Doing so would have summoned or recalled one of the High Tower keys to his hand.

Daz cast *telekinesis* to wrench the sapphire from A'lai’s grasp in the previous session, then secretly passed it to Gyrgum. A'lai and the assassin nevertheless believed Daz still had it.

The sapphire is bound to the real High Tower key already held by Gyrgum. Consequently, A'lai’s plan to smash it would not have located the other key; it would simply have recalled Gyrgum’s key. At session end, Gyrgum possesses both that key and its corresponding sapphire.
