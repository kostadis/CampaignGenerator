A towering demon lord, thirty feet tall or more, with two baboon-like heads, wickedly curved tusks, oily tentacles, and red eyes burning with bloodlust and madness. It rose from the Darklake immediately after Bloppblippodd's death and came straight at the Kuo-toa, bringing chaos and destruction to their settlement.

### Chapter 009 — Demogorgon

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/009-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Demogorgon`

The Prince of Demons, a towering, two-headed monstrosity with powerful tentacles. He erupts from the Darklake to bring destruction to the kuo-toa village of Sloobludop, his mere presence causing madness and chaos among all who witness him. Witnessing his rise forces a DC 13 Charisma save against madness, a one-time effect. He comes to slaughter his former worshippers and is distracted by the feasting. Only an avatar has come through, but summoning even that lies far beyond a lone low-level caster.
