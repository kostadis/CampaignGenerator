### Chapter 032 — Araumycos

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/032-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Araumycos`

A massive, sentient fungal growth in the Underdark that serves as the habitat for myconids and is the intended “groom” in Zuggtmoy’s demonic wedding ritual. The party’s combined lore describes it as an epically large fungal expanse that may have existed for centuries or millennia.

Araumycos is not the organism upon which the Neverlight Grove myconids currently live; it is elsewhere in the Underdark. It is powerful enough that Zuggtmoy appears to require extensive ritual preparation before confronting or possessing it. It is not present at the rehearsal, preventing the true ceremony from beginning.
