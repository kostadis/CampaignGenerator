### Chapter 055 — Waterdeep

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/055-session-the-ember-grapple-s-journey-through-the-north.md`
- Original heading: `Waterdeep`

A massive, bustling metropolis where the party spends several days shopping, exploring, and causing philosophical controversy. Notable for a bronze statue in Castle Ward depicting the chess match between Stroud and Protanther rendered in inlaid marble with a brass plaque reading *This is the position. The move is yours*, the bookshop Rishaal's Pageturners, and a Field Ward street preacher shouting prophecies about a kraken. Oozes are not tolerated within the city walls. Described by Gyrgum as being like New Jersey in its expense.
