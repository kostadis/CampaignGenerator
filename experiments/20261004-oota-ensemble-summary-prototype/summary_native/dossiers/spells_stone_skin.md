# Spells — Stone Skin

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 045 — Stone Skin

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/045-session-gnome-more-elemental-slavery.md`
- Original heading: `Stone Skin`

A buff received by the entire party after activating Entémoch's boon; the GM added it as a homebrew reward for this adventure. Once per long rest, each player can use a reaction to turn their skin to stone when hit by an attack, gaining resistance to that attack's damage type for the rest of the round, so it could cover several sword blows in one round. The buff lasts for one week of in-game time. Zalthir volunteered to remind the party of it.

### Chapter 047 — Stone Skin

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/047-session-the-fall-of-the-pudding-king.md`
- Original heading: `Stone Skin`

A temporary magical effect that grants resistance to a chosen damage type for one round, usable once per long rest. It is declared when used. Multiple party members used it to resist acid damage from the Pudding King's slime wave attacks. Zalthir's acid resistance also halved the damage from passing through Glabbagool.
