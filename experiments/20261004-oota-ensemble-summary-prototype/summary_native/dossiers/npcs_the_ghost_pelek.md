# NPCs — The Ghost (Pelek)

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 018 — The Ghost (Pelek)

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/018-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Ghost (Pelek)`

Pelek's ghost, an ethereal companion of the party who cannot interact with physical objects but provides a menacing presence during negotiations with Werz, delivering a well-timed "boo."
- He insists any gold earned on his behalf be deposited in his grave in Blingdenstone. He had Gyrgum, who carries his bones, pick up his obsidian gem for that purpose.
- He cannot haunt objects: "Trust me, I tried."

### Chapter 020 — The Ghost (Pelek)

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/020-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Ghost (Pelek)`

Pelek's ghost, a spectral ally bound to the party via his bones. Its first attempt to possess the Master Thief failed on his saving throw; it attacked enemies with a withering touch, was resistant to non-magical attacks, and ultimately flew across the battlefield to possess the body of the enemy spy. It acts on Gyrgum's turn, was blinded by the darkness like everyone else, dropped to 15 hit points, and answered the cultists' sacrifice threat in a voice Gyrgum's player called "scary."
