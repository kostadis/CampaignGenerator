### Chapter 053 — Living Serpent Head Scourge

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/053-session-out-of-the-dark-and-into-the-darkness.md`
- Original heading: `Living Serpent Head Scourge`

A five-tailed whip whose lashes terminate in living serpent heads. Zuggtmoy's corruption caused it to aerosolize poisonous spores whenever it was used to attack, potentially poisoning its wielder. The party purified it during a long rest with Dawnbringer's reluctant assistance.
