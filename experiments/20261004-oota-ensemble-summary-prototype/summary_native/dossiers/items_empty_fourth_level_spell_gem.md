# Items — Empty Fourth-Level Spell Gem

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 043 — Empty Fourth-Level Spell Gem

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/043-session-harvesting-horror-in-the-underdark.md`
- Original heading: `Empty Fourth-Level Spell Gem`

A powerful spell gem given to the party by Gurnik Tapfinger as a reward. It can be attuned to and imbued with a spell up to fourth level by a magic user. Once imbued, it provides an extra spell slot every other day. The gem can be loaded using a ritual spell without consuming a spell slot, and once loaded, anybody can cast the spell from it. One of the party members attunes to it and imbues it with Mass Healing Word.
- Gyrgum takes the gem. Daz defers until he can cast fourth-level spells.
- Attunement is required to load it but not to cast from it.
- Casting from the gem does not take the ritual's casting time.
- It is not an official item; Gyrgum tracks it in his own item list.
