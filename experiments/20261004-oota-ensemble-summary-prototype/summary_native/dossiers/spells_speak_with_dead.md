# Spells — Speak with Dead

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 057 — Speak with Dead

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/057-session-candlekeep-s-cold-case-the-gyrgumite-gambit.md`
- Original heading: `Speak with Dead`

The spell grants a semblance of life and intelligence to a corpse, allowing it to answer questions. It requires the corpse to still have a mouth and fails if the spell has already been cast on the same corpse within the last ten days. The party wonders whether someone had already cast it, but the GM answers only "perhaps" and notes that Tadric does not know what happened inside the room. Kalan's involvement remains the party's hypothesis, not an established fact. The party also theorizes that Janussi's heart was forcibly removed after death to prevent *Raise Dead*, which requires vital organs to be present. They discuss waiting ten days to attempt *Speak with Dead* themselves, though the corpse will deteriorate significantly by then — prompting Thorin to ask whether any ice is available.
