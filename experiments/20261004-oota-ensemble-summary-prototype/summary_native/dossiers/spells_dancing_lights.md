# Spells — Dancing Lights

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 021 — Dancing Lights

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/021-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Dancing Lights`

Cast by Daz to create four floating orbs of light that hovered and spun slowly around the party for the duration. It created a dramatic 'shock and awe' effect ("Disco") that helped convince the Derro guards they were facing a supremely powerful mage. The lights were retconned into floating embers to match the Ember Vanguard name, and the Derro mistook them for a meteor swarm.

### Chapter 049 — Dancing Lights

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/049-session-a-sign-from-lolth-or-just-a-spider.md`
- Original heading: `Dancing Lights`

Cast by Daz to create a medium, drow-like glowing form of Lolth with a fuzzy lower half above the spider familiar, then letting it fall like a column of light into the spider and blink out. The illusion simulated a divine manifestation to convince Asha Vandree she was receiving a message from her deity, and earned Daz an Inspiration point.

### Chapter 050 — Dancing Lights

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/050-session-fungus-among-us.md`
- Original heading: `Dancing Lights`

Used by Daz to create a dazzling, shrinking light effect that mimicked the form of Lolth being absorbed into his spider familiar and then into his body, as part of the ruse to convince Asha Vandree.
