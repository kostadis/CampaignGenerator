### Chapter 014 — Holy Symbol of Laduguer

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/014-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Holy Symbol of Laduguer`

A religious icon provided to the party by the Keepers of the Flame. Along with the golden badges, it grants the party the legal right to travel beyond the Darklake District of Gracklstugh. It was given alongside gold pins bearing Themberchaud's profile, brought by an acolyte.
