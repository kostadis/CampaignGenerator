# Locations — The Zombie Pit Cavern

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 022 — The Zombie Pit Cavern

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/022-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Zombie Pit Cavern`

A massive, foul-smelling chamber beneath Gracklstugh with a deep central pit full of zombies. The glow of faerzress flows like fog through the space. A lone derro cultist stood on a ledge overlooking the pit, and a derro savant works from the back of the room, about 80 feet away. A passage runs around the pit. The GM noted that entering from the other side of a marked line would have triggered an attack.
