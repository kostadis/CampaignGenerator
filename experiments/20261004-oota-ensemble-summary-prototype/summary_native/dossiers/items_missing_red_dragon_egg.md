# Items — Missing Red Dragon Egg

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 014 — Red Dragon Egg

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/014-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Red Dragon Egg`

A highly valuable and politically sensitive egg stolen by the Gray Ghosts from the Keepers of the Flame. It was intended to hatch a replacement for Themberchaud, and its recovery is the central mission the Keepers have assigned to the party. Themberchaud himself is unaware of its existence.

### Chapter 017 — Red Dragon Egg

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/017-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Red Dragon Egg`

A stolen egg currently held by the Gray Ghosts in their lair near the obelisk. It is being subjected to cruel magical experiments by the derro savant Plinki, who hopes to mutate it into a two-headed mount for Demogorgon, and it bears inscriptions that Gartokkar considers evidence. There are no signs it is about to hatch. Gartokkar Xundorn has tasked the party with retrieving it, and Themberchaud is being kept ignorant of it.

### Chapter 018 — Red Dragon Egg

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/018-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Red Dragon Egg`

A highly valuable and dangerous object, still in derro hands, that the party has been paid to retrieve and must keep secret. During the meeting with Errde Blackskull, Errde notices the egg in the flumph's visions but cannot imagine it is the egg the Keepers of the Flame have lost. The party keeps its true nature secret, with Thorin's blurt roll of 20 holding his tongue.

### Chapter 020 — Missing Red Dragon Egg

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/020-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Missing Red Dragon Egg`

A mysterious and highly coveted object that the Derro cultists believed granted leadership to whoever held it, screaming 'he who has the egg rules.' Gyrgum had been protecting it, Daz secretly snatched it up and concealed it beneath his robes using Misty Step to escape. The party knows it as the red dragon egg stolen from the Keepers of the Flame; the derro call it the egg of Demogorgon. Earlier, Thorin had asked, "does it have two yolks?" The cultists never learned it was gone; the session ended with the party in possession of it.

### Chapter 022 — The Egg

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/022-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Egg`

A mysterious and valuable object in the party's possession. They are trying to carry it safely out of the caverns beneath Gracklstugh, and their stated goal is to escape with it while facing as few encounters as possible. The GM reminded the players that Plinki is the one who had the egg.
