# NPCs — Flumph

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 017 — The Flumph

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/017-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Flumph`

A cheerful floating telepathic creature that feeds on background psionic energy. It collects this energy with a distinctive tendril, acting as a filter feeder rather than harming its sources. It routinely relieves its own distress by psychically sharing unpleasant memories with others, a habit it adopted the party's term "trauma dumping" for. Its home in the tunnels used to be a peaceful place to "chill out and shillax" before the faerzress faltered, and it complains that all the good people have disappeared, leaving no one to unburden itself on. It treats a nearby mind flayer as "a tasty snack" and feeds heavily on a psionic group the party takes to be the Council of Savants. It provided the party with crucial intelligence: the Gray Ghost lair and obelisk, the route and a secret passage to reach it, the Assassin's Guild, and the Council of Savants. It decided to join the party on their mission, and in Gracklstugh it served as living evidence by projecting the egg into Gartokkar's mind.

### Chapter 018 — Flumph

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/018-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Flumph`

A sentient, floating creature accompanying the party.
- It communicates by sharing psychic trauma and changes color based on its emotions. The GM confirmed the colors it shows are literally its emotional colors.
- It has adopted the term "trauma dump" and plans to share it with all the other flumphs in the Underdark.
- By this session, trauma dumping has become "no longer as satisfying," and it asks the party to cause some new trauma — not on it.
- It grows bored with its gemstone and finds the city fascinating but concerning.

### Chapter 019 — Flumph

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/019-session-05-20.md`
- Original heading: `Flumph`

A strange, telepathic Underdark creature accompanying the party. It feeds on psychic energy and expresses dark amusement regarding the party's safety, refusing to enter the Empty-Scabbard Killers' lair because a mind flayer was inside, preferring to wait outside and pick up the party's traumatic vibes. Flumph knows only the routes where there was psychic energy to eat, and was disappointed that no one's brain got devoured: "There's still time."
