# NPCs — Specters

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 028 — Specters

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/028-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Specters`

Four malevolent, incorporeal undead guardians that haunt the sarcophagi chamber of the tomb. They possess a life-draining touch that reduces a victim's maximum hit points until they take a long rest, and they are capable of moving through the air without leaving tracks. Daz determines they likely respawn every twenty-four hours. They have only 22 HP each but are immune to much. They can float away without provoking opportunity attacks, and every one still standing resisted Gyrgum's DC 15 Turn Undead. One indignantly insisted on being called a specter rather than a ghost.
