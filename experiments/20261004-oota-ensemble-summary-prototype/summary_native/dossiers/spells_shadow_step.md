# Spells — Shadow Step

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 038 — Shadow Step

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/038-session-wererats-wisdom-and-the-war-on-ooze.md`
- Original heading: `Shadow Step`

An ability used by Zalthir, allowing him to teleport sixty feet through darkness or dim light. He demonstrates it to the deep gnomes by teleporting around the dimmed chamber, performing flips and punches in the air. He plans to use it to quickly reach the Pudding King, grapple him, and escape.

The ability forms the core of the party’s strategy. If allied forces can distract the ooze army, Zalthir can bypass much of the hazardous terrain, seize the physically unimposing Pudding King, and fly or carry him back to the party. The green slime coating the throne room’s ceiling remains a significant complication.

### Chapter 044 — Shadow Step

### Chapter 056 — Shadow Step

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/056-session-candlekeep-trials-truths-and-therapy.md`
- Original heading: `Shadow Step`

A teleportation ability used by Zalthir during his Trial of the Broken Mirror at Candlekeep. The blind half-elf monk immediately criticized Zalthir's form — "You skip the third breath. You don't enter your ki in the hara. You let it bleed into your fingertips like a poison. It is technically wrong in every scroll" — but then acknowledged Zalthir arrived at his destination three seconds faster than the grandmaster. Zalthir later combined shadow step with a precise strike to smash a thrown brick mid-air (rolling 29 to hit), which the monk declared redeemed his lack of traditional skill in every other area. This combination — shadow step plus precise strike — was previously demonstrated in the Underdark when Zalthir created a precise puncture, and forms the basis for the specialized technique Master Kenshi ultimately teaches him.
