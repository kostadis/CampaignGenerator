# Spells — Bless

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 019 — Bless

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/019-session-05-20.md`
- Original heading: `Bless`

A divine blessing cast by Gyrgum upon his companions just as combat began, bolstering their focus, accuracy, and resilience during the chaotic skirmish at the obelisk. Gyrgum explained it as a d4 bonus on attack rolls and saving throws for the next minute or while he holds concentration. He framed the casting to Plinki as a blessing "in my magnanimity," and Zalthir added the d4 to his second attack.

### Chapter 064 — Bless

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/064-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Bless`

Cast by Gyrgum on “the three of us who are here” as they approach the High Tower, granting each target an additional 1d4 on attack rolls and saving throws. The group briefly forgets to apply it to Gyrgum's opening Guiding Bolt, but the added die is insufficient to turn the miss into a hit. Bless remains active through most of the confrontation but ends when Gyrgum begins concentrating on Tasha's Caustic Brew.
