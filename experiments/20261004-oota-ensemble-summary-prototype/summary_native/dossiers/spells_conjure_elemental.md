### Chapter 039 — Conjure Elemental

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Conjure Elemental`

A spell mentioned as the closest comparison for the effect of the Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals.

The stone summons an Earth Elemental that is friendly to its user and the user's companions for a limited duration. The summoned creature is not understood to remain permanently, though the party hopes it may be possible to befriend a recurring elemental and persuade it to become a willing companion.
