### Chapter 069 — Magma Chamber

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/069-session-one-manshoon-dead-at-least-one-or-two-more-to-go.md`
- Original heading: `Magma Chamber`

A perilous subterranean chamber reached after passing through a shaft containing a permanent magical free-fall effect. A thirty-foot basalt walkway and an extendable mechanical bridge cross a pool of molten magma toward the tower.

A section near the tower is enveloped in a standing anti-magic zone where magical effects are suppressed. The field begins at the marked edge of the bridge and covers the final approach to the basalt platform. Anyone relying on magical flight to cross would lose that protection and fall into the magma.

The chamber became the site of the battle against the first Manshoon simulacrum and the subsequent interrogation of Edvaldo. Zalthir repeatedly used the anti-magic field to neutralize spellcasting and the nearby lava as an implicit threat.
