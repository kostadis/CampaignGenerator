# NPCs — Jadger

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 037 — Burrow Warden Jadger

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/037-session-slime-spirits-and-svirfneblin-squabbles.md`
- Original heading: `Burrow Warden Jadger`

A three-foot-tall ghost of a former protector of Blingdenstone who defended the city against the Drow, oozes, and other Underdark creatures. Jadger is proud, long-winded, and deeply invested in Blingdenstone's survival. He seeks to re-establish the Burrow Wardens and secretly trains promising young gnomes, including one he believes will soon lead the renewed order.

Jadger offers one truthful answer about the Underdark for every ghost the party puts to rest and counts Pelek as their first success. He knows of two more troubled ghosts they could help. Although the party initially fears a trickster-genie arrangement, Jadger insists, “I'm not some trivial genie. I am Burrow Warden Jadger.” He genuinely wants the gnome community to succeed.

Jadger explains that massed oozes are unnatural because the creatures are normally solitary. His analysis of Juiblex's influence is that a demon probably empowered a proxy to organize the infestation rather than controlling every ooze directly. He distinguishes devils, who corrupt through deals, from demons, who grant destructive power to agents capable of causing widespread carnage.

### Chapter 039 — Uth-Jadger

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Uth-Jadger`

A ghostly Burrow Warden who attends the moot. He enthusiastically praises Daz's plan as an example of the technical brilliance once displayed by the Burrow Wardens.

Uth-Jadger identifies the Rockblight as an obstacle to using the Temple of the Steadfast Stone and frames Blingdenstone's unity as essential to victory.

He has spoken with other Burrow Warden spirits and says they will help in the battle if the party handles a quest on their behalf. When the party initially prioritizes the other preparations, he looks disappointed until Glabbagool, Eldeth, and Jimjar are assigned to assist the ghosts.

### Chapter 040 — Burrow Warden Jadger

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/040-session-crystals-ghosts-and-crazed-earth.md`
- Original heading: `Burrow Warden Jadger`

An individual pleased by the decision to address Blingdenstone’s ghost problem. He wished the volunteers well and noted that even if they died, they could still fight in the coming battle as ghosts. His explanation prompted Glabbagool to ask whether he would become the first gelatinous cube ghost. Jadger had previously asked the party to recover specific lost spirits, including Udhask, by finding and returning their remains.

### Chapter 043 — Burrow Warden Jadger

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/043-session-harvesting-horror-in-the-underdark.md`
- Original heading: `Burrow Warden Jadger`

A friendly ghost who materializes to thank the party for putting two ghosts to rest. He offers two questions as boons for their service and mentions he will be training Burrow Wardens in the catacombs at The Ruby in the Rough temple. He had earlier promised his ghosts would aid the fight against the Ooze King once the ghost problem was handled. The GM calls him "Jadger, the friendly ghost," prone to "sanctimonious preaching," and he evaporates after making his offer.
