### Chapter 061 — Sylvira

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/061-session-the-colloquium-was-well-received.md`
- Original heading: `Sylvira`

A tiefling researcher with copper-red skin, magnetic golden eyes, and blue-black hair, currently bedridden with an abyssal plague contracted from her son. She had an intermittent romantic relationship with Janussi and becomes distraught upon learning that someone has used her likeness. She identifies prejudice against tieflings and her infernal-plane research as reasons she would make a convenient scapegoat. She points suspicion toward Daral Yashenti, Kazryn Nyantani, Teles Ahvoste, Kalan Strongbranch, and ultimately Bookwyrm’s opposition to Kalan’s methodology.

### Chapter 067 — Sylvira Savikas

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/067-session-08-17.md`
- Original heading: `Sylvira Savikas`

A powerful and knowledgeable scholar at Candlekeep who arrives at the investigator's office and assists the party in solving the vault's riddles. She provides the true name of Miirym the Sentinel Wyrm and identifies the Eastern Light of Mystra's Mantle, also warning of suspicious figures in the Orrery of the Astronomicon.

Sylvira is weakened by illness but believes it is her responsibility to help Candlekeep during the crisis. She is also Moziqodo's mother and does not yet know that the party killed her son. She warmly thanks Thorin for saving Tadric, prompting a carefully guarded response.
