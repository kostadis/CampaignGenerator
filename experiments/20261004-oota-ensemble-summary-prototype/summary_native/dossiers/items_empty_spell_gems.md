### Chapter 018 — Empty Spell Gems

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/018-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Empty Spell Gems`

Five special gems provided by Werz Saltbaron as a symbol of good faith and valued at 100 GP each. Jimjar identifies them as resembling empty spell gems used in the defense of Blingdenstone. They are worth far more to a gnomish alchemist named Kazook Pickshine than their face value as ordinary gems; otherwise they are "kind of banal gems." How Werz obtained them remains an open question.
