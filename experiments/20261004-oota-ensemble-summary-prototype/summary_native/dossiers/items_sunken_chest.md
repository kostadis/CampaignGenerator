### Chapter 011 — Sunken Chest

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/011-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Sunken Chest`

A small chest found at the bottom of the Darklake among the wreckage of Brannum Redmarch's lost crew boats, retrieved by Thorin using a magical air bubble. It contained 120 silver pieces, 30 gold pieces, four spell scrolls, and a driftglobe. Brannum claimed it came from his crew.
