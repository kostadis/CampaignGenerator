- Original heading: `Kestler's Note`

A written message from Kestler, a half-orc in Triboar, intended for a brother at a southern temple who has not been heard from in five years. The temple fire still burns untended, and Kestler has not seen the brother in three years. The party agrees to deliver it; Gyrgum immediately volunteers, calling it an omen.
