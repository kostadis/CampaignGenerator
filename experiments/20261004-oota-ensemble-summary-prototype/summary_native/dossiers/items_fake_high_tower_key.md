## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 063 — Fake High Tower Key

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `Fake High Tower Key`

A non-magical decoy key given to Gyrgum by Kalan Strongbranch and later transferred to Daz. Kalan intended the visible transfer to make the party a target while keeping the genuine artifact safely hidden.

The fake was confirmed to have no magical properties when examined with Detect Magic, even as every genuine magical object around it glowed. The party retained it after securing Tadric's real key and considered using the pair to mislead their enemies.

### Chapter 065 — Fake High Tower Key

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/065-session-08-03.md`
- Original heading: `Fake High Tower Key`

A plain, non-magical key given to the party by Kalan Strongbranch to serve as a decoy while the real key remained with Tadric. Its lack of magical aura was detected previously, explaining why the party’s attempts to use or analyze it had produced no useful magical response.

Gyrgum still held the fake key at the end of the session, giving the party another possible means of misdirection.
