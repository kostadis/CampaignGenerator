- Original heading: `Leuwin`

An elderly, absent-minded satyr druid who operates the Oak Tree Apothecary. He spends his days distilling tinctures, crushing seeds, and macerating herbs. He provided Fheminor with horehound and licorice, then accompanied A’lai to the greenhouse for fennel, unknowingly leaving his dangerous-toxins shelf accessible to a Dragonborn intruder.
