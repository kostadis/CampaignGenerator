### Chapter 057 — Fembris Lancer

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/057-session-candlekeep-s-cold-case-the-gyrgumite-gambit.md`
- Original heading: `Fembris Lancer`

An enthusiastic, boyish, somewhat out-of-shape young half-elf acolyte, adjutant to the First Reader, who brings the party the news of the Keeper's death and serves as their guide and adjutant throughout the investigation. He led the morning tour of the Candlekeep grounds with great energy, pointing out the Astronomicon, the Whispering Dome, and the Drakonoikos. He arrived at the Chapter House with aching legs. He dismissed the Avowed's reverence for the mysterious gray cat as overblown superstition — until Zalthir's single quiet "Is it?" gave him pause. Upon entering the Keeper's chambers, he took one look at the body, sprinted to the nearest window, flung it open, and vomited outside the tower, thereafter remaining outside. When assigned as the party's adjutant and offering to help with "anything," he hastily qualified his offer upon noticing the composition of the party.

### Chapter 062 — Fembris Lancer

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/062-session-academic-vindication-and-an-assault.md`
- Original heading: `Fembris Lancer`

The party's adjutant, who steps away during the party's private deliberations and returns with Bookwyrm's summons. He remains with the party while they are confined to Deneir's Sanctum, apparently as a punishment.

Fembris becomes an unwitting food taster when the suspicious party lets him eat the delivered meal first. He was also present when Gyrgum handed the mysterious key to Daz. Because the horrors subsequently attacked its actual bearer, Fembris's knowledge of the exchange makes him a person of interest, though the party also considers the possibility that the constructs could detect the key independently.

### Chapter 063 — Fembris Lancer

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `Fembris Lancer`

An acolyte adjutant who served as a food tester for the suspicious party in the sanctum. He was present when Gyrgum transferred the apparent key to Daz and therefore became the party's primary suspect for leaking its location.

Fembris was paralyzed with fear after Bookwyrm's murder. Under pressure, he admitted that A'lai Aivenmore was present when he reported the key transfer to Bookwyrm. This explained how A'lai could have learned whom to target and shifted suspicion away from Bookwyrm as the sole architect of the helmed-horror attack. Thorin warned Fembris not to disclose Tadric's location, telling him, “Loose lips sink ships, dumbass.”
