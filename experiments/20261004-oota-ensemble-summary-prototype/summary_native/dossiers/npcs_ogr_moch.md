# NPCs — Ogrémoch

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 042 — Ogrémoch

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/042-session-zalthir-s-reflection-thorin-s-stand.md`
- Original heading: `Ogrémoch`

A powerful entity from the elemental plane of earth whose spirit, referred to as Ogrémoch's Bane, has infected the temple. It causes Earth Elementals to go insane and keeps the Galeb Duhr from their duty as guardians. Defeating the Earth Elementals weakens Ogrémoch's control over the temple. The GM described Ogrémoch as having chosen to linger in a room "full of drow statues."
