# Spells — Wall of Force

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 065 — Wall of Force

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/065-session-08-03.md`
- Original heading: `Wall of Force`

Conjured by Manshoon’s simulacrum upon his arrival to facilitate his breach of the High Tower’s inner sanctum, shattering the magical door that the High Tower keys were meant to protect.

The breach required enormous magical expenditure. Manshoon’s simulacrum arrived alone and depleted, unable to bring the larger force that would otherwise have accompanied him.
