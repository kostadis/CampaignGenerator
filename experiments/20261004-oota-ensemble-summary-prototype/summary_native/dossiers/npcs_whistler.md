# NPCs — Whistler

### Chapter 055 — Whistler

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/055-session-the-ember-grapple-s-journey-through-the-north.md`
- Original heading: `Whistler`

A Kenku hero from the War of the Dragons whose redemption arc and supposed death at Glazhael's lair are subjects of intense public debate. Some factions have declared him fallen, with a sign in Waterdeep reading *Whistler, Kenku of the First Faction, fallen at Glazhael's lair, the year of the Alliance, redeemed in life and death. May his silence speak.* Fans are split between the "Whistlerites" — who debate whether Milo's account of his morally confused post-lycanthropy stage is too generous — and the "Miloites," who argue that the "operational expenses, cost of living" framing represents a philosophical breakthrough about the impossibility of pure heroism. The rumors of the Ardragon (a black-hooded Kenku on the caravan trails who takes nothing and mimics a hawk) circulate separately from the literary debates, though the party recognizes the connection.

### Chapter 055 — Ardragon

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/055-session-the-ember-grapple-s-journey-through-the-north.md`
- Original heading: `Ardragon`

A figure of legend discussed by caravan drovers in Triboar and beyond. Described as a Kenku hooded in black who hovers a foot off the ground, appears at a distance on caravan trails north of Yartar, mimics a hawk, takes nothing, and vanishes before anyone can approach. One drover offers the history: formerly known as the Ardragon, he flew routes for the Black Network and used to steal anything not nailed down — then "something happened out east," and now he stops banditry, takes nothing, doesn't speak, mimics a hawk, and rides the next thermal. Locals leave a coin on a rock for him, which he never picks up.
