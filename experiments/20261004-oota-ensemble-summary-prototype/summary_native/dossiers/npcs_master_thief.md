## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 020 — Master Thief

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/020-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Master Thief`

A skilled derro rogue (Uskvil, per the GM's ruling; the name is not said at the table) who fought fiercely against the party in the darkness. He was repeatedly grappled by Zalthir, locked down by Thorin's sentinel ability, and ultimately slain by Zalthir. The GM framed him as roughly a fifth-level rogue; he was twice dropped off the ledge, still had about 30 hit points mid-fight, and repeatedly attacked the ghost.
