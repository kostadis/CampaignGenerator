### Chapter 063 — Detect Magic

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `Detect Magic`

Cast by Daral using a scroll to examine the key held by the party. The spell caused all magical equipment in the room — including armor and Dawnbringer — to glow brightly, while the key remained stubbornly inert. Daral immediately concluded that it was not one of the magical keys to the high tower.
