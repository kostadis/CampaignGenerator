### Chapter 063 — North Galleries

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `North Galleries`

A section of Candlekeep away from the main barracks where Kalan had hidden Tadric to protect the real key. Tadric had not been told what he carried or what might be coming for it. The area was accessed through a barred shortcut opened by a Watcher's stair key and featured a domed rotunda at the top, which became the site of the confrontation with Moziqodo.
