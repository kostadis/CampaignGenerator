# Spells — Divine Spark

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 037 — Divine Spark

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/037-session-slime-spirits-and-svirfneblin-squabbles.md`
- Original heading: `Divine Spark`

A holy ability used by Gyrgum that channels divine energy through his holy symbol to inflict necrotic damage upon a target. The target must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw, taking full damage on a failure and reduced damage on a success. Gyrgum uses it twice against the cubes: the first target resists the worst of the effect but still suffers damage, while the second fails and takes the full necrotic force.
