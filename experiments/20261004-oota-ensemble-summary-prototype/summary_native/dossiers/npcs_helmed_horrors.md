# NPCs — Helmed Horrors

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 062 — Helmed Horrors

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/062-session-academic-vindication-and-an-assault.md`
- Original heading: `Helmed Horrors`

Three mindless animated suits of plate armor with glowing eyes, capable of walking, flight, and hovering. They possess formidable armor, general resistance against magic, and immunity to three spells selected by their creator.

They smash into Deneir's Sanctum and pursue whoever carries the mysterious key, first converging on Daz and then redirecting toward Gyrgum after the handoff. Their target selection demonstrates purpose rather than emotion. They cannot speak or identify their master and collapse into piles of rust and ruined armor when destroyed.

### Chapter 063 — Helmed Horrors

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `Helmed Horrors`

Three towering animated suits of plate armor with cold, glowing eyes that smashed through the windows and door of Deneir's Sanctum. They ignored most of the party and converged almost entirely on Daz, seeking the key he carried. Though mindless, they somehow knew the key's precise holder despite the object having no detectable magical aura. The party destroyed all three, reducing them to rust and broken metal.
