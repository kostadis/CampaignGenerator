### Chapter 051 — Lightning Bolt

- Original heading: `Lightning Bolt`

Used by both the House T'sarran mage — who fired it blindly into the darkness and missed everyone — and by Kaelira Duskryn, who directed it at Jorlan Duskryn during the final stages of the battle.

The first bolt streaked harmlessly through the cavern. The second struck Jorlan after Nym authorized an attack against her brother; Jorlan made his saving throw and suffered fourteen damage.
