# Spells — Gyrgum’s heal on Daz (spell not named at the table)

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 065 — Gyrgum’s heal on Daz (spell not named at the table)

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/065-session-08-03.md`
- Original heading: `Gyrgum’s heal on Daz (spell not named at the table)`

After the Zhentarim assassin’s brutal assault, Gyrgum brought Daz back from the brink of death with a more potent restorative spell. Daz had one successful death save and one failed save at the time.

The table did not name the spell. Gyrgum asked Daz’s hit-point maximum—fifty-six—before rolling and restored forty hit points.
