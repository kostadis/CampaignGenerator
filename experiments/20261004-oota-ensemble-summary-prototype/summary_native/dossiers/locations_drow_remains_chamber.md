# Locations — Drow Remains Chamber

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 034 — Drow Remains Chamber

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/034-session-the-wet-bet-and-the-wondrous-cube.md`
- Original heading: `Drow Remains Chamber`

An area containing the remains of a drow, a dark metal mace, and scattered coins, all apparently suspended above the floor. The objects are actually held within Glabbagool’s transparent body.

This is where the party first meets the sentient gelatinous cube. Glabbagool explains that the drow is almost fully digested and that, until recently, he had been an ordinary nonsentient cleaner and scavenger.
