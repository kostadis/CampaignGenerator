## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 068 — Dust of Mechanus — NOT obtained

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/068-session-08-24.md`
- Original heading: `Dust of Mechanus — NOT obtained`

A perfect lubricant from the plane of Mechanus, required by the cryptogram’s instructions to sprinkle on dormant gears as part of the sequence to open the vault. **The party never obtained the dust.** Spanner would only yield it by the death of a modron, which the party refused; what they came away with was the mechanism—the modron tools.

*GM note:* killing a modron was rejected as a design choice, so the tools are the sanctioned route to the dust. **Whether the tools by themselves satisfy riddle line 4, or still have to be used to make the dust, was never ruled on tape and is open.** The tools are what the party is carrying, and they successfully operated the dormant bridge mechanism.
