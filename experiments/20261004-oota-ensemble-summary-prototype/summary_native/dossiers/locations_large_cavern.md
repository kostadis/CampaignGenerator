### Chapter 034 — Large Cavern

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/034-session-the-wet-bet-and-the-wondrous-cube.md`
- Original heading: `Large Cavern`

A vast underground space reached by swimming approximately 30 feet upward through the breached river passage. It provides breathable air, safety from the flooding Oozing Temple, and a new point from which the party can continue toward Blingdenstone.

Jimjar identifies the cavern’s position as evidence that his alleged navigational error actually produced a shortcut.
