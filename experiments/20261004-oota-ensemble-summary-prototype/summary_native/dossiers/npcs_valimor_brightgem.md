# NPCs — Valimor Brightgem

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 045 — Valimor Brightgem

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/045-session-gnome-more-elemental-slavery.md`
- Original heading: `Valimor Brightgem`

A renowned gnome blacksmith in Blingdenstone who runs The Gilded Sheath, famous throughout the Underdark for his distinctive and polished scabbards. He leverages the presence of gem mines in his craft and travels for business. He steered Thorin toward the practical locking scabbard. He becomes excited by Thorin's marketing ideas about product numbering and platform-based business models ("opened my eyes to the possibility of marketing and financial management"), though he was offended by the titanium model. He offers the party a generous discount on a Vaultmaster locking scabbard for Dawnbringer.
