# NPCs — A’lai Aivenmore

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 060 — A’lai Aivenmore

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/060-session-never-mind-the-murders.md`
- Original heading: `A’lai Aivenmore`

A Great Reader and Daral’s political rival. Daral believes A’lai wants to become Keeper of Tomes and regards Daral’s advocacy of poetry and fiction as an obstacle. On the day of the murder, A’lai visited the apothecary for fennel and drew Leuwin into the greenhouse while a Dragonborn entered the pharmacy. His dove cage, sized for five messenger doves, held only two, suggesting that three had recently been sent with messages.
