### Chapter 040 — Cultivation Cave

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/040-session-crystals-ghosts-and-crazed-earth.md`
- Original heading: `Cultivation Cave`

A massive cavern complex managed by the Stoneheart Enclave and devoted to growing useful fungi for deep gnome cooking and crafting. A blocked route through its dirt and gravel provided an alternative entrance to the Rockblight. Glabbagool excavated the passage by plowing through debris and eating mushrooms along the way.
