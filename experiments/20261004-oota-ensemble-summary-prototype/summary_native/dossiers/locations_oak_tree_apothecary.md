### Chapter 060 — Oak Tree Apothecary

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/060-session-never-mind-the-murders.md`
- Original heading: `Oak Tree Apothecary`

A wooden treehouse built into the boughs of a huge evergreen oak west of the Great Library, between the Sea Warden’s Tower and the House of Mechanus. Its main chamber is a pharmacy filled with medicines, ointments, balms, seeds, herbs, and tinctures. A rear exit leads to the greenhouse below, where Leuwin grows herbs year-round with magic. A broken vial from the dangerous-toxins shelf was found concealed beneath a peppermint-scented rag.
