# NPCs — Brannum Redmarch

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 011 — Brannum Redmarch

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/011-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Brannum Redmarch`

A duergar who attempted to enslave the party but was captured. He and his crew had spotted the party's boat and taken them for "easy pickings." He has very low Charisma, which makes his lies easy to read. He serves as a reluctant navigator to Gracklstugh under the constant threat of death, and is revealed to have lost his crew to the dangers of the Darklake shortcut he chose. He tries to claim a share of the sunken chest and offers to grow to giant size to help row. He may be ransomed in Gracklstugh.

### Chapter 012 — Brannum Redmarch

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/012-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Brannum Redmarch`

A duergar captive found on a boat who had previously plotted against the party. He bargains for his freedom by offering to use his connections to act as a middleman for the party to acquire uncommon magical items from restricted parts of the city.
- Fears being turned over to the duergar. He asks whether helping with "this one thing" would clear the debt, and insists the arrangement is "not under duress."
- Protests Hemeth's competing offer: "I thought we had a deal."
