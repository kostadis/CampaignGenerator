# NPCs — Naomi Pathshutter

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 043 — Naomi Pathshutter

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/043-session-harvesting-horror-in-the-underdark.md`
- Original heading: `Naomi Pathshutter`

An exhausted deep gnome wizard who has been up for 48 hours trying to summon earth elementals. She is frustrated that hallowing Ogrémoch's altar did not open the path to Entémoch's boon. She initially mistakes Gyrgum for an ogre and glares at Daz for being a drow, leading to an awkward moment, but becomes impressed when learning the party dealt with four insane earth elementals and a Medusa. She tasks the party with investigating strange vibrations in the tunnels. She is sharp-tongued ("Clean your ears") and blames the cleric whose theory failed. The one elemental she summoned went insane on her, and once impressed she blames everyone but herself for not being told sooner.
