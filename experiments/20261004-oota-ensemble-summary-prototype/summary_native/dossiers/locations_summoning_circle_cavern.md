# Locations — Summoning Circle Cavern

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 044 — Summoning Circle Cavern

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/044-session-from-zuggtmoy-to-entemoch-s-boon.md`
- Original heading: `Summoning Circle Cavern`

A large cavern within the Whiteshell Mine containing a summoning circle with runes written in giant script. Two mechanical-looking basilisks guard this location, which is the site of Entémoch's boon. The runes require sacrifices of wealth, a weapon, and blood to free Entémoch. The runes glow yellow at rest and turned blinding orange, with the air growing hot, after the weapon sacrifice.
