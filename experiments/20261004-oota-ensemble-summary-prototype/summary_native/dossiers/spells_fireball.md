### Chapter 065 — Fireball

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/065-session-08-03.md`
- Original heading: `Fireball`

Cast by Daz after using Misty Step to reposition on the High Tower walkway. The spell dealt twenty-five points of fire damage; the acid-wounded thug made his saving throw and survived by exactly one hit point.

Its successful detonation was more consequential than its damage. Candlekeep’s legendary magical wards should have suppressed such magic, so the blast announced the defensive failure to the entire fortress. Screams and bells followed immediately, while the Avowed began recording the unprecedented event.
