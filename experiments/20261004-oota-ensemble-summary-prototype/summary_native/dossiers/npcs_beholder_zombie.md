# NPCs — Beholder Zombie

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 011 — Beholder Zombie

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/011-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Beholder Zombie`

A ten-foot diameter floating undead sphere that lurked near the limestone islet. It ambushed the party from behind a large stalactite, emitting paralyzing and poisonous rays before being destroyed by a coordinated crossbow volley from the companion crew. It does not understand Common, is immune to being knocked prone, and bit Zalthir for 16 damage.
