### Chapter 002 — Ilvara's Treasure Chest

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Ilvara's Treasure Chest`

A secure chest in the high priestess's private chamber. Thorin observed it while filling Ilvara's bathtub. Based on drow customs that award captured treasure to the highest-ranking leader—and the fact that no guards have been seen using the prisoners' possessions—the party believes the chest probably contains their confiscated weapons and equipment.
