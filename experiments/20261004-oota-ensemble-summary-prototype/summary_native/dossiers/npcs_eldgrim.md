# NPCs — Eldgrim

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 019 — Eldgrim

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/019-session-05-20.md`
- Original heading: `Eldgrim`

A powerful duergar soulblade and leader within the Empty-Scabbard Killers. He is pragmatic and business-minded, willing to negotiate deals for the right price or service. He proves susceptible to flattery regarding his fame, which allows Daz to orchestrate a successful distraction and theft from his desk. The DM notes he is "tougher than your average Soul Blade" and is backed by several more soulblades. He took a liking to Thorin's bluntness, was dismissive of any "proof" the party might carry away, and after the fire banished the party on pain of death.
