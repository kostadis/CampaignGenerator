### Chapter 025 — Zuggtmoy

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/025-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Zuggtmoy`

The Demon Queen of Fungi, also known as the Dark Lady and Lady of Rot and Decay. She appeared as a spectral figure shifting between beauty and decay within a spore-induced vision, offering Thorin and Gyrgum a corruptive gift to join her endless mycelium forever.

### Chapter 032 — Zuggtmoy

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/032-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Zuggtmoy`

The Demon Queen of Fungi and Lady of Rot. She leads a disturbing bridal procession as part of a ritual to mate with Araumycos, moving through the grove as if she owns it and ignoring lesser beings entirely. Her behavior evokes a monarch whose subjects dare not look directly at her.

Zuggtmoy embodies the death and virulence represented by fungi but rejects decomposition’s natural role in renewal and rebirth. She seeks destruction for its own sake and is preparing to take on, mate with, or possess Araumycos through a ritual of incomprehensible power. A vision reveals her as a towering humanoid figure made entirely of fungi and mold, floating inside a great tower while fungal bridesmaids weave her a gown of lichen and mycelium.

Her power is completely beyond the party’s current abilities. A direct confrontation would almost certainly destroy them.
