### Chapter 018 — Obsidian Gemstones

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/018-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Obsidian Gemstones`

Small black gems worth ten gold pieces each, given by Werz Saltbaron to each member of the party, including the flumph and the ghost, as an initial token of gratitude for saving his life. The flumph plays with one using its tentacles before dropping it, and Daz picks it up. The ghost's gem goes into Gyrgum's bag, to be deposited in the ghost's grave in Blingdenstone.
