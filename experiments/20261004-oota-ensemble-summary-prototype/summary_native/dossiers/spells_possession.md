### Chapter 020 — Possession

The party's ghost ally used its supernatural essence to inhabit and take control of the spy's body after flying across the battlefield in response to Daz's cry for help, incapacitating the spy and effectively adding him to the party's side. DC 13 Charisma save. An earlier attempt against the Master Thief failed, and the ability had to recharge. The possession lasts until the host dies or the ghost leaves, and the possessed spy's eyes periodically glow.
