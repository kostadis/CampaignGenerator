# Items — Ilvara's Mushroom

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 050 — Ilvara's Mushroom

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/050-session-fungus-among-us.md`
- Original heading: `Ilvara's Mushroom`

A small, corrupted mushroom carried by Ilvara since her visit to the Neverlight Grove. It serves as a conduit for Zuggtmoy's control and must be destroyed to break Zuggtmoy's influence over Ilvara. If it is left intact, someone else could pick it up and restore that influence.

It is located near Ilvara and is targeted by Daz's fireball at the end of the session, taking 12 damage. Thorin and Gyrgum were warned to keep away from it.
