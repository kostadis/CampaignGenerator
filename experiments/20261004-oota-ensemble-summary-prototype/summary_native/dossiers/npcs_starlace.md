# NPCs — Starlace

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 045 — Starlace

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/045-session-gnome-more-elemental-slavery.md`
- Original heading: `Starlace`

A traveling magic merchant who sets up a store called Starlace Curios and Arcana in Blingdenstone's market. She offers various magical items, enchantments, potions, and services including magic item recharging and minor gear enchantment. She had no scabbards but pointed the party elsewhere in the market. She can enchant at most three items because of limited supplies. She is willing to buy magic items from adventurers and engages in negotiations over pricing. She praised the studded leather +2 as "a fantastic piece of armor" and muttered after the sale in a way that left it unclear who she thought the sucker was.
