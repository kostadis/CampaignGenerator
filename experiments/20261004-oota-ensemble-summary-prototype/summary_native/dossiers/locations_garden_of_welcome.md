A restricted area within Neverlight Grove where Sovereign Phylo takes guests. A previous group of travelers disappeared there and were never seen again. Basidia suspects it is the source of the corrupting spores and fears entering it herself. Phylo intends to bring the party there tomorrow.

### Chapter 031 — Garden of Welcome

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Garden of Welcome`

A horrifying high plateau in Neverlight Grove screened by zurkhwood stalks. Victims are buried alive in the soil to be consumed by mold and fungal growths, and it serves as a site for grotesque rituals tied to Zuggtmoy's wedding. The garden contains victims from numerous Underdark races, along with corpses that Yestabrod can detonate or consume. It can be reached covertly through a secret passage known to Rumpadump.
