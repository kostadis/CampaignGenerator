### Chapter 037 — Eldritch Claw Tattoo

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/037-session-slime-spirits-and-svirfneblin-squabbles.md`
- Original heading: `Eldritch Claw Tattoo`

A magical tattoo worn by Zalthir that causes tentacle-like claws to extend from his hands, allowing him to make melee attacks at range. Its power recharges at dawn, leading Zalthir to determine how soon dawn would occur despite the party being underground. When activated, the tentacles resemble pseudopods, prompting Glabbagool to ask whether Zalthir is an ooze. Zalthir uses the tattoo to destroy one cube, damage another, and deliver the final blow from a distance.
