### Chapter 067 — Vault Cryptogram

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/067-session-08-17.md`
- Original heading: `Vault Cryptogram`

A letter-substitution cipher containing the instructions needed to access the Vault of Secrets beneath Candlekeep. Two answers were supplied by A'lai Aivenmore and two by Sylvira Savikas. Thorin tracked the substitutions using an online-style cryptogram aid while Gyrgum independently verified much of the solution. Kalan Strongbranch arrived only after it was solved.

Its decoded instructions direct the Reader to feed Alaundo's quill, descend the number of steps matching his lifespan, recite his original prophecy, attend to dormant gears with Dust of Mechanus, satisfy a final guardian of knowledge, and pass through an obsidian door.
