### Chapter 008 — Hemeth

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/008-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Hemeth`

A bruised and bedraggled Duergar arms dealer held captive by the Kuo-toa and prepared for sacrifice. He was aghast that the party seemed willing to "show up here and die." He whispered to Gyrgum to tell him when to act and to look him up in Gracklstugh if they survived. He explained that he had tried to close a deal with one of the two factions and picked the wrong one, protesting that he was "just a neutral arms dealer."

### Chapter 009 — Hemeth

- Original heading: `Hemeth`

A duergar arms dealer who flees with the party toward the Darklake. He harbors a deep distrust of kuo-toa due to past experiences of being captured, urging the party to avoid diplomacy with the refugees they encounter on the water. He claims to have connections in Gracklstugh ("I know people"). He sees the refugees' boat as insurance: "If our boat sinks, we're screwed."

### Chapter 012 — Hemeth

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/012-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Hemeth`

A duergar arms dealer who was previously saved by the party. He provides information about Gracklstugh's culture and laws, and offers the party a small discount on his high-quality weapons as a token of gratitude. He is easily offended by competition and boasts about the superior craftsmanship of his suppliers.
- Willing to keep quiet about the party's origins.
- Offended that the party bought from "those crusty old fools," he offers "at least a 2% discount" for saving his life.
- Can source a +1 weapon himself.
- Leaves with Brannum on an errand as the bazaar strangeness escalates.
