# NPCs — Basilisk

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 044 — Basilisk

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/044-session-from-zuggtmoy-to-entemoch-s-boon.md`
- Original heading: `Basilisk`

Two creatures guarding the summoning circle in the Whiteshell Mine, moving with mechanical precision rather than like natural animals. They possess a petrifying gaze requiring a DC 12 Constitution save and appear to be under some form of control, viewing the party as 'a flaw in the perfect stone'. One was grappled by Zalthir while the other was charmed and incapacitated by Daz.
- The gaze is a 30-foot cone.
- Gyrgum dubbed them "Clockwork basilisk."
- When the dart was sacrificed, one became frantic while the other stayed "chilling."

### Chapter 045 — Basilisk

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/045-session-gnome-more-elemental-slavery.md`
- Original heading: `Basilisk`

Two reptilian creatures with petrifying gazes encountered by the party. One was charmed by Hypnotic Pattern and remained immobile throughout the encounter; it got no saving throw, and the party left it untouched once they learned damage would wake it. The other lurked inside Zalthir's magical darkness. It bit at Daz and missed, and was defeated by Zalthir through repeated punches, dying "in a burning gusto of flame."
