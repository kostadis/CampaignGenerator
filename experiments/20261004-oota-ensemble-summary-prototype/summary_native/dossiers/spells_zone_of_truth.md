## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 061 — Zone of Truth

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/061-session-the-colloquium-was-well-received.md`
- Original heading: `Zone of Truth`

Cast by Kalan Strongbranch during Alkrist’s interrogation. Alkrist fails to resist it and cannot knowingly speak deliberate lies. He struggles against the compulsion, attempts evasions, and eventually confesses the full mechanics of the poisoning while claiming that the truthful testimony amounts to entrapment or mind control.

### Chapter 062 — Zone of Truth

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/062-session-academic-vindication-and-an-assault.md`
- Original heading: `Zone of Truth`

Previously used to compel Alkrist's confession. Kalan cast it without Alkrist realizing what was happening until it was too late.

The party considers using the spell against Bookwyrm. A target can resist it with a saving throw, but the caster knows whether the spell has taken hold and therefore whether the target remains capable of lying. Kalan refuses to use the same concealed tactic against Bookwyrm and instead intends to demand her voluntary submission, either immediately or after a new leader is selected.
