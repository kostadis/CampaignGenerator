# Locations — Bookwyrm's Office

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 062 — Bookwyrm's Office

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/062-session-academic-vindication-and-an-assault.md`
- Original heading: `Bookwyrm's Office`

A regal space where the First Reader receives guests and conducts official business. Bookwyrm pours tea and invites the party to sit during the tense presentation of the evidence against Alkrist. Thorin refuses the tea in favor of his own flask, while Zalthir secretly disposes of his through Glabbagool.

The office is also where Bookwyrm publicly acknowledges Kalan's methodology, restores him to his former position, and announces that the party will remain under protection until the naming ceremony.

### Chapter 063 — Bookwyrm's Office

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `Bookwyrm's Office`

The site of two significant events: first, a tense confrontation where Kalan was reinstated after evidence was presented; later, the scene of Bookwyrm's gruesome murder. Her throat was torn out with claws and teeth, considerable blood covered the room, and a half-finished note reading *He is using the beast to—* was found on her desk. The killing demonstrated that Bookwyrm's confidence in the safety of her tower was disastrously misplaced.
