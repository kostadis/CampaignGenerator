### Chapter 037 — Catacombs

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/037-session-slime-spirits-and-svirfneblin-squabbles.md`
- Original heading: `Catacombs`

The cemetery lies beside the Temple of the Ruby in the Rough, with underground burial chambers below the temple. The catacombs contain sealed tombs, lights, earthen spaces suitable for burial, and a large skull decoration at the north end. Gyrgum interred Pelek's small collection of bones in a quiet corner here. Moments after Pelek passed on, Burrow Warden Jadger appeared and offered the party knowledge in return for helping other ghosts.
