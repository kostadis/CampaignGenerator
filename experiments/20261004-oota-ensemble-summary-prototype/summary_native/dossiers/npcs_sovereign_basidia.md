# NPCs — Sovereign Basidia

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 029 — Sovereign Basidia

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/029-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Sovereign Basidia`

The co-sovereign of Neverlight Grove and a voice of reason within the colony. She is deeply suspicious of Phylo's recent behavior and the new Circle of Masters, and fears the grove is being corrupted by unknown spores. She tasked the party with investigating the Garden of Welcome, provided them a scroll of protection against fiends, and offered to craft potions as a reward.
- She says she and Phylo "were such good friends."
- She promised to vouch for the party throughout the grove, everywhere except the Garden of Welcome.
- She endorsed the plan to reanimate the grick as an ally.

### Chapter 030 — Basidia

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/030-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Basidia`

A Myconid Sovereign who would reanimate the Grick Alpha that Loobamub of the Circle of Hunters had asked the party to hunt (chapter 29). She expressed mild disappointment at the state of the hide but accepted the remains, musing that it "will be an interesting transformation." She reminded the party of her promised magic and treasure for exploring the Circle of Masters, lectured on the circles during their rest, and guided them toward the Circle of Builders.

### Chapter 031 — Basidia

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Basidia`

A myconid sovereign who guides the party through the grove's circles, communicating through rapport spores. She is deeply concerned about the corruption spreading through the colony and the changes imposed by Phylo. Basidia supports broad communal participation in myconid society and fears that Phylo's restructuring will irreparably damage its traditional organization. She is not especially subtle when investigating, openly prompting the party to ask Hebopbe what he thinks of Phylo. She trusts Rumpadump enough to involve him in plans for investigating or escaping the grove.

### Chapter 032 — Basidia

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/032-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Basidia`

A Myconid Sovereign who is horrified by the party’s discoveries but initially insists that Phylo must be confronted. She publicly accuses her co-sovereign of betraying the circle and nearly commits herself to a hopeless battle against him.

Zalthir physically pulls her away before she can attack. Seeing Stool, Rumpadump, Rasharoo, and the other survivors departing persuades her that everyone she cares about is leaving and that remaining would only mean death.

Basidia apologizes for failing to complete the party’s pet grick and promises to make additional potions when time permits. She gives the party potions of greater healing and a box of treasure for saving the members of her circle who can still be spared.
