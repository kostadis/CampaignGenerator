# NPCs — Bookwyrm (First Reader)

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 054 — The First Reader

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/054-session-04-28.md`
- Original heading: `The First Reader`

The head of the Avowed at Candlekeep. She is an enthusiastic collector of rare texts, particularly those concerning drow imprisonment, and receives the Velkynvelve Prisoner Journal with barely contained excitement, calling it "a masterpiece of interesting value" that will "go great with my other editions in drow prison journals." She efficiently directs the party to the appropriate scholars for their questions.

### Chapter 056 — Bookwyrm

### Chapter 057 — Bookwyrm

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/057-session-candlekeep-s-cold-case-the-gyrgumite-gambit.md`
- Original heading: `Bookwyrm`

A green dragonborn and the First Reader of Candlekeep. She appeared shaken and weary following the murder but took firm charge of the chaotic council meeting, silencing the arguing council members with a sharp tap on the table and a repeated "FOCUS, PEOPLE!" She proposed delegating the investigation to the party to ensure impartiality, agreed to the copy-of-a-book reward after negotiation, publicly stripped Kalan Strongbranch of the investigation, personally escorted the party to the crime scene, and stepped carefully around the mysterious gray cat on the stairs. She deflected questions about what specific negligence Kalan had been accused of, and when asked where she herself was the night of the murder, she arrived at the crime scene door before answering. She instructed the party to remove nothing from the room and report back when done.

### Chapter 059 — Bookwyrm

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/059-session-questions-answers-and-a-key.md`
- Original heading: `Bookwyrm`

The First Reader of Candlekeep who tasked the party with the investigation. She officially removed Kalan from the case, which has made both Kalan and the party suspicious of her motives. She assigned Deneir's Sanctum as the party's base of operations and provided Fembris and Vooshadi Moonriver as support. During the debrief she probed for specific suspects ("was there nobody that you wished to start your investigation with?") but the party declined to share. She had a marked preference for the party to speak with Sylvira Savikas, though she never named her directly. Daral Yashenti named her as a potential suspect with the aside: "there's no telling what's going on in her lizard brain."

### Chapter 061 — Bookwyrm

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/061-session-the-colloquium-was-well-received.md`
- Original heading: `Bookwyrm`

Alkrist’s beloved aunt and a high-ranking Candlekeep scholar who believed she deserved to succeed Janussi. Janussi told her directly that she would not become Keeper of Tomes, leaving her distraught and betrayed. Shortly after midnight, Alkrist visited with wine and announced that Janussi had been poisoned. Bookwyrm reacted with horror, argued with him, and stormed away. She later withheld this information from the investigators and had previously pushed hardest against Kalan’s investigative reforms, making her a central suspect in the post-mortem mutilation and cover-up.

### Chapter 062 — Bookwyrm

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/062-session-academic-vindication-and-an-assault.md`
- Original heading: `Bookwyrm`

The current First Reader and acting head of Candlekeep—real name Skoda Vanaster, a green dragonborn—and Alkrist's aunt. She receives the party with regal composure and tea, then attempts to discredit Alkrist's confession by invoking the weaknesses identified in Kalan's own paper.

When Kalan presents the poison, book, and confession together, Bookwyrm performs heartbreak but is observed by Daz to be curiously relieved. She reacts theatrically to the possibility of accomplices and repeatedly calls Janussi beloved. She does not possess the sapphire sought through Locate Object.

Bookwyrm reinstates Kalan, promises the party a hearing before the naming ceremony, and places them under close protection in Deneir's Sanctum. Her concern for their safety is sincere, but she is also pleased to prevent them from pursuing further inquiries.

### Chapter 063 — Bookwyrm

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `Bookwyrm`

A high-ranking eighteenth-level magic user of the Avowed who was suspected of covering up Janussi's murder. During the party's confrontation with her, she performed grief and outrage convincingly, though Daz perceived relief beneath the surface.

Bookwyrm had entered Janussi's room after his murder, knew about his key, and cut out his heart. She was later found dead in her own office with her throat torn apart, likely murdered for the high tower key she possessed. Her heart remained untouched, distinguishing the killing from Janussi's death. A half-finished note on her desk read *He is using the beast to—*, implying that she recognized both the immediate threat and the intelligence directing it.

### Chapter 065 — Bookwyrm (First Reader)

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/065-session-08-03.md`
- Original heading: `Bookwyrm (First Reader)`

**Dead.** Murdered by Moziqodo, who took her High Tower key and delivered it to A'lai. Her death was one outcome of A'lai Aivenmore’s wider plot, alongside the death of Janussi. With Bookwyrm gone and several other senior Avowed neutralized, Candlekeep’s intended final line of defense was critically weakened.
