# NPCs — Edvaldo Sedanur

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 068 — Edvaldo Sedanur

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/068-session-08-24.md`
- Original heading: `Edvaldo Sedanur`

A scholar the party hired for expertise in the history of Candlekeep, who accompanied them into the depths. He *presents* as Avowed and is prone to lengthy academic monologues; none of his credentials have been verified. **GM-only: he is the doppelganger from `notes/session_prep/20260824_worth_two_faces.md`, still unexposed.**

Edvaldo jumps into the shaft because the unexplored depths present an unprecedented research opportunity, announcing, “I intend to write a very long monograph.” He recognizes the central tower’s architectural style and immediately lectures on the loss of similar examples to Candlekeep’s historical renovation and expansion.

At the guardian’s first question, he claims to know the answer but steps away to let the party complete the trial. For the final question, he explains that every Avowed was once expected to memorize the High Tower’s height, although the practice fell out of fashion, and supplies only that the answer is over five hundred feet.

The party debated asking Miirym whether everyone was who they claimed to be—the one boon that would have exposed him—but chose the anti-magic curtain instead. He therefore remains with them, unexposed, when the fight begins. The “over 500 feet” figure is his claim, not confirmed fact.

### Chapter 069 — Edvaldo

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/069-session-one-manshoon-dead-at-least-one-or-two-more-to-go.md`
- Original heading: `Edvaldo`

A man presenting himself as the Avowed researcher Edvaldo, who accompanied the party into the tower because his claimed research concerned Candlekeep lore. His commoner token appeared as a “Babushka lady,” causing some initial confusion about his identity.

Edvaldo survived damage that straightforward calculation suggested should have killed him, raising the party's suspicions. He had also behaved strangely earlier when he knew the tower was approximately five hundred feet high but could not provide a precise measurement.

When Zalthir attacked and grappled him, Edvaldo pleaded innocence and demonstrated knowledge of Candlekeep lore, speaking fluently about the Endless Chant and the Word Lattice. Daz's investigation found his account convincing, but the party remained suspicious. Edvaldo later asked to avoid further magical violence, then agreed to remain involved if he received second authorship on the eventual paper about the combat. He was bound and left in the anti-magic zone.

Asked what is consumed to make light, Edvaldo first answered “Books!” and then “Candles,” insisting the latter should be obvious in Candlekeep. The party concluded that he did not actually know how to open the Obsidian Door.

### Chapter 070 — Edvaldo

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/070-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Edvaldo`

A doppelganger who had been posing as an Avowed Reader. The party had already exposed him as suspicious when he survived more damage than an ordinary low-level Avowed priest should have been able to endure. Although tied securely, he escaped by changing into a smaller or thinner form.

The party learned his true nature when he appeared at the stairs in a pale, featureless doppelganger form. He served Manshoon and warned the party about the explosive fail-safe protecting the simulacrum. When Manshoon was carried away, Edvaldo found himself caught between a party likely to kill him and a master who would punish his failure.

In a panic after Manshoon's disposal, he smashed two prophecy crystals with debris from a dead owlbear. He then tried to force the party to open the door, only to be deceived by Daz's illusion that the book had turned to ash. Thorin struck and frightened him, while Zalthir grappled and dragged him toward the stairs. Edvaldo shapeshifted into a halfling and fled toward the anti-magic barrier along the only route not blocked by Thorin.
