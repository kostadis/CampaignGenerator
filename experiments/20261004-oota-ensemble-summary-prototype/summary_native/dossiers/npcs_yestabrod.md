# NPCs — Yestabrod

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 031 — Yestabrod

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Yestabrod`

A disgusting larval creature that serves as a leader in the Garden of Welcome. It speaks both aloud and telepathically, obsesses over a “wedding” to Zuggtmoy, commands drow spore servants, and has the gruesome ability to explode nearby corpses to absorb their remains and heal itself. It can also detonate corpses offensively in showers of gore and acid and release spores that inflict madness. Vestigial fungal growths suggest that it might once have been an ordinary myconid. Unlike the adult myconids, it is not debilitated by Dawnbringer's sunlight.

### Chapter 032 — Yestabrod

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/032-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Yestabrod`

A deceased servant of Zuggtmoy whose corpse is animated by three spore servants during the demonic wedding rehearsal. They lift him from the garden and manipulate his body like a puppet so that he can play the priest.

Spores released by Yestabrod during the earlier fight continue to affect Daz and Zalthir during this session.
