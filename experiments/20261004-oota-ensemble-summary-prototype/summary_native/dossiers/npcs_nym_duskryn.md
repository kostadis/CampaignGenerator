# NPCs — Nym Duskryn

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 051 — Nym Duskryn

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/051-session-a-spore-filled-finale.md`
- Original heading: `Nym Duskryn`

A drow warrior who works with Kaelira to protect Daz and assists the party. She survives Ilvara's Flamestrike after taking thirteen damage.

When Daz refuses to withdraw from combat, Kaelira asks her, “Can I kill your brother?” Nym responds that her brother Jorlan was always the dumber one and confirms that he is now covered in fungus before granting permission.

She shows little mercy for Jorlan Duskryn, granting Kaelira permission to strike him with a Lightning Bolt. Jorlan partially avoids the blast but still takes fourteen damage. Nym then mutters about the stupidity of those around her and dashes to a safer position as the battlefield continues to shift.

### Chapter 053 — Nym Duskryn

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/053-session-out-of-the-dark-and-into-the-darkness.md`
- Original heading: `Nym Duskryn`

A professional Drow mercenary and fighter hired in Menzoberranzan to protect Daz from Ilvara of House Mizzrym and House T'sarran. Nym is blunt, arrogant, and highly conscious of professional standards. He criticized Kaelira for not preparing dispel magic and was appalled when Thorin merely grappled a cleric of Lolth. He regards Asha Vandree as too trivial a threat to fall within the contract and believes needing protection from her would insult both his abilities and his patron's judgment. Although his assignment was complete, he agreed to escort Daz the remaining few miles to the surface.
