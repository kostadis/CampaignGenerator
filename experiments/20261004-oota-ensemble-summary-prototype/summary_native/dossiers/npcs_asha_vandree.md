# NPCs — Asha Vandree

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 049 — Asha Vandree

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/049-session-a-sign-from-lolth-or-just-a-spider.md`
- Original heading: `Asha Vandree`

A junior drow priestess (under-priestess) in Ilvara's camp who remains loyal to Lolth and accuses Ilvara of heresy for embracing the fungal corruption, insisting that Lolth is no bride. She is obsessively clean, scraping fungus from her clothes, and maintains a secret stash of sealed food that has kept her uninfected. She is on the far edge of sanity. The party attempts to manipulate her with a false divine vision of Lolth, and she appears convinced.

### Chapter 050 — Asha Vandree

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/050-session-fungus-among-us.md`
- Original heading: `Asha Vandree`

A drow priestess who has remained loyal to Lolth and opposes Ilvara's fungal 'bride' heresy. She is paranoid and somewhat unstable, "seeing paranoid shadows everywhere," but willing to use the party as tools to see Ilvara killed.

She has diligently avoided the fungal spores. She keeps off the dust, stays beyond 20 feet of Ilvara, and eats her own food. She provides tactical information about the heart fungus and Ilvara's spore servants, and tested the party's tactical judgment ("this was just a test").

She offered Ilvara's treasures, though they belonged to her house, in exchange for Ilvara's death and the mushroom's destruction. She is also exasperated by being stuck between Ilvara and the bitter Jorlan.

She holds drow-male mages in contempt but was impressed by Daz's apparent "servants" and ooze familiar. She accepted "boss," Zalthir's word for Daz, and concluded it must be a Dragonborn term.

### Chapter 051 — Asha Vandree

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/051-session-a-spore-filled-finale.md`
- Original heading: `Asha Vandree`

A drow combatant who has been tricked into believing Daz is a follower of Lolth. She regards him as an insane mage who has somehow converted to Lolthism, but the important fact is that she believes his story.

When the House T'sarran drow appeared to drag Daz away, Asha assumed they were attacking or abducting him and denounced them as “Traitors to Lolth!” Daz reassured her that they were friends of Lolth who had come to help. His successful persuasion prevented her from turning on the newcomers.

Following Ilvara's death, Asha cried out about “the evil” and “the undead,” then began chanting prayers and beatitudes to Lolth amid the settling spores.

### Chapter 053 — Asha Vandree

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/053-session-out-of-the-dark-and-into-the-darkness.md`
- Original heading: `Asha Vandree`

A Drow cleric of Lolth who survived the recent chaos and became convinced that she had been divinely chosen. She mistook Daz's deception for a genuine sign from Lolth and interpreted the destruction of everyone around her as confirmation that she alone had kept the faith. Her immediate attempt to command the party ended when Gyrgum attacked, Thorin grappled and gagged her, and Zalthir killed her with a barrage of strikes. Her belongings included Drow armor, Lolthian holy symbols, obsidian-and-bone prayer beads, coins, and a sealed theological text written after her corruption.
