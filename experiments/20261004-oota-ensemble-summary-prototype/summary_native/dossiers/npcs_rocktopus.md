# NPCs — rocktopus

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 032 — rocktopus

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/032-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `rocktopus`

A monstrous, stone-like octopus predator that ambushes the party from the ledges of the gas-filled cavern. It blocks the route to the exit and sees the adventurers as prey.

It strikes Thorin with a tentacle and attempts to grapple him, but he shakes free. After being wounded by Thorin, restrained and pummeled by Zalthir, and struck by Daz’s Magic Missile, it is slain by Gyrgum’s mace.
