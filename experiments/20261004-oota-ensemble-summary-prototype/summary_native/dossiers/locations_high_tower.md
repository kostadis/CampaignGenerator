# Locations — High Tower

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 059 — High Tower

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/059-session-questions-answers-and-a-key.md`
- Original heading: `High Tower`

A restricted area within Candlekeep housing powerful magical artifacts of the sort that every evil entity in the world would want access to, requiring both of two specific keys to enter simultaneously. It is currently under doubled guard following the murder. The DM notes that entry to the High Tower does not go unobserved — watchers are always posted regardless of where the keys are; walking in on a Monday would be noticed.

### Chapter 063 — High Tower

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `High Tower`

The ultimate destination of the two magical keys and the suspected location where A'lai Aivenmore waited for Moziqodo. A secured room within it contains the source of the ward that prevents teleportation and suppresses combustion throughout Candlekeep. Whoever acquires both keys may be able to reach that ward and compromise the fortress's primary defense.

### Chapter 065 — High Tower

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/065-session-08-03.md`
- Original heading: `High Tower`

A prominent structure within Candlekeep where the party confronted A'lai Aivenmore in a fierce magical battle on the second-floor landing. The tower’s magical dampening effects failed during the conflict, allowing destructive magic to function freely.

Daz’s fireball detonated on its indoor walkway, the last Zhentarim thug died there, and the assassin later knocked Daz unconscious before being killed by Thorin. A sealed magical door within the tower protected the security control room until Manshoon’s simulacrum shattered it.

### Chapter 067 — High Tower

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/067-session-08-17.md`
- Original heading: `High Tower`

The site of the brutal battle that ended Chapter 65, and the location where Manshoon's simulacrum breached Candlekeep's inner sanctum using a wall of force to shatter the magically protected door. The party began this session just outside the breached chamber with A'lai in custody and Tadric nearby.
