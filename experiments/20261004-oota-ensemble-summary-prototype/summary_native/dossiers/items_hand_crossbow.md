### Chapter 003 — Hand Crossbow

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/003-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Hand Crossbow`

Small ranged weapons stored in the upper armory. Six are available, forcing the prisoners to keep track of who has one during the fight. Eldeth, Topsy, Turvy, Jimjar, Buppido, and Shuushar use them to fire into the lower guard room. Several shots miss, but the bolts that land badly wound both an ordinary drow and the Elite Warrior.

The group does not track individual crossbow bolts after the battle.

### Chapter 051 — Hand Crossbow

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/051-session-a-spore-filled-finale.md`
- Original heading: `Hand Crossbow`

Small, concealable ranged weapons used by the Drow warriors to fire bolts at Daz from the darkness. The first resolution of the volley appeared to hit Daz multiple times, prompting him to cast Shield.

After Zalthir reminded the DM that magical darkness obscured the attackers, the exchange was rewound. The drow fired again with disadvantage, while Daz benefited from partial cover or obscurity, and all three attacks missed. A later blind crossbow attack from the House T'sarran elite warrior also missed badly.
