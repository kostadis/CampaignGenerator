### Chapter 056 — Lesser Restoration

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/056-session-candlekeep-trials-truths-and-therapy.md`
- Original heading: `Lesser Restoration`

Cast by Dawnbringer on the afflicted girl Elin in Daggerford, after the party failed their medicine checks to diagnose the condition conventionally. Mechanically, the spell originated from Dawnbringer's own sentient-weapon properties and was channeled through Thorin as her attuned wielder — not from any spell slot of Thorin's (Fighter 8, Battle Master, no spellcasting). Dawnbringer performed it with pompous ceremony — "Let me bring the light to you." While it allowed Elin to speak briefly — uttering "The wells are not wells anymore" — it could not fully cure the profound mental twisting she was experiencing, as the condition was determined to be something interior and beyond a standard disease or magical effect.
