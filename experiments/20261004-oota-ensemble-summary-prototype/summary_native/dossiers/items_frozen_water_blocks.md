### Chapter 006 — Frozen Water Blocks

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/006-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Frozen Water Blocks`

Gallons of collected water frozen into solid blocks using Daz's magic, providing a clever solution for transporting water without containers. Party members could chisel off pieces with a dagger to drink while on the move, and the blocks needed to be refrozen every hour. Each of the ten travelers received a one-gallon block, since the rules require one gallon per person per day.
