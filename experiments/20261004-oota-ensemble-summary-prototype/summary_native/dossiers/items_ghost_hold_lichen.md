# Items — Ghost Hold Lichen

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 043 — Ghost Hold Lichen

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/043-session-harvesting-horror-in-the-underdark.md`
- Original heading: `Ghost Hold Lichen`

A specific fungi usually found in Neverlight Grove but discovered by Bimble in a 'garden of stone' within the mines. It grows from the corpse of a dead giant in the Festering Fissure. It needs to be carefully cut without bursting poisonous sacs next to it, which deal 3d8 poison damage if ruptured. A warding prayer must be chanted within 10 feet while it is cut to keep demonic spirits at bay. It is needed as an ingredient for Kazook Pickshine's alchemical solution.
- Cutting it is an Acrobatics check, not an attack. The GM rules the lichen is not a creature.
- The GM later relaxes the 10-foot requirement: the chanter must chant while the lichen is cut but need not be physically present.
- Zalthir's first, imperfect cut harvested some lichen and allows another attempt.
