### Chapter 010 — Duergar Keelboat

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/010-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Duergar Keelboat`

A sturdy subterranean vessel belonging to the gray dwarf slavers, found floating near rocks with its cargo still aboard. It served as the staging point for the ambush and was captured by the party after the skirmish, effectively becoming part of their small armada. It also contained oars, which the party noted would be useful, since their own boat had no oars or poles.
