### Chapter 011 — Aquatic Troll

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/011-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Aquatic Troll`

A large (10-by-10) hungry creature that speaks Giant and attempted to snatch a party member from their boat for food. It wanted something "live or meaty" and regenerates unless burned with fire or acid. It retreated into the depths after being struck with magic and pushed away, grumbling that it was only trying to be helpful.
