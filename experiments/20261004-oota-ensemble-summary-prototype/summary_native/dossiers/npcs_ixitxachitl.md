# NPCs — Ixitxachitl

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 009 — Ixitxachitl

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/009-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Ixitxachitl`

Intelligent, demonic manta-ray-like creatures that serve the Prince of Demons. They hunt in the waters of the Darklake, aggressively ambushing party members who fall into their domain. They have roughly 18 hit points each, bite, and lash with barbed and poisonous tails. They have no apparent vulnerability to heat, cold, or light. Three attacked the party: two were killed, and one remained hidden in Zalthir's darkness.
