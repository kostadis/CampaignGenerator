# NPCs — Manshoon

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 064 — Manshoon

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/064-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Manshoon`

A legendary and feared wizard associated with the Zhentarim, the Moonsea, and Zhentil Keep. A'lai identifies him as the true authority behind eleven years of directed book thefts from Candlekeep. Manshoon sends lists naming the required volumes, and a courier with a metal right hand concealed beneath a glove delivers them.

A'lai warns that ignorance of Manshoon is deliberate and professionally useful: “You have not heard of it. That is not an accident. It is a career.” The party's historical knowledge establishes that Manshoon has died more than once and nevertheless returned. His present objective is a single book out of Candlekeep.

### Chapter 065 — Manshoon

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/065-session-08-03.md`
- Original heading: `Manshoon`

A legendary and feared wizard tied to the Zhentarim, described as the man who died more than once and kept returning—“the ultimate bad penny.” He was associated with the Zhentarim’s transformation into an evil cult and repeatedly returned from apparent death without ever becoming a better person.

He is the mysterious figure A'lai Aivenmore served for eleven years. His courier concealed a metal hand beneath a glove, a clue to Manshoon’s identity.

What breached Candlekeep this session was **his simulacrum** (GM ruling 2026-08-19). The simulacrum expended massive magical resources and artifacts to enter, arriving alone and weakened rather than at full power with a retinue. It used a wall of force to facilitate the destruction of the High Tower’s magical door. It does not yet know the truth A'lai withheld about the depository.

### Chapter 067 — Manshoon

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/067-session-08-17.md`
- Original heading: `Manshoon`

A legendary and immensely powerful wizard. What reached Candlekeep is his **simulacrum**, arriving alone and visibly weakened after expending enormous magical resources. It conjures a wall of force and shatters the magically protected door to breach the inner sanctum, seeking something of extraordinary value. Its complete disregard for A'lai exposes the traitorous scholar's true insignificance.
