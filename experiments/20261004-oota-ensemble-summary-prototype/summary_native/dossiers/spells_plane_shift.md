# Spells — Plane Shift

### Chapter 060 — Plane Shift

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/060-session-never-mind-the-murders.md`
- Original heading: `Plane Shift`

Discussed as a way to evade Midnight Tears by leaving the plane before the poison’s local-midnight trigger. It is particularly relevant to creatures such as mind flayers or githyanki with innate planar travel, though much less practical than *Lesser Restoration* for most victims.
