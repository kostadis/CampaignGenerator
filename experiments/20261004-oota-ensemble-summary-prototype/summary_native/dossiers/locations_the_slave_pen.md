# Locations — the Slave Pen

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 002 — The Slave Pen

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Slave Pen`

This is the cramped, guarded chamber where the prisoners are held between labor shifts. They use their time here to exchange intelligence through conversation and Stool's rapport spores. Buppido announced his divine plan here, and Jorlan later offered to leave its gate unlocked during the changing of the guard. The guard tower and its armory stand directly in front of the pen.

### Chapter 003 — The Slave Pen

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/003-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Slave Pen`

A dark and miserable holding area where the party and several other prisoners are kept. The cell is secured by a door too heavy to break without producing an enormous racket. The drow assume escape is impossible because the prisoners possess neither weapons nor the ability to cast their way through the barrier.

When the door is unexpectedly left open, the prisoners gain a brief opportunity to act. Stool’s spores fill the pen, connecting the captives telepathically while they finalize their route and decide who will approach the guard tower first.
