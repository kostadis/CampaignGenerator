# Locations — Oozing Temple

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 034 — Oozing Temple

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/034-session-the-wet-bet-and-the-wondrous-cube.md`
- Original heading: `Oozing Temple`

An ancient structure in the Underdark, currently experiencing rapid flooding due to a tremor that caused an underground river to break through its stone walls. It has 10-foot-high ceilings, natural and constructed passageways, water-filled chambers, hidden pit traps, and an extraordinary concentration of oozes.

Its hazards include gray oozes, a black pudding associated in the party’s memory with Juiblex, and a pit trap concealed along one of several western passages. The temple’s narrow corridors make Glabbagool both an effective barrier and a significant obstruction.

The central fountain room contains a raised stone basin filled with shallow, brackish water, the remains of a statue with clawed feet, seven wall niches, and four formless sculptures that transform into gray oozes when touched. A treasure cache rests inside the fountain.

At the end of the eastern passage, cracks in the wall and ceiling admit water at a rate sufficient to raise the level by roughly one foot per hour. Breaking through the damaged formation reveals the diverted underground river and the party’s escape route.
