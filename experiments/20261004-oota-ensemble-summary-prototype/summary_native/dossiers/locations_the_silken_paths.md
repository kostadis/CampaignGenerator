# Locations — the Silken Paths

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 006 — The Silken Paths

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/006-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Silken Paths`

A staggering subterranean chasm measuring 500 feet deep and 2,000 feet wide, filled with an intricate, ever-changing network of thick, five-foot-wide spider web strands spun by giant spiders. The webs serve as treacherous bridges over a massive drop, and any fire damage or broken strand can send travelers plummeting into the void.
- The chasm is a massive junction with many entrances, some of which open and close over time.
- Its major "highways" are easily traversed.
- The webs are difficult terrain for anyone without a climbing speed.
- Damage or breakage forces a DC 15 Dexterity save, with falls of 10–100 feet on a failure.
- Fire breaks strands and forces saves within a 30-foot radius.
- Carrying light attracts hostile creatures.
