### Chapter 026 — Misty Step

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/026-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Misty Step`

A spell considered by Daz as a contingency plan to teleport past the guards in a silvery mist if their bluff at the West Cleft checkpoint failed.
- It teleports the caster up to 30 feet to a visible unoccupied space.
- Daz planned to claim divine favor with the incriminating items on his person. The spell was not needed.

- Original heading: `Misty Step`

Cast by Daz, who was surrounded by a silvery mist and teleported up to 30 feet to a spot right next to the flying Zalthir after losing his footing during the dragon's sharp mid-air turn.

### Chapter 046 — Misty Step

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/046-session-zalthir-s-daring-grapple.md`
- Original heading: `Misty Step`

A spell on Daz's list. Daz asks whether it could carry someone he has grappled (the GM: it moves only the caster); later Gyrgum asks whether Zalthir could "Misty Step" past the ochre jelly, and Zalthir says he can teleport to somewhere he can see.

### Chapter 052 — Misty Step

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/052-session-the-ember-grapple.md`
- Original heading: `Misty Step`

A teleportation spell that Daz already had access to; Fey Touched grants him one casting per long rest without expending a spell slot, after which he can still cast it normally using slots. This provides a reliable way to escape grapples or reposition during battle without provoking opportunity attacks. Because Fey Touched supplied the spell separately, Daz could replace his previously learned version with another spell from his available options.

### Chapter 054 — Misty Step

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/054-session-04-28.md`
- Original heading: `Misty Step`

Cast by Daz to teleport into the branches of a large tree to scout for the tracker, requiring him to see his landing spot clearly. He vanished in a curl of smoke. He rolled Stealth with advantage and got a 12 (rolling a 1 and an 11), which was enough to beat the spy's Perception roll of 3 — though the GM ruled she noticed where he landed.

### Chapter 064 — Misty Step

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/064-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Misty Step`

Used by A'lai Aivenmore to teleport across the room and escape the restraining earthen hand after failing his save against Daz's second Maximilian's Earthen Grasp. Because teleportation does not use ordinary movement, the restrained condition does not prevent it. The escape costs A'lai another spell and keeps him from responding with a more devastating leveled spell during the same turn.

### Chapter 070 — Misty Step

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/070-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Misty Step`

Used by Daz as a bonus action to instantly teleport into the inner book chamber, reaching the Book of Vile Darkness before Edvaldo could get to it. The spell allowed him to bypass the contested doorway and position himself directly beside the podium.
