# NPCs — Stonespeaker Hgraam

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 015 — Hgraam

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/015-session-04-14.md`
- Original heading: `Hgraam`

The Stone Giant clan leader of Clan Cairngorm. He is thoughtful, stony, and patient, waiting for the party to initiate conversation at the entrance of his home.
- He senses the growing demonic presence and the madness affecting the rock itself.
- He grieved over his apprentice, who was driven mad by the stones.
- He warned of an evil stirring in the blackest depths of the Underdark.
- He gifted the party a polished crystal.
- He speaks in omens and "what the stones tell me."
- He advised the Deepking toward patience and generosity, but his advice has been ignored since Shal arrived.
- He calls the surface world "the Dreamlands" because he has never been there.
- He is prone to long rants about everyone being in a rush.

### Chapter 026 — Hgraam

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/026-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Hgraam`

A Stone Giant leader who is uncharacteristically talkative for his kind. He seeks to cure his brothers of a magical curse and rewards the party for their assistance.
- He probed whether the derro council was behind the curse and whether it aimed to set the giants against the duergar.
- He chose to "let the stones speak before we act" and closed his cavern to outsiders.
- He does not know whether the Overbright exists.
