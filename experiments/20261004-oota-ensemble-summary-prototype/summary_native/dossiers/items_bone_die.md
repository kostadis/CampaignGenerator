## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 048 — Bone Die

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/048-session-the-knucklebone-of-truth.md`
- Original heading: `Bone Die`

A 20-sided die carved from the knucklebone of a saint who never told a lie, won by Jimjar from a blind Duergar in Gracklstugh. When tossed onto Glabbagool, it triggered a mysterious 'blink' that pacified the entire ooze army by correcting the 'world's math.' It is now considered spent; Jimjar let the party keep it, and Gyrgum put it in his pouch. Jimjar won it in "a back-alley game of Three-Dragon Ante" and was told it was a one-time thing. Gyrgum judged it "probably not worth anything, but it's cool."
