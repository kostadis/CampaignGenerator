### Chapter 041 — Earth Elemental

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/041-session-mirror-mirror-on-the-wall-who-s-the-maddest-of-them-all.md`
- Original heading: `Earth Elemental`

A large, mad elemental creature driven insane by the area's curse. It was slow and vulnerable to Dexterity saving throws but powerful in melee combat. It was grappled by Zalthir and eventually defeated by the party.
- The GM described it as "maddeningly insane"; it simply struck at the nearest obvious target, which was Zalthir.
- Its slams missed Zalthir repeatedly while it was grappled and unable to move.
- It was reduced to exactly one hit point before Thorin's Hill Strike toppled it.

### Chapter 042 — Earth Elemental

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/042-session-zalthir-s-reflection-thorin-s-stand.md`
- Original heading: `Earth Elemental`

Creatures of living stone that erupt from the temple walls when the Ruby spell gem is placed in the menhir. They are unintelligent (intelligence of 3), have poor dexterity, and are compelled to attack due to Ogrémoch's influence. They are resistant to sensory effects like being blinded, charmed, or deafened. The party fights three of them in succession.
- They are "as dry as dirt" and "very, very dumb."
- Their Dexterity modifier is −1. The third elemental's AC was 17.
- They do not understand Common; the GM clarified that Stone Golems are a different creature.
- They focused attacks on Zalthir in the second fight and on Thorin in the third.
- The first elemental missed twice in a row, with a 5 and a 1.

### Chapter 048 — Earth Elemental

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/048-session-the-knucklebone-of-truth.md`
- Original heading: `Earth Elemental`

A massive being of stone that sacrificed its energy to hold open a breach in the throne room. It was healed by Gyrgum's magic before merging back into the earth, and left behind a glowing geode as a token of favor. After the healing it gave an Atlas-like stretch, looked at the party, and sank into the dirt, leaving the hole stable.
