# NPCs — Prince Derendil

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 002 — Prince Derendil

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Prince Derendil`

A quaggoth prisoner who claims to be a cursed elf prince. He was assigned to haul water alongside Thorin and Ront and accompanied Thorin into Ilvara's quarters. Derendil demanded the dignity appropriate to his claimed station even while performing menial labor, prompting Ilvara to draw a knife and threaten to deal with him. He backed down rather than force the confrontation.

### Chapter 003 — Prince Derendil

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/003-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Prince Derendil`

A Quaggoth who claims to be an elven prince transformed by a curse. Most Quaggoths are described as violent creatures bent on destruction and mayhem, making Derendil’s identity and behavior notably strange. He freely admits that he is not stealthy but offers his strength to the breakout, proposing to follow once the others obtain weapons and then “start bashing heads.”

When the stealth attempt fails, Derendil remains in the lower guard room while many of the other prisoners rush upstairs. He accepts a short-sword wound to enter the battle and attacks with both claws. His presence helps occupy Imbros and keeps the route to the armory open, but it also leaves him exposed to the Elite Warrior’s poisoned blade. Imbros ultimately kills him during the final phase of the fight.
