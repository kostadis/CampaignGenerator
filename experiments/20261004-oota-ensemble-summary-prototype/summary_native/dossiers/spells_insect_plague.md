# Spells — Insect Plague

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 051 — Insect Plague

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/051-session-a-spore-filled-finale.md`
- Original heading: `Insect Plague`

Cast by Ilvara, this spell filled a twenty-foot-radius sphere at the cave entrance with biting insects, targeting Gyrgum, Daz, and Glabbagool. It required Constitution saving throws and dealt piercing damage.

The swarm killed Ilvara's own thirteen-hit-point minions outright and inflicted twenty-six damage on Glabbagool, although he survived. It also created difficult, lightly obscured terrain extending from the floor to the ceiling of the forty-foot passage.

The plague became a tactical barrier that blocked the arriving group of five drow from seeing Daz and advancing. One rescuer crossed it to pull Daz to safety, taking thirteen damage despite making the save. Ilvara later dropped concentration on the spell to free her focus for another assault.
