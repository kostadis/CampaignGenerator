# Locations — Whorlstone Caverns

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 018 — Whorlstone Caverns

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/018-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Whorlstone Caverns`

A dangerous cave system beneath Gracklstugh inhabited by the Derro, where the party's key objectives lie.
- Multiple factions want evidence from inside but cannot enter because of the Derro's heightened paranoia and traps built to catch invisible intruders.
- Thangus Ironhead publicly calls it a place "for children to go play."
- The party told Errde the caves are "not as safe as they used to be."

### Chapter 019 — Whorlstone Caverns

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/019-session-05-20.md`
- Original heading: `Whorlstone Caverns`

A complex underground network beneath Gracklstugh filled with competing factions, including duergar assassins and derro demon worshippers. The party navigated its tunnels, uncovered a royal conspiracy, and ultimately confronted a mad cultist near a corrupted dragon egg. An intersection past the assassins' territory offers two routes to the obelisk chamber. One is the main entrance, behind big double doors. The other runs north past the chambers of the Gray Ghosts and the Council of Savants. An unexplored northwest passage remains unknown even to Flumph.

### Chapter 025 — Whorlstone Caverns

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/025-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Whorlstone Caverns`

A complex of natural caves beneath Gracklstugh where the party completed a major confrontation, secured powerful artifacts, encountered infected Myconids, and experienced a demonic vision before resting and departing. Thorin and Gyrgum awoke from the vision "back in the Whorlstone Tunnels." The party spent three days here in total.
