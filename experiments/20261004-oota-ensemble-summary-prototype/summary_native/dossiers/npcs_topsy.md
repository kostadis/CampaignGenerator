### Chapter 002 — Topsy

- Original heading: `Topsy`

A deep gnome prisoner and twin to Turvy. Topsy is an experienced thief who advises that theft should be done carefully and quietly, without drawing attention. Topsy and Turvy initially tried to distract the drow while Gyrgum stole a knife, but his conspicuous attempt failed. On the following day, the twins took over the theft themselves and successfully acquired two kitchen knives.
