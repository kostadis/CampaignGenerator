### Chapter 031 — Inflict Wounds

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Inflict Wounds`

A touch-based spell cast by Gyrgum against Yestabrod and a myconid adult, channeling destructive necrotic energy directly into the creatures on contact. Gyrgum's first casting severely wounds Yestabrod and forces it to consume the remains of an exploding corpse to recover. His later casting leaves an adult myconid with only a few remaining hit points.
