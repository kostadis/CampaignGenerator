### Chapter 034 — Silver Pieces

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/034-session-the-wet-bet-and-the-wondrous-cube.md`
- Original heading: `Silver Pieces`

A cache of 112 silver pieces discovered by Glabbagool in the brackish fountain. He absorbs the coins, meaning the party must wait several days for them to pass through his body.

Thorin promises ten of these pieces to Jimjar in settlement of their wager after the escape, warning that the payment may be somewhat slimy.
