# NPCs — The Pudding King

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 038 — Pudding King

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/038-session-wererats-wisdom-and-the-war-on-ooze.md`
- Original heading: `Pudding King`

A small, insane gnome who sits on a slime-covered throne in a cavern filled with oozes. He believes himself to be the king of Blingdenstone and speaks of cleansing it. He is the source of the disembodied voice heard by the party and controls the vast ooze army. Though not physically imposing, his control over hundreds of oozes makes him extremely dangerous.

He holds court alone in the manner of an irrational monarch, speaking of cleansing Blingdenstone of everyone who has irritated him. His voice carries magically into the larger cavern, where it invokes the Faceless Lord and calls Glabbagool toward the assembled oozes. The party identifies him as Juiblex’s likely proxy. Removing him is tactically possible, but doing so without preparations could release his army from control and send hundreds of hungry oozes wandering toward Blingdenstone.

### Chapter 039 — Pudding King

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Pudding King`

A former gnome tunnel worker who now commands Blingdenstone's oozes and slimes. Chief Chipgrin knew him before his transformation and knows his current location.

His actions are driven by vengeance, wounded pride, resentment, and madness. The party believes Juiblex or another demonic influence has empowered him and amplified his worst existing traits rather than simply replacing his personality.

His influence is not confined to his own body or ooze followers. Greed and aggression are bleeding into Blingdenstone's population and leadership, with Chief Dorbo's increasingly irrational fixation on money serving as one conspicuous example.

The party intends to use magic to seize the Pudding King while Blingdenstone's combined forces distract his ooze army.

### Chapter 046 — Pudding King

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/046-session-zalthir-s-daring-grapple.md`
- Original heading: `Pudding King`

A gnomish figure possessed by the demon lord Juiblex, who seeks to convert Blingdenstone's biomass into ooze biomass. He monologues to his two ooze henchmen about the impending defeat of Blingdenstone and his conflict with Zuggtmoy, whom he calls “the queen of rot,” over the kingdom. He turned into an ooze form when Zalthir grappled him; the GM ruled that in that form he automatically hits anyone grappling him. He pulses with a rhythmic heartbeat that the ochre jelly barricade matches. He promises to turn Blingdenstone into "Blingdenpuddle" and instructs his oozes to "Ooze them slowly."

### Chapter 047 — Pudding King

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/047-session-the-fall-of-the-pudding-king.md`
- Original heading: `Pudding King`

A former humanoid who transformed into a sentient, telepathic ooze after embracing his connection to Juiblex, the demon lord of oozes. He commanded an army of oozes and possessed powerful abilities including slime waves and green slime attacks. The slime wave was a cone that knocked targets prone, and the green slime could be hurled anywhere within his lair. He was immune to being knocked prone. He screamed telepathically and resented being asked to quiet down, insisting that screaming was the point of serving a demon. He started the fight at 85 hit points. He was grappled and dragged out of his lair by Zalthir, and at one point swapped bodies with Glabbagool. He was ultimately defeated and dissolved, screaming 'Behold the true feast, the soul's final blend!' as he died.
