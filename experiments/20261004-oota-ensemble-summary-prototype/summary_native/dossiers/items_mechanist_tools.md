### Chapter 068 — Mechanist Tools

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/068-session-08-24.md`
- Original heading: `Mechanist Tools`

Specialized mechanical tools borrowed from Spanner at the House of Mechanus. Daz identifies the machinery embedded in the lava-cavern ledge as a bridge mechanism, and the party uses these tools to activate it. The resulting stone bridge grinds outward across the molten lake to the Obsidian Tower. Without the tools, the nearby anti-magic field would have prevented a simple magical flight across the gap.
