# Locations — Underdark Tunnels

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 004 — Underdark Tunnels

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/004-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Underdark Tunnels`

A vast network of narrow, uneven, twisting passages and caverns characterized by difficult terrain, indirect routes, steep climbs, and dangerous descents. Travelers unfamiliar with the passages can become hopelessly lost, making a knowledgeable navigator and a map essential. Portions of the tunnels are suffused by Faerzress. The tunnels also contain edible fungi and unstable passages, including the site of the rockfall that trapped several members of the group.

### Chapter 005 — Underdark Tunnels

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/005-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Underdark Tunnels`

A treacherous, pitch-black network of subterranean passages and intersecting corridors where the party is forced to flee from their former captors, the Drow scouts. The GM compared it to "a collection of tunnels, turns, twists, and intersecting" passages, and a player likened it to "the maze in Zork." The tunnels hold rocks and boulders, a sudden drop, and the alcove where the party hid. The party is travelling toward a lake.

### Chapter 049 — Underdark Tunnels
