# NPCs — Galeb Duhr

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 042 — Galeb Duhr

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/042-session-zalthir-s-reflection-thorin-s-stand.md`
- Original heading: `Galeb Duhr`

Boulder-like creatures with stumpy appendages that act as arms and legs, serving as stone guardians. They are slow-witted and lethargic but can turn into balls to move quickly. They are lawfully bound to their charge and cannot be bribed. Ogrémoch's infection kept the Galeb Duhr in the temple from their duty to guard the area. They are freed as each Earth Elemental is defeated, but cannot act until all their 'brothers' are freed. They attack by throwing rocks.
- Some drow spellcasters prize them as an "ultimate fire and forget security system."
- Drow slang for them is "doorstop."
- They are also known as "a rock that hits back."
- A Galeb Duhr "may have been placed there by somebody centuries ago."
- They cannot spawn Earth Elementals.
- Three had appeared by session's end, one after each elemental fell.

### Chapter 043 — Galeb Duhr

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/043-session-harvesting-horror-in-the-underdark.md`
- Original heading: `Galeb Duhr`

Three talkative Galeb Duhrs who acknowledge the party's actions in hallowing the temple. They explain the significance of Ogrémoch and Entémoch, share 'dwarf jokes' about 'hollow' versus 'hallowed', and argue amongst themselves about what information to share. They are patient, slow-moving, thoughtful creatures who sit next to the menhir after speaking. One recognizes Gyrgum as a cleric of Bahamut. They insist this is "the Realm of the Rock," puzzle over Thorin's "giant clothes," and trade puns about space and emptiness that make sense only in their own language. The GM says the drow would call them "door stops."

### Chapter 044 — Galeb Duhr

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/044-session-from-zuggtmoy-to-entemoch-s-boon.md`
- Original heading: `Galeb Duhr`

Four sentient stone creatures blocking the path in Pickshine Mine, vibrating rhythmically in a pattern described as 'singing the first half of a duet'. They acted as dutiful sentinels waiting for a divine signature before allowing passage, ignoring Dasco Pickshine's attempts to move them including being struck with a pickaxe. They relaxed and opened the path after the party completed the proper 'handshake protocol' using resonant geodes.
- Their rhythm is "Throm, thrum, silence."
- When no answer comes, "they lock their joints and wait to ask again."
- They had reverted to the instruction "Trust no one."
