### Chapter 028 — Turn Undead

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/028-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Turn Undead`

Gyrgum channeled divine energy through his holy symbol in an attempt to repel the remaining specters, but all of them resisted the effect, leaving Gyrgum to shake his head in disappointment. The effect targets undead within 30 feet with a DC 15 Wisdom save, and doesn't require them to see the symbol. The GM hinted at Bahamut's displeasure over the dragon egg.
