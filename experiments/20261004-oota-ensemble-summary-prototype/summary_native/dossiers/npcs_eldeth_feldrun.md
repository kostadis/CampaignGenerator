### Chapter 034 — Eldeth

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/034-session-the-wet-bet-and-the-wondrous-cube.md`
- Original heading: `Eldeth`

A dwarf who provides crucial insight into the geological phenomena causing the flooding. After Thorin’s initial examination identifies the cracks as significant, Eldeth recognizes the signs of an underground river breaking through the stone.

She has heard stories of trapped people breaking open similar formations and finding passages large enough to swim through to safety. However, she soberly acknowledges the survivor bias behind those tales: “I didn’t hear about all the people who did that and drowned anymore.” Her assessment helps the party understand both the potential escape route and the mortal risk of widening the breach.

### Chapter 035 — Eldeth

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/035-session-prejudice-promises-and-the-prince-of-puddles.md`
- Original heading: `Eldeth`

A dwarven party member with a dim view of gnomes, whom she considers clever with magic and gems but less capable as warriors. She recognizes that dwarves and gnomes compete for finite underground resources, while also acknowledging that they can become allies against deeper Underdark threats.

Eldeth was raised to believe that all drow were evil and that “The only good drow is a dead drow.” Her captivity reinforced this prejudice, but traveling with Daz has forced her to reconsider it. She now distinguishes drow individuals from the fanatical cult of Lolth and accepts that people raised within that cult may still be redeemable. Daz’s example prompts her to question her culture’s assumption that entire peoples can be inherently evil and deserving of destruction.
