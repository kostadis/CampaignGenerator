### Chapter 054 — Candlekeep

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/054-session-04-28.md`
- Original heading: `Candlekeep`

A legendary, impregnable fortress-library on the Sword Coast. Entry is granted only in exchange for a unique book not already in its vast collection; donating more books earns more time with the Avowed — the scholars, sages, and monk-professors who staff it. The First Reader heads the institution. The GM describes it as "basically an impregnable fortress" and analogizes it to "the Alexandrian Library" in terms of its scope. The party notes that once inside, it will be practically impossible for House T'sarran to send agents after them.

### Chapter 065 — Candlekeep

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/065-session-08-03.md`
- Original heading: `Candlekeep`

A legendary library fortress. It is famously impossible to steal books within its walls, yet A'lai secretly removed selected books for eleven years. The keep also has a force field, yet there were raiders inside; whatever means smuggled the books out may also have brought them in.

Its magical security wards were compromised during this session, allowing Daz’s fireball to detonate on the High Tower walkway. The explosion prompted screaming, ringing bells, and panic, while scholars characteristically began taking notes and preparing monographs on a phenomenon they had waited years to study.

Manshoon’s simulacrum breached the defenses separately and at enormous magical cost. The attack became possible because five key Avowed defenders were dead, neutralized, captured, or absent, leaving Candlekeep without its intended final line of resistance.

### Chapter 070 — Candlekeep

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/070-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Candlekeep`

A great library fortress where the party has been following prophecies of doom and internal strife among the Readers. Four of its nine leading Readers had already been eliminated, weakening the institution and helping Manshoon justify expending enormous magical resources to breach its defenses.

The party descended through a hidden stairway that had gone undiscovered for more than 150 years. Candlekeep's surviving Avowed Readers were expected to arrive shortly after the session's end and would not allow artifacts of extraordinary power to disappear unnoticed.
