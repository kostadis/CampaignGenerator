# Locations — Traders' Grotto

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 036 — Traders' Grotto

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/036-session-not-an-ooze-just-a-cube.md`
- Original heading: `Traders' Grotto`

A trading area within Blingdenstone through which the party passes on its way to Diggermattock Hall. Deep gnomes conduct trade and daily activities there. Residents notice Glabbagool accompanying the party, and some less adventurous Svirfneblin react nervously despite the party’s explanation that he is friendly.

### Chapter 037 — Traders' Grotto

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/037-session-slime-spirits-and-svirfneblin-squabbles.md`
- Original heading: `Traders' Grotto`

Blingdenstone's central market, a moist cavern where many kinds of fungi once grew before a medusa's spell turned them to stone centuries ago. Merchants operate makeshift stalls among the petrified growths, while arriving caravans lower cargo into warehouses carved beneath the grotto. Common merchandise and a limited selection of potions are available, but it is not a dedicated magic-item market. The party was attacked here by several non-sentient gelatinous cubes, forcing Daz to place his Fireballs carefully around civilians, stalls, and merchandise.

### Chapter 038 — Traders' Grotto

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/038-session-wererats-wisdom-and-the-war-on-ooze.md`
- Original heading: `Traders' Grotto`

A bustling market area within Blingdenstone, currently in disarray after a recent battle with gelatinous cubes. Merchants are present, assessing damage and interacting with the adventurers.

The aftermath becomes an impromptu political forum. Merchants inspect their damaged shops, citizens question why their taxes failed to provide effective protection, and the sergeant attempts to transform the party’s independent heroism into a public-relations victory for Chief Dorbo. The party recovers a magical short sword here and receives a Chamber of Commerce discount coupon.
