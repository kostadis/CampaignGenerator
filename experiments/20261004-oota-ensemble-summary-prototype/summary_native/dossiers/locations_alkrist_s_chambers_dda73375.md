### Chapter 060 — Alkrist’s Chambers

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/060-session-never-mind-the-murders.md`
- Original heading: `Alkrist’s Chambers`

Lavishly decorated quarters filled with expensive furniture, fine materials, fashionable artwork, and Alkrist’s spellbook. The room contained sealed jars of dead rats with blistered, pustulated skin; a locked safe; and a bookmarked copy of *1001 Tashalian Nights* open to a tale about poisoning a commander with Midnight Tears applied to a gifted horn.
