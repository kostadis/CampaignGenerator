# NPCs — Drow Scouts

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 005 — Drow Scouts

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/005-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Drow Scouts`

Advanced trackers from the Velkynvelve prison sent ahead of the main force to locate and recapture the escaped prisoners. They have the party's scent and 120-foot darkvision. They encounter a sudden drop in the cavern floor during the chase but make their Dexterity save and avoid falling. They eventually lose the party's scent and double back down a different corridor. Killing them would not stop the hunting party behind them.
