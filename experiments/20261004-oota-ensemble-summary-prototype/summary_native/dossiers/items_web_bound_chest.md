### Chapter 007 — Web-Bound Chest

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/007-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Web-Bound Chest`

A mysterious chest discovered stuck in the webs near the end of the Silken Paths. Thorin immediately pegged it as a mimic, and the party chose to leave it undisturbed after an attempt to communicate with it through the spores failed. Zalthir later joked about sending the goblins back to retrieve gold from it.
