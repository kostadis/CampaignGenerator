### Chapter 003 — Toll the Dead

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/003-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Toll the Dead`

A ranged cleric cantrip available to Gyrgum. It forces its target to resist the sound of a dolorous bell and deals greater damage to a creature that has already been wounded, increasing from a d8 to a d12. Gyrgum turns to it after recognizing that Tasha’s Caustic Brew would also strike an ally in the crowded lower chamber.

### Chapter 016 — Toll the Dead

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/016-session-04-28.md`
- Original heading: `Toll the Dead`

Daz cast this necrotic cantrip against the centipede swarms twice during the ambush in the fungi forest. The first cast called for a DC 14 Wisdom save, against 9 necrotic damage on the already-wounded swarm; the swarm saved and took no damage. The DM summarized cantrips as "save or suck." The second cast hit a wounded swarm for 6 necrotic damage.

A cantrip cast by Daz against a wounded gray ooze. It requires the target to make a Wisdom saving throw and deals necrotic damage on a failure. The ooze fails its save, suffers 18 damage, and dies.
