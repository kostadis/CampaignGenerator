### Chapter 029 — Neverlight Grove

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/029-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Neverlight Grove`

A hidden, enchanted cavern in the Underdark filled with a brilliantly colorful bioluminescent mushroom forest covering every surface including the ceiling. A majestic mushroom tower can be glimpsed through rising mists across a ravine. It is the communal home of the myconids and is currently experiencing a schism between its two sovereigns due to a mysterious fungal infection.
- Most passages into the grove are narrow fissures. Only one tunnel, lit by glowing lichen, is fit for easy travel, and it opens above the grove floor.
- The cavern contains a lake.
- The grove has seven circles, each gathered around a circle mound.

### Chapter 031 — Neverlight Grove

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Neverlight Grove`

A subterranean fungal forest where the myconid colony resides, filled with various circles dedicated to different societal roles and increasingly corrupted by dark spores. ### Chapter 032 — Neverlight Grove

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/032-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Neverlight Grove`

A once-beautiful fungal sanctuary now falling into decay and madness as Zuggtmoy’s influence takes hold. The party witnesses the betrayal of its Sovereign Phylo, a violent confrontation between its leaders, and the collapse of the circle before fleeing.

As the survivors escape, it becomes clear that the grove’s former glory and beauty have been fully consumed by Zuggtmoy’s evil.

### Chapter 039 — Neverlight Grove

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Neverlight Grove`

A location the party has previously visited and one of the places where it encountered evidence of demonic corruption.

Kazook Pickshine needs a large quantity of ingredients from the grove to prepare a treatment that protects weapons and armor from ooze acid. The party chooses to revisit the grove rather than rely on trade with Gracklstugh.

Travel to the grove is treated as familiar enough that the route itself need not occupy substantial time.
