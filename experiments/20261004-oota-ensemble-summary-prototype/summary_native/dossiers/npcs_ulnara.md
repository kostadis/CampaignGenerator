# NPCs — Ulnara

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 022 — Ulnara

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/022-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Ulnara`

A derro savant and fervent cultist of Demogorgon who carries out macabre experiments on the undead. She is borderline insane. She came out with a hulking zombie to confront the party after Skiit's screaming alerted her, and she questioned them like a bureaucrat demanding to know their boss.
- Daz's display of violence, together with her fear of drow, turned her fawning: she called Daz "your lordship" and praised the name Ember Vanguard.
- She thanked the party for the "excellent supplies" and asked them to tell Plinki she had been "nothing but a wonderful ally."
- Skiit called the zombie work her "big project" when Gyrgum posed as a supervisor.
