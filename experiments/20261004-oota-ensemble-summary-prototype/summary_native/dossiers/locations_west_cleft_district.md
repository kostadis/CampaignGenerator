### Chapter 015 — West Cleft District

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/015-session-04-14.md`
- Original heading: `West Cleft District`

A dark, dangerous, and foul-smelling ghetto carved haphazardly into the cavern walls, serving as the chaotic and crowded home for Gracklstugh's unwanted Derro population. The party entered here with Buppido to track Droki.
- It was originally the home of the city's derro slaves. The East Cleft District was settled after the derro won their freedom.
- The smell shifts from metallic to repugnant at the gate, and "the fires of industry" are "replaced by squalor."
- It holds an entrance to the Whorlstone Tunnels, which explains why the Deepking cannot root out the factions below.
