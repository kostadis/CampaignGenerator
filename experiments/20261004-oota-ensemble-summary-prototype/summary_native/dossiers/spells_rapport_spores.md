# Spells — rapport spores

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 002 — Rapport Spores

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Rapport Spores`

A natural ability of Stool that creates a shared telepathic link among creatures within fifteen feet of the Myconid. It functions as a “party line” rather than a private one-to-one connection. Because the ability belongs to Stool, the prisoners must remain close to him and rely on his cooperation to use it.

### Chapter 003 — Rapport Spores

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/003-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Rapport Spores`

A natural myconid ability used by Stool to establish telepathic communication among intelligent creatures gathered near him. The effect lasts for one hour, which the party observes is effectively an entire adventuring day when measured against six-second combat rounds.

The link allows creatures to communicate silently with selected participants rather than forcing a universal broadcast. It enables the prisoners in the cell to know when Zalthir and Daz are discovered and to coordinate their immediate rush toward the guard tower.

### Chapter 005 — Rapport Spores

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/005-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Rapport Spores`

A magical spore cloud released by Stool that establishes a shared telepathic link among nearby creatures, allowing the party to communicate silently without alerting their Drow pursuers during the chase. It was suggested by the GM, and Zalthir later used it as the "party line" to point everyone to the hiding spot.

### Chapter 006 — Rapport Spores

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/006-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Rapport Spores`

A natural myconid ability used by Stool to establish a telepathic link, allowing the party to communicate with Fargas Rumblefoot while he was still trapped inside the spider web cocoon. The spores last about an hour, so by nightfall they could not be used to overhear Topsy and Turvy.

### Chapter 031 — Rapport Spores

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Rapport Spores`

A natural myconid ability used by Basidia to establish a telepathic link with the party, allowing them to communicate and sense the surface thoughts and emotional states of others, including the corrupted myconid leaders. Through the link, the party hears an echo of the discordant cacophony inside Yrberop's mind. Basidia confirms that the rapport spores are hers, not Yrberop's, reassuring the party that the telepathic connection is not itself exposing them to his corruption.
