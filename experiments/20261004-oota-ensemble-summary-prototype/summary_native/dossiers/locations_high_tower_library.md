### Chapter 058 — High Tower Library

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/058-session-the-case-of-the-missing-tickles.md`
- Original heading: `High Tower Library`

A restricted area of Candlekeep that the party was told requires two special keys to enter. They believed that one key had been kept in a locket worn by Janussi and stolen during the murder, and that the Gatewarden, Kalan, held the other. A separate prophecy—*“On the longest night, the keeper falls”*—appeared to concern the timing of Janussi's death.
