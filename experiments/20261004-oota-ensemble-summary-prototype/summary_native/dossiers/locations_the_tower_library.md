### Chapter 069 — The Tower Library

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/069-session-one-manshoon-dead-at-least-one-or-two-more-to-go.md`
- Original heading: `The Tower Library`

A room within the basalt tower filled from floor to ceiling with every manner of evil and powerful tome imaginable. The books include materials capable of improving extraordinary abilities and contain knowledge of enormous value to an academic such as Manshoon.

The collection is heavily protected by magical wards and *Glyphs of Warding*. The party understood that mishandling the books could produce multiple explosions, potentially triggering a catastrophic cascade throughout the library.
