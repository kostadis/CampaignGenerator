### Chapter 013 — Gartokkar Xundorn

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/013-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Gartokkar Xundorn`

A duergar cleric and high-ranking representative of the Keepers of the Flame. He is analytical and literal-minded, capable of dissecting a joke to death. He wields significant legal authority in Gracklstugh, and a flash of his badge sends onlookers away. He claims the Deepking treats the Keepers as his most prized advisors. He sought to recruit the party for an unspecified task, asking them to come to his quarters by the following day. In return he offered city passes and limited legal protection, hedged with "a standard set of disclaimers."

### Chapter 014 — Gartokkar

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/014-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Gartokkar`

A high-ranking member of the Keepers of the Flame who serves as a liaison between Themberchaud and the Duergar leadership. He revealed to the party that the Keepers secretly control the dragon and are desperate to recover a stolen red dragon egg before Themberchaud discovers he is being groomed for replacement. He threatened the party with fabricated charges if they refused the mission.

He believes the Gray Ghosts caused the psionic disturbance in the Underdark. He claims the old dragon will be "set free" once replaced. He tolerates Thorin's needling with a mix of alarm and grudging amusement.

### Chapter 017 — Gartokkar Xundorn

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/017-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Gartokkar Xundorn`

The intense and politically driven head of the Keepers of the Flame. He is desperate to secure the red dragon egg and destroy the Gray Ghosts, using gold, badges, and severe threats to ensure the party's compliance and discretion. He wants the egg, a Demogorgon statuette, and incriminating paperwork as political leverage against the Gray Ghosts. He is short, as duergar are, but stood up to Gyrgum over his poor dealmaking. He is prone to flashes of near-genocidal madness, and he fears that news of the egg's survival could strip away his leverage over Themberchaud.
