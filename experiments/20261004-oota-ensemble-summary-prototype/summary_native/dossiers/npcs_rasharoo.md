# NPCs — Rasharoo

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 032 — Rasharoo

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/032-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Rasharoo`

A Myconid who is horrified by the party’s report of the wedding rehearsal. Unlike Basidia, he immediately favors escape over confronting Phylo.

When the grove turns violent, Rasharoo cries, “If you want to live, come with me,” and leads survivors toward his escape route. He later provides myconid scouts capable of guiding the party safely through the first third of the journey to Blingdenstone. He also warns them that Ilvara’s drow hunters may still be gaining ground.
