### Chapter 037 — Pelek's bones

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/037-session-slime-spirits-and-svirfneblin-squabbles.md`
- Original heading: `Pelek's bones`

The skeletal remains of Pelek, carried by Gyrgum in a bag. They consist of “Catholic relic-sized” fragments rather than a complete skeleton, with no skull or full femur. Gyrgum jingles the bag while introducing Pelek's situation to Glyphic Shroomlight. The bones are ultimately placed in a quiet corner of the Blingdenstone catacombs and covered with dirt, allowing Pelek's spirit to pass on.
