# NPCs — Alaundo the Seer

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 069 — Alaundo

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/069-session-one-manshoon-dead-at-least-one-or-two-more-to-go.md`
- Original heading: `Alaundo`

A legendary, long-dead seer whose recorded voice provides the final chamber's instructions. Daz recognized the voice as artificial or conjured because it did not breathe and showed a slight fluctuation in vocal timbre.

Alaundo's voice explained that the Obsidian Door has no conventional lock because “a lock is a promise that a thing can be stolen.” Instead, he gave the door a question in place of a lock. Alaundo also left ten prismatic gemstones in a velvet tray, apparently containing prophecies, including one concerning a dwarf, the Hall of Stone, demons, an unlit forge, a giant-raised dwarf, and a Shadow Walker.

### Chapter 070 — Alaundo

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/070-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Alaundo`

A legendary seer whose prophecies were stored in ten gems; four had already played, leaving the six unplayed stones Manshoon threatened to destroy. His prophecies consistently come true and tend to be clustered around related events, suggesting that several of the remaining stones might concern the party.

One prophecy foretold Eldeth Feldrun's return to a hall of stone bearing the names of demons, followed by deep dwarves summoning Thorin, Zalthir, Daz, and Dawnbringer to an ancient forge. Alaundo also designed the last door's unusual protection, substituting a question and an act of freely given intent for a conventional lock.
