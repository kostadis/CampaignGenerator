# Locations — Janussi's Chambers

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 057 — Janussi's Chambers

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/057-session-candlekeep-s-cold-case-the-gyrgumite-gambit.md`
- Original heading: `Janussi's Chambers`

A spacious circular chamber at the top of a spiraling staircase within a tower of Candlekeep, ringed by a dozen lancet windows that fill the room with natural light. Features an elegant mahogany desk in the center, a seven-armed bronze candelabra, a marble statue (now toppled), a safe, and a wooden staircase leading to a second floor. The staircase ascending to the chamber passes a reception chamber, a shrine of Oghma, and a large apartment with an ajar door. The chamber was heavy with tobacco smoke at the time of the party's arrival and contained disturbed furnishings and other evidence of violence. A mysterious gray cat was found sleeping on the stairs below the chamber door; she opened her eyes without otherwise moving as the party approached.

### Chapter 061 — Janussi's Chambers

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/061-session-the-colloquium-was-well-received.md`
- Original heading: `Janussi's Chambers`

The private quarters of the late Keeper of Tomes. Their third floor served as a discreet “love nest,” consistent with Janussi’s history of affairs. Gyrgum found a note there demonstrating Janussi’s contempt for Kalan Strongbranch’s investigative methodology and his role in blocking Kalan’s career. The chambers were also the site of Janussi’s poisoning and death, followed by the removal of his heart and the theft of his locket and sapphire.
