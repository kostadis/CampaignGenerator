### Chapter 060 — Poisoned Book

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/060-session-never-mind-the-murders.md`
- Original heading: `Poisoned Book`

A volume from Daral’s private collection, wrapped and placed beneath the evergreen tree at approximately 11 a.m. on Deadwinter’s Eve. Janussi received it during the post-dinner gift exchange. Orrin observed that the string had been cut and retied, establishing that someone tampered with the package before Janussi opened it. The book is believed to have delivered Midnight Tears while simultaneously framing Daral.
