A Candlekeep clerk who admires Thorin’s kitchen investigation. He delivers Kalan Strongbranch’s paper on magical criminal investigations and argues that “Magic does not make evidence meaningful. It makes evidence adversarial.” He suspects opponents of rigorous investigation forced Kalan out and eagerly seeks Thorin’s endorsement for a future monograph.
