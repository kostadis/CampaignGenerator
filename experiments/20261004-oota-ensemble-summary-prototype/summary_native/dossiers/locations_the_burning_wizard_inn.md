### Chapter 056 — The Burning Wizard Inn

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/056-session-candlekeep-trials-truths-and-therapy.md`
- Original heading: `The Burning Wizard Inn`

An inn in Beregost named after a wizard who was burned alive during an earlier dragon cult uprising. The gnome innkeeper Festrum tells the story to anyone who buys a second pint, always pointing to a permanent soot mark on the south wall: "That is where he fell. We scrub the floor every season. We do not paint over the wall. My grandfather said the wall remembers." The inn is lively enough for a traveling bard to perform a full evening set.
