# NPCs — Errde Blackskull

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 013 — Errde Blackskull

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/013-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Errde Blackskull`

The captain of the Stone Guard in Gracklstugh. Observant and pragmatic, she arrived with four guards and noted she would have brought a whole platoon if she meant to arrest anyone. She commended the party's efficiency and Thorin's attempt to spare the stone giant. She tasked the party with tracking a rogue derro named Droki, suspecting a conspiracy involving the Council of Savants and the clan lairds. She offered equipment, gold, safe passage out of the city, and protection from pursuing drow in exchange for their help. She insisted the work was "for the city," and the party suspects she may be a little mad herself.

### Chapter 014 — Errde Blackskull

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/014-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Errde Blackskull`

Captain of the Stone Guard and the party's patron in Gracklstugh, who provides the party with store credit and tasks them with investigating the Empty-Scabbard Killers. She is deeply skeptical of the legend, believing the myth is being exploited to cover up government corruption, though she is personally motivated by the mysterious deaths of two members of her own clan. She is also hunting the derro messenger Droki, the same agent the Keepers of the Flame have now sent the party after.

She personally found one of the bodies, a clan member in the prime of health, and bristled when Gyrgum asked who had discovered it. She is consistent in her conspiracy thinking: "the powers that be know that I am onto their corruption." She refers to her subordinates, awkwardly, as "resources."

### Chapter 018 — Errde Blackskull

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/018-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Errde Blackskull`

The captain of the Stone Guard in Gracklstugh. She is tough and barely contains her seething rage while attempting charm.
- She seeks proof of the Gray Ghosts' and Council of Savants' conspiracies, and secretly wants evidence of the Assassin's Guild targeting her clan. She won't admit the guild exists, as it reflects poorly on her.
- Her deeper aim is evidence that would get the Deepking to sign off on a violent pacification mission against the Derro.
- She leaps from the flumph's images to a Gray Ghost–Council alliance, but concedes the point when Daz challenges her logic.
- She notices the egg in the flumph's visions without recognising it as the red dragon egg.
- She praises Daz as leadership material.
- She agrees to arm the party's allies in exchange for evidence, and directs the party to visit Clan Ironhead and Clan Xardelvar as cover for their true mission.

### Chapter 019 — Errde

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/019-session-05-20.md`
- Original heading: `Errde`

### Chapter 021 — Errde Blackskull

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/021-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Errde Blackskull`

The leader of the Stone Guard who seeks proof of the Council of Savants' corruption. The DM gave two scenarios:
- If told about the Assassin's Guild, she might try to kill the king.
- If told about the derro's demonic dealings, she would try to kill all the derro.

If the party left without her knowledge, she would likely set the drow on them. The party decides to lie to her, or "selectively respond," and withhold the incriminating list to avoid triggering immediate chaos.

### Chapter 022 — Errde Blackskull
