### Chapter 028 — Magical Sword

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/028-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Magical Sword`

A mysterious enchanted blade found resting atop a withered, mummified corpse inside the gilded sarcophagus in the lower chamber. Its discovery coincided with a disembodied feminine voice offering help to the party from within the sarcophagus. Zalthir spotted it after checking for "an obvious talking magical weapon," which the GM called a good question.
