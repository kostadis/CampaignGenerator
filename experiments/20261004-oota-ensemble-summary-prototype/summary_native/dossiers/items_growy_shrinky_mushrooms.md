### Chapter 025 — Growy Shrinky Mushrooms

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/025-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Growy Shrinky Mushrooms`

Fungi found in the Underdark that can alter a creature's size. The party gathered a pile of them and asked whether the sketchy mushrooms of Rumpadump's theory, the ones the Myconids ate after teleporting, still existed. The DM confirmed these are "the mushrooms that grow and shrink you," the same ones the party gathered, and that Stool had not eaten them.
