### Chapter 015 — Bigwigs

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/015-session-04-14.md`
- Original heading: `Bigwigs`

A four-inch tall mushroom with a thin stem and a wide purple cap found in the Whorlstone Tunnels. When consumed, it causes the eater to grow significantly in size.
- Its effect is comparable to an enlarge/reduce spell and is probably not permanent. The party took one.
