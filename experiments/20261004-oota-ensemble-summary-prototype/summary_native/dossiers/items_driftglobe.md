### Chapter 011 — Driftglobe

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/011-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Driftglobe`

A magical sphere recovered from the sunken chest that can emit light or daylight upon command and follow its owner. It is activated with a Magic action and does not require a magic-user, so anyone in the party can use it. Its light covers a 20-foot radius and its daylight a 60-foot radius. The daylight dispels overlapping magical darkness of 3rd level or lower.
