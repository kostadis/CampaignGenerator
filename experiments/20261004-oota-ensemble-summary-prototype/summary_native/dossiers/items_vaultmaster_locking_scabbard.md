## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 045 — Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/045-session-gnome-more-elemental-slavery.md`
- Original heading: `Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard`

A custom-made scabbard designed by Valimor Brightgem for Dawnbringer. It features a locking mechanism to prevent theft, and since Dawnbringer "doesn't quite have a blade," it hooks her in so she won't fall out. Valimor explicitly denied it was the "Vaultmaster 9000." Purchased by Thorin for 180 gold pieces after the 10% discount (200 before). Dawnbringer is pleased to finally have a proper scabbard to fit in.

### Chapter 053 — Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard

A prestigious locking scabbard purchased for Dawnbringer despite the sunblade's lack of a conventional physical blade. The party previously joked about a scalable line of upgrades, including a “Vaultmaster 2000.” Reminding Dawnbringer of the scabbard helped persuade her to participate in purifying the serpent-headed scourge.
