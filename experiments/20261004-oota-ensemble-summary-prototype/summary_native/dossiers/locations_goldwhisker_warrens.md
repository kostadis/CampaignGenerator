# Locations — Goldwhisker Warrens

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 038 — Goldwhisker Warrens

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/038-session-wererats-wisdom-and-the-war-on-ooze.md`
- Original heading: `Goldwhisker Warrens`

An area to the north of the Traders' Grotto, described as contested territory occupied by wererat gnomes who are considered squatters by some. The party is tasked with bringing this clan on board to help fight the oozes.

The Goldwhiskers occupied the abandoned territory after the drow withdrew but before Clan Diggermattock returned and reclaimed Blingdenstone. Dorbo and his supporters deny their legitimacy, while Senni favors reconciliation. Their lycanthropic resilience makes them valuable potential allies against an ooze army. The party ends the session preparing to secure their participation under the adventurers’ own command.

### Chapter 039 — Goldwhisker Warrens

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Goldwhisker Warrens`

Home of Clan Goldwhisker, a community of unaffiliated wererat gnomes. The warrens are accessed through locked, monumental double doors roughly fifty feet high.

Within is a raised audience area covered by soft, well-manicured green moss. Chief Chipgrin's stone chair stands there, draped with a cave badger pelt and flanked by two giant rats.

The party's accepting treatment of the wererat guards creates an unusually positive first impression before its negotiations with Chipgrin.
