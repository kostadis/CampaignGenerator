### Chapter 004 — Giant's Tooth Pendant

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/004-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Giant's Tooth Pendant`

A sentimental keepsake belonging to Thorin, carved down from a giant's tooth into a smaller pendant that reminds him of his family. Because he wore it around his neck and it was not absurdly large, the drow did not confiscate it with the rest of his equipment.
