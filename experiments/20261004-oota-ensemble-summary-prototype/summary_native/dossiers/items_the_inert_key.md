### Chapter 062 — The Inert Key

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/062-session-academic-vindication-and-an-assault.md`
- Original heading: `The Inert Key`

A mysterious key that appears non-magical under detection spells but becomes the apparent objective of the helmed horror assassins. Gyrgum had passed it to Daz earlier in Fembris's presence. The horrors ignore convenient targets and immediately converge on Daz, revealing that they either know or can sense who currently possesses it.

After the constructs nearly kill him, Daz passes the key back to Gyrgum. Their attention shifts accordingly, demonstrating that they are tracking the key rather than Daz personally. They do not know the key is inert. As of session end, the party has not determined whether the key is genuine, why it reads inert, how the horrors located it, who sent them, or whether one of the ruined constructs might be used to locate the matching key.
