### Chapter 061 — Polymorph

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/061-session-the-colloquium-was-well-received.md`
- Original heading: `Polymorph`

A transformation spell considered as the likely means by which someone assumed Sylvira’s appearance. It can affect a creature within sixty feet, but its standard one-hour duration does not comfortably explain sightings of Sylvira’s double spanning multiple hours. The party considers repeated castings, a special variant, or some more powerful transformation effect.
