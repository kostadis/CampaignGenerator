### Chapter 005 — Breath Weapon (Fire)

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/005-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Breath Weapon (Fire)`

Zalthir exhaled a line of intense flame to scorch the carrion crawler during the ambush, using it twice over the course of the fight. The GM corrected a declared cone to a line. The first blast dealt 10 damage on a failed Dexterity save, and the second did only 2 damage when the crawler made its save.
