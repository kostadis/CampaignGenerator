### Chapter 025 — Stone Giant Statue

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/025-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Stone Giant Statue`

A ritual object related to the magical affliction causing stone giants to grow second heads. One statue was completed while a second was interrupted by the party. The party believes it can be brought to the stone giant leader Hgraam to potentially reverse the curse, or at least to give him the information he needs.
