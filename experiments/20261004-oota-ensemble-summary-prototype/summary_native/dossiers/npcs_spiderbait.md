### Chapter 006 — Spiderbait

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/006-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Spiderbait`

Yuk Yuk's goblin companion and fellow guide through the Silken Paths, known for his daring maneuvers weaving between spider legs on the webs. He negotiated the deal for the goblins to guide the party in exchange for a share of future gold. He teases Yuk Yuk for taking "the easy route."

### Chapter 007 — Spiderbait

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/007-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Spiderbait`

A goblin guide and companion to Yuk Yuk, equally comfortable traversing the dangerous web networks of the Underdark. He suggested going back to see the spiders again. He demands his share of the gold alongside Yuk Yuk and joins the party's journey when Jimjar promises future payment.

### Chapter 010 — Spiderbait

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/010-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Spiderbait`

A goblin ally, one of the two goblin guides who helped the party across the Silken Paths; the other, Yuk Yuk, has since been murdered. The GM noted he is the one the party owes money to. He was briefly grappled and nearly kidnapped by duergar slavers during the skirmish but was rescued. Afterward, he expressed a strong desire to leave the group, citing the death of his best friend and the trauma of recent events.

### Chapter 054 — Spiderbait

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/054-session-04-28.md`
- Original heading: `Spiderbait`

A small goblin who has never been on the surface before and is genuinely excited about it — until the party informs him that goblin-surface relations are not as harmonious as he had assumed. He asks to travel with the party to Candlekeep in hopes of finding safe, innocuous work.
