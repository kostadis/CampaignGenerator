# Locations — The Ritual Cavern

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 023 — The Ritual Cavern

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/023-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Ritual Cavern`

A large cavern filled with the acrid stench of brimstone and fungi, illuminated by flickering campfires. A raised platform at the center serves as the site for the Derro's dark ritual, and fungal alcoves provide hiding spots for lurking creatures. The ritual circle imposes a Wisdom save against a momentary madness on anyone standing in it. Locked chests are scattered about. The party observed the ritual here, pinned the location on their map, and later ambushed Narrak and his cultists.
