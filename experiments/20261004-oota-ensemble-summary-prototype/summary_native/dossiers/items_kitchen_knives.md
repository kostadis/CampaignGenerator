### Chapter 002 — Kitchen Knives

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Kitchen Knives`

Two mundane knives stolen from the drow food preparation area by Topsy and Turvy during Gyrgum's second kitchen shift. They can be treated as ordinary daggers and distributed to whichever prisoners can use them effectively. Gyrgum sharpened them. The group jokingly described them as “magical plus zero” weapons with no special powers.

Gyrgum's first attempt to steal a knife was noticed when he held it up conspicuously. He escaped punishment by claiming he intended to sharpen it and relying on the goodwill generated by his excellent meal.
