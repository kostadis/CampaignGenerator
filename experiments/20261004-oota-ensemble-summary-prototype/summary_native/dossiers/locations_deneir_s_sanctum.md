# Locations — Deneir's Sanctum

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 059 — Deneir's Sanctum

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/059-session-questions-answers-and-a-key.md`
- Original heading: `Deneir's Sanctum`

A shrine and library dedicated to the art of writing and study, provided to the party by Bookwyrm as a private office for their investigation. The room's name derives from the deity Deneir. Vooshadi Moonriver is posted outside as guard; Bookwyrm noted no one she does not want will be permitted to enter.

### Chapter 062 — Deneir's Sanctum

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/062-session-academic-vindication-and-an-assault.md`
- Original heading: `Deneir's Sanctum`

A chamber within Candlekeep furnished with bookshelves and tables. Bookwyrm confines the party here under the stated justification of protecting its members and the evidence they possess. Fembris Lancer remains as their minder, food is delivered toward evening, and Daral is cured here.

The chamber remains locked even after Daral attempts to leave. Its supposed security is violently disproved when three flying helmed horrors smash through the windows and door in pursuit of the mysterious key.

### Chapter 063 — Deneir's Sanctum

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/063-session-the-key-is-secured.md`
- Original heading: `Deneir's Sanctum`

A supposedly secure room within Candlekeep where the party was escorted for protection and perhaps to prevent further investigation. The door was locked from outside. Three helmed horrors smashed through the windows and door, targeting Daz for the key he carried and leaving the chamber filled with broken armor and wreckage. The destroyed door later offered Daral a route of escape, though he chose to remain with the party.
