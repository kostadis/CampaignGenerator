# NPCs — Shuushar the Awakened

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 002 — Shuushar the Awakened

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Shuushar the Awakened`

A calm, articulate kuo-toa prisoner who translates between the drow and captives who do not speak Undercommon. Shuushar deliberately filters the drow's most colorful insults to preserve his spiritual and mental balance, though he still conveys their general contempt. He knows a route toward Darklake and may be able to guide the group there after the escape. Gyrgum's cooking impressed him enough to “penetrate” his calm demeanor and forced affectation.

### Chapter 003 — Shuushar the Awakened

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/003-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Shuushar the Awakened`

A kuo-toa prisoner who arms himself with one of the limited hand crossbows. He joins the assault from above and successfully strikes the Drow Elite Warrior for five points of damage, contributing to the accumulation of wounds that finally brings Imbros down.

### Chapter 004 — Shuushar the Awakened

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/004-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Shuushar the Awakened`

A kuo-toa hermit and pacifist who seeks to return to his home at Sloobludop near the Darklake. He favors the Darklake route, refuses to follow Ront into a suicidal fight with the vrock, and offers solemn spiritual commentary on Underdark flora, claiming that the Tongue of Madness allows an eater to “connect with the gods.”

### Chapter 005 — Shuushar

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/005-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Shuushar`

A kuo-toa prisoner who was rescued from the rockfall and was among those most likely to die there. He assisted in the fight against the carrion crawler using a crossbow and becomes visibly distressed by the presence of Faerzress in the tunnels. Despite his pacifism, he fired a hand crossbow here too.

### Chapter 009 — Shuushar

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/009-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Shuushar`

A pacifist kuo-toa companion who seeks enlightenment. Deeply shaken when an escapee tells him that what rose was Demogorgon, not the Leemooggoogoon his people believed they had summoned, he ultimately leaves the party to lead a group of kuo-toa refugees and establish a new, peaceful society. He was struck by madness at Sloobludop and carried to the boat. He bids an emotional, sticky farewell to the party, offering a final lesson in mindfulness to Buppido: "remember to always practice your mindfulness."
