# Locations — Drow Dining Hall and Kitchen

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 002 — Drow Dining Hall and Kitchen

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/002-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Drow Dining Hall and Kitchen`

A cave containing four circular zurkhwood tables with five chairs each. The adjoining preparation and storage area holds fresh and dried fungi, dried fruit, cheese, preserved meat, and clay jars of spices. A heavy iron brazier provides heat and dim light, although much of the food is served cold. Drow warriors eat, gamble, and play cards here while prisoners prepare their meals. Gyrgum's celebrated cooking earned him grudging approval and gave Topsy and Turvy an opportunity to steal two knives.
