### Chapter 037 — Sergeant

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/037-session-slime-spirits-and-svirfneblin-squabbles.md`
- Original heading: `Sergeant`

A gnome guard who gives the party directions and leads them to the Traders' Grotto. He provides a proud, colorful tour of the market, recounting its petrified fungi, medusa-related history, merchant stalls, and underground warehouses. He also identifies the wall beyond which the ooze problem can be investigated, though he initially points out the wrong area before correcting himself.

### Chapter 038 — Sergeant

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/038-session-wererats-wisdom-and-the-war-on-ooze.md`
- Original heading: `Sergeant`

A local authority figure who previously escorted the party. He is nervous about the party's revolutionary tactics and tries to manage public opinion by crediting Chief Dorbo Diggermattock for hiring them, while also urging them to continue their mission to solve the ooze problem.

He repeatedly assures the crowd that “Chief Dorbo Diggermattock is on top of everything” and portrays the adventurers’ victory as proof of Dorbo’s wisdom in employing them. He becomes uncomfortable when citizens criticize the chief or when the party suggests spending public wealth to rebuild damaged shops. Although he calls that proposal worthwhile, he quickly redirects the adventurers toward their mission. He dismisses Clan Goldwhisker as wererat squatters without rights or legitimate clan standing.
