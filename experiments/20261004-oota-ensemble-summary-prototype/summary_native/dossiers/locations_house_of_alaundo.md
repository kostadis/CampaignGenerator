# Locations — House of Alaundo

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 067 — House of Alaundo

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/067-session-08-17.md`
- Original heading: `House of Alaundo`

A building in Founders Court whose ground floor stretches to the top with a gallery mezzanine. It features a large brass statue of Alaundo the Seer on a dais, which conceals a hidden staircase triggered by filling the statue's empty ink pot. Generations of scholars touched its quill for academic luck without discovering the mechanism.
