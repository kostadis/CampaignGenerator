### Chapter 041 — Drow Statue Chamber

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/041-session-mirror-mirror-on-the-wall-who-s-the-maddest-of-them-all.md`
- Original heading: `Drow Statue Chamber`

A large room filled with many statues of Drow warriors standing as if on guard.

Six statues animate and attack when the party enters, with more animating as others are defeated.
- The six nearest the entrance animate first.
- The statues stop moving when the party leaves.
