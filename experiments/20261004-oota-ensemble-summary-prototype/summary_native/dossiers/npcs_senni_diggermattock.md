### Chapter 036 — Quartermaster Senni Diggermattock

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/036-session-not-an-ooze-just-a-cube.md`
- Original heading: `Quartermaster Senni Diggermattock`

Chief Dorbo Diggermattock’s female assistant and fellow authority in Diggermattock Hall. She sits at the second stone desk on the dais and confers closely—and at times heatedly—with Dorbo.

Senni directly links the party’s desired assistance to Blingdenstone’s immediate crisis. She says the clan would ordinarily be delighted to help them reach the Overbright, but its community is suffering from an ooze problem. When the party volunteers interest in an ooze-related quest, she deftly treats that offer as permission to send them: “Since you so graciously volunteered to go look at it, we won’t feel at all encumbered or guilty about asking you to do that before we help you.”

### Chapter 038 — Senni Diggermattock

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/038-session-wererats-wisdom-and-the-war-on-ooze.md`
- Original heading: `Senni Diggermattock`

Senni serves as Blingdenstone’s civilian leader, representing its non-mining population. A citizen describes her as an advocate for the people, contrasting her with Chief Dorbo's perceived greed. She supports the adventurers and their plan, often mediating Chief Dorbo's objections. She advocates for cooperation with the Clan Goldwhisker and ultimately tasks the party with securing their alliance.

Senni quickly grasps the tactical value of Zalthir’s Shadow Step demonstration and articulates the plan more clearly than Dorbo: the gnomes will occupy the ooze army while Zalthir infiltrates, grabs the Pudding King, and kills or extracts him. When Dorbo calls the idea foolish, she asks whether he has a better one. She pushes for a full clan meeting involving the Stoneheart Enclave, the Miners’ Guild, and Clan Goldwhisker, arguing that the wererats’ lycanthropic resilience makes them especially valuable. After Daz assumes operational command, Senni accepts the arrangement and gives the party authority to recruit the Goldwhiskers.

### Chapter 039 — Senni Diggermattock

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/039-session-from-exasperation-to-expedition-blingdenstone-s-new-hope.md`
- Original heading: `Senni Diggermattock`

Aligned with Blingdenstone's broader population, including its merchants, farmers, settlers, and ordinary residents. She emphasizes quality-of-life concerns while Dorbo emphasizes profit.

Senni is fully supportive of Daz's direct approach and the plan to unite the settlement's factions against the ooze threat. She speaks in favor of common community and shared purpose, though Daz reframes this as the need for “a common outcome and a common drive to that outcome.”

At the moot, Senni treats Daz as the emerging leader and asks him to call the meeting to order. She considers Earth Elemental support an important safeguard for Blingdenstone's fighters and favors preparations that reduce avoidable casualties.

### Chapter 045 — Senni Diggermattock

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/045-session-gnome-more-elemental-slavery.md`
- Original heading: `Senni Diggermattock`

A representative of the workers and people of Blingdenstone who do the mining. She is enthusiastic about the party's proposal to prevent the use of Earth Elementals for hard labor. She sees it as addressing fundamental problems with an economy built on summoned creatures: "this is exactly the problem with that, an economy built on summoned creatures." She seizes control of the meeting from Chief Dorbo after the party's persuasive arguments, credited with bringing Chipgrin aboard and forming "a coalition of the willing" to save Blingdenstone from the Pudding King.

### Chapter 046 — Senni Diggermattock

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/046-session-zalthir-s-daring-grapple.md`
- Original heading: `Senni Diggermattock`

A people-focused gnomish leader deeply concerned about the safety of Blingdenstone's civilian areas and the community she has worked to build. She worries that without a diversion, the party's failure could result in the town being overrun, and that her reforms might fall apart if the party dies. She cares about the party members. She also warns that undistracted oozes could swamp the party with "an almost infinite supply."
