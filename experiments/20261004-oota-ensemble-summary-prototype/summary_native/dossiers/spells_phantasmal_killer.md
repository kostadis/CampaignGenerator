A fourth-level illusion spell in Gyrgum's arsenal, discussed during planning as an option to frighten and deal psychic damage to Ilvara, though ultimately not used this session. It has a 120-foot range, a Wisdom save at DC 15, and deals 4d10 psychic damage per failed save for up to a minute.

### Chapter 068 — Phantasmal Killer

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/068-session-08-24.md`
- Original heading: `Phantasmal Killer`

Cast by Daz at fourth level at the start of combat against Manshoon, weaving a terrifying mental assault designed to manifest the wizard’s deepest fears. Daz’s casting would deal 26 psychic damage and impose disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls if Manshoon failed.

Manshoon rolls an 8 but adds +9 Wisdom, exactly meeting the DC 17 save. Under **PHB 2024 (p. 304)**, a successful save still deals **half damage and ends the spell**. Therefore **13 psychic damage stands**, Manshoon avoids the disadvantage rider, and Daz’s concentration is free.

### Chapter 069 — Phantasmal Killer

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/069-session-one-manshoon-dead-at-least-one-or-two-more-to-go.md`
- Original heading: `Phantasmal Killer`

A terrifying illusion cast by Daz to strike at Manshoon's mind at the start of the encounter. Manshoon made his later saving throw, limiting the damage to thirteen points rather than suffering the spell's full continuing effect.

The wizard had considered using his reaction and a spell slot on *Counterspell* but chose to risk the saving throw instead.
