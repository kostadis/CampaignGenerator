### Chapter 044 — Deep Gnome Miners

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/044-session-from-zuggtmoy-to-entemoch-s-boon.md`
- Original heading: `Deep Gnome Miners`

Three-foot-tall, beefy miners working in the Pickshine and Whiteshell mines. They expressed gratitude to the party for resolving the Galeb Duhr situation and were eager to use their specialized explosives (rude powder) to breach the time-snagged wall.
- The Whiteshell crew numbered some 30 or 40.
- They bristled when Gyrgum questioned their digging.
- They took credit for the perfectly placed charge.
- They growled at the order to get back to quota.
