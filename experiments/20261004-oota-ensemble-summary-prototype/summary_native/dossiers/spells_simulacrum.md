# Spells — Simulacrum

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 069 — Simulacrum

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/069-session-one-manshoon-dead-at-least-one-or-two-more-to-go.md`
- Original heading: `Simulacrum`

A complex spell that creates a partially real duplicate of a beast or humanoid from ice or snow. The duplicate uses the original creature's statistics but has only half its maximum vitality, is treated as a construct, cannot learn or grow more powerful, and cannot regain expended spell slots.

When reduced to zero hit points, a simulacrum reverts to snow and melts instantly. The party discovered the first Manshoon's true nature only after killing him. A second, more cheaply dressed simulacrum was subsequently found waiting in the upper tower chamber.
