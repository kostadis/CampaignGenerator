# NPCs — Derro Cultists

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 020 — Derro Cultists

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/020-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Derro Cultists`

Mad, fanatical underdark dwellers obsessed with the egg, believing that whoever possesses it holds absolute authority. They screamed about sacrificing the cleric of Bahamut and attacked the party with hooked spears and crossbows, though their coordination was hampered by the darkness. Per the GM, derro were once a slave race to the Mind Flayers, are predisposed to madness, and cooperate only "out of necessity and when riled up by a charismatic leader." Their hits could knock targets prone (DC 9 Strength save).

### Chapter 023 — Derro Cultists

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/023-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Derro Cultists`

A group of five Derro who chant in a discordant chorus to fuel Narrak's dark ritual. Most have about 13 hit points; two tougher ones have 27. They are fanatical but easily disrupted, and Narrak frequently berated them for their lack of vigor. Several were coated in acid and one was incinerated during the party's ambush. The death dog's handler avoided his boss's wrath by grooming the dog.
