### Chapter 021 — The Corrupted Chamber

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/021-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `The Corrupted Chamber`

A sinister room teeming with evidence of foul deeds and documentation linking the Council of Savants to local corruption. It functions as a lab rather than a lair, "not the kind of place you store your treasure." The party looted it after their victory, finding 170 gold and a mysterious dragon baby rattle.
