# Spells — Glyph of Warding

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 050 — Glyph of Warding

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/050-session-fungus-among-us.md`
- Original heading: `Glyph of Warding`

A magical trap inscribed by Gyrgum on a bridge, set to trigger if anyone approached from Jorlan's side of the cavern. It was cast at 3rd level with the explosive-runes option.

It explodes in a 20-foot radius, and targets make a DEX save against DC 15, taking 21 damage or 10 on a success. It must be inscribed in person and takes an hour.

When Jorlan was lured into it, it detonated in a large explosion, dealing area-of-effect damage to Jorlan and several nearby sporewalkers.

### Chapter 069 — Glyph of Warding

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/069-session-one-manshoon-dead-at-least-one-or-two-more-to-go.md`
- Original heading: `Glyph of Warding`

Magical traps placed upon the books in the tower library, designed to trigger destructive magic if the tomes are handled improperly. The party compared the danger to a book carrying a separate *Fireball* glyph on every page.

Hundreds of these glyphs line the upper chamber. Manshoon warned that an explosion there could trigger the other wards and cause a catastrophic chain reaction, making combat in the library dangerous for everyone.

### Chapter 070 — Glyph of Warding

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/070-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Glyph of Warding`

Numerous magical traps warding Candlekeep's dangerous tomes throughout the room. Edvaldo warned the party that if the simulacrum exploded inside the room, these glyphs would trigger a catastrophic chain reaction.

The glyphs were not themselves tied directly to the simulacrum's death. Manshoon's fail-safe would cause the initial explosion, which would then activate the surrounding wards. This is why Zalthir dragged the simulacrum out of the room and into the anti-magic field before disposing of him.
