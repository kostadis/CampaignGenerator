### Chapter 020 — Derro Spies

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/020-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Derro Spies`

Hidden attackers who used crossbows to ambush the party from the shadows. One severely wounded Eldeth with a crossbow bolt, while the other missed Daz. One spy, freaked out by the ghost, decided to run away. A spy (the tape does not say whether it was the same one) was later restrained by Daz's earthen hand, took 8 damage, broke free, moved to attack Daz, and was possessed by the party's ghost ally; the possessed spy is a derro with the spy's abilities.
