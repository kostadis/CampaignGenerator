# Locations — The Obsidian Tower

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 068 — The Obsidian Tower

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/068-session-08-24.md`
- Original heading: `The Obsidian Tower`

A three-story stone structure standing on a thin pedestal of glassy black obsidian rising from the lava lake. Its style resembles Candlekeep’s oldest spires and is sufficiently rare that Edvaldo regards the intact structure as worth the entire journey. It contains a guardian chamber, a vault of dangerous tomes, and an upper chamber holding the means to destroy the collection. Its stone walls are largely unadorned, but its interior is lit by *Continual Flame* sconces and guarded by four mounted crossbows.
