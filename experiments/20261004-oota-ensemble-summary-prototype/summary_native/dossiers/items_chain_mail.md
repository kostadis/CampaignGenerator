### Chapter 013 — Chain Mail

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/013-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Chain Mail`

Heavy interlocking metal rings selected by Thorin from the Stone Guard armory to replace his lighter chain shirt, providing superior protection at the cost of stealth. It costs 75 gp and raises his AC to 16, with disadvantage on stealth ("I'm not stealthy anyway").
