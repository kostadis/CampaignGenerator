# Locations — Southern Dining Hall

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 060 — Southern Dining Hall

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/060-session-never-mind-the-murders.md`
- Original heading: `Southern Dining Hall`

A large dining hall with long tables, an evergreen tree used for the Deadwinter gift exchange, and an alcove containing a statue of Milil holding a brass chalice. It hosted the twelve-course Deadwinter’s Eve supper beginning at the 5 p.m. bell. Daral left Janussi’s wrapped gift beneath the tree at 11 a.m., and Orrin later observed that its string had been cut and retied. Janussi’s severed heart and a blood-stained meat cleaver were found hidden in Milil’s lead-lined chalice.
