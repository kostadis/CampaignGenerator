# NPCs — Hebopbe

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 031 — Hebopbe

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/031-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Hebopbe`

The leader of the Circle of Growers. An obsessive farmer proud of his zurkhwood cultivation who is indifferent to the political drama between sovereigns. He has been infected by evil spores, though his behavior appears mostly as dismissiveness toward the growing danger. Hebopbe emphasizes that myconids are sentient beings rather than ordinary fungi and explains that the colony cultivates numerous foods suited to differing tastes and cooking styles. He favors specialization because it permits growers to discuss yields and favored crops without losing the attention of the entire colony.
