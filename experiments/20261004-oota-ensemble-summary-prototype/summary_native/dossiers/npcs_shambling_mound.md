# NPCs — Shambling Mound

## Exact Source Appendix

### Chapter 030 — Shambling Mound

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/030-session-untitled.md`
- Original heading: `Shambling Mound`

A Large creature made of moss and swamp matter. It camouflages itself in wet areas by flopping down as if it were part of a mossy swamp. It is resistant to fire and cold, and not immune to bludgeoning. It can electrify its tendril attacks, dealing bludgeoning and lightning damage, and can attempt to engulf creatures, blinding them. It has good wisdom but low intelligence. It carries no treasure, but its vines are valuable for crafting. The Myconids do not reanimate Shambling Mounds.

### Chapter 043 — Shambling Mound

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/043-session-harvesting-horror-in-the-underdark.md`
- Original heading: `Shambling Mound`

A creature resembling a mass of white bridal veils, gray mushrooms, and sweeping spores found tending to Ghost Hold Lichen growing from a dead giant. It has low wisdom but successfully resisted Hypnotic Pattern. It grapples opponents and deals bludgeoning damage, and moves 20 feet per round. It has AC 15 and is immune to the blinded condition. Zalthir's counter-grapple kept it from engulfing him.

### Chapter 044 — Shambling Mound

- Source: `/home/kostadis/out-of-the-abyss/out-of-the-abyss/docs/summaries/044-session-from-zuggtmoy-to-entemoch-s-boon.md`
- Original heading: `Shambling Mound`

A large plant creature composed of vegetation and detritus encountered in the Festering Fissure. It grappled Zalthir during combat and attacked Thorin. The creature was resistant to cold and fire damage and immune to lightning, making it a challenging opponent before Thorin's opportunity attack finished it.
- It has 75 hit points and AC 15.
- It used its reaction to try to lure Daz closer.
- It rolled two consecutive natural-20 saves.
- The DM gave it a defiant line: "with my last breath. I spit on thee."
- The DM compared its grapple with Zalthir to a hockey scrum.
