## PARTY ROSTER

**All four are 4th level** — advancement to level 4 came at the close of Chapter 11.
Zenvon's feat/ASI and additional prepared spell were still unchosen when the
session ended.

| Character | Player | Race / Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zenvon Forepot** | Nikhil | Halfling Rogue — **Arcane Trickster** | The **sole player character**. No darkvision. Took the archetype in Ch. ~8: *Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*, *Thunderwave*. Party face and scout. Carries **the black stone**. |
| **Pip Thistlewick** | DM-run sidekick | Human Fighter | No darkvision. Tank and negotiator. Wields **Talon**. Military-minded — reads enemy hierarchy and advises accordingly. Has a personal thread: a blackstone shard he sold and now dreams of. |

**Party standing:** the acknowledged **heroes of Phandalin** after breaking the
Redbrands. Toblen gives them free lodging; Sildar pays in full and offers more;
Daran and Garaele greet them as heroes.

**Current position (end of Chapter 11):** at **Old Owl Well**, having just
finished dinner with Hamun Kost. They have cleared Wyvern Tor and returned to
collect their payment — **and Hamun did not hand over the route to Cragmaw
Castle.** They have the castle's name and no road to it.

## KEY NPCs

### Allies & employers

- **Sildar Hallwinter** — Lords' Alliance agent. Rescued at 1 HP in Ch. 3,
  escorted to Phandalin, now a Phandalin-based employer rather than an escort.
  Has paid **250 gp in full** (50 escort + 100 upfront + 150 on the Spider's
  letter). Standing offers: **200 gp for Iarno alive / 100 dead**, and **500 gp
  to locate Cragmaw Castle and drive out its leaders**. Class-conscious —
  repeatedly backhanded about Zenvon's low birth ("for someone of your
  background, you have done well"). His remark on Pip's "fine, noble sword" read
  to Zenvon (Insight 20) as a hint at Lords' Alliance membership for Pip;
  **Zenvon has not explained this to Pip.**
- **Toblen Stonehill** — Proprietor, Stonehill Inn. Gave the party a free night
  after the Redbrands fell. Warned them off Conyberry, though an Insight check
  showed the warning was imagination rather than experience.
- **Elsa** — Dwarf barkeep at the Stonehill Inn. Proud of her scholar sister in
  Neverwinter, who may have known Orryn Voss. **The sister is never named.**
- **Daran Edermath** — Retired adventurer, orchard on the town edge. **A drow,
  over 500 years old.** Identified Glasstaff and the Tresendar hideout early.
  Offers **Boots of Striding and Springing** for investigating Old Owl Well —
  **still unclaimed**. Source of the party's Netheril background: Karsus, the
  breaking of the Weave, artifacts scattered across the north.
- **Sister Garaele** — Elf acolyte of Tymora at the Shrine of Luck. Nearly died
  approaching the banshee Agatha. Commissioned the party to deliver a **jeweled
  silver comb** to Agatha and ask after **Bowgentle's spellbook**, for three
  potions of healing.
- **Harbin Wester** — Town Master. Nervous, and blamed locally for inaction.
  Hired the party for the **Wyvern Tor marauders at 120 gp** (negotiated up from
  100 by Pip). Was impressed enough by Pip to offer him private work.
- **Elmina Barthen** — Barthen's Provisions. Gundren's close friend; paid the
  original 19 gp delivery fee and demanded the party go after him.
- **Mirna Dendrar** — Widow of Thel, freed from the Redbrand cells with her
  children **Nars (13)** and **Nilsa (18)**. Delivered to the outskirts of
  Phandalin. Gave up the **Thundertree emerald necklace** and asked the party to
  recover Thel's body — **Zenvon agreed; the promise is outstanding.**

### Ambiguous

- **Halia Thornton** — Guildmaster, Miner's Exchange. Congratulated the party,
  then cooled the instant she heard Glasstaff had *escaped* rather than died, and
  pressed to buy his correspondence for **100 gp**. Refused. *GM-side, recorded
  in the Ch. ~8 summary: her trade-protection line is a cover — she is a
  **Zhentarim** agent who wanted Glasstaff removed so she could take the
  Redbrands herself. **The party has learned none of this on screen.***
- **Hamun Kost** — **Red Wizard of Thay**, necromancer, excavating Old Owl Well
  with twelve zombie labourers (the **Kost Wardens**). Told Zenvon plainly:
  *"if I wanted my secrets to be safe, I would kill you."* Claims no interest in
  harming nearby settlements. Knows where Cragmaw Castle is. Traded that
  information for clearing Wyvern Tor — **and then served dinner instead of
  delivering it.** Reacted sharply to the crystal-headed goblin drawing, then
  claimed not to understand it; Pip caught suppressed excitement at mention of
  the black obelisk. Identified **Talon** as Aldith Tresendar's blade.
- **Warden Holl** — Hamun's former apprentice, now undead, serving dinner. Hamun:
  he serves better in death than in life.
- **Ssarnak (the nothic)** — Still alive in the Tresendar crevasse. Ate Rondar
  and four Redbrand dead. Answered to Glasstaff, who paid it in bodies. **Told
  Veyra her teacher is not dead — "not in a place where dead is easy."** Maela
  physically restrained her from going back to ask more.

### Antagonists

- **The Black Spider** — Unidentified. Ordered Gundren's capture for the Wave
  Echo Cave map; commands goblins, the Redbrands through Iarno, and a bugbear
  detachment. **Located at Cragmaw Castle**, per Nosk in Ch. ~8.
- **Iarno "Glasstaff" Albrek** — Lords' Alliance wizard turned Redbrand leader.
  **Escaped by teleport in Ch. ~6**, leaving behind the Spider's letter, his
  chest, and his workshop. Still at large. Sildar believes he fled to his master.
- **Nosk** — Bugbear leader of the Black Spider's reinforcement detachment
  ("the Scarlet Fist"). Rage-baited into naming Cragmaw Castle, then beat the
  party in a straight fight. **His fate is unconfirmed** — one of four bugbears
  died in the oil fire, and the casualty was never identified.
- **Brughor Axe-Biter** — Orc chief of the Wyvern Tor marauders. **Dead** (Ch. 11).
  Physically the weakest of his band but its actual commander.
- **Gog** — Ogre, Brughor's companion — Gog's grief established the relationship
  was romantic. Refused to flee. **Dead** (Ch. 11), killed by Pip after Zenvon's
  interrogation produced nothing.

### Dead or resolved

Yeemik (Ch. 3) · Klarg (Ch. 3a) · Rondar — fed to the nothic (Ch. ~6) ·
Dosa Rook (Ch. 7) · Wick — executed after interrogation (Ch. 7) ·
Brughor, Gog, and the Wyvern Tor sentry (Ch. 11)

## FACTIONS

- **The Redbrands** — **Broken as a Phandalin power.** Leadership gone
  (Glasstaff fled), barracks and cellars cleared, captives freed. Their scarlet
  cloaks remain a live asset: the crypt skeletons stand down for them, and the
  disguise has repeatedly opened doors.
- **The Black Spider's network** — Intact and partly mapped by the party: Cragmaw
  goblins, the Redbrands, and a bugbear detachment under one chain of command.
- **Lords' Alliance** — Employer, via Sildar. Wants Iarno tried at Neverwinter
  and Cragmaw Castle broken. Claims authority over Wave Echo Cave.
- **Cragmaw goblins** — Hideout destroyed (Ch. 3–3a). **Cragmaw Castle remains**,
  location unknown.
- **The Zhentarim** — Present in Phandalin through Halia Thornton. **GM-side
  only; the party knows nothing.**
- **Red Wizards of Thay** — Present through Hamun Kost at Old Owl Well.
- **Axe-Biter Marauders** — **Destroyed as a unit** (Ch. 11). Leaders dead;
  **four bugbears fled alive**, released deliberately.
- **The "altered" goblins** — Not a faction the party can name. Elongated skulls,
  filthy rags, a crystal in the skull in one drawing. Connected to **Ruxithid**,
  a name spoken once and never explained.

## LOCATIONS

- **Phandalin** — Home base. Party treated as heroes. Stonehill Inn, Shrine of
  Luck, Miner's Exchange, Barthen's Provisions, Edermath Orchard, Townmaster's
  Hall, the Sleeping Giant.
- **Tresendar Manor** — Cleared of Redbrands but **not secured**. Still contains:
  **Ssarnak** in the crevasse, **three wounded bugbears last heard hammering on a
  locked door**, **Droop** the goblin (left fainted in a room the party set on
  fire; fate unknown), and **Thel Dendrar's remains, never searched for**.
- **Cragmaw Hideout** — Cleared (Ch. 3–3a). Klarg and Yeemik dead.
- **Old Owl Well** — Netherese watchtower ruins NE of Phandalin. Hamun Kost's
  dig, twelve Kost Wardens. **Where the party is now.**
- **Wyvern Tor** — Cleared (Ch. 11). Contained the marauder cave and the
  **charcoal drawing of a crystal-skulled goblin**.
- **Cragmaw Castle** — **The main objective. Location still unknown.** The Black
  Spider is there (Nosk); Gundren is presumed there. Hamun knows the way.
- **Thundertree** — Ruined, overrun by undead. The Dendrar emerald necklace is
  hidden in the southeast parcel.
- **Conyberry** — Ruined by the Spellplague, then Uthgardt raiders. On the road
  to Agatha.
- **Agatha's Lair** — Near Conyberry. *GM-only, from the Ch. ~8 summary: Agatha
  traded Bowgentle's spellbook to the necromancer **Tsernoth** over a century
  ago. She answers one question.*
- **Wave Echo Cave** — The Phandelver Pact mine and the **Forge of Spells**.
  Location known only from Gundren's stolen map. The object of the conspiracy.

## NOTABLE ITEMS

- **The black stone** — Zenvon's, pocketed at the roadside in Ch. 1 and concealed
  from everyone until Ch. ~8. **Resonates with Veyra's blue crystal.** Disclosed
  to Veyra only.
- **Veyra's blue candle / crystal** — Reacts to obelisk fragments and psionic
  magic. Flared at: the roadside stone, writing "Ruxithid", Zenvon's black stone,
  and the charcoal goblin drawing. **Deliberately concealed from Hamun.**
- **Talon** — +1 longsword, Pip's. Aldith Tresendar's blade, "the Black Hawk" —
  identified by Hamun in Ch. 11, not by the party.
- **Pip's sold shard** — Won at dice near Neverwinter's docks, sold to a peddler
  bound for Phandalin. Pip dreams of black stone underground and hears his name.
  **Not in the party's possession. The peddler has never been found.**
- **The Black Spider's letter** — Spider-sealed, from Glasstaff's desk. Ordered
  Iarno to take the party's dwarven maps. Given to Sildar as proof.
- **Urmon's journal** — Dwarvish. Lost Mine, Forge of Spells, and **Lightbringer**,
  a mace lost with Wave Echo Cave.
- **Jeweled silver comb** — Garaele's gift for Agatha. Undelivered.
- **Scarlet Redbrand cloaks** — Worn by the whole party. Disguise, and skeleton
  pacifier.
- **Potions held:** healing, invisibility, climbing, growth (one healing was
  drunk in the bugbear fight).
- **Owed / unclaimed:** Daran's **Boots of Striding and Springing**; Garaele's
  **three potions of healing**; Harbin's **120 gp**; the **Thundertree emerald
  necklace**; the **Lionshield Coster goods**, recovered in Ch. 3a and *never
  returned* — they belong to **Linene Graywind**.

## OPEN THREADS & MYSTERIES

**The main line**
1. **Cragmaw Castle.** Named, never located. Hamun owes the route and did not
   deliver it at dinner. Black Spider and Gundren both presumed there.
2. **Gundren Rockseeker.** Taken alive at the Triboar Trail in Ch. 1 for his map.
   **Last mentioned in the session record in Ch. ~6** (directory `006`). Not seen, not found, not
   rescued. His fate is entirely unknown from the play record.
3. **Iarno "Glasstaff" Albrek.** Teleported away. Bounty live.

**The obelisk line**
4. **Orryn Voss.** Veyra's mentor. Ssarnak: not dead, "not in a place where dead
   is easy." Unexplained, and Veyra was dragged away before she could ask more.
5. **The black stone / blue crystal resonance.** Two objects, confirmed reactive
   to each other. No explanation.
6. **The altered goblins.** Elongated skulls in the crevasse; Sildar's
   interrogator at the ambush; a crystal-skulled goblin drawn on a cave wall at
   Wyvern Tor. **Hamun's manner changed when told about the drawing.**
7. **"Ruxithid."** Spoken once, by the elongated-headed goblin at the Ch. 1
   ambush: *"You're not what Ruxithid wants."* Never explained.
8. **Pip's shard and the peddler.** Open since Ch. ~5.

**Loose ends the party chose to leave**
9. **Three wounded bugbears, loose in Tresendar Manor.** Zenvon deliberately left
   them, curious what they would do.
10. **Nosk's fate unconfirmed.**
11. **Droop**, left fainted in a burning room.
12. **Thel Dendrar's body**, never searched for — a promise made to his widow.
13. **Halia Thornton's real agenda.** Party suspicious, and correct, but knows
    nothing.
14. **Wick's claim of deeper dungeon levels** beneath Tresendar — *"something
    really wrong over there that messes with your head."* **Unresolved in the
    session record.** He may have been describing the caverns and the nothic, but
    no summary says so.

**Commissions outstanding**
15. Agatha + the comb (Garaele) — and Hamun's second question for her.
16. Old Owl Well investigation for Daran — arguably answered, never reported.
17. Lionshield Coster goods, unreturned since Ch. 3a.
18. Harbin's 120 gp for Wyvern Tor — job complete; the Ch. 11 summary records it
    as **"now collectible on their return to Phandalin"**.
    *Neither Hamun nor the party ever remarked that the Wyvern Tor job was one
    they had already been paid to do.*
