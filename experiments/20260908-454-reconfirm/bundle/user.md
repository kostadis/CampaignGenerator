# A Banker's Revelation

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Outside the Spire of the Morninglord, the party meets with Aurelan Vance, who provides critical information regarding a missing gnome named Bimble.
- Aurelan Vance catches up with the party outside the Spire of the Morninglord, appearing out of breath after visiting the counting house, patting himself down into proper decorum before speaking.
- He explains what moved him: "Valphine's sermon was inspiring... it inspired me to action." He had planned to wait for the party at the bank, but instead went over Bimble's paperwork the very night before.
- Brewbarry and Aurelan establish a friendly rapport, with Aurelan asking, "Is it Mr. Brewbarry? Is that the correct appellation? I'm sorry, I'm not familiar with Guardian norms" — and Brewbarry replying, "So just call me Brew, that's fine." Aurelan reciprocates: "And since we're on such friendly terms, my friends call me Ori."
- Aurelan reveals he investigated paperwork for a gnome named Bimble, pulling out a piece of paper to show the correct spelling — which does nothing to stop the party from calling the gnome "Bimbo" for the rest of the evening.
- The banker explains that Bimble was on a maintenance roll for 11 years until payments abruptly stopped seven tendays ago — "on the day, not the tenday" — without severance, final reconciliation, or forwarding instructions: "That is not how a contract ends... Brew, that is how a contract is abandoned!"
- Aurelan notes that Bimble has not banked, borrowed, or left the city, suggesting someone is keeping him locally: "Your Harper friends would tell you the same if you had any, so somebody is keeping him."
- Aurelan advises the party to find who is feeding the gnome to find the gnome himself.
- Brewbarry grimly counters, "Sounds like he's feeding worms," but promises: "We'll get to the bottom of it, my friend."
- Soma remembers where she used to get the best turtle soup in the whole area — the Board Laid Bare, run by Lim — as a place with good prices, good food, and good atmosphere where a small-business proprietor might know how a hidden man could be fed.

# Arrival at the Spire of the Morninglord

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party arrives at the temple at dawn for Valphine's sermon and a performance by Vukradin's new band, finding a diverse and factional crowd already gathered.
- The DM frames the morning: Vukradin's announcement of the band's name and the impromptu performance with Valphine at the Moonstone Mask "has created quite the stir at the Spire of the Morninglord." Valphine has been invited to deliver the morning sermon — "Sermon first. Followed by music and sacraments." Soma: "It's a Unitarian service."
- The party arrives at the Spire of the Morninglord at dawn, finding a large and diverse crowd gathered at the threshold. "Everybody seems to have arrived very early in the morning," the DM notes; "this has turned into a thing."
- Awareness rolls: Valphine 28 insight; Brewbarry 15 perception; Vukradin a natural 1 ("My naivete is peaking").
- Valphine observes the crowd, noticing skeptical Lathanderite clerics scattered around the room and agents of Loviatar lurking in the shadows. The DM explains the Loviatarite grievance: the goddess of pain's followers regard the searing sacrament as stolen — "It's heretical."
- Lord Neverember is spotted among the crowd of faithful and curious onlookers, to the party's surprise. The DM stresses "factions within factions" — the ones they recognize are not all of them.
- Even Vukradin's perception notices that nobody seems to be armed.
- An armorsmith arrives to fit Valphine with her new gleaming white dragon scale armor just before she enters the temple, causing a stir among the onlookers. He "spent all night working on it"; the DM clarifies he isn't advertising — "he wants the blessing of Lathander... He's one of the devout." Valphine: "Which is a great advertisement for his business." Whispers and commentary follow as she puts it on; the smith is "very proud of himself."
- Brewbarry casts Thaumaturgy on himself — "my eyes light up with light. As I touched Valphine's shoulder" — and produces "a phantom sound coming from the door as we walk in... ominous Whispers, just for theatrics." Valphine's own gleaming eyes are visible to the onlookers.
- The DM offers options: walk in "like an avatar, eyes blazing" (religion check), pause to bless the crowd (persuasion), let Vukradin "do his shtick," or ignore them.
- Valphine casts a powerful daylight spell to illuminate the chamber and announce the start of the morning service, causing some in the crowd to squint: "I'm going to raise my hand up high. Casting a daylight spell, and say, it is time for the morning sermon." The DM notes people usually use Light for this; "you went the next level with Daylight." Vukradin: "Hey, it wasn't Sunbeam."
- Religion 16: a very positive impression. The crowd is "in awe of your spellcasting. It's not very often people see a daylight spell in the middle of the day inside of the Church"; some squint, and the DM assumes Valphine "admire[s] their pain for just a moment."
- Brewbarry whispers to Valphine to make sure they collect the donations at the end of the sermon. Vukradin: "I am here to serve, by the way, anything you want me to do, just ask."
- Brother Aldric meets the party in a private receiving chamber to discuss the theological implications of Valphine's new approach before the sermon begins. He "kind of goes like this and ponders... all the liturgical and ceremonial elements that you have forgotten," but is in a good mood. "You weren't supposed to do that."
- Aldric: "I've heard of the blessed temple that you've built in Phandalin... You keep talking about this blessing... What is the meaning of the blessing?" Valphine: "It allows us to gain a deeper appreciation of the glory and power of the Morninglord... We must gain a deeper spiritual communion."
- First persuasion (8, even with Bardic Inspiration and Bless): "Brother Aldric is not impressed... a little bit disappointed." Brewbarry steps in to help — eyes gleaming, ominous whispers from the back of the room — and the reroll with advantage lands an 18. Aldric accepts the theological points but warns: "I warn you to stay away from this path of pain. This is not the Lathander's way."
- A scarred sun priest interrupts: "is pain the point or the healing?" Valphine: "The light is the point. He is the Morninglord, the Lord of Light." Brewbarry: "Imagine life taught you that healing can be painful... But healing doesn't come cheap, my friend." Valphine: "Embrace the fullness of the light, you must experience it in its totality. Otherwise, it's just a pale shadow." Pressed on the purpose of healing: "Our Lord has great... ambitions for us on this world, and therefore, we must be able to then see the lit path."
- Valphine successfully defends her unorthodox views on pain and redemption to Brother Aldric and a skeptical sun priest, threading a theological trap set by the other cleric — a natural 20 on the second persuasion. Aldric smiles and makes the sign of the Morninglord; "the other cleric was there to trap you, but that you managed to thread the trap." Vukradin: "Going to the Sanhedrin, man."
- Valphine's answer, out of character, to how she got through: "By doing a good job with my theology."
