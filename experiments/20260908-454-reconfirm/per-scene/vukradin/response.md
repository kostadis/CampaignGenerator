I tell Harbin Wester to call the Privy Council. Not next tenday. Now.

He looks at me the way a man looks at a bill he did not expect. "So soon? So quickly? Right now?"

I lay it out for him properly — the dragon is dead, the orcs are driven off, the town is awake and grateful and this is the hour in which decisions get made — and I can hear the phrasing landing well, the clauses stacking the way they do when the case is sound. He hears it too. He simply does not care. So I drop the register and let him understand that I am not asking.

He turns around. "Well. Your intimidation lands as flatly as your sweet tongue would have landed well."

Right. The hubris is rising, and I let it. I do not need Harbin Wester to call a meeting. "I'll gather the people myself."

The way to gather people is not a proclamation nailed to a post. "We could put on a rock concert," I say to the others, "and get them all out there, get them all happy and bouncing around before I make my big speech."

"I can make my voice boom," Brewbarry offers, "to announce that there is a gathering." He can. It is one of the finest instruments in Phandalin and I have never once had to tell him how to use it.

So it is the green. An outdoor performance is never as good as an inside one — the sound goes up into the sky instead of coming back off the beams — but the crowd is drunker at this hour, and a drunk crowd is a persuadable crowd. Decent performance. Good enough.

[GM NARRATION — TO BE WRITTEN: the townsfolk and the council members gather on the green as the party plays — Harbin, Petunia, Big Al Kalazorn, Qelline Alderleaf, Linene Graywind, Ser Kaelen, Toblen and Elsa and the tavern staff, Barthen, the woodworker.]

[GM NARRATION — TO BE WRITTEN: the mood of the crowd — dancing, loose, relaxed.]

I let the last chord ring out and step up where they can all see me.

"Ladies and gentlemen, we are adventurers! We've killed the dragon! We drove out the orcs! We saved Phandalin! Yay!"

[GM NARRATION — TO BE WRITTEN: Brin and Giles are in the crowd, engaged and sunburnt again.]

"Kissed by Lathander," Soma says beside me, flat as a plate.

Now the speech. "What I wanna say is that your government, led by Wester over here—" and I make sure they all see which Wester I mean, "—believes that the way adventurers should work is that they should go do heroic things, and then claim all the treasure for themselves."

[GM NARRATION — TO BE WRITTEN: the crowd falls into a staring silence.]

Good. Silence is attention. "That treasure came from you. That is your treasure. It was stolen from you." I let it sit. "I want to give it back to you."

Linene Graywind raises her hand, because of course the one person on this green who runs a ledger for a living wants to check my arithmetic. "Well, strictly speaking, it may not have been stolen from me — but it may have been stolen from somebody else a long time ago. And so by you bringing it into the market… hold on, by you bringing it into the market — I get to… it recirculates. Right? And so when you spend that money, you're making it mine. Because when it's over there in the hands of the monsters—"

"Hang on," I tell her. "All your questions will be answered."

Jenna Roscoe, who I do not believe I have ever spoken to and who has the look of a woman with a letter from Neverwinter in her pocket, adds that this has all been very well covered in the dialectics of the financials. Yes. I'm sure it has.

Here is the thing they need to hear, and I say it slowly. "If we can find the rightful owner of that treasure, then it needs to be returned. But we have found, as adventurers, that many times we cannot find the rightful owners of this treasure." That is the honest truth of it. Chests off drowned ships, hoards under dragons, coin that has been in the dark so long there is no name left on it. "And so I swear to you, now, here: a new, universal, basic treasure."

"UBT…" Soma murmurs, trying the shape of it.

There is a moment on any stage when you feel the room decide. I put everything the College taught me behind those words, and I know it is the training and not the day that carries them — on my own I would have flubbed it badly, I could feel the flub sitting right there — but the training holds.

[GM NARRATION — TO BE WRITTEN: the crowd erupts in cheering, while Jenna looks appalled.]

I turn, so the whole green turns with me, toward the mayor. "And I would like to know what Harbin Wester thinks about this plan. Is he in favor of it, or does he need to be replaced?"

"I am so glad!" says Harbin. "This was a… this was… I'm…" He pauses. He gathers himself. "My good friend, and member of the Privy Council — V-Vukradin!"

He stutters on my name. He has to reach for it. "Sounds about right, yup."

He plows on before anyone can note it. "Because I think that that is an extraordinary idea, and that is why we have declared him extraordinary — we have declared the income that he produces as part of his adventuring career: Vukradin's UBT. The Privy Council had heard this proposal, and we were waiting for a few more days before we would make a formal announcement — but I think that since we didn't know it, this is as good a way to make an announcement as possible. All hail to Vukradin and the…" He stops. "What is the name of your adventuring group?"

Days. The Privy Council had been considering it for days. The Privy Council that would not meet this afternoon. I file that where I file everything about Harbin Wester and I do not lose the crowd over it, because the crowd is cheering, and cheering people get paid.

"Did we ever make one?" Soma asks.

"Slayers of Cryovain," I say, which is accurate and which has a rhythm to it.
