## Factions and Powers

### The Party's Own Identity

**Ember Grapple / Ember Vanguard**
- **Current name:** the party formally adopts "Ember Grapple" as its group identity for future missions, including telling Sylvira of her son's death [ch 064 / 064.01].
- **Earlier name:** "Ember Vanguard" was the party's adopted, fabricated identity from Gracklstugh [ch 021 / 021.04; ch 021 / Memorable Moments; ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / 026.03].
- **Reputation:** the rumoured Ember Vanguard became a legend among Derro and a feared rumour in Gracklstugh, blamed for the derro High Council murders [ch 021 / 021.04; ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / 026.03]. It was also taken for a feared agent group of the Drow Empire, with the party mistaken for them [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / Memorable Moments].
- **Conflicting rumours (GM note):** the rumoured descriptions disagree, and none of them is accurate:
  - a marauding drow army led by a 20th-level archmage [ch 021 / 021.04; ch 021 / Memorable Moments];
  - a red dragonborn and wizened drow assassins [ch 026 / 026.01];
  - Daz as an 18th-level mage [ch 027 / Memorable Moments].

### Drow Powers

**Lolth and her priesthood**
- **Worship:** Lolth is worshipped at Velkynvelve, where the shrine has a 10-foot idol with jet eyes [ch 002 / locations; ch 002 / items].
- **Distrust of mages:** drow clerics distrust mages who claim divine communion. Asha's suspicion reflects this [ch 050 / 050.01].

**Zuggtmoy's "bride" heresy**
- Ilvara follows this heretical cult, which turns followers into fungal servants [ch 050 / 050.02].
- Asha sees it as a betrayal of Lolth [ch 050 / 050.02].

**Drow (general) / Velkynvelve**
- **Culture:** ruthless slave-owners who value hierarchy. The highest-ranking hoard treasure, and public humiliation is used as a political weapon [ch 002 / 002.05; ch 002 / 002.06].
- **Velkynvelve's condition:** the outpost's guard tower has been overrun and its commander slain [ch 003 / locations].
- **Last noted move:** a hunt for the escaped prisoners, with darkvision-and-tracking scouts out ahead of a full hunting party [ch 005 / 005.04; ch 005 / npcs]. The later notes do not update this.

**House T'sarran**
- **Last known move:** it deployed agents to the surface to track Daz and identify his mysterious benefactor, not to kill him [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / npcs].
- **Earlier operation against Daz:** it sent a force to extract Daz alive. A signet ring and burned orders confirm this, and the party knows of the house's involvement [ch 053 / 053.04].
- **Losses and threat:** it suffered heavy losses, and its elite warrior retreated with a threat that suggests future retaliation [ch 051 / 051.04; ch 051 / 051.06].
- **Valen:** a scout from this house, whose infection reflects the house's weakness [ch 049 / 049.02].
- **Conflict (GM note):** the notes disagree on the house's standing.
  - ch 049 calls it a minor house loyal to Ilvara that is currently weak [ch 049 / 049.02].
  - ch 054 calls it a powerful house ruled for 200 years by its Matron Mother [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / npcs]. The later entry wins.

**House Duskryn**
- Actively protecting Daz [ch 051 / 051.03; ch 051 / 051.06].
- Kaelira and Nym showed lethal coordination and loyalty [ch 051 / 051.03; ch 051 / 051.06].

**House Mizzrym**
- Ilvara was its loyal agent and took its orders until she disappeared [ch 053 / 053.03].
- Its frantic messages suggest it lost contact with her and is now looking for her [ch 053 / 053.03].

**House Baenre (Menzoberranzan)**
- A'lai deduces that this house has funded the eleven-year campaign against Daz, citing its unparalleled patience and resources [ch 067 / 067.02].
- This is a deduction only. A'lai offers no direct proof [ch 067 / 067.02].

### Gracklstugh (Duergar and Derro)

**Duergar (general) and the city guard**
- **Attitudes:** strict laws against foreigners and a deep hatred of Drow. Gracklstugh offers safety from Drow pursuit [ch 011 / 011.07].
- **City guard:** enforces the law with invisible patrols and lethal efficiency. It responded to the stone giant's rampage [ch 012 / 012.01; ch 012 / 012.05].

**Deepking's regime / Clan Steelshadow**
- Clan Steelshadow is the Deepking's clan. It holds exclusive weapons contracts, which breeds resentment across Gracklstugh [ch 018 / 018.02].
- The regime is confirmed allied with assassins trying to wipe out opposition, and the party holds proof [ch 025 / 025.01].

**Keepers of the Flame**
- **Role:** a high-ranking zealot order close to the king. It tends and psionically controls the red dragon Themberchaud to keep the forges burning [ch 013 / 013.04; ch 014 / 014.07; ch 021 / NPCs].
- **Authority claimed:** they say the Deepking treats them as his most prized advisors, and they wield legal authority [ch 013 / 013.04; ch 013 / NPCs].
- **Leadership:** led by Gartokkar, a high-ranking member [ch 025 / 025.01; ch 025 / npcs; ch 026 / 026.06].
- **Their interest in the egg:** they commissioned the dragon egg retrieval, and they fear Themberchaud's awakening [ch 026 / 026.06]. They are obsessed with reclaiming the egg and willing to commit genocide against derro [ch 021 / NPCs].
- **Threat to the party:** they will pursue the party eternally, with permanent hostility, if deceived or betrayed [ch 018 / 018.02; ch 021 / 021.02].
- **Relationship to the party:** the original plan was to deliver the egg to them. Their current stance toward the party is unknown [ch 025 / 025.01].

**Stone Guard**
- **Role:** the military and police arm of the Stone Clan, led by Captain Errde Blackskull [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 013 / NPCs; ch 014 / 014.02].
- **Integrity:** once known for absolute integrity, it is now suspected of taking bribes [ch 018 / 018.04].
- **Errde's aims:** he seeks to justify a purge against the Derro and to expose the Council's corruption. He will act violently if given the full truth [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].
- **Investigations:** it was investigating the Empty-Scabbard Killers and seeking Droki [ch 014 / 014.02; ch 014 / npcs].
- **Last move:** paranoid about the Ember Vanguard, it doubled patrols, and it issued the party passes [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / 026.06].

**Clan Ironhead**
- A weaponsmithing clan opposed to the Deepking's monopoly and barred from direct weapons contracts [ch 018 / 018.07; ch 021 / NPCs].
- Grinta offered the party future trade advantages if the Deepking is removed [ch 018 / 018.07].
- The clan would trade with gnomes, and the party hopes to strengthen it indirectly [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].

**Clan Xardelvar**
- Chafes under new regulations and fears the Deepking will take over its work [ch 018 / 018.03].
- Errde mentioned it as part of the cover story [ch 018 / 018.03].

**Clan Thrazgad**
- A steelsmithing clan whose name is on the dragon rattle [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.03].
- Its involvement with the egg is unknown [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.03].

**Empty-Scabbard Killers**
- Duergar assassins who wield psionic blades and keep no physical talismans [ch 019 / npcs; ch 019 / items].
- At war with Demogorgon-aligned derro [ch 019 / npcs; ch 019 / items].
- A stolen royal contract is missing from their leader's desk [ch 019 / npcs; ch 019 / items].

**Gray Ghosts**
- Stole the red dragon egg, and are suspected of causing the psionic disturbance in the Underdark [ch 014 / 014.07].
- In conflict with the Keepers of the Flame and the Crown. The Keepers are secretly at war with them over the egg [ch 013 / 013.05; ch 014 / 014.07].
- The Council of Savants ordered them to kill the Duergar who brought the Ember Vanguard into the city. A recovered letter reveals this [ch 026 / 026.07].

**Council of Savants (Derro)**
- The derro governing body. Buppido claims it promises freedom but always fails [ch 015 / 015.04].
- The party has killed two of its members (Narrak and one other) [ch 024 / 024.04].
- The party holds evidence of the Council's involvement in demonic rituals [ch 024 / 024.04].

**Derro (general)**
- Once slaves to Mind Flayers, they are predisposed to madness and cooperate only under charismatic leadership [ch 020 / 020.01; ch 020 / locations].
- They revere the egg as a source of absolute authority [ch 020 / 020.01; ch 020 / locations].
- Fully confirmed as aligned with demonic powers [ch 025 / 025.01].
- All derro in the Whorlstone Caverns are dead [ch 025 / 025.01].

**Duergar Slavers**
- Kidnap travellers on the Darklake to sell as slaves in Gracklstugh [ch 010 / 010.03; ch 010 / NPCs].
- Use invisibility and enlargement magic [ch 010 / 010.03; ch 010 / NPCs].

### Neverlight Grove (Myconids)

**Myconid colony**
- **General nature:** usually peaceable but dangerous in large numbers [ch 025 / 025.02].
- **Corruption:** confirmed susceptible to Zuggtmoy's corruption and to fungal teleportation [ch 025 / 025.02; ch 025 / 025.04].
- **Divided state:** the colony is now split. The Circle leaders are infected, while explorers such as Rumpadump resist [ch 031 / 031.02; ch 031 / npcs].
- **The sovereign:** its authority is being used to facilitate demonic worship [ch 031 / 031.02; ch 031 / npcs].

**The Myconid Circles**
- **Circle of Builders:** devoted to architecture and construction, led by Gasbide [ch 030 / 030.05].
- **Circle of Hunters:** tasked the party with hunting the Grick Alpha. Loobamub warned them about the Shambling Mound [ch 030 / 030.01].
- **Circle of Welcome:** a corrupted ritual that turns visitors into rooted heads. Phylo laments the party's refusal of it, which confirms it is deadly [ch 032 / 032.03].

### Blingdenstone

**Blingdenstone (overall)**
- Now officially open for trade [ch 048 / 048.06].
- Miners, wererats, ghosts and spiritual leaders have brokered a fragile alliance under Dorbo's leadership [ch 048 / 048.06].
- The community showed strong cohesion when collective outrage shut down Nomi's racism [ch 043 / 043.04].

**Clan Diggermattock / divided leadership**
- **Standing:** the ruling deep gnome clan of the sector. It took the territory from the drow [ch 036 / 036.01; ch 036 / 036.04].
- **Priorities:** focused on defence and resource management, and treats every Drow escapee as a valuable intelligence source [ch 036 / 036.01; ch 036 / 036.04].
- **The split:** the clan divides into laborers and civilians led by Senni, and the mining coalition and corporations led by Chief Dorbo [ch 037 / 037.01; ch 045 / 045.02].
- **The authorities' conduct:** they tried to claim credit for the party's victory and suppress "revolutionary tactics" [ch 038 / 038.01; ch 038 / 038.06].
- **Conflict (GM note):** ch 045 says the party's intervention shifted power to the labor faction [ch 045 / 045.02]. ch 048 places the final alliance under Dorbo's leadership [ch 048 / 048.06].

**Clan Goldwhisker (wererats)**
- **Leader:** Chipgrin [ch 039 / 039.05].
- **Status:** once rumoured and excluded from Blingdenstone's power structure. Dorbo dismissed them as "squatters" [ch 037 / 037.01; ch 038 / 038.02].
- **Allies:** Senni and the party value them because they are immune to non-silver weapons [ch 038 / 038.02; ch 038 / 038.06].
- **Earlier position:** they refused subordination to Dorbo but agreed to fight the oozes [ch 039 / 039.05].
- **Current:** they hold territorial control of the upper boroughs as a self-funded defence force [ch 048 / 048.06].

**Burrow Wardens**
- Ghostly spirits led by Uth-Jadger. They offered aid in the coming battle in exchange for a task [ch 039 / 039.09].
- They now hold sacred rights to the North Tunnels as a graveyard [ch 048 / 048.06].

**Pickshine Mines / Miners' Guild**
- **Pickshine Mines:** led by Dasco Pickshine. Its branding extends to tea packaging, patches and clothing [ch 046 / 046.02; ch 046 / npcs].
- **Miners' attitude:** the miners care more about "gross margins" than combat [ch 046 / 046.02].
- **Miners' Guild:** represented by Kazook Pickshine. It is concerned about acid corrosion of weapons and armour and wants protective treatments [ch 039 / 039.09].

**Other Blingdenstone groups**
- **Stoneheart Enclave:** can protect Earth Elementals from Ogrémoch's Bane if the Temple of the Steadfast Stone is cleansed [ch 039 / 039.08].
- **Chamber of Commerce:** represents merchant interests. It offered the party a 10% discount coupon [ch 038 / 038.02].
- **Blingdenstone militia:** had mixed reactions to the plan. Officers and ghosts wanted a big battle, while many others were relieved by the minimal distraction [ch 046 / 046.01].

### Candlekeep

**Candlekeep (institution)**
- A scholarly fortress housing dangerous tomes. Its scholars know the lore of the Endless Chant and the Word Lattice, and the Obsidian Door is part of its ancient protections [ch 069 / npcs; ch 069 / locations].
- It prefers exile over imprisonment [ch 056 / 056.04; ch 056 / 056.07].
- Its public records are curated and sometimes deliberately sealed by authority [ch 056 / 056.04; ch 056 / 056.07].

**Council of Twelve / Council of Great Readers**
- **Composition:** the Keeper, the First Reader, eight Great Readers, the Gatewarden and the Lorekeeper [ch 057 / 057.05].
- **Named members:** A'lai, Fheminor, Daral, Teles and Bookwyrm [ch 059 / 059.02; ch 059 / npcs].
- **State:** in uproar over the leadership succession and the murder investigation [ch 057 / 057.05; ch 059 / 059.02].

**The Avowed**
- **Leadership:** Bookwyrm is acting head [ch 062 / npcs; ch 062 / 062.02].
- **Succession:** the naming ceremony for the new Keeper of Tomes is set for "tomorrow" (as of ch 062). Teles Ahvoste and Fheminor are the likely candidates [ch 062 / npcs; ch 062 / 062.02].
- **Earlier sighting:** a hooded member hurried past with something hidden during dinner [ch 057 / 057.02].
- **Access to magic:** Alkrist said that obtaining powerful transformation scrolls requires "the right goblin and the right Avowed" [ch 061 / 061.07].
- **Latest move:** they are documenting the ward failure as an unprecedented phenomenon and preparing experiments [ch 065 / 065.01; ch 065 / Memorable Moments].
- **Historical practice:** Avowed scholars once memorized the height of the High Tower of Exaltation, a practice Edvaldo references [ch 068 / 068.03].
- **Conflict (GM note):** the notes disagree on the Avowed's role.
  - ch 057 calls them the administrative and security order [ch 057 / 057.02].
  - ch 062 calls them the governing body [ch 062 / npcs; ch 062 / 062.02].
  - ch 065 calls them the scholarly guardians [ch 065 / 065.01].

**Temple of Oghma**
- Candlekeep's spiritual authority, which oversees purification rites [ch 059 / 059.01; ch 059 / npcs].
- Kei Tigersteel serves as priest [ch 059 / 059.01; ch 059 / npcs].

**The Threefold Proof / Candlekeep Investigative Methodology**
- Kalan's unapproved methodology for criminal investigation argues against relying on a single magical result [ch 061 / 061.02; ch 062 / items].
- It now underpins the party's investigation and is being considered for scholarly publication [ch 061 / 061.02].
- Bookwyrm now publicly endorses it, despite having suppressed it before [ch 062 / items; ch 062 / 062.02].

**Theological schools**
- **Stroudite School:** holds that mortal choice is sovereign. Gyrgum's "fixed points" theory now challenges it [ch 057 / 057.01].
- **Bahamutian School:** holds that divine providence arranges all things. Vareth aligns with it, and Gyrgum's theory is seen as a compromise toward it [ch 057 / 057.01].
- **Draconic School:** holds that ancient agency sets the frame for mortal action. Vareth presented it as one of the three schools [ch 057 / 057.01].

**Watcher's Bell**
- Candlekeep's institutional alarm for crises [ch 063 / 063.01].
- When it last rang, its tolling pattern was distinct from the hour [ch 063 / 063.01].
