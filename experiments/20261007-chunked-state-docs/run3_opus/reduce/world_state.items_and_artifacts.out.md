## Items and Artifacts

### Dawnbringer (Thorin)
- **What it is:** a sentient sun-sword of late Netherese origin [ch 056 / 056.06]. It is a +2 weapon that deals radiant damage, adds 1d8 against undead, and needs a bonus action to ignite [ch 029 / items]. It has a hilt but no physical blade [ch 041 / 041.01].
- **Light:** it emits genuine sunlight [ch 031 / items].
  - The radius was noted as 15 feet in ch 031 [ch 031 / items] and as 20 feet in ch 047 [ch 047 / 047.06]. The later figure is 20 feet.
  - Its light enabled Zalthir's Shadow Step [ch 047 / 047.06].
  - It was the primary light source in the Vault's upper chamber [ch 069 / items].
- **Powers:**
  - Casts Lesser Restoration once per long rest [ch 049 / items]. It was used to halt the fungal infection in Valen [ch 049 / items] and to cure Zalthir's madness [ch 032 / 032.02].
  - Applies a "sap" effect, which gives the target disadvantage on its next attack [ch 043 / 043.10; ch 050 / items].
  - It is nonmetallic and is not corroded by gray ooze [ch 034 / items].
- **Temperament:**
  - It fears darkness and abandonment [ch 031 / items], and is "very upset" in poorly lit places [ch 035 / 035.05].
  - It may try to control Thorin in magical darkness, through a Charisma save [ch 031 / items; ch 041 / 041.06].
  - It criticised Thorin's ranged attacks [ch 037 / 037.06] and refused to be thrown as a javelin [ch 069 / items].
  - It supports ooze rights [ch 055 / items] and was "very happy" healing orphans [ch 055 / items].
  - It reacted to the Vault fall with "Not this again!" [ch 068 / items].
  - It is female [ch 065 / 065.02].
- **Ongoing:**
  - It cannot be polished [ch 056 / 056.06].
  - It needs therapeutic recovery: first remembering daylight, then grieving [ch 056 / 056.06].
  - It wanted a scabbard [ch 041 / 041.01]. It now has the Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard, which hooks the sword in; Valimor denies it is the "Vaultmaster 9000" [ch 045 / items].
