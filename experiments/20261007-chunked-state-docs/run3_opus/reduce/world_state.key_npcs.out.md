### Candlekeep: the Manshoon affair

**Manshoon**
- Legendary, feared wizard tied to the Zhentarim, the Moonsea and Zhentil Keep [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 064 / npcs].
- "reportedly killed 170 years ago but continues to reappear" [ch 069 / npcs].
- Directed book thefts from Candlekeep for eleven years through a courier with a metal hand. The courier hides the hand beneath a glove [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 065 / NPCs; ch 065 / end].
- The party knows he seeks one specific book from Candlekeep [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 064 / npcs].
- His simulacrum breached Candlekeep alone and depleted [ch 065 / NPCs; ch 065 / end].
- How his simulacra work [ch 069 / npcs]:

  - half his vitality
  - cannot regain spells
  - melt at zero hit points
- Status of the simulacra per the status table:
  - The first simulacrum is dead in the magma chamber [ch 069 / 069.02].
  - A second is alive in the upper tower chamber, "Neutral, bargaining" [ch 069 / 069.05].

**A'lai Aivenmore**
- 31-year Candlekeep scholar. Fearing the loss of his academic chair, he orchestrated book thefts for eleven years under Manshoon's orders. He claims he never chose the books, only followed lists [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 064 / npcs].
- Earlier crimes:
  - Political rival of Daral who sought to become Keeper of Tomes. He orchestrated a diversion so a Dragonborn could steal poison [ch 060 / 060.08].
  - He was present when Fembris reported the key transfer, and he coordinated attacks on both apparent key holders [ch 063 / 063.03].
- He sought prison within the library to survive its collapse [ch 065 / NPCs].
- He has realized he was never important to Manshoon. The party now has his insight into the Vault and his deduction about House Baenre [ch 067 / 067.02].
- Status table: imprisoned in Candlekeep's null magic prison, "Grateful and resigned" [ch 067 / 067.02].
