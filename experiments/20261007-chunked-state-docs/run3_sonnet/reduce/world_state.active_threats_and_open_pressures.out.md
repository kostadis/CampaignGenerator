## Active Threats and Open Pressures

### Manshoon and the Candlekeep fallout
- **Manshoon's vengeance:** The real Manshoon has lost a simulacrum and stolen-from, and is expected to pursue the party "with full fury" over the dead duplicate and the Book of Vile Darkness. His simulacrum's final words promised eternal vengeance. [ch 070 / 070.02; ch 070 / npcs]
- **Simulacra count and conflicting notes:**
  - Ch 069 records the first simulacrum destroyed by Zalthir [ch 069 / 069.02]. It also records a second one, in cheaper robes, waiting in the upper chamber with a bargain: open the Obsidian Door for ten prismatic gemstones and one book [ch 069 / 069.05].
  - Ch 070 records "the duplicate" killed by being thrown into the lava pit [ch 070 / 070.02]. The notes do not say whether this is the first or the second simulacrum, so the second one's fate is unclear.
  - Gyrgum remarked, "There must be a whole factory of these things." [ch 069 / 069.04]
- **Manshoon's reach:**
  - A'lai Aivenmore confessed to removing books for Manshoon for eleven years, with orders delivered by a courier with a concealed metal hand. Manshoon wants one specific book. [ch 064 / 064.04]
  - Manshoon does not know the full nature of the vault's contents. A'lai says he never disclosed it, and A'lai is now in Candlekeep's null magic prison. [ch 065 / 065.03; ch 067 / 067.02]
- **Avowed Readers:** The last surviving Avowed Readers are expected shortly and will not let the party leave with the Book of Vile Darkness. [ch 070 / 070.04; ch 070 / locations]
- **Book of Vile Darkness:** The party holds the sentient artifact. Daz has begun reading it and is receiving Underdark information from a potentially untrustworthy source. [ch 070 / 070.04; ch 070 / items]
- **Edvaldo (doppelgänger):**
  - Ch 069 had him bound and under guard [ch 069 / 069.01; ch 069 / 069.04].
  - Ch 070 (later, so it wins) has him fleeing toward the anti-magic barrier and gone from the chamber [ch 070 / 070.03].
  - He destroyed two of the six prophecy crystals. Gyrgum secured the other four, whose unrevealed prophecies are still active. [ch 070 / 070.03; ch 070 / items]
- **Orrery infiltrators:** Three non-staff individuals were seen inside the Orrery of the Astronomicon, likely Manshoon's agents or another hostile force. The notes show no follow-up. [ch 067 / 067.03]
- **Wards and glyphs:**
  - Candlekeep's wards were shown to be broken, first by Daz's fireball and again by Manshoon's wall of force. [ch 065 / 065.01; ch 065 / Memorable Moments]
  - Hundreds of *Glyphs of Warding* on the tower's books could set off a chain explosion if disturbed. [ch 069 / spells; ch 069 / locations]
  - Ch 070 closes only the simulacrum-disposal explosion, not the wider risk. [ch 070 / 070.02]
- **Vault destruction:** A note in the Vault says the means to destroy the collection are in the chamber above. The party has not located it. [ch 068 / 068.05]

  - Neverlight Grove is judged fully lost to her. [ch 032 / 032.03]

  - Derro cultists acted under his banner, and the cult's reach was still expanding at last note. [ch 022 / 022.02; ch 026 / 026.06]
