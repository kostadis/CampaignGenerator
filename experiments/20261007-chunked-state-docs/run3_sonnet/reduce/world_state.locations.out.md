## Locations

### Velkynvelve (drow outpost)
- Drow prison outpost in an Underdark cavern with hanging towers, rope bridges, spider-silk lines, and a waterfall feeding a 20-foot-deep pool [ch 002 / locations].
- The slave pen and guard tower were breached; two drow guards and Elite Warrior Imbros are dead, and the armory was looted [ch 003 / locations].
- The garrison abandoned it during the vrock attack, and the party escaped from it [ch 004 / locations; ch 004 / 004.01].
- Its name triggers Blingdenstone's standing order to escort all escapees to Chief Dorbo for intelligence gathering [ch 036 / 036.01].

### Darklake
- Vast network of underground rivers, canals, and flooded caverns over a hundred miles long [ch 004 / locations].
- Hazards recorded: Ixitxachitl, submerged stone teeth, low ceilings, beholder zombies, aquatic zombies, and regenerating trolls [ch 009 / 009.03; ch 011 / locations].
- The party navigated it for four days and scuttled their boat on its shore [ch 027 / 027.05; ch 027 / 027.06].
- **Bone Pyramid Islet:** a 40-foot-wide limestone islet with an eight-foot pyramid of bones crowned by a horned ram skull, now guarded by aquatic zombies [ch 011 / locations].
- **The Hidden Beach:** Sarith identified it as the path to the Lost Tomb of Khaem; the party's boat lies sunk offshore [ch 027 / locations; ch 027 / 027.06].

### Sloobludop
- Kuo-toa village on the Darklake, left in ruins after Demogorgon's attack; blood-filled pools teem with demonic fish [ch 009 / 009.01].
- **Altar of the Deep Father:** a blood-stained ritual site near the docks with a grate over the Darklake; site of the failed sacrifice and the Kuo-toan civil war [ch 008 / locations].
- **Ploopploopeen's Hut:** looted by Gyrgum [ch 008 / locations; ch 008 / items].
- **Docks:** jetties with rafts and boats, guarded by Kuo-toan monitors [ch 008 / locations].

### Underdark routes (Velkynvelve to Gracklstugh)
- **Faerzress Tunnels:** magical light that disrupts divination and teleportation; the party passed through them at high speed [ch 005 / locations; ch 005 / 005.06].
- **Rockfall Cavern:** the ceiling collapse trapped companions; the party took a long rest here [ch 005 / locations].
- **Narrow Tunnel:** lined with Ormu moss; the party blocked it behind them [ch 006 / 006.01; ch 006 / locations].
- **Silken Paths:** a 500-foot-deep, 2,000-foot-wide chasm of ever-changing spider webs; spiders have 26 HP and AC 14, and web strands have 5 HP. The party escaped it after a coordinated retreat and bypassed a suspicious chest [ch 006 / 006.04; ch 007 / locations; ch 007 / 007.04].
- **Lost Tomb of Khaem** (first noted as a comparison point): a floating Netherese tomb of the half-elf sorcerer Brysis of Khaem that fell into the Underdark after Netheril's collapse [ch 007 / locations].

### Gracklstugh (duergar "City of Blades")

- Power is contested among the Stone Guard, Keepers of the Flame, and Themberchaud; the Deepking secretly employs assassins [ch 014 / 014.02; ch 019 / locations].
- **Gates sealed:** after three days in the Whorlstone Caverns the party found the gates sealed and guarded by armored Duergar and agitated Drow [ch 025 / 025.08; ch 025 / locations].
- **Latest state:** the city is in controlled chaos. Authorities blame the Ember Vanguard, derro are executed publicly, surface coins are appearing, and the Assassin's Guild is rumored to have returned [ch 040 / 040.02; ch 040 / locations]. Ilvara's force was ambushed there after being mistaken for the Ember Vanguard [ch 049 / locations].
- **Districts and sites:**

  - **Blade Bazaar:** merchants show erratic, possibly magical behavior (phantom twins, shifting prices, invisibility) [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 013 / 013.06].
  - **Overlake Hold:** prison and Stone Guard front office of Captain Errde Blackskull [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 018 / locations].
  - **West Cleft District:** derro slum with the hidden Whorlstone entrance; the Stone Guard enters only in force [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 015 / 015.05]. The West Cleft exit is patrolled by Duergar under lockdown [ch 026 / locations].
  - **Flowstone District:** Duergar-only; the party was escorted through by priests to Themberchaud's Lair, a cavern where the red dragon sits on a mountain of gold [ch 014 / 014.05; ch 014 / 014.06].
  - **Laduguer's Furrow:** affluent Ironhead district, polluted by Clan Thrazgad's forge smoke [ch 026 / locations]. The Forge of Thrazgad was ceremonially lit by Themberchaud during the party's flight [ch 027 / 027.03].
  - **Southfurrow District:** holds the tunnel to Cairngorm Cavern [ch 015 / 015.01].
  - **Ghohlbrorn's Lair:** the party's lodging; Buppido was imprisoned upstairs [ch 015 / 015.04; ch 018 / locations].
  - **Clan Ironhead Forge:** barred from direct weapons contracts and openly opposed to the Deepking [ch 018 / locations].
  - **Gartokkar's Residence:** private quarters of a Keeper of the Flame, used for clandestine dealings [ch 026 / locations].

- **Cairngorm Cavern:** home of Clan Cairngorm and Hgraam; where Rihuud went mad and sprouted a second head. It is now closed to outsiders while Hgraam tries to cure his kin using the ritual statue [ch 013 / 013.03; ch 015 / 015.01; ch 026 / locations].
