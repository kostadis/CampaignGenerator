## Items and Artifacts

### Dawnbringer (Thorin's sentient sunblade)
- A +2 weapon dealing radiant damage, +1d8 against undead, granting lesser restoration; bonded to Thorin [ch 029 / items]. Sentient; emits light, refuses to tolerate darkness, glows fiercely in the Underdark [ch 030 / items].
- Gilded longsword emitting real sunlight in a 15-foot radius; fears darkness and abandonment; may try to control Thorin if he enters magical darkness [ch 031 / items]. Later described as having a hilt but no physical blade, desiring a scabbard, and able to impose control via Charisma save [ch 041 / 041.01; ch 041 / 041.06].
- Applies "sap" (disadvantage on the target's next attack) [ch 043 / 043.10; ch 050 / items]. Unaffected by gray ooze corrosion because it is nonmetallic [ch 034 / items].
- Used to cure Zalthir's madness and wound the rocktopus [ch 032 / 032.02; ch 032 / 032.05]. Halted fungal infection in Valen with Lesser Restoration [ch 049 / items]. Helped destroy the heart fungus [ch 051 / items; ch 052 / items]. Aided in purifying the Living Serpent Head Scourge [ch 053 / 053.03; ch 053 / items].
- Late Netherese origin; cannot be polished; needs therapeutic recovery (remembering daylight, then grieving) [ch 056 / 056.06]. Supports ooze rights and heals orphans, which makes it "very happy" [ch 055 / items]. Cited to prove the party's credentials to the Council [ch 057 / items].
- Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard purchased for it; Valimor denies it is the "Vaultmaster 9000" [ch 045 / items]. Last noted still in Thorin's possession, reacting to the fall with "Not this again!" [ch 068 / items]. Twitched at the Glyphs of Warding and refused to be thrown as a javelin [ch 069 / items].
