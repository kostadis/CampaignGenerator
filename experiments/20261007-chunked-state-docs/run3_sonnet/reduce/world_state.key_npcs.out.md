## Key NPCs

### Manshoon
- Legendary Zhentarim archmage tied to the Moonsea and Zhentil Keep. A'lai confirmed he has directed Candlekeep book thefts for eleven years through a courier with a metal hand. The party now knows he seeks one specific book from Candlekeep [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 064 / npcs].
- His simulacrum breached Candlekeep alone and was depleted. The courier conceals the metal hand beneath a glove [ch 065 / NPCs; ch 065 / end].
- He is reportedly killed 170 years ago but keeps reappearing. His simulacra are made of ice and snow, have half his vitality, cannot regain spells, and melt at zero hit points [ch 069 / npcs].
- The first simulacrum died in the magma chamber [ch 069 / 069.02]. A second one is in the upper tower chamber, neutral and bargaining [ch 069 / 069.05].

### A'lai Aivenmore
- A 31-year Candlekeep scholar who, fearing loss of his academic chair, orchestrated book thefts for eleven years under Manshoon's orders. He claims he only followed lists and never chose the books [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 064 / npcs].
- He realized he was never important to Manshoon. His insight into the Vault and his deduction about House Baenre are now in the party's possession [ch 067 / 067.02].
- He is imprisoned in Candlekeep's null magic prison, grateful and resigned [ch 067 / 067.02].
- He earlier sought prison within the library to survive its collapse [ch 065 / NPCs].
- Alkrist named him as mastermind [ch 062 / npcs; ch 062 / 062.03].

### Alkrist
- Dragonborn Great Reader. He burned a note during the feast, kept poisoned rats, and owns a book matching the murder method [ch 060 / 060.07; ch 060 / 060.09].
- He is imprisoned in Candlekeep. He is hostile to the party and confessed under compulsion, naming A'lai [ch 062 / npcs; ch 062 / 062.03].
- His aunt is Bookwyrm (Skoda Vanaster) [ch 062 / npcs; ch 062 / 062.03].

### Bookwyrm
- Green dragonborn First Reader. She took charge of the Council and appointed the party as investigators. She assigned Fembris Lancer to them [ch 057 / npcs; ch 058 / 058.04].
- She believed she deserved to succeed Janussi as Keeper of Tomes. Janussi told her directly she would not, and she later concealed knowledge of the poisoning [ch 061 / 061.07].
- Bookwyrm (Skoda Vanaster) is named as acting head of Candlekeep and Alkrist's aunt. She suppressed Kalan's Threefold Proof and sought to contain the party's investigation [ch 062 / npcs; ch 062 / 062.03].
- **Latest state:** Moziqodo is responsible for Bookwyrm's murder [ch 063 / 063.02; ch 063 / 063.05]. Her key was stolen and delivered to A'lai, and she is recorded as dead [ch 065 / NPCs; ch 065 / end].
- **Moziqodo:** Abyssal spawn son of Sylvira. He also attacked Tadric and was slain by the party [ch 063 / 063.02; ch 063 / 063.05].

### Kalan Strongbranch
- He championed the methodology that "Magic does not make evidence meaningful. It makes evidence adversarial". Janussi blocked his career and he was fired. He now holds evidence against Alkrist [ch 061 / 061.02; ch 061 / 061.07].
- He was reinstated after the party's evidence vindicated his Threefold Proof [ch 062 / npcs; ch 062 / 062.02].
- He is now Head of the Avowed. He admitted using the party as decoys with a fake key, and deputized them as Watchers of Candlekeep [ch 063 / 063.03].
- He is relieved and eager to contribute, working from the investigator's office [ch 067 / 067.03].

### Sylvira Savikas and Tadric
- Sylvira is grateful and helpful, and is in the investigator's office. Tadric is in Candlekeep's null magic prison, cooperative and supportive [ch 067 / 067.03; ch 067 / 067.02].
- Moziqodo is Sylvira's son [ch 063 / 063.02].

### Miirym the Sentinel Wyrm
- Spectral silver dragon guardian of Candlekeep. Her true name, Vydykyq, is the first answer to the Vault's cryptogram [ch 067 / 067.03; ch 068 / npcs].
- She is bound to be a witness, not a weapon, and cannot fight Manshoon directly [ch 068 / npcs].

### Alaundo
- Legendary seer. His recorded voice speaks the riddle of the Obsidian Door. It is artificial, lacking breath and with fluctuating timbre [ch 069 / npcs].
- His door was protected by a riddle requiring genuine intent. His prophecies remain active in the remaining crystals [ch 070 / npcs].

### Edvaldo Sedanur
- Presented as a Candlekeep historian but actually a doppelgänger, unexposed to the party [ch 068 / npcs].
- Later he is bound and resentful in the anti-magic zone of the lower tower [ch 069 / 069.04].

### Glabbagool
- Intelligent gelatinous cube. He became a sentient grey ooze, now acts as a jailer and student of shadow monk philosophy [ch 056 / 056.04; ch 056 / 056.05].
- He guards the shrunken drow spy nicknamed Polly Pocket in the party's bag of holding. The spy is hostile but psychologically broken [ch 054 / 054.05; ch 054 / 054.06].
- He is friendly and respectful in the upper tower chamber [ch 069 / 069.04].
- Earlier he admitted hearing a voice from a "big pool of slimes" [ch 046 / 046.08].

### Dawnbringer
- Sentient +2 sunblade who is terrified of darkness and insists on remaining active at all times [ch 029 / items; ch 029 / 029.01].
- She resists being treated as a ritual tool. She is aware of her trauma from darkness and may seek help at Candlekeep [ch 053 / 053.05; ch 053 / npcs].
- Master Archivist Philemon is assigned to Thorin and Dawnbringer. He is an Avowed monk specializing in sentient artifacts, with a "tremendous track record of success" [ch 054 / 054.06; ch 056 / 056.06].

### Daz's threats and protectors
- Daz's mysterious background is now the focus of investigation. He is the target of both protection and extraction contracts [ch 053 / 053.04; ch 053 / 053.05].
- The Matron Mother of House T'sarran has ruled 200 years. She believes Daz's surface arrival will reveal his unknown protector, and has sent agents to observe [ch 054 / 054.04].

### Zuggtmoy
- Demon Queen of Fungi. She is preparing to mate with or possess Araumycos through an incomprehensible ritual. Direct confrontation would mean new characters [ch 032 / 032.01].
- She psychically contacted the party, revealed her plan to claim an outpost, and called the Pudding King "my little king" [ch 044 / 044.03; ch 044 / npcs].
- Ilvara invoked her as "the bride" who would come to spread chaos and mayhem [ch 051 / 051.05]. Ilvara Mizzrym is dead [ch 051 / 051.06].
- The Pudding King is dead [ch 047 / 047.08; ch 047 / npcs].
- Her last recorded state is active, encroaching via twisted fungi [ch 047 / 047.10; ch 047 / npcs].

### Jimjar
- Not a gnome: immune to poison, psychic damage, charm, and fear. He vanished after revealing this, possibly a celestial being [ch 048 / 048.07].
- A later note calls him a gnomish god who offered only a "used token" as a boon [ch 049 / npcs; ch 049 / 049.01].

### Candlekeep scholars and others
- **Khell-Vire:** Shadow monk master, "the best of the best". Zalthir has a letter of introduction [ch 055 / npcs]. He gives Zalthir a cryptic note and a trial [ch 056 / 056.05].
- **Sister Yvenne:** Public record of Candlekeep. Her silences and body language suggest she knows more about drow history than she can say [ch 056 / 056.07].
- **Brother Vareth:** Elder scholar who was electrified by Gyrgum's "fixed points" theory [ch 057 / npcs].
- **Spanner:** Gnome librarian of the House of Mechanus. He refuses to kill modrons for Dust of Mechanus but lends tools under strict conditions [ch 067 / 067.05].

### Vecna
- Identified as the purported author of the Book of Vile Darkness. Even drow use his name to frighten children [ch 070 / npcs].
