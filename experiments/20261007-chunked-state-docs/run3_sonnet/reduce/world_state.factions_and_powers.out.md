## Factions and Powers

### Drow (general), Lolth, and Velkynvelve
- Drow are ruthless slave-owners who use hierarchy, treasure hoarding by the highest-ranking, and public humiliation as political weapons [ch 002 / 002.05; ch 002 / 002.06].
- Lolth is worshiped at Velkynvelve; its shrine has a 10-foot idol with jet eyes, and drow hierarchy and ritual center on her [ch 002 / locations; ch 002 / items].
- Velkynvelve's guard tower has been overrun and its commander slain [ch 003 / locations].
- The drow of Velkynvelve were hunting the escaped prisoners: scouts with darkvision and tracking skills went ahead, with a full hunting party following [ch 005 / 005.04; ch 005 / npcs]. The notes record no later outcome of this pursuit.
- Lolth's priesthood: drow clerics distrust mages who claim divine communion (Asha's suspicion reflects this) [ch 050 / 050.01].

### Drow houses
- **House T'sarran**
  - Minor drow house loyal to Ilvara; Valen is a scout from this house, and his infection reflects its weakness [ch 049 / 049.02].
  - Suffered heavy losses; its elite warrior retreated with a threat, suggesting future retaliation [ch 051 / 051.04; ch 051 / 051.06].
  - Sent a force to extract Daz alive, confirmed by a signet ring and burned orders [ch 053 / 053.04].
  - Latest note (ch 054): ruled for 200 years by its Matron Mother; it has deployed agents to the surface to track Daz and identify his mysterious benefactor, not to kill him [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / npcs]. (Ch 049 calls it "minor" and ch 054 "powerful"; the later note governs.)
- **House Duskryn**: actively protecting Daz; Kaelira and Nym showed lethal coordination and loyalty [ch 051 / 051.03; ch 051 / 051.06].
- **House Mizzrym**: Ilvara was a loyal agent receiving its orders until her disappearance; its frantic messages suggest it lost contact and is seeking her [ch 053 / 053.03].
- **House Baenre**: A'lai deduces this Menzoberranzan house has funded the eleven-year campaign against Daz, given its patience and resources; he offers no direct proof [ch 067 / 067.02].
- **Zuggtmoy's "bride" heresy**: Ilvara follows this cult, turning followers into fungal servants; Asha sees it as a betrayal of Lolth [ch 050 / 050.02].

### The Ember Vanguard / Ember Grapple (party identity)
- "Ember Vanguard" is the party's adopted name, now a legend among Derro, described as a marauding army of drow led by a 20th-level archmage [ch 021 / 021.04; ch 021 / Memorable Moments].
- In Gracklstugh it is a fabricated identity and feared rumor, described as a red dragonborn and wizened drow assassins, and blamed for the derro High Council murders [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / 026.03].
- It is also rumored as an agent group of the Drow Empire; Daz is thought to be an 18th-level mage [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / Memorable Moments].
- The party later formally adopted "Ember Grapple" as its group identity for future missions, including informing Sylvira of her son's death [ch 064 / 064.01].

### Duergar and Gracklstugh
- **Duergar (general)**: strict laws against foreigners and deep hatred of Drow; Gracklstugh is their city and offers safety from Drow pursuit [ch 011 / 011.07].
- **Duergar Slavers**: kidnap travelers to sell in Gracklstugh; use invisibility and enlargement magic; operate on the Darklake [ch 010 / 010.03; ch 010 / NPCs].
- **Duergar city guard**: invisible patrols and lethal efficiency; responded to the stone giant's rampage [ch 012 / 012.01; ch 012 / 012.05].
- **Stone Guard**
  - Military arm of the Stone Clan, led by Captain Errde Blackskull [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 013 / NPCs].
  - Investigating the Empty-Scabbard Killers and seeking Droki [ch 014 / 014.02; ch 014 / npcs].
  - Once known for integrity, now suspected of bribes; Errde seeks to justify a purge against the Derro [ch 018 / 018.04].
  - Seeks to expose the Council's corruption and will act violently if given the full truth [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].
  - Latest: paranoid and doubling patrols over fears of the Ember Vanguard; issued passes to the party [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / 026.06].
- **Keepers of the Flame**
  - High-ranking order tending the red dragon Themberchaud to keep the forges burning; claim to be the Deepking's prized advisors [ch 013 / 013.04; ch 013 / NPCs].
  - Control Themberchaud by psionic means; secretly at war with the Gray Ghosts over the stolen dragon egg [ch 014 / 014.07].
  - Threatened the party with permanent hostility if betrayed [ch 018 / 018.02]; willing to commit genocide against derro and will pursue the party eternally if deceived [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].
  - Led by Gartokkar; the original plan was to deliver the egg to them, and their current stance toward the party is unknown [ch 025 / 025.01; ch 025 / npcs].
  - Commissioned the egg retrieval; Gartokkar is a high-ranking member; they fear Themberchaud's awakening [ch 026 / 026.06].
- **Gray Ghosts**: shadowy organization that stole the red dragon egg and is suspected of causing the psionic disturbance [ch 014 / 014.07]. Per a halfling, in conflict with the Keepers and the Crown [ch 013 / 013.05]. Ordered by the Council of Savants to kill the Duergar who brought the Ember Vanguard into the city [ch 026 / 026.07].
- **Empty-Scabbard Killers**: duergar assassins with psionic blades and no physical talismans; at war with Demogorgon-aligned derro; a royal contract is missing from their leader's desk [ch 019 / npcs; ch 019 / items].
- **Deepking's regime**: confirmed allied with assassins trying to wipe out opposition; the party holds proof [ch 025 / 025.01].
- **Clans**
  - Ironhead: opposed to the Deepking's monopoly and barred from direct weapons contracts; Grinta offers the party future trade advantages if the Deepking is removed [ch 018 / 018.07]. Would trade with gnomes if he were removed; the party hopes to strengthen them indirectly [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].
  - Steelshadow: the Deepking's clan, with exclusive weapons contracts, breeding resentment [ch 018 / 018.02].
  - Xardelvar: chafes under new regulations and fears the Deepking will take over their work; mentioned by Errde in the cover story [ch 018 / 018.03].
  - Thrazgad: steelsmithing clan whose name is on the dragon rattle; involvement with the egg unknown [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.03].

### Derro
- Once slaves to Mind Flayers, prone to madness, cooperating only under charismatic leadership; they revere the egg as a source of absolute authority [ch 020 / 020.01; ch 020 / locations].
- **Council of Savants**: Buppido claims they promise freedom but always fail [ch 015 / 015.04]. The party has killed two members (Narrak and one other Derro) and holds evidence of their demonic rituals [ch 024 / 024.04]. They ordered the Gray Ghosts' hit, shown by a recovered letter [ch 026 / 026.07].
- Derro are confirmed aligned with demonic powers; all in the Whorlstone Caverns are dead [ch 025 / 025.01].

### Myconids and Zuggtmoy
- Myconids are peaceable but dangerous in numbers, and are susceptible to Zuggtmoy's corruption and fungal teleportation [ch 025 / 025.02; ch 025 / 025.04].
- Circle of Builders (Neverlight Grove; led by Gasbide) [ch 030 / 030.05]. Circle of Hunters tasked the party with hunting the Grick Alpha [ch 030 / 030.01].
- The colony is divided: Circle leaders are infected, explorers like Rumpadump resist, and the sovereign's authority facilitates demonic worship [ch 031 / 031.02; ch 031 / npcs].
- The Circle of Welcome is a corrupted ritual turning visitors into rooted heads; Phylo laments the party's refusal of it [ch 032 / 032.03].
- **Zuggtmoy**, Demon Queen of Fungi, is moving to claim the vacuum left by the Pudding King's defeat, shown by twisted fungi [ch 047 / 047.10; ch 047 / npcs].
- **Juiblex**, the Faceless Lord, is not destroyed; a voice declares he will be reborn and consume Zuggtmoy's "banquet" [ch 047 / 047.10; ch 047 / npcs].
- **Mad Gnolls**: demon-possessed, implanting spawn in living bodies; Fargas claims his band was attacked by them [ch 006 / 006.07].

### Blingdenstone
- **Clan Diggermattock**: ruling deep gnome clan of this sector, took the territory from the drow, treats Drow escapees as intelligence sources [ch 036 / 036.01; ch 036 / 036.04]. Split between laborers (Senni) and mining corporations (Chief Dorbo); the party's intervention shifted power to the labor faction [ch 045 / 045.02].
- **Clan Goldwhisker**: wererat gnomes, led by Chipgrin, who refuse subordination to Dorbo but agreed to fight the oozes [ch 039 / 039.05]; dismissed by Dorbo as "squatters," valued by Senni and the party [ch 038 / 038.02; ch 038 / 038.06]. Now granted territorial control of the upper boroughs as a self-funded defense force [ch 048 / 048.06].
- **Burrow Wardens**: ghosts led by Uth-Jadger [ch 039 / 039.09]; retain sacred rights to the North Tunnels as a graveyard [ch 048 / 048.06].
- **Blingdenstone (latest)**: officially open for trade; miners, wererats, ghosts and spiritual leaders brokered a fragile alliance under Dorbo's leadership [ch 048 / 048.06].
- **Authorities**: tried to claim credit for the party's victory and suppress "revolutionary tactics" [ch 038 / 038.01; ch 038 / 038.06].
- **Others**
  - Chamber of Commerce offered a 10% discount coupon [ch 038 / 038.02].
  - Stoneheart Enclave can protect Earth Elementals from Ogrémoch's Bane if the Temple of the Steadfast Stone is cleansed [ch 039 / 039.08].
  - Miners' Guild (Kazook Pickshine) wants protective treatments against acid corrosion [ch 039 / 039.09].
  - Pickshine Mines, led by Dasco Pickshine, is brand-focused [ch 046 / 046.02; ch 046 / npcs].
  - Militia reactions to the plan are mixed [ch 046 / 046.01].
  - The community is cohesive; Nomi's racism was shut down by collective outrage [ch 043 / 043.04].
- **Ogrémoch**: elemental earth entity lingering in the Steadfast Stone, source of the temple's corruption [ch 042 / 042.02; ch 042 / npcs].
- **Galeb Duhr**: stone sentinels programmed to "Trust no one"; respond to sonic resonance and ritual alignment [ch 044 / 044.04; ch 044 / npcs].
- **Goldwhisker note** (earlier): territory later taken by Dorbo's faction; Blingdenstone's leadership is split between Dorbo's mining coalition and Senni's civilians [ch 037 / 037.01].

### Candlekeep
- **Candlekeep**: exile over imprisonment; public records curated and sometimes sealed [ch 056 / 056.04; ch 056 / 056.07]. A scholarly fortress with dangerous tomes; the Obsidian Door is part of its protections [ch 069 / npcs; ch 069 / locations].
- **Council of Twelve**: Keeper, First Reader, eight Great Readers, Gatewarden, Lorekeeper; in uproar over leadership and murder [ch 057 / 057.05].
- **Council of Great Readers**: A'lai, Fheminor, Daral, Teles, Bookwyrm; tensions over succession and the murder [ch 059 / 059.02; ch 059 / npcs]. Bookwyrm is acting head; the naming of the new Keeper of Tomes is the next day, with Teles Ahvoste and Fheminor likely candidates [ch 062 / npcs; ch 062 / 062.02]. (Ch 062 labels this body "Candlekeep Avowed"; the notes also use "Avowed" differently elsewhere.)
- **Avowed**: administrative and security order; a hooded member was seen hurrying past with something hidden [ch 057 / 057.02]. Alkrist says powerful transformation scrolls need "the right goblin and the right Avowed" [ch 061 / 061.07]. Now documenting the ward failure and preparing experiments [ch 065 / 065.01; ch 065 / Memorable Moments]. Historically memorized the High Tower of Exaltation's height [ch 068 / 068.03].
- **Temple of Oghma**: Kei Tigersteel is priest; oversees purification rites [ch 059 / 059.01; ch 059 / npcs].
- **Schools of thought**: Stroudite (mortal choice sovereign; challenged by Gyrgum's "fixed points" theory), Bahamutian (divine providence; Vareth aligns), Draconic (ancient agency sets the frame) [ch 057 / 057.01].
- **Investigative methodology**: Kalan's paper argues against relying on single magical results and underpins the party's investigation [ch 061 / 061.02]. The Threefold Proof is now publicly endorsed by Bookwyrm despite her earlier suppression [ch 062 / items; ch 062 / 062.02].
- **Watcher's Bell**: crisis alarm; its tolling pattern was distinct from the hour [ch 063 / 063.01].

### Zhentarim
- Two raiders were confirmed as Zhentarim looting books from a written manifest; one carried the bow that killed the Watcher [ch 064 / 064.03; ch 064 / npcs].
- Their agents in the High Tower are all dead and the incursion failed, but Manshoon's breach indicates deeper involvement [ch 065 / 065.01; ch 065 / 065.02; ch 065 / NPCs].

### Other
- **Netherese**: fallen empire of floating cities and advanced magic; artifacts heavily plundered; alien concepts of good and evil [ch 028 / locations].
- **House of Mechanus**: provided the Mechanist Tools that activated the bridge; the party owes collateral (Glabbagool and Zalthir) [ch 068 / items; ch 068 / npcs].
