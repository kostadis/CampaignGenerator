## Items and Artifacts

### Major Artifacts (current at ch 070)

**Book of Vile Darkness**
- Sentient artifact of cosmic evil written by Vecna; secured by Daz, who is reading it with Mage Hand; cannot be permanently destroyed while evil exists [ch 070 / items].
- Open question: what reading it does to Daz; the notes are silent on any effect.

**Robe of the Archmagi**
- Held by Daz; he will attune at his next long rest, possibly replacing an existing attuned item [ch 070 / items].

**Staff of Power**
- Used by Manshoon to breach Candlekeep's wards; now lost with the simulacrum in the lava [ch 070 / items].

**Prophecy Crystals / Prismatic Gemstones**
- Ten gemstones in a velvet tray, four dark; they hold prophecies placed by Alaundo, including one concerning Thorin, Eldeth, and the unlit forge [ch 069 / items].
- At ch 070: four of the six prophecy crystals remain intact and are held by Gyrgum; their prophecies are still active and likely tied to the party [ch 070 / items].
- Conflict: ch 069 counts ten gemstones and ch 070 counts six crystals. The GM should settle the count.

**Dangerous Tomes (the Vault)**
- One hundred hazardous magical tomes stored in the Vault; Manshoon was reading one; the vault's note forbids removing them [ch 068 / items].

### Candlekeep: Keys, Sapphire and Murder Evidence

**Real High Tower Keys**
- There are two; both are required to open the sealed inner door [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 064 / items].
- **Gyrgum's key:** bound to the stolen sapphire; smashing the sapphire recalls this key [ch 065 / 065.03; ch 065 / items; ch 065 / end].
- **Glabbagool's key:** hidden inside Glabbagool; taken from Moziqodo and stripped from A'lai; kept separate from Gyrgum's key on purpose [ch 065 / 065.03; ch 065 / items; ch 065 / end].
- The Inert Key appears non-magical to Detect Magic but is tracked by helmed horrors [ch 062 / items; ch 062 / 062.06].

**Fake High Tower Key**
- Non-magical decoy from Kalan; held by Gyrgum for possible deception [ch 063 / 063.01; ch 065 / 065.03; ch 065 / items].

**Watcher's Stair Key**
- From Kalan; a shortcut to the North Galleries; confirmed genuine after Kalan swore "on my honor" [ch 063 / 063.04].

**Stolen Sapphire**
- Taken from Janussi's chambers; a component for Drawmij's Instant Summons [ch 059 / 059.04; ch 059 / items].
- A'lai meant to smash it [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 064 / items].
- Now held by Gyrgum; smashing it would recall his key, not locate the other [ch 065 / 065.03; ch 065 / items; ch 065 / end].

**The Locket**
- Torn from Janussi's neck; only broken lead-chain links remain; believed to hold a key to the High Tower Library [ch 058 / items; ch 061 / 061.01; ch 061 / items].

**Janussi's Severed Heart and Meat Cleaver**

- The heart is now in the party's possession [ch 060 / items].
