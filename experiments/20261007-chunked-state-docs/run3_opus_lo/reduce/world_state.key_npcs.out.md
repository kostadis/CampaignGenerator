## Key NPCs

### Manshoon
- Legendary Zhentarim archmage, tied to the Zhentarim, the Moonsea and Zhentil Keep; reportedly killed 170 years ago but keeps reappearing [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 064 / npcs] [ch 069 / npcs].
- Directed book thefts from Candlekeep for eleven years through A'lai, via a courier who hides a metal hand beneath a glove; the party knows he wants one specific book from Candlekeep [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 064 / npcs] [ch 065 / NPCs; ch 065 / end].
- His simulacrum breached Candlekeep alone and was depleted [ch 065 / NPCs; ch 065 / end].
- Simulacra rules: built from ice and snow, half his vitality, cannot regain spells, melt at zero hit points [ch 069 / npcs].
- Current state per the code table: one simulacrum is dead in the magma chamber; a second is alive in the upper tower chamber and "Neutral, bargaining" [ch 069 / 069.02] [ch 069 / 069.05].

### A'lai Aivenmore
- Drow scholar with 31 years at Candlekeep. Fearing the loss of his academic chair, he ran the book thefts for Manshoon for eleven years. He claims he never chose the books, only followed lists [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 064 / npcs].
- Earlier schemes:
  - Political rival of Daral who sought to become Keeper of Tomes [ch 060 / 060.08].
  - Orchestrated the diversion that let a Dragonborn steal poison [ch 060 / 060.08].
  - Coordinated the attacks on both apparent key holders [ch 063 / 063.03].
- Has realized he was never important to Manshoon. Sought a prison cell within the library to survive its collapse [ch 065 / NPCs] [ch 067 / 067.02].
- His insight into the Vault and his deduction about House Baenre are now in the party's possession [ch 067 / 067.02].
- Table: imprisoned in Candlekeep's null magic prison, "Grateful and resigned" [ch 067 / 067.02].

### Kalan Strongbranch
- Former Gate Warden. Janussi asked him to resign the morning of the murder and later blocked his career; he was then fired [ch 057 / npcs] [ch 061 / 061.02; ch 061 / 061.07].
- His methodology is the Threefold Proof: "Magic does not make evidence meaningful. It makes evidence adversarial" [ch 061 / 061.02; ch 061 / 061.07].
- The party's evidence vindicated him and he was reinstated as Head of the Avowed [ch 062 / npcs; ch 062 / 062.02] [ch 063 / 063.03].
- Admitted he used the party as decoys with a fake key. Deputized them as Watchers of Candlekeep [ch 063 / 063.03].
- Table: "Relieved and eager to contribute" [ch 067 / 067.03].

### Miirym, the Sentinel Wyrm
- Spectral silver dragon guardian of Candlekeep [ch 068 / npcs].
- Her true name, Vydykyq, is the first answer to the Vault's cryptogram [ch 067 / 067.03].
- Bound to be a witness, not a weapon; she cannot fight Manshoon directly [ch 068 / npcs].

### Edvaldo Sedanur
- Presents himself as a Candlekeep historian but is actually a doppelgänger [ch 068 / npcs].
- Claims knowledge of the High Tower's height [ch 068 / npcs].
- **Conflict for the GM:** ch 068 notes say he is unexposed to the party. The later table shows "Edvaldo" bound and hostile in the lower tower's anti-magic zone [ch 069 / 069.04].

### Alaundo
- Legendary dead seer. His recorded voice speaks the riddle of the Obsidian Door; it is artificial, lacking breath, with fluctuating timbre [ch 069 / npcs].
- The door's riddle required genuine intent. His prophecies remain active in the remaining crystals [ch 070 / npcs].

### Vecna
- Identified as the purported author of the Book of Vile Darkness [ch 070 / npcs].
- His name is so feared that even drow use it to frighten children [ch 070 / npcs].

### Glabbagool

- Current roles:

  - Guards the shrunken House T'sarran drow spy, who keeps trying to kill him [ch 056 / 056.04].

- Has promised loyalty to the party, but once heard a voice from a "big pool of slimes" [ch 046 / 046.08].
- Table: in the upper tower chamber, "Friendly, respectful" [ch 069 / 069.04].

### Dawnbringer
- +2 sentient sword:
  - Deals radiant damage and adds 1d8 against undead.
  - Grants lesser restoration.
  - Speaks and reads Common.
  - Is terrified of darkness and insists on staying active at all times [ch 029 / items; ch 029 / 029.01].
- Described as kind and feminine, though the party treats her as genderless. She wants aesthetic upgrades [ch 045 / npcs].
- Resists being treated as a ritual tool and is now aware of her trauma from darkness [ch 053 / 053.05; ch 053 / npcs].
- Philemon is treating her at Candlekeep [ch 054 / 054.06].

### Philemon

- Has a "tremendous track record of success" in resolving artifact interpersonal conflicts [ch 054 / 054.06].
