## Factions and Powers

### Party identities
**Ember Grapple** (current name)
- The party formally adopted this name as its group identity for future missions, including informing Sylvira of her son's death [ch 064 / 064.01].

**Ember Vanguard** (earlier fabricated identity, still circulating as rumor)
- This was the party's earlier adopted name. Among Derro it became a legend: a marauding army of drow led by a 20th-level archmage [ch 021 / 021.04; ch 021 / Memorable Moments].
- In Gracklstugh it became a feared rumor:
  - described as a red dragonborn and wizened drow assassins [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / 026.03];
  - blamed for the derro High Council murders [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / 026.03];
  - thought to be a legendary agent group of the Drow Empire, with Daz rumored to be an 18th-level mage [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / Memorable Moments].

### Demon lords and elemental powers
**Zuggtmoy** (Demon Queen of Fungi)
- She is actively moving to claim the power vacuum left by the Pudding King's defeat, as shown by the twisted fungi [ch 047 / 047.10; ch 047 / npcs].
- Myconids are confirmed susceptible to her corruption and to fungal teleportation [ch 025 / 025.02; ch 025 / 025.04].
- **Zuggtmoy's "bride" heresy**: Ilvara follows this heretical cult, which turns followers into fungal servants. Asha sees it as a betrayal of Lolth [ch 050 / 050.02].

**Juiblex** (the Faceless Lord, demon lord of oozes)
- He is not destroyed. A voice declares he will be reborn and consume Zuggtmoy's "banquet" to grow stronger [ch 047 / 047.10; ch 047 / npcs].

**Ogrémoch**
- A powerful entity from the elemental plane of earth who chose to linger in the Steadfast Stone among drow statues [ch 042 / 042.02; ch 042 / npcs].
- His spirit is the source of the temple's corruption [ch 042 / 042.02; ch 042 / npcs].

### Drow
**Lolth and her priesthood**
- Lolth was worshiped at Velkynvelve, whose shrine has a 10-foot idol with jet eyes [ch 002 / locations; ch 002 / items].
- Drow clerics distrust mages who claim divine communion; Asha's suspicion reflects this [ch 050 / 050.01].

**Drow (general)**
- Ruthless slave-owners who value hierarchy. Treasure is hoarded by the highest-ranking, and public humiliation is used as a political weapon [ch 002 / 002.05; ch 002 / 002.06].
- The Velkynvelve outpost's guard tower was overrun and its commander slain [ch 003 / locations].
- Velkynvelve drow hunted the escapees, sending darkvision scouts ahead of a full hunting party [ch 005 / 005.04; ch 005 / npcs].

**House T'sarran**
- A drow house ruled for 200 years by its Matron Mother [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / npcs].
- It is described as minor and loyal to Ilvara. Its scout Valen's infection reflects the house's weakness [ch 049 / 049.02].
- It sent a force to extract Daz alive, confirmed by a signet ring and burned orders [ch 053 / 053.04].
- That force suffered heavy losses, and its elite warrior retreated with a threat [ch 051 / 051.04; ch 051 / 051.06].
- It has agents on the surface tracking Daz and trying to identify his benefactor. Their aim is not to kill him [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / npcs].

**House Duskryn**
- Actively protecting Daz. Kaelira and Nym showed lethal coordination and loyalty [ch 051 / 051.03; ch 051 / 051.06].

**House Mizzrym**
- Ilvara received orders from this house until her disappearance [ch 053 / 053.03].
- Its frantic messages suggest it lost contact with her and is now seeking her [ch 053 / 053.03].

**House Baenre**
- A'lai deduces this Menzoberranzan house has funded the eleven-year campaign against Daz [ch 067 / 067.02].
- He bases this on the house's patience and resources and offers no direct proof [ch 067 / 067.02].

### Gracklstugh (duergar) and the derro
**Duergar (general)**
- Strict laws against foreigners and a deep hatred of drow [ch 011 / 011.07].
- **Duergar Slavers** kidnap travelers on the Darklake to sell as slaves in Gracklstugh, using invisibility and enlargement magic [ch 010 / 010.03; ch 010 / NPCs].
- The city guard enforces the law with invisible patrols [ch 012 / 012.01; ch 012 / 012.05].

**Deepking's regime / Clan Steelshadow**
- The Deepking's clan, Steelshadow, holds exclusive weapons contracts, which breeds resentment across Gracklstugh [ch 018 / 018.02].
- The regime is confirmed allied with assassins trying to wipe out opposition, and the party holds proof [ch 025 / 025.01].

**Keepers of the Flame**
- A zealot order close to the king, led by Gartokkar [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02; ch 025 / 025.01; ch 025 / npcs].
- Controls Themberchaud through psionic means [ch 014 / 014.07].
- Commissioned the retrieval of the dragon egg [ch 026 / 026.06].
- Fears Themberchaud's awakening [ch 026 / 026.06].
- Willing to commit genocide against the derro [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].
- Threatened to pursue the party eternally if deceived [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].
- The party's original plan was to deliver the egg to them. Their current stance toward the party is unknown [ch 025 / 025.01; ch 025 / npcs].

**Stone Guard**
- Gracklstugh's police and military arm, led by Captain Errde Blackskull [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 013 / NPCs].
- Suspected of accepting bribes [ch 018 / 018.04].
- Errde seeks to expose the Council's corruption and justify a purge of the derro [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].
- Now paranoid and doubling patrols for fear of the Ember Vanguard [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / 026.06].
- Issued passes to the party [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / 026.06].

**Gray Ghosts**
- Stole the red dragon egg [ch 014 / 014.07].
- Secretly at war with the Keepers over the egg [ch 014 / 014.07].
- The Council of Savants ordered them to kill the duergar who brought the Ember Vanguard into the city [ch 026 / 026.07].

**Empty-Scabbard Killers**
- Duergar assassins with psionic blades [ch 019 / npcs; ch 019 / items].
- At war with Demogorgon-aligned derro [ch 019 / npcs; ch 019 / items].
- A royal contract is missing from their leader's desk [ch 019 / npcs; ch 019 / items].

**Duergar clans**
- **Clan Ironhead**: barred from direct weapons contracts. Grinta offered the party future trade advantages if the Deepking is removed [ch 018 / 018.07; ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].
- **Clan Xardelvar**: chafes under the new regulations [ch 018 / 018.03].
- **Clan Thrazgad**: its name is on the dragon rattle. Its involvement with the egg is unknown [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.03].

**Council of Savants / Derro**
- The party has killed two Council members, including Narrak, and holds evidence of their demonic rituals [ch 024 / 024.04].
- The derro are confirmed aligned with demonic powers, and all derro in the Whorlstone Caverns are dead [ch 025 / 025.01].

### Blingdenstone
**Overall state**
- Blingdenstone is now officially open for trade. Miners, wererats, ghosts and spiritual leaders hold a fragile alliance under Dorbo's leadership [ch 048 / 048.06].

**Clan Diggermattock**
- The ruling clan. It treats drow escapees as valuable intelligence sources [ch 036 / 036.01; ch 036 / 036.04].
- It is split between laborers led by Senni and mining interests led by Chief Dorbo [ch 045 / 045.02].
- The party's intervention shifted power toward the labor faction [ch 045 / 045.02].
- **Possible conflict for the GM:** ch 045 says power shifted to Senni's labor faction, but the later ch 048 places the alliance "under Dorbo's leadership" [ch 045 / 045.02; ch 048 / 048.06].

**Clan Goldwhisker**
- Wererat gnomes led by Chipgrin [ch 039 / 039.05].
- Dorbo dismissed them as "squatters" [ch 038 / 038.02; ch 038 / 038.06].
- They now hold territorial control of the upper boroughs as a self-funded defense force [ch 048 / 048.06].

**Burrow Wardens**
- Ghostly spirits led by Uth-Jadger [ch 039 / 039.09].
- They retain sacred rights to the North Tunnels as a graveyard [ch 048 / 048.06].

**Other Blingdenstone groups**
- **Pickshine Mines / Miners' Guild**: led by Dasco Pickshine and represented by Kazook Pickshine. Concerned about acid damage to gear [ch 039 / 039.09; ch 046 / 046.02; ch 046 / npcs].
- **Stoneheart Enclave**: can protect Earth Elementals from Ogrémoch's Bane if the Temple of the Steadfast Stone is cleansed [ch 039 / 039.08].
- **Chamber of Commerce**: gave the party a 10% discount coupon [ch 038 / 038.02].

### Candlekeep
**Governance**
- The **Council of Twelve** is in uproar over leadership and murder [ch 057 / 057.05].
- Bookwyrm is acting head. The naming ceremony for the new Keeper of Tomes was due "tomorrow," with Teles Ahvoste and Fheminor as likely candidates [ch 062 / npcs; ch 062 / 062.02].
- Candlekeep exiles rather than imprisons, and it curates and sometimes seals its records [ch 056 / 056.04; ch 056 / 056.07].

**The Avowed**
- Documenting the ward failure as unprecedented and preparing experiments [ch 065 / 065.01; ch 065 / Memorable Moments].
- Alkrist says the Avowed can supply powerful transformation scrolls with "the right goblin and the right Avowed" [ch 061 / 061.07].

**Temple of Oghma**

**The Threefold Proof**
- Kalan's investigative methodology, now publicly endorsed by Bookwyrm [ch 062 / items; ch 062 / 062.02].
