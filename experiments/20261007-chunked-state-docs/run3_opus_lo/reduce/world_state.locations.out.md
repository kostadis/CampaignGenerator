## Locations

### Candlekeep and the Vault (current location, ch 070)

**Candlekeep**
- Fortress-library south of Waterdeep; entry requires donating a unique book not already in the collection, and more donated books buy more time with the Avowed; the First Reader oversees admissions and scholar assignments [ch 053 / 053.06; ch 054 / 054.05; ch 054 / 054.06]
- Holds prisoners in antimagic cells only briefly and exiles offenders with a warning [ch 056 / 056.04]
- Main defence is a ward that blocks teleportation and suppresses fire, sourced from the High Tower [ch 063 / 063.01; ch 063 / 063.02]
- The wards are now proven broken: Daz's fireball broke them, and Manshoon's simulacrum then breached them again [ch 065 / 065.01; ch 065 / locations; ch 065 / Memorable Moments]
- Its reputation for inviolability is shattered by multiple murders and an assault by helmed horrors [ch 063 / 063.01; ch 063 / 063.02]
- The endless chant of prophecies has fallen silent for the first time in centuries [ch 064 / 064.01; ch 064 / locations]
- The inner vault beneath the library has been breached. **The surviving Avowed Readers will not let the party leave with the Book of Vile Darkness** [ch 070 / 070.04; ch 070 / locations]

**High Tower / Security Control Room**
- Restricted; entry needs two keys and is always observed, under doubled guard [ch 058 / Locations; ch 059 / 059.03]
- One key was held by Janussi (in the locket) and the other by Kalan Strongbranch [ch 058 / 058.06]
- Later state: A'lai held key #1, and Gyrgum held key #2 and the sapphire [ch 064 / 064.03; ch 064 / items]
- Manshoon's wall of force shattered the magical door to the security control room. The artifacts inside remain intact but are now unguarded [ch 065 / 065.03; ch 065 / locations]
- Zhentarim raiders were found looting books from the lobby against a written manifest. One of them carried the bolt type that killed a Watcher on the walkway, which proves outside infiltrators reached the tower [ch 064 / 064.02; ch 064 / 064.03]

**Vault of Secrets / Obsidian Tower**
- Hidden beneath Candlekeep. Filling the ink pot of the brass Alaundo statue in the House of Alaundo (Founders Court) opens a staircase down to it [ch 067 / 067.02; ch 067 / 067.04]
- Riddle answers found by the party:
  - Fustilugs, on the black knight in Philosopher's Court [ch 067 / 067.02]
  - The bowing Batbayar statue in the School of the Drama Library [ch 067 / 067.02]
  - The Hearth [ch 068 / 068.03]
  - The height of the High Tower of Exaltation [ch 068 / 068.03]
- **The Great Shaft:** a drop of roughly 1,000 ft with a feather-fall zone near the bottom [ch 068 / locations]
- **Lava cavern:** contains the obsidian bridge, whose first 10 ft lie in an anti-magic field. Miirym can widen the bridge to 30 ft for ten minutes [ch 068 / locations]
- **The lava pit:** the final resting place of Manshoon's simulacrum; its heat and anti-magic field remain active [ch 070 / 070.02; ch 069 / locations]
- **Tower library:** about 100 dangerous warded books; any disturbance risks chain-reaction explosions [ch 069 / locations]
- **Upper chamber:** a velvet tray of ten prismatic gems (four dark) and an obsidian door with eleven scorch marks [ch 069 / locations]
- **Inner Book Chamber:** the door is now open and unguarded, and the Book of Vile Darkness sits on its podium [ch 070 / 070.03; ch 070 / locations]

**Other Candlekeep sites**
- **Deneir's Sanctum:** the party's official base [ch 059 / 059.01]. It was later used to lock them in, and helmed horrors smashed the door open to target Daz [ch 062 / 062.05; ch 063 / 063.01]
- **Bookwyrm's Office:** Bookwyrm reinstated Kalan here [ch 062 / 062.03]. Later her throat was torn out by a beast, leaving a note: *He is using the beast to—* [ch 063 / 063.02]
- **North Galleries:** Kalan hid Tadric there with the real key; Moziqodo attacked and died there [ch 063 / 063.04; ch 063 / 063.05]
- **Sea Warden's Tower:** Kalan deputised the party as Watchers of Candlekeep here [ch 063 / 063.03]. It was also the site of Zalthir's Trial of the Broken Mirror [ch 056 / 056.05]
- **Candlekeep Prison (null-magic):** A'lai seeks incarceration here as his only hope against Manshoon, though he fears even it after the breach [ch 065 / 065.01; ch 067 / 067.02]
- **Janussi murder sites:**
  - Janussi's Chambers: the crime scene; the third floor was a love nest, and the note there shows his contempt for Kalan's reforms [ch 058 / Locations; ch 061 / 061.04]
  - Southern Dining Hall: hiding place of the heart and cleaver inside Milil's lead-lined chalice; the Deadwinter Tree there is an evidence site [ch 060 / locations; ch 061 / 061.07]
  - Oak Tree Apothecary: Midnight Tears were stolen while A'lai distracted Leuwin, and Nibbles witnessed it [ch 061 / 061.07]
  - Alkrist's Room: safe opened, holding 750 gp, a stolen potion of flying, a potion of superior healing, and a red herring coin [ch 062 / 062.04]
  - The Baths: site of the 11:45 a.m. Alkrist–A'lai meeting [ch 061 / 061.07]
- **Orrery of the Astronomicon:** Sylvira noted three suspicious non-staff people, a possible sign of Manshoon's agents [ch 067 / 067.03]
- **House of the Binder:** promised a copy of any book on successful resolution of the investigation [ch 057 / locations]
- **Whispering Dome:** Glabbagool formalised his sidekick bond with Zalthir here [ch 059 / 059.06]
- **House of Mechanus:** home to Spanner and thirteen modrons [ch 067 / 067.05]

- **Silver Marches:** ravaged by Uthgardt raids [ch 055 / locations]

- **Daggerford:** the River Shining Tavern, where the party met Maerith and Elin [ch 056 / 056.01; ch 056 / 056.02]

- **Way of the Lion:** the pressure in Daz's head intensifies along this road [ch 056 / 056.04]
- **Menzoberranzan:** the contract protecting Daz was funded here; the patron is unknown [ch 053 / 053.01]

### Blingdenstone
- **Status:** deep gnome city retaken by Clan Diggermattock [ch 035 / 035.02]
- **Settlement terms (fragile peace):** miners are paid fair wages, wererats control the upper boroughs, ghosts keep sanctum rights, and the city is open for trade but not tourism [ch 048 / 048.06]
- **Earth Elementals:** banned from labour and restricted to defence [ch 045 / 045.02]
- **Traders' Grotto:** the ooze breach is sealed and should be clear within days to a week [ch 048 / 048.05]. Starlace Curios and Arcana opened here; the 10% coupon applies [ch 045 / 045.03]
- **Pudding King's Throne Room:** abandoned. Green slime remains but only drops on those directly beneath it, and acid pools still nourish oozes [ch 047 / 047.09]. Fungi there grow unnaturally fast, a sign of Zuggtmoy's creeping influence [ch 048 / 048.01]
- **Hall of Melting Mirrors:** neutralised [ch 048 / 048.02]
- **Steadfast Stone:** now houses three freed Galeb Duhr [ch 042 / 042.02]
- **Neheedra's Lair:** abandoned; held five spider-silk dresses worth 200 gp each [ch 042 / 042.01]
- **Festering Fissure:** Zuggtmoy has planted nightmare mushroom children here [ch 044 / 044.01]
- **Whiteshell Mine:** contains a time-snagged wall and the Summoning Circle Cavern, site of Entémoch's boon [ch 044 / 044.05; ch 044 / 044.07]
- **Northern exit tunnels:** a Zuggtmoy puffball is growing in a wall crack [ch 048 / 048.07]
