## Events
- The party arrives in Daggerford during a flashback sequence of their journey south from the Silver Marches, noting an unsettling, heavy silence among the townsfolk. [ch 056 / 056.01]
- Daz examines a bronze monument to Travis Houlin in the village square, identifying a Dwarven inscription reading "The move is yours" that mirrors Stroud’s statue in Waterdeep, while noting Milo Goodbarrel recorded the site. [ch 056 / 056.01]
- The local village priest explains the monument was funded by the Metalworkers Guild and declares himself a "pure Bahamutian," though he tolerates the Stroudite inscription as a gift; Gyrgum donates a gold piece for a pewter medallion. [ch 056 / 056.01]
- Maerith of the Ford approaches the party at the River Shining Tavern, seeking help for her daughter Elin, who has been silent for six days and drawing a recurring shape. [ch 056 / 056.02]
- Gyrgum and Daz fail to diagnose Elin medically; Dawnbringer pompously casts *lesser restoration*, causing Elin to briefly speak the cryptic phrase "The wells are not wells anymore" before falling silent again. [ch 056 / 056.02]
- The party concludes Elin’s condition is an internal twisting of the mind, not a disease, marking the presence of Underdark madness seeping into the surface world; Gyrgum confirms a tenuous link between Elin’s state and Daz’s worsening headache. [ch 056 / 056.02]
- The party travels to Beregost and stays at the Burning Wizard Inn, where innkeeper Festrum recounts the tale of a wizard burned alive by a dragon cult and points to a permanent soot mark on the south wall. [ch 056 / 056.03]
- A bard performs a song about a Kenku thief; Gyrgum requests a song about Stroud, which she sings as a romanticized drinking ballad, noting similarities to Whistler’s ethics. [ch 056 / 056.03]
- Daz notices a human scholar reciting a chant with syncopated breathing errors identical to those of Eldred and the Field Ward Preacher, and records it for investigation at Candlekeep. [ch 056 / 056.03]
- Thorin sits by the sea for the first time, reflecting on life’s unpredictability with Dawnbringer illuminating the darkness, while Zalthir meditates on the inn roof and finds unexpected peace. [ch 056 / 056.03]
- The party arrives at Candlekeep via the Way of the Lion; Daz’s headache intensifies as they approach, confirming a connection to the spreading Underdark madness. [ch 056 / 056.04]
- Bookwyrm, the First Reader, assigns the party to mentors: Daz to Sister Yvenne, Zalthir to Brother Khell-Vire, Thorin to Master Archivist Philemon, and Gyrgum to Vareth. [ch 056 / 056.04]
- Gatewarden Kalan Strongbranch explains Candlekeep’s policy on prisoners (exile rather than execution) regarding the shrunken House T’sarran spy; the party declines to hand her over. [ch 056 / 056.04]
- Glabbagool explains his routine of "jailbreaking" the spy by letting her attack him and then teaching her meditation; Zalthir confirms this is valid pre-monk training, and the party decides to keep the spy under Glabbagool’s care. [ch 056 / 056.04]
- Zalthir meets Brother Khell-Vire, who shows him a cryptic note reading "Zalthir, the Underdark, until the Bone King sings" and sends him to the Trial of the Broken Mirror. [ch 056 / 056.05]
- During the martial trial, Zalthir fails formal skill demonstrations but impresses Master Kenshi by landing a powerful hit and smashing a brick mid-air with a combined Shadow Step and strike, earning respect for his unconventional technique. [ch 056 / 056.05]
- A xenobiology sage asks if a mindless ooze can achieve enlightenment; Zalthir defends Glabbagool’s sentience, resulting in the party receiving a vial of Resonant Slime. [ch 056 / 056.05]
- Master Kenshi acknowledges Zalthir’s unique mastery of combining Shadow Step with strikes and teaches him one of three specialized shadow techniques; Zalthir selects option C. [ch 056 / 056.05]
- Thorin meets Master Archivist Philemon, who identifies Dawnbringer as a traumatized Netherese artifact; Thorin corrects Philemon, insisting she is his friend. [ch 056 / 056.06]
- Philemon outlines a two-phase therapy for Dawnbringer: the work of light (remembering daylight without fighting) and the work of mourning; Thorin agrees to the process. [ch 056 / 056.06]
- Daz meets Sister Yvenne in the Hall of Divination, exchanging information about his amnesia, the Underdark, and his worsening headaches for knowledge about drow politics. [ch 056 / 056.07]
- Yvenne explains the "rule of intrigue" and house forfeiture laws in Menzoberranzan; Daz identifies the word "struck" in a record of extinct houses as evidence of paid censorship rather than elimination. [ch 056 / 056.07]
- Daz discovers a razored appendix in a heraldry book and a footnote referencing "Daz'issin," a softening name for boys mothers hoped would survive; Yvenne implies this connects to his identity but denies it openly. [ch 056 / 056.07]
- Daz questions if the erased houses were truly gone, sensing Yvenne’s body language confirms he is close to a dangerous truth about his lineage that Candlekeep cannot explicitly share. [ch 056 / 056.07]
