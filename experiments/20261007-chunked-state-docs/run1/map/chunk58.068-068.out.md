- In the Vault of Dangerous Secrets, the party found Manshoon (actually a simulacrum) reading a forbidden tome; Gyrgum successfully persuaded him to move their duel to the lower chamber to protect the books [ch 068 / 068.05].
- Combat began in the lower chamber; Manshoon cast *Mirror Image* before descending [ch 068 / 068.05].
- Daz cast *Phantasmal Killer*; Manshoon declined to use *Counterspell* and succeeded on his Wisdom save by rolling an 8 plus +9, taking 13 psychic damage but avoiding the disadvantage effect [ch 068 / 068.05].
- Daz moved onto the bridge, placing himself inside the active anti-magic band, setting up a strategy to lure Manshoon into the suppression zone for mundane attacks [ch 068 / 068.05].
