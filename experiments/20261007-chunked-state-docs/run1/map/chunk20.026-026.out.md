## Events
- The party levels up to 5th level; Gyrgum gains Spirit Guardians, Daz gains Fireball, and Thorin gains Tactical Shift and a second attack [ch 026 / 026.01].

- Captain Gorglak demands proof the party are not the Ember Vanguard; Gyrgum performs the secret Stone Guard salute with advantage, and Daz identifies Gorglak as corrupt, leading to a contingency plan involving Misty Step [ch 026 / 026.01].
- Gorglak concedes they are not the Ember Vanguard but demands a bribe of twenty gold pieces; the party pays, avoiding inspection by five invisible guards [ch 026 / 026.02].
- Gorglak warns the party that the Deepking fears a drow raid and the derro suspect the Deepking is behind the unrest [ch 026 / 026.02].

- The party encounters a Duergar informant near Clan Thrazgad's forge, paying him a gold piece (funded by Thorin's wager) for advice on navigating the city and news about the assassination of the derro High Council [ch 026 / 026.03].
- The party decides to visit the stone giants first, reasoning they are the most level-headed faction, and travels to Cairngorm Cavern [ch 026 / 026.03].
- Thorin presents Hgraam with the two-headed statue recovered from the derro; Hgraam accepts it to begin curing his kin and rewards the party with five hundred gold pieces [ch 026 / 026.04].
- Hgraam closes the cavern to outsiders to consult the stones and warns the party that the Stone Guard are agitated [ch 026 / 026.04].
- Zalthir casts Darkness to guide the party through the smog-filled streets of Gracklstugh toward Gartokkar's residence [ch 026 / 026.05].
- The party meets Gartokkar, who is surprised by their survival and confused by rumors of powerful wizards; they hand over the corrupted dragon egg, noting its ritual markings and uncertain viability [ch 026 / 026.06].
- Gartokkar explains the egg is meant to replace Themberchaud before the dragon realizes he is not in control of the city, then pays the party the remaining 2,400 gp [ch 026 / 026.06].
- The party attempts to sneak toward the Darklake District but fails their stealth check despite advantage from darkness, triggering an ambush by five Gray Ghosts (derro assassins) [ch 026 / 026.07].
- Zalthir kills two assassins quickly; Gyrgum casts Spirit Guardians, killing two more; Thorin kills the final assassin [ch 026 / 026.07].
- The party loots the bodies, finding a letter from the Council of Savants ordering the Gray Ghosts to kill the duergar who helped the Ember Vanguard, revealing the depth of the conspiracy [ch 026 / 026.07].
