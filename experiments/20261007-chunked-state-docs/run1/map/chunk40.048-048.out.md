- Jimjar explained the die was won from a blind Duergar in Gracklstugh and carved from a saint's knucklebone; Gyrgum paid the bet and pocketed the spent die [ch 048 / 048.05].
- The Ember Vanguard mediated the Council of Blingdenstone, brokering a wage agreement, granting the Goldwhisker wererats control of the upper boroughs for defense, and establishing sustainable mining practices for the sanctum [ch 048 / 048.06].
- Dorbo Diggermattock, fearful of Daz's rising popularity, announced the party would be sent to the Overbright the next morning to proclaim Blingdenstone "open for business" [ch 048 / 048.06].
- Following a long rest, the party departed Blingdenstone with an honor guard; Daz received the Voucher of the Varmint from Chipgrin [ch 048 / 048.07].
- The party overheard gnomes recounting absurd legends of the "Ember Vanguard," mistaking their deeds for tales of an evil drow strike force and exaggerating their abilities [ch 048 / 048.07].
- Jimjar warned of a rapidly growing puffball mushroom (Zuggtmoy's influence) before vanishing; Thorin used Know Your Enemy to reveal Jimjar possessed divine-like immunities and resistances [ch 048 / 048.07].
