## Events
- The party secured a larger boat on the Darklake and interrogated their duergar prisoner, Brannum Redmarch, under threat of death to ensure he navigated them to Gracklstugh. [ch 011 / 011.01]
- On the fifteenth day of their voyage, the party encountered a diamond-shaped limestone islet featuring a bone pyramid topped with a ram skull; Brannum panicked upon seeing it, realizing they had taken a shortcut associated with his lost crew. [ch 011 / 011.02]
- A lightning bolt struck the boat, followed by an attack from a beholder zombie emerging from behind a stalactite; Gyrgum’s Command spell failed as the undead creature did not understand Common. [ch 011 / 011.02]
- The party battled the beholder zombie; Zalthir created magical darkness to blind the creature, Thorin attacked from the shadows, and the companion crew provided crossbow support after initial hesitation. [ch 011 / 011.03]
- The beholder zombie paralyzed Daz and bit Zalthir for 16 damage, but was finally destroyed by a combination of Zalthir’s strikes and the crew’s volley, exploding into ash. [ch 011 / 011.03]
- Gyrgum healed Zalthir, and Brannum confessed that his previous crews had vanished at this location, mentioning rumors of an "evil altar." [ch 011 / 011.04]
- Daz discovered the sunken wrecks of Brannum’s lost boats and zombies on the lakebed below the islet; the party avoided landing on the islet after Gyrgum’s Find Traps spell revealed no traps. [ch 011 / 011.04]
- The party retrieved a sunken chest from the zombie-infested waters using a coordinated dive by Thorin, protected by Shield of Faith and hidden by Zalthir’s Darkness. [ch 011 / 011.05]
- The chest contained 120 silver pieces, 30 gold pieces, four spell scrolls, and a driftglobe; Brannum’s claim to the treasure was rebuffed by Daz. [ch 011 / 011.05]
- Two days later, an aquatic troll demanded a meal; the party refused to surrender anyone, fought briefly with magic and oars, and fled the creature, which regenerated and retreated. [ch 011 / 011.06]
- The party arrived at the duergar city of Gracklstugh, planning to enter via the docks to avoid guards, sell gear at the Blade Bazaar, and consider ransoming Brannum. [ch 011 / 011.07]
