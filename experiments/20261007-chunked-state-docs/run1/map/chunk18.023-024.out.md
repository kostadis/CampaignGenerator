## Events
- The party discovers a vast, foul-smelling cavern in the Whorlstone Tunnels where five Derro cultists are performing a ritual to sprout second heads on stone giant statues [ch 023 / 023.01].
- The party identifies the cult as followers of Demogorgon, linked to the previous demonic infusion of the dragon egg, and realizes the ritual caused the rampaging two-headed giant in the market [ch 023 / 023.01].
- Gyrgum deduces that bringing a corrupted stone giant statue to Stonespeaker Hgraam could reverse the curse on his people [ch 023 / 023.01].
- The party retreats to Buppido’s former resting place for a long rest to recover magical strength [ch 023 / 023.02].
- During the rest, the party binds their ghost companion Pelek with silk rope to prevent the host’s consciousness from resurfacing; Gyrgum wins a 5gp wager against Jimjar that Pelek will not escape [ch 023 / 023.02].
- The party returns to the ritual cavern to find Narrak preparing a third statue; they launch a surprise ambush using stealth and magical darkness [ch 023 / 023.03; ch 023 / 023.04].
- Narrak casts Fear, routing three party members until Zalthir repositions the darkness sphere to break the line of sight and Daz uses Earthen Grasp to pin Narrak [ch 023 / 023.04].
- In the ensuing chaotic battle within the darkness, Gyrgum is knocked prone and loses concentration on his acid spell, while Narrak casts Silence to block verbal components [ch 024 / 024.01; ch 024 / 024.02].
- Zalthir uses his Eldritch Claw Tattoo to transform into tentacled darkness, grappling and damaging the ettin Grula-Munga and eventually the savant Narrak [ch 024 / 024.02].
- Daz uses Earthen Grasp to crush and restrain Narrak repeatedly, breaking his concentration and preventing him from moving [ch 024 / 024.02; ch 024 / 024.03].
- Grula-Munga is slain by Zalthir’s tentacle strikes, ending her service to the cult [ch 024 / 024.02; ch 024 / 024.03].
- Thorin delivers the killing blow to Narrak, who gasps that the ritual cannot be undone and that someone has been "sacrificed" [ch 024 / 024.03].
- The party loots Narrak’s chamber, opening his iron chest with a key from his neck to find 45gp, 15sp, and five doses of Keoghtom’s Ointment [ch 024 / 024.04].
- The party recovers dark texts detailing the cult’s methods and discusses the political implications of killing two Council of Savants members [ch 024 / 024.04].
- Stool interrupts the looting to remind the party to rescue his friend Rumpadump [ch 024 / 024.04].

## Concluded
- The Derro cult’s ritual to transform stone giants into two-headed beasts is disrupted, and the ritual leader Narrak and all his cultists are slain [ch 023 / 023.04; ch 024 / 024.03].
- The party secured the evidence of the cult's activities, including the texts and the corrupted statues, though the statue itself did not "come alive" but serves as a focus [ch 024 / 024.04].
