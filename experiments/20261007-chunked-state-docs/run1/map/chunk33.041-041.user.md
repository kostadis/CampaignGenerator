# Chapter 41 Mirror, Mirror, On the Wall, Who's the Maddest of Them All?

Date: 2025-12-15

Daz's player joined late, while the party planned for the Medusa; the GM ran Daz until then.

## Scenes
### 041.01 Battle with Earth Elemental and Gargoyle

The party found themselves locked in fierce combat with a maddened Earth Elemental and a gargoyle in a darkened chamber.

Zalthir stood about 30 feet above the fight. The GM offered him a jump down for 3d6 falling damage, halved on a DC 13 Acrobatics check. Instead he used the shadows, teleporting through the darkness to appear beside the elemental. He struck it for 14 and 8 and then grappled it to pin it in place.

Daz unleashed a fireball for 24 damage that engulfed both enemies. The elemental failed its save "predictably," and the gargoyle took the full 24 as well. Thorin missed the gargoyle at first, then used a feinting attack to land blows of 13 and 10, which left it at two hit points.

Gyrgum spoke "some mystical words about a Bahamut" and summoned spectral guardians in the form of fey. Their radiant energy destroyed the gargoyle and dealt 13 damage to the elemental.

Daz was reluctant to spend more spells ("there might be a Medusa, so I'm going to keep my spells for that") and grudgingly landed one Scorching Ray. Thorin then negotiated with his intelligent sword Dawnbringer about entering the darkness. Dawnbringer threatened that she could impose her will through a Charisma save, and Thorin promised her a finer scabbard in exchange for her cooperation. He then rolled two natural 20s on a single attack, "a supernatural 20."

The elemental kept trying to strike back at Zalthir and kept missing. Spirit Guardians and Thorin's opportunity attack left it at exactly one hit point. Thorin's final blow, a Hill Strike that rolled a 1, "just push[ed] him over," and the massive creature collapsed into a pile of rubble.

Daz called upon fire once more ("this is why I saved the fireball from last time"). The GM, running Daz, found his Sculpt Spells feature mid-cast, so Daz shaped the flames around his allies and dealt 21 damage while the animated warriors burned. Gyrgum positioned himself among the statues and summoned more spectral guardians. Their holy energy caused the stone warriors to crumble. But as soon as the first wave fell, six more statues came to life, revealing the endless nature of the threat.

### 041.05 Investigation of the Malevolent Entity

Through careful investigation, the party worked out what was happening. Daz rolled a 28 on Arcana thanks to his expertise. The group deduced that a malevolent entity was animating the statues, but it could only control six at a time. Statues of Drow petrified by a Medusa should not animate on their own.

They tested this theory by stepping in and out of the chamber, watching the statues animate and return to stillness. Glabbagool found this particularly entertaining and played a game of entering and exiting the room. Daz sent his bat familiar into the chamber, and the six animated statues immediately gave chase. Their stone feet echoed through the cavern as they pursued the small creature.

The party concluded that this entity was likely impervious to normal attacks and would require specific weapons or magic to defeat. Daz reminded them of their actual task: "Our job was to cleanse the temple." They decided to gather more information before confronting the entity directly.

### 041.06 Preparing for the Medusa

Knowing a Medusa lurked in an adjacent chamber, the party prepared their strategy carefully. They had already acquired special eyewear that would protect them from the creature's petrifying gaze by granting advantage on the Constitution save. They still wanted additional safeguards.

Thorin asked whether a reflective surface could turn the Medusa's deadly stare back upon herself. Several ideas were floated and rejected: pots and pans bought with their deep gnome discount, polishing Zalthir's scales, and Minor Illusion. Zalthir then suggested shaping water into an ice mirror, and Thorin pointed out that Dawnbringer could supply the bright light it needed.

Daz worked out how to craft a highly reflective surface from shaped water, gems, and ice, and silvered the water to sharpen the reflection. The party agreed to construct this mirror before entering the Medusa's lair.

### 041.07 Confronting the Medusa

When they finally entered the chamber, they found it decorated in the style of a Drow city. Spider-themed tapestries hung from the walls, and fine furniture and racks of old, expensive clothing were arranged throughout. At the center sat a female Drow upon a throne, her white hair writhing with pale serpents. The moment she sensed their presence, her eyes opened, and she charged forward, driven mad by her time there.

Daz immediately unleashed a fireball at the approaching Medusa for 30 damage, halved to 15 by her save. He cleverly used the same flames to ignite a bed in the chamber, creating the bright light needed for their mirror strategy to work. The GM called it "a clever use of fireball."

Zalthir rushed forward with the ice mirror in hand. He readied an action to raise it if the Medusa attempted to use her petrifying gaze. He struck at her with his free hand, landing two solid blows for 21 while keeping the mirror ready.

When the Medusa finally turned her deadly stare upon him, Zalthir thrust the mirror before her face. She screamed in horror upon seeing her reflection and lamented her disheveled appearance, but managed to resist being turned to stone by her own power. In retaliation, she lashed out with her claws and the venomous serpents that formed her hair. Her snake bites pierced Zalthir's defenses and injected him with poison, for 15 damage in total.

Gyrgum attempted to strike from a safe distance with a Spiritual Weapon, which missed. Thorin positioned himself carefully, staying just beyond the 30-foot range of the Medusa's petrifying gaze, about 55 feet away and within his darts' 60-foot long range. He hurled darts at the creature, and through a combination of skill and fortune, his projectiles found their mark. A luck point turned a miss into a hit.

One throw was particularly devastating: the Hill Strike dart struck with such force that it knocked the Medusa off her feet on a failed DC 15 Strength save, sending her crashing to the ground. As she lay prone before them, Thorin hurled one more dart into her for another 6. The party readied themselves to finish the battle they had so carefully prepared for, and the session ended mid-fight to "continue next week."
