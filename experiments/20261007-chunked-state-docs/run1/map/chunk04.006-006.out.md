## Events
- On the third day of travel, the party enters a narrow tunnel lined with glowing Ormu moss and realizes they are dangerously low on supplies. [ch 006 / 006.01]
- The party forages for water, gathering ten gallons which Daz freezes into portable ice blocks to solve their lack of containers. [ch 006 / 006.01]
- The party forages for food, successfully gathering seventeen pounds (seven extra) to sustain the group for more than a day. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- Eldeth leads an effort to cover the party's tracks, successfully reducing the drow pursuit level from four to three. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- The party blocks the narrow tunnel behind them, with Jimjar disguising the blockage so it does not appear intentional; Jimjar owes Gyrgum five gold from this wager. [ch 006 / 006.03]
- That night, Daz notices the deep gnome twins Topsy and Turvy whispering secretly, though his Insight check reveals nothing of their intent. [ch 006 / 006.03]
- Two days later, the party finds more water and food, and Sarith guides them to the Silken Paths, a massive chasm filled with shifting spider webs. [ch 006 / 006.04]
- Goblin guides Yuk Yuk and Spiderbait emerge and offer their services for two gold per day; the party, being destitute, cannot pay upfront. [ch 006 / 006.04; ch 006 / 006.05]
- Jimjar wagers that the goblins cannot navigate the webs; Yuk Yuk counters with a 20-gold bet that he can get across. [ch 006 / 006.05]
- The goblins demonstrate their ability to slide and surf across the webs; Gyrgum realizes Jimjar has no money to pay the debt but plays along, offering to sign over Jimjar's five-gold IOU. [ch 006 / 006.05]
- Spiderbait proposes the goblins guide the party in exchange for a share of future gold; the party agrees, gaining Yuk Yuk and Spiderbait as guides. [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 006 / 006.06]
- The party prepares to cross the Silken Paths without light sources, tethering themselves together with rope to prevent fatal falls if strands break. [ch 006 / 006.06]
- The party discovers a moving cocoon in the webbing; Stool establishes telepathic contact with the trapped halfling, Fargas Rumblefoot. [ch 006 / 006.07]
- Fargas explains he was fleeing mad gnolls and bargains for his rescue by promising to lead the party to a lost tomb filled with treasure. [ch 006 / 006.07]
- Yuk Yuk warns that giant spiders are approaching; Thorin slashes through the cocoon, freeing Fargas. [ch 006 / 006.07]
- Initiative is rolled with Thorin at 17 and Zalthir at 5 as the spiders close in. [ch 006 / 006.07]
- Zalthir cuts a web segment to force the spiders to spin a new bridge and strikes a second strand, which frays but holds; the session ends on this cliffhanger. [ch 006 / 006.08]
