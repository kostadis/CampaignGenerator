A sentient gelatinous cube traveling with the party. He took longer than the others to climb Blingdenstone’s gnome-sized stairs, and his sudden appearance at the gate nearly provoked an attack by the guards.

Glabbagool was once an ordinary, unintelligent ooze. Something unknown caused him to begin thinking and speaking. He now considers himself possibly the only talking gelatinous cube in the universe. He is friendly, eats only dead garbage rather than living creatures, and has repeatedly aided the party in dangerous situations.
