- Stool pleads to rescue his friend Rumpadump, a myconid sprout seen near strangely dancing myconids, and the party agrees to prioritize the assassin job while detouring to check on him [ch 018 / 018.06].
- The party visits Clan Ironhead to establish their cover as dissidents; Thangus Ironhead publicly dismisses the Whorlstone dangers as children's stories [ch 018 / 018.07].
- Grinta Ironhead secretly offers the party favorable trade terms from Clan Ironhead if they facilitate the Deepking's removal from power [ch 018 / 018.07].
