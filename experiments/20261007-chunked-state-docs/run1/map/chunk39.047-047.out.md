## Events
- The Pudding King, revealed as a sentient ooze and minion of Juiblex, telepathically screamed his intention to turn all gnomes into ooze while being grappled by Zalthir [ch 047 / 047.01].

- Thorin attacked what he thought was the Pudding King, only to hear Glabbagool’s voice, revealing that Glabbagool and the Pudding King had swapped bodies [ch 047 / 047.06].

- The Pudding King, separated from his lair, was hit by Thorin’s opportunity attack, reducing his speed to zero [ch 047 / 047.07].
- Zalthir delivered the final blows to the Pudding King using Shadow Step and Eldritch Claw strikes, causing him to dissolve into inert puddles [ch 047 / 047.08].
- Daz cast Fireball on Prince Livid and Princess Ebonmire, dealing significant damage; Thorin then killed both minions with his attacks [ch 047 / 047.09].
- The toxic influence of Juiblex receded from the area, but fungal growth twisted, suggesting Zuggtmoy was seizing the power vacuum [ch 047 / 047.10].
- Glabbagool, now in the Pudding King’s ooze body, expressed excitement about being carried in a jar or the Bag of Holding and serving as a garbage disposal [ch 047 / 047.10].
- The party discovered two spellbooks containing many spells in Princess Ebonmire’s stomach [ch 047 / 047.10].
- The party leveled up and secured their ticket to escape the Underdark [ch 047 / 047.10].
