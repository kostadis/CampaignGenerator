### 043.01 Hallowing the Temple and Galeb Duhr Revelations

After defeating the deranged earth elementals, the party witnessed a white light shine from the ancient menhir, creating a powerful hallowing effect that cleansed the temple. The three Galeb Duhr, their rocky forms pulsing with satisfaction, acknowledged the party's success with a grave "well done." The first Galeb Duhr recognized Gyrgum as a cleric of Bahamut, and Gyrgum proclaimed this "the Realm of the Dragon." The Galeb Duhrs quickly corrected him that this was actually "the Realm of the Rock." The creatures explained that the party had successfully hallowed the temple from the corrupting power of Ogrémoch, the evil elemental earth god who had infested these rocks for centuries. They argued first about whether to explain who Ogrémoch was, because telling the party might be "insulting." When Daz admitted, "We could use a hint on who Ogremoch is," one of them said, "see, I told you that you should have told them."

Thorin could not resist making a pun: "I mean, we're in here. It's a cavern. Of course, it's hollow."

They questioned Thorin about his unusual choice to wear giant clothes, and he explained his complicated relationship with giants, having been raised by them despite being a dwarf. He called himself "a study in contrasts." However, the Galeb Duhr revealed that the party's work was not yet complete. They still needed to obtain Entémoch's boon, the blessing of the good elemental prince who had once aided the deep gnomes of Blingdenstone. The Galeb Duhr gave a long, rambling account of Entémoch's boons, which the GM compared to a "grandpa Simpson kind of 20-minute speech." From it the party gathered that the boon might help in the coming fight against the Pudding King. The creatures argued amongst themselves about where Entémoch might be found before finally agreeing that the gnomes would surely know, having visited him regularly in ages past. Having done a century's worth of talking, they sat down and stared at the menhir.

A companion who, along with Jimjar, successfully dealt with the ghost Vazuk in Blingdenstone.

## Items
### Empty Fourth-Level Spell Gem

A specific fungi usually found in Neverlight Grove but discovered by Bimble in a 'garden of stone' within the mines. It grows from the corpse of a dead giant in the Festering Fissure. A warding prayer must be chanted within 10 feet while it is cut to keep demonic spirits at bay. It is needed as an ingredient for Kazook Pickshine's alchemical solution.
- Cutting it is an Acrobatics check, not an attack. The GM rules the lichen is not a creature.
- The GM later relaxes the 10-foot requirement: the chanter must chant while the lichen is cut but need not be physically present.

A weapon wielded by Thorin that deals additional damage against undead. She is used in combat against the shambling mound. Thorin's attacks also apply "sap," giving the target disadvantage on its next attack.

A spell that one of the party members chooses to imbue into the empty fourth-level spell gem, indicating its potential use for widespread healing in future encounters. Gyrgum loads it: "In that case, I will load it with mass healing word for now."
