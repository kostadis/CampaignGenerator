## Locations

### Underdark & Subterranean Travel
*   **The Overbright** [ch 049 / 049.01; ch 053 / 053.05; ch 053 / locations; ch 054 / locations]: The surface world, referred to by Underdark dwellers. Sunlight destroys Drow-crafted items like the cloak of elvenkind and causes discomfort for drow.
*   **Darklake** [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / locations; ch 009 / locations; ch 011 / locations; ch 011 / 011.02; ch 027 / locations]: A massive network of underground waterways stretching over 100 miles, serving as a major travel route. Characterized by low ceilings, submerged hazards, blind cavefish, bone pyramids, undead, trolls, and dangerous aquatic life. It is described as a "Swiss cheese" of passages. The tunnel forked around the Bone Pyramid Islet.
*   **Faerzress Suffused Area** [ch 028 / 028.01]: Region with magical radiation blocking teleportation and causing madness; linked to recent demonic incursions.
*   **Underdark** [ch 034 / locations]: Characterized by shifting passages and geology destabilized by recent tremors, causing flooding in the Oozing Temple.

### Velkynvelve & Escape Routes
*   **Velkynvelve** [ch 002 / locations; ch 004 / 004.01; ch 004 / locations]: A drow outpost/prison suspended in a cavern, featuring hanging towers, rope bridges, webs, a slave pen, and a waterfall feeding a pool and underground river. It was under attack by demons.
*   **The Lift** [ch 002 / locations; ch 002 / 002.02]: A winch-and-basket mechanism attached to the barracks, supervised by quaggoths, and the only safe visible descent from the barracks level.
*   **Ilvara’s Quarters** [ch 002 / locations; ch 002 / 002.03]: Located beneath the shrine to Lolth in the largest hanging tower, containing a bathtub, a treasure chest, and a passage to Shoor Vandree’s quarters.
*   **Velkynvelve Guard Tower** [ch 003 / 003.04]: The lower chamber is now secured by escaped prisoners; the upper armory has been looted of most weapons and armor.
*   **Narrow Tunnel** [ch 006 / 006.01; ch 006 / 006.03]: A tight passage lined with Ormu moss, now blocked and disguised to look natural.
*   **Silken Paths** [ch 006 / 006.04; ch 006 / locations; ch 007 / 007.01; ch 007 / 007.04]: A massive chasm (500 ft deep, 2000 ft wide) filled with shifting giant spider webs acting as bridges; paths are unstable, change frequently, and feature five-hit-point strands. The party exited toward Sloobludop.

### Sloobludop & Demogorgon Impact
*   **Sloobludop** [ch 008 / 008.01; ch 008 / locations; ch 009 / 009.01]: A Kuo-toa settlement on the Darklake featuring docks, jetties, and a sacrificial altar. It was devastated by Demogorgon's arrival.
*   **Bone Pyramid Islet** [ch 011 / locations; ch 011 / 011.02; ch 011 / 011.04]: A diamond-shaped limestone islet with a bone monument, sunken boats, and undead guardians; associated with an "evil altar" rumor.

### Gracklstugh (Duergar City)
*   **Gracklstugh** [ch 011 / locations; ch 011 / 011.07; ch 012 / 012.01; ch 012 / locations; ch 013 / locations; ch 013 / 013.05; ch 014 / locations; ch 018 / 018.04; ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / locations; ch 049 / 049.03]: A grim duergar city in the Underdark with draconian laws against foreigners, currently plagued by a wave of citizen madness and paranoid laws. Guards are at the doors rather than the docks, and non-duergar are restricted to the Darklake district. It is a political tinderbox where misinformation could trigger all-out war; Stone Guard integrity is declining due to bribery. The party caused mass panic by riding Themberchaud. Gracklstugh later ambushed Ilvara's party, mistaking them for the Ember Vanguard due to rumors.
*   **Darklake District** [ch 012 / locations]: A walled-off area in Gracklstugh where outsiders can trade.
*   **Blade Bazaar** [ch 011 / locations; ch 011 / 011.07; ch 012 / 012.02; ch 012 / locations]: A trading hub within the Darklake district where the party planned to sell stolen gear and buy equipment. It sells non-magical goods, and merchants display signs of madness.
*   **Overlake Hold** [ch 013 / locations; ch 013 / 013.06]: The prison and Stone Guard front office where Errde Blackskull conducted her meeting with the party.
*   **Laduguer's Furrow** [ch 026 / 026.03; ch 026 / 026.05]: The affluent district of Gracklstugh, home to the Ironhead faction. It has a "Dickensian London" or "19th-century Pittsburgh" industrial vibe with heavy smog.
*   **West Cleft District** [ch 013 / locations; ch 013 / 013.06; ch 015 / 015.05; ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / 026.02]: A volatile, squalid derro slum and the original home of derro slaves. It is home to the derro Droki and the entrance to the Whorlstone Tunnels. The district contains the West Cleft Exit, a heavily guarded checkpoint currently under lockdown due to fears of a drow raid, featuring a 30-foot passage with invisible guards and corrupt officials demanding bribes.
*   **Cairngorm Cavern** [ch 013 / locations; ch 013 / 013.03; ch 015 / 015.01; ch 026 / 026.04]: The residence of the stone giants in the Southfurrow District, accessed via a long tunnel. Rihuud went mad here while communing with the stone. It is now closed to outsiders while Hgraam consults the stones regarding the curse.
*   **Themberchaud's Lair** [ch 014 / locations]: A massive cavern in the Flowstone District containing a mountain of gold.
*   **Forge of Thrazgad** [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / locations]: Major industrial forge in Gracklstugh; lit by Themberchaud's breath.
*   **Clan Ironhead** [ch 018 / 018.03]: The clan is barred from direct weapon orders and takes surplus work from Clan Steelshadow; they are openly opposed to the Deepking.
*   **Clan Xardelvar** [ch 018 / 018.03]: The clan fears the Deepking will take over their work as it resembles weaponsmithing; they are chafing under new regulations.

### Whorlstone Tunnels (Beneath Gracklstugh)
*   **Whorlstone Tunnels** [ch 015 / 015.05; ch 016 / locations; ch 016 / 016.02; ch 018 / 018.01; ch 019 / 019.01; ch 019 / locations; ch 022 / locations; ch 025 / locations]: A vast complex cave system beneath Gracklstugh, filled with faerzress, size-altering fungi, pits of zombies, and derro cult activity. It holds factions plotting against the Deepking and is known to both Keepers and Stone Guard as a source of dangerous secrets. Inhabited by Derro who have built traps capable of catching invisible intruders. It contains the territory of the Empty-Scabbard Killers and connections to spore networks.
*   **Obelisk Chamber** [ch 017 / locations; ch 020 / locations]: A room/cavern featuring a ten-foot ledge and a lower level, plunged into magical darkness. It contains an obelisk and the dragon egg, guarded by ten derro (though later notes suggest the party cleared enemies).
*   **The Corrupted Chamber** [ch 021 / locations]: A lab-like space containing evidence of the Council of Savants' corruption, distinct from a treasure storehouse.
*   **Brimstone Cavern** [ch 022 / locations]: A damp chamber smelling of brimstone, filled with fungi, a pit trap, and a ritual platform.
*   **Ritual Cavern** [ch 023 / 023.01; ch 024 / 024.04]: A foul-smelling cavern used by Derro cultists for Demogorgon rituals; currently empty of enemies but littered with loot and evidence.
*   **Buppido's Resting Place** [ch 023 / 023.02]: A secluded, safe alcove in the tunnels where the party took a long rest; previously used by Buppido without issue.
*   **Neverlight Grove** [ch 025 / locations; ch 029 / 029.05; ch 030 / 030.01; ch 030 / 030.05; ch 031 / locations; ch 032 / 032.01; ch 032 / locations]: A hidden myconid colony in a vast, bioluminescent mushroom cavern with a lake and a visible mushroom tower. It is larger than previously mapped and filled with psychedelic mushrooms and mossy terrain. The source of the corruption (Zuggtmoy’s influence) was released by Voosbur.
*   **Garden of Welcome** [ch 029 / 029.06; ch 031 / 031.02; ch 031 / locations]: A high plateau/restricted area where victims are buried alive to feed fungal growths and serve as sacrifices. Accessible via a secret passage known to Rumpadump. Basidia suspects it is the source of the corruption.
*   **Circle of Builders** [ch 030 / 030.05]: A vast cavern featuring terraced irrigation systems and thousands of glowing fungi, led by Gasbide.
*   **Yggmorgus** [ch 030 / 030.05]: A colossal mushroom in Neverlight Grove that Gasbide dreams of surpassing with a new tower.
*   **Circle of Masters** [ch 031 / locations]: An area beyond the Garden of Welcome dominated by a massive mushroom, serving as the source of the wedding procession's music.
*   **Circle of Hunters** [ch 029 / 029.07]: A circle led by Loobamub that tracks dead creatures for reanimation rather than hunting live prey.
*   **Majestic Mushroom** [ch 032 / 032.01; ch 032 / locations]: A colossal fungal structure serving as a staging ground for Zuggtmoy’s wedding rehearsal, filled with atonal music and toxic spores.
*   **Gas-Filled Cavern** [ch 032 / 032.05]: A chamber with a floor depression containing heavy, toxic, and combustible gas, safe to navigate via five-foot ledges if no open flames are present.
*   **Collapsed Tunnels** [ch 032 / 032.06; ch 032 / locations]: A maze of passages created by a tremor, blocking the party’s original route north toward Blingdenstone.

### The Oozing Temple & The Hidden Beach
*   **Oozing Temple** [ch 034 / locations]: A flooded Underdark temple with 10-foot ceilings, pit traps, and high concentrations of oozes. Now breached and drained by the party's escape.
*   **Large Cavern** [ch 034 / locations]: A vast underground space with breathable air, accessible by swimming up through a breached river passage.
*   **The Hidden Beach** [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 027 / locations]: Desolate shore on the Darklake; entry point to the Lost Tomb of Khaem.
*   **Lost Tomb of Khaem** [ch 028 / 028.04]: Ancient Netherese tomb built for sorcerer Brysis of Khaem; contents are oddly well-preserved after thousands of years.
*   **Lower Chamber of the Tomb** [ch 028 / 028.06]: Hidden vault accessible via hole under a rolling sarcophagus; contains a gilded sarcophagus, invisible stone chest, and rich murals.

### Blingdenstone (Deep Gnome City)
*   **Blingdenstone** [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 035 / 035.02; ch 036 / locations; ch 045 / 045.02; ch 048 / 048.06]: An ancestral home of deep gnomes. The number eight is taboo due to its association with spiders and Lolth. It is a settlement in disarray due to an "oozapalooza" or aggressive ooze infestation, featuring gates of steel, mithral, and adamantine. It is now stabilized politically after the party brokered peace; considered "open for business" but not for tourism.
*   **The Maze** [ch 035 / locations]: A defensive network within Blingdenstone consisting of twisting tunnels and deadly traps designed to halt invading armies.
*   **Diggermattock Hall** [ch 037 / 037.01; ch 043 / 043.04; ch 043 / locations]: Central meeting place in Blingdenstone where leadership debates policy. Now crowded with ~60 people.
*   **Traders' Grotto** [ch 037 / 037.05; ch 045 / 045.03; ch 048 / 048.05]: Blingdenstone's central market, a moist cavern where fungi were petrified by a medusa's spell centuries ago. The ooze breach was pacified here, with oozes returning to mindless states and dispersing into the stonework. Starlace Curios and Arcana is set up here.
*   **Catacombs** [ch 037 / 037.03]: Burial chambers beneath the Temple of the Ruby in the Rough containing sealed tombs and a large skull decoration.
*   **Goldwhisker Warrens** [ch 038 / 038.02; ch 038 / locations; ch 039 / 039.03; ch 039 / locations]: Contested territory north of the Traders' Grotto occupied by wererat gnomes who claim squatter's rights following the drow abandonment. Home of Clan Goldwhisker, accessed via massive 50-foot locked doors; contains a moss-covered court for Chief Chipgrin.
*   **Residential Cave** [ch 038 / 038.03; ch 038 / locations]: An abandoned neighborhood with homes dug into walls, now a refuse pile containing evidence of ooze predation on gnomes.
*   **Pudding King's Throne Room** [ch 038 / 038.04; ch 038 / locations; ch 047 / 047.01; ch 047 / 047.09]: A cave illuminated by phosphorescent lichen, covered in green slime pools and drapes, with a slime-covered ceiling. The Pudding King's lair. Green slime hazards on the ceiling now fall only on those directly beneath them after his death.
*   **Foaming Mug Tavern** [ch 040 / 040.02; ch 040 / locations]: A large inn in Blingdenstone built for non-gnome visitors, serving Darklake Stout from Mantol-Derith.
*   **Cultivation Cave** [ch 040 / 040.03; ch 040 / locations]: A complex managed by the Stoneheart Enclave that grows useful fungi and connects to the Rockblight via a blocked path.
*   **Rockblight** [ch 039 / 039.08; ch 039 / locations; ch 040 / 040.04; ch 040 / 040.06; ch 040 / locations]: Corrupted district surrounding the Temple of the Steadfast Stone, influenced by Ogrémoch's Bane. Dark, damp caverns mirroring Inner Blingdenstone, featuring gaze-protecting crystals and affected by a force that drives earth elementals mad. Must be cleansed to protect Earth Elementals.
*   **Temple of the Steadfast Stone** [ch 039 / 039.08; ch 039 / locations; ch 042 / 042.02]: Proposed staging ground for the attack, currently under malign influence. Requires placement of a Hallow gem in its menhir to cleanse. A temple chamber with a menhir of stalagmite featuring gem sockets; the walls outline large humanoid shapes.
*   **Dark Chamber with Earth Elemental** [ch 041 / locations]: A dark area with an upper level 30 feet above the floor, containing magical darkness where the elemental fought.
*   **Passageway** [ch 041 / locations]: A barren, narrow path illuminated by Dawnbringer that leads to a junction with east and west caves.
*   **Drow Statue Chamber** [ch 041 / locations]: A room filled with statues of Drow warriors; six animate when entered, and the statues stop moving if the party leaves.
*   **Medusa's Chamber** [ch 041 / locations; ch 041 / 041.07]: A chamber decorated with Drow city-style items, including spider-themed tapestries, zurkhwood furniture, and expensive clothing, where a bed was ignited for light.
*   **Neheedra's Lair** [ch 042 / 042.01]: The Medusa's lair contained a rack of five dresses made of drow spider silk worth 200 gold pieces each.
*   **Pickshine Mine** [ch 044 / 044.04; ch 044 / locations]: A gem mine co-owned by Dasco Pickshine, recently unblocked by the party solving a Galeb Duhr puzzle; the air smells of ozone and crushed quartz.
*   **Whiteshell Mine** [ch 044 / 044.05; ch 044 / locations]: Blingdenstone's largest salt mine, located south of Pickshine Mine; it produces salt for Blingdenstone and Mantol-Derith.
*   **Summoning Circle Cavern** [ch 044 / 044.07; ch 044 / locations]: A cavern in Whiteshell Mine with runes in Giant script; it guards Entémoch and requires specific sacrifices to open.
*   **Entémoch's Boon** [ch 045 / 045.01]: Ritual site where the boon was released, granting Stone Skin and Earth Elemental summoning rights.
*   **The Gilded Sheath** [ch 045 / 045.04]: Valimor Brightgem's workshop, famous for scabbards; smells of metal, gem dust, and oil.
*   **Area 22 (Steadfast Stone)** [ch 046 / locations]: Staging area for allied forces, located near the Traders' Grotto and accessible through rubble that also allows ooze access.
*   **House of Horrors Corridor** [ch 046 / 046.06]: A tunnel with polished salt walls that have become psychic slime mirrors, guarded by a brain slime.
*   **Black Pudding Pit Passageway** [ch 046 / locations]: A passage with a massive black pudding pit bridged by mithral braces; it blocks ooze movement if maintained.

### The Fungal Altar (Drow Camp)
*   **The Fungal Altar** [ch 049 / 049.04; ch 050 / locations; ch 051 / locations]: A drow camp/cavern in the Underdark dominated by a massive Heart Fungus creature. Features elevated platforms/walkways, shallow pools, bridges, and randomly erupting vents that cause blindness or poison. Ilvara commanded the altar, which was later destroyed and littered with spores and rubble.

The wards were broken by Daz’s *fireball*, causing alarm among the Avowed who began recording the failure. The inner sanctum door was later shattered by Manshoon's simulacrum using a wall of force.
