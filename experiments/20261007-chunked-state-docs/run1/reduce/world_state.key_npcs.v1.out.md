## Key NPCs

### Party & Allies
- **Sarith** — A drow prisoner and former party navigator. He is dead, killed at the Garden of Welcome [ch 004 / npcs; ch 031 / 031.04].
- **Shuushar** — A pacifist kuo-toa. He departed with kuo-toa refugees toward Darklake, heading deeper into the Underdark [ch 004 / npcs; ch 009 / 009.06].
- **Topsy and Turvy** — Deep gnome twins and wererats. They are currently alive in Darklake and friendly, having departed with kuo-toa refugees [ch 004 / 004.01; ch 009 / npcs].
- **Spiderbait** — A goblin guide who specializes in "surfing" spider webs. He is currently traveling with the party in Candlekeep, described as friendly and dependent [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 054 / 054.01].
- **Gartokkar Xundorn** — Head of the Keepers of the Flame in Gracklstugh. He hired the party to retrieve a dragon egg and evidence against the Gray Ghosts. He is alive, residing in the Darklake District, and maintains a professional, discreet, and distrustful/mercenary disposition [ch 014 / npcs; ch 017 / npcs; ch 026 / 026.06].
- **Daz** — A party member currently alive at a hidden beach, traveling with the party [ch 027 / 027.03].
- **Nym and Kaelira Duskryn** — Drow mercenaries who were paid to protect Daz. They are alive on the surface (Overbright), professional and sardonic, having completed their contract escorting Daz [ch 053 / 053.01; ch 053 / npcs].
- **Gyrgum** — A cleric of Bahamut. He is currently in Candlekeep, described as being in control and withholding healing from Daral [ch 043 / 043.01; ch 061 / 061.01].
- **Thorin** — A party member in Candlekeep, described as confident in his deductive work [ch 061 / 061.02].
- **Zalthir** — A party member currently alive and unconscious in the High Tower Lobby at Candlekeep. He was defeated and is being protected by Glabbagool [ch 064 / 064.04].
- **Dawnbringer** — A party companion currently with Thorin. She is a reluctant participant in item purification and insists she dislikes rather than fears darkness [ch 053 / 053.05; ch 053 / npcs].
- **Glabbagool** — A sentient gelatinous cube who insists he is a distinct species, not an ooze. He is currently alive in the lower chamber of the Obsidian Tower at Candlekeep, acting as an ally [ch 035 / 035.04; ch 068 / 068.01].
- **Jimjar** — A deep gnome gambler with celestial or deity-like properties (immune to poison/psychic damage, resistant to non-magical weapons). He identified Juiblex as the source of ooze corruption. His current status is "Unknown" (last seen in Blingdenstone Tunnels), but he is friendly and enigmatic [ch 010 / 010.03; ch 035 / 035.04; ch 048 / 048.07].
- **Jadger's Trainee** — A young gnome mentored by Jadger who will soon lead the new Burrow Wardens. He can be consulted for insight on the oozes [ch 037 / 037.04].

### Antagonists & Hostile Entities
- **Themberchaud** — An overweight, vain red dragon and Wyrmsmith of Gracklstugh. He serves as a "glorified lighter" for the Keepers but is beginning to realize he is being manipulated and is being groomed for replacement. He currently believes the party are his agents [ch 013 / npcs; ch 018 / 023; ch 027 / npcs].
- **Horgar Steelshadow** — The Deepking of Gracklstugh. He is alive but rumored to be suffering from paranoid madness caused by his advisor, Shal [ch 013 / npcs; ch 015 / 015.02].
- **Plinki** — A derro savant dedicated to Demogorgon who used an obelisk to corrupt a dragon egg. Plinki is listed as "Dead" (mentioned only) [ch 017 / npcs; ch 022 / npcs].
- **Narrak** — A derro savant and Council member. He is dead, killed by Thorin after casting Fear and Silence [ch 023 / 023.01; ch 024 / 024.03].
- **Grula-Munga** — A massive female ettin who served as cult muscle. She is dead, killed by Zalthir’s tentacles [ch 023 / 023.01; ch 024 / 024.03].
- **Peleck** — A ghost of a deep gnome murdered by Buppido. He is departed/spirit passed on at the Blingdenstone Catacombs [ch 016 / npcs; ch 037 / 037.03].
- **Zuggtmoy** — A demon lord confirmed to be the entity Thorin previously encountered. She is alive (hostile) and was preparing to wed Araumycos to spread her spores [ch 031 / 031.06; ch 047 / 047.10].
- **Araumycos** — A sentient fungal growth intended as Zuggtmoy’s groom [ch 032 / 032.01; ch 032 / npcs].
- **Juiblex** — The demon lord of oozes, identified as responsible for the corruption of the Abyss and ooze aggression. He is hostile and heard via voice [ch 035 / 035.04; ch 047 / 047.10].
- **The Pudding King** — An insane deep gnome formerly acting as a proxy for Juiblex. He is dead, dissolved into puddles in the Throne Room [ch 038 / 038.04; ch 047 / 047.08].
- **Senni Diggermattock** — A former Pudding King proxy who seized control of a leadership meeting. He is alive, pragmatic, and imposing the ooze quest on the party [ch 045 / 045.02; ch 036 / 036.04].
- **Ilvara** — A drow priestess formerly corrupted by Zuggtmoy (believing it was Lolth). She is dead at the Fungal Altar; evidence confirmed she was following House Mizzrym orders [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 053 / 053.03].
- **Asha Vandree** — A paranoid drow priestess loyal to Lolth who avoided fungal infection via strict hygiene. She is dead, killed by Zalthir after attempting to command the party [ch 050 / 050.02; ch 053 / 053.02].
- **Valen** — A drow scout of House T'sarran who was infected but guided the party to Ilvara. He is alive at the Fungal Altar Camp, grateful and cooperating as a guide [ch 049 / 049.03].
- **Jorlan** — A bitter drow warrior and Ilvara's ex-lover. He is alive at the Fungal Altar, wounded/sapped, and hostile/independent [ch 050 / npcs; ch 051 / npcs].
- **Kalan Strongbranch** — Former Gatewarden and author of the *Threefold Proof*. He was reinstated and is currently in charge of the guard, described as supportive/disappointed [ch 061 / 061.02; ch 062 / 062.02; ch 067 / 067.03].
- **Sylvira Savikas** — A tiefling researcher with an Abyssal Plague who was framed using magic. She is alive, ill, cooperative, and provided the true name of Miirym (Vydykyq). She is unaware the party killed her son [ch 061 / 061.05; ch 067 / 067.03].
- **Moziqodo** — Sylvira’s abyss-born son, directed to steal keys. He is dead, slain by the party [ch 063 / 063.02; ch 063 / npcs].
- **Manshoon** — The hidden mastermind behind 11 years of book thefts. There are three known instances: "Original" (alive/hostile), "Simulacrum #2" (alive/hostile in Tower Chamber), and "Simulacrum" (dead, lava pit). The original and second simulacrum remain active threats [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 069 / 069.05; ch 070 / 070.02].
- **Zubra** — Referenced as "Zubiska" in the table, alive near a secret passage at Candlekeep [ch 017 / npcs].

### Neutral or Situational Figures
- **Hemeth** — A Duergar arms dealer traveling with the party. He claims connections in Gracklstugh ("I know people") and advised quiet entry. He is alive in the Blade Bazaar, grateful but offended by competition [ch 008 / 008.01; ch 009 / 009.03; ch 011 / 011.07; ch 012 / 012.02].
- **Hgraam** — Stone Giant clan leader and Stonespeaker of Clan Cairngorm. He can potentially reverse a two-headed curse and sensed evil near the cult's defeat. He is currently alive, grateful/cautious, and gifted the party a crystal [ch 015 / 015.02; ch 023 / 023.01; ch 026 / 026.04].
- **Basidia** — Myconid Sovereign of Neverlight Grove. She requested a Grick hunt and holds the party to a treasure promise. She is alive, grateful/apologetic, and cooperative with the party (distrustful of Phylo) [ch 030 / 030.05; ch 032 / 032.03].
- **Gasbide** — Leader of the Circle of Builders, obsessive about architecture. He is alive, curious/enthusiastic, and unaware of the true nature of Araumycos [ch 030 / 030.05].
- **Sovereign Phylo** — Co-sovereign of Neverlight Grove who foretold a "great awakening." He is alive, delusional/hostile, and corrupted [ch 030 / 030.05; ch 032 / 032.03].
- **Chief Dorbo Diggermattock** — Clan leader of the Diggermattocks in Blingdenstone. He is alive, cooperative but politically calculating, and relieved the party arrived [ch 045 / 045.02; ch 046 / 046.03].
- **Dasco Pickshine** — Overseer of Pickshine Mines. He is alive, a reluctant ally, and now excited about Earth Elementals [ch 045 / 045.02; ch 048 / 048.06].
- **Starlace** — Traveling magic merchant in Blingdenstone offering enchantments. He is alive, friendly/neutral [ch 045 / 045.03].
- **Valimor Brightgem** — Renowned blacksmith in Blingdenstone inspired by Thorin's marketing ideas. He is alive, friendly/inspired [ch 045 / 045.04].
- **Galeb Duhr** — Earth elementals governing the temple. They are alive, amused by dwarf jokes, and respectful; they recognize Gyrgum as a cleric of Bahamut [ch 043 / 043.01; ch 043 / npcs].
- **Entémoch** — The "stone prince" or Prince of Good Elementals. He is alive (Planar), favorable toward the party for aiding his subjects, though the path to his boon was sealed [ch 043 / 043.04; ch 048 / 048.01].
- **Vareth (Brother Vareth)** — An elderly half-elf scholar whose research on orc Bahamut converts is academically relevant. He is alive, excited/academic, and leading debates [ch 054 / npcs; ch 060 / 060.01].
- **Bookwyrm** — Identified as Green dragonborn Skoda Vanaster, acting head of Candlekeep. She is dead, having opposed Kalan and advised burying his paper [ch 061 / 061.07; ch 062 / npcs; ch 063 / 063.01].
- **Daral Yashenti** — A rival scholar suspected of theft and visiting the victim. He is alive in Candlekeep, anxious but supportive, relying on the party for safety [ch 058 / 058.05; ch 063 / 063.01].
- **Hollypocket** — An elderly gnome housekeeper at Candlekeep. She is alive, a helpful witness, and currently in her apartment [ch 058 / 058.05].
- **Queenie** — The tower cat at Candlekeep who provided testimony via *Speak with Animals*. She is alive and dismissive [ch 058 / 058.06].
- **Edvaldo Sedanur** — A doppelganger currently alive in the lower chamber of the Obsidian Tower, cooperative with the party (unaware he is a doppelganger) [ch 068 / 068.01; ch 068 / npcs].
- **Miirym, the Sentinel Wyrm** — A spectral silver dragon guardian of Candlekeep. She is alive in the lava chamber, neutral/supportive (witness only), and her true name is Vydykyq [ch 068 / npcs].
- **Alaundo** — A legendary seer whose recorded voice guards the Obsidian Door with a riddle [ch 069 / 069.05].
- **Brannum Redmarch** — A low-Charisma duergar who led crews to doom. He is alive and willing to assist the party for freedom [ch 011 / npcs; ch 012 / 012.02].
- **Fargas Rumblefoot** — A halfling adventurer rescued from a cocoon, referenced as an unreliable witness regarding a lost tomb [ch 006 / 006.07; ch 028 / npcs].
- **Brysis of Khaem** — A Netherese sorcerer depicted in a diorama. She is dead/deceased [ch 028 / 028.04; ch 029 / 029.01].
