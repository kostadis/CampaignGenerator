## Items and Artifacts

**Sentient & Magical Weapons**
*   **Dawnbringer** — A legendary sentient sunblade held by Thorin. It emits sunlight, deals radiant damage, and can cast *lesser restoration* [ch 029 / 029.01; ch 031 / items; ch 049 / 049.03]. It fears darkness and abandonment, becoming agitated or attempting to control its wielder in the dark [ch 029 / 029.07; ch 035 / items; ch 056 / 056.03]. Thorin purchased a **Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard** for 180 gold to secure it [ch 045 / 045.04].
*   **Mace +1** — Owned by Gyrgum; enchanted by Starlace for 360 gold [ch 045 / 045.03].
*   **Shield +1** — Owned by Thorin; enchanted by Starlace for 270 gold [ch 045 / 045.03].
*   **Javelin of Lightning** — A magical weapon available for purchase in Waterdeep; Gyrgum expressed interest [ch 055 / 055.09].
*   **Javelins** — Non-magical thrown weapons Thorin plans to acquire to utilize his Sharpshooter feat for high damage against cover [ch 052 / items].

**Spell Gems & Scrolls**
*   **Empty Spell Gems** — Five gems identified by Jimjar as empty, highly valuable to gnomish alchemists and used in Blingdenstone’s defenses [ch 018 / 018.06].
*   **Empty Fourth-Level Spell Gem** — Currently held by Gyrgum; can be attuned to and loaded via ritual [ch 043 / 043.02].
*   **Red Spell Gem** — Contains the *Hallow* spell; must be placed in the Steadfast Stone's menhir and defended to awaken three temple guardians [ch 039 / 039.08].
*   **Ruby Spell Gem** — Provided by Gurnik Tapfinger to hallow the temple; placing it in the menhir triggers the emergence of elementals [ch 042 / 042.02].
*   **Scroll of Protection against Fiends** — Given to the party by Basidia; lasts five minutes [ch 029 / 029.06].
*   **Scroll of Remove Curse** — Capable of removing curses from items or areas [ch 041 / items].
*   **Spell Scrolls** — Four scrolls found in a sunken chest: *Poison Spray*, *Burning Hands*, *Mirror Image*, and *Inflict Wounds* [ch 011 / items].

**Key Artifacts & High Tower Keys**
*   **High Tower Keys** — Two genuine magical keys are required simultaneously to open the High Tower.
    *   **Key #1 (Held by Tadric)** — One of the two real keys [ch 063 / items].
    *   **Key #2 (Held by Gyrgum)** — The second real key, secretly transferred by Kalan Strongbranch to protect it [ch 059 / items]. It is bound to a **Stolen Sapphire**; smashing the sapphire would recall *this* key [ch 065 / items].
    *   **Status Update:** As of ch 067, a High Tower Key is stored inside Glabbagool for safekeeping [ch 067 / items].
    *   **Fake High Tower Key** — A mundane decoy given to the party by Kalan to draw attention away from the real artifacts [ch 063 / items].
    *   **The Inert Key** — A mysterious key held by Gyrgum that reads as non-magical under *Detect Magic* but attracts helmed horrors [ch 062 / items].
    *   **Watcher's Stair Key** — A physical key granting shortcut access to the North Gallery [ch 063 / items].
*   **Sapphire Artifact** — A magical gem held by Gyrgum; smashing it triggers an effect related to the High Tower keys [ch 064 / items].
*   **Statue of Alaundo** — A brass statue in the House of Alaundo that reveals a hidden staircase when ink is poured into its empty ink pot [ch 067 / 067.04].

**Dragon Eggs & Demonic Artifacts**
*   **Red Dragon Egg / Corrupted Dragon Egg** — Stolen from the Keepers of the Flame. Originally intended by the Gray Ghosts to hatch Themberchaud's successor [ch 014 / items]. It was later corrupted by Plinki using a **Black Metal Obelisk** to channel demonic energy, destined to hatch a two-headed dragon possessed by Demogorgon [ch 017 / items; ch 019 / items]. The corruption prevents hatching for 44 weeks and is not removed by *lesser restoration* [ch 021 / items]. The Derro believe it is the egg of Demogorgon [ch 020 / items]. It was intended for Gartokkar [ch 025 / items].
*   **Dragon Baby Rattle** — Heavy, inscribed with Clan Thrazgad; usable as an improvised weapon (1d4) [ch 021 / items].
*   **Black Metal Obelisk** — 50-foot structure used by Plinki to channel demonic energy into the egg [ch 019 / items].
