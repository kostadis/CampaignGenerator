## Factions and Powers

### Drow and Elven Powers
*   **Ilvara's Pursuers**: Led by Ilvara Mizzrym, this group includes guards Jorlan Duskryn and Shoor Vandree. They rely on intimidation and assets like spider-silk spools [ch 002 / npcs; ch 002 / 002.06]. Ilvara's pursuit is active and potentially divertible [ch 032 / 032.04]. House Mizzrym issued frantic orders to Ilvara as she stopped responding due to Zuggtmoy's influence [ch 053 / 053.03; ch 053 / items].
*   **House T'sarran**: A drow house ruled for 200 years by a Matron Mother. They dispatched an assassination squad [ch 051 / 051.06] and funded a signet-ring-wielding mage to extract Daz alive [ch 053 / 053.04; ch 053 / items]. They are currently surveilling the party to find Daz's protector [ch 054 / 054.04]. A spy from this house is captured and held by the party [ch 056 / 056.04].
*   **House Baenre**: A drow house identified by A'lai as the only entity in Menzoberranzan capable of sustaining an eleven-year operation without demanding immediate results [ch 067 / 067.02].

### Underdark Powers and Beings
*   **Demogorgon**: The Prince of Demons whose avatar manifested in the Underdark, causing madness and destruction [ch 009 / npcs].
*   **Zuggtmoy**: The "Lady of Rot" and Demon Queen of Fungi. She is actively corrupting mines via the Festering Fissure [ch 043 / 043.05] and is moving to claim the Pudding King's former territory as Juiblex's influence recedes [ch 047 / 047.10]. She is influencing Ilvara's behavior [ch 053 / 053.03].
*   **Juiblex**: The Demon Lord of Oozes, associated with the concentration of oozes found in the temple [ch 034 / 034.05]. His influence is currently receding [ch 047 / 047.10].
*   **Ogrémoch**: A being whose banishment/hallowing from a temple was successfully completed by the party [ch 043 / 043.01].
*   **Faerzress**: An ancient magical energy in the Underdark that disrupts teleportation and causes distress to creatures like Shuushar [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 005 / 005.06].
*   **Mad Gnolls**: Described by Fargas as demon-possessed entities that implant spawn in living hosts [ch 006 / 006.07].

### Duergar of Gracklstugh
*   **Deepking's Clan**: The Deepking granted all weapons contracts to Clan Steelshadow, breeding resentment and causing competitors in other clans to disappear [ch 018 / 018.02; ch 018 / 018.04].
*   **Stone Guard**: Led by Captain Errde Blackskull. This is the King's police and a militia faction investigating corruption in the Council of Savants and clan lairds [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 014 / npcs; ch 021 / npcs]. They seek proof of corruption and would kill derro if informed of demonic dealings [ch 021 / 021.02]. Errde publicly refuses to admit the Assassin's Guild exists to protect her authority, but secretly seeks evidence of their attacks on her clan [ch 018 / 018.01]. Patrols are doubling due to rumors of a drow raid and internal unrest [ch 026 / 026.04; ch 026 / 026.06]. Guards can turn invisible and enforce laws with immediate lethal force [ch 012 / 012.01].
*   **Keepers of the Flame**: A clerical/psionic order headed by Gartokkar. They tend the red dragon Themberchaud to light the city's forges and claim to be the Deepking's most prized advisors [ch 013 / 013.04; ch 014 / npcs; ch 017 / 017.05]. They are close to the king, want the dragon egg returned to hatch against Themberchaud, and are vengeful [ch 021 / npcs; ch 021 / 021.02]. They seek to destroy the Gray Ghosts using evidence of demon worship [ch 017 / 017.05].
*   **Gray Ghosts**: A thieves' guild in conflict with the Keepers of the Flame and the Crown [ch 013 / 013.05]. They are credited with stealing the dragon egg [ch 014 / npcs] and are working with the derro Plinki to create a mount for Demogorgon [ch 017 / 017.04]. They were sent for revenge against the Ember Vanguard after the derro High Council members were assassinated [ch 026 / 026.03; ch 026 / 026.07].
*   **Council of Savants**: Identified as corrupt and entangled with demonic powers; their local cell has been dismantled [ch 021 / 021.01]. The derro governing body; two members were killed by the party, escalating political tensions [ch 024 / 024.04].
*   **Clan Ironhead**: A weaponsmithing clan favorable to gnomes, which would benefit from the Deepking's removal [ch 021 / 021.02].
*   **Clan Thrazgad**: A steelsmithing clan linked to the Dragon Baby Rattle [ch 021 / npcs; ch 021 / 021.03].
*   **Assassin's Guild**: An organization hiding in the tunnels near the party's path. Confirmed by the flumph despite Errde's public denial [ch 017 / 017.04].
*   **Empty-Scabbard Killers**: Duergar assassins who use psionic blades (empty scabbards); led by Eldgrim; currently banned the party from their lair [ch 019 / 019.01; ch 019 / items].

### Kuo-toa
*   **Kuo-toa Civil War**: The settlement of Sloobludop is divided between the Sea Mother faction (led by Ploopploopeen) and the Deep Father faction (led by Bloppblippodd) [ch 008 / 008.01; ch 008 / 008.02]. These rival cults' conflict erupted into open violence at the altar [ch 008 / 008.02].

### Myconids of Neverlight Grove
*   **Sovereign Phylo**: Has instituted segregation and created a Circle of Masters, centralizing leadership to further Zuggtmoy's plans [ch 029 / 029.05; ch 029 / 029.06; ch 031 / 031.02].
*   **Sovereign Basidia**: Opposes Phylo's changes [ch 029 / 029.06].
*   **Myconid Circles**: Divided into specialized circles (Growers, Explorers, etc.) undergoing reorganization under Phylo [ch 031 / 031.02].

### Gnomes of Blingdenstone
*   **Clan Diggermattock**: The ruling clan of the section the party visited. They seized the territory from the drow and view them as critical enemies [ch 036 / 036.01]. A guild of miners who value traditional construction methods and have accepted Thorin as an honorary member [ch 048 / 048.03].
*   **Clan Goldwhisker**: Lycanthropic deep gnomes (wererats) resistant to non-silver weapons [ch 038 / 038.06]. They value ecological niche arguments, refuse subordination to the Diggermattocks [ch 039 / 039.04; ch 039 / 039.05], and were granted control of Blingdenstone's upper boroughs as a self-funded defense force [ch 048 / 048.06].
*   **Burrow Wardens**: An order of defenders, most of whom died during the Drow invasion. Currently being secretly re-established by the ghost Jadger [ch 037 / 037.04].
*   **Mining Coalition**: Led by Chief Dorbo, focused on mining expansion and currently excluding the Goldwhisker Clan [ch 037 / 037.01].
*   **Chamber of Commerce**: A merchant organization that granted the party a discount coupon for aid against oozes [ch 038 / 038.02].
*   **Pickshine Miners**: A mining operation overseen by Dasco Pickshine, focused on branding and profit, which provided bridge construction [ch 046 / 046.02].
*   **Stoneheart Enclave**: A faction managing fungi cultivation; claimed to be solving Ogrémoch’s Bane by sending the party to the Rockblight [ch 040 / 040.02; ch 040 / 040.03].

### Candlekeep and North
*   **Candlekeep**: A library currently in a state of emergency with doubled guards at the High Tower following Janussi’s murder [ch 059 / locations; ch 059 / 059.03]. The organization prioritizes academic debate over active murder investigation; Avowed and scholars deserted duties for Vareth’s disputation [ch 060 / 060.01]. Janussi’s poor management of annual reviews created widespread resentment among powerful spellcasters like Alkrist, Bookwyrm, and A'lai [ch 061 / 061.07; ch 061 / 061.08].
*   **Watchers of Candlekeep**: The party was formally deputized as Watchers, receiving a badge and a symbolic honorarium [ch 063 / 063.03].
*   **Avowed**: Staff of Candlekeep. One hooded member was seen hurrying with hidden items at dinner [ch 057 / 057.02].
*   **Zhentarim**: Operatives (raiders) are present in Candlekeep looting books according to a specific manifest [ch 064 / 064.03; ch 064 / 064.04]. Their leadership is linked to Manshoon, whose courier features a metal hand [ch 065 / npcs].
*   **Simulacra of Manshoon**: Manshoon utilizes ice/snow constructs that mimic his abilities but have half vitality and revert to snow upon death; multiple exist in the tower [ch 069 / 069.02; ch 069 / 069.04].
*   **The Stroudite School**: A philosophical/religious faction promoting the "human agency hypothesis," arguing mortal will, not gods or dragons, decided historical outcomes [ch 055 / 055.03; ch 055 / items]. Associated with the phrase "The move is yours" and viewed as heretical by some Bahamutians [ch 056 / 056.01].
*   **The Uthgardt**: Barbarians who launched crusades against civilized lands after their religious artifacts were stolen during the War of the Giants [ch 055 / 055.02].
*   **Order of the Gauntlet**: An organization receiving donations via the sale of Travis Houlin medallions; Milo Goodbarrel is a member who recorded the Daggerford monument [ch 056 / 056.01].

### Other Derro and Factions
*   **Derro**: Mad underdark dwellers who were once slaves to Mind Flayers. They believe whoever holds the egg rules them [ch 020 / npcs].
*   **Cult of Demogorgon**: A derro faction in Gracklstugh devoted to Demogorgon, led by figures like Narrak and Plinki. They seek to elevate derro and destroy duergar [ch 022 / 022.02; ch 022 / 022.06].
