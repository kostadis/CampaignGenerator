## Events
- Drow overseers divide the prisoners into labor groups and assign them menial tasks around Velkynvelve. `[ch 002 / 002.01]`
- Zalthir is forced to operate and clean the lift mechanism, enduring quaggoth guards who drop and jerk the basket to intimidate him. `[ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / 002.02]`
- Thorin, Ront, and Prince Derendil are assigned to haul water barrels from the waterfall to the outpost. Thorin notes the waterfall’s potential as an escape route. `[ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / 002.03]`
- Gyrgum is assigned to prepare meals in the drow dining hall; he uses performance to impress the drow with his cooking, earning grudging praise. `[ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / 002.02; ch 002 / 002.03]`
- Daz, Stool, and another drow prisoner are assigned to clean chamber pots near the lower pool and underground river, giving Daz insight into the lower levels. `[ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / 002.03]`
- Zalthir discovers a mysterious list of names from a nearby settlement in the lift basket’s detritus. `[ch 002 / 002.02]`
- Eldeth finds a comb in the filth of her labor, a rare practical item. `[ch 002 / 002.02]`
- Jimjar begins taking bets on lift activity among the prisoners, offering credit despite their lack of currency, and demonstrates an uncanny memory for debts. `[ch 002 / 002.02]`
- Gyrgum attempts to steal a kitchen knife but is caught; he improvises by claiming he intends to sharpen it, and the drow spare him due to his meal’s impact. `[ch 002 / 002.02; ch 002 / Memorable Moments]`
- Topsy and Turvy successfully steal two kitchen knives during Gyrgum’s second shift, after he delegates the theft to them. `[ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / Memorable Moments]`
- Gyrgum sharpens the stolen knives; the group jokes they are “magical plus zero” weapons. `[ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / Memorable Moments]`
- Thorin and Prince Derendil are ordered to deliver water to Ilvara’s private quarters; Derendil demands dignity and is threatened with a knife. `[ch 002 / 002.03]`
- Thorin observes Ilvara’s shrine to Lolth, including the ten-foot spider idol with jet eyes and silver candlesticks, and notices a treasure chest and a passage to Shoor’s quarters. `[ch 002 / 002.03]`
- Thorin senses the drow at Velkynvelve are “crazily off,” bordering on insanity. `[ch 002 / 002.03]`
- Daz observes the layout of the lower cavern and underground river while cleaning chamber pots. `[ch 002 / 002.03]`
- Gyrgum’s cooking becomes legendary in the slave pen; Shuushar claims it could inspire kuo-toa worship of a god resembling Gyrgum. `[ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / Memorable Moments]`
- Buppido declares his “divine plan” requires everyone to distract the drow while he escapes through the waterfall. `[ch 002 / 002.04]`
- After being flattered, Buppido revises his plan to include the party as his protectors and pledges their swords, spells, and lives to his cause. `[ch 002 / 002.04]`
- Jorlan Duskryn, a disfigured former lieutenant of Ilvara, approaches the prisoners during guard duty and offers to help them escape to sabotage Ilvara and Shoor. `[ch 002 / 002.05; ch 002 / Memorable Moments]`
- Jorlan reveals the armory is above the guard post, and suggests escaping via the webs into the pool below, stating their survival is “not my problem.” `[ch 002 / 002.05]`
- The prisoners debate escape routes and destinations: Shuushar knows the way to Darklake; Gracklstugh is a possible southern route; Menzoberranzan is rejected. `[ch 002 / 002.05]`
- The party concludes their confiscated gear is likely in Ilvara’s treasure chest, based on drow customs and the absence of their items among guards. `[ch 002 / 002.05]`
- Zalthir proposes using his fire breath to burn spider-silk spools and the lift’s rope; Daz suggests freezing the lift mechanism with Shape Water. `[ch 002 / 002.04; ch 002 / 002.06]`
- The group plans to scout and disable spider-silk spools near the escape route to deny drow pursuit. `[ch 002 / 002.06]`
