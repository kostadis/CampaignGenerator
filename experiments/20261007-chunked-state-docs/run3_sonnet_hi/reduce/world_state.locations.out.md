## Locations

### Velkynvelve (drow outpost, escaped)
- A drow outpost suspended in an Underdark cavern, with hanging towers, rope bridges, spider-silk lines and a waterfall feeding a 20-foot-deep pool. Its vertical layout enables sabotage and escape routes. [ch 002 / locations]
- Known features:
  - The Lift is a winch-and-basket run by a hand-cranked zurkhwood spool and spider-silk cord, supervised by quaggoths. [ch 002 / locations; ch 002 / items]
  - The dining hall has four zurkhwood tables with five chairs each. [ch 002 / locations]
  - Ilvara's Quarters are reached by rope ladder from a Lolth shrine with a 10-foot spider idol. [ch 002 / locations; ch 002 / items]
  - The slave pen is where prisoners were held and where escape planning took place. [ch 002 / locations]
- The slave pen and guard tower were breached, two drow guards and the Elite Warrior Imbros died, and the armory was looted. [ch 003 / locations]
- The armory held spare short swords, six hand crossbows, studded leather armor, 100 feet of rope and six bags of caltrops. [ch 003 / locations; ch 003 / items]
- The garrison abandoned the outpost during the vrock attack, and the party escaped. [ch 004 / locations; ch 004 / 004.01]
- Blingdenstone has a standing order to escort all Velkynvelve escapees to Chief Dorbo for intelligence gathering. [ch 036 / 036.01]
