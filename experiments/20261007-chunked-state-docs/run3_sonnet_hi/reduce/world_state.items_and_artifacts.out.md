## Items and Artifacts

### Dawnbringer (held by Thorin)
- Sentient sunblade bonded to Thorin. It is a +2 weapon dealing radiant damage, +1d8 against undead, with Lesser Restoration once per day, and it must be ignited with a bonus action [ch 029 / items; ch 031 / items].
- Its light is genuine sunlight. It was recorded as a 15-foot radius in ch 031 and a 20-foot radius in ch 047 (the later figure is the current one) [ch 031 / items; ch 047 / items].
- It fears darkness and abandonment, and may try to control Thorin if he enters magical darkness [ch 031 / items]. It has "a hilt but no physical blade", is said to want a scabbard, and can impose control via a Charisma save [ch 041 / 041.01; ch 041 / 041.06]. It was described as "very upset" in the poorly lit Maze [ch 035 / 035.05].
- Its hits can apply a "sap" effect, giving the target disadvantage on its next attack [ch 043 / 043.10; ch 050 / items].
- It is nonmetallic and unaffected by gray ooze corrosion [ch 034 / items]. It supports ooze rights and heals orphans, which makes it “very happy.” [ch 055 / items]
- It is of late Netherese origin and cannot be polished. It "requires therapeutic recovery: remembering daylight, then grieving" [ch 056 / 056.06].
- It is referred to as female [ch 065 / 065.02]. In the Vault it twitched at the *Glyphs of Warding* and refused to be thrown as a javelin [ch 069 / items]. It reacted to the fall with “Not this again!” [ch 068 / items].
- **Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard:** designed to hook Dawnbringer in. Valimor explicitly denies it is the “Vaultmaster 9000”. The notes do not record whether it was bought [ch 045 / items].

### Candlekeep Vault aftermath (ch 067–070)
- **Book of Vile Darkness:** a sentient artifact written by Vecna. Daz has secured it and reads it with Mage Hand. It cannot be permanently destroyed while evil exists [ch 070 / items].
- **Robe of the Archmagi:** Daz possesses it and will attune at his next long rest, potentially replacing an existing attuned item [ch 070 / items].
- **Staff of Power:** Manshoon used it to breach Candlekeep's wards. It is lost with the simulacrum in the lava [ch 070 / items].
- **Prophecy Crystals:** four of the six remain intact and are in Gyrgum's possession. Their prophecies are still active and likely tied to the party [ch 070 / items].
- **Prismatic Gemstones:** the ch 069 note describes ten gemstones in a velvet tray, four of them dark, holding prophecies placed by Alaundo, including one concerning Thorin, Eldeth and the unlit forge [ch 069 / items].
  - The notes use different names and counts (ten gemstones in ch 069, six crystals in ch 070). They do not say how the two relate.
- **Dangerous Tomes:** one hundred hazardous magical tomes are stored in the Vault. Manshoon was reading one, and the vault's note forbids removing them [ch 068 / items].
- **Mechanist Tools:** from the House of Mechanus. They were used to activate the bridge mechanism, and the party lacked the dust of Mechanus [ch 068 / items].
- **Modron Tools:** lent by Spanner as a substitute for Dust of Mechanus. Their use is conditional on a post-expedition study of Zalthir and Glabbagool. The party never obtained the dust itself [ch 067 / 067.05; ch 067 / items].
  - The notes name Mechanist Tools and Modron Tools separately.
- **Vault Cryptogram:** a letter-substitution cipher with seven instructions. A'lai supplied two answers, Sylvira two, and the party solved the rest [ch 067 / 067.03].
- **Empowered Tattoo (Zalthir):** manifests fifteen-foot tendrils of eldritch darkness. He used it to seize Manshoon and pull him into the anti-magic zone [ch 069 / items].

### High Tower keys and the sapphire (ch 059–065)
The notes disagree on who held the real key. Ch 063 puts it with Tadric, but ch 064–065 move it to Gyrgum, and the later chapters govern [ch 063 / 063.05; ch 064 / 064.04; ch 065 / 065.03].
- **Real High Tower Key (Gyrgum's):** it is bound to the stolen sapphire, which recalls it when smashed [ch 065 / 065.03; ch 065 / end].
- **Real High Tower Key (Glabbagool's):** hidden inside Glabbagool. It is the second real key, taken from Moziqodo and stripped from A'lai, and is deliberately kept separate from Gyrgum's key [ch 065 / 065.03; ch 065 / end]. Two keys are required to open the sealed inner door [ch 064 / 064.04].
- **Stolen Sapphire:** held by Gyrgum. Smashing it would recall the key he already holds, not locate the other [ch 065 / 065.03; ch 065 / end]. A'lai had intended to smash it to trigger an unknown effect, and its function remains uncertain [ch 064 / 064.04].
- **Fake High Tower Key:** non-magical decoy from Kalan. It is held by Gyrgum [ch 063 / 063.01; ch 065 / 065.03].
- **The Inert Key:** non-magical under Detect Magic but tracked by helmed horrors. It is held by Gyrgum, and the notes do not say whether it is the fake key [ch 062 / 062.06].
- **Brass Key:** one of two keys for the High Tower, given secretly by Kalan to Gyrgum [ch 059 / 059.03]. The notes do not link it to the entries above.
- **Watcher's Stair Key:** from Kalan, a shortcut to the North Galleries. It was confirmed genuine after Kalan swore “on my honor” [ch 063 / 063.04].
- **Kalan Strongbranch's Key:** *Identify* found no summoning magic on it, which shifted suspicion to the sapphire [ch 060 / items].

### Janussi murder evidence (ch 057–062)
- **Severed Heart:** removed to prevent resurrection or *Speak with Dead*, and now in the party's possession. It was hidden with a Blood-stained Meat Cleaver in the Lead-lined Chalice of Milil, which blocks divination [ch 060 / items]. Alkrist was unaware of its removal [ch 061 / 061.07].
- **The Golden Ass / the poisoned book:** Daral's gift to Janussi, tampered with Midnight Tears. It is in the party's custody as evidence [ch 059 / 059.05; ch 061 / 061.01].
- **Midnight Tears:** a Feywild poison with no effect until midnight. It was stolen from the Oak Tree Apothecary, and the vial's label was tied to the Deadwinter Tree [ch 059 / 059.04; ch 061 / 061.07].
- **1001 Tashalian Nights:** bookmarked at a passage paralleling the murder. It was found in Alkrist's chambers and Zalthir revealed it during interrogation [ch 060 / items; ch 061 / 061.07].
- **Wrapping Paper:** restored with Mending, showing a deliberate second cut that proves premeditated tampering [ch 061 / 061.06].
- **Locket (open):** it was torn from Janussi's neck. Its contents are unknown but believed to be a key to the High Tower Library [ch 058 / items]. It was stolen after death, and its resistance to summoning while its bearer lives complicates the single-thief theory [ch 061 / 061.01].
- **Alkrist's safe:** Zalthir secretly took the Potion of Flying using Glabbagool, and it is now in party possession. The Potion of Superior Healing and the Red Herring Coin stayed in the safe [ch 062 / 062.04].
- **Other scene items:** Written Manifest (a Zhentarim book-theft list) and Foreign Crossbow Bolt [ch 064 / 064.03; ch 064 / 064.02]. Messenger Dove Cage: five doves, two remain [ch 060 / items].

### The dragon egg and Gracklstugh evidence (ch 014–026)
- **The egg:** a red dragon egg stolen from the Keepers of the Flame, covered in demonic markings and infused by the black metal obelisk to hatch a two-headed dragon [ch 014 / 014.07; ch 019 / items]. It would hatch in 44 weeks without intervention [ch 021 / items].
  - Ch 025 has the party holding it as political leverage. Ch 026, the later note, says it was delivered to Gartokkar, viability unknown, and could not be hidden at the checkpoint [ch 025 / 025.01; ch 026 / items].
- **Dragon Baby Rattle:** made by Clan Thrazgad, with no gems, usable as a 1d4 improvised weapon [ch 021 / 021.03].
- **Empty-Scabbard Killers Hit List:** signed by the Deepking, bearing the royal seal of Gracklstugh, and stolen by Daz [ch 019 / items]. It is hidden from all factions and kept secret by the party [ch 021 / 021.02].
- **Incriminating Notes:** link the Council of Savants to demonic dealings and are in the party's possession [ch 021 / 021.01].
- **Letter from the Council of Savants:** orders the Gray Ghosts to assassinate the Duergar who brought in the Ember Vanguard, and proves derro are manipulating factions [ch 026 / items].
- **Narrak's loot:** *The Rituals of the Two-Headed Beast*, Narrak's Key, five doses of Keoghtom's Ointment, and ritual Stone Giant Statues [ch 024 / 024.04]. The party holds one completed statue and may bring it to Hgraam [ch 025 / 025.01].
- **Diplomatic Pouch:** the party claims it holds sensitive materials to avoid inspection. Its legitimacy is unverified [ch 025 / 025.08].

### Pelek and Thorin's pendant
