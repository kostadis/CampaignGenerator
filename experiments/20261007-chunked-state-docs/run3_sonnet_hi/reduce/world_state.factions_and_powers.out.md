## Factions and Powers

### Drow and Underdark powers

**Lolth and the drow**
- Lolth is worshiped at Velkynvelve. Its shrine has a 10-foot idol with jet eyes, and drow hierarchy and ritual are centered on her [ch 002 / locations; ch 002 / items].
- The drow are ruthless slave-owners. They use hierarchy, treasure hoarding by the highest-ranking, and public humiliation as political weapons [ch 002 / 002.05; ch 002 / 002.06].
- The Velkynvelve guard tower was overrun and its commander slain [ch 003 / locations].
- The Velkynvelve drow are hunting the escaped prisoners. Scouts with darkvision and tracking skills went ahead, and a full hunting party follows [ch 005 / 005.04; ch 005 / npcs].
- Lolth's priesthood: drow clerics distrust mages who claim divine communion, and Asha's suspicion reflects this [ch 050 / 050.01].

**House T'sarran**
- Ch 049 calls it a minor drow house loyal to Ilvara. Valen is one of its scouts, and his infection reflects its weakness [ch 049 / 049.02].
- Ch 051: it suffered heavy losses. Its elite warrior retreated with a threat, which suggests future retaliation [ch 051 / 051.04; ch 051 / 051.06].
- Ch 053: it sent a force to extract Daz alive. The signet ring and burned orders confirm this, and the party now knows of its involvement [ch 053 / 053.04].
- Ch 054, the latest note, calls it a powerful house ruled for 200 years by its Matron Mother. It has deployed agents to the surface to track Daz and identify his mysterious benefactor, not to kill him [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / npcs].
- **Conflict:** "minor" in ch 049 against "powerful" in ch 054. The later chapter wins.

**House Mizzrym**
- Ilvara was a loyal agent taking orders from this house until she disappeared. Its frantic messages suggest it lost contact and is now seeking her [ch 053 / 053.03].

**House Duskryn**
- It is actively protecting Daz. Kaelira and Nym showed lethal coordination and loyalty [ch 051 / 051.03; ch 051 / 051.06].

**House Baenre**
- A'lai deduces that this Menzoberranzan house has been funding the eleven-year campaign against Daz, based on its patience and resources. He offers no direct proof [ch 067 / 067.02].

**Zuggtmoy's "bride" heresy**
- Ilvara follows this heretical cult, which turns followers into fungal servants. Asha sees it as a betrayal of Lolth [ch 050 / 050.02].

### Gracklstugh (duergar, derro, and the egg)

**Duergar and Duergar Slavers**
- The duergar have strict laws against foreigners and a deep hatred of drow. Gracklstugh is their city and offers safety from drow pursuit [ch 011 / 011.07].
- Duergar Slavers are gray dwarves who kidnap travelers to sell in Gracklstugh. They use invisibility and enlargement magic and operate on the Darklake [ch 010 / 010.03; ch 010 / NPCs].
- The duergar city guard enforces the law with invisible patrols and lethal efficiency. It responded to the stone giant's rampage [ch 012 / 012.01; ch 012 / 012.05].

**Keepers of the Flame**
- They are a high-ranking duergar order. They tend the red dragon Themberchaud to keep the forges burning, and claim the Deepking treats them as his most prized advisors [ch 013 / 013.04; ch 013 / NPCs].
- They control Themberchaud through psionic means and are secretly at war with the Gray Ghosts over the stolen dragon egg [ch 014 / 014.07].
- They threatened the party with permanent hostility if betrayed, and are frantic over the egg [ch 018 / 018.02].
- They are zealots close to the king. They are obsessed with reclaiming the egg and willing to commit genocide against the derro. They will pursue the party eternally if deceived [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].
- Gartokkar leads them. The party's original plan was to deliver the egg to them, and their current stance toward the party is unknown [ch 025 / 025.01; ch 025 / npcs].
- They commissioned the egg retrieval. Gartokkar is a high-ranking member, and they fear Themberchaud's awakening [ch 026 / 026.06].

**Stone Guard**
- It is the military arm of the Stone Clan, led by Captain Errde Blackskull. It is pragmatic and suspicious of other factions [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 013 / NPCs].
- It is investigating the Empty-Scabbard Killers and seeking Droki [ch 014 / 014.02; ch 014 / npcs].
- It was once known for integrity but is now suspected of taking bribes. Errde seeks to justify a purge against the derro [ch 018 / 018.04].
- It seeks to expose the Council's corruption and will act violently if given the full truth [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].
- Latest (ch 026): it is paranoid and doubling patrols over fears of the Ember Vanguard. It issued passes to the party [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / 026.06].

**Gray Ghosts**
- A halfling mentioned them as in conflict with the Keepers of the Flame and the Crown, nature unknown [ch 013 / 013.05].
- They are a shadowy organization that stole the red dragon egg. They are suspected of causing the psionic disturbance in the Underdark [ch 014 / 014.07].
- The Council of Savants ordered them to kill the duergar who brought the Ember Vanguard into the city. This was revealed by a recovered letter [ch 026 / 026.07].

**Empty-Scabbard Killers**
- They are duergar assassins with psionic blades and no physical talismans. They are at war with Demogorgon-aligned derro. A royal contract is missing from their leader's desk [ch 019 / npcs; ch 019 / items].

**Duergar clans**
- Clan Steelshadow is the Deepking's clan. Its exclusive weapons contracts breed resentment across Gracklstugh [ch 018 / 018.02].
- Clan Ironhead opposes the Deepking's monopoly and is barred from direct weapons contracts. Grinta offers the party future trade advantages if the Deepking is removed [ch 018 / 018.07]. The party hopes to indirectly strengthen it [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].
- Clan Xardelvar chafes under new regulations and fears the Deepking will take over their work. Errde mentioned it as part of the cover story [ch 018 / 018.03].
- Clan Thrazgad is a steelsmithing clan whose name is on the dragon rattle. Its involvement with the egg is unknown [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.03].

**Deepking's regime**
- It is confirmed to be allied with assassins trying to wipe out opposition. The party holds proof [ch 025 / 025.01].

**Derro and the Council of Savants**
- The derro were once slaves to Mind Flayers. They are predisposed to madness, cooperate only under charismatic leadership, and revere the egg as a source of absolute authority [ch 020 / 020.01; ch 020 / locations].
- The Council of Savants is the derro governing body. Buppido claims it promises freedom but always fails [ch 015 / 015.04].
- The party has killed two Council members (Narrak and one other derro) and holds evidence of its involvement in demonic rituals [ch 024 / 024.04].
- The derro are confirmed aligned with demonic powers, and all in the Whorlstone Caverns are dead [ch 025 / 025.01].
- The Council still acted through the Gray Ghosts in ch 026 [ch 026 / 026.07].

### The party's reputations

**Ember Vanguard**
- It is the party's adopted name, now a legend among the derro: a marauding army of drow led by a 20th-level archmage [ch 021 / 021.04; ch 021 / Memorable Moments].
- Ch 026 calls it a fabricated identity and a feared rumor in Gracklstugh. It is described as a red dragonborn and wizened drow assassins, and blamed for the derro High Council murders [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / 026.03].
- Ch 027 describes a legendary, feared agent group of the Drow Empire, for which the party is mistaken. Its reputation is inflated, and Daz is thought to be an 18th-level mage [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / Memorable Moments].

**Ember Grapple**
- The party formally adopts this name as its group identity for future missions, including informing Sylvira of her son's death [ch 064 / 064.01].

### Fungal and demonic powers

- **Mad Gnolls** are demon-possessed and implant spawn in living bodies. Fargas claims his band was attacked by them [ch 006 / 006.07].
- **Zuggtmoy** is moving to claim the power vacuum left by the Pudding King's defeat, as the twisted fungi show [ch 047 / 047.10; ch 047 / npcs].
- **Juiblex** is not destroyed. A voice declares he will be reborn and consume Zuggtmoy's "banquet" to grow stronger [ch 047 / 047.10; ch 047 / npcs].
- **Myconids** are generally peaceable but dangerous in large numbers. They are confirmed susceptible to Zuggtmoy's corruption and fungal teleportation [ch 025 / 025.02; ch 025 / 025.04].
- **Neverlight Grove Circles:**
  - The Circle of Builders handles architecture and construction and is led by Gasbide [ch 030 / 030.05].
  - The Circle of Hunters tasked the party with hunting the Grick Alpha. Loobamub warned them about the Shambling Mound [ch 030 / 030.01].
  - The Circle of Welcome is a corrupted ritual that transforms visitors into rooted heads. Phylo laments the party's refusal of it [ch 032 / 032.03].
- **Myconid colony:** once communal, now divided. Circle leaders are infected, while explorers like Rumpadump resist. The sovereign's authority is being used to facilitate demonic worship [ch 031 / 031.02; ch 031 / npcs].

### Blingdenstone

- **Clan Diggermattock** rules this sector, having taken it from the drow. It treats drow escapees as valuable intelligence sources and focuses on defense and resource management [ch 036 / 036.01; ch 036 / 036.04].
- **Internal split:** the mining coalition led by Dorbo against the civilian community led by Senni [ch 037 / 037.01].
  - Ch 045: the party's intervention shifted power to the labor faction (Senni) [ch 045 / 045.02].
  - Ch 048, the latest note: miners, wererats, ghosts, and spiritual leaders brokered a fragile alliance under Dorbo's leadership, and Blingdenstone is officially open for trade [ch 048 / 048.06].
- **Clan Goldwhisker / Goldwhisker Wererats** are wererat gnomes. They were rumored squatters, excluded from the power structure [ch 037 / 037.01].
  - Dorbo dismisses them as "squatters", while Senni and the party see them as valuable allies, immune to non-silver weapons [ch 038 / 038.02; ch 038 / 038.06].
  - Chipgrin leads them. They refused subordination to Dorbo but agreed to fight the oozes [ch 039 / 039.05].
  - Now (ch 048) they hold territorial control of the upper boroughs as a self-funded defense force [ch 048 / 048.06].
- **Burrow Wardens** are ghostly spirits led by Uth-Jadger. They agreed to aid in the battle if the party completed a task for them [ch 039 / 039.09]. Now they retain sacred rights to the North Tunnels as a graveyard [ch 048 / 048.06].
- **Blingdenstone Authorities** tried to claim credit for the party's victory and suppress "revolutionary tactics" [ch 038 / 038.01; ch 038 / 038.06].
- **Chamber of Commerce** offered the party a 10% discount coupon [ch 038 / 038.02].
- **Miners' Guild:** represented by Kazook Pickshine. It is concerned about acid corrosion to weapons and armor [ch 039 / 039.09].
- **Pickshine Mines:** led by Dasco Pickshine, with branding on tea packaging, patches, and clothing. Miners care more about "gross margins" than combat [ch 046 / 046.02; ch 046 / npcs].
- **Stoneheart Enclave** can protect Earth Elementals from Ogrémoch's Bane if the Temple of the Steadfast Stone is cleansed [ch 039 / 039.08].
- **Blingdenstone militia:** reactions to the plan were mixed. Officers and ghosts wanted a big battle, and many others were relieved by the minimal distraction [ch 046 / 046.01].
- **Community cohesion:** collective outrage shut down Nomi's racism [ch 043 / 043.04].
- **Ogrémoch** is an elemental-earth entity who chose to linger in the Steadfast Stone among drow statues. His spirit is the source of the temple's corruption [ch 042 / 042.02; ch 042 / npcs].
- **Galeb Duhr** are ancient stone sentinels programmed to "Trust no one" and wait for divine authority. They respond to sonic resonance and ritual alignment [ch 044 / 044.04; ch 044 / npcs].

### Candlekeep and the Zhentarim

**Candlekeep (institution)**
- It prefers exile to imprisonment. Its public records are curated and sometimes deliberately sealed [ch 056 / 056.04; ch 056 / 056.07].
- It is a scholarly fortress housing dangerous tomes. Its scholars know lore of the Endless Chant and Word Lattice, and the Obsidian Door is part of its ancient protections [ch 069 / npcs; ch 069 / locations].
- The Watcher's Bell is its crisis alarm. Its tolling pattern was distinct from the hour [ch 063 / 063.01].
- The Temple of Oghma is its spiritual authority. Kei Tigersteel serves as priest and oversees purification rites [ch 059 / 059.01; ch 059 / npcs].

**Candlekeep leadership**
- The Council of Twelve includes the Keeper, First Reader, eight Great Readers, Gatewarden, and Lorekeeper. It is in uproar over leadership and murder [ch 057 / 057.05].
- The Council of Great Readers includes A'lai, Fheminor, Daral, Teles, and Bookwyrm. There is tension over succession and the murder investigation [ch 059 / 059.02; ch 059 / npcs].
- Bookwyrm is acting head. The naming ceremony for the new Keeper of Tomes is "tomorrow" as of ch 062, and Teles Ahvoste and Fheminor are likely candidates [ch 062 / npcs; ch 062 / 062.02].

**The Avowed**
- They are Candlekeep's administrative and security order. A hooded member was seen hurrying past with something hidden during dinner [ch 057 / 057.02].
- Alkrist says obtaining powerful transformation scrolls requires "the right goblin and the right Avowed" [ch 061 / 061.07].
- They are now documenting the ward failure as an unprecedented phenomenon and preparing experiments [ch 065 / 065.01; ch 065 / Memorable Moments].
- Historically they memorized the height of the High Tower of Exaltation, as Edvaldo references [ch 068 / 068.03].
- **Conflict:** ch 062 labels "Candlekeep Avowed" as the governing body, while ch 057 and ch 059 place governance with the Councils. The Councils reading is better supported.

**Theological schools**
- The Stroudite School holds that mortal choice is sovereign. It is now challenged by Gyrgum's "fixed points" theory [ch 057 / 057.01].
- The Bahamutian School argues divine providence arranges all things. Vareth aligns with it, and Gyrgum's theory is seen as a compromise toward it [ch 057 / 057.01].
- The Draconic School claims ancient agency sets the frame for mortal action [ch 057 / 057.01].

**Investigative methodology / Threefold Proof**
- Kalan's paper argues against relying on single magical results. It now underpins the party's investigation and is being considered for scholarly publication [ch 061 / 061.02].
- The Threefold Proof is Kalan's unapproved methodology for criminal investigation. Bookwyrm now publicly endorses it despite her prior suppression of it [ch 062 / items; ch 062 / 062.02].

**Zhentarim**
- Two raiders are confirmed as Zhentarim operatives, looting books on a written manifest. One carries the bow that killed the Watcher, which proves direct involvement in the murder and theft [ch 064 / 064.03; ch 064 / npcs].
- Their agents in the High Tower are all dead and the direct incursion failed, but Manshoon's breach indicates deeper involvement [ch 065 / 065.01; ch 065 / 065.02; ch 065 / NPCs].

### Other powers

- **Netherese** were an ancient, fallen empire known for floating cities and advanced magic. Their artifacts are highly valued and heavily plundered, and their concepts of good and evil are alien [ch 028 / locations].
- **House of Mechanus** provided the Mechanist Tools used to activate the bridge. The party owes them collateral (Glabbagool and Zalthir) for their use [ch 068 / items; ch 068 / npcs].
