## Party

### Identity, level and standing
- **Group name:** "Ember Grapple" was formally adopted in ch 064 [ch 064 / 064.01]. Ch 065 lists the name as "Not stated" and calls them guest seekers of the Avowed [ch 065 / end], and ch 067 says the name is "unaltered" [ch 067 / 067.02]. The later notes do not contradict the ch 064 adoption, but they do not repeat it.
- **Earlier names and the legend:** The "Ember Vanguard" was a name the party adopted after intimidating Derro guards [ch 021 / 021.04]. The drow know it as a false legend created by the Derro High Council [ch 049 / 049.03]. In Blingdenstone the party avoided "Emerald Vanguard" as a tainted name [ch 036 / 036.04].
- **Level:** The party is 9th level, confirmed in ch 065 [ch 065 / spells; ch 047 / 047.10]. Ch 048 says "seventh level" [ch 048 / 048.07], which conflicts with ch 047; the later ch 065 confirmation supports 9th.
- **Standing in Candlekeep:**
  - They are guest seekers of the Avowed with Inner Ward access [ch 054 / 054.06; ch 062 / 062.03].
  - They are independent investigators appointed by Bookwyrm, able to access any book in the library [ch 057 / 057.05].
  - They are deputized agents of Candlekeep, recognized by Tadric and Kalan [ch 067 / 067.02; ch 067 / 067.03].
  - Thorin negotiated a reward: a copy of any book in the library from the House of the Binder on successful resolution [ch 057 / 057.05].

### Location and immediate situation (end of ch 070)
- The party is in the hidden volcanic cavern and obsidian tower beneath Candlekeep. They stand in the inner book chamber, whose door Gyrgum opened by speaking the answer to Alaundo's riddle [ch 070 / 070.03].
- The chamber holds the Book of Vile Darkness on a podium. Daz concealed it behind a Minor Illusion of ashes, so Edvaldo believes it destroyed [ch 070 / 070.03; ch 070 / 070.04].
- The last surviving Avowed Readers are expected within minutes. The party was warned they will not be able to leave with artifacts of such power unnoticed [ch 070 / 070.04].
- Gyrgum's summary of the session: "We made a few irrevocable choices in this one." [ch 070 / 070.04]
- The dragon's ten-minute anti-magic field had run six minutes when ch 070 began [ch 070 / 070.02].
- **Consequences the party carries:**
  - They killed one Manshoon simulacrum, from which Daz recovered a Robe of the Archmagi [ch 070 / 070.01].
  - Zalthir threw a second simulacrum into the lava pit. It promised the real Manshoon's revenge [ch 070 / 070.02].
  - Edvaldo smashed two prophecy crystals [ch 070 / 070.03].
  - Edvaldo is loose. He shapeshifted into a halfling, broke away from Zalthir's grapple, and fled toward the anti-magic barrier [ch 070 / 070.03].
