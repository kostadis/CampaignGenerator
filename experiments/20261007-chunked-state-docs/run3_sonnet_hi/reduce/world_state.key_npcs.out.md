## Key NPCs

State is as of the end of the notes (ch 070). The Candlekeep arc (ch 054–070) is the live context; earlier-region NPCs are listed at the end with their last recorded state.

### Manshoon
- Legendary Zhentarim archmage tied to the Moonsea and Zhentil Keep. A'lai says Manshoon has directed Candlekeep book thefts for eleven years through a courier with a metal hand, and the party knows he seeks one specific book from Candlekeep. [ch 064 / 064.04] [ch 064 / npcs]
- His simulacrum breached Candlekeep alone and was depleted. The courier conceals the metal hand beneath a glove. [ch 065 / NPCs] [ch 065 / end]
- He is reportedly killed 170 years ago yet keeps reappearing. His simulacra are built from ice and snow, have half his vitality, cannot regain spells, and melt at zero hit points. [ch 069 / npcs]
- The first simulacrum is dead in the magma chamber. A second is alive in the upper tower chamber, neutral and bargaining. [ch 069 / 069.02] [ch 069 / 069.05]

### A'lai Aivenmore
- Drow scholar of 31 years at Candlekeep who served Manshoon for eleven years, fearing loss of his academic chair. He claims he never chose the books, only followed lists. [ch 064 / 064.04] [ch 064 / npcs] [ch 065 / NPCs]
- He coordinated the attacks on both apparent key holders. [ch 063 / 063.03]
- He realized he was never important to Manshoon. His insight into the Vault and his deduction about House Baenre are now in the party's possession. [ch 067 / 067.02]
- Now imprisoned in Candlekeep's null magic prison, grateful and resigned. [ch 067 / 067.02]
- Earlier, he was political rival of Daral and wanted to become Keeper of Tomes. [ch 060 / 060.08]

### Bookwyrm (First Reader / Skoda Vanaster)
- Green dragonborn First Reader. At ch 062 she is named Skoda Vanaster, acting head of Candlekeep and aunt to Alkrist. She suppressed Kalan's Threefold Proof and sought to contain the party's investigation. [ch 062 / npcs] [ch 062 / 062.03]
- She believed she deserved to succeed Janussi as Keeper of Tomes. He told her directly she would not. [ch 061 / 061.07]
- **Later chapters conflict with ch 062's "Alive":** she was murdered by Moziqodo, and her key was stolen and delivered to A'lai. She is dead. [ch 063 / 063.02] [ch 065 / NPCs] [ch 065 / end]

### Kalan Strongbranch
- Former Gate Warden, fired by Janussi, who blocked his career. His methodology: “Magic does not make evidence meaningful. It makes evidence adversarial”. [ch 061 / 061.02] [ch 061 / 061.07]
- Reinstated after the party's evidence vindicated his Threefold Proof. [ch 062 / npcs] [ch 062 / 062.02]
- Now Head of the Avowed. He admitted using the party as decoys with a fake key, and deputized them as Watchers of Candlekeep. [ch 063 / 063.03]
- Relieved and eager to contribute, working from the investigator's office. [ch 067 / 067.03]

### Alkrist
- Dragonborn Great Reader who proposed *Tales of War by the Generals That Waged Them*. He burned a note during the feast, kept poisoned rats, and owns a book matching the murder method. [ch 060 / 060.07] [ch 060 / 060.09]
- Imprisoned in Candlekeep prison and hostile. He confessed under compulsion and named A'lai as mastermind. [ch 062 / npcs] [ch 062 / 062.03]

### Moziqodo, Sylvira Savikas, Tadric
- **Moziqodo** was an Abyssal spawn, son of Sylvira. He was responsible for Bookwyrm's murder and the attack on Tadric, and the party slew him. [ch 063 / 063.02] [ch 063 / 063.05]
- **Sylvira Savikas** is alive in the investigator's office, grateful and helpful. [ch 067 / 067.03]
- **Tadric** is alive in the null magic prison, cooperative and supportive. [ch 067 / 067.02]

### Miirym the Sentinel Wyrm
- Spectral silver dragon guardian of Candlekeep. Her true name, Vydykyq, is the first answer to the Vault's cryptogram. [ch 067 / 067.03] [ch 068 / npcs]
- She is bound to be a witness, not a weapon, and cannot fight Manshoon directly. [ch 068 / npcs]

### Edvaldo Sedanur
- Presented as a Candlekeep historian but actually a doppelgänger, unexposed to the party at ch 068. [ch 068 / npcs]

### Alaundo
- Legendary dead seer whose recorded voice speaks the riddle of the Obsidian Door. The voice is artificial, with no breath and fluctuating timbre. [ch 069 / npcs]
- His door was protected by a riddle requiring genuine intent. His prophecies remain active in the remaining crystals. [ch 070 / npcs]

### Vecna
- Purported author of the Book of Vile Darkness. His name is so feared that even drow use it to frighten children. [ch 070 / npcs]

### Dawnbringer
- +2 sentient sword that deals radiant damage, adds 1d8 against undead, grants lesser restoration, and speaks and reads Common. She is terrified of darkness and insists on remaining active at all times. [ch 029 / items] [ch 029 / 029.01]
- Described as kind and feminine, though the party treats her as genderless. She is popular with local children and wants aesthetic upgrades. [ch 045 / npcs]
- She resists being treated as a ritual tool. She is aware of her trauma from darkness and may seek help at Candlekeep. [ch 053 / 053.05] [ch 053 / npcs]
- Master Archivist Philemon begins therapy with her. [ch 056 / 056.06]
