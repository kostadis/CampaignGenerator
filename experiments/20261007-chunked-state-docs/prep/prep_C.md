## The Win Condition (state plainly to yourself)

The session has landed when **the Book of Vile Darkness has a custodian everyone in the room has agreed to, or has refused to agree to, out loud** — and the Avowed have formed a public verdict on the party: heroes who saved the vault, vandals who lost two prophecies, or thieves holding Vecna's book. Edvaldo is either caught or confirmed loose with a face nobody knows. Everything else (crystals, succession, Sylvira) can stay as live tension.

## The Default Trajectory (what happens if the party does nothing)

- **The Readers arrive** to an open door, a pile of ash (or no ash, if the illusion has lapsed), two shattered prophecy gems on the floor, and four in Gyrgum's hands. [OVERLAY staging of a TABLE setup]
- **Kalan** declares a scene of crime and begins assembling his Threefold Proof — testimony, physical evidence, magical confirmation. He asks for Zone of Truth from **Kei**. [OVERLAY; Kei/ZoT from MODULE Restoring Calm]
- **Sylvira** goes straight for the Book. She is a demonologist dying of an abyssal plague; "how to defeat the demons" is the one sentence she has waited her life to hear. [OVERLAY]
- **Teles and Fheminor** turn the custody of the Book into a succession contest: whoever secures it looks like the next Keeper. [OVERLAY]
- **Edvaldo** gets out — either up the shaft once the field drops, or by joining the crowd wearing a Reader's face. [OVERLAY]
- **The Book** keeps talking to Daz, and starts talking to whoever else it can reach. [OVERLAY]

## The Clever Play (what the optimal party would do — the GM should know this)

1. **Tell the truth first, in order.** Kalan respects method. A party that volunteers the sequence (simulacrum #1, simulacrum #2, the doppelganger, the gems, the door) before being asked wins the room.
2. **Offer Zone of Truth themselves** — Gyrgum can cast it. Volunteering takes the weapon out of the Avowed's hands.
3. **Name the Keeper-of-Secrets clause.** The vault's own inscription addresses a "Keeper of Secrets" who may destroy the collection if foul hands threaten it [MODULE B2]. Whoever holds that title has standing; the party can argue the Book belongs under the Keepers, not under them.
4. **Trade the Book for access, not ownership.** Leave it in the Side Vault, keep visiting rights (and Daz's untrustworthy notes), and get the gem readings done by Candlekeep's own seers.
5. **Hand Edvaldo to the Watchers** — or warn them before he wears a familiar face.
6. **Claim Bookwyrm's reward** — a copy of any book in the library [MODULE Bookwyrm's Reward]. The smart ask is the sealed records on "Daz'issin."

## Scene 1 — The Last Few Minutes

### Setup
- **Time/place:** Seconds after Ch70. Upper tower chamber and the Inner Book Chamber beyond the open Obsidian Door, beneath Candlekeep. Dark except for Dawnbringer [TABLE ch69 069.07]; the Side Vault is darker still [MODULE B3: "a thick darkness emanates from the chamber's centre"].
- **Present:** Daz at the podium with the Book (Mage Hand, illusion). Gyrgum with the four crystals and a readied Guiding Bolt. Thorin on the stairs between the gems and the exit. Zalthir coming back up from dragging Edvaldo downstairs [TABLE ch70 070.03].
- **Offstage:** Edvaldo (halfling, fleeing toward the barrier); Miirym; the Owlbear; the Readers starting down.

### Approach — the Book talks
Open on Daz, not on the room. The Book has already started. It speaks like a footnote that wants to be a chapter.

> *"You chose the demons. Good. Everyone else in that tower would have chosen themselves."* [OVERLAY]

Give Daz **one** usable, checkable, slightly poisoned fact here — the GM picks it (see Open decisions). Keep its delivery calm and accurate-sounding; the untrustworthiness should show up later, not now.

### Approach — the boon gutters
Miirym's voice rolls up the shaft, grouchy: *"Four minutes, Reader. Then I am a witness again, and so is everyone else."* [OVERLAY; boon timing TABLE ch70 070.02]

### Branch — if the party chases Edvaldo
He is a halfling-sized shape near the bridge's anti-magic band. Inside the band he cannot shapeshift (a doppelganger's Shapechanger is not a spell, so this is **GM call** — see Open decisions). If caught, he babbles: Manshoon's plan involved Alkrist, the breached wards, and "an army" [TABLE ch70 070.03]. **Failure is material:** if he gets away, the party loses the only witness who can prove what Manshoon wanted — and the Readers have only the party's word.

### Branch — if the party secures the Book first
Daz can't touch it safely: in the module a good creature touching it saves DC 18 Charisma or takes 8d6 necrotic; a neutral creature saves with advantage [MODULE B3]. Daz is chaotic neutral [TABLE ch70 070.04]. The Book can be moved by Mage Hand only a little at a time (Mage Hand caps at 10 lb; the Book is "large"). **Bag of holding** is the obvious answer — and what does the Book say to a bag that already holds a shrunken drow spy? [OVERLAY; the spy's location is an Open decision]

### Branch — if the party rests on the stairs and waits
Fine. The Readers arrive into silence, which plays as guilt. Let it.

### NPC notes
- **The Book** — wants to be carried out. Voice tic: compliments that are really appraisals. Wants from this scene: a second reader.
- **Edvaldo** — wants to live, and to bring Manshoon *something*. Voice tic: academic self-justification under panic ("Do you have any idea what you have done?" [TABLE ch70 070.03]).

## Scene 2 — The Readers Arrive

### Setup
- **Time/place:** 3–6 minutes later. The upper tower chamber: ~100 glyph-warded books on the east wall, a desk with a note, the velvet tray with ten sockets — four dark, two now shattered, four empty because Gyrgum holds them [TABLE ch69 069.05; ch70 070.03].
- **Who comes:** Recommended delegation of five (**GM picks**): **Kalan**, **Tadric**, **Kei Tigersteel**, **Sylvira** (carried or leaning on Daral), **Fheminor** or **Teles**. [OVERLAY selection from TABLE/MODULE cast]
- **How they come:** down the shaft through the feather-fall zone, quizzed by the Owlbear, past Miirym. The Owlbear's verdict precedes them up the stairs: *"These ones knew the height of the High Tower. Barely."* [OVERLAY]

### Approach — the first look
Stage direction: **let Kalan arrive last and out of breath** [TABLE ch63 063.04; ch67 067.03 — this is his established entrance]. Tadric comes first, lantern up. Kei looks at the shattered gems before he looks at anyone. Sylvira looks at the open door.

Lines to lift:
- **Tadric:** *"You opened it. Of course you opened it. Who said the word?"* [OVERLAY]
- **Kei Tigersteel:** *"Two of the Echoes. Gone. Do you understand there is no copy?"* [OVERLAY]
- **Kalan (arriving):** *"Nobody — touch — anything. I want three accounts that do not depend on each other."* [OVERLAY; Threefold Proof TABLE ch62 062.02]
- **Sylvira, at the Side Vault threshold:** *"It's warm. Why is it warm?"* [OVERLAY]

### Branch — if the party leads with the truth
Kalan takes notes visibly. Kei asks Gyrgum — *the verified Reader* [TABLE ch69 069.05] — to confirm it under Zone of Truth. The Avowed's mood turns from "scene of crime" to "debrief." Move to Scene 3 with the party holding the initiative.

### Branch — if the party hides the Book (illusion, bag, silence)
Kei and Kalan are archmage/priest grade; Detect Magic or a simple Investigation will crack a one-minute illusion. Kalan's response is not violence — it's procedure: he informs them the Side Vault is now a sealed room, everyone present is a witness, and **no one leaves the cavern until the Threefold Proof is complete**. The party is "deputized Watchers" [TABLE ch63 063.03]; Kalan can un-deputize them. **Failure is material:** they lose the title, and with it their standing at the Council.

### Branch — if Daz refuses to stop reading
Sylvira steps between Daz and the Readers and *defends* him — because she wants the next page. That splits the Avowed, which is the most interesting outcome. [OVERLAY]

### Branch — if someone mentions Moziqodo
Sylvira does not know her son is dead or who killed him [TABLE ch67 067.03]. If this lands now, it lands in front of the entire leadership of Candlekeep, under a possible Zone of Truth. **Do not force it** — but Zone of Truth makes it far likelier, which the players will realise. See Stage directions.

### NPC notes
- **Kalan** — wants procedure to win and his method vindicated again; tic: "I'm relieved to see that" when outpaced [TABLE ch67 067.03]; wants a clean report.
- **Tadric** — wants the party to be the heroes he already believes they are; tic: practical, short sentences; wants someone to have told Sylvira.
- **Kei Tigersteel** — wants the truth sworn, not argued; tic: speaks in liturgy; wants the gems counted.
- **Sylvira** — wants the Book's demon knowledge before her plague takes her; tic: laughs at things that should frighten her (she called the cryptogram "almost too easy" [TABLE ch67 067.03]); wants a reason to stay alive.
- **Fheminor / Teles** — wants to emerge holding something that looks like the Keeper's authority; tic (Fheminor): fussy about "the will of the previous Keeper" [MODULE Restoring Calm].

## Scene 3 — The Accounting

### Setup
- On the spot in the tower, or (if the party consents) back up through the reverse-gravity exit to a council chamber [MODULE Returning to the Surface]. On the surface the Endless Chant is still silent [TABLE ch64 064.01] — let that hang over the meeting.
- A council of surviving Great Readers, the acting Gatewarden (likely Tadric), and Kei Tigersteel with Zone of Truth [MODULE Restoring Calm].

### Approach — the three questions
1. **Who opened the door?** Gyrgum, freely, with intent [TABLE ch70 070.03]. Readers will ask *why* — "we just did our own murder… where's the payoff?" is on tape and will be read back to him if Zone of Truth is up.
2. **Who broke the Echoes?** Edvaldo, a doppelganger who walked into Candlekeep as one of them [TABLE ch70 070.03]. Nobody in the room checked his credentials. This is Candlekeep's embarrassment, not the party's — if the party says so.
3. **What did the Book say?** Daz is the only witness. Kalan wants it written down; Sylvira wants it read aloud; Kei wants it burned.

### Branch — the Book goes back in the Side Vault
The door closes again behind it. Who holds the answer now? Gyrgum is the verified Reader; does the next opening require him? [OVERLAY; GM ruling] The party keeps their reputation and loses the book; Daz keeps whatever he already heard.

### Branch — the Book is destroyed / launched
The upper chamber holds "the means to destroy the collection" [TABLE ch68 locations]; the module's B4 Attic lever drops the tower into the lava or, in the rocket alternative, fires it out to sea [MODULE B4; Rocket Alternative]. **Lava will not destroy the Book** [MODULE Rocket Alternative], and the Book "cannot be permanently destroyed while evil exists" [TABLE ch70 070.04]. Destruction only relocates it. That's a hook, not an ending.

### Branch — the party walks out with it
The summary already ruled this would not go unnoticed [TABLE ch70 070.04]. Candlekeep doesn't keep prisoners — it exiles at the gate [TABLE ch56 056.04]. So the stakes are **exile, loss of the library, and the Avowed as an enemy institution** — not a fight.

### NPC notes
- As Scene 2, plus: **the Book**, if present, makes a quiet bid to each Reader in turn. [OVERLAY]
