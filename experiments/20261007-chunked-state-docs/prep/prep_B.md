# The Readers Come Down the Stairs

Co-GM working doc. Not canon yet — staging for the session after Ch70 (the Obsidian Door session, played 2026-09-28).
Party: level 9 — Zalthir (Monk 9, Shadow; Gabe), Thorin Giantfriend (Fighter 9, Battle Master; Joe Beda), Gyrgum (Cleric 9, Life, of Bahamut; Ben Pfaff), Daz (Wizard 9, Evoker; Mike Hall). All four player-run. GM-run companions: Glabbagool (ooze apprentice), Dawnbringer (Thorin's sentient sunblade).
Register: chamber piece with a custody fight in it — institutional politics at the bottom of a hole, with a shapeshifter in the room. Not a dungeon crawl; the dungeon is done.

**What I understood from the docs (catch me if I misread):** Ch70 ended seconds after the Obsidian Door opened. Daz is beside the Book of Vile Darkness, hiding it under a Minor Illusion of ash and reading it by Mage Hand; the book offered him demons-or-self and he chose demons, and it has *begun* telling him something about the Underdark — what, exactly, was explicitly left for this session. Edvaldo (a doppelganger, Manshoon's) smashed two of Alaundo's six unplayed Echoes and fled in halfling form toward the anti-magic band; Gyrgum holds the four survivors. The surviving Avowed Readers are minutes out, pleased the vault was found, and will not let an artifact walk out unnoticed. Simulacrum #2 is in the lava; the real Manshoon is at large.

> **Scope:** Opens in the last minutes before the Readers arrive (the table is still mid-aftermath of Ch70). Intended order: **(1) Four Minutes** — settle the book's first revelation and Edvaldo's run → **(2) The Descent** — the Readers cross the bridge and pass the owlbear → **(3) Custody** — the argument in the upper chamber over the book, the open door, and the two smashed Echoes → **(4) The Way Up** — leaving the vault. **Stop** when the party is out of the vault with the custody question answered *by a decision at the table*, or when the session clock runs out mid-argument (that is a fine cliffhanger).
> **Do NOT pull forward:** the real Manshoon in person; the Gauntlgrym summons / Eldeth's return; the Keeper-of-Tomes succession vote and any trial; telling Sylvira about Moziqodo (only if a player raises it); the four surviving Echoes being *played* (they can be asked about, not opened — see Open decisions).

## The Win Condition (state plainly to yourself)

The session lands if **the party makes a deliberate, in-the-open decision about who holds the Book of Vile Darkness, and the Avowed answer it** — agree, refuse, or force the question — so that the book's custody stops being a secret four people are keeping in a dark room. Along the way the book must have *said something* about the Underdark that is concrete enough to check later, and Edvaldo must be either caught or visibly loose with a known destination. The party should leave aware that their "irrevocable choices" (Manshoon in the lava, two Echoes gone, the door opened, the book read) now have an institutional owner who is keeping score.

## The Default Trajectory (what happens if the party does nothing)

- **The book** `[OVERLAY]`: the ash illusion lapses before the Readers reach the chamber. The first Reader through the door sees an intact Book of Vile Darkness and an evoker standing next to it with an open spellbook's worth of guilt. The party is now explaining instead of disclosing.
- **Kalan** `[OVERLAY, grounded in MODULE B2 note]`: invokes the note — consult here, remove nothing. He orders the book left on its pedestal and the door re-sealed if it can be. He thanks the party in the tone of a man writing an incident report.
- **Teles** `[OVERLAY, grounded in MODULE ch4]`: argues the book be *studied* under his supervision "given the demonic crisis" — using the party's own demon intel as his argument. He is running for Keeper and this is a platform.
- **Fheminor** `[OVERLAY]`: wants the matter put to the surviving Readers and the previous Keeper's wishes honoured; asks, pointedly, what was on the two Echoes that were destroyed.
- **Edvaldo** `[OVERLAY]`: hides at the anti-magic band, lets the Readers pass, takes the face of the last Watcher/acolyte down the stairs (Fembris if Fembris came — the module's chosen victim), and walks out with the procession. Manshoon learns the book survives.
- **The Echoes** `[OVERLAY]`: the Readers ask for the four survivors as Candlekeep property. Gyrgum is asked to hand them over.
- **Net:** the party leaves the vault without the book, without the Echoes, with Edvaldo gone, and with a reputation note in the Avowed's ledger that says "opened the forbidden door; two prophecies lost on their watch."

## The Clever Play (what the optimal party would do — the GM should know this)

1. **Disclose first.** Drop the illusion (or let Daz say it) and tell the Readers what's behind the door *before* anyone finds it. Thorin's radical candor is built for this beat `[TABLE world_state §2]`. Disclosure turns them from suspects into witnesses.
2. **Treat the arrivals as possible doppelgangers.** One shapeshifter is loose and the arrivals are the perfect cover. Use the owlbear's three questions as a checkpoint, or Zalthir's "tell me the history of Candlekeep" test again `[TABLE ch69]`. Count heads coming down and going up.
3. **Catch Edvaldo alive.** He is the only living link to Manshoon's plans and staff. The permanent anti-magic band at the bridge doesn't stop shapechange `[OVERLAY rules read]`, but a frightened halfling on a 10-ft-wide stone bridge has nowhere to go.
4. **Trade the Echoes, not the book.** The Readers want the four surviving Echoes more than they want to fight over the book. Gyrgum holding them is leverage the party may not notice they have.
5. **Get what the book knows, then let the institution keep it.** Daz extracts the demon intel tonight and the book stays sealed, ideally with the party's name on the custody record as the people who *found and returned* it.
6. **Point out the self-destruct is useless against this book.** Lava makes it unreachable, not destroyed `[MODULE B4 sidebar]`; the table already knows it can't be destroyed while evil exists `[TABLE ch70]`. Whoever proposes "drop the tower" can be talked down with that.

## Scene 1 — Four Minutes

### Setup
- **Time/place:** Seconds after Ch70's last line. Upper chamber of the Obsidian Tower and the inner book chamber behind the open door; dark except for Dawnbringer. `[TABLE ch69–70]`
- **Present:** Daz at the podium (Mage Hand on the pages, illusion of ash over the book); Gyrgum with the four Echoes and a readied Guiding Bolt; Thorin holding the stairs/crystals with Dawnbringer; Zalthir mid-pursuit of Edvaldo toward the stairs and bridge. Glabbagool — place him. `[TABLE ch70]`
- **Offstage:** Edvaldo (halfling, running); the Readers (on the stairs far above, descending).
- **Decide before play:** are you still in initiative? Ch70's last exchanges were in combat order (readied action, frightened condition). See Open decisions.

### Approach — the book finishes its sentence
Resolve the revelation first, while it's just Daz and the table. The book chose its own question — "about how to defeat the demons" — so whatever it says should be *true enough to act on and shaped to cost something* `[OVERLAY]`. The voice is patient and courteous; it never threatens; it makes offers.

Lift-ready lines `[OVERLAY]`:
> "You asked how to defeat them. That is a better question than most of my readers ask. Most ask how to *become* them."
> "I will tell you one true thing for free, because you will need to come back for the second."
> "Your friend with the sword is listening. Tell her I remember the people who forged her."

The content is yours to pick (see Open decisions); three shapes that fit what the docs already have, all `[OVERLAY]`:
- **A date.** When Zuggtmoy's wedding to Araumycos happens `[TABLE thread]` — useful, checkable, and it starts a clock the party now owns.
- **A mechanism.** That the demon lords can be drawn into one place and made to fight — which is the module's endgame idea in the shape of a temptation (it's the right answer delivered by the wrong mouth).
- **An explanation.** What Narrak's dying "the ritual cannot be undone… sacrificed" meant `[TABLE thread]`.

Whatever it is, the book closes the offer with a price for the *next* answer. Daz doesn't have to pay it tonight.

### Approach — Dawnbringer meets the book
Dawnbringer is a sunblade of late Netherese make who spent 2,000 years in the dark and is in therapy `[TABLE world_state §6]`. Have her speak in Thorin's mind the moment the book speaks `[OVERLAY]`: not rage — revulsion and something close to fear. "Thorin. That thing is reading *him*." Her blade cannot be dimmed `[TABLE]`, and in a room the book darkens from its centre `[MODULE B3]`, the two lights push against each other. Good place for Thorin's player to choose: hold the stairs, or walk the sword over.

### Branch — if Zalthir chases Edvaldo now
Edvaldo is frightened of Thorin, not of Zalthir `[TABLE ch70]`. On the bridge he's a halfling on 10 ft of anti-magic stone over lava. He doesn't fight; he talks — fast, the way he did on the walkway in Ch69 `[TABLE ch69]`:
> "I was *useful* to him. You understand? Useful things get kept. Useless things get—" (looks at the lava) "—you saw." `[OVERLAY]`

If grappled, he offers what he knows in exchange for not going back to Manshoon: that the real Manshoon sent him ahead to be "the Reader the door would accept" and the plan failed because a doppelganger is not a verified Reader `[OVERLAY — consistent with TABLE ch70 "depends on a Reader"]`. If Zalthir dangles him again, he names whatever channel he reports through (see Open decisions: does he carry a sending stone? `[MODULE ch5 Final Confrontation]` has one).

### Branch — if nobody chases Edvaldo
He goes to ground at the anti-magic band and waits for the Readers (Scene 2's infiltration branch).

### Branch — if Daz keeps reading
He can. Second answer costs. The DMG artifact entry governs anything mechanical `[MODULE B3 "Turn Up the Vileness" → DMG]`; this campaign's module note adds that touching it drains non-evil creatures (DC 18 Cha or 8d6 necrotic; neutral creatures with advantage) `[MODULE B3]`. Daz (chaotic neutral `[TABLE party.md]`) has so far avoided touching it. Keep him off the hook unless he puts his hand on it.

### NPC notes
- **The Book** — wants a reader who comes back; voice: courteous, unhurried, flatters the question; wants from this scene: a second visit.
- **Edvaldo** — wants to live, and failing that to fail *somewhere Manshoon can't see*; tic: academic indignation as a reflex ("second author," "outrageous"); wants: a face and a way out.
- **Dawnbringer** — wants Thorin away from the book; tic: talks over the room in Thorin's head; wants: light on the book, or distance from it.

## Scene 2 — The Descent

### Setup
- **Place:** the lava cavern, the bridge, and the iron owlbear's post at the tower foot `[TABLE ch68]`.
- **Who comes down:** see the cast. Recommended `[OVERLAY]`: Kalan, Teles, Fheminor, and Fembris, with two or three Watchers carrying light. Add Daral if you want an ally in the room. Kei Tigersteel only if you want *zone of truth* on the table tonight.
- **Their mood:** delighted the vault is found `[TABLE ch70]`. They are scholars who just got a thousand-foot fall, a dragon, and lava; they are giddy and frightened in equal parts `[OVERLAY]`.

### Approach — the owlbear does its job
The iron owlbear asks every arrival its three questions (the Hearth; the triumvirate of Oghma, Deneir and Milil; the High Tower's height) `[TABLE world_state §4]`. Play it *straight* and a little bored — and let it comment `[OVERLAY]`:
> "You are the fourth group today. The first one didn't say hello. The second one was very rude about the riddles. You" — to the party, if visible — "were my favourite."

This is the identity checkpoint. In Ch69 the party already noticed Edvaldo could give a tower's height only roughly ("about five hundred feet"), not precisely `[TABLE ch69]` — a doppelganger wearing a Reader's face may know the owlbear's answers *or may not*. That's your tell, if you want one.

### Approach — Miirym watches
She appears to the Readers, not to the party `[OVERLAY]`. A witness, not a weapon `[TABLE]`. If asked, she says only what she saw — and she saw everything, including what went into the lava and who smashed what. She is the fairest witness the party has; she is not on their side.

### Branch — Edvaldo infiltrates
If he was not caught in Scene 1: at the anti-magic band he can't use magic, but shapechange isn't magic `[OVERLAY rules read]`. He takes the shape of the last Watcher or of Fembris (the module's chosen victim `[MODULE ch4]`) after the procession passes. The real person is unconscious behind a basalt outcrop. Tells for the party: one extra person going up than came down; "Fembris" is suddenly quiet `[TABLE: Fembris talks too much]`; he doesn't know the owlbear's answers.

### Branch — the party meets them at the bridge
Good instinct. They control the first impression and the headcount. Kalan arrives winded and annoyed; Teles arrives looking at the tower, not at the party `[OVERLAY]`.

### Branch — the party stays upstairs
The Readers arrive at the chamber door and find whatever is there. If the illusion has lapsed, Teles sees the book first `[OVERLAY]`.

### NPC notes
- **Kalan Strongbranch** — the institution's custodian after a season of murder; wants the vault's rules honoured and nobody else dead; tic: procedure as comfort, the Threefold Proof `[TABLE]`; wants from this scene: a count of what's missing.
- **Teles Ahvoste** — wants the Keepership and knows a crisis is a platform; tic: speaks to the room, not the person; wants: to be the one who reads the book "responsibly."
- **Fheminor Scrivenbark** — wants Janussi's wishes kept; tic: quotes precedent, asks who authorised things; wants: to know what was on the two smashed Echoes.
- **The Iron Owlbear** — wants to do its job; voice: bored sports commentator; wants: a good show.

## Scene 3 — Custody

### Setup
- **Place:** the upper tower chamber — the shelves of warded tomes, the desk with the note, the tray with its empty sockets, the open Obsidian Door with eleven scorch marks around it `[TABLE ch69–70]`. Dark except for Dawnbringer and whatever the Watchers carry.
- **Stakes on the table:** the book; the four surviving Echoes (Gyrgum); two destroyed Echoes; a door that Alaundo built to stay shut until a Reader *gave* it an answer — and it was given `[TABLE ch70]`.

### Approach — the accounting
Kalan, or whoever leads, does the inventory out loud `[OVERLAY]`:
> "Ten Echoes. Four played. Two—" (looks at the shards) "—Gyrgum, you have four? Then two. Two of the Seer's last words, gone." (beat) "Who?"

The answer is Edvaldo `[TABLE ch70]`, and it matters to the party's standing that they say so — and that Zalthir carried Manshoon out *because* of the glyphs, not in spite of them `[TABLE ch70]`. Let the facts land in their favour if they tell it straight.

### Approach — the note on the desk
Someone reads it aloud: "Consult here what can benefit civilisation, but leave the tomes on these shelves… should circumstances lead you — Keeper of Secrets — to believe that such dogma might be grasped by foul hands, you will find the means to destroy the contents of this vault in the chamber above." `[MODULE ch5 B2, verbatim]`. Kalan is a Keeper of Secrets (Gatewarden) `[MODULE inventory]`; Teles would like to be the other one. Watch the room hear that.

### Branch — the party asks to keep the book
Their argument is strong: the demon crisis is real, they're the ones fighting it, and the book has already offered demon intel to Daz. Teles agrees *for himself*. Kalan refuses on the note. Fheminor wants a vote of surviving Readers. **Nobody here can say yes cleanly** — that's the point `[OVERLAY]`. If they press, offer a compromise: the book stays sealed; Daz gets supervised access later; the party's name goes on the custody record.

### Branch — the party hands it over
Kalan seals the door again (does it re-lock? Open decision). The Readers are relieved and suspicious in equal parts — *why* did the drow wizard volunteer? Daz's sealed-house history at Candlekeep `[TABLE: Daz'issin, sealed record]` makes him a person of interest no matter how he plays it `[OVERLAY]`.

### Branch — someone proposes dropping the tower
The lever above does it `[MODULE B4]`. It wouldn't destroy the book — lava makes it unreachable, not gone `[MODULE B4 sidebar]` — and it would destroy a hundred other tomes Candlekeep values. Teles fights this hardest. Leave it on the table as the "nuclear" branch; do not fire it without a deliberate player decision.

### Branch — the party tries to smuggle it out
Possible. The ward question matters here (Open decision: does B3 carry B2's *suggestion* glyph?). If yes, a carrier 30 ft from the pedestal gets "Put the book back, leave the Vault of Secrets, and forget its location!" — DC 17 Wis. That is "failure is comedic and material": they can lose the *memory of where the vault is*.

### Branch — Edvaldo is in the room
If he's wearing Fembris: he is the most helpful person present, enthusiastically pushing for the book to be "moved somewhere safer — tonight." That's the tell `[OVERLAY]`.

### NPC notes
- **Kalan** — stake: the order surviving its own season; worry: he was gone during Ch65 and people remember `[TABLE]`; small goal: leave with nothing missing he can't account for.
- **Teles** — stake: the Keepership; worry: someone else is more useful in a crisis than he is; small goal: get his hands on the book's index, at least.
- **Fheminor** — stake: Janussi's wishes; worry: the order deciding by who's loudest; small goal: get the two lost Echoes written into the record with names attached.
- **Daral** (if present) — stake: gratitude; worry: being a suspect again; small goal: back the party, and ask Dawnbringer how she's doing.
- **Fembris** (real) — stake: being useful; worry: he told Bookwyrm the key's location in front of A'lai `[TABLE world_state §3]`, and (overlay) knows it; small goal: don't talk. Fails.

## Scene 4 — The Way Up

### Setup
- **Route:** the module's exit — the walkway round the tower's base, the second bridge, a ten-minute passage, a reverse-gravity shaft into a feather-fall zone, a one-way *wall of force*, a hidden door into a disused outhouse or turret `[MODULE ch5 Returning to the Surface]`. Climbing back up the thousand-foot shaft the party came down is possible only for exceptional climbers, and Candlekeep's anti-flight wards still hold down here `[MODULE ibid.]`. Whether your vault has this exit is an Open decision.
- **Who:** the party, the Readers, whoever's carrying what.

### Approach
Keep it short. The reverse-gravity shaft is wonder, not danger — let the Readers shriek. Count heads at the top.

### Branch — Edvaldo is still loose
The module stages its final confrontation *at the exit*, because Manshoon learns the exit point by divination or through a doppelganger's sending stone `[MODULE ch5 Final Confrontation]`. Tonight there is nobody waiting — but leave something there `[OVERLAY]`: a black "M" stitched on a scrap of cloth (Manshoon's raiders wear one `[MODULE ch4]`), or a dropped stone that is still warm. Someone knew where they'd come out.
