# The Keepers of Secrets

Co-GM working doc. Not canon yet. This stages the session after Chapter 70, starting with the Avowed arriving at the Inner Book Chamber.
Party: level 9 [TABLE ch63 063.06]. Thorin Giantfriend (Fighter 9), Zalthir (Monk 9), Gyrgum (Cleric 9), Daz (Wizard 9), all player-run. Glabbagool and Dawnbringer are GM-run companions.
Register: a custody hearing held in a doorway. A political thriller in academic robes. The party just did the impossible and now has to explain it to the people whose house it was.

**What I understood from the docs (catch me if I misread):** Ch70 ended in the hidden volcanic cavern under Candlekeep. Simulacrum #2 is in the lava, Edvaldo (a doppelganger) smashed 2 of Alaundo's 6 unplayed prophecy crystals and fled in halfling form, Gyrgum opened the Obsidian Door with "A candle", and Daz is reading the sentient **Book of Vile Darkness** by Mage Hand behind an illusion that it turned to ash. The book offered him self-knowledge or knowledge of the demons, and he chose the demons. The surviving Avowed Readers are "expected to arrive within minutes" and will not let the party walk off with artifacts of this power unnoticed [TABLE ch70 070.04]. Candlekeep is also leaderless: the Keeper of Tomes (Janussi) and the First Reader (Bookwyrm) are both dead [TABLE ch65 Session-End State; ch56 056.04].

> **Scope:** Open at the moment the first Avowed lantern appears on the bridge. The session is about **who holds the Book when everyone leaves the tower**, plus the 4 crystals and Edvaldo as complications. It stops when the party and the Avowed leave the cavern with a custody settlement, whether agreed, forced or broken.
> **Intended order:** (1) the arrival and first impressions → (2) the custody argument → (3) Edvaldo surfaces inside the delegation → (4) the settlement, and optionally the attic lever.
> **Do NOT pull forward:** the real Manshoon in person, the Gauntlgrym or forge summons, Eldeth, House T'sarran or Baenre payoffs, or the succession vote itself. The vote can be *set up* tonight but should not be held.

## The Win Condition (state plainly to yourself)

The session has landed when **the Book of Vile Darkness has a custodian that the party chose, argued for, or failed to prevent, and Candlekeep's surviving Avowed have formally reacted to the party's "irrevocable choices"**. That reaction can be thanks, an indictment, or a bargain. The 4 crystals and Edvaldo must have moved at least one step each. The party should leave the cavern knowing exactly what Candlekeep now thinks of them.

## The Default Trajectory (what happens if the party does nothing)

- **The Avowed** arrive "delighted that the hidden books had been found" [TABLE ch70 070.04]. They take inventory, then ask for the book and the crystals. Without a strong counter-claim they **re-shelve the book in the side vault and appoint a watch**. The Avowed are "circumspect" by temperament [MODULE Ch5 §Resurrection]. [OVERLAY]
- **Kalan** insists on the Threefold Proof for everything the party claims ("Manshoon was here", "the book is safe", "Edvaldo was a doppelganger"). Evidence, testimony and corroboration. He will not take magic alone. [TABLE ch62 NPCs] + [OVERLAY application]
- **Edvaldo** slips in among the arriving Avowed wearing the face of one of them and tries to leave with the book. He believes it is ash, so his first goal is simply to **get out alive and report**. He changes plan the moment he learns the book is real. [OVERLAY, with module precedent: MODULE Ch5 §Final Confrontation]
- **The Book** stays silent to the Avowed and goes on speaking only to Daz. Its feed continues and the party has no corroboration. [OVERLAY]
- **The crystals** are claimed by the Avowed as Alaundo's property, and Gyrgum is asked to surrender them. [OVERLAY]

## The Clever Play (what the optimal party would do; the GM should know this)

1. **Claim the title.** The vault note addresses whoever reads it as "Keeper of Secrets" [MODULE Ch5 §B2; table note text TABLE ch68 068.05]. Miirym **verified Gyrgum as the Reader** [TABLE ch68 068.04]. With Miirym as witness, Gyrgum has a legitimate institutional claim to *custody* that does not depend on the Council's goodwill.
2. **Corroborate before you're asked.** The Owlbear saw the simulacrum fight [TABLE ch69 069.02], the snow token marks where #1 fell, and Kei Tigersteel can cast *zone of truth* [MODULE]. Volunteering for zone of truth disarms Kalan's methodology.
3. **Smoke out Edvaldo with the riddle trick.** He failed the Candlekeep-lore tests twice: the tower height [TABLE ch68 068.03] and "Books!" [TABLE ch69 069.06]. Ask each arriving Avowed one question only a real member would know. Better still, keep the ash illusion up and watch who goes for the podium.
4. **Tell Sylvira first, on their own terms.** If the party discloses Moziqodo's death before someone else does, she stays an ally. She is also the best-qualified reader of the book's demon intelligence.
5. **Do not carry the book out.** Leave it where it is under a joint seal (party + Avowed + Miirym as witness), and keep **access** for Daz. That gives the party the intel without making them the target.

## Scene 1: Lanterns on the Bridge

### Setup
- **In-fiction time:** a few minutes after the door opened. The Avowed come down the shaft and across the bridge, the way the party came. [OVERLAY]
- **Present:** the whole party in or at the Inner Book Chamber. Daz is beside the podium, with the illusory ash over the real book [TABLE ch70 070.04]. Gyrgum holds the 4 crystals and has a Guiding Bolt readied [TABLE ch70 070.03]. Zalthir is back from the lava. Thorin is on the stairs.
- **Offstage:** Edvaldo, somewhere near the anti-magic band. Miirym is in the lava chamber.
- **Map:** the lava cavern, bridge, tower guardian chamber, the vault and door chamber, and the Inner Book Chamber (B3). The attic (B4) is above and unvisited.

### Approach: the arrival
First a sound: many feet on obsidian, and a voice saying "Mind the — *it's real*" as someone sees the bridge. Then lantern-light, which is wrong here because Dawnbringer has been the only light [TABLE ch69 069.05].

Stagger the arrivals. **Tadric and two Watchers** come first, weapons out, and lower them when they see the party. Then **Kalan**, winded ("ninety-seven steps is a *young* man's riddle"). Then **Teles and Fheminor** together, pointedly not looking at each other. **Kei Tigersteel** brings up the rear with a holy symbol of Oghma. If you want Sylvira on stage, she is carried in a chair by two acolytes, because she insisted. [OVERLAY: order and lines]

The Owlbear, cheerfully: *"More of you! These ones knocked."* [OVERLAY, riffing on TABLE ch69 069.02]

Lift-able lines:
- **Kalan:** "Watchers. Report. And I want it in three parts."
- **Teles** (to the vault shelves): "Wish. That's a spellbook detailing *wish*. On a shelf. In a *cave*." [the book list is MODULE Ch5 §B2]
- **Fheminor:** "Who opened the last door?" A pause. "And who gave them the right?"

### Branch: if the party leads with the truth (Manshoon, two simulacra, Edvaldo, the book)
Kalan starts his Threefold Proof. Testimony: the party. Evidence: the snow token, 11 scorch marks [TABLE ch69 069.05], and the smashed crystals. Corroboration: the Owlbear and Miirym. If the party offers zone of truth, Kei casts it. **The Avowed relax by one notch**, and the argument moves on to custody (Scene 2).

### Branch: if the party keeps the ash illusion going
The Avowed believe the book is destroyed, for now. This is the cleanest way to catch Edvaldo (see Scene 3), but the lie has a fuse: Kei or Teles will investigate the "ash", and Kalan's whole career is built on not trusting appearances. If the lie is discovered *by the Avowed*, the party loses its custody claim and is treated as suspects (a material failure: their Watcher deputization is suspended). [OVERLAY]

### Branch: if the party tries to leave with the book before the Avowed arrive
Only one way out: back across the bridge, or through the module's rear exit (the reverse-gravity well), which the party does not know about [MODULE Ch5 §Returning to the Surface]. The Avowed are coming down the front route. The book will *enjoy* being carried (see NPC notes). Anyone good-aligned who touches it risks the module's DC 18 Charisma save or 8d6 necrotic; neutral characters roll with advantage [MODULE Ch5 §B3]. Tell the player the book is "heavy in a way that isn't weight".

### NPC notes
- **Tadric:** wants to be a hero *and* do this by the book. He is the party's friend in the room. His tic: he states his own name and rank when nervous.
- **Kalan:** wants his methodology to be the thing that settles this. The man who "chose validation over a conviction" [TABLE ch63 NPCs]. His tic: counts on his fingers, one, two, three.
- **Kei Tigersteel:** wants the truth spoken aloud in a sacred register. He is quiet and does not argue. He waits to be asked.

## Scene 2: The Custody Argument

### Setup
- Everyone is crowded into the vault and door chamber, under ~100 glyph-warded books [TABLE ch69 069.05]. **Nobody casts fire in here.** Remind the table that an explosion here starts a chain reaction [TABLE ch70 070.02].
- The real book (or the "ash") sits through the open door.

### Approach: who has standing?
Present this as a three-way argument. Do not resolve it as a single roll.

1. **The Avowed's claim:** it is Candlekeep's vault, Candlekeep's book, Candlekeep's prophecies. "Consult here what can benefit civilisation, but leave the tomes on these shelves" [MODULE Ch5 §B2; TABLE ch68 068.05 paraphrase]. [OVERLAY: Fheminor voices it]
2. **The party's claim:** Gyrgum is the **verified Reader**, verified by the Sentinel Wyrm herself [TABLE ch68 068.04]. The note addresses the "Keeper of Secrets", and someone with no seat on the Council answered it. They also saved the vault from Manshoon. [OVERLAY framing]
3. **The book's own claim:** it wants to *leave*. (GM-only, see notes.)

> **GM-only:** There is **no one with clear authority** to rule. The Keeper and the First Reader are both dead. That is why this scene is political rather than legal. Whoever proposes a procedure first will probably win. If the party suggests "let Miirym witness the settlement", Miirym accepts. She is bound to witness, and this is witnessing. [OVERLAY]

### Branch: if the party argues for joint custody (the clever play)
A sealed side vault. Access requires the verified Reader (Gyrgum) **plus** a Great Reader. Daz is granted supervised reading rights, with Sylvira present as demon-lore expert. The Avowed accept if the party has been honest so far. **The cost:** the crystals go to the Avowed for study, with Gyrgum named co-investigator. [OVERLAY]

### Branch: if the party wants to keep the book
The Avowed refuse. Kalan says flatly, "That is how the last eleven years started." Pushing turns the Watchers hostile and makes the party *exactly* what A'lai was. **The material failure:** they lose their Watcher deputization and Candlekeep access, and the Gyrgumite School loses its sponsor. Let them try. It is a real choice, and it is a terrible one. [OVERLAY]

### Branch: if the party wants to destroy it
Daz knows it "cannot be permanently erased while evil continued to exist" [TABLE ch70 070.04]. The vault note points to the means of destruction "in the chamber above" [TABLE ch68 068.05]. The module's attic lever drops the tower into the lava, and the module itself says even lava would not destroy the book, only make it "rather inaccessible" [MODULE Ch5 §B4, §Rocket Alternative]. **That would also destroy the other ~100 tomes.** The Avowed will physically stop this unless the party convinces them the book is about to fall into foul hands. See Open decisions on whether the attic is in play.

### Branch: if the party hands it over outright
The Avowed are pleased. Teles quietly asks Daz what it *said*. The book's feed stops when Daz no longer has access. Or does it? (Open decision.)

### NPC notes
- **Fheminor:** wants the previous Keeper's will honored and the institution restored [MODULE Ch5 §Restoring Calm]. She worries that Teles will use the vault to buy votes. Her tic: she corrects people's titles.
- **Teles Ahvoste:** wants to read the *wish* spellbook, and he wants to be Keeper. He is an archmage with an ego. He is the only Avowed who might secretly *agree* with keeping the book accessible. His tic: he calls everyone younger than him "child". [OVERLAY interiority]
- **Sylvira:** wants to know what the book says about demons, because she has spent a career on the Infernal Fortress. Underneath that, she wants to know where her son is. **If she asks, "Has anyone seen Moziqodo?", let the silence sit.** [TABLE ch67 NPCs + OVERLAY]

## Scene 3: Two Faces in the Delegation

### Setup
Edvaldo has had minutes alone near the bridge while the Avowed passed him. [OVERLAY]

### Approach
> **GM-only [OVERLAY, with module precedent]:** Edvaldo has taken the shape of **Fembris Lancer**, or of one of the Watchers if Fembris is not present. The module's own suggestion is that the doppelganger "switched for Fembris Lancer" [MODULE Ch5 §Final Confrontation]. He is terrified. He thinks the book is ash, so his goal is **to leave alive with something that will save his life with Manshoon**. The crystals would do. A *real* book would do far better.

Tells the party can catch:
- "Fembris" has stopped talking too much. He is quiet, and Fembris is never quiet [TABLE ch63 063.06].
- He cannot answer a Candlekeep-lore question correctly. He failed twice before [TABLE ch68; ch69 069.06].
- Dawnbringer twitches near him the way she did near the glyphs [TABLE ch69 069.05] (overlay use).

### Branch: if the party exposes him
He bolts. Without the 30-ft curtain the bridge's anti-magic band is narrow. Zalthir has thrown one Manshoon into the lava already and can do it again. Captured alive, Edvaldo becomes the **party's best evidence** for Kalan's Threefold Proof: a living witness to a decade of Manshoon's planning ("Alkrist, the breached wards, and an intended army", [TABLE ch70 070.03]). Killed, he becomes a featureless corpse that *proves* there was a doppelganger.

### Branch: if the party misses him
He leaves with the delegation. Two things follow. (a) If the ash illusion was dropped in front of him, **Manshoon learns the book is real and where it is**. (b) Otherwise Manshoon believes the book is ash, which is a huge strategic gift to the party. Either way, a "Fembris" goes missing later. The real Fembris turns up confused and bound somewhere. [OVERLAY]

### NPC notes
- **Edvaldo:** a decade of planning collapsed in front of him. His stake is survival, and his worry is that Manshoon "does not tolerate failure" [TABLE ch70 070.03]. His small goal is to buy his life with *any* trophy. His tic: academic over-explanation when frightened ("second author on the paper", [TABLE ch69 069.04]).

## Stage directions (timing and silence cues)

- When the first lantern appears, **stop talking**. Let Dawnbringer's light and the lanterns be the only description for a beat.
- When Gyrgum says "I opened the door", give the Avowed **three seconds of silence** before anyone answers.
- If Sylvira asks about her son, **do not fill the silence**. Let a player break it.
- Let the Owlbear interrupt exactly once, at the worst possible moment.
- If Edvaldo is exposed, have the "Fembris" face **melt slowly** while he is still talking. He keeps arguing as his features go.
- If the book speaks, drop your voice and talk to Daz's player only. Make the others ask what it said.
