## Key NPCs

- **Glabbagool** — Intelligent ooze companion traveling with the party; an eighth-level sidekick bonded to Zalthir; hero-worships the party. [ch 059 / npcs; ch 069 / 069.03]
- **Daz** — Drow party member; House T'sarran spy held captive in the party's bag of holding. [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 056 / 056.04]
- **A'lai Aivenmore** — Traitor scholar, now imprisoned in Candlekeep's null-magic prison; traded vault secrets for protection. [ch 067 / 067.02]
- **Manshoon** — Legendary Zhentarim wizard behind eleven years of Candlekeep book thefts; his simulacrum was defeated, swore revenge as he fell. [ch 064 / npcs; ch 070 / 070.02]
- **Edvaldo** — Doppelganger posing as an Avowed Reader; served Manshoon, smashed two prophecy crystals, fled toward the anti-magic barrier. [ch 070 / 070.02; ch 070 / npcs]
- **Tadric** — Kalan's loyal lieutenant; carries one genuine High Tower key; desperate to be heroic, insists on accompanying the party. [ch 064 / npcs]
- **Kalan Strongbranch** — Candlekeep's Gatewarden; reinstated, deputized the party; values his Threefold Proof methodology above all. [ch 062 / npcs; ch 067 / 067.03]
- **Sylvira Savikas** — Tiefling scholar of demons; Moziqodo's mother, unaware the party killed her son; provided two vault answers. [ch 067 / 067.03]
- **Miirym** — The Sentinel Wyrm, ghost-dragon guardian of Candlekeep; true name *Vydykyq* answers the first vault riddle. [ch 068 / 068.04]
- **Alaundo** — Long-dead seer; his recorded voice guards the Obsidian Door; four unplayed prophecy gems remain after Edvaldo destroyed two. [ch 070 / npcs]
- **Daral Yashenti** — Scholar cured by Dawnbringer; promised to research sentient swords; remains close to Thorin for protection. [ch 063 / npcs]
- **Dawnbringer** — Sentient +2 sword wielded by Thorin; lightsaber-like blade; loyal, objects to ritual use. [ch 029 / items; ch 053 / 053.05]
- **Spiderbait** — Goblin companion, survives from the Underdark; travels with the party. [ch 010 / npcs; ch 054 / 054.01]
- **Skoda Vanaster (Bookwyrm)** — First Reader of Candlekeep, Alkrist's aunt; dead, murdered by Moziqodo who took her key. [ch 063 / npcs; ch 065 / NPCs]
- **Moziqodo** — Sylvira's demon-spawn son; killed by the party in one round, after he murdered Bookwyrm. [ch 065 / 065.02; ch 065 / Session-End State]
- **Jimjar** — Deep gnome with divine traits (immune to poison/psychic, can't be charmed); vanished after winking, hinted they'll meet again. [ch 048 / 048.07]
- **Valen** — Young drow scout, fungal-infected, allied with the party, grateful for mercy; terrified of Ilvara who is now dead. [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 051 / npcs]
- **Kaelira Duskryn** — Hired drow mercenary protecting Daz; parted at the surface exit. [ch 053 / 053.01; ch 053 / 053.05]
