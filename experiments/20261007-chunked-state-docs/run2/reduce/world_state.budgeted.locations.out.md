## Locations

- **Candlekeep** — The party is here, appointed investigators of Janussi's murder and the theft of the High Tower keys. Bookwyrm was murdered in her office; her note reads *"He is using the beast to—"* [ch 063 / locations]. The prophecy chant fell fully silent, which "never happens" [ch 064 / locations].
- **High Tower** — Both keys together unlock the room containing the source of Candlekeep's anti-teleportation and fire-suppression wards. The party carries the real key (Tadric had it); A'lai carried a fake [ch 063 / 063.03]. Manshoon's simulacrum bypassed the Security Control Room's protections with a wall of force [ch 065 / Locations].
- **Vault of Secrets** — A hidden chamber beneath Candlekeep holding extraordinary artifacts, protected by a cryptogram and riddles; Manshoon seeks something within [ch 067 / 067.02].
- **The Great Shaft** — A ~1,000-foot drop beneath the Alaundo statue, leading to the Obsidian Tower; the party descended it [ch 068 / locations].
- **The Obsidian Tower** — Three-story structure on an obsidian pedestal above lava; contains the Vault of Dangerous Secrets, a warded library, and an upper chamber holding the means to destroy the collection. Two Manshoon simulacra were fought here [ch 068 / locations; ch 070 / locations].
- **The Obsidian Door** — Seamless black stone door with eleven scorch marks, protected by Alaundo's riddle: a single phrase spoken in darkness by the verified reader. The Inner Book Chamber beyond held the **Book of Vile Darkness** [ch 069 / 069.05; ch 070 / locations].
- **Whorlstone Caverns** — Beneath Gracklstugh; all derro killed, party departed after three days [ch 025 / 025.08].
- **Neverlight Grove** — Consumed by Zuggtmoy's corruption; Sarith feels a compulsion to go there [ch 032 / 032.03; ch 027 / locations].
- **Blingdenstone** — Factions at fragile peace, reopening trade; party was sent to the Overbright to proclaim it "open for business" [ch 048 / 048.06].
