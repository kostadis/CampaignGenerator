## Locations

### Velkynvelve
- A treacherous drow outpost suspended high within a cavern of the Underdark, with hanging towers, rope bridges, extensive webs, spider-silk lines, a guarded slave pen, and platforms far above the cavern floor [ch 002 / locations].
- Includes the **Velkynvelve Waterfall** (water vents through a crack in the cavern ceiling near the eastern wall, pours between stalactites into a twenty-foot-deep pool, then flows into an underground river that travels for miles; Thorin determined a person could slide down safely, making it the party's strongest known escape route) [ch 002 / locations; ch 002 / 002.03].
- Includes **The Lift** (a winch-and-basket mechanism at the edge of the barracks platform, the only obvious safe means of descending from the barracks; supervised by two quaggoths; identified as a target for jamming, freezing, or fire) [ch 002 / locations; ch 002 / 002.02].
- Includes the **Drow Dining Hall and Kitchen** (a cave with four circular zurkhwood tables and five chairs each; holds fresh and dried fungi, dried fruit, cheese, preserved meat, and clay jars of spices; a heavy iron brazier provides heat and dim light) [ch 002 / locations; ch 002 / 002.02].
- Includes **Ilvara's Quarters** (a circular shrine to Lolth with a lifelike ten-foot-tall spider idol with eight polished-jet eyes, heavy silver candlesticks, and a semicircle of pillows; below, Ilvara's living quarters contain a bathtub and a treasure chest, with a passage descending toward Shoor's Quarters) [ch 002 / locations; ch 002 / 002.03].
- The **Slave Pen** is a dark, miserable holding area secured by a door too heavy to break without producing an enormous racket; the drow assume escape is impossible because the prisoners have no weapons or spell ability, but the door was unexpectedly left open [ch 002 / locations; ch 003 / locations].
- The **Guard Tower** is a hollowed-out hanging stalactite tower reached by rope bridges; its lower chamber holds a table, three chairs, a smaller side table, and spider-silk webbing with equipment hooks, and a stairway leads to the armory above; its interior is dimly lit by phosphorescent fungal lanterns [ch 003 / locations].
- The **Armory** is on the upper level of the Guard Tower, holding short swords, six hand crossbows, studded leather armor, rope, and six bags of caltrops; the prisoners' confiscated gear is not stored here [ch 003 / locations].
- The party escaped it during a vrock attack [ch 004 / locations].
- Their escape triggers Blingdenstone's standing order that all Drow escapees be brought before Chief Dorbo Diggermattock [ch 036 / locations].

### Darklake
- A network of underground rivers, tunnels, canals, and flooded caverns stretching over a hundred miles, with ceilings miles high and unfathomable depths; the party travels toward it to find or steal a boat [ch 004 / locations].
- A vast underground body of water with "no waves," whose shallows the Kuo-toa use for ritual processions, and which hides a predator capable of dragging Kuo-toa under [ch 008 / 008.02; ch 008 / npcs].
- Characterized by low ceilings, submerged stone "teeth," dangerous aquatic predators, and refugees; its water is drinkable once clear of the blood [ch 009 / locations; ch 010 / locations].
- Described by the DM as a three-dimensional "Swiss cheese" of passages; home to dangerous flying predators like the stirges that lurked in the cavern ceiling [ch 027 / locations; ch 027 / 027.05].
- The party reached its shores drenched, cold, and miserable after leaving their boat, and safely completed a long rest there with no pursuers [ch 035 / locations].

### The Narrow Tunnel
- A tight, constricting Underdark passage lined with Ormu, a green phosphorescent moss providing dim illumination in a five-foot radius; the party passed through it on the third day and blocked it behind them with Jimjar disguising the blockage so it did not look intentional [ch 006 / locations].

### The Silken Paths
- A subterranean chasm 500 feet deep and 2,000 feet wide filled with an ever-changing network of thick, five-foot-wide spider web strands spun by giant spiders, serving as treacherous bridges; it is a massive junction with many entrances, some of which open and close over time, and its major highways are easily traversed [ch 006 / locations].
- A "treacherous, three-dimensional network of interconnected giant spider webs suspended over deep chasms," traversed by the party and left behind [ch 007 / locations].
- **Travel rules:** The webs are difficult terrain for anyone without a climbing speed; damage or breakage forces a DC 15 Dexterity save with falls of 10–100 feet on a failure; fire breaks strands and forces saves within a 30-foot radius; carrying light attracts hostile creatures [ch 006 / locations].

### Lost Tomb of Khaem
- An ancient floating tomb from the era of Netheril that plunged into an Underdark crevasse after the fall of the Empire of Netheril and its flying cities; it holds the remains of Brysis of Khaem, with a decoy sarcophagus and a secret vault behind the servant's tomb [ch 007 / 007.02; ch 007 / locations].
- A Netherese tomb deep in the Underdark; features a stone diorama of sorcerer Brysis of Khaem, murals of floating cities, a long staircase over a hundred feet, a Netherese calendar stone, a defaced shrine with cracked marble altar, a sarcophagi chamber, and a hidden lower vault [ch 028 / 028.04; ch 028 / locations].
- The **Lower Chamber** is a hidden vault accessed through a hole beneath a rolling sarcophagus, about five feet below; richly pigmented walls with semi-precious stone inlays, a stone bier holding a gilded sarcophagus, and an invisible stone chest [ch 028 / 028.06; ch 028 / locations].
- The party's boat lies sunk offshore at **The Hidden Beach**, the lonely, desolate stretch of shore on the Darklake that Sarith identifies as the landing point for the trek toward the tomb [ch 027 / locations].

### Sloobludop

### Gracklstugh
- A major Underdark city the party intends to reach for equipment and supplies, called "a proper city" by the GM; the party was about ten days out when they met the refugees [ch 009 / locations; ch 009 / 009.05].
- A subterranean Duergar city known for its draconian laws regarding foreigners and the Blade Bazaar; potential sanctuary from Drow pursuit due to the Duergar's lack of affection for the Drow; guards watch the doors rather than the docks [ch 011 / locations; ch 011 / 011.07].

- Characterized by paranoia, invisible sentries, strict literal laws, and a rigid penal code; non-duergar who stay uninvolved are treated as civilians, "not relevant to the city" [ch 013 / locations].
- A massive cavern city home to duergar and the red dragon Themberchaud; the atmosphere is thick with forge heat, the air is foul, and multiple guarded gates control movement between districts; the city sits in a huge cavern but connects onward only through small tunnels, so Themberchaud cannot really leave [ch 027 / locations; ch 027 / 027.03].

- The city remained intact and conducting business after the party's departure, but increasingly tense and held together by controlled chaos, with authorities blaming the Ember Vanguard, the Assassin's Guild rumored returned, unexplained surface-world coins appearing, and derro subjected to summary executions, decapitations, and heads placed on pikes [ch 040 / 040.02; ch 040 / locations].
