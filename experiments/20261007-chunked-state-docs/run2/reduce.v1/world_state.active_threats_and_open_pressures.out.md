## Active Threats and Open Pressures

### Demonic Threats

- **Zuggtmoy** — The demon queen of fungi remains the most persistent supernatural pressure. She was witnessed leading a wedding procession toward a union with Araumycos, chanting "Araumycos and Zuggtmoy joined together heart to heart, becoming one till death do part" [ch 031 / 031.06]. Her intended groom is not yet present, so the ceremony cannot begin [ch 032 / 032.01; ch 032 / npcs]. Since the Pudding King's death her influence has visibly spread: fungi throughout the cavern became "more twisted and malevolent" [ch 047 / 047.10; ch 047 / npcs], fungi grew at accelerated rates in the throne room, and a pale puffball mushroom grew "at impossible speed from a crack in the wall at the departure tunnels" [ch 048 / 048.01; ch 048 / 048.07]. The DM is explicit: "this is not the last you and Zuggtmoy are going to be talking to each other" [ch 048 / 048.07]. She had previously tried to possess Thorin [ch 044 / npcs].
- **Zuggtmoy's plot on the outpost** — She intends to use oozes to "soften the shell" so her garden may "take root in the flesh beneath," and declared "Digest them all. I will claim this outpost" [ch 044 / 044.03; ch 044 / npcs]. She has planted mushroom "children" in the Festering Fissure [ch 044 / npcs]. Her presence in the Festering Fissure adds a third demonic threat on top of Juiblex and the Pudding King [ch 043 / 043.05].
- **Ilvara's corruption by Zuggtmoy** — The now-dead Ilvara had been seduced by the "bride" heresy after visiting the Neverlight Grove, carrying a corrupted mushroom artifact as a conduit [ch 050 / 050.02]. During her final stand she muttered that "the bride" would come to spread chaos and mayhem, and a mysterious icon linked to Zuggtmoy remained near her position, "deemed dangerous to those already touched by her power" [ch 051 / 051.05]. Asha made clear the corrupted mushroom artifact must be destroyed to break Zuggtmoy's influence; if it survives, someone else will pick it up and they are "back to where they started" [ch 050 / 050.02; ch 050 / items].
- **Juiblex** — The demon lord known as the Faceless Lord is likely working through a local proxy rather than appearing personally, since a demon lord could destroy Blingdenstone with little effort [ch 038 / 038.04]. After the Pudding King's death, a demonic voice, possibly Juiblex himself, declared he would be reborn and consume the banquet of the queen of the fungi to grow even stronger [ch 047 / 047.10; ch 047 / npcs]. Jimjar bet the party will need acid-resistant gear to equip an army [ch 035 / 035.04].
- **Demons loose in the Underdark** — The party connected rumors of demons loose in the Underdark, the demons at Velkynvelve, and the demonic encounters since, concluding whoever caused it brought through an avatar rather than the whole being [ch 009 / 009.03; ch 009 / npcs]. The dying spectator's warning that "the demons are coming" proved connected to Demogorgon's rise [ch 007 / 007.03; ch 008 / 008.04]. The spreading demonic presence is "not a localized phenomenon" — far more widespread than the party's scattered sightings suggested; "For what you normally expect outside of the abyss, it is crawling with demons," implying a powerful magical force behind it [ch 015 / 015.02]. An evil demonic force was still trying to kill the party, outside the scope of the mercenaries' contract [ch 053 / 053.01].
- **The two-headed curse** — Despite Narrak's death, his dying words claimed the ritual cannot be undone and someone was "sacrificed," suggesting consequences already set in motion [ch 024 / moments].
