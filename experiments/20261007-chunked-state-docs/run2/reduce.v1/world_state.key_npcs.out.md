## Key NPCs

### The Party's Companions and Allies

- **Glabbagool** — An intelligent gelatinous cube that inexplicably gained sentience, sight, and speech; does not breathe; polite about eating the party after their deaths; joined the expedition [ch 034 / npcs]. Once unintelligent until an unknown event made him think and talk; he considers himself possibly the only talking gelatinous cube in the universe, eats only dead garbage, reports agitated oozes across the region, and can punch as well as absorb things [ch 035 / npcs; ch 036 / npcs]. He desires friends and purpose, cleared rubble as a living bulldozer, hears voices calling him from the ooze cavern, senses the assembled oozes are "not nice," and considers Zalthir a kindred spirit [ch 038 / npcs; ch 038 / 038.02; ch 038 / 038.03]. He was previously affected by demonic power; his acidic body damages anyone inside him and he cannot control it. During the battle with the Pudding King he swapped bodies with the King and remained in the Pudding King's ooze body as a gray ooze with googly eyes after the King's defeat [ch 047 / npcs]. Now a sentient grey ooze formerly a gelatinous cube who accompanies the party, poor at stealth and disguise [ch 050 / npcs]. He stayed back from Whiteshell Mine because of the salt, and encouraged Daz after his missed spells ("don't worry, you'll get better next time") [ch 044 / npcs; ch 044 / moments]. He formalized his apprenticeship and sidekick bond with Zalthir at the Whispering Dome, becoming an eighth-level sidekick [ch 059 / npcs]. Accompanied Zalthir into the battle at Candlekeep, asked about using "the flying spell," and after Zalthir fell remained on top of him, protecting him and positioned to stabilize him [ch 064 / npcs]. Projects earnest hero-worshipping energy; questioned why Manshoon was not dropped into the lava and was impressed by Zalthir's binding speed [ch 069 / 069.03; ch 069 / npcs].

- **Jimjar** — A deep gnome companion with an obsessive gambling habit who cares far more about the thrill of the bet than winning money; he bets even against himself, refuses pointless bets, and keeps track of all his "debts and victories" [ch 006 / npcs]. Comments on duergar's reputation as ruthless slavers from Gracklstugh and bets they would attack while Hemeth was still invisible [ch 010 / npcs]. "Seems to know way more about things than anybody should"; advised using fire or acid against the regenerating aquatic troll [ch 011 / npcs; ch 011 / 011.06]. Theorized the kuo-toa either created a god through mass psychosis or actually summoned Demogorgon [ch 012 / npcs]. Told the party the deep gnomes know a way out (confirmed by Hgraam) and gambled with locals at Ghohlbrorn's Lair [ch 015 / npcs; ch 015 / 015.04]. Still insistent he was not lost, treats danger as fodder for bets and wordplay; settled his "tears" wager with Thorin after the escape [ch 034 / npcs]. Knows Blingdenstone's history, leaders, and dangers in extraordinary detail, reacts painfully to Juiblex's name, and predicts the party will equip an army with acid-resistant gear and recover ingredients from a fungal wedding [ch 035 / npcs]. Made an extraordinary bet in the party's favor with Chief Dorbo, wager that everyone would be "living happily ever after," which helped overcome Dorbo's resistance [ch 038 / 038.06; ch 038 / npcs]. Makes a bet that the party will want to hold onto its collected evidence [ch 024 / npcs]. Has an uncanny ability to show up just when you don't want him to make a bet; he uses a bone die carved from a saint's knucklebone and frames reality as probability; Thorin's Know Your Enemy revealed he is immune to poison and psychic damage, cannot be charmed, frightened, or poisoned, and is resistant to radiant and non-magical physical attacks, suggesting a divine or angelic nature [ch 048 / 048.05; ch 048 / 048.07]. Vanished from existence near the departure tunnels, heading back toward Blingdenstone; winked and vanished after scrutiny, hinting this is not the last they'll meet [ch 048 / 048.07].

- **Dawnbringer** — A sentient, talking magic sword found within Brysis's sarcophagus; she is +2, deals radiant damage instead of slashing plus 1d8 against undead, grants lesser restoration, and ignites as a bonus action; she can speak, read, and understand Common [ch 029 / items]. Intelligent magical sword with a hilt but a blade made of light; dislikes darkness, can force a Charisma save to control her wielder, desires a nicer scabbard appropriate for her light-blade [ch 041 / 041.01; ch 041 / 041.06]. Wielded by Thorin, appears like a flaming lightsaber, famous and impressing local children; expresses her own desires, initially wanting an over-the-top scabbard but pleased with the locking one; the table treated her as genderless this session [ch 045 / 045.04; ch 045 / npcs]. Loyal to Thorin; grudgingly cooperative, objected to ritual use [ch 053 / 053.05; ch 053 / 053.06]. Cured Daral of poisoning; he promised to make sentient magical swords an active area of research [ch 062 / npcs; ch 062 / 062.05].

- **Eldeth** — A dwarf companion who harbors a deep, vocal hatred for the duergar, viewing them as "demonic spawn of dwarven corruption"; Gyrgum's religion check suggested the enmity is as much political as religious, since she belongs to the ruling class [ch 010 / npcs; ch 010 / moments]. Recognized the underground river breaking through the rock and recalled both escape stories and the survivor bias behind them; a "more successful geological assessment" than Thorin's [ch 034 / npcs]. Raised to believe "The only good drow is a dead drow," but now distinguishes drow individuals from the fanatical cult of Lolth and accepts her culture's assumptions may need reconsideration [ch 035 / npcs]. Departed from the party after farewell at the Blingdenstone surface exit, Silver Marches [ch 055 / 055.01; ch 055 / 055.02].

- **Sarith** — A drow prisoner turned navigator who claims to know the Underdark passages, is prideful about navigation, and has a survival bonus of +0 [ch 005 / npcs]. Urges the party to save Eldeth, resists the demon lord's madness, warns that Shuushar's map is imperfect, and claims expertise in questioning from his time among Lolth's own guards [ch 009 / npcs; ch 010 / npcs]. Traveling with the party, infected by Zuggtmoy's influence; he had been unstable when the party met him in prison and grew dangerously drawn to the dancing myconids [ch 025 / npcs]. Feels a strange, possibly demonic compulsion to visit the Neverlight Grove; identified the hidden beach as the path toward the lost tomb and proposed sinking the boat for later recovery [ch 027 / npcs]. At peace on arrival at Neverlight Grove, but infected with unknown spores [ch 029 / npcs; ch 029 / 029.06].

- **Hemeth** — A "neutral arms dealer" Duergar and a prisoner at the altar, he told Gyrgum to "look me up in Gracklstugh" if they survive [ch 008 / 008.01; ch 008 / npcs]. Traveling with the party, claims connections in Gracklstugh ("I know people") and harbors deep distrust of kuo-toa; can turn invisible and demonstrated it while warning the party of duergar invisibility [ch 009 / npcs; ch 010 / npcs]. Claims to "know a guy" who could facilitate the sale of their prisoner [ch 011 / npcs; ch 011 / 011.01]. Previously saved by the party, now providing information about Gracklstugh's culture and laws, easily offended by competition, boasting about his suppliers' craftsmanship, and willing to keep quiet about the party's origins [ch 012 / npcs]. Friendly; willing to keep quiet about the party's origins, offers a discount, offended at being passed over [ch 012 / npcs; ch 012 / 012.04].

- **Brannum Redmarch** — A duergar who attempted to enslave the party but was captured; has very low Charisma, making his lies easy to read; lost his crew to the dangers of the Darklake shortcut he chose; serves as a reluctant navigator under the constant threat of death [ch 011 / npcs; ch 011 / 011.04]. Bargaining for his freedom by offering to use his connections to acquire uncommon magical items from restricted parts of the city; fears being turned over to the duergar [ch 012 / npcs; ch 012 / 012.04].

- **Buppido** — A derro prisoner with severe delusions of grandeur who appears to believe he is divine and interprets captivity and escape alike as elements of his "divine plan" [ch 002 / npcs]. A derro companion with a sinister demeanor, intrigued by the party's cunning and apparently sincere in wanting them to join his divine plan [ch 007 / 007.05; ch 007 / npcs]. Suggests heading to Gracklstugh, resists the demon lord's madness, and lies flat pretending to be invisible during the duergar ambush, insisting it is part of the divine plan; offers "expert" interrogation services with "many techniques" [ch 009 / npcs; ch 010 / npcs]. Believes he is on a divine mission, anxious to leave the party to visit his personal shrine in the caverns near the city, erratic with a history of murderous behavior [ch 012 / npcs]. Dead; he had succumbed to demonic insanity, pre-asylum resenting the Council of Savants as "simply a mechanism of control by the duergar" and coming to see himself on a "mission from God" to free the Derro, summed up as "I am the force of history" [ch 018 / 018.02; ch 018 / npcs]. He murdered and dismembered the deep gnome Pelek about a year ago; his body lies among Pelek's bones [ch 016 / npcs]. Killed by the party [ch 016 / npcs].

- **Pelek** — The ghost of a deep gnome traveler from Blingdenstone, murdered and dismembered by Buppido about a year ago; bound to his bones, calls himself "a very well-adjusted ghost" [ch 016 / npcs]. Cannot interact with physical objects and cannot haunt objects: "Trust me, I tried" [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 018 / npcs]. A spectral ally bound to the party via his bones, resistant to non-magical attacks, and capable of possession; successfully possessed the enemy spy, with the GM declaring "We have a new party member now" [ch 020 / npcs; ch 020 / moment]. Possessing an assassin's body, slept peacefully tied up with silk rope, worried the original host's consciousness might resurface [ch 023 / npcs]. Interred in the Blingdenstone catacombs, spirit passed on; grateful to the party [ch 037 / 037.03].

- **Spiderbait** — A goblin ally, one of the two goblin guides who helped the party across the Silken Paths; the other, Yuk Yuk, has been murdered [ch 010 / npcs]. Traveling with the party to Candlekeep, seeking innocuous work [ch 054 / 054.01]. (See also **Yuk Yuk and Spiderbait** — Eccentric goblins with surfer-dude attitudes, highly skilled at sliding and surfing across web strands and dodging giant spiders; they keep an informal score of who does the coolest thing [ch 006 / npcs]. Yuk Yuk is dead [ch 010 / npcs].)

- **Stool** — A small, sentient myconid sprout who moves only 10 feet, has only four spores, and cannot mind-control drow [ch 005 / 005.05; ch 005 / npcs]. Cheerfully persistent about rescuing his friend Rumpadump, pleading, "just open the cell" [ch 024 / npcs]. Departed with Basidia and surviving myconids; friendly [ch 032 / 032.04].

- **Fargas Rumblefoot** — A halfling adventurer found cocooned in the Silken Paths; claimed his adventuring band was searching for a long-lost tomb when mad gnolls attacked, and he insists he was never infected by the gnolls, whose spawn he described as erupting from living bodies [ch 006 / npcs]. The party reads him as desperate rather than deceitful, and he told Daz to keep away after the examination [ch 007 / npcs].

- **Fuurm and Baedora** — Two former Drow slaves, a human farmer and a human woman; Fuurm entered the Underdark to save his sister stolen from the Overbright, was captured, and escaped during a demonic attack on his captors [ch 028 / 028.01; ch 028 / 028.03]. Both grateful, desperate, taken in by the party [ch 028 / npcs].

- **Valen** — A young, lean drow scout from House T'sarran, sent by Ilvara as an advance scout; his skin is marbled with fungal veins, mushrooms grow from his patchy white hair, and his eyes are a mix of drow red and fungal yellow; disillusioned with Ilvara's madness and fearful of becoming a "sporish thingy-moblop" [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 049 / npcs; ch 049 / moment]. Aligned with the party, grateful for their mercy and healing; terrified of Ilvara [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 049 / npcs].

- **Shuushar** — A pacifist kuo-toa who sought enlightenment, deeply shaken when told what rose was Demogorgon rather than the Leemooggoogoon his people believed they summoned; he left to lead a group of kuo-toa refugees and build a new peaceful society [ch 009 / npcs]. Departed with kuo-toa refugees into the Underdark; friendly [ch 009 / 009.06; ch 009 / npcs].

### Drow Foes and Their Circles

- **Ilvara Mizzrym** — The cruel drow high priestess and commander of Velkynvelve; follows Lolth; treats prisoners with contempt; Thorin found her smug and comfortable in Shoor Vandree's company, and sensed the same disturbing instability he noticed in the other drow [ch 002 / npcs; ch 002 / 002.03]. Sent a patrol to search for and capture the party near Neverlight Grove, reserving the privilege of killing them for herself [ch 031 / 031.03]. Hunting the party across the Underdark since their escape from her prison; had made promises to two powerful people in Menzoberranzan that the party's escape ruined; she has descended into madness communing with a fungal mass she believes is Lolth, is devoted to "the bride," commands a heart fungus creature, can track the party through the pervasive fungi, and discarded her lover Jorlan after he was scarred [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 049 / npcs; ch 049 / moment]. A former high priestess of Lolth corrupted by Zuggtmoy's influence after visiting the Neverlight Grove, visibly fungal, still armed with her cat-o'-nine-tails scourge; carries a small magical mushroom artifact as a conduit for Zuggtmoy's control and can meld into fungal patches and command the heart fungus [ch 050 / npcs]. A high-ranking drow priestess partially corrupted with myconid-like hybrid parts, invoked Zuggtmoy and was particularly vulnerable to radiant damage, which caused her to explode into a twenty-foot cloud of poisonous spores upon death [ch 051 / npcs]. Dead; her belongings and corruption examined posthumously; she had continued receiving and obeying house orders while Zuggtmoy's influence took hold, then disappeared and stopped responding [ch 053 / 053.03].

- **Jorlan Duskryn** — A drow guard whose face was melted and scarred in an encounter with a black pudding, costing him his position as Ilvara's favored lieutenant and lover; Shoor Vandree replaced him, and Jorlan is bitter and vengeful [ch 002 / npcs; ch 002 / 002.05]. A maimed drow warrior and former lover of Ilvara, cast aside after being scarred; he feels dissed by the breakup and, pushed further by the fungus, has turned homicidal toward Ilvara; Valen identifies him as someone who might sabotage her mission out of spite [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 049 / npcs]. A drow warrior and former lover of Ilvara, bitter over their breakup, one of the few drow in the cavern not yet turned into a spore servant [ch 050 / npcs]. Scarred drow warrior, Ilvara's former lover, discarded after being scarred; he broke from the party to hunt Ilvara himself and survived the battle, including a lightning bolt from Kaelira that he partly avoided [ch 051 / npcs; ch 051 / 051.06]. Nym Duskryn identified him as his brother, dismissing him as "the dumber one" before granting Kaelira permission to attack him [ch 051 / npcs].

- **Shoor Vandree** — A drow warrior who replaced Jorlan Duskryn as Ilvara's favored lieutenant and lover after Jorlan was disfigured [ch 002 / npcs]. Present alongside Ilvara; favored lieutenant [ch 002 / npcs; ch 002 / 002.03].

- **Asha Vandree** — A junior drow priestess in Ilvara's camp who remains loyal to Lolth and accuses Ilvara of heresy, insisting "Lolth is not a bride"; obsessively clean and maintains a secret stash of sealed food that has kept her uninfected; appears convinced by the party's false divine vision [ch 049 / 049.05; ch 049 / npcs; ch 049 / moment]. Paranoid and somewhat unstable, holding drow-male mages in contempt; avoids fungal dust, stays beyond 20 feet of Ilvara, and eats her own food; exasperated by being stuck between Ilvara and the bitter Jorlan, who used to have an affair [ch 050 / npcs; ch 050 / moment]. A drow combatant who believes Daz is a follower of Lolth; she denounced the House T'sarran drow as traitors and after Ilvara's death knelt praying and beating beatitudes to Lolth [ch 051 / npcs; ch 051 / 051.06]. A Drow cleric of Lolth who believed herself divinely blessed by Lolth after surviving her crew's ruin, unaware Daz's apparent sign was a deception [ch 053 / 053.02]. Killed by the party after attempting to command them [ch 053 / 053.02].

- **Nym and Kaelira Duskryn** — Professional Drow mercenaries hired in Menzoberranzan by an unknown patron to protect Daz from Ilvara of House Mizzrym and House T'sarran, highly regarded among circles wealthy enough to hire Drow assassins [ch 053 / 053.01]. Kaelira, a specialized drow combatant of House Duskryn under contract with Nym, pulled Daz through Ilvara's insect plague and told him "We only get paid if you live" [ch 051 / npcs; ch 051 / 051.06]. Nym survived Ilvara's Flamestrike and identified Jorlan as his brother [ch 051 / npcs]. Both escorted Daz to the surface exit, then departed; guarded but professionally cooperative toward Daz; Kaelira sardonic toward the rest, Nym dismissive [ch 053 / 053.01; ch 053 / 053.04; ch 053 / 053.05].

- **The Drow Spy** — An agent of House T'sarran assigned to track Daz, not to engage; she refused to give her name, offered her piwafwi as a trophy, and was revealed to be specifically watching Daz (Insight 27 by Daz) so her matron could identify whoever paid to protect him [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / npcs]. Imprisoned in the party's bag of holding under Glabbagool's watch; furious and hostile toward Glabbagool and her captivity [ch 056 / 056.04]. Defiant but demoralized, beginning to wonder at other fates [ch 054 / 054.05; ch 054 / npcs].

### Gracklstugh Powerbrokers

- **Themberchaud** — A pampered red dragon kept to rekindle the city's forges; kept satisfied with a large hoard, but the Keepers periodically replace aging dragons with new ones raised from eggs (purchased or stolen) when they become too difficult to control [ch 013 / 013.05; ch 013 / npcs]. The Wyrmsmith of Gracklstugh, a massive, overweight red dragon with lava-colored scales and bright yellow eyes; paranoid about invisible spies, contemptuous of Bahamut and brass dragons, approves of Drow for "conspiracies and secrecy," and pleased by agents with no one to blackmail [ch 014 / 014.06; ch 014 / npcs]. Believes he is in charge, though his keepers disagree; upset over the stolen egg [ch 015 / npcs]. A dragon whose potential replacement is the very egg the party is tracking; the Keepers of the Flame do not want him to know about the egg's existence [ch 019 / npcs]. A "fat, blimped-out, overly pedantic red dragon" whom the egg was meant to replace before he realized he was not truly in control of Gracklstugh; the party is his agent as long as he doesn't notice them [ch 026 / 026.06]. A vain, out-of-shape young adult red dragon who serves as a "glorified lighter" for the city's forges; he styles himself "father of the flame" and "bringer of the flame," believes the party are his agents, and knows little about where his "vast network" should be looking [ch 027 / npcs; ch 027 / 027.02]. In his lair in Gracklstugh, after departing the docks; believes the party are his personal agents on a vital mission [ch 027 / npcs; ch 027 / 027.04].

- **Gartokkar Xundorn** — High-ranking Keepers of the Flame member and liaison between Themberchaud and Duergar leadership; threatened the party with fabricated charges if they refused his mission and believes the Gray Ghosts caused the psionic disturbance [ch 014 / 014.07; ch 014 / npcs]. Linked to the stolen red dragon egg; the Keepers want the party to recover the egg from the Gray Ghosts, but the party never committed [ch 015 / npcs]. The intense, politically driven head of the Keepers of the Flame; wants political leverage against the Gray Ghosts and fears news of the egg's survival could strip away his leverage over Themberchaud [ch 017 / npcs]. Pragmatic, somewhat condescending, secured a dragon egg to maintain control over the city's power source, paying handsomely for discretion [ch 026 / npcs]. Satisfied and transactional; paid the party 2,400 gp, considered his "reputation and my position in the kingdom" secured by the egg [ch 026 / 026.06; ch 026 / npcs].

- **Errde Blackskull** — Captain of the Stone Guard, the party's patron; skeptical the Empty-Scabbard Killers exist ("I don't believe in this secret organization, but I do believe that people are using the secret organization to advance other agendas"), but two members of her own clan died mysteriously, one whose body she found [ch 014 / 014.02; ch 014 / npcs]. Praised Daz as leadership material: "That is the kind of thinking that is needed to lead these kinds of operations" [ch 018 / 018.03]. Looking for physical evidence of a demonic conspiracy involving the Council of Savants; she would always prefer real evidence to evidence she had to manufacture [ch 022 / npcs]. Expected to pursue the party; a guard was sent to inform her of the party's presence and deception [ch 027 / npcs].

- **Grimholl Forgebrand (Hulg)** — Errde's subordinate who found bodies with no visible wounds, no magical residue, and no signs of trauma that clerics couldn't revive; he firmly believes psionic assassins are responsible [ch 014 / 014.02; ch 014 / npcs].

- **Deepking Horgar Steelshadow** — Reportedly gone mad with paranoia and increasingly arbitrary laws, "the kind of madness that you see when a person in power gets disconnected from society" [ch 013 / 013.05]. The ruler of Gracklstugh who "used to be much calmer," now paranoid, erratic, "always in a rush," distant from old allies, and asked Hgraam about the Gray Ghosts; his crackdown has driven factions underground [ch 015 / 015.02; ch 015 / npcs]. The mad ruler of the Duergar city who secretly supports an illegal Assassin's Guild; his city keeps part of its population enslaved, captures slaves, and is "run by a mad king"; he is withholding materials the gnome kingdoms want [ch 021 / 021.02; ch 021 / npcs]. (Note: the code-built table lists him as **Deepking Tarngardt** [ch 021 / 021.02; ch 021 / npcs], a name conflict with the earlier **Horgar Steelshadow** [ch 013 / 013.05].)

- **Hgraam** — The thoughtful, patient Stone Giant clan leader of Clan Cairngorm in Gracklstugh; speaks in omens and "what the stones tell me," advised the Deepking toward patience and generosity, and calls the surface world "the Dreamlands" [ch 015 / 015.02; ch 015 / npcs]. Uncharacteristically talkative for his kind, seeks to cure his brothers of a magical curse, and closed his cavern to outsiders while consulting the stones [ch 026 / npcs]. Grateful to the party; paid them 500 gp and closed his cavern to outsiders while beginning the cure [ch 026 / 026.04; ch 026 / npcs].

- **Shal** — The Deepking's new favored advisor, never visited Hgraam, suspected to be the cause of the King's recent paranoia and isolation [ch 015 / npcs]. Never visited Hgraam; suspected to influence the Deepking's paranoia; unencountered by the party [ch 015 / 015.02; ch 015 / npcs].

- **Droki** — A Derro courier with a pale face and a tentacled floppy hat; moves with magical speed and talks about himself in the third person while delivering messages between factions [ch 015 / npcs; ch 015 / 015.05]. Running his daily circuit in the Whorlstone Tunnels [ch 016 / npcs].

- **Gorglak** — A Duergar captain about four feet tall with ashen gray skin, scars, and tattoos, wearing a heavy iron chain with a clenched-fist pendant symbolizing loyalty to the Deepking; suspicious, authoritative, and open to bribery [ch 026 / npcs]. Hostile-but-bribed; took the party's 20 gp bribe and let them pass with parting warnings [ch 026 / 026.02; ch 026 / npcs].

- **Werz Saltbaron** — Duergar merchant with suspicious knowledge of assassins ("They are professionals. They're paid to do a job"); admits he may have helped clans sell goods "on the down low" [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 018 / moment]. Wants the assassins off his back; offers better trade deals with the gnomes [ch 019 / npcs].

- **Eldgrim** — A powerful duergar soulblade and leader within the Empty-Scabbard Killers, pragmatic and business-minded, susceptible to flattery regarding his fame, backed by several more soulblades; "tougher than your average Soul Blade" [ch 019 / npcs]. Angered by the fire, banished the party from his territory under threat of death; took a liking to Thorin's bluntness; dismissive of proof [ch 019 / npcs].

### The Derro and Demonic Threats

- **Plinki** — A derro savant obsessed with mutating a stolen red dragon egg using dark rituals into a two-headed pet and eventual grand mount for Demogorgon; also works on the obelisk [ch 017 / npcs]. A fanatical female derro mad scientist dedicated to Demogorgon, using a massive black obelisk to infuse a stolen dragon egg with demonic energy; easily swayed by Gyrgum's deception but turned violently hostile upon revealing the ritual requires the beating heart of a cleric of Bahamut; her AC is 13 [ch 019 / npcs]. Killed by Zalthir's breath attack [ch 020 / npcs; ch 020 / 020.01].

- **Narrak** — A zealous derro cult leader and the obvious head of the crew running dark experiments in the brimstone cavern, who guards the knowledge in the trillimac cap books jealously and declared that his work serves the elevation of the derro, the triumph of Demogorgon, and the destruction of the duergar [ch 022 / npcs; ch 022 / 022.06]. A derro leader and Council of Savants member who refuses to curse ("I don't curse. I just invoke demonic powers."), speaking in rhyming couplets [ch 023 / npcs; ch 024 / npcs]. Slain by the party after his dying words claimed the ritual could not be undone [ch 024 / npcs].

- **Ulnara** — A derro savant and fervent cultist of Demogorgon who carries out macabre experiments on the undead, is borderline insane, and responded to gratuitous violence with deference, fearfully calling Daz "your lordship" because of his drow nature [ch 022 / npcs; ch 022 / 022.02]. Turned fawning and deferential, calling Daz "your lordship," praising the name Ember Vanguard, and letting the party pass [ch 022 / 022.02; ch 022 / npcs].

- **Zubriska** — A leader-type figure among the psionic savants, always accompanied by four bodyguards; the group meets near the secret passage toward the obelisk [ch 017 / npcs].

- **The Pudding King** — A small, insane gnome who sits on a slime-covered throne facing east, believes himself to be the king of Blingdenstone, and controls hundreds of oozes; he is the source of the disembodied voice invoking the Faceless Lord [ch 038 / 038.04; ch 038 / 038.06]. A former gnome tunnel worker commanding the oozes beneath Blingdenstone; Chipgrin knew him before his transformation and knows where he can be found; his motives blend vengeance, pride, madness, and resentment [ch 039 / 039.06; ch 039 / npcs]. An insane gnomish figure who monologued to his two ooze henchmen, claiming the queen of rot (Zuggtmoy) thinks she'll take his kingdom but Juiblex promised it to him, boasting that Blingdenstone's "pathetic defenses" would be overwhelmed and it would become "Blingdenpuddle"; he transformed into ooze form when grappled, in which form he automatically hits his grappler [ch 046 / 046.09]. A former humanoid who transformed into a sentient, telepathic ooze after embracing his connection to Juiblex, the demon lord of oozes; commanded an army of oozes, used slime waves (a cone that knocked targets prone and was DC 15 Dexterity), hurled green slime anywhere within his lair, was immune to being knocked prone, started the fight at 85 hit points [ch 047 / npcs]. Dead — dissolved into inert puddles in the throne room [ch 047 / 047.08]. After his defeat, a demonic voice claimed Juiblex would be reborn by consuming the banquet of the queen of the fungi [ch 047 / npcs].

- **Zuggtmoy** — The Demon Queen of Fungi, Lady of Rot and Decay, an alien being whose sole desire is to infect the living with spores to transform them into mindless servants and eventually into "decomposing hosts for the mushrooms mold and other fungi that she spa[w]ns" [ch 025 / 025.04]. Embodies death and virulence, rejecting decomposition's role in renewal, preparing to mate with, take on, or possess Araumycos through a ritual of incomprehensible power [ch 032 / npcs].
