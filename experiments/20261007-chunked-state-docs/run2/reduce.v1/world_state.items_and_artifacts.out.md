## Items and Artifacts

### Major Weapons of the Party
- **Dawnbringer** — A sentient sunblade of late Netherese origin wielded by Thorin, identifying as female [ch 031 / items; ch 056 / 056.03]. A plus-two weapon dealing radiant damage and an additional 1d8 against undead, able to cast lesser restoration once per day, emitting genuine sunlight in a fifteen-foot radius, speaking with a kind feminine voice, and suffering intense anxiety about darkness and abandonment [ch 031 / items]. She compulsively illuminates darkness because she does not like the dark, and cannot be polished; a two-phase therapeutic process (light, then mourning) has been started with Candlekeep's scholars [ch 056 / 056.03; ch 056 / 056.06]. She insists "I'm not afraid of the dark, I just don't like the dark" [ch 053 / 053.05]. She dealt repeated radiant blows to Moziqodo and struck the final critical blow that killed him [ch 063 / 063.05]; used two-handed to strike Jorlan for 14 damage, applying sap [ch 050 / items]; dealt nineteen damage to the rocktopus and channeled Lesser Restoration [ch 032 / items]; used to cast lesser restoration on Valen, halting his fungal infection and shrinking his growths once per long rest per Thorin's belief [ch 049 / 049.03]. Not made of metal but described as a "light weapon" like a "lightsaber," hence invulnerable to gray ooze corrosion [ch 034 / items]. Housed in a **Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard** — a custom scabbard with a locking mechanism hooking her in since she "doesn't quite have a blade"; purchased by Thorin for 180 gold after discount [ch 045 / 045.04]. Referred to in Alaundo's prophecy as the "bright blade" [ch 070 / 070.03].
- **Eldritch Claw Tattoo** — Zalthir's magic tattoo, activated to manifest tentacles before being thrown into the throne room and used to grapple the Pudding King from reach [ch 046 / 046.09]. Also manifests tendrils of eldritch darkness that strike from fifteen feet, grapple, and deal force damage; used to seize Manshoon and drag him into the anti-magic zone [ch 069 / 069.01]. Originally sought to enhance his unarmed strikes, with Brannum claiming a contact may procure it [ch 012 / items].
- **Javelin of Lightning** — A magical javelin wielded by Gyrgum, hurled at Moziqodo after careful repositioning but narrowly missing [ch 063 / 063.05].
- **Short Sword +1** — A magical short sword recovered in the Traders' Grotto aftermath, taken by Daz as an upgrade over his quarterstaff [ch 038 / items].
- **Drow dagger** — A magical plus-one dagger found by Glabbagool in the fountain and absorbed; the party cannot recover it until it passes through him [ch 034 / 034.06; ch 034 / items].
- **Spiritual Weapon** — A fifth-level spectral sword summoned by Gyrgum that struck Manshoon for nineteen damage and remained hovering for following rounds [ch 069 / 069.01; ch 069 / spells].
