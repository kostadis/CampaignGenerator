- Daz rolled a 20 on group intimidation (Thorin failed); the Derro mistook the ember-like lights for a meteor swarm and became convinced they faced a 20th-level drow archmage and his elite retinue [ch 021 / 021.04].
- The party let the four Derro guards go alive to spread the legend of the Ember Vanguard [ch 021 / 021.04].

- Gyrgum was driven to a single hit point, using his orcish ability to drop to 1 instead of 0, and failed his escape roll with a 9 [ch 021 / 021.06].
- Daz fired magic missiles into the Trapper and cast Silvery Barbs and Toll the Dead; the Trapper resisted [ch 021 / 021.06].
- Thorin landed heavy longsword blows, including a Hill Strike for 4 extra damage and a Sap mastery giving the creature disadvantage [ch 021 / 021.06].
- Zalthir seized the Trapper with his bare hands in a counter-grapple, landing repeated strikes, and beat the creature to death, releasing a battered and acid-covered Gyrgum [ch 021 / 021.06].
