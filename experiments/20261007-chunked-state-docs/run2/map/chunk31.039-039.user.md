### 039.05 Negotiating with Chief Chipgrin

The party found Chief Chipgrin seated atop a rise carpeted with soft, well-manicured green moss. His stone chair was draped with a cave badger pelt, and he was flanked by two giant rats and numerous guards. Chipgrin immediately assumed that the Diggermattocks had sent the party to drive his clan out. Daz instead explained that the party wanted to escape the Underdark and had learned that helping Blingdenstone defeat its ooze infestation would secure that aid.

Daz offered to arrange the conversation Chipgrin had long wanted with Chief Dorbo, but imposed strict conditions: the meeting would be for cooperation, not political maneuvering, positioning, recrimination, or opportunities to say “I told you so.” Otherwise, the party would leave and allow the gnomes to deal with the oozes themselves. Chipgrin remarked, “I've been told that Drow are a difficult group to negotiate with, but you take it to the next level.” Daz replied, “It's not a negotiation. I'm here to ask you for your help.”

Chief Chipgrin agreed to join the fight but refused to place the Clan Goldwhisker under Dorbo's command or participate in a “grand crusade” designed to send his people to slaughter. Daz clarified that the party would formulate the plan and make the operational decisions. He personally accepted responsibility if Goldwhisker fighters died because of those choices, even acknowledging that Chipgrin could seek vengeance against him afterward. Daz had no ambition beyond eliminating the threat and leaving Blingdenstone. The blunt arrangement satisfied Chipgrin, producing a moment of “shock and awe” among his listeners before he agreed to attend a broader meeting of the clan leaders.

### 039.10 Quest Selection and Party Division

The party seriously considered ignoring the preparations and trusting their magical “snatch and grab” plan. As a monk who did not wear armor, Zalthir was less worried about corrosion than the armored combatants, but everyone recognized that the gnome army depended heavily on its equipment. They also worried that allowing the entire army to be slaughtered would reflect poorly on them. Thorin settled the cautious-versus-reckless question with a d20: above ten meant safety, below ten meant “YOLO.” He rolled a 17, selecting caution.

The group therefore chose to cleanse the Rockblight first, earning both elemental support for the battle and the Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals. They would also revisit Neverlight Grove for the acid-protection ingredients. They explicitly rejected the Gracklstugh option because no one knew what condition the city was in or how the party would be received there.

To maximize their efforts, the party assigned Glabbagool, Eldeth, and Jimjar to assist Uth-Jadger and the other Burrow Warden ghosts while the main adventuring party handled the Rockblight and Neverlight Grove. Their initial joking description of this assignment as “fight the ghosts” was corrected to helping them, though the party compared the arrangement to defeating creatures in order to recruit them in a game.
