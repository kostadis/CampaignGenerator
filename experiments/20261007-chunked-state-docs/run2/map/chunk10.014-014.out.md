## Events
- The party reviewed their recovered gear (bedroll, rope, tinderbox, torches, waterskin) and used their 175 gold-piece store credit, good only at that store for common potions and scrolls priced from "Sane Magical Prices," to buy a week's rations and healing potions [ch 014 / 014.01].
- Gyrgum wanted scrolls of Thunderwave and Heroism but learned he "can only cast spells that are on your spell scroll on your class spell list," so he bought a scroll of Guiding Bolt and reserved the rest of his funds; the shopkeeper was disappointed she got "the two who don't need any magic, who don't need any weapons" [ch 014 / 014.01].
- The party ended with nine healing potions (two already on hand, two each from Daz and Thorin, three added by the GM), held jointly; Daz was left with 105 gold [ch 014 / 014.01].
- Captain Errde Blackskull of the Stone Guard pulled the party aside and asked them to investigate rumors of the "Empty-Scabbard Killers" (also Forak-Erach-Naek), a supposed order of psionic assassins; she introduced her subordinate Grimholl Forgebrand (nicknamed Hulg), who described finding bodies with no visible wounds that clerics couldn't revive [ch 014 / 014.02].
- Errde said the deaths of two of her own clan members made the matter personal; she had found one of the bodies herself, and snapped when Gyrgum asked who found it; she believed corrupt forces were using the myth to cover their tracks and pointed the party toward the Whorlstone Tunnels beneath Gracklstugh [ch 014 / 014.02].
- The party took a long rest in a shared bunk-bed room costing five copper pieces per person per night at the lodging where their crew had been put up, with Daz down to five hit points [ch 014 / 014.03].
- In the morning, Jimjar urged heading to Blingdenstone, noting "We could just get up and leave today," but Daz insisted on visiting the Keepers of the Flame first, arguing "I think gathering intelligence is just as important as stocking up," and the party agreed [ch 014 / 014.03].
- The party noticed the Topsy-Turvy twins had slipped away during the night; Zalthir asked "Were they the wererats?" and the GM noted they "haven't" necessarily left for good; Buppido still looked cranky but everyone's health seemed fine [ch 014 / 014.03].
- At the end of a pier on the Darklake Docks, two masked, hooded, previously invisible assassins shimmered into view and stabbed a Duergar merchant named Werz with glowing blades; the party charged to intervene, and Gyrgum dubbed the group "Firefly Tactics" [ch 014 / 014.04].
- Daz dealt nine damage with magical bolts; Zalthir conjured a 15-foot sphere of magical darkness, missed on a natural 1, then spent a focus point to attack again and killed one assassin outright [ch 014 / 014.04].
- Within the darkness, Thorin used a Precision Attack superiority die (advantage from blindsight) for six damage, Gyrgum struck with radiant energy for two damage via True Strike, and Daz finished the last assassin with Scorching Ray at disadvantage from four and six damage [ch 014 / 014.04].
- The assassins' glowing blades vanished upon their deaths, revealing them to be psionic weapons; the party was disappointed, Thorin declaring "God damn it. Come on. It's a psychic blade. This is bullshit, man." [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 014 / moment].
- Thorin cautioned before helping Werz that "He might be a bad guy, and the guys we just killed might be the good guys"; Gyrgum healed Werz with a prayer, and Werz thanked them and invited the party to meet him at the Shattered Spire for a reward, claiming he had "no idea why I was targeted by assassins" [ch 014 / 014.04].
- The party searched the assassin bodies and found eighteen silver pieces, a trinket, bloodstained gloves (not magical), and a box of scented candles, plus a fish-skin parchment with a charcoal likeness of Werz, confirming he was a deliberate target [ch 014 / 014.05].
- Gyrgum pushed the corpses into the water and the party slipped away before the Stone Guards arrived; they took a short rest, during which Daz used Arcane Recovery to regain a second-level spell slot [ch 014 / 014.05].
- The party, escorted by Duergar priests on the strength of their permit, traveled through the Flowstone District, territory "nobody but Duergar," to meet Themberchaud, the Wyrmsmith of Gracklstugh, who announced himself with "You didn't say I was having surfacers for dinner tonight" [ch 014 / 014.06].
- In his lair, Themberchaud breathed fire in all directions ("Anyone who's invisible will be turned to ash") to root out invisible spies, then settled onto a mountain of gold and interrogated each party member, dismissing Gyrgum's devotion to Bahamut with "Next!", mildly disdaining Zalthir's brass dragonborn heritage, approving of Daz's Drow origins from Menzoberranzan, and delighting in Thorin's rootless backstory ("Nobody who can black, nobody to be blackmailed. That's wonderful.") [ch 014 / 014.06; ch 014 / moment].
- Themberchaud, without waiting for acceptance, declared the party his personal agents, summoned his liaison Gartokkar, and gave each a golden badge bearing his profile; their first mission was to do whatever the Keepers ask but report back to Themberchaud first [ch 014 / 014.06].
- Gartokkar revealed the Keepers' power "derive in equal measure from our psionic abilities, the leadership of the Deepking, and our influence over Themberchaud," and that the dragon did not know he was being groomed for replacement via a stolen red dragon egg taken by the Gray Ghosts [ch 014 / 014.07].
- Gartokkar insisted the old dragon would be "set free" rather than disposed of, described a great psionic disturbance in the Underdark he believed the Gray Ghosts caused (the party's lore checks failed, Daz rolling a natural 1), and tasked the party with tracking down the derro messenger Droki, who carries messages and supplies for the Gray Ghosts [ch 014 / 014.07].
- Gartokkar gave the party a holy symbol of Laduguer and gold pins bearing Themberchaud's profile (brought by an acolyte), granting passage beyond the Darklake District, and threatened that the Stone Guards would "invent an appropriate crime" if they refused [ch 014 / 014.07].
- Thorin provocatively suggested the Stone Guard should know about the stolen egg, alarming Gartokkar ("Theft of the egg would be a disaster... Oh, for whom?"), and refused to commit to returning the egg to the Keepers, saying "If we find the egg, we'll decide what to do then," prompting Gartokkar to call him "a funny person," followed by banter about heights [ch 014 / 014.07; ch 014 / moment].
- The party realized they now operated as agents of the dragon, servants of the faction replacing him, and investigators for a Stone Guard captain hunting the same derro; Thorin floated escaping "in a blaze of glory on the back of a dragon" [ch 014 / 014.07; ch 014 / moment].

## Concluded
