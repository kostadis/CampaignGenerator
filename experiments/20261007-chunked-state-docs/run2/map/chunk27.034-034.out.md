## Events

- Jimjar, the deep gnome guide, defended his navigational error through the Underdark as an expected consequence of shifting passages and claimed their current location was a convenient shortcut to Blingdenstone; Thorin predicted, “Yeah, this will end in tears,” and after clarifying the wager's terms (“there will be some unfortunate event before we get back on our way”), the bet was accepted [ch 034 / 034.01].
- The apparent route forward was the only open one, the alternate passage blocked by extensive rubble; the group briefly struggled to remember Blingdenstone as their destination, and Thorin chose the left branch at the next intersection by a die roll [ch 034 / 034.01].
- In a 10-foot-high glistening passage, Thorin refused to step into the pool fearing “creepy crawlies,” then discovered the water was rising; Daz determined the entire complex was leaking and would fill completely, due to a tremor cracking an underground reservoir or river into the temple [ch 034 / 034.02].
- The party rushed into the flooded square constructed chamber; a gray ooze emerged and Zalthir stepped forward and destroyed it with two powerful unarmed blows (10 then 12 damage) before anyone else could act [ch 034 / 034.02].
- Beyond the chamber, the party discovered the remains of a drow, a dark metal mace, and scattered coins held suspended inside a gelatinous cube with two visible eyes, which greeted them (“Well, hello, hello, hello.”) and introduced himself as Glabbagool, a recently sentient former ordinary cube [ch 034 / 034.03].
- Thorin invited Glabbagool to join the expedition, promising companionship, adventure, and food; Glabbagool accepted after asking, “You won’t be upset if you all die and I eat you, right?”, with the condition that he allow natural causes to do the killing [ch 034 / 034.03].
- Gyrgum tossed Glabbagool a lucky foot from his backpack as a welcoming gift, which he absorbed and declared tasty; the party declined to feed him ghost bones because the associated ghost reacts angrily [ch 034 / 034.03].
- In a narrow corridor, Thorin hurled darts at a gray ooze (two hits for 8 each), while Glabbagool's ten-foot body completely blocked the passage, separating Thorin from the party; Daz then cast Toll the Dead for 18 necrotic damage, destroying it (Thorin withdrew first, keeping armor out of reach) [ch 034 / 034.04].
- The party continued with the water rising “sort of like a bathtub that doesn’t drain well,” Jimjar asking whether the “tears” wager could be declared a mulligan on account of Glabbagool; Thorin was willing to call the bet a draw [ch 034 / 034.04].
- At a junction with four western passages and one eastern route, Thorin used a d5 die to pick a western passage, fell into a concealed pit but caught the edge and hauled himself out (“aided substantially by his strength”) [ch 034 / 034.05].
- A black pudding climbed from the pit; an Arcana check associated the ooze concentration with the demon lord Juiblex, and the party remembered black puddings can split when struck with certain weapons, so they retreated to a larger chamber and destroyed it with ranged attacks; Glabbagool eagerly consumed the remains [ch 034 / 034.05].
- Reaching a temple chamber with a stone fountain, broken statue (only clawed stone feet recognizable), seven niches (one admitting water through a crack, two empty, four with formless sculptures), and skeletons in the chamber, the party accepted Glabbagool's offer to scout (“Well, you know, the oozes can’t go through me.”) [ch 034 / 034.06].
- When Glabbagool touched a formless sculpture, it materialized into a gray ooze, prompting his complaint, “Well, that’s rude,” and Zalthir rushed in and punched it apart; the party then activated the remaining sculptures one at a time, Zalthir destroying each ooze, Glabbagool absorbing the debris until the floor was clean [ch 034 / 034.06].
- Glabbagool searched the fountain and discovered 112 silver pieces, three gold pieces, a drow dagger, and a vial of oil of slipperiness — then absorbed all of it, which the party joked made him “a cube of holding” whose contents they would have to wait days to retrieve [ch 034 / 034.06].
- The fountain room did not provide the exit, so the party retraced its path and took the eastern route; near a rough-hewn wall they heard pouring water, and streams entered through cracks in the 10-foot-high ceiling, with water rising roughly one foot per hour [ch 034 / 034.07].
- Jimjar bet that a massive quantity of water waited behind the stone; Thorin pointed out nobody had accepted that wager; Eldeth examined the site, recognized the signs of an underground river breaking through, and recalled stories of escape through such breaches, adding grimly, “I didn’t hear about all the people who did that and drowned anymore.” [ch 034 / 034.07].
- Daz determined Shape Water could affect a five-foot cube, last an hour when non-instantaneous, and support two maintained effects at once; with the only alternative escape hatch suspected to be just more oozes and a dead end, the party planned a moving, ten-foot-long air-pocket refuge [ch 034 / 034.07].
- Daz created successive water-free pockets by redirecting water to the cube's edges while the party beat the rising water; Glabbagool could not fit but said, “Oh, that’s totally fine. I don’t breathe,” also promising, “If any of you were to perish, I will absolutely grab you and digest you.” [ch 034 / 034.08].
- Glabbagool caused more of the ceiling to collapse, doubling the water inflow and revealing the diverted underground river; the party, protected by Daz's air pockets, endured the inundation and swam upward approximately 30 feet to surface in a large cavern [ch 034 / 034.08].
- Jimjar declared, “See, it did not end in tears,” while Thorin acknowledged shenanigans and promised Jimjar ten silver pieces once they emerged from Glabbagool (possibly “a little slimy”); Jimjar insisted, “I found us a shortcut, right?”, and the party conceded the shortcut either found them or the reverse, and that they had gained a friend [ch 034 / 034.08].
- The party gained a level for surviving the Oozing Temple, reaching level 6 [ch 034 / 034.08; ch 034 / end].

## Concluded

- The bet between Thorin and Jimjar over whether the path would “end in tears” was settled: the party conceded there had been shenanigans, Thorin agreed to pay Jimjar ten silver pieces (once they passed out of Glabbagool), and Jimjar reframed the whole detour as a convenient shortcut [ch 034 / 034.01; ch 034 / 034.08].
- The flooded Oozing Temple was escaped: the party broke through the damaged rock into the diverted underground river, swam upward through a passage, and surfaced in a large cavern, ending their time in the flooding complex [ch 034 / 034.08].
- The first gray ooze encounter was defeated: Zalthir destroyed it with two unarmed strikes before the rest of the party was committed [ch 034 / 034.02].
- The second gray ooze encounter was defeated: Daz destroyed it with Toll the Dead for 18 necrotic damage [ch 034 / 034.04].
- The black pudding was defeated via kiting: the party retreated to a larger chamber, kept the slow creature out of melee range, destroyed it with ranged attacks, and Glabbagool consumed the remains [ch 034 / 034.05].
- The fountain chamber's four gray oozes were all destroyed: the party activated the formless sculptures one at a time, Zalthir punching each to death [ch 034 / 034.06].
- The party reached level 6 as the direct result of surviving the Oozing Temple [ch 034 / 034.08].
