## Events
- The party investigated the body of Janussi, the Keeper of Tomes, in his chamber, finding his rib cage smashed by an axe-like weapon and his heart removed from his chest, with blood strangely localized around the wound [ch 058 / 058.01].
- Daz conducted a meticulous examination, uncovering clotted blood beneath the victim's nose and smeared ink stains along the right sleeve of his tunic [ch 058 / 058.01].
- Daz noticed a dark, blackened substance on Janussi's left forefinger, lower lip, and tongue, darker than the blue ink he used for notes; Glabbagool attempted to identify it using his knowledge of Underdark substances but failed his Intelligence check (6) [ch 058 / 058.01].
- The party theorized Janussi had been licking his finger to turn pages and ingested poison applied to a book [ch 058 / 058.01].
- Gyrgum's medicine check confirmed Janussi's nose was fractured and bruised before death, since bruising cannot occur after the heart stops, placing a violent altercation hours before the killing [ch 058 / 058.01].
- The party discovered a chipped front tooth, with the fragment found a few feet from the body, suggesting Janussi fell face-first upon death and was then rolled over by whoever removed his heart [ch 058 / 058.02].
- Daz identified three roughly circular impact wounds on the back of an armchair — two punched clean through, scattering horsehair stuffing — as the signature of magic missiles [ch 058 / 058.02].
- The party examined a wall-mounted safe left open, finding one sapphire and one scrap of parchment missing from among spell component pouches, while a fortune in diamonds, platinum, and gold was left untouched [ch 058 / 058.02].
- Beneath the victim's neck lay broken lead-chain links, remnants of a locket forcibly torn away [ch 058 / 058.02].
- The party found a silver pedestal with a finely carved ebony wood pipe and cherry-flavored tobacco, which may have been used to mask the scent of poison [ch 058 / 058.02].
- Tadric explained that a fallen three-foot marble statue of a hooded, bearded man holding a candle was likely knocked over by the Great Reader A'lai Aivenmore, who discovered the body at dawn [ch 058 / 058.02].
- Thorin's question about shaking the candle caused Tadric to become visibly distressed, fixating on the candle as if it might awaken the statue [ch 058 / 058.02].
- The party found a gift tag in the wastebasket written in a "sour, cramped hand" reading: "To Janussi, read it and weep, you old snob, a proper Deadwinter gift, your loving rival." [ch 058 / 058.02].
- On the desk, the party found two sets of notes written in blue ink — one annotating a comedic book called *The Golden Ass*, and another with musings on unfamiliar spells from *The Discoveries* [ch 058 / 058.03].
- The party realized *The Golden Ass* was missing from the room despite Janussi having recently taken notes from it [ch 058 / 058.03].
- Daz theorized the missing book might be a key to a cipher or code, possibly hiding arcane instructions within its jokes [ch 058 / 058.03].
- Zalthir noted the heavy tobacco smoke in the room might have been used to mask the scent of poison; the DM confirmed Janussi kept his chamber full of tobacco smoke despite periodically needing magical treatment for lung cancer [ch 058 / 058.03].
- Their assistant Fembris Lancer identified Daral Yashenti as Janussi's most bitter professional rival — the same man who had dismissively called the party "sell swords" upon their arrival [ch 058 / 058.04].
- The DM clarified that Fembris is a male half-elf assistant assigned to the party by Bookwyrm (spelled with a Y), the green dragonborn First Reader, and warned that Fembris will repeat everything the party says [ch 058 / 058.04].
- The party descended to the apartment of Hollypocket, the elderly gnome housekeeper, who received them with herbal tea and a flood of carefully observed gossip [ch 058 / 058.04].
- Hollypocket recounted that Kalan Strongbranch had stormed out of Janussi's office with a face like thunder, nearly knocking the tray from her hands [ch 058 / 058.05].
- Hollypocket recounted that Teles Ahvoste visited around the ninth bell, with the meeting descending into shouting, including Teles yelling "hypocrite!" before storming out [ch 058 / 058.05].
- Hollypocket recounted that Daral Yashenti visited around the eleventh bell while hungover, shouting that Janussi was "not fit to be Candlekeep's Keeper" and calling him "nothing but a glorified book peddler" [ch 058 / 058.05].
- Hollypocket recounted that Daral returned later that night, drunk, pounding on Janussi's door until admitted, then having another loud argument before fleeing down the stairs [ch 058 / 058.05].
- Hollypocket described a violent confrontation with the tiefling scholar Sylvira Savikas, whom she called the "devil woman," during which Sylvira screamed that Janussi was a traitor before storming out [ch 058 / 058.05].
- The DM clarified that "devil woman" is being used as a description of Sylvira's work studying demons and devils, not as an insult [ch 058 / 058.05].
- Hollypocket reported hearing a loud thud from the chamber above shortly after the midnight bells, but did not investigate as Janussi did not like to be disturbed after eleven [ch 058 / 058.05].
- Hollypocket claimed to have seen a vision of Sylvira Savikas's face glowing red in the candlelight around two or three in the morning, going downward — notable because Sylvira is a tiefling with darkvision and would have no need for a lantern [ch 058 / 058.05].
- Hollypocket added that Sylvira and Janussi had a volatile, cyclical romantic relationship — "the hookups were explosive, the breakups were explosive" [ch 058 / 058.05].
- Hollypocket confirmed Janussi always wore a locket on his person, never removed it, and it was "intimate" [ch 058 / 058.05].
- Daz used the Stonespeaker Crystal to communicate with Queenie, the tower's resident five-year-old female cat, while Zalthir and the others spoke through him [ch 058 / 058.06].
- Queenie revealed that Sylvira Savikas blasted an armchair with magic missiles on the morning of Janussi's final day during what appears to have been a lover's quarrel with him [ch 058 / 058.06].
- Queenie described seeing Fheminor Scrivenbark, the "short-legged one," arrive at the chamber in the evening, followed later by Daral, the "stinky breath one" [ch 058 / 058.06].
- Queenie recalled seeing Daral flee the room carrying a "hypnotic block," which the party identified as the missing book, *The Golden Ass* [ch 058 / 058.06].
- Queenie mentioned seeing a figure that looked like Sylvira leaving the tower going downstairs, but noted the person did not smell like her, suggesting a magical disguise or shapeshifter [ch 058 / 058.06].
- Fembris informed the party that Janussi possessed one of the two keys to the High Tower Library and that Kalan Strongbranch holds the other, and the two keys may be required together to access it [ch 058 / 058.06].
- Queenie ended the interview with a haughty dismissal: "You may now leave, and please give yourself a proper licking before returning to my tower." [ch 058 / 058.06].
- The party believed the locket torn from Janussi's neck contained one of the two keys to the High Tower Library, and suspected the murder, the missing book, sapphire, parchment, and locket were connected parts of a targeted scheme rather than an ordinary robbery [ch 058 / 058.06].
- Daz earned an Inspiration point for referencing Milo's Murder Mystery as in-world research that informed his investigation technique [ch 058 / moment].
- Gyrgum deduced that Janussi's nose was broken before he died, narrowing the window of the attack [ch 058 / moment].
