## Events
- The party pressed deeper into the Underdark and on the third day entered a narrow tunnel whose walls shimmered with a soft, eerie green glow from a moss Jimjar identified as Ormu [ch 006 / 006.01].
- The group realized their food and water were dangerously low and spread out to forage, gathering ten gallons of water, enough for all ten travelers (four adventurers and six allies) for one day [ch 006 / 006.01].
- Daz used Shape Water to freeze the collected water into one-gallon blocks since the party had no containers, and refroze them every hour; the ice would melt overnight, requiring a fresh water search each morning [ch 006 / 006.01].
- Zalthir raised a rules question from last session about missing stealth checks during a chase escape; the GM confirmed "that was a mistake" and thanked him for catching it [ch 006 / 006.01].
- Food foraging gathered about seventeen pounds of food, covering the ten pounds the group needed for the day with roughly eight pounds to spare; only two allies turned up anything [ch 006 / 006.02].
- Eldeth took charge of covering the group's tracks with party help, and her roll of 21 dropped the pursuit level from four to three [ch 006 / 006.02].
- The GM clarified the chase rules: a true chase begins only when pursuit reaches five, and the hunt ends when it reaches zero [ch 006 / 006.02].
- Jimjar identified Ormu as a moss that grows in warm, damp places, sheds dim light in a five-foot radius, and can be harvested and dried into glowing powder or pigment; when asked if Ormu paint would help them hide, he said no, "but it would mean that we looked better" [ch 006 / 006.01; ch 006 / 006.02].
- The party harvested some Ormu [ch 006 / 006.02].
- The party discussed blocking the narrow passage with a boulder or collapsing it to delay pursuit, and the GM noted a blockage would buy time but wouldn't stop someone very determined [ch 006 / 006.02].
- The party decided to block the tunnel behind them and disguise the blockage so pursuers would not realize it was sealed on purpose, which raised the question of who would do the deceiving [ch 006 / 006.03].
- Jimjar refused to help without a wager: he asked for five gold pieces, Zalthir countered with a pound of food which Jimjar valued at a single copper, and since Zalthir had vowed never to gamble again due to a past gambling problem (as Daz explained), Gyrgum took the bet instead [ch 006 / 006.03].
- Jimjar, who bet against himself, disguised the blockage successfully, which meant he lost the bet and now owes Gyrgum five gold pieces he does not have; Gyrgum admitted afterward he didn't even know which side of the bet he was on [ch 006 / 006.03].
- The party realized Jimjar did not bet to win but because "For him, if there's no bet, what's the point of doing it?", and Zalthir tested this by betting Jimjar couldn't fly to the roof of the cave, which Jimjar declined, explaining that anyone can decline to do something stupid [ch 006 / 006.03].
- That night, Daz noticed the deep gnome twins Topsy and Turvy huddled together talking quietly, but his insight check revealed nothing about their intentions [ch 006 / 006.03].
- After two more days of travel, on the fifth day Thorin led a search finding ten gallons of water; with Zalthir's help and a strong second roll from Gyrgum, the group gathered twenty pounds of food, with ten extra for tomorrow [ch 006 / 006.04].
- Sarith, who had been guiding them, broke into barely contained excitement before an enormous chasm five hundred feet deep and two thousand feet wide strung with a shifting network of enormous spider webs, saying "Yes, I'm very excited. This means we're in the right direction. All we have to do is cross this cavern." [ch 006 / 006.04].
- Sarith explained the place was known as the Silken Paths: the major highways were easy to travel, but old webs disintegrate and giant spiders are always spinning new ones, so the paths are always changing, and his advice amounted to "cross your fingers" [ch 006 / 006.04].
- Just as Sarith was about to step onto the web, two goblins — Yuk Yuk and Spiderbait — popped up and introduced themselves, offering their services as guides through the Silken Paths for two gold pieces per day [ch 006 / 006.04].
- Daz told the goblins the party was "destitute and without funds," and Gyrgum mentioned that Jimjar owed him five gold, at which point Jimjar began listing all of his debts, "both public and private" [ch 006 / 006.04].
- Thorin proposed the goblins prove they could cross, and Gyrgum suggested Jimjar bet them; Jimjar did, with a persuasion roll of 15, and a very annoyed Yuk Yuk countered with a bet of twenty gold pieces that he could get "from here to over there" [ch 006 / 006.05].
- Gyrgum's insight (14) told him Jimjar would not be paying, but he played along, told the goblins Jimjar was honest and would pay when he had the money, and offered to sign over Jimjar's five-gold IOU as well [ch 006 / 006.05].
- Yuk Yuk and Spiderbait put something strange on their feet and proceeded to slide and surf across the strands, dodging spiders with impressive agility, and won the bet [ch 006 / 006.05].
- Jimjar cheerfully conceded "yep, you won the bet," then admitted he had no money to pay; Yuk Yuk was very annoyed [ch 006 / 006.06].
- Spiderbait proposed a new deal: the goblins would guide the party in exchange for a share of future gold to cover their fee plus the twenty gold Jimjar now owed them, reasoning "the bet was entertaining and fun. Plus, we need to follow you guys to get the money." [ch 006 / 006.06].
- The party agreed, gaining Yuk Yuk and Spiderbait as guides through the Silken Paths, and the GM described them as "two goblin surfer dudes," with the goblins keeping an informal score of "who does the coolest thing" [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 006 / 006.06].
- The goblins explained the party would not be surfing ("that's advanced techniques. We'll just walk") and that the webs could not be burned down: a crazy wizard had tried, but there were simply too many spiders rebuilding faster than he could burn [ch 006 / 006.06].
- Thorin set the terms plainly: if the spiders attacked, "the deal's off because you haven't delivered what you promised" [ch 006 / 006.06].
- The party tied themselves together with rope (advantage on the save to avoid falling), went without light to avoid attracting predators, and set off along the five-foot-wide strands over the void [ch 006 / 006.06].
- Along the way the goblins sang about web surfing and brought up the time their friend Bobby was eaten by a spider, a detail quickly shouted down: "Dude, why are you bringing us down, man?" [ch 006 / 006.06].
- The party spotted a moving cocoon in the webbing, and Stool used his rapport spores to establish a connection, revealing a voice: "My name is Fargas Rumblefoot. Can you please get me out of here?" [ch 006 / 006.07].
- Fargas said his adventuring band had been searching for a long-lost tomb when a pack of mad gnolls attacked; he described gnolls as demon-possessed creatures that implant their spawn in living bodies where it later erupts; he had escaped and fled deeper into the Underdark, only to be caught by spiders and "placed here as a snack," insisting he was not infected [ch 006 / 006.07].
- Thorin answered, "That's what I used to say in the zombie apocalypse," and the party debated whether Fargas could be infected, with Daz and Zalthir floating the idea of leaving the cocoon behind [ch 006 / 006.07].
- Yuk Yuk warned "dudes, I have a really bad feeling about this, but I think there are some spiders that are coming here," and Fargas played his last card: "I know the location of the tomb, and more importantly, I know the location of the treasure." [ch 006 / 006.07].
- The GM ruled the cocoon was AC 10 with five points of slashing damage to cut; Thorin drew his short sword and cut through it in one stroke, "the first good roll I've gotten since we started playing," freeing Fargas [ch 006 / 006.07].
- Initiative was rolled with Thorin at 17 and Zalthir at 5 as the spiders closed in [ch 006 / 006.07].
- The GM drew the major web strands on the map: each strand was five feet wide, gaps were empty space, and cutting a strand dropped the segment between two junctions [ch 006 / 006.08].
- Zalthir stepped to a junction and cut one segment, forcing the pursuing spiders to spin a new bridge before they could follow, then struck a second strand which frayed but held by a single hit point [ch 006 / 006.08].
- Daz floated an Indiana Jones-style plan to cut everything and swing to safety, and Thorin suggested crossing one bridge and severing all the rest; the GM realized aloud he had "created a graph problem for a bunch of computers" [ch 006 / 006.08].
- The session paused mid-combat with Zalthir's bonus action still waiting for the next round and the far edge of the chasm still very far away; the group agreed to resume after New Year's [ch 006 / 006.08].

- [LOCATION] **Silken Paths travel rules** — The webs are difficult terrain for anyone without a climbing speed; damage or breakage forces a DC 15 Dexterity save with falls of 10–100 feet on a failure; fire breaks strands and forces saves within a 30-foot radius; carrying light attracts hostile creatures [ch 006 / locations].
