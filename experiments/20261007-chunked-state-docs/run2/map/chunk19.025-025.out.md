## Events
- The party took stock of their haul inside the Whorlstone Caverns: a two-headed dragon egg, proof of derro demonic alignment, proof the Deepking allied with assassins, proof Themberchaud is "Themberchaud the 28th," and a ritual statue tied to the stone-giant second-head affliction [ch 025 / 025.01].
- The party discussed what to do with their evidence; Jimjar advised holding onto everything for future leverage, Zalthir proposed a magical dead man's switch, and Thorin suggested visiting the merchant they had helped but worried about betrayal [ch 025 / 025.01].
- Stool threw a tantrum, threatening to go alone to find his friend Rumpadump, and tried to win Thorin over by declaring him "much nicer than Zalthir" [ch 025 / 025.02].
- Zalthir objected to visiting Rumpadump, recalling infected creatures including "a massive ape creature that looked not particularly friendly"; Stool reassured the party that spores require death before zombification and that myconids only calm and talk [ch 025 / 025.02].
- Daz recalled myconid lore: peaceable walking mushrooms used as drow target practice, spores that stun within 5 feet, communication within 20 feet, and bludgeoning fists [ch 025 / 025.02].
- Gyrgum cast Aid on three party members, granting 5 temporary hit points for eight hours, and the party followed Stool deeper into the caverns [ch 025 / 025.02].
- The party arrived at a natural cave where several myconids danced frantically, with two large ape-like creatures covered in yellow mold and mushroom growths hunched nearby [ch 025 / 025.03].
- Sarith became agitated upon seeing the dancing myconids and lurched toward them wanting to join; Zalthir grappled him and pulled him back around the corner [ch 025 / 025.03].
- Rumpadump appeared calm and studious; Stool erupted with excitement ("I found you. I found you. I found you."), and Rumpadump said "something is not right here" [ch 025 / 025.03; ch 025 / 025.04].
- Rumpadump explained that a myconid named Voosbur released strange spores back in the Neverlight Grove, after which the group walked through some fungi and materialized in the Whorlstone Caverns [ch 025 / 025.04].
- Rumpadump avoided madness by not eating "the silly mushrooms," which proved to be the same growy-shrinky mushrooms the party had gathered; Stool disputed this theory [ch 025 / 025.04].
- Rumpadump urged the party to help him return to the Neverlight Grove, noting the Myconid Sovereign might know a way out of the Underdark [ch 025 / 025.04].
- Sarith began speaking about a Dark Lady calling to him and showing visions; the party's arcana checks failed, but Gyrgum's religion check of 23 identified the Dark Lady as Zuggtmoy, the Demon Queen of Fungi, Lady of Rot and Decay [ch 025 / 025.04].
- The party theorized Sarith was infected by Zuggtmoy's influence before they met him in prison, and noted it seemed "entirely possible that Zuggtmoy is also walking around" after seeing Demogorgon's influence [ch 025 / 025.04].
- The party approached the infected myconid Voosbur, who spoke euphorically about the Lady's dream and invited them to "drop in, tune out, and dance with the spores" [ch 025 / 025.05].
- Thorin, after a die roll, took Voosbur's outstretched hand and entered the vision; Gyrgum followed as the "designated driver," while Daz declined and Zalthir stayed back holding Sarith [ch 025 / 025.05].
- Thorin and Gyrgum found themselves in a bioluminescent spore glade where glowing fungi pulsed brighter when touched [ch 025 / 025.06].
- They passed into the Vine Wood, a maze of slime-covered sentient vines that began advancing; with a group stealth roll of 7, the pair ran and barely escaped as the vines collapsed behind them [ch 025 / 025.06].
- They emerged into a grim clearing with a fungal throne and mushroom sentinels, where a spectral Zuggtmoy appeared and offered the gift of joining her "endless mycelium" forever [ch 025 / 025.07].
- Thorin felt keen temptation but refused the offer, saying he had too many responsibilities in the waking world; Zuggtmoy lamented and the vision dissolved [ch 025 / 025.07].
- Voosbur told Thorin "Hey, man, I guess you didn't want to join the party. I got to go now," and he and the infected myconids stepped through a spore cloud and vanished [ch 025 / 025.08].
- The party took a long rest and rose to fifth level; Daz gained Fireball, and Thorin's longsword became +8 to hit with an extra attack [ch 025 / 025.08].
- After three days underground, the party emerged to find Gracklstugh's gates sealed, guarded by grim Duergar; the drow on the near side were even more on edge [ch 025 / 025.08].
- A guard named Gorglak blocked the party and demanded to inspect all of their possessions for contraband, ending the session on a cliffhanger [ch 025 / 025.08].
