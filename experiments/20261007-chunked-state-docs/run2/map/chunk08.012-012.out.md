## Events
- The party arrived at the duergar city of Gracklstugh by boat across the Darklake, tying up at the Darklake district where non-duergar are permitted, and were met by heat, smog, forge-light and din [ch 012 / 012.01].
- Hemeth warned the party that non-duergar are restricted to the Darklake district, that duergar guards can turn invisible, that theft is punishable by immediate death without trial, and that escaped slaves risk being repossessed or sold back to the drow; Thorin objected they were only "temporarily detained" [ch 012 / 012.01].
- The party set their goals in Gracklstugh: resupply with better weapons and armor and find information leading out of the Underdark, with the known route running Gracklstugh to the lost tomb on the dwarf's map, then to Blingdenstone as a possible exit; the party is level 3 [ch 012 / 012.01].
- At the Blade Bazaar, Gyrgum approached merchant Dulgir, who insulted him until learning he was buying, then adopted a posh accent and gave a ten percent discount on a mace in exchange for Gyrgum's short sword; Gyrgum also bought a hand axe and picked up a light crossbow [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 012 / npcs].
- Zalthir recalled a mysterious list of names found while cleaning, learning one name belongs to someone in Gracklstugh and that the people on the list may have connections to the drow slave trade, whether as suppliers or worried family members being unclear [ch 012 / 012.02].
- The party offered their captive Brannum Redmarch his freedom in exchange for using his underworld connections to procure uncommon magical goods from restricted parts of the city; Brannum eagerly agreed, insisting the deals be "done within the context of a free exchange of goods and services and not under duress" [ch 012 / 012.02].
- Hemeth, offended at being passed over, offered his own high-quality weapons at a discount boasting his handaxes would be easily reused by whoever killed their owner; he can source a +1 weapon himself but uncommon magic items must be named specifically [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 012 / npcs].
- Zalthir expressed interest in an Eldritch Claw tattoo, and Brannum claimed he may know a contact who can procure it, requesting two days; the party can shake its pursuers in the city for now [ch 012 / 012.02].
- Gyrgum purchased scrolls of Detect Magic and Identify from a merchant who punctuated his sales pitch with sudden, violent threats before continuing as if nothing happened; a first-level spell scroll costs about 60 gold pieces [ch 012 / 012.02].
- Daz sought a magical light crossbow imbued with True Strike for accuracy but was told nothing like that is sold in the bazaar; he decided against buying anything, saying "I'm happy with what I have and I need to level up before anything makes sense" [ch 012 / 012.02].
- Thorin asked for heavy armor, confirmed available in the Darklake district, and settled on darts as his ranged weapon after confirming the game isn't using pistols [ch 012 / 012.02].
- The party debated Buppido's fate—release, kill, or turn him over—with Gyrgum as a neutral good cleric unwilling to slay him in cold blood and Zalthir noting Buppido knows the tomb's location and could point the drow pursuers at it [ch 012 / 012.03].
- At Zalthir's and Gyrgum's suggestion, the party had Buppido eat a Tongue of Madness mushroom, after which he unleashed a five-day backlog of petty insults against every party member in one long, unbroken stream of consciousness [ch 012 / 012.03; ch 012 / items].
- Jimjar theorized the kuo-toa either created a god through mass psychosis or actually summoned Demogorgon, dismissed the idea that Buppido could summon a demon lord, and lamented he should have bet on Buppido screaming at him to stop betting [ch 012 / 012.03; ch 012 / npcs].
- Zalthir asked whether Buppido might summon another horror like the kraken the kuo-toa raised; the party considered marooning Buppido in the Underdark when they leave, and Jimjar and the companions were asked to guard Buppido while the party shops [ch 012 / 012.03].
- After the tirade, Zalthir concluded they could simply cut Buppido loose once they are done with the city [ch 012 / 012.03].
- Gyrgum noticed unsettling behavior in the Blade Bazaar: a duergar merchant consulting a non-existent twin brother, merchants quoting wildly different prices for the same goods, and one merchant turning completely invisible mid-transaction while continuing to speak; Gyrgum rolled Insight (15) and sensed "there's something off" [ch 012 / 012.04].
- Gyrgum stepped outside the bazaar and looked around just as a two-headed stone giant burst through one of the district's gates, howling with madness and lashing out, sending a duergar soldier's broken body crashing near the party and barreling straight at Gyrgum [ch 012 / 012.04; ch 012 / 012.05].
- Thorin recognized the giant's chaotic violence as extremely abnormal, since stone giants are normally deeply taciturn [ch 012 / 012.05].
- Gyrgum rolled Investigation (15) to test for illusion after a pebble struck his foot, concluding it was either real or an illusion so convincing the difference is immaterial [ch 012 / 012.05].
- Gyrgum brandished his holy symbol at the giant as it barreled toward the party; the GM confirmed they can fight it and that duergar troops will arrive, and helping the duergar might earn goodwill while fleeing might get them blamed [ch 012 / 012.05].
- Daz opened the engagement with Magic Missile dealing 15 damage, and Zalthir rushed into melee range, conjuring a sphere of magical darkness that swallowed the giant and blinded it [ch 012 / 012.05].
- Thorin exploited the giant's blindness, striking with his short sword with advantage for 6 damage and holding his Action Surge in reserve; Gyrgum channeled radiant energy dealing 11 damage after the giant failed a DC 13 Constitution save [ch 012 / 012.05].
- The giant lashed out blindly at Thorin with disadvantage, rolling a 25 (+9) for 10 bludgeoning damage, but Zalthir deflected the blow, a reaction reducing damage by 1d10 plus a modifier, so no damage was taken [ch 012 / 012.05].
- A squad of duergar city guards arrived, two acting at initiative 10, ready to join the fray [ch 012 / 012.05].
- The party continued assaulting the giant with Magic Missile for 9, Zalthir hitting for 11 and again with a focus point, Thorin using a feinting attack for 9 and then 7, and Gyrgum hitting with a crossbow bolt for 9; the session stopped with the duergar guards up next [ch 012 / 012.05].
- Hemeth and Brannum went off on their errand together, marked by an arrow on the map [ch 012 / 012.04].
- The party told its six traveling companions to wait in the district, with Hemeth willing to keep quiet and Buppido eager to leave [ch 012 / 012.02].
