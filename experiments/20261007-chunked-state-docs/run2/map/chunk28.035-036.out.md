- Glabbagool explained gelatinous cubes reproduce by leaving a smaller cube behind, cannot do so rapidly, and take a long time to grow; he had once left a smaller cube behind out of loneliness, but it could not speak, and he sadly concluded "I think I'm the only one in the entire universe that can speak" [ch 035 / 035.04].
