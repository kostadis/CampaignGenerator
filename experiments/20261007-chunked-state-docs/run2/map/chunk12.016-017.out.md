- The next session, the party finished off the centipede swarms; Thorin used his new Sentinel feat, Gyrgum channeled radiant energy through his hand axe with True Strike, Daz fired magic missiles, and Thorin delivered the killing blow to the last cluster [ch 017 / 017.01].
- Gyrgum cast Prayer of Healing on Thorin, Daz, Zalthir, himself, and the ghost, granting each a short rest's benefit plus an additional 2d8 healing, allowing the party to keep moving [ch 017 / 017.02].
- The party peered into a natural cave and saw three myconid adults and several sprouts dancing frantically to a silent tune, accompanied by two quaggoth spore servants covered in mushroom growths and yellow mold; they chose to leave it undisturbed [ch 017 / 017.03].
- A flumph appeared and spoke into the party's minds; it said it feeds on the psionic energy of evil creatures such as aboleths, gith, and mind flayers [ch 017 / 017.04].

- The flumph shared locations: the Assassin's Guild with a mind flayer it feeds on, the Council of Savants, and a leader-type figure named Zubriska always surrounded by four bodyguards [ch 017 / 017.04].
- The flumph lamented that its once "hippy-dippy" home had been corrupted since the faerzress blinked out and back some six or seven months ago; the flumph declared it would accompany the party [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 017 / npcs].
- The party left the Whorlstone Tunnels and returned to the Darklake District of Gracklstugh; the ghost stayed invisible to avoid panicking locals [ch 017 / 017.05].
- The party went to the Keepers of the Flame and met Gartokkar Xundorn, who pulled them aside into a separate chamber; the flumph projected an image of the egg into his mind [ch 017 / 017.05].
- Gartokkar tasked the party with retrieving the egg, a small statuette of Demogorgon, and any incriminating paperwork, which combined with the egg's inscriptions could persuade the Deepking to "wage unholy war" on the Gray Ghosts [ch 017 / 017.05].

- The party raised a confidentiality rider to keep the egg secret from Themberchaud; after a flash of murderous madness, Gartokkar agreed, muttering "What Themberchaud doesn't know doesn't hurt Themberchaud" [ch 017 / 017.05].
- Thorn demanded double for the discretion, Daz hinted at other bidders, and Gartokkar conceded an extra 1,000 gold; the upfront terms settled at 1,100 gold split evenly at 275 apiece, plus official badges for the party's companions [ch 017 / 017.05].
- Gartokkar delivered a fierce threat, enumerating every cave, rock, tunnel, and pool in the Underdark where the party might think to hide should they fail to deliver [ch 017 / 017.05].
