- The party descended through the waterfall into the pool, avoiding the giant spider-infested webs. [ch 004 / 004.02]
- A gray ooze emerged from the water and struck Thorin for twelve damage, but the party outran it after Eldeth warned its acid could consume armor. [ch 004 / 004.03]

- The vrock menaced Daz with its beak but flew off; the party fled into the Underdark tunnels. [ch 004 / 004.03]
- The party took a long rest, then learned Underdark travel paces: eight miles per day fast, six normal, four slow. [ch 004 / 004.04]
- Sarith stepped forward as navigator and appeared truthful about knowing the passages. [ch 004 / 004.04]
- The party learned of the Faerzress, a magical energy suffusing parts of the Underdark that gives dim light, resists divination, and disrupts teleportation. [ch 004 / 004.04]

- A sudden rockfall collapsed the tunnel ceiling, knocking Daz unconscious, dropping Gyrgum to zero hit points (kept conscious by Relentless Endurance), and trapping Buppido and others. [ch 004 / 004.06; ch 005 / 005.01]
- Zalthir and Thorin avoided the collapse and organized the rescue; Zalthir freed Daz first, Thorin freed Gyrgum, and Stool pulled Shuushar and Buppido to safety. [ch 004 / 004.06; ch 005 / 005.01]
- A carrion crawler erupted from the earth during the rescue; Zalthir blasted it with fire breath, the companions volleyed crossbow bolts dealing 20 damage, and the party killed it. [ch 005 / 005.02]
- Eldeth and Jimjar were dug out mid-fight at Thorin's call, "dwarves stick together." [ch 005 / 005.02; ch 005 / moments]
- The party harvested the carrion crawler carcass, yielding nine rations (about two days' food) that tasted like chicken. [ch 005 / 005.02; ch 005 / items]

- About an hour into the march, Daz and Zalthir detected drow scouts from Velkynvelve trailing the party; Zalthir recalled that killing scouts would not stop the main force. [ch 005 / 005.04]
- The party fled; Thorin carried Stool Yoda-style, Stool released rapport spores for silent telepathic communication, and Zalthir found a hiding alcove. [ch 005 / 005.05]
- The party's stealth check at advantage held against DC 12, the drow scouts lost the scent and doubled back down a different corridor. [ch 005 / 005.05]
- The party traveled at fast pace for days; Sarith nearly led them astray in the Faerzress tunnels, and Gyrgum corrected the course. [ch 005 / 005.06]
