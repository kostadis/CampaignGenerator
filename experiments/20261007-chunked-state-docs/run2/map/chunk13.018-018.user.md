### 018.01 A Tense Meeting with Errde Blackskull

At the start of the session the GM posted a corrected map. One area previously marked as Mind Flayer territory is actually where the cult of Demogorgon and the assassins keep fighting each other. The magic items the party had ordered would not arrive until the next morning. Gyrgum judged himself "not in bad shape," so the party went to the Stone Guard straight away.

The party made their way to the Stone Guard headquarters to meet with Errde Blackskull, the captain of the Stone Guard. The ghost was invisible again, and the flumph came along, "very impressed with all the things that it's seeing and very concerned, but very excited." Errde received them with a thinly veiled attempt at charm that barely concealed her seething intensity. She demanded to know what useful information they had brought her.

The group learned several things about Errde:
- She was desperate for documented proof that the Gray Ghosts and the Council of Savants were involved in sinister conspiracies.
- She secretly wanted evidence of the Assassin's Guild targeting her clan, and wanted to know who had been giving the orders to kill her clan members.
- She refused to openly admit the guild existed, because a guild operating "under her nose" reflected poorly on her authority.
- What she really wanted was evidence she could take to the Deepking to get sign-off on a "pacification mission" against the Derro. When Zalthir asked which kind, the GM said "the more violent one."

The party also came to understand that both Errde and the Keepers of the Flame knew the answers they all sought lay deep within the Whorlstone Caverns. They had known Droki would lead them there. Neither faction could send its own people in without setting off the Derro's hair-trigger paranoia. The Derro, once slaves of the Duergar, had also learned to build specialized traps that catch even invisible intruders.

### 018.02 Political Deliberations in Gracklstugh

The conversation turned to the broader political landscape of Gracklstugh, and the party found themselves weighing a grim set of options.
- **The Deepking** had grown increasingly erratic, and the party wondered aloud whether some "evil vizier" was behind it. His decision to grant all weapons contracts exclusively to his own clan, Clan Steelshadow, had bred deep resentment across the city.
- **Themberchaud the red dragon**, whose fire was essential to the city's industrial output, had been convinced by his handlers in the Keepers of the Flame that he was the real power in the city. He had lately "started to grow up" and noticed things might not be as they seemed. That was why Gartokkar had become so frantic about the dragon egg. Without Themberchaud, the Keepers of the Flame might face "a layoff."

The whole power structure of Gracklstugh felt like a house of cards in a strong wind. Daz pointed out that the party had never actually seen evidence the Derro were behind any of this. Thorin warned against giving Errde "air cover to go on a slaughter rampage." The party debated whether their best move was to gather evidence, play the factions against one another, or simply arm themselves and flee. The GM reminded them that leaving did not mean never coming back, but also that the Keepers of the Flame had formally threatened permanent hostility if betrayed. Unfinished business and unpaid contracts kept pulling them back toward the depths. Their only truly paid job was to find out what was going on with the egg, retrieve it, and investigate the Council of Savants.

Beneath his smooth merchant's charm, however, Daz perceived that Werz was buttering them up for a favor. ### 018.07 A Secret Offer from Clan Ironhead

As a final stop before descending back into the depths, the party visited Clan Ironhead under the pretense of establishing their cover as dissidents against the Deepking. Thangus Ironhead, the clan's leader, publicly dismissed the dangers of the Whorlstone Tunnels as nothing more than stories told to frighten children. Thorin replied, "Okay, we'll go play there."

As the party turned to leave, his granddaughter Grinta Ironhead pulled them aside and spoke far more candidly. She acknowledged that the city was a powder keg and that her clan had suffered greatly under the Deepking's trade monopoly. She said that if the Deepking were unseated, Clan Ironhead — which would rise to become the premier clan — would offer generous trade terms to those who had helped bring about his departure. Jimjar's eyes lit up, and he noted quietly that such an arrangement would be enormously beneficial to the people of Blingdenstone.

With new weapons, new allies, new secrets, and a growing web of political entanglements, the party prepared to descend once more into the darkness of the Whorlstone Caverns. The next session's goal is to get Werz removed from the assassins' list.
