## Events
- The party interrogated their duergar prisoner, Brannum Redmarch, on the Darklake after securing a larger boat; Thorin made clear his survival depended on guiding them faithfully to Gracklstugh [ch 011 / 011.01].
- The party decided Brannum would ride in their own boat as a hostage, tied up; Gyrgum warned he would be "the first to go" if he tried to betray them [ch 011 / 011.01].
- Sarith admired Thorin's direct interrogation technique but expressed disappointment that torture wasn't used [ch 011 / 011.01].
- Thorin floated selling Brannum into slavery; Buppido called it an excellent idea and Hemeth said he "knew a guy" [ch 011 / 011.01].
- An insight check of 18 confirmed Brannum was telling the truth about the directions, because he knew staying alive depended on it [ch 011 / 011.01].
- On the fifteenth day of travel, the party encountered a diamond-shaped limestone islet with an eight-foot pyramid of bones topped by a horned ram skull; Brannum panicked, saying "this was a mistake. I thought we were going the other way" [ch 011 / 011.02].
- A violent beam of lightning erupted from behind a stalactite and struck at the party's boat; a group check kept the boat seaworthy with some damage [ch 011 / 011.02].
- A ten-foot beholder zombie rose from behind a stalactite; Gyrgum's Command spell failed because the undead doesn't understand his language [ch 011 / 011.02; ch 011 / 011.03].
- Zalthir conjured a 15-foot sphere of magical darkness around the beholder zombie, keeping part visible for targeting, and struck it with unarmed blows [ch 011 / 011.03].
- Thorin fought from inside the darkness with advantage, used a feinting attack, and tried Hill Strike, but learned the zombie is immune to being knocked prone [ch 011 / 011.03].
- Daz cast Magic Missiles for 15 damage; Gyrgum used Divine Spark. The zombie hit Gyrgum for 10 damage and poisoned him, and paralyzed Daz with a ray [ch 011 / 011.03].
- Stool, Hemeth, and the companion crew rowed to the fight and fired crossbow volleys; the second volley, combined with Zalthir's assault, destroyed the beholder zombie, which exploded into ash and freed Daz from paralysis [ch 011 / 011.03].
- Gyrgum cast Cure Wounds on Zalthir, restoring 15 of the 16 hit points lost to the zombie's bite [ch 011 / 011.03; ch 011 / 011.04].
- Brannum confessed this route was a shortcut his crew used, where two of his boats had vanished; a survivor had spoken of "some kind of evil altar" [ch 011 / 011.04].
- Daz peered into the water and discovered the sunken wrecks of Brannum's lost boats on the lakebed, with bodies moving among them, possibly zombies [ch 011 / 011.04].
- Gyrgum cast Mending to repair the boat hull and Find Traps on the islet, which found no traps; the party never set foot on the islet [ch 011 / 011.04; ch 011 / 011.05].
- Daz spotted a small chest on the lakebed surrounded by zombies; after debate, Thorin volunteered to dive for it [ch 011 / 011.05].
- The party planned the dive: Gyrgum cast Shield of Faith on Thorin, Daz shaped an air bubble with Shape Water, and Zalthir created magical darkness to blind the zombies [ch 011 / 011.05].
- Thorin retrieved the chest without alerting a single undead; it contained 120 silver pieces, 30 gold pieces, four spell scrolls, and a driftglobe [ch 011 / 011.05].
- Brannum claimed a share of the treasure since it belonged to his crew, but Daz rebuffed him, offering that he could argue it out with the zombies below [ch 011 / 011.05].
- The DM explained scrolls can be copied into a spellbook or kept for one use outside spell slots, and explained the "Magic action" [ch 011 / 011.05].
- Two more days later, within a day of Gracklstugh, an enormous aquatic troll speaking Giant demanded one of the party for a meal; Gyrgum refused to hand over Brannum on neutral-good grounds, and Thorin called for them to run [ch 011 / 011.06].
- Jimjar shouted a warning that the troll would regenerate unless burned with fire or acid [ch 011 / 011.06].
- Daz's Scorching Ray missed, but his Magic Missiles hit for 9; the troll pulled the boat down and clawed Daz, while Zalthir struck it, making it release the hull, and pushed it five feet back [ch 011 / 011.06].
- Gyrgum cast Tasha's Caustic Brew, vomiting a wave of acid, but the troll dodged the worst of it; Brannum offered to grow giant-size and row if untied, but Thorin refused [ch 011 / 011.06].
- The troll retreated into the depths, grumbling it was only trying to be helpful by reducing the party's mouths to feed [ch 011 / 011.06].
- The party arrived at Gracklstugh; Buppido and Hemeth explained the Duergar's strict laws and dislike of Drow make it a safe haven from Drow pursuit [ch 011 / 011.07].
- Hemeth advised entering quietly through the docks, saying "The guards guard the doors, not the docks," and warned "this is not the time or place for heroics" [ch 011 / 011.07].
- The party discussed ransoming Brannum; Thorin pointed out they never promised to let him go, and he had tried to sell them first [ch 011 / 011.07].
- The party counted their funds: 27 platinum pieces and 1,100 gold pieces, after a spreadsheet error had listed the gold as platinum [ch 011 / 011.07].
