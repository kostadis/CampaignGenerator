## Events

- The Avowed organized an emergency disputation in the Oval Theatre, and most scholars and Avowed abandoned their duties to attend, including Bookwyrm, Teles, Fheminor, and half the Avowed [ch 060 / 060.01].
- Fembris Lancer sheepishly asked the party for permission to attend the disputation, and his departure left the keep's corridors and sensitive areas unmonitored [ch 060 / 060.01].
- Daral clung to Thorin and Daz, having concluded they were the only two people in Candlekeep who did not actively want him dead [ch 060 / 060.01].
- Gyrgum encountered the party while traveling from the library and handed Daz the key previously worn around Kalan Strongbranch's neck, preferring not to carry it into a crowded room of Avowed [ch 060 / 060.01].
- The party discussed Midnight Tears: midnight is determined by the sun being directly opposite one's location, and escape strategies include continually traveling west, changing planes, or receiving *Lesser Restoration* [ch 060 / 060.01].
- The party noted why Midnight Tears appeals to drow: underground victims may have no way to know when local midnight will occur [ch 060 / 060.01].
- Daz cast *Identify* as a ritual on Kalan Strongbranch's key, confirming it was not linked to an instant-summoning spell [ch 060 / 060.02].
- The party clarified that the sapphire—not the summoned object—must remain with the caster to retrieve the linked item, and that a dead person counts as possessing nothing [ch 060 / 060.02].
- Thorin concluded that possession of the sapphire might function as a concealed proxy for possession of the key, provided the bearer knows the appropriate magic [ch 060 / 060.02].
- The party reconstructed the timeline of the poisoned book: Daral placed it wrapped beneath the evergreen tree in the Southern Dining Hall at approximately 11 a.m. on Deadwinter's Eve, and Kazryn Nyantani distributed it to Janussi after dinner [ch 060 / 060.03].
- The party determined the poison turns black only after activation by contact with a living humanoid, so unused residue would remain inconspicuous [ch 060 / 060.03].
- The party divided the work: Thorin and Daral questioned the dining-hall staff, Daz inspected the apothecary, and Zalthir and Glabbagool searched suspects' private chambers [ch 060 / 060.03].
- Daral argued that half the Great Readers would do anything to obtain Candlekeep's highest office, naming Teles, A'lai Aivenmore, Fheminor, and others among his suspicions [ch 060 / 060.04].
- Asked who would benefit from both Janussi's death and Daral's disgrace, Daral identified A'lai Aivenmore, who he believes wants to rule Candlekeep and sees Daral's love of poetry and fiction as a threat [ch 060 / 060.04].
- Thorin and Daral went to the Southern Dining Hall, where Thorin discovered a severed heart and a blood-stained meat cleaver hidden inside Milil's lead-lined brass chalice [ch 060 / 060.05; ch 060 / moment].
- The party identified the heart as one of the missing pieces of Janussi's body and inferred its removal was intended to interfere with resurrection or magical questioning [ch 060 / 060.05].
- The party noted the chalice's lead lining blocked divination such as *Locate Object*, confirming the killer's intimate knowledge of Candlekeep's artifacts and magical resources [ch 060 / 060.05].
- The waitstaff interviews revealed the Deadwinter supper began at the 5 p.m. bell with twelve dishes served on communal platters and shared carafes [ch 060 / 060.06].
- Irony, a junior Avowed with a compulsive need to tell the truth, recounted that Janussi praised Alkrist's proposed work *Tales of War by the Generals That Waged Them*, and that Daral muttered "Oh, you've been right behind one of us all night," after which Sylvira Savikas stormed out [ch 060 / 060.06].
- Irony described a suspicious encounter at approximately 1 a.m. when a figure resembling Sylvira Savikas searched the kitchens by candlelight, refused to speak by pointing at her throat, and accepted help finding a pot of honey [ch 060 / 060.06].
- The party noted two incongruities: Sylvira's voice was avoided entirely, and a tiefling with darkvision should not have needed a lantern, suggesting magical impersonation [ch 060 / 060.06; ch 060 / moment].
- Sprig Summerfoot disclosed that Irony had been fending off unwanted advances from Great Reader Teles Ahvoste, and recommended Orrin Glass as a witness [ch 060 / 060.07].
- Orrin Glass, deaf in one ear but highly observant, reported seeing Alkrist burn a small scrap of paper toward a candle when two junior Avowed arrived [ch 060 / 060.07].
- Orrin also noticed that the string around Janussi's gift had clearly been cut and retied, establishing the package had been tampered with while beneath the evergreen tree between 11 a.m. and the post-dinner gift distribution [ch 060 / 060.07].
- At the Oak Tree Apothecary, Daz discovered broken glass and a peppermint-covered rag below a shelf labeled "dangerous toxins" in Elvish [ch 060 / 060.08].
- Leuwin recalled Fheminor visiting at approximately 2:30 p.m. for cold medicine and A'lai arriving around 3 p.m. for fresh fennel, which led him to the greenhouse [ch 060 / 060.08].
- Daz cast *Speak with Animals* and, bribing the squirrel Nibbles with nuts, learned that a "huge lizard skin" entered the pharmacy while Leuwin was with A'lai, caused a bang, shattered the vial, and departed [ch 060 / 060.08; ch 060 / moment].
- Daz concluded that A'lai's request for fennel was a diversion allowing a Dragonborn accomplice to steal a dangerous toxin or precursor [ch 060 / 060.08].
- Zalthir and Glabbagool searched A'lai Aivenmore's chambers and found a cage sized for five messenger doves but only two birds, with signs three had been recently dispatched [ch 060 / 060.09].
- In Alkrist's chambers, Zalthir found sealed glass jars of dead rats with blistered and pustulated skin, a locked safe, and a copy of *1001 Tashalian Nights* bookmarked to a tale of a commander poisoned by Midnight Tears applied to a gifted horn [ch 060 / 060.09; ch 060 / moment].
- Zalthir's search of Bookwyrm's spacious chambers uncovered nothing significant, and Glabbagool detected no trace of poison there [ch 060 / 060.09].
