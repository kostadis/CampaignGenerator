- Jimjar explained he won the bone die off a blind Duergar in Gracklstugh in "a back-alley game of Three-Dragon Ante," and that the Duergar claimed it was carved from the knucklebone of a saint who never told a lie, a die with a memory for the truth [ch 048 / 048.05].

- The party overheard gnomes recounting wild tales of the "Ember Vanguard" as an ultra-powerful evil drow strike force: Daz a 20th-level magic user casting meteor swarm, Gyrgum using Earthquake to shatter mountains, Thorin smashing rocks with his biceps, Zalthir a Red Dragonborn, and the Vanguard slaying an ancient red dragon rather than Themberchaud "the fat and tired and overweight" [ch 048 / 048.07].

- Jimjar pointed toward a pale white puffball mushroom growing at impossible speed from a crack in the wall with a cloyingly sweet scent, remarking "Juiblex was a loud, messy neighbor, but this sister, she prefers to move in while you're sleeping," then turned and walked back toward Blingdenstone; Gyrgum fed the mushroom to Glabbagool ("That was good calories") [ch 048 / 048.07].
- Thorin used Know Your Enemy to size up Jimjar and discovered a profile matching no ordinary gnome: immune to poison and psychic damage, could not be charmed, frightened, or poisoned, and shrugged off radiant energy and mundane weapons alike [ch 048 / 048.07].
- Zalthir noted the profile was "suspiciously similar to the stat block for angels"; the DM reminded the table that deities are not supposed to walk Faerûn since the Sundering [ch 048 / 048.07].
- Jimjar noticed the scrutiny, offered a knowing wink, and vanished entirely from existence, leaving only the faint metallic clink of platinum coins echoing in the tunnel and the distinct sense that this was not the last they would see of him [ch 048 / 048.07].
- The session concluded with the party preparing to finally exit the Underdark and see the sun; the DM suggested sages on the surface might shed light on who Jimjar is [ch 048 / 048.07].
