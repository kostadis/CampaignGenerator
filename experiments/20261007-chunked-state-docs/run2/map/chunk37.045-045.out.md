## Events
- The party completed the ancient ritual at Entémoch's Boon while fending off two basilisks; Thorin, who speaks Giant, directed the ritual with his spare dart already in the circle as the weapon sacrifice [ch 045 / 045.01].

- The party gained the ability to summon Earth Elementals for the gnomes of Blingdenstone and a homebrew Stone Skin buff lasting one week of in-game time; Zalthir volunteered to remind the party [ch 045 / 045.01; ch 045 / spells].
- Dasco Pickshine was delighted that the gnomes could now hire Earth Elementals for mining, since they "don't cost a lot other than the summoning spell," prompting Thorin to object to subjugating them as unpaid workers [ch 045 / 045.02].
- The party met with Senni Diggermattock, Chief Dorbo, and Chipgrin to advocate that Earth Elementals be used for defense but not hard labor; Thorin argued "you can have this under the condition that you use it just for fighting, not for hard labor" [ch 045 / 045.02].
- Insight checks revealed tales of the Ember Vanguard's rampage in Gracklstugh had reached Blingdenstone's bars, with some speculating it was "merely the first foray of Menzoberranzan's plans to take over other parts of the Underdark"; the party stayed incognito [ch 045 / 045.02].
- Gyrgum argued on religious grounds that Bahamut and the good dragons oppose enslaving summoned creatures; the local clerics agreed it violated the Gnomish gods [ch 045 / 045.02].
- Zalthir argued from a monastic perspective for peace, harmony, light and shadow, and Daz compared elemental labor to Menzoberranzan's slave society, which was "the killer" [ch 045 / 045.02; ch 045 / memorable].
- With three of four natural 20s on persuasion checks, Senni seized control of the meeting from Chief Dorbo, formed "a coalition of the willing" against the Pudding King, and shouted "Gnome Labor, not Summoned Labor!" [ch 045 / 045.02; ch 045 / memorable].
- The leaders decided elementals could be used for war but not for building or labor, disrupting Blingdenstone's economic plans and making the party "the toasts of the town"; Thorin conceded remuneration would have to wait, "one thing at a time" [ch 045 / 045.02].
- During the pre-battle montage, Kazook Pickshine brewed the lichen into a weapon coating, Nomi prepared summoning spells, Chief Dorbo told the Captain of the Wall "prepare your men for combat. For tomorrow we shall go," and the Pudding King readied his oozes [ch 045 / 045.03; ch 045 / npcs].
- The traveling merchant Starlace set up Starlace Curios and Arcana in the Traders' Grotto; the party used their 10% coupon while shopping before taking a long rest [ch 045 / 045.03].
- Daz purchased Dustsight Spectacles (able to see invisible creatures and, per GM ruling, through Zalthir's magical darkness) [ch 045 / 045.03; ch 045 / items].
- Gyrgum had his mace enchanted to +1 for 360 gold, leaving him with exactly 1,024 gold pieces [ch 045 / 045.03; ch 045 / items].
- Thorin had his shield enchanted to +1 for 270 gold, bringing his AC to 20, and purchased the Stonegnarl Warband Token for a +1 AC bonus near stone for 270 gold after the discount [ch 045 / 045.03; ch 045 / items].
- The party sold their studded leather armor +2 to Starlace for 600 gold after both sides rolled natural 20s in a persuasion contest; both sides walked away feeling good [ch 045 / 045.03; ch 045 / items].
- The party sought out Valimor Brightgem at The Gilded Sheath to commission a scabbard for Dawnbringer; Valimor offered a 600-gold gem-encrusted showpiece or a practical Vaultmaster locking scabbard [ch 045 / 045.04].
