## Active Quests & Open Threads
- **Daz’s hidden origins and lineage** — Daz suspects his true name is “Daz’issin,” a softened form used by mothers hoping their sons would survive, and that his lineage was deliberately erased; House Baenre in Menzoberranzan is suspected of funding his pursuers for eleven years [ch 056 / 056.07; ch 067 / 067.02].
- **The Book of Vile Darkness** — Daz has begun reading the sentient artifact, receiving information about the Underdark from an untrustworthy source; its corruption remains active, and the surviving Avowed Readers will not permit the party to leave with it [ch 070 / 070.04; ch 070 / items].
- **The four surviving Alaundo prophecy crystals** — Gyrgum secured them after Edvaldo destroyed two; their unrevealed prophecies are likely tied to the party, including one foretelling Eldeth’s return to a hall of stone bearing demon names and Thorin’s summoning to an ancient forge [ch 070 / 070.03; ch 070 / items].
- **The metal-hand courier of Manshoon** — A'lai confirmed Manshoon’s agent delivers book lists with a glove concealing a metal hand; the courier’s identity and connection to the broader conspiracy remain unknown [ch 064 / 064.04].
- **The obsidian door’s final guardian and mechanism** — The door opened after Gyrgum spoke “a candle,” but the chamber above contains the means to destroy the Vault’s collection, which the party has not yet located [ch 068 / 068.05; ch 069 / 069.05].
- **The second Manshoon simulacrum’s bargain** — A second, cheaper-robed simulacrum offered ten prismatic gemstones and one book in exchange for opening the Obsidian Door; the gemstones remain in his possession and their prophecies are unexplored [ch 069 / 069.05].
- **The “over 500 feet” tower claim** — Edvaldo asserted Candlekeep’s High Tower is “over 500 feet” tall without verification; whether this is accurate or a deception remains unresolved [ch 068 / 068.03; ch 068 / npcs].
- **The “factory” of Manshoon simulacra** — Gyrgum observed that multiple duplicates must be produced, implying an active, hidden mechanism for creating them; the location and method of production are unknown [ch 069 / 069.04].
- **The real Manshoon’s vengeance** — The real Manshoon’s final words promised eternal vengeance after his simulacrum was destroyed; the party now faces the wrath of the original, unbound archlich [ch 070 / 070.02; ch 070 / npcs].
- **The “dancing myconid parade” and fungal wedding** — The party observed a procession of myconids announcing a wedding celebration, strongly tied to Zuggtmoy’s influence; the nature, location, and participants of this fungal wedding remain unknown [ch 040 / 040.02; ch 035 / 035.04].
- **The fungal wedding ingredients for acid-resistant gear** — Jimjar’s bet requires the party to recover ingredients from the fungal wedding celebration to craft acid-resistant gear; the location and method of acquisition are unresolved [ch 035 / 035.04].
- **The identity of the “bride” (Zuggtmoy)** — Ilvara’s devotion to Zuggtmoy as “the bride” and the fungal corruption spreading through drow and myconids confirm her active influence; the party has not yet acted to stop her encroachment [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 048 / 048.07].
- **Glabbagool’s evolution and sentience** — Glabbagool now occupies the Pudding King’s body, demonstrates control over viscosity, and is being considered for monk training; his potential to become a T-1000-style entity and his role as a mobile waste processor remain open-ended [ch 047 / 047.10; ch 048 / 048.07].
- **The Bone Die** — A 20-sided die carved from a saint’s knucklebone, won in Gracklstugh, pacified an ooze army once and is now inert; its origin and potential future use are unknown [ch 048 / 048.05].
- **The Voucher of the Varmint** — A braided rat’s foot gifted to Daz by Chipgrin, recognized by wererats and outcasts from Blingdenstone to Menzoberranzan; its diplomatic limits and full utility are untested [ch 048 / 048.07].
- **The six-pointed star symbol** — Elin draws this shape repeatedly; it matches charcoal rubbings from Daggerford and the Black-Banner Five marker from the Underdark; its meaning and origin are unknown [ch 056 / 056.02; ch 056 / 056.07].
- **The spreading surface madness** — Fractured speech patterns in Eldred, the Field Ward preacher, and the Beregost scholar, along with the syncopated chant heard on the wind near Candlekeep, confirm the Underdark’s corruption is bleeding into the surface world [ch 055 / 055.04; ch 056 / 056.03].
- **The Endless Chant** — The same syncopated chant pattern appears in multiple surface locations; its ritualistic nature and source are unknown, but it is linked to Zuggtmoy’s influence [ch 056 / 056.03; ch 056 / 056.07].
- **The sealed records of Candlekeep** — Sister Yvenne confirmed the public record is sealed on one house; Daz realizes Candlekeep itself is complicit in erasing knowledge, including the erased fourth seat of Menzoberranzan [ch 056 / 056.07].
- **The Ardragon** — A black-hooded Kenku hovering a foot off the ground, mimicking a hawk, leaving coins uncollected on caravan trails north of Yartar; its connection to Whistler and purpose are unknown [ch 055 / 055.04].
- **Kestler’s Temple Fire** — A southern temple’s fire still burns; its keeper has not written in five years and has not been seen in three; Gyrgum calls it an omen, but its significance is unexplained [ch 055 / 055.04].
- **Eldred’s fractured voice** — A courier speaks in two voices — his own and a younger version — identified as the same madness from the Underdark; the cause and spread of this condition are unresolved [ch 055 / 055.04].
- **Mortal Agency Doctrine** — Gyrgum received the pamphlet *Stroud and the Human Agency Hypothesis*, introducing a philosophical faction rejecting divine influence; its impact on the party’s divine ties is unexplored [ch 055 / 055.03].
- **Glabbagool’s cheese curiosity** — Glabbagool asked if dairy is bad for people, marking a new dimension of his sentience and curiosity; his dietary preferences and social integration remain open [ch 055 / 055.04].
