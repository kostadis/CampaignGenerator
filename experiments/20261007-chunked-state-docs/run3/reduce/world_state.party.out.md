## Party
- The party is known as the Ember Vanguard, a name adopted after intimidating Derro guards and now widely rumored in Gracklstugh; they are also informally referred to as “Ember Vanguard and Groupies” in Roll20, though they have no official new title beyond this [ch 021 / 021.04; ch 022 / 022.01].
- The party is level 9, having leveled up after defeating the Pudding King; they are currently based in Candlekeep’s Deneir’s Sanctum, under the authority of Bookwyrm as deputized investigators with full access to the Inner Ward [ch 047 / 047.10; ch 059 / 059.01; ch 065 / end].
- Daz is a 9th-level wizard with expertise in Arcana (27), somatic field-perception, and Sculpt Spells; he holds the Book of Vile Darkness, which he is manipulating with Mage Hand and Minor Illusion to avoid direct contact; he has cast Fireball, Telekinesis, Misty Step, Hypnotic Pattern, and Scorching Ray in recent sessions; he has a spider familiar and a bat familiar; he retains his Ring of Protection and Cloak of Elvenkind; he suspects his true name is Daz'issin and seeks to uncover sealed drow records; he is attuned to the Robe of the Archmagi (gained from the first simulacrum, powers pending long rest); he has no new injuries but carries the weight of the Book’s influence [ch 047 / 047.10; ch 056 / 056.07; ch 065 / 065.02; ch 070 / 070.01; ch 070 / 070.03; ch 070 / items].
- Zalthir is a 9th-level monk (Shadow Monk) with mastery over Shadow Step, Stunning Strike, and Eldritch Claw Tattoo (now manifesting tentacles); he has activated his tattoo to grapple and drag both Manshoon simulacra and Edvaldo; he used his wings to catch Thorin mid-fall; he is dragonborn and offended by Thorin’s wish for a baby dragon; he has received one specialized shadow technique from Khell-Vire and confirmed Glabbagool’s pre-monk training is valid; he is currently carrying the vial of Resonant Slime; he has no new injuries but is emotionally detached from divine interference [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 037 / 037.06; ch 056 / 056.05; ch 059 / 059.06; ch 069 / 069.01; ch 070 / 070.02; ch 070 / 070.03].
- Thorin is a 9th-level fighter with the Sentinel, Tactical Shift, and Hill Strike features; he wields Dawnbringer, a sentient +2 radiant weapon with an anxiety disorder, now bonded to him as a “marriage”; he has mithral plate armor (AC 22), a light crossbow, and the Diggermattock Miners patch; he used Action Surge and Menacing Attack to defeat the Zhentarim assassin and frighten Edvaldo; he is emotionally invested in Dawnbringer’s healing and has begun a two-phase therapeutic process with Philemon; he has no new items or injuries but is physically strong and reliable in melee [ch 048 / 048.01; ch 055 / 055.05; ch 056 / 056.06; ch 065 / 065.02; ch 069 / 069.01; ch 070 / 070.03].
- Gyrgum is a 9th-level cleric of Bahamut with Divine Strike, Mass Healing Word, and the ability to cast 5th-level Spiritual Weapon; he holds the real High Tower key, the fake key, and the four surviving prophecy crystals; he has a holy symbol of Laduguer, a chessboard holy symbol, and a toothbrush used as a material component for Tasha’s Caustic Brew; he used Insight to resolve Jimjar’s bet and declared “a candle” to open the Obsidian Door with genuine intent; he is weary of prolonged negotiations and has accepted the emotional weight of laying Pelek’s ghost to rest; he is now aware of the connection between the monument and Underdark madness and carries the Stroudite pamphlet and Travis’s Hand Medallion; he has no new injuries but is the party’s primary healer and moral anchor [ch 048 / 048.01; ch 065 / 065.03; ch 069 / 069.01; ch 070 / 070.03; ch 056 / 056.01].
- Glabbagool is an 8th-level sentient gelatinous cube sidekick to Zalthir with Shadow Step, Flurry of Blows, Acidic Shadow-Ki, and Corrosive Embrace; he now manifests several eyes on Zalthir’s arm; he has absorbed the Pudding King’s ooze body and is cheerful, seeking purpose and viewed as a “friendly ooze”; he holds one real High Tower key inside his body; he has no new injuries but is a silent tactical asset and has been granted his own question to Candlekeep’s scholars; he is now fully integrated as a party member and is guarded by the party against intolerance [ch 034 / 034.08; ch 047 / 047.10; ch 059 / 059.06; ch 065 / 065.03; ch 069 / 069.04].
- The party possesses: the Book of Vile Darkness (concealed by illusion), four prophecy crystals, the real and fake High Tower keys, the Robe of the Archmagi (attunement pending), Dawnbringer, the vial of Resonant Slime, the spider medallion, the bone die, the Voucher of the Varmint, the glowing geode, the shrunken drow spy “Polly Pocket” (in a Bag of Holding with prayer beads), and the Stroudite School Notes; they have no remaining gold or currency tracked, having spent or absorbed all prior assets [ch 070 / items; ch 059 / items; ch 048 / 048.01; ch 054 / 054.05].
- The party’s immediate intention is to survive the imminent arrival of the last surviving Avowed Readers, who will not permit them to walk away with the Book of Vile Darkness or the prophecy crystals; they are debating whether to flee Candlekeep, surrender the book under duress, or use the knowledge within it to confront the demonic threat while resisting its corruption [ch 070 / 070.04].
- The party has made irrevocable choices: Manshoon’s simulacrum was thrown into lava, two prophecy crystals were destroyed, the Obsidian Door was opened by Gyrgum, and Daz has begun reading the Book of Vile Darkness—each act altering their standing with Candlekeep, the Underdark, and the multiverse [ch 070 / 070.04].
