## Key NPCs

### **Dawnbringer**  
A sentient +2 sunblade that deals radiant damage, adds 1d8 against undead, grants lesser restoration, speaks and reads Common, and is terrified of darkness. Insists on remaining active at all times. Now aware of her trauma from darkness and seeks help at Candlekeep. Popular with local children and desires aesthetic upgrades. The party treats her as genderless. `[ch 029 / items; ch 029 / 029.01; ch 041 / 041.01; ch 041 / 041.06; ch 045 / npcs; ch 053 / 053.05; ch 053 / npcs]`

### **Glabbagool**  
A sentient gelatinous cube who distinguishes himself from mindless oozes and believes he is the only speaking one in the universe. Friendly, childlike in curiosity, enjoys games with animated statues, and seeks societal role and friendship. Offers to consume dead matter. Vulnerable to alcohol, which the party plans to weaponize. Acknowledges hearing a voice from a “big pool of slimes,” suggesting susceptibility to the Pudding King. Now acts as a jailer and student of shadow monk philosophy. `[ch 034 / npcs; ch 035 / 035.04; ch 036 / 036.04; ch 037 / 037.06; ch 037 / npcs; ch 038 / 038.01; ch 038 / 038.04; ch 040 / 040.02; ch 041 / 041.03; ch 041 / 041.05; ch 046 / 046.08; ch 056 / 056.04; ch 056 / 056.05; ch 069 / 069.04]`

### **Gartokkar Xundorn**  
Leader of the Keepers of the Flame and high-ranking advisor to the Deepking. Analytical, literal-minded, politically motivated, and condescending. Tasked the party with retrieving the dragon egg, Demogorgon statuette, and paperwork. Fears Themberchaud’s reaction and vows to pursue the party to the end of time if betrayed. Confirms the egg’s purpose is to replace Themberchaud. Currently cautiously satisfied with payment received and secret kept. `[ch 013 / 013.04; ch 013 / NPCs; ch 014 / 014.07; ch 014 / npcs; ch 017 / NPCs; ch 018 / npcs; ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02; ch 026 / 026.06; ch 026 / npcs]`

### **Errde Blackskull**  
Captain of the Stone Guard, pragmatic, observant, and possibly unstable. Seeks proof of the Council of Savants’ corruption and collusion with Gray Ghosts. Wants evidence to justify a violent pacification mission against the derro. Refuses to admit the Assassin’s Guild exists but will march on the Deepking if told he funds it. Believes the party is being hunted by drow and offers protection while pulling them into her conspiracy. Last known to be informed of the party’s presence at the docks. `[ch 013 / 013.06; ch 013 / NPCs; ch 014 / 014.02; ch 014 / npcs; ch 018 / npcs; ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02; ch 027 / npcs; ch 027 / 027.04]`

### **Themberchaud**  
A young adult red dragon, vain and out-of-shape, who believes himself “father of the flame” and “bringer of the flame.” Serves as a glorified lighter for Gracklstugh’s forges and is completely unaware he is being manipulated by Gartokkar. Arrogant, paranoid, and desires full knowledge of local events. If informed of the dragon egg, he will likely burn the city to cinders. Views the party as loyal and efficient agents. `[ch 014 / 014.06; ch 014 / npcs; ch 017 / NPCs; ch 017 / 017.04; ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02; ch 026 / 026.06; ch 026 / npcs; ch 027 / npcs; ch 027 / 027.02; ch 027 / 027.04]`

### **Zuggtmoy**  
The Demon Queen of Fungi, Lady of Rot and Decay. Appears as a feminine entity who demands firsthand experience, not explanation. Rejects decomposition’s natural role, seeks destruction, and is preparing to mate with or possess Araumycos through an incomprehensible ritual. Her power is so vast that direct confrontation would mean new characters. Previously encountered by Thorin and Gyrgum. Invoked by Ilvara as “the bride.” Now active and encroaching via twisted fungi. Dismissed the Pudding King’s forces as “crude.” `[ch 025 / 025.04; ch 025 / npcs; ch 031 / npcs; ch 031 / 031.06; ch 032 / 032.01; ch 044 / 044.03; ch 044 / npcs; ch 050 / NPCs; ch 051 / 051.05; ch 051 / npcs; ch 053 / 053.04; ch 053 / npcs]`

### **Araumycos**  
A vast, sentient fungal entity in the Underdark, potentially existing for millennia. It is the habitat of myconids and the intended “groom” in Zuggtmoy’s ritual. Not present at the wedding rehearsal, preventing the true ceremony. Its nature and form remain unknown. Named in the wedding hymn as being joined “heart to heart” until death. `[ch 031 / npcs; ch 031 / 031.06; ch 032 / 032.01]`

### **Sovereign Phylo**  
Co-sovereign of the Neverlight Grove who prophesied a “Great Awakening” and “great celebration” involving Araumycos. Secretly infected and steering the colony toward Zuggtmoy’s plans. Has broken communal norms by segregating circles and forming the Circle of Masters. Revered by Gasbide as a prophet. `[ch 029 / npcs; ch 029 / 029.05; ch 030 / npcs; ch 030 / 030.05; ch 031 / 031.02; ch 031 / npcs]`

### **Sovereign Basidia**  
Myconid sovereign who opposes Phylo. Recognizes unknown spores on Sarith and fears the grove’s corruption. Tasks the party with investigating the Garden of Welcome. `[ch 029 / npcs; ch 029 / 029.06; ch 031 / 031.02; ch 031 / npcs]`

### **Pudding King**  
A small, insane gnome possessed by Juiblex. Commands Princess Ebonmire and Prince Livid. Seeks to turn Blingdenstone into “Blingdenpuddle.” Transforms into ooze form when grappled, gaining auto-hit ability. Addressed by Zuggtmoy as “my little king.” Killed and dissolved in the throne room. `[ch 038 / 038.04; ch 038 / npcs; ch 044 / 044.03; ch 044 / npcs; ch 046 / 046.09; ch 046 / npcs; ch 047 / 047.08; ch 047 / npcs]`

### **Jimjar**  
A deep gnome with an obsessive gambling habit, who bets for the thrill, not the prize. Now claims to have found a shortcut to Blingdenstone. Knows Blingdenstone’s leaders, history, and crises in excessive detail; treated with deference by gnomes and suspected of being a concealed dignitary. Vanished after revealing immunity to poison, psychic damage, charm, and fear, and resistance to radiant and non-magical attacks. Possibly a celestial being or gnomish god. `[ch 006 / 006.03; ch 006 / moment; ch 006 / npcs; ch 034 / npcs; ch 035 / 035.04; ch 036 / 036.02; ch 048 / 048.07]`

### **Jorlan Duskryn**  
Former lieutenant of Ilvara, disfigured by black pudding. Bitter and uninfected, one of only two drow in the cavern not turned into a sporewalker. Now wounded and furious. Seeks revenge by enabling prisoner escape. Hostile toward Ilvara. `[ch 002 / NPCs; ch 050 / NPCs; ch 051 / 051.06]`

### **Ilvara Mizzrym**  
Former high priestess of Lolth, corrupted by Zuggtmoy after visiting Neverlight Grove. Now fungal, uses a mushroom artifact as a conduit, and can melt into fungal patches. Particularly vulnerable to radiant damage. Invoked Zuggtmoy before death. `[ch 050 / NPCs; ch 051 / npcs]`

### **Sarith Kzekarit**  
Drow prisoner who served as the party’s navigator, claiming knowledge of the Underdark tunnels. Prideful, nearly led them astray in Faerzress but accepted Gyrgum’s correction. Transformed into a drow spore servant and killed by Eldeth and Jimjar. `[ch 004 / npcs; ch 005 / 005.06; ch 031 / 031.04; ch 031 / npcs]`

### **Gyrgum**  
Cleric who used Cure Wounds to revive Thorin and chose rope and caltrops over weapons. Proficient only in simple weapons. Calm demeanor pierced by Gyrgum’s cooking. Treated as “bestest friend” by Stool. Corrected Sarith’s navigation. The ghost of Pelek now inhabits a derro spy and is bound to Gyrgum via his bones. `[ch 003 / npcs; ch 004 / npcs; ch 005 / 005.05; ch 020 / NPCs; ch 020 / spells]`

### **Stool**  
A myconid sprout who releases rapport spores to enable telepathic communication among nearby intelligent creatures. Has only four rapport spores and cannot mind-control drow. Moves at 10 feet and rides on Thorin’s back. Treats Gyrgum as his “bestest friend.” Declined to rejoin adventuring and remains in Neverlight Grove. `[ch 003 / npcs; ch 004 / npcs; ch 005 / 005.05; ch 030 / 030.05; ch 030 / npcs]`

### **Flumph**  
A telepathic creature that feeds on psionic energy from evil beings. Relieves distress by trauma-dumping memories. Now traveling with the party, emotionally drained, and seeking new trauma. `[ch 017 / NPCs; ch 018 / 018.03; ch 018 / npcs]`

### **Pelek (Ghost)**  
A well-adjusted ghost of a deep gnome from Blingdenstone, murdered and dismembered by Buppido. Bound to his bones. Now inhabits the body of a derro spy and is allied with the party. `[ch 016 / NPCs; ch 020 / NPCs; ch 020 / spells; ch 037 / 037.03; ch 037 / npcs]`

### **Buppido**  
Derro who believed he was divine and on a divine mission. Murdered Pelek with an axe. Now dead. `[ch 002 / NPCs; ch 003 / npcs; ch 012 / 012.03; ch 012 / npcs; ch 016 / NPCs]`

### **Demogorgon**  
A 30-foot-tall demon lord with two baboon-like heads, tusks, oily tentacles, and red eyes. Rose from the Darklake after Bloppblippodd’s death. His emergence confirms a major demonic threat. Hostile. `[ch 008 / NPCs; ch 008 / 008.04; ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / NPCs]`

### **Manshoon**  
Legendary Zhentarim archmage, reportedly killed 170 years ago but continues to reappear. His simulacra are constructed from ice and snow, have half his vitality, cannot regain spells, and melt at zero hit points. Directed A’lai Aivenmore for eleven years to steal specific books from Candlekeep. No direct appearance; known through A’lai’s testimony. `[ch 064 / 064.04; ch 064 / npcs; ch 065 / NPCs; ch 065 / end; ch 069 / npcs]`

### **A’lai Aivenmore**  
Thirty-one-year veteran scholar of Candlekeep who served Manshoon for eleven years. Orchestrated book thefts under Manshoon’s orders. Now imprisoned in Candlekeep’s null magic prison, grateful and resigned. Realized he was never important to Manshoon. His insight into the Vault and deduction about House Baenre are in the party’s possession. `[ch 060 / 060.08; ch 064 / 064.04; ch 064 / npcs; ch 065 / NPCs; ch 067 / 067.02]`
