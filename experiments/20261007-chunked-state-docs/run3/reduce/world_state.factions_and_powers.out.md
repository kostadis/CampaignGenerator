## Factions and Powers

- **Lolth** — Worshiped at Velkynvelve; her shrine contains a 10-foot idol with jet eyes, and drow rituals remain centered on her hierarchy and dominance [ch 002 / locations; ch 002 / items]. Her priesthood distrusts mages claiming divine communion, and her cult is challenged by the heretical “Zuggtmoy’s bride” movement led by Ilvara [ch 050 / 050.01; ch 050 / 050.02].

- **Drow** — Ruthless slave-owners who value hierarchy, treasure hoarding by the elite, and public humiliation as political tools [ch 002 / 002.05; ch 002 / 002.06]. The drow of Velkynvelve are actively hunting escaped prisoners with scouts and a full hunting party in pursuit [ch 005 / 005.04; ch 005 / npcs]. House T'sarran, once powerful, suffered heavy losses and now seeks to extract Daz alive, not kill him, and has deployed agents to the surface to identify his benefactor [ch 049 / 049.02; ch 051 / 051.04; ch 053 / 053.04; ch 054 / 054.04]. House Mizzrym lost contact with Ilvara and is now urgently seeking her [ch 053 / 053.03]. House Duskryn is protecting Daz with lethal coordination [ch 051 / 051.03; ch 051 / 051.06]. A'lai deduces House Baenre in Menzoberranzan has funded an eleven-year campaign against Daz, though without direct proof [ch 067 / 067.02].

- **Drow Outpost (Velkynvelve)** — A drow-run facility that held prisoners; its guard tower has been overrun and its commander slain [ch 003 / locations].

- **Duergar Slavers** — Gray dwarves who kidnap travelers to sell as slaves in Gracklstugh; use invisibility and enlargement magic; operate on the Darklake [ch 010 / 010.03; ch 010 / NPCs].

- **Duergar** — A subterranean race with strict laws against foreigners and deep hatred of Drow; Gracklstugh is their city and offers safety from Drow pursuit [ch 011 / 011.07].

- **Duergar city guard** — Enforce Gracklstugh’s laws with invisible patrols and lethal efficiency; responded to the stone giant’s rampage [ch 012 / 012.01; ch 012 / 012.05].

- **Keepers of the Flame** — A powerful duergar order that tends Themberchaud through psionic control; claim to be the Deepking’s most prized advisors and wield legal authority [ch 013 / 013.04; ch 013 / NPCs]. They are secretly at war with the Gray Ghosts over the stolen dragon egg [ch 014 / 014.07]. Led by Gartokkar, they commissioned the egg’s retrieval, fear Themberchaud’s awakening, and will pursue the party eternally if deceived [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02; ch 025 / 025.01; ch 026 / 026.06]. They threatened the party with permanent hostility if betrayed [ch 018 / 018.02].

- **Stone Guard** — The military and law enforcement arm of Gracklstugh, led by Captain Errde Blackskull; once known for integrity, now suspected of corruption and bribery [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 018 / 018.04]. They are investigating the Empty-Scabbard Killers and seeking Droki [ch 014 / 014.02; ch 014 / npcs]. They are paranoid about the Ember Vanguard and have doubled patrols, issuing passes to the party [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / 026.06]. They seek to expose the Council of Savants’ corruption and will act violently if given full truth [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].

- **Gray Ghosts** — A shadowy faction that stole the red dragon egg and is suspected of causing psionic disturbances in the Underdark [ch 014 / 014.07]. They were ordered by the Council of Savants to kill the duergar who brought the Ember Vanguard into Gracklstugh [ch 026 / 026.07]. They are in open conflict with the Keepers of the Flame and the Crown [ch 013 / 013.05].

- **Council of Savants** — Derro governing body; Buppido claims they promise freedom but always fail; likely corrupt and oppressive [ch 015 / 015.04]. The party has killed two of their members (Narrak and another), and possesses evidence of their demonic rituals [ch 024 / 024.04]. They are confirmed as aligned with demonic powers; all in the Whorlstone Caverns are dead [ch 025 / 025.01]. They ordered the Gray Ghosts to eliminate the duergar who introduced the Ember Vanguard [ch 026 / 026.07].

- **Clan Ironhead** — A duergar weaponsmithing clan barred from direct contracts with the Deepking; Grinta offered the party future trade advantages if the Deepking is removed [ch 018 / 018.07]. The party hopes to strengthen them indirectly [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].

- **Clan Steelshadow** — The Deepking’s clan; granted exclusive weapons contracts, breeding resentment across Gracklstugh [ch 018 / 018.02].

- **Clan Xardelvar** — Chafes under new regulations; fears the Deepking will take over their work; mentioned by Errde as part of a cover story [ch 018 / 018.03].

- **Empty-Scabbard Killers** — Duergar assassins wielding psionic blades, with no physical talismans; at war with Demogorgon-aligned derro; a stolen royal contract is missing from their leader’s desk [ch 019 / npcs; ch 019 / items].

- **Derro** — Once slaves to Mind Flayers, predisposed to madness; revere the dragon egg as a source of absolute authority [ch 020 / 020.01; ch 020 / locations]. Fully confirmed as aligned with demonic powers; all in the Whorlstone Caverns are dead [ch 025 / 025.01].

- **Ember Vanguard** — A fabricated identity of the party, now a feared legend in Gracklstugh; described as a red dragonborn and wizened drow assassins blamed for derro High Council murders [ch 021 / 021.04; ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / 026.03]. Rumored to be a drow empire agent group; Daz is mistaken for an 18th-level archmage [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / Memorable Moments]. The party now formally uses “Ember Grapple” as their official name [ch 064 / 064.01].

- **Myconids** — Generally peaceable but dangerous in numbers; now confirmed susceptible to Zuggtmoy’s corruption and fungal teleportation [ch 025 / 025.02; ch 025 / 025.04]. The Neverlight Grove colony is divided: Circle leaders infected, explorers like Rumpadump resist; sovereign’s authority used for demonic worship [ch 031 / 031.02; ch 031 / npcs]. The Circle of Hunters tasked the party with hunting the Grick Alpha [ch 030 / 030.01]. The Circle of Builders, led by Gasbide, focuses on architecture [ch 030 / 030.05]. The Circle of Welcome is a corrupted ritual turning visitors into rooted heads [ch 032 / 032.03].

- **Clan Ironhead** — A duergar weaponsmithing clan that would trade with gnomes if the Deepking were removed; the party hopes to indirectly strengthen them [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.02].

- **Clan Thrazgad** — A duergar steelsmithing clan whose name appears on the dragon rattle; their involvement with the egg is unknown [ch 021 / NPCs; ch 021 / 021.03].

- **Netherese** — An ancient, fallen empire known for floating cities and alien concepts of good and evil; their artifacts are highly valued and heavily plundered [ch 028 / locations].

- **Blingdenstone Authorities** — Divided between mining coalition led by Dorbo and civilian community led by Senni; party’s intervention shifted power to the labor faction [ch 037 / 037.01; ch 045 / 045.02]. Attempted to claim credit for party’s victories and suppress “revolutionary tactics” [ch 038 / 038.01; ch 038 / 038.06]. Now unified under Dorbo’s leadership with fragile alliance among miners, wererats, ghosts, and spiritual leaders [ch 048 / 048.06].

- **Clan Diggermattock** — Ruling deep gnome clan; took territory from drow; treat all Drow escapees as intelligence sources; focused on defense and resource management [ch 036 / 036.01; ch 036 / 036.04]. Split between laborers (Senni) and mining corporations (Dorbo); party’s actions shifted power to labor [ch 045 / 045.02].

- **Goldwhisker Clan** — Wererat gnomes previously occupying contested territory; dismissed as “squatters” by Dorbo but recognized as valuable allies by Senni and the party due to immunity to non-silver weapons [ch 037 / 037.01; ch 038 / 038.02; ch 038 / 038.06]. Led by Chipgrin; now granted territorial control of upper boroughs as a self-funded defense force [ch 039 / 039.05; ch 048 / 048.06].

- **Chamber of Commerce** — Represents merchant interests in Blingdenstone; offered the party a 10% discount coupon [ch 038 / 038.02].

- **Stoneheart Enclave** — A gnome faction that can protect Earth Elementals from Ogrémoch’s Bane if the Temple of the Steadfast Stone is cleansed [ch 039 / 039.08].

- **Miners’ Guild** — Represented by Kazook Pickshine; concerned about acid corrosion to weapons and armor, seeking protective treatments [ch 039 / 039.09].

- **Burrow Wardens** — Ghostly spirits led by Uth-Jadger; will aid in the coming battle if the party completes a task on their behalf [ch 039 / 039.09]. Retain sacred rights to the North Tunnels as a graveyard [ch 048 / 048.06].

- **Ogrémoch** — A powerful earth elemental entity lingering in the Steadfast Stone among drow statues; his spirit is the source of the temple’s corruption [ch 042 / 042.02; ch 042 / npcs].

- **Blingdenstone Community** — Deep gnomes are united in defending allies; Nomi’s racism was shut down by collective outrage, showing strong internal cohesion [ch 043 / 043.04].

- **Galeb Duhr** — Ancient stone sentinels programmed to “Trust no one” and wait for divine authority; respond to sonic resonance and ritual alignment [ch 044 / 044.04; ch 044 / npcs].

- **Pickshine Mines** — Led by Dasco Pickshine; branding extends to tea packaging and clothing; miners prioritize “gross margins” over combat [ch 046 / 046.02; ch 046 / npcs].

- **Blingdenstone militia** — Reactions to the planned battle are mixed: officers and ghosts want a big fight; many others are relieved by minimal distraction [ch 046 / 046.01].

- **Juiblex** — The Faceless Lord, demon lord of oozes, is not destroyed; a voice declares he will be reborn and consume Zuggtmoy’s “banquet” to grow stronger [ch 047 / 047.10; ch 047 / npcs].

- **Zuggtmoy** — The Demon Queen of Fungi is actively moving to claim the power vacuum left by the Pudding King’s defeat, as evidenced by twisted fungi [ch 047 / 047.10; ch 047 / npcs].

- **Candlekeep** — A scholarly fortress housing dangerous tomes; lore of the Endless Chant and Word Lattice is known to its scholars; the Obsidian Door is part of its ancient protections [ch 069 / npcs; ch 069 / locations]. Maintains a policy of exile over imprisonment; public records are curated and sometimes sealed [ch 056 / 056.04; ch 056 / 056.07].

- **The Stroudite School** — One of Candlekeep’s three dominant theological schools; holds that mortal choice is sovereign; now challenged by Gyrgum’s “fixed points” theory [ch 057 / 057.01].

- **The Bahamutian School** — Argues divine providence arranges all things; Vareth aligns with this, and Gyrgum’s theory is seen as a compromise toward it [ch 057 / 057.01].

- **The Draconic School** — Claims ancient agency sets the frame for mortal action; presented as one of the three schools by Vareth [ch 057 / 057.01].

- **The Avowed** — Candlekeep’s administrative and security order; a hooded member was seen hurrying past with something hidden [ch 057 / 057.02]. Now documenting the ward failure as unprecedented and preparing experiments [ch 065 / 065.01; ch 065 / Memorable Moments]. Alkrist states obtaining powerful transformation scrolls requires “the right goblin and the right Avowed” [ch 061 / 061.07]. Bookwyrm is acting head; Teles Ahvoste and Fheminor are likely candidates for new Keeper [ch 062 / npcs; ch 062 / 062.02].

- **The Council of Twelve** — Governing body of Candlekeep; includes the Keeper, First Reader, eight Great Readers, Gatewarden, and Lorekeeper; currently in uproar over leadership and murder [ch 057 / 057.05].

- **Temple of Oghma** — Spiritual authority in Candlekeep; Kei Tigersteel serves as priest; oversees purification rites [ch 059 / 059.01; ch 059 / npcs].

- **Candlekeep Council of Great Readers** — Governing body; members include A'lai, Fheminor, Daral, Teles, and Bookwyrm; tensions over succession and murder investigation [ch 059 / 059.02; ch 059 / npcs].

- **The Threefold Proof** — Kalan’s unapproved academic methodology for criminal investigation, now publicly endorsed by Bookwyrm despite prior suppression [ch 062 / items; ch 062 / 062.02].

- **Watcher's Bell** — Candlekeep’s institutional alarm system; its tolling pattern was distinct from the hour [ch 063 / 063.01].

- **Zhentarim** — Two raiders confirmed as operatives; one carried the bow that killed the Watcher, proving direct involvement in murder and theft [ch 064 / 064.03; ch 064 / npcs]. Their agents in the High Tower are all dead; their incursion failed, but Manshoon’s breach indicates deeper involvement [ch 065 / 065.01; ch 065 / 065.02; ch 065 / NPCs].

- **House of Mechanus** — Provided the Mechanist Tools used to activate the bridge; the party owes them collateral (Glabbagool and Zalthir) for their use [ch 068 / items; ch 068 / npcs].

- **Candlekeep Avowed** — Historical scholars who once memorized the height of the High Tower of Exaltation; Edvaldo references this practice [ch 068 / 068.03].
