# Chapter 62 Academic vindication and an assault.

Date: 2026-06-29

### 062.01 The Murder Mystery Sidebar

With Alkrist's confession secured and the physical evidence in hand, the party gathered to debate their next move. Their options included handing the case to Kalan Strongbranch, confronting A'lai, or going directly to Bookwyrm and demanding justice. Dawnbringer lamented, with genuine feeling, that the promised therapy with Candlekeep's scholars had stalled after a very productive first session. Thorin could only offer a sympathetic response, agreeing that they had been making progress. After some deliberation, the group settled on a patient strategy: hand the credit to Kalan, let him save face after his humiliating dismissal, and collect their promised reward—all while keeping their suspicions about Bookwyrm close to the chest. They also weighed whether solving such a prominent murder would make them famous, infamous, or simply easier targets.

### 062.02 The Academic's Dilemma

Kalan was quick to temper their expectations. “You have a confession, a heart, and a cleaver,” he told them, but none of those things placed Bookwyrm in the room or proved who concealed the crime. Under the rigorous standards of his own investigative framework, one magically compelled confession was not enough. He shoved his paper—the Threefold Proof—into their faces and insisted that the clever play was patience. Bookwyrm was only the acting head of Candlekeep; with the naming ceremony approaching, the stench of suspicion might be enough to deny her the position of Keeper of Tomes. A'lai would likely be tainted by the same suspicions, while Alkrist was finished by his confession and the physical evidence. Kalan expected Teles Ahvoste or Fheminor to be elevated instead, after which the new leader would be motivated to force Bookwyrm either to submit to a Zone of Truth or surrender her place among the Avowed.

The party added a cunning wrinkle of its own. They would collect their reward and use it to obtain some tome or magical resource capable of definitively establishing Bookwyrm's role as an accomplice. If she believed Alkrist's confession had brought the investigation to an end, they could return with proof when she least expected it. Kalan enthusiastically approved of this delayed approach and promised to identify a suitable book before the party left Candlekeep. Thorin envisioned the eventual confrontation as a properly dramatic “one more thing” moment in the style of Columbo.

### 062.03 Confrontation in Bookwyrm's Office

Fembris Lancer returned from a discreet distance to report that Bookwyrm wished to see them. The confrontation in her office was a masterclass in controlled tension. She received them with regal composure, poured tea, and gestured for them to sit. The party declined the tea with varying degrees of suspicion: Thorin produced his own flask, while Zalthir quietly directed Glabbagool to absorb the tea through his arm. The ooze burned it away with acid, leaving only a faint singe that Zalthir noticed.

Bookwyrm initially dismissed Alkrist's confession as the product of a borrowed compulsion spell, even citing Kalan's own paper against it. Kalan responded by laying out the other evidence: Alkrist had obtained the poison, possessed the relevant book, and confessed. Bookwyrm then appeared to collapse in heartbreak. Daz's careful scrutiny revealed something beneath the performance, however: she did not seem genuinely sad and instead looked curiously relieved.

Bookwyrm acknowledged that her nephew had committed a grave crime but argued that trials and judgments should wait until after the next head of the Avowed was named. The party debated how much of Alkrist's accusation against A'lai—and how much of their suspicion of Bookwyrm—to reveal. They ultimately said only that Alkrist appeared to have had accomplices and that the investigation was continuing. Bookwyrm performed outrage beautifully, declaring it unthinkable that anyone else could have conspired in the death of the “beloved” Janussi.

### 062.04 The Opening of Alkrist's Safe

The group also tried to close several evidentiary gaps. They still had not found the missing key or determined who had performed the suspected polymorph. A Locate Object attempt failed to find the sapphire on Bookwyrm, indicating that she did not have it with her. Kalan ordered Alkrist imprisoned and promised that his safe would be guarded if it could not be opened immediately.

A search of Alkrist's safe produced little of obvious consequence: a potion of flying, a potion of superior healing, and 750 gold pieces, including a coin bearing a literal red herring. While the Avowed dismissed the contents and closed the safe, Zalthir used Glabbagool to quietly pocket the potion of flying. Everyone was too distracted by the investigation and Candlekeep's politics to notice.

Bookwyrm then declared the party's assigned task complete, praised their work, and publicly acknowledged the value of Kalan's investigative methodology. She apologized for removing him and offered him his former position. Kalan accepted graciously but warned that his reinstatement would not alter the report he intended to deliver before the naming ceremony. Bookwyrm assured him that she expected complete honesty and promised the party an opportunity to state its suspicions before the Council of the Avowed.

### 062.05 Waiting in Deneir's Sanctum

She arranged for Kalan to escort the party to Deneir's Sanctum, supposedly to protect the possessors of Candlekeep's most important evidence from the other criminal elements still at large. When Zalthir asked whether they were under house arrest, Bookwyrm emphatically denied it. Zalthir read her concern for their safety as sincere, but Daz saw that she was equally delighted by the prospect of keeping them contained and unable to investigate further.

The evening in Deneir's Sanctum passed quietly at first. Fembris remained with the party as part of his punishment, and food arrived around evening. Distrusting anything served inside Candlekeep, the party let Fembris eat first and jokingly adopted the practice of using him as their official taster.

Daral continued pleading to be cured. Concluding that he had probably played no role in the murder and had nothing more to reveal, the party allowed Dawnbringer to heal him. Overjoyed, Daral announced that he would make sentient magical swords—and Dawnbringer's intelligence and beauty in particular—a major subject of future research. He launched into an extended academic discourse on the semiotics of magical swords, which Dawnbringer eagerly absorbed. Daral then tried to leave, only to discover that the door remained locked.

### 062.06 Ambush of the Helmed Horrors

The peace shattered without warning. Three helmed horrors—towering animated suits of plate armor with cold, glowing eyes—flew to the sanctum, smashed through its windows and door, and attacked. Daral reacted with disbelief, insisting that such creatures could not possibly be there. The constructs ignored Gyrgum and other convenient targets to converge almost entirely on Daz. Their behavior was not emotional or retaliatory: as constructs, they were following an objective.

Daz opened with three scorching rays, striking one horror for twenty-four points of fire damage. Another horror battered Zalthir with slashing and force, while the horrors surrounding Daz nearly killed him. One devastating sequence of blows dealt thirty-two damage and left him standing with only three hit points. At that moment Daz realized what made him different from the other targets: he carried the mysterious key, which had appeared inert under Detect Magic.

This realization made the attack even more disturbing. Gyrgum had previously held the key and passed it to Daz in Fembris's presence, yet the horrors had immediately gone for its current bearer. Daz handed the key back to Gyrgum, and the constructs redirected their efforts toward him, confirming that they were tracking the object rather than Daz himself.

The battle was fierce and disorienting. Zalthir drove his fists into the animated armor and grappled his targets while Glabbagool unleashed corrosive embraces that ate through their plating and lowered their defenses. Gyrgum released a Word of Radiance that seared two horrors at once and repeatedly assured the badly wounded Daz that he had ample healing—and even Revivify—available if matters became worse. Daz continued attacking rather than retreating, using Magic Missile to bypass the uncertainty surrounding the horrors' magical defenses.

Thorin struck with Dawnbringer and used a sapping blow to impose disadvantage on one horror's next attack. When that construct attacked Gyrgum instead of him, Thorin's training allowed him to retaliate immediately and destroy it before its second blow could land. Zalthir and Glabbagool brought down another, then grappled and corroded the last. Thorin closed and delivered the final pair of strikes, reducing the remaining horror to rust and broken armor.

The party stood amid the wreckage deeply unsettled. The assassins could not speak, could not be interrogated, and had possessed no will of their own. Nevertheless, they had known exactly where to find the party and exactly who held a key that had read as magically inert. Fembris had witnessed the earlier exchange of the key, leaving open whether the constructs had sensed it directly or had been sent by someone with access to that information.

## NPCs

### Kalan Strongbranch

An academic investigator and stickler for his own Threefold Proof methodology. He argues that Alkrist's confession alone is insufficient to convict Bookwyrm, insisting that a single magical source of truth cannot establish a complete case. He refuses to ambush Bookwyrm with a Zone of Truth and favors using the naming ceremony to strip away her political protection first.

Kalan expects suspicion to prevent both Bookwyrm and A'lai from becoming the new head of the Avowed, with Teles Ahvoste or Fheminor likely to prevail. He promises a complete report and agrees to identify a book that the party can request as its reward and later use against Bookwyrm. After presenting the evidence against Alkrist, he is publicly reinstated and becomes extremely pleased that the party has vindicated his scholarly methods. His renewed authority also places him back in charge of the guard.

### Bookwyrm

The current First Reader and acting head of Candlekeep—real name Skoda Vanaster, a green dragonborn—and Alkrist's aunt. She receives the party with regal composure and tea, then attempts to discredit Alkrist's confession by invoking the weaknesses identified in Kalan's own paper.

When Kalan presents the poison, book, and confession together, Bookwyrm performs heartbreak but is observed by Daz to be curiously relieved. She reacts theatrically to the possibility of accomplices and repeatedly calls Janussi beloved. She does not possess the sapphire sought through Locate Object.

Bookwyrm reinstates Kalan, promises the party a hearing before the naming ceremony, and places them under close protection in Deneir's Sanctum. Her concern for their safety is sincere, but she is also pleased to prevent them from pursuing further inquiries.

### Alkrist

Bookwyrm's nephew, who has confessed to killing Janussi and has named A'lai as the mastermind. Evidence connects him to the poison and the relevant book, but the party remains convinced that his actions do not explain the missing heart, the concealed evidence, the suspected polymorph, the pigeons, or the missing key.

Kalan orders Alkrist confined securely in Candlekeep's prison so he cannot escape. His safe contains money and useful potions but no further incriminating evidence.

### Janussi

The deceased whose murder is the central mystery of the investigation. His practice of giving harsh reviews prompted the party to observe that any number of magically powerful academics might have wanted him dead.

Multiple characters refer to him with exaggerated reverence as “beloved by all” and lament his “untimely demise,” even though many of the people around him appear privately pleased that he is gone. He had previously rejected Kalan's investigative paper.

### Fembris Lancer

The party's adjutant, who steps away during the party's private deliberations and returns with Bookwyrm's summons. He remains with the party while they are confined to Deneir's Sanctum, apparently as a punishment.

Fembris becomes an unwitting food taster when the suspicious party lets him eat the delivered meal first. He was also present when Gyrgum handed the mysterious key to Daz. Because the horrors subsequently attacked its actual bearer, Fembris's knowledge of the exchange makes him a person of interest, though the party also considers the possibility that the constructs could detect the key independently.

### Daral

A man the party suspected had been poisoned after licking his finger while handling the poison-laced book. By this point, the party no longer believes he played any role in the murder or has useful information left to reveal.

Dawnbringer cures him in Deneir's Sanctum. In gratitude, Daral promises to make sentient magical swords an active area of research and begins an extended scholarly discourse about Dawnbringer's sentience, beauty, themes, and semiotics. Dawnbringer enthusiastically welcomes the attention. Daral remains trapped in the locked sanctum with the party when the helmed horrors attack and reacts with disbelief that the constructs could be there.

### Helmed Horrors

Three mindless animated suits of plate armor with glowing eyes, capable of walking, flight, and hovering. They possess formidable armor, general resistance against magic, and immunity to three spells selected by their creator.

They smash into Deneir's Sanctum and pursue whoever carries the mysterious key, first converging on Daz and then redirecting toward Gyrgum after the handoff. Their target selection demonstrates purpose rather than emotion. They cannot speak or identify their master and collapse into piles of rust and ruined armor when destroyed.

A sentient magical sword wielded by Thorin that communicates directly with him and can produce sounds in his mind. Dawnbringer remains invested in the therapy begun with Candlekeep's scholars and feels that the initial session established genuine progress and connection.

Dawnbringer cures Daral of his suspected poisoning. His promise to undertake extensive academic research into sentient magical swords greatly pleases her. Thorin also wields Dawnbringer against the helmed horrors, using a sapping strike to hinder one construct before destroying it with a retaliatory blow.

### The Threefold Proof

An academic paper written by Kalan Strongbranch detailing rigorous standards for criminal conviction and emphasizing that a single source of truth is insufficient. Kalan uses it to argue that Alkrist's magically compelled confession cannot independently prove Bookwyrm's involvement, at one point shoving the paper toward the party and pointing directly at the relevant lines.

The paper had been denied and administratively buried at Bookwyrm's urging: she privately advised Janussi to deny it, log it as incomplete research, restrict its circulation, and keep Kalan away from sensitive inquiries—a motive detail, given that she is a suspect. Despite never being formally approved, Kalan remains committed to its methodology, and Bookwyrm later cites its principles before publicly praising and vindicating them.

### Potion of Flying

A magical elixir found in Alkrist's safe. While everyone else is preoccupied with the investigation and the surrounding political spectacle, Zalthir uses his Glabbagool arm to steal it. His sleight of hand succeeds, and the Avowed close the safe without noticing that it is gone.

### Potion of Superior Healing

A potent restorative draught discovered among Alkrist's belongings in his private safe. Unlike the potion of flying, it remains in the safe when the Avowed declare the contents unimportant and close it.

### Red Herring Coin

A gold coin with a literal red herring engraved on it, found among the 750 gold pieces in Alkrist's safe. It provides no useful evidence and serves as a fitting joke about the investigation's many misleading trails.

### The Inert Key

A mysterious key that appears non-magical under detection spells but becomes the apparent objective of the helmed horror assassins. Gyrgum had passed it to Daz earlier in Fembris's presence. The horrors ignore convenient targets and immediately converge on Daz, revealing that they either know or can sense who currently possesses it.

After the constructs nearly kill him, Daz passes the key back to Gyrgum. Their attention shifts accordingly, demonstrating that they are tracking the key rather than Daz personally. They do not know the key is inert. As of session end, the party has not determined whether the key is genuine, why it reads inert, how the horrors located it, who sent them, or whether one of the ruined constructs might be used to locate the matching key.

## Spells

### Zone of Truth

Previously used to compel Alkrist's confession. Kalan cast it without Alkrist realizing what was happening until it was too late.

The party considers using the spell against Bookwyrm. A target can resist it with a saving throw, but the caster knows whether the spell has taken hold and therefore whether the target remains capable of lying. Kalan refuses to use the same concealed tactic against Bookwyrm and instead intends to demand her voluntary submission, either immediately or after a new leader is selected.

### Locate Object

Cast in an attempt to find a specific sapphire on Bookwyrm's person during the investigation. The spell fails to locate the object, indicating that she does not have it with her. The party had hoped the attempt might expose the sapphire as plainly as calling a hidden phone and hearing it ring in someone's pocket.

### Scorching Ray

Cast by Daz after he decides against using a psychic illusion on a construct. He directs all three rays of magical flame at the same helmed horror, striking it for a total of twenty-four fire damage.

### Word of Radiance

Cast by Gyrgum after he positions himself beside two helmed horrors. The burst of holy radiance damages both constructs simultaneously for ten points each.

### Magic Missile

Cast by Daz while he has only three hit points remaining. Its unerring darts avoid the difficulty of striking the horrors' heavy armor and deal fifteen damage despite the uncertainty surrounding their magical resistance and specific spell immunities.

## Abilities

### Corrosive Embrace

A ki-powered ability—not a spell—delivered by Glabbagool, a monk sidekick drawing on his own ki pool while fused to Zalthir's arm. After Zalthir lands a blow and grapples a target, Glabbagool forces it to resist the embrace and then inflicts physical acid corrosion.

Because the acid is physical rather than magical, it bypasses the helmed horrors' Magic Resistance. Glabbagool uses Corrosive Embrace twice during the battle, dealing acid damage and melting portions of the constructs' plate armor so that their defenses are reduced.
