# Chapter 44 From Zuggtmoy to Entémoch's Boon

Date: 2026-01-19

## Scenes
### 044.01 Battle with the Shambling Mound

The party found themselves locked in fierce combat with a Shambling Mound in the Festering Fissure, while charmed Violet Fungus stood by in confusion. The DM reminded Daz that as an evoker he could sculpt spells. Daz unleashed a carefully controlled explosion of fire, sculpting the flames to avoid both Zalthir and the precious lichen they had come to harvest. A natural-20 Arcana check told Daz that the 75-hit-point Mound resisted cold and fire and was immune to lightning. The party switched to radiant and force damage. The Shambling Mound proved resilient, shrugging off magical assaults with uncanny luck: it rolled natural 20s on back-to-back saves against Maximilian's Earthen Grasp and Tasha's Caustic Brew. Zalthir's relentless strikes and Thorin's devastating blows eventually brought the creature down. Its end came when Zalthir deflected its slam and Thorin landed an 8-point opportunity attack.

#### The party continues their intense combat against a Shambling Mound and charmed Violet Fungus in the Festering Fissure.
- The Violet Fungus remained stunned and charmed at the start of the round. Zalthir was no longer stunned, but he and the Shambling Mound were grappling each other ("We're passionately grappling in both directions").
- Daz casts Fireball, carefully sculpting the spell to avoid hitting Zalthir and the valuable lichen, targeting only the area with the Violet Fungus.
  - The DM reminded Daz of his evoker Sculpt Spells ability.
  - The DM warned: "The only real danger of firing the fireball is that it might destroy all the lichen."
  - The table also clarified that the fungi were already charmed and did not need to be killed.
- Daz rolls a natural 20 on his check to learn the Shambling Mound is resistant to cold and fire damage, and immune to lightning.
  - The DM tied this to a class on "creatures that were created as a result of magical experiments gone awry," and suggested radiant or force damage instead.
  - The DM ribbed Zalthir for skipping the same class.
- Daz casts Magic Missile, dealing minimal damage to the Shambling Mound. The roll was 1d4+1 = 2 per missile, for 6 total. DM: "the target barely registers."
- The DM revealed the Mound has 75 hit points: "This was why the whole plan of Zalthir stepping in and stealing the thing was such a good one... But then there were bad rolls and the universe hated you."
- Gyrgum casts Guiding Bolt, hitting the Shambling Mound with radiant damage and granting advantage to the next attack against it. The spell attack rolled 17 against AC 15, and the bolt dealt 13 damage.
- The Shambling Mound attempts to draw Daz closer with its reaction, but Daz successfully resists with a Wisdom save of 14.
- The Shambling Mound attacks Thorin, dealing 10 points of damage.
  - Its first attack had disadvantage from Thorin's earlier effect and missed. Its second hit with a 23.
- The DM read the Mound's flavor text: "The smell of rot and stagnant water is overwhelming, rushing over you in a fetid wave as the shambling mound reaches its full height."
- Zalthir punches the Shambling Mound multiple times with advantage from the Guiding Bolt and his grappling feat, landing hits for 10 and 9 damage.
- Thorin attacks the Shambling Mound, knocking it prone with a Hill Strike and dealing additional damage.
  - The first hit did 13 damage. The Hill Strike added 5 more and forced a DC 15 Strength save, which the Mound failed.
  - Thorin's player: "I don't know what it means for a shambling mound to be prone, but it knocked it on its ass."
  - Thorin's follow-up attack with advantage dealt another 10.

  - The Mound was down to 8 hit points, and Gyrgum upcast the spell at 2nd level.
  - Gyrgum, on the aftermath: "Can you imagine cleaning all that stuff off of the floor?"
- The Shambling Mound attacks Zalthir, but Zalthir uses Deflect Attacks to take no damage, and Thorin's opportunity attack deals the last 8 points, killing the creature. Its first attack (an 8) missed; the second was bludgeoning damage, which Zalthir deflected.
- The Violet Fungi remain charmed and "very confused" after the fight.

### 044.02 Harvesting the Lichen

As they finished harvesting the rare lichen needed for an acid-resistant weapon coating, a chilling voice echoed in their minds. It was Zuggtmoy, the Lady of Rot, speaking to the Pudding King about her sinister plans to use oozes to "soften the shell" so her garden could "take root in the flesh beneath." She ended with "Digest them all. I will claim this outpost." The DM then reminded the party that pursuing Entémoch's boon was optional. It would help summon Earth Elementals for the coming battle. Thorin summed up the case for going: "I mean, we could get some mad loot, just saying."

#### After defeating the Shambling Mound, the party successfully harvests the rare lichen needed for an acid-resistant weapon coating.
- Gyrgum performs careful chanting during the harvesting process for luck. Gyrgum: "Well, let's chant. It sounds good anyway. I've got a nice voice."
- The DM called for three checks: Nature, Sleight of Hand, and Arcana or Religion.
- Daz rolls a 22 on an Arcana check to identify the purest lichen. Daz has +10 Arcana.
- The party rolls a 26 on a Nature check for careful handling. Gyrgum had offered his +7 Nature.
- Despite Zalthir's poor sleight of hand, Daz and Gyrgum's combined efforts secure enough good lichen to compensate.
  - Nobody had better than +1 Sleight of Hand.

- Gyrgum: "I know those chanting lessons would come in handy someday."

### 044.03 Zuggtmoy's Psychic Message

The party arrived at Pickshine Mine, where four massive Galeb Duhr blocked the path. They were vibrating rhythmically like stones singing the first half of a forgotten duet. Dasco Pickshine, one of the mine's owners, had tried everything to move them, even striking them with his pickaxe, but the ancient sentinels refused to budge. Gyrgum sensed they were waiting for divine authority. Daz perceived their vibrations as a linguistic pattern, a call waiting for an echo that never came. The DM framed it as a broken network handshake: the stones were sending packets and never getting an acknowledgement. After discovering resonant geodes and arranging them while humming, Daz completed the ancient handshake protocol, and the Galeb Duhr finally relaxed and opened the way. Dasco first suggested the party shouldn't expect payment. Daz answered with a veiled threat to put everything back the way they found it. Dasco then grudgingly offered, through a crowd of miners, two ruby gems worth five hundred gold pieces as thanks.

#### As the party finishes harvesting, they are pulled into a psychic conference call where Zuggtmoy reveals her sinister plans.
- The party hears Zuggtmoy's voice in their heads, recognized immediately by Thorin and Gyrgum. The DM: "It feels like they've been added to a psychic conference call that you just got placed on."
- Zuggtmoy speaks to the Pudding King, saying the ooze will 'soften the shell' so her garden may 'take root in the flesh beneath'. She opens with "the ooze is crude, my little king" and adds "Digest them all."
- Zuggtmoy declares her intention to claim the outpost, revealing her plans to use the ooze crisis to expand her influence.
- The party realizes they need to deal with the Pudding King to save the gnomes from Zuggtmoy's plot.
  - Daz observed that the demons of the underdark "never waste a crisis and are all trying to use the various crises to raise their station."
  - The DM: "I mean, they are demons."
- The DM recapped the Entémoch thread. After the hollow was hallowed, the gnome leaders asked where Entémoch's boon was. A deep gnome wizard spread a map and said: "The stone prince is silent. The bane is gone, but the earth isn't healing. It's frozen. The tunnels to his throne have shifted and sealed themselves. However, our miners in Pickshine and Whiteshell are reporting strange vibrations, like the rock is trying to speak, but can't find the words."
- The DM stressed that the boon (help summoning Earth Elementals for the battle) was optional. The party chose to continue.

### 044.04 The Galeb Duhr Puzzle at Pickshine Mine

The geode, now acting as a beacon locator, led them deeper into the earth to the Whiteshell Mine, Blingdenstone's largest salt mine. The mine was ominously quiet. Glabbagool stayed back because of the salt. There, Perigrog Scrapedust explained that a tunnel had become impassable. It was impossibly dry, with blinding crystallite walls and absolute, suffocating silence, and even the lizards refused to go down it. Shadows cast by torches appeared painted on the walls rather than flickering, as if time itself had stopped. Zalthir recalled a metaphysical puzzle from his training. Magical pressure had caused time to snag at this point, and breaking it directly would unleash a catastrophic explosion that would crush this tunnel and all the tunnels around it. The solution required creating a hole for the backlog of reality to pass through safely, which only a Shadow Monk could accomplish.

#### The party arrives at Pickshine Mine to find four Galeb Duhr blocking the path, vibrating rhythmically and refusing to move.
- The party encounters Dasco Pickshine, a sales-focused gnome frustrated by the Galeb Duhr blocking his mine operations. The DM: "Think of him as a sales VP, cares about quota, doesn't care about anything else."
- Four Galeb Duhr block the iron cart tracks, vibrating rhythmically and ignoring Dasco's attempts to move them, including being struck with a pickaxe.
  - DM: "A low rhythmic thrum vibrates through the soles of your boots. It sounds like a massive heart beating deep underground. Throm, thrum, silence."
  - Dasco, shouting: "By the contract of the Stoneheart Enclave, move."
  - The guardian "simply absorbs the blow and continues its endless, endless waiting rhythm."
- Daz perceives the vibrations as linguistic, like a call waiting for an echo that never returns, realizing the stones are 'singing the first half of a duet'.
  - Daz's drow senses tune out Dasco's shouting, since "You've heard people like this shout all your life."
  - DM: "It's a call followed by a wait, like a drum beating into a canyon, waiting for an echo that never returns."
- Gyrgum senses the Galeb Duhr are dutiful sentinels waiting for a divine 'signature' and not recognizing Dasco's authority.
  - DM: "they have reverted to their oldest, most primitive, primal instruction. Trust no one."
  - DM: "They are waiting for a signature that carries the weight of a god."
  - Gyrgum: "Ah, well, as one who carries the imprimatur of religion myself, how can I help?"
- The DM described the problem as a handshake protocol gone wrong. Somebody is "sending out the first two packets and waiting for a responding packet," gets no ACK, and has "defaulted to a denial traffic mode."
- Gyrgum holds up his holy symbol, causing the Galeb Duhr to pause and look at him, though they continue thrumming.
- Daz investigates and discovers resonant geodes that can mimic sounds.
  - Daz rolled Investigation 21.
  - Gyrgum: "Well, geodes aren't supposed to be useful. They're supposed to be pretty."
  - Daz had heard of such stones, which is how he recognized them.
- Daz arranges the geodes and hums, rolling a 21 on Arcana to successfully complete the 'handshake protocol'.
  - The geodes emit a "hollow, resonant, humming, faint, high-pitched note."
  - Held near the Galeb Duhr alongside Gyrgum's ritual, they identified the party as the good guys.
- The Galeb Duhr relax and open the path, convinced the party is a trusted authority.
- The geode begins to resonate and point toward the Whiteshell Mine, acting as a signal tracer for Entémoch's location.
- Dasco Pickshine, after a brief intimidation, offers the party two ruby gems worth 500 gold pieces as a token of appreciation.
  - Dasco opens with: "well, I don't think you're expecting any payment as a result of this."
  - Daz's threat to undo the fix leaves Dasco "very confused" and "very intimidated."
  - He then brings a crowd of three-foot-tall miners, who say: "thank you very much for your support and help. And as a token of our appreciation, we'd like to provide you two Ruby gems worth a total of 500 gold pieces."
  - Daz logged 500 gold in the party inventory. Gyrgum joked that party inventory is suspicious "because you never know when Whistler is going to try to take some of it."

### 044.05 The Time-Snagged Wall at Whiteshell Mine

The gnomes eagerly presented their specialized explosives, and Zalthir meditated on the challenge, channeling all his frustration into the void. With Gyrgum's blessing to aid him, and a +2 and advantage from the DM for the roleplay, Zalthir positioned himself as the explosives detonated. He executed a perfect maneuver (Acrobatics 23) to redirect the magical blast through shadow space. The wall shattered without collapsing the tunnels. The gnomes celebrated their success, largely ignoring the possibility that Zalthir had been blown to smithereens, while the party caught their breath with a short rest.

#### The party follows the geode to Whiteshell Mine and encounters a mysterious wall where time has stopped.
- On the way, the geode is "humming violently and pulling magnetically toward the south directly at the Whiteshell salt mine."
- The party arrives at the Whiteshell Mine, Blingdenstone's largest salt mine, and finds it eerily quiet: "what should be a very busy place is quiet, as if nobody is digging."
- They meet Perigrog Scrapedust, the mine's owner, who explains that a tunnel has become impassable.
  - Perigrog: "The heroes of Blingdenstone. In the flesh."
  - Perigrog: "I knew they would come here and save the day."
  - Gyrgum: "It's us and our friend the Ooz. Say hi to Glabbagool." Glabbagool had stayed back because of the salt. Gyrgum: "Next time we'll get you some boots."
  - Perigrog: "We have a serious problem. We can't get down." Even the lizards won't go down the tunnel.
  - Gyrgum said that normally they would just get Glabbagool to clear the way. The miners were offended: "This is not a digging problem."
- Perigrog describes the tunnel as impossibly dry with blinding crystallite walls and absolute, suffocating silence.
  - "The moisture is sucked from your mouth the moment you breathe."
  - "It wasn't the quiet of a sleeping house, but the absolute suffocating silence of the tomb. There was no rot here, no decay."
- He notes that shadows cast by torches appear painted on the wall rather than flickering, suggesting time has stopped. Gyrgum: "It's as if time stopped, huh?"
- Daz rolls a 28 on Arcana, triggering Zalthir's memory of a metaphysical puzzle from his training. Zalthir: "I know this metaphysical challenge."
- Zalthir explains the wall is a point where magical pressure has caused time to 'snag', and breaking it directly would cause a catastrophic explosion.
  - DM: "if you simply break it, the backlog of reality will crash forward all at once, crushing this tunnel and all the tunnels in this area instantly."
  - The fix: "you need to create a hole for it to pass through without touching the pillars of the earth."
  - Gyrgum compared it to surfacing from a deep dive and letting everything out slowly.
  - Gyrgum asked, "Can we direct them toward our enemies?" DM: "No, no, no."
- Zalthir recalls that a Shadow Monk can create a hole for the backlog of reality to pass through safely using Shadow Step.
  - Daz rolled Arcana 16 and Zalthir rolled History 19. Zalthir remembered a story of an ancient monk.
  - DM: "if you can time going into the shadow space, just as they break the wall, you could cause the explosion to be directed... to a completely different location."
- The party decides to use gnome explosives to breach the wall while Zalthir redirects the blast.
  - Zalthir: "These are gnomes, don't they have tons of explosives?" and "What could possibly go wrong?"
  - Daz first considered Magic Missile, then read the spell text: "Oh, it's like a creature of your choice that you see within range. Never mind." It can't target a wall.

### 044.06 Breaching the Wall with Shadow Step

Beyond the breached wall, they discovered a large cavern containing a summoning circle inscribed with runes in giant script. Two basilisks guarded the circle, moving with mechanical precision rather than natural animal behavior, as if they viewed the party as flaws in perfect stone. The party worked through the petrification rules:
- Looking at the basilisk is what causes petrification (DC 12 Constitution, 30-foot cone), not the basilisk seeing you.
- Averting your eyes means fighting as if blinded.
- Magical darkness blocks the gaze.
- Greater Restoration would undo petrification (the DM called Stone to Flesh an older-edition rule), and Gyrgum only has the lesser version.

#### The party executes a plan to safely breach the time-snagged wall using gnome explosives and Zalthir's Shadow Step ability.
- The gnomes eagerly present their specialized explosives called 'rude powder' for the operation. They are "all sort of championing their explosive device versus that explosive device."
- The DM invoked an old saying: wizards' life expectancy is very short after graduating, because they experiment "faster than a gnome does and blow themselves up." Daz: "I resemble that remark."
- Zalthir meditates on the challenge, channeling his frustration about gardening into the void to prepare.
- Gyrgum offered to cast Bless on Zalthir. After that offer and Zalthir's meditation roleplay, the DM gave +2 and advantage on the check.
- The gnomes set up the explosive charge against the wall, shaped to direct the blast. They built a platform beside Zalthir so he could step into the blast.
- Zalthir rolls a 23 on Acrobatics, successfully executing his Shadow Step as the explosives detonate. He chose Dexterity (Acrobatics) over Sleight of Hand.
- The magical blast is safely redirected through the void, and the wall is breached without collapsing the tunnels. DM: "some distance away, people hear the effect of the magic exploding, and the cave is still standing."
- The gnomes celebrate their successful explosion, high-fiving and crediting their precise setup.
- The mine boss claps: "All right, boys, let's get back to making money. You know, we're behind on quota." The miners growl, and the mine fills with the clink and clank of work again.
- The party takes a short rest before proceeding through the newly opened passage. Zalthir: "By the way, I just remembered last time I got pretty badly damaged. Is anyone able to heal, or can we take a short rest?"

### 044.07 Encounter in the Summoning Circle

Zalthir engaged one basilisk in close combat inside his darkness while averting his eyes. Daz successfully enchanted the other, freezing it in place with an incapacitating charm.

#### The party enters a large cavern containing a giant summoning circle guarded by two mechanical basilisks.
- The party discovers a large cavern with a summoning circle featuring runes in giant script rather than gnomish. Gyrgum: "How big are they?"
- Two basilisks appear in the circle, moving with mechanical precision rather than like natural creatures. Thorin: "Can we use all the tricks we used on the Medusa?"
- Daz observes the basilisks seem to be under some form of control, viewing the party as 'a flaw in the perfect stone'. DM: "they don't look like hungry animals. They move with mechanical precision."
- The party discusses strategies to avoid the basilisks' petrifying gaze, which requires a DC 12 Constitution save.
  - The gaze covers a 30-foot cone. Thorin has +6 Constitution.
  - Averting your eyes means fighting as if blinded, but the save is rolled with advantage.
  - The DM called Stone to Flesh an older-edition rule and named Greater Restoration as the cure. Gyrgum: "I only have lesser."
  - Staying more than 30 feet back is another option.
- They learn the gaze works by seeing the basilisk, not by the basilisk seeing them, and that magical darkness would block it.
  - The DM ruled that magical darkness blocks the gaze: "if dark vision can't penetrate, then its vision can't penetrate."
  - Thorin: "if we're not going to look, then it at least, then it'll be blinded too."
- The DM offered two options: Thorin could rush in and read the rune tablet, or the party could fight the basilisks head-on. Thorin volunteered: "I can tank it for a little bit and see what happens."
- Initiative is rolled: Zalthir, Gyrgum, Thorin, then Daz. Daz initially forgot to roll, then got a 14.
- Zalthir engages one basilisk in melee while averting his eyes, punching and grappling it.
  - He brought his darkness in.
  - Zalthir: "I have two advantages and a disadvantage. I think that means I have advantage."
  - Damage rolls were 14, 11, and 11.
  - DM: "the tavern brawler is a completely underrated feat in the hands of a monk."
- Daz successfully casts Hypnotic Pattern on the second basilisk, incapacitating it and reducing its speed to zero.
  - It was a DC 15 Wisdom save against the basilisk's -4; the DM rolled an 18 and it failed.
- Thorin rushes to the summoning circle to read the giant runes. Gyrgum deferred to him.

### 044.08 Unlocking Entémoch's Boon

While his companions held the basilisks at bay, Thorin rushed to the summoning circle and deciphered the giant runes. To free Entémoch and claim his boon, three sacrifices were required: wealth, a weapon, and blood. Thorin placed a dart onto the runes as the weapon sacrifice, and the stone instantly swallowed it, rusting it to dust in seconds. The runes transformed from yellow to blinding orange, the air grew hot, and the uncharmed basilisk became frantic as the ritual began. The party now stood on the threshold of completing their quest, with only two sacrifices remaining to unlock Entémoch's power. The DM warned that finishing would end the fight but would "take a few rounds."

#### Thorin deciphers the runes and begins the ritual to free Entémoch by making the required sacrifices.
- Thorin reads the giant runes and discovers that freeing Entémoch requires three sacrifices: wealth, a weapon, and blood.
  - The DM first described putting a ruby into a slot. Thorin: "Wait, there's slots now?"
  - DM: "to free Entémoch, you need to place, you need to sacrifice wealth, a weapon, and blood."
- The DM said that completing the sacrifices would end the fight "right away, but it'll take a few rounds to do it." The party noted they still hold two rubies.
- Thorin places a dart onto the runes as the weapon sacrifice. Thorin: "I take one of my darts and put it on there because that's a weapon and that's easily disposable."
- The stone instantly swallows the dart, rusting it to dust in seconds.
- The runes transform from yellow to blinding orange, and the air becomes hot.
- The uncharmed basilisk becomes frantic as the ritual begins, while the other basilisk "is still chilling."
- The session ends with the party needing to complete the remaining two sacrifices. Thorin: "All right, so we got to do three turns of this and we're good to go."
