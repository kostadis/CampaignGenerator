### 011.01 Interrogating the Duergar

With their larger boat secured and the battle behind them, the party turned their attention to their duergar prisoner, Brannum Redmarch. He introduced himself with a gruff attempt at dignity, distancing himself from the "mad" kuo-toa and their strange ways, though his bravado quickly crumbled under the weight of his situation. Barely conscious, he was surrounded by thirteen people he had just tried to enslave. Thorin fixed him with a cold, unflinching stare and made the terms of his survival perfectly clear: his only path to living was to guide them faithfully to Gracklstugh. At Gyrgum's suggestion, Brannum would ride in the party's own boat as a hostage, tied up, with Gyrgum's warning that if he tried to screw them over he would be the first to go. Sarith, visibly impressed by the directness of the approach, said he admired the technique, though he was a little disappointed that torture hadn't been deemed necessary. Thorin also floated the idea of selling Brannum into slavery, as turnabout for what he had tried to do to them. Buppido called it an excellent idea, and Hemeth announced that he "knew a guy." Brannum grumbled and muttered but ultimately began pointing the way forward across the Darklake. An insight check of 18 confirmed that he was being straight with them, because he knew staying alive depended on it.

### 011.02 The Bone Pyramid Islet

Days passed in the eerie quiet of the subterranean waterway, the sound of paddles lapping against the dark water echoing off unseen walls above. Blind cavefish drifted mindlessly near the surface, occasionally bumping against the hull of the boat, utterly unaware of the travelers passing through their world. On the fifteenth day of their voyage, the tunnel forked around a diamond-shaped limestone islet, forty feet wide, with its jagged stalagmites jutting upward from the water like broken teeth. What drew every eye, however, was the grim monument at its center. It was a pyramid of bones, eight feet high and five feet wide at its base, crowned with a great horned ram skull that had been carefully positioned so its empty sockets stared directly at them. Brannum Redmarch took one look at it and panicked, shouting "shit, dock! Do something!" and admitting "this was a mistake. I thought we were going the other way."

Before they could react, a violent beam of lightning erupted from behind a stalactite above the islet and tried to put a hole in their boat. The party made a group check to keep the vessel from taking on water. Not enough of them succeeded to avoid all harm, but their quick reflexes kept the boat seaworthy with only some damage. Then, rising from behind the great stalactite, a ten-foot floating sphere of rotting flesh and dead eyes drifted toward them: a beholder zombie, drawn by the commotion and hungry for destruction. Gyrgum cast Command and ordered the creature to flee, only to realize with a sinking feeling that the undead horror understood nothing he said. The battle was joined in earnest.

### 011.03 Battle with the Beholder Zombie

Zalthir moved with fluid precision, conjuring a 15-foot shroud of magical darkness around the creature while keeping just enough of it visible for his companions to target. He struck the zombie with his bare fists in a series of devastating blows, landing one thunderous critical hit that sent a shudder through the floating monstrosity. Thorin fought from inside the darkness, where the blinded creature could not see him, and hammered it with advantage. He used a feinting attack that left the zombie vexed, then tried a Hill Strike to knock it prone, only to learn that a floating beholder zombie is immune to being knocked prone. Daz unleashed a barrage of Magic Missiles for 15 damage, while Gyrgum channeled divine power to scorch the undead from within. The zombie retaliated viciously. A ray of sickly light, which the DM compared to a Dalek, hit Gyrgum for 10 damage and poisoned him. Another beam struck Daz, who missed the save by a single point and was left paralyzed and helpless.

With Daz paralyzed and the creature still looming, Stool, Hemeth, and the rest of the companion crew debated only briefly before rowing hard toward the fight. The crew raised their crossbows and let fly a first volley. The zombie bit Zalthir for 16 damage, and Gyrgum's attempt to Turn Undead failed against the creature's saving throw. The crew's second volley, combined with Zalthir's relentless assault, finally proved too much for the undead horror. The beholder zombie shuddered, crumpled, and exploded into ash, its threat extinguished at last, and Daz's paralysis ended with its death. Gyrgum moved quickly to Zalthir's side and cast Cure Wounds, restoring 15 of the 16 hit points the monk had lost to the creature's savage bite. With the immediate danger gone, the party turned back to Brannum and demanded the truth about the shortcut he had chosen. Thorin called his first answer "bullshit," and an insight check proved him right.

### 011.04 The Mystery of the Missing Crew

Slumping down in the boat, Brannum finally confessed. This route had once been a quick shortcut his crew used regularly, but two of his boats had never come back. One survivor had claimed there was some kind of evil altar here, and Brannum had assumed that crew had simply abandoned him, until he saw the bone pyramid and realized, with dawning horror, that they had not. "If they're dead, where are they?" he asked. There were bones, but there was no boat. Daz peered over the side into the dark water below and found the grim answer: the sunken wrecks of Brannum's lost boats rested on the lakebed, and bodies moved among them, possibly zombies. Gyrgum used his Mending cantrip on the damaged hull. The curious cleric also cast Find Traps on the islet and found none, but the party hesitated to set foot on it.

### 011.05 The Sunken Treasure of the Darklake

Among the wreckage, Daz spotted a small chest resting on the lakebed, surrounded by the undead. Daz first wanted to use Shape Water to float the chest up. The DM called that a stretch of the rules, though a clever one, and suggested an air bubble instead. Zalthir pointed out that someone other than a squishy magic-user should make the dive, so Thorin volunteered: "I'm going to regret this, but, you know, somebody's got to do it." The party devised a careful plan. Gyrgum cast Shield of Faith on Thorin, Daz shaped a bubble of breathable air, and Zalthir created magical darkness around the dive site to blind the zombies lurking below. Thorin slipped into the water and descended without making a sound, moving through the darkness with ease while the undead remained oblivious. He returned to the surface with the chest, and not a single zombie had stirred. When they pried it open, they found 120 silver and 30 gold pieces, four spell scrolls, and a drifting globe of light. Brannum immediately suggested he deserved a share, since the treasure had belonged to his crew. Daz dismissed the idea by inviting him to argue it out with the zombies below and twinkling his fingers to send water spraying up around the duergar.

### 011.06 Ambush by an Aquatic Troll

Two more days of paddling brought them within a day of Gracklstugh, and then a new threat rose from the depths. Zalthir and Daz noticed it just in time: an enormous aquatic troll surging up alongside the boat. Speaking in the rumbling tongue of giants, it demanded that the party hand over one of their number for a meal. Daz suggested giving it Brannum, but Gyrgum refused: "I'm neutral good here. I don't think I can be tossing people to the trolls." Thorin called for them to run. Jimjar shouted "Does anybody have fire?" and warned that the creature would simply regenerate from any wound unless it was burned with fire or acid. Daz's Scorching Ray missed, but his Magic Missiles hit for 9. The troll grabbed the boat and pulled it down, raking Daz with a claw, though its bite missed. Zalthir struck the troll hard enough that it released the hull, and his class technique shoved it five feet back. Gyrgum, with a Strength of only 8 and no use at the oars, vomited forth a wave of acid with Tasha's Caustic Brew, but the troll twisted away from the worst of it. Brannum offered to grow to giant size and help row if they untied him. Thorin refused, reasoning that it would end with the duergar smashing the boat or killing them all. Thorin and the others threw their backs into the oars. The troll, singed and frustrated, sank back into the dark water with a grumbling complaint that it had only been trying to help by reducing the number of mouths the party had to feed.

### 011.07 Arrival at Gracklstugh

At last, weary and battle-worn, the party arrived at Gracklstugh. Buppido and Hemeth explained the city's advantages. The Duergar had no love for the Drow, and their draconian laws kept all foreigners in line, offering a measure of safety from any Drow pursuit. The duergar also traded actively with other subterranean and surface races. The city's Blade Bazaar was said to be the ideal place to sell stolen gear, buy better equipment, and find traders or explorers heading to the surface world. Gyrgum voiced some concern about those laws against foreigners. Hemeth warned the group that this was "not the time or place for heroics" and advised them to enter quietly through the docks, because "The guards guard the doors, not the docks." As the boats glided toward the dark harbor, the party also began to discuss what Brannum Redmarch might be worth as a ransom. Thorin pointed out that they had never promised to let him go, and anyway he had tried to sell them first. They also counted up their funds: 27 platinum pieces and 1,100 gold pieces, which Zalthir briefly mistook for a small fortune in platinum.

## NPCs

He has very low Charisma, which makes his lies easy to read. A companion of the party who expresses distrust toward the duergar and their traditions, calling them followers of an evil god.

A companion traveling with the party who, after some debate, decided to help the adventurers fight the beholder zombie.

## Locations
### Darklake
A vast, eerie subterranean network of waterways in the Underdark. The party navigates a narrow tunnel characterized by dripping water, blind cavefish, an eerie purple luminescence, and hidden dangers including a beholder zombie, aquatic zombies, and a hungry aquatic troll.

### Bone Pyramid Islet
A diamond-shaped, 40-foot-wide limestone islet in the Darklake where the tunnel forks left and right. It features a macabre eight-foot-high, five-foot-wide pyramid of bones topped with a horned ram skull that gazes down the tunnel. It was once a shortcut used by Brannum's crew, and a survivor spoke of an "evil altar" there. Sunken boats and zombie-infested waters lie beneath it. Find Traps revealed no traps, and the party never set foot on it.

### Gracklstugh
A subterranean Duergar city known for its draconian laws regarding foreigners and the Blade Bazaar. It serves as a potential sanctuary from Drow pursuit due to the Duergar's lack of affection for the Drow. Its guards watch the doors rather than the docks.

### Blade Bazaar
A major trading hub within Gracklstugh where the party hopes to sell stolen gear, buy new equipment, and find information on reaching the surface.
