# Chapter 42 Zalthir's Reflection, Thorin's Stand

Date: 2025-12-22

Attendance: Zalthir's player was absent; the GM ran Zalthir.

## Scenes
### 042.01 Defeating the Medusa

The party stood victorious over the Medusa, whose serpentine hair lay still after a harrowing battle. Throughout the fight, Zalthir showed remarkable composure, repeatedly deflecting the creature's deadly petrifying gaze by shoving an ice mirror in her face. He kept that tactic ready as a prepared action. The Medusa cursed him with venomous taunts about his draconic heritage, the circumstances of his birth, and the idea that some people see Dragonborn as "a chemical experiment gone horribly wrong." The monk still kept the restraint expected of his order.

Earlier, Daz tried to pin the Medusa with Maximilian's Earthen Grasp, and she beat the DC 15 Strength save with an 18. The GM then ruled that Zalthir would have stopped him first, since he could grapple her in place, so Daz cast Scorching Ray instead. It came up with three 1s out of six dice. Zalthir struck her with advantage while she lay prone from Thorin's Hill Strike, dealing 19 damage. When her gaze was reflected back at her, she failed her save and was restrained by her own power, and the party pressed their advantage. The GM remarked that the current rules "got rid of all of the single save and throw death things."

The Medusa's attacks soon knocked Zalthir unconscious. He had given up his usual darkness because the mirror needed light. Gyrgum stayed outside the 30-foot cone of her gaze and rolled a natural 20 on Guiding Bolt, dealing 22 radiant damage. Thorin then took up the ice mirror, held it as a prepared action, and cut into the Medusa with Dawnbringer. With the Medusa down to about eight hit points, the GM suggested Magic Missile. Daz took the final shot for 12 damage and ended the creature's life. Zalthir was brought back to 1 hit point, dusted himself off, and asked, "How do you people fight in the light?"

Searching the Medusa's lair, the adventurers found five exquisite dresses made of spider silk and shaped by drow magic, each worth 200 gold pieces in the markets of the Underdark. The treasure went into the party inventory, to be divided later. Glabbagool, the intelligent gelatinous cube who traveled with them, was delighted to taste the Medusa's remains. He cheerfully noted that the party provided an excellent supply of dietary flavoring as he slowly cleaned up the aftermath of battle. The GM observed that the party no longer needed to carry proof of the kill.

### 042.02 Hallowing the Temple

With the Medusa defeated, the party turned to their true objective. They meant to hallow the ancient temple against Ogrémoch's Bane, an evil force from the elemental plane of earth. The GM stressed that the goal "isn't so much to get rid of it, but to hallow the area." Gyrgum answered, "I'm pretty good at hallowing things. I am a cleric." As the party's cleric and self-described "official emissary of whatever gods are appropriate for this place," he volunteered to place the Ruby spell gem that Gurnik Tapfinger had provided. It went into the great menhir at the temple's center, and Gurnik had assured them that "nothing bad would happen."

The players were openly skeptical. Thorin asked why every room was a battle arena, and Gyrgum joked that one "might summon a world-ending dragon, but that's merely a side effect." The chamber itself was ominous. Daz's passive Perception of 15 revealed rough walls that outlined several large, vaguely humanoid shapes. Gyrgum placed the gem while most of the party hung back. Thorin and Zalthir took the center, convinced the threat would come out of the walls. When the gem began to glow against the menhir, the walls began to rumble ominously.

### 042.03 First Earth Elemental Battle

Part of the wall shifted and detached, revealing an Earth Elemental that charged toward the party with hostile intent. Thorin wondered aloud whether they could talk it down, but the GM explained two problems. Ogrémoch's spirit drives these elementals insane, and none of the party spoke the elemental's language. Daz then cast Hypnotic Pattern, a mesmerizing pattern of swirling colors, which incapacitated the creature and dropped its speed to zero. Knowing the enchantment would break the moment the elemental took damage, the party coordinated their efforts and readied their attacks to land all at once. When Daz's Magic Missile shattered the spell's hold, Zalthir rushed forward, rolled a natural 20, and delivered a devastating series of blows. Gyrgum's first Guiding Bolt of the follow-up round hit for 17. The elemental whiffed its counterattacks with a 5 and then a 1. Gyrgum's next bolt was a critical hit, a brilliant flash of divine light that struck for 36 damage and reduced the creature to rubble.

### 042.04 The Galeb Duhr Appears

As the first elemental fell, a Galeb Duhr materialized from the stone: a boulder-like ancient stone guardian with stumpy limbs. Daz's Arcana check of 26 recalled that these creatures are lawfully bound to their charge and cannot be bribed. He also knew the drow value them as a "fire and forget security system" and use "doorstop" as slang for them. The party cautiously tried to communicate with it. The creature spoke in measured tones and explained that by defeating the elemental, they had freed it from Ogrémoch's control. However, it could do nothing until all of its brothers were similarly freed. The Galeb Duhr revealed that Ogrémoch had infected the temple and kept these guardians from their sacred duty to protect it. Even as it spoke, another Earth Elemental emerged from the northern wall.

### 042.05 Second Earth Elemental Battle

The second battle proved more challenging, and the elemental focused its attacks on Zalthir, dealing significant damage to the monk. The GM noted that the party was assumed to have taken a short rest after the Medusa. The party fought with determination. Gyrgum's radiant bolts and Daz's necrotic magic weakened the creature, and Thorin engaged it directly with his glowing sword Dawnbringer. Thorin chose to hold his Action Surge in reserve "and see if things get really desperate." Zalthir took heavy blows but kept fighting. When the elemental seemed poised to overwhelm him, Thorin intercepted one of its attacks with a Sentinel opportunity attack, and his Sap had already imposed disadvantage on the elemental's swing. The battle turned when Gyrgum declined to spend a healing spell and cast True Strike through his crossbow. The divine energy he channeled through the weapon pierced the elemental's stony hide for 19 damage and destroyed it.

### 042.06 Third Earth Elemental Battle

A second Galeb Duhr appeared as the second elemental crumbled, but so did another Earth Elemental. It startled Gyrgum, who yelped as it materialized behind him. The cleric quickly backed "the heck away" to a safer position while Daz unleashed a Fireball, a massive explosion that scorched the creature for 27. Zalthir, wounded from the previous fight, hung back about 15 feet and drank a Potion of Greater Healing from the party's supplies before preparing to rejoin the fray. Thorin positioned himself to draw the charge, with two Dawnbringer attacks readied. As the elemental rushed toward him, he struck it twice with devastating precision for 9 and 23.

The third battle became a test of the party's remaining resources and endurance. The elemental struck Thorin with crushing blows, but the fighter stood firm, his magical sword cutting deep into the creature's rocky form. Daz's fiery rays found their mark repeatedly, while Gyrgum provided support from range. Thorin decided "now's the time" and drew on his Action Surge for a flurry of additional attacks. His four hits totaled 39 damage and left the elemental at 15 hit points. Finally, Zalthir stepped forward and delivered an Enhanced Unarmed Strike that shattered the creature into fragments. As the dust settled, a third Galeb Duhr materialized from the stone. The GM called the session there, noting the fight was "a bit more longer than our usual combats," and wished everyone a Merry Christmas and Happy New Year.

She cursed and taunted the party during combat, especially Zalthir, with insults about his draconic heritage and the circumstances of his birth. She was defeated when her own petrifying gaze was reflected back at her with a mirror, restraining her before the party finished her off.

- She rolled an 18 against DC 15 on Maximilian's Earthen Grasp before the GM took the cast back.
- She resisted Thorin's second Hill Strike but had been knocked prone by the first.
- In the GM's words: "I really want Zalthir dead. Not me, but if I was a Medusa."
- She dropped Zalthir to unconsciousness before falling to Daz's Magic Missile.
