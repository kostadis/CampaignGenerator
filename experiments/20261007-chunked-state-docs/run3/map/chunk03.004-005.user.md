The drow and their quaggoth servants scrambled to their defensive positions, no longer concerned with their prisoners in the slightest. The companions recognized that they could not survive a fight against the creatures and decided to use the confusion to escape.

### 004.04 Trekking into the Underdark

Twisting tunnels, uneven ground, climbs, and indirect routes reduced travel to eight miles per day at a fast pace, six at a normal pace, or four while moving slowly. Fast travel prevented foraging and made danger harder to notice, while slow travel allowed stealth and improved the group's chances of finding food. Becoming lost was a serious threat, so Sarith stepped forward as navigator and appeared to be telling the truth about his knowledge of the passages. The party also arranged a marching order, keeping sturdier companions toward the front and the unarmored Daz farther back.

The group learned of the Faerzress, a strange and ancient magical energy that suffused parts of the Underdark. It bathed affected passages in dim light, protected creatures from divination, and made teleportation both unreliable and physically dangerous. Even a successful attempt to teleport could go badly astray. The phenomenon helped explain why the Underdark's powers could not simply teleport armies into one another's strongholds.

Topsy and Turvy did not want to return to Blingdenstone after a disagreement with its inhabitants concerning their “profession,” reinforcing the widespread suspicion that the twins were thieves. Gracklstugh lay roughly twenty-eight days away, while Sloobludop—the home sought by Shuushar—was only eight days distant. Sarith explained that the Darklake was not a single lake but an enormous network of underground rivers, canals, natural tunnels, and flooded caverns extending for more than a hundred miles, with ceilings miles high and unfathomable depths. Reaching it would allow the party to find or steal a boat and travel onward toward Gracklstugh or Blingdenstone. Buppido approved because the route could eventually take him to Gracklstugh.
