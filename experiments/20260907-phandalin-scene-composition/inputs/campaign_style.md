# Phandalin style for this experiment

Character-centered literary fantasy with earnest moral conviction, dark and absurd comedy, political intrigue, and concrete physical life. The comedy comes from these people's incompatible assumptions and the world answering them; it does not require a detached narrator explaining the joke. Give the places and encounters enough space to become scenes. Dialogue needs enough attribution and physical context to remain easy to follow.

Vukradin sincerely believes in persuasion, his friends, clean funding, and the studio. His arguments can gather clauses and enthusiasm before landing in a short decision. He is neither a cynical con artist nor an author smirking at his own moral ideals. Music is a natural association, not a metaphor to insert everywhere.

Soma notices practical, bodily things and regards the party as her bale. Her dry humor and impatience coexist with real care. Short practical sentences can form developed paragraphs. Let her age and tortle perspective affect what she notices without turning every observation into an aphorism about age or a catchphrase.

Use the supplied character descriptions to understand the other speakers as well. A supporting character's dialogue should sound like that character, not the current narrator. Characters can misread one another without the prose turning those interpretations into objective truth.

The campaign already incorporates economic and scientific language: supply chains, charitable funds, patronage tiers, marketing, and physics are part of its humor. Quest vocabulary, class names as professions, and named spells are ordinary adventuring language here. Do not scrub these away to imitate generic medieval fantasy. Use existing Faerun terms where applicable, such as tenday. The broad register is relaxed ren-faire fantasy rather than mock-archaic speech.

Modern devices, companies, and real-world place names in table jokes need not become literal features of Neverwinter. Where possible, convey the same meaning or joke in ordinary in-world terms, without inventing a named institution, a magical device, or new lore. Preserve the joke's social function and the character's intention. Do not reproduce player names, recording logistics, or editorial notes as fictional dialogue.

Favor specific perception over stock fantasy ornament and portable aphorisms. Character references and established examples supply perspective and cadence, not a list of signatures that must appear. This experiment does not impose numerical stylistic quotas.
