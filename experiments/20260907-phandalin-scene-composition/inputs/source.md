# The Dead Drop at the House of a Thousand Faces

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party observes Denvar collecting his payment from a hidden dead drop and investigates the building behind it.

- After the several-hour wait, Soma recasts *Find Familiar* and spends another slot to restore her rat.
- Vukradin reunites with the others, receiving their Sending Stone updates and sharing his encounter with Rsolk.
- He frames the spider infestation as a new income opportunity for the music studio: “Finally a business opportunity. Fair Trade Gold.”
- When Denvar wakes, the party follows him to a stone wall adjoining a building.
- Denvar looks around discreetly, removes or opens a stone, retrieves a bag, closes the hiding place, and departs smiling.
- The party interprets the mechanism as a two-way dead drop but cannot initially determine whether the payment was inserted from inside the building or from the street.
- Inspecting the building’s perimeter reveals that it is an early-opening tavern called the House of a Thousand Faces.
- Brewbarry, who had heard of the high-end tavern when he was too poor to afford it, immediately enters while the others remain outside to investigate the compartment.
- The group considers entering under the guise of proposing a performance or booking arrangement, which might give Vukradin access to staff-only areas.
- Vukradin raises the larger mystery of the party’s unexplained fame. The party knows that someone is deliberately amplifying Lathander’s Death’s fame, but not who or why.
- While Brewbarry is inside, the others open their side of the dead drop and use *Knock* to release the inaccessible inner closure.
- Soma sends the rat familiar through the resulting opening.
- The rat enters a windowless basement back office containing a desk, papers, and supplies.
- It finds the unmistakable symbol of the Harpers, identifying the location as a local Harper headquarters.
- The party concludes that the Harpers operate the dead drop, but it does not establish whether they are arranging the ninth crate, receiving it, or acting for another client.
- The DM reminds the group that the Harpers in this campaign are not good people.
- The party contrasts them with the reformed Order of the Gauntlet, which has rejected the political masters who previously manipulated it.
- The House of a Thousand Faces is unusually elegant for the Bluelake District, with mirrors, mannequins wearing antiquated fashions, lush couches, and a properly drawing fire.
- The venue’s luxury gives it a subtly out-of-place quality in the lower-end neighborhood.
- The session ends with Brewbarry inside the House of a Thousand Faces, the others gathered at the establishment after scouting the dead drop, and directions sufficient to revisit Rsolk’s spider-infested tunnels.
