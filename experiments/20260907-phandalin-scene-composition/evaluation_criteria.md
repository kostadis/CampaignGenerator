# Evaluation criteria fixed before generation

This is one exploratory pair, not a statistically reliable result. Both versions
receive the same source and reference text, model, effort, and output scope.
Only one composition-instruction block differs. That block combines a planning
instruction and scene-specific editorial direction; it does not isolate those
two mechanisms from each other.

## Factual integrity: check independently of preference

- Soma restores the familiar during the wait; Vukradin rejoins the others.
- The wererat/spider job is reported as future work and studio income, not done.
- Denvar collects a bag from the concealed wall compartment and leaves.
- The party initially cannot tell which side supplied the payment.
- The building is the House of a Thousand Faces, open early.
- Brewbarry enters; his earlier inability to afford the place is respected.
- A performance/business proposal is discussed, not negotiated or performed.
- The street-side compartment, Knock on the inner closure, and familiar provide access.
- Soma sees the windowless basement office, desk, papers, supplies, Harper symbol.
- Others learn the rat's discovery through communication, not shared sight.
- The Harper headquarters/dead-drop connection is established; their exact role
  in the ninth crate remains uncertain. The contents and destination are not invented.
- Someone amplifies the band's fame; who and why remain unknown.
- The campaign's adverse view of the Harpers and reformed Order of the Gauntlet
  are not replaced with generic setting assumptions.
- The tavern's mirrors, old-fashion mannequins, couches, and properly drawing fire
  fit the source; outside-POV access to them must be plausible.
- Brewbarry remains inside and the others outside. No confrontation, rescue,
  flight, or new commitment is invented to finish the scene.

Invented thoughts, mundane connective movement, reconstructed dialogue, and
emotional interpretation are allowed within the established world. Do not count
their absence from the transcript as factual failure. Distinguish actual new
facts from a character's speculation. Flag uncertain cases rather than inventing
an upstream ruling.

## Reading comparison

Read the anonymous A/B drafts before opening the assignment key. The primary
assistant knows the proposed treatment but should not inspect its mapping until
its initial comparative assessment is written. The user sees neutral A/B labels.
This is presentation blinding, not a double-blind study.

1. Does the scene feel like Soma pursuing something and reacting, rather than a
   series of source statements with transitions?
2. Are the players recognizable? Do selected comic or relational exchanges
   preserve their setup and payoff without an explanatory tail after every joke?
3. Do action, perception, thought, and dialogue develop together? Short paragraphs
   are not intrinsically bad; long ones are not intrinsically good.
4. Does the Harper discovery affect the reading of the situation without inventing
   plot or imposing an unsupported emotional climax?
5. Which version would the user rather keep reading, and which exact passages
   support that preference? A tie or tradeoff is a legitimate result.

Word and paragraph counts are diagnostics only. Do not use a longer draft, fewer
short paragraphs, or a successful format check as proof of literary improvement.
Keep both raw responses, even if one has defects. No repair or reroll before comparison.
