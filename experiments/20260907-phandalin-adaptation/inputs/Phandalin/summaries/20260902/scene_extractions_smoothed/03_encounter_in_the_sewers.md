# Encounter in the Sewers

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### Vukradin becomes separated from the party in the sewers and encounters a group of tunnel dwellers who manage the area.

- Vukradin wanders into a different section of the sewers after the magical area effect makes his intended path seem unworthy of pursuit.
- Rsolk and several associates confront him, with their leader demanding, “How the hell did you get over here?”
- Vukradin answers that he does not know where he is and later explains his presence with, “Because I’m not over there.”
- His poor Insight roll of 7 reveals nothing useful about the men or their motives.
- Vukradin instead rolls 23 on Deception and convinces the group that he is genuinely lost.
- He asks for directions to Lim’s Cafe, claiming that Lim is his friend. This makes Rsolk newly suspicious.
- Recognition then changes the encounter: the leader asks, “Are you that singer?” while one of his associates beams at the possibility that the stranger is really Vukradin.
- Vukradin offers autographs but insists that he must then leave.
- The group offers to escort him personally so he will not become lost again.
- Rsolk explains that he manages the local tunnels—not every tunnel in Neverwinter, but those in this area.
- He recognizes Vukradin’s connection to Zeleen and the Mountain’s Toe Mine, admitting that he had thought Zeleen’s claim of a business relationship was false.
- Vukradin confirms the relationship while noting that the mine has not produced much yet, which makes Rsolk laugh.
- Around two months earlier, creatures began appearing along a tunnel near the floodline. Rsolk has lost men there and now avoids the area.
- A survivor described something spider-like with entirely wrong colors and webs that were visibly unnatural.
- The description reminds the party of strange webbing encountered during an earlier confrontation with such creatures.
- Vukradin offers the party’s aid, explaining that he and his friends possess useful abilities and are seeking ways to earn money.
- Rsolk needs little persuasion to accept the offer.
- Rather than naming a conventional reward immediately, he asks Vukradin to try to secure tickets to the Common Chord.
- Vukradin promises to ask but warns that the party has an arm’s-length relationship with the venue.
- Autographs are exchanged, and the tunnel dwellers escort Vukradin toward familiar ground.
- The group leaves with the information required to relocate Rsolk and pursue the spider-clearing job later.
