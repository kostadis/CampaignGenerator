# Rumors and Preparations at the Common Chord

## Scene summary (from gm-assist, verbatim)

#### The party discusses upcoming social obligations and rumors of a new Drow advisor to Lord Neverember while preparing for a late-night stakeout.

- Aldus has not yet confirmed the exact time of the party’s dinner with Lord Neverember, so the group decides the lord can wait.
- Rumors claim Lord Neverember has acquired a new Drow advisor. Valphine and the others suspect the advisor could be one of her siblings.
- Valphine views House Margaster as competition.
- The GM confirms that funding for Brewbarry’s bathrobe business has been secured, but imports remain expensive because the shipping portals or lanes are disrupted and House Margaster controls the available logistics.
- House Margaster is nevertheless acknowledged to deliver goods on time and on schedule.
- The party plans to reach Lim’s food deliveries by 3:00 a.m., timing the operation around low tide.
- At the Common Chord, a local complains that the venue has become unaffordable because of Vukradin without realizing the bard is nearby.
- Vukradin pitches an independent, low-cost, nonprofit studio where audiences could regularly hear Lathander’s Death and encourages the patron to spread the word.
- His first successful donor contributes ten gold pieces and asks whether contributors receive public recognition. Vukradin promises annual program credit and declares ten gold the “Plus” tier, just short of “Premier.”
- Vukradin approaches five more patrons, rolling persuasion results of 25, 26, 25, 17, and 17.
- One patron recognizes the real Vukradin but has already completed his charitable donations for the year. Vukradin refuses to pressure him.
- Wick does not know Lathander’s Death and is unconvinced that Vukradin is authentic, but enthusiastically supports what he assumes will be a music school.
- Sabbatha contributes while openly admiring Brewbarry’s dedication to his distinctive, “bulging” and “rippling” appearance.
- Old Hesp recognizes Vukradin and recalls attending when the bard promised refunds to anyone who did not rate the performance ten out of ten.
- Old Hesp complains that everyone at the Common Chord now has to look fashionable and that Petra has turned it into a money-making operation.
- He remembers when Brewbarry’s table lacked its leather finish and plaque and when the venue did not serve fancy food.
- Wick, Sabbatha, Old Hesp, and Ondrelle Kass are recorded for future program credits.
- The evening raises 20 gp for the studio; Petra’s separate 500 gp likeness payment remains pending until the performance two days later.
- The party learns from ambient tavern conversation that Neverwinter’s sewers experience two high and two low tides each day.
- They estimate that entering at low tide gives them roughly three or four useful hours before conditions become increasingly uncomfortable.
