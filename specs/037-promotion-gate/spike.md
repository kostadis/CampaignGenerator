## What existing machinery can and cannot prove

- The authority record format can carry `subject`, `claim_key`, `normalized_value`, audience, interval, and projection references. If reviewed records encode both assertions, exact incompatible values over overlapping scopes can be compared deterministically.
- This campaign does not currently contain a structured authority ledger or migration that encodes the five pairs. Its evidence is Markdown prose, generated projections, summaries, and GM notes.
- Existing document review proves exact-file sign-off, freshness, dependencies, pointers, outline, and audience constraints. It does not extract these semantic relations or prove that a citation supports the asserted meaning.
- Therefore an optional extractor may propose candidates and locations, but a GM must settle actor assignment, faction/posture, identity/certainty, contractual supersession, and narrative ordering. Promotion itself can remain deterministic by consuming only completed, freshness-bound review decisions.
