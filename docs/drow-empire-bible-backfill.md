# Drow Empire: bible backfill, end to end

2026-11-13 · exported from the shared doc https://claude.ai/artifact/BoxL6oaVEBnr3N6PG7SqHL

In three days (2026-11-11 to 11-13), one hand-written campaign bible became a fully cross-checked campaign: 52 chapters, 52 verified enhanced summaries, an entity registry of 411 names, a ruled Greyhawk calendar and a players' calendar page. Every spelling, identity, date and wording was ruled by the GM. Claude found, counted, drafted and applied; it decided nothing.

The campaign is *Drow Empire* (the bible is `docs/Drow Conspiracy.md`, about 111,000 words), played through the 21 Dan Hass Drow Campaign books. It had no recordings, so the bible was the only source. The process is the reusable `/gm-bible-backfill` skill (`../gm-assistant/skills/gm-bible-backfill/SKILL.md`), first run on Storm King's Thunder and then on Curse of Strahd.

All paths below are relative to `drow-empire/` in the campaigns repo. Each stage shipped as its own pull request, PRs #686 to #705 in the campaigns repo.

## The ground rules

Four rules held through every stage. They are why the output can be trusted without rereading the whole bible.

1. **The LLM drafts; the GM decides.** Spelling, identity (is X the same person as Y), dates and wording are precision decisions. Each went to the GM, usually on a review page with Approve / Reject / Discuss per card. An unmarked card was re-asked, never defaulted; a Discuss note was confirmed before it was applied.
2. **Edit the origin, regenerate the replica.** The bible is the origin. `docs/chapters/` is re-split from it after every edit and never hand-edited. `summaries/` is its own origin and `docs/summaries/` is republished from it. `docs/arcs/calendar.md` is generated by a script from the ruled data.
3. **Every edit is scoped and counted.** A fix is applied inside one chapter's block and asserts how many times it matches. A bible-wide replace of a short name once hit 3,001 matches on an earlier campaign; scoping is the fix. The bible is backed up before each pass and diffed after.
4. **A verifier, not self-report, is the gate.** `verify_enhanced.py` checks that every quote in a summary appears verbatim in its chapter and every proper noun exists there. All 52 summaries print `RESULT: CLEAN` after every stage that touched them.

Every name change was shown before → after, and contested spellings were spelled out letter by letter: transposed-letter pairs are the ones a reviewer misses at a glance.

## What was new on this campaign

Five things went beyond the earlier runs. Each became part of the shared tooling or the skill.

- **A local model routes the review queue, and decides nothing.** Clef, a model running on a DGX Spark, scored each concrete fix for how likely the GM was to approve it exactly as written. High scorers went to a bulk list and the rest to individual cards, but the GM still ruled on every item. On the typo pass the GM approved all 351 concrete fixes, including the 126 Clef scored below 80%. The exact text sent to Clef is stored next to each score (`notes/spelling_review/score_clef.py`, `scores.json`; `summaries/chapter_enhance_review/typos/score_clef.py`).
- **Module books as a source of truth.** Names were checked against the 21 DC books in the GM's rpg-library, not just against the bible's majority spelling. Unnamed extras were given names drawn from name lists in the GM's own supplements (Stage 8).
- **The calendar follows the story, including off-world.** The party spends Ch 26–51 off Oerth. The GM ruled that time is counted per realm (the Abyss, Brungril Forge, the Feywild, the giants' plane, Lolth's demi-plane), with the Abyss's Day 1 being the arrival. `docs/arcs/calendar.md` gained Realm and Realm days columns; the Greyhawk dates stay there as the GM's record.
- **What the players cannot know is shown as unknown.** Off Oerth, the bible and summary date lines read e.g. "The Abyss, Day 98 (??? on Oerth)". On the players' page those months collapse into one "???" card.
- **One players' page, ending on the epilogue.** The page holds the Greyhawk calendar, then Beyond Oerth by realm, then the epilogue's "A few years later", which leads into the GM's next campaign. The calendar tool gained four options for this (`realms`, `parts`, `unknown`, `later`), documented in `../gm-assistant/skills/gm-player-calendar/SKILL.md` (PR #703).

Every stage folder follows the same pattern: `build_items.py` drafts the cards, `review.html` is the page the GM marked, `decisions.json` is the GM's saved verdicts, and `apply.py` reads those verdicts and applies only what was approved, under count guards.
