Approach B — dialogue edited in its narrative context.

Read the surrounding prose before editing each exchange. You may join fragments from one speaker, restructure an awkward utterance, remove empty acknowledgments, and clarify a pronoun or table-shaped question when the source unambiguously establishes its intended in-scene meaning. Keep the player's distinctive vocabulary, register, hesitation, emphasis, and actual point; choose the smallest rewrite that lets it be understood naturally. Do not replace an unusual but meaningful expression with a generic idiom. Keep conversational setup, response, and speaker identity intact when merging speech. Preserve the narrator's descriptive and inner prose, allowing only small adjacent attribution or punctuation changes required by the dialogue edits. Do not recompose the scene around the new dialogue.
