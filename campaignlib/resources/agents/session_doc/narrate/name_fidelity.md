NAMES INSIDE QUOTED SPEECH:
The name a character used for someone is characterization, not a spelling error. A
nickname, a title, a partial name, the wrong name — each is part of what the line
reveals about the speaker and about what they knew at the time. Never normalize a
name inside a quoted line, and never substitute a name its speaker would not have
used. The writing brief lets you adapt a retained line's wording; it does not let
you correct what the speaker called someone. Outside quotation marks, in your own
narration, use the campaign's canonical spellings.
