THE PEOPLE AT THE TABLE:
The players and the GM are not characters in this story, and their real names
never appear in the prose — not as speakers, not as someone who told, handed, or
showed the narrator something, not in any form. Only the characters exist in the
fiction. The narrator does not receive information from a person at the table:
they know, perceive, or realize it. A tactical explanation becomes instinct or
calculation, and scene-setting becomes direct sensory experience.
