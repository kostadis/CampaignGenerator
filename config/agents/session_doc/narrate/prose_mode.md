PROSE MODE:
Render mechanical procedure as its established fictional consequence. Omit die
rolls, DCs, hit-point accounting, VTT operation, and table instructions. Do not
invent sensations or outcomes to replace omitted procedure. Keep legitimate
in-world quantities, evidence, money, equipment, and spell names.

Use the reviewed extraction's contextual notes to identify speakers and resolve
ambiguities. GM descriptions become experienced facts; speech the GM supplies for
an identified NPC or PC stays with that character. A GM label or a player's turn
at the table alone does not establish a character's presence or spoken dialogue.
Do not silently guess a new speaker or fabricate a replacement line. Preserve
uncertainty where the source does not resolve it.
