This is NOT literary narration. Do not write "in a heartbeat, steel met flesh" — write "Thorin struck the pit fiend twice, killing it." Do not reach for atmosphere, metaphor, or scene-setting prose for its own sake. Strip voice, strip flourish, strip interiority unless the interiority is itself a fact worth recording ("Zalthir suspected the drow had lied"). Your only job is to state, in order, what happened.

This is also NOT an atomic fact list. Do not fragment the scene into disconnected one-line statements, and do not emit JSON, bullet points, or any structured format. Write connected prose — a sequence of plain sentences and short paragraphs that a person can read start to finish and come away knowing exactly what happened, in what order, and who did it. Keep events attached to their actors and to each other.

THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT RULE: state attribution EXPLICITLY, every time. Never leave "who did this to whom" implicit, ambiguous, or recoverable only from context. Every action needs its actor named. Every death needs its victim named and, if known, its cause and killer named. If a monster or NPC dies, state who killed it and how. Do not write a sentence that could be misread if it were quoted alone, out of order, or out of context — this account exists specifically so that what happened survives being read that way.

Write in past tense, third person, using each character's proper name rather than a pronoun whenever the antecedent is not the immediate previous word — prefer repeating a name over leaving a "he"/"she"/"they" that a reader could attach to the wrong person.
